## Role in the Development Flow

This document serves as the **master guide** for all work in the 40-series playground. It defines how the three core flows interact to keep the project aligned, evidence-driven, and progressive.

## The Three Core Flows

### 1. Forward Flow
20-series (Guidance & Intent) → 40-series (Exploration & Evidence) → 50-series (Design Decisions) → 10-series (Canonical Requirements)

### 2. Backward Flow
Evidence and discoveries from the 40-series flow backward to refine the 50-series and 10-series.

### 3. Iterative Design Flow
When the 50-series makes significant design decisions, it drives **targeted iterations** back into the 40-series playground for validation.

**This iterative loop between 40 and 50 is expected and encouraged.**

## Documentation Requirement During Iteration

When performing an **Iterative Design Flow** update in any 40.xx module, the following documents **SHALL** explicitly record the contribution of each flow:

- `software_description.md`
- `requirements_delta.md`
- `verification_capsule.md`

Each document must clearly state:

- Which parts were driven by **Forward Flow** (20-series guidance)
- Which parts were driven by **Backward Flow** (evidence from previous prototypes)
- Which parts were driven by **Iterative Design Flow** (new decisions from 50-series)

A short **Agreement Statement** must also be included explaining why the three flows are in agreement (or noting any remaining tension).

This creates a clear breadcrumb trail and prevents architectural drift.

The 40_thought_simulator_playground is an **experimental environment** where we test ideas, discover practical challenges, generate evidence, and validate architectural intent from the 20-series.

- The 40-series remains exploratory — nothing here is final.

## Verification Completion Requirements

**After completing or updating verification in the 30-series**, the following **MUST** be done before considering the iteration complete:

- Run the verification capsule (`verification_capsule.md` + associated `.py` if present)
- Update `requirements_delta.md` with the three-flow breakdown
- Update `software_description.md` with the three-flow alignment statement
- Ensure all three documents clearly show Forward, Backward, and Iterative contributions

**Please refer to 30.00_verification_guide.md** for detailed verification procedures and standards.
