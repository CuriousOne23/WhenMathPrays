## Role in the Development Flow

This document serves as the **master guide** for all work in the 40-series playground. It defines how the three core flows interact to keep the project aligned, evidence-driven, and progressive.

## The Three Core Flows

### 1. Forward Flow
20-series (Guidance & Intent) → 40-series (Exploration & Evidence) → 50-series (Design Decisions) → 10-series (Canonical Requirements)

### 2. Backward Flow
Evidence and discoveries from the 40-series flow backward to refine the 50-series and 10-series.

### 3. Iterative Design Flow
When the 50-series makes significant design decisions, it drives **targeted iterations** back into the 40-series playground for validation.

**This iterative loop between 40 and 50 is expected and encouraged.**

## Documentation Requirement During Iteration

When performing an **Iterative Design Flow** update in any 40.xx module:

The `software_description.md` **SHALL** explicitly document:

- Which parts were driven by **Forward Flow** (20-series guidance)
- Which parts were driven by **Backward Flow** (evidence from previous prototypes)
- Which parts were driven by **Iterative Design Flow** (new decisions from 50-series)

It must also include a short statement explaining why the three flows are in agreement (or note any remaining tension).

This ensures transparency, traceability, and architectural coherence.

## Role in the Development Flow
This document defines the desired behavior for the Governing Basin prototype. It is guided by the 20-series and refined through iteration with the 50-series.

## 1. Purpose

Define a deterministic **Governing Basin (GB)** as a **non-mutating supervisory subsystem** with a minimal 3-tier hierarchy.

The GB **SHALL**:
- Monitor stability through specialized signal monitors
- Integrate signals and make supervisory decisions
- Apply actions only through safe boundaries

**Core Principle**: The GB operates strictly as a supervisory layer (20.10 + 20.16).

---

## 2. 3-Tier Structure (from 50.36)

- **Tier 1 — Signal Monitors**: Drift, Oscillation, Contradiction, Population
- **Tier 2 — Supervisory Integrator**: Core decision logic
- **Tier 3 — Boundary Enforcer**: Applies actions via safe boundaries only

---

## 3. Non-Goals (Phase B)

- Full production implementation
- Learned components inside GB
- Semantic reasoning
- Complex multi-step planning
