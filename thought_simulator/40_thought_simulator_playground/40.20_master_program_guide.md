## Role in the Development Flow

This document serves as the **master guide** for all work in the 40-series playground. It defines how the three core flows interact to keep the project aligned, evidence-driven, and progressive.

## The Three Core Flows

### 1. Forward Flow
20-series (Guidance & Intent) → 40-series (Exploration & Evidence) → 50-series (Design Decisions) → 10-series (Canonical Requirements)

This is the primary direction of progress.

### 2. Backward Flow
Evidence and discoveries from the 40-series flow backward to refine the 50-series and 10-series.  
Failures, limitations, and unexpected behaviors discovered in the playground are **highly valuable** and must be documented.

### 3. Iterative Design Flow
When the 50-series makes significant design decisions, it may drive **targeted iterations** back into the 40-series playground for validation.

**This iterative loop between 40 and 50 is expected and encouraged.** It prevents painting ourselves into architectural corners while maintaining discipline.

## Documentation Requirement During Iteration

When performing an **Iterative Design Flow** update in any 40.xx module:

The `software_description.md` **SHALL** explicitly document:

- Which parts were driven by **Forward Flow** (20-series guidance)
- Which parts were driven by **Backward Flow** (evidence from previous prototypes)
- Which parts were driven by **Iterative Design Flow** (new decisions from 50-series)

It must also include a short statement explaining why the three flows are in agreement (or note any remaining tension).

This ensures transparency, traceability, and architectural coherence.

## Purpose

The 40_thought_simulator_playground is an **experimental environment** where we test ideas, discover practical challenges, generate evidence, and validate architectural intent from the 20-series.

## Key Principles for 40-series Work

- All prototypes must respect the architectural principles in **20.10**.
- Exploration is primarily driven by 20-series guidance.
- Every major experiment should produce **verifiable evidence** (verification capsules).
- The 40-series remains exploratory — nothing here is final.
