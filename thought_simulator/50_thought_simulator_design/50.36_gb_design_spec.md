## 1. Design Overview

The Governing Basin (GB) is a **dedicated supervisory subsystem** that operates on read-only snapshots of the Thought Simulator state. It does **not** perform meaning construction or mutate TP/MTP semantic content.

**Core Design Principle (from 20.10 & 20.16):**  
GB is a **non-mutating arbitrator and governor** only.

---

## 2. Core Design Requirements

**GB-001:** GB SHALL be implemented as a separate module that receives read-only state snapshots.

**GB-002:** GB SHALL make deterministic decisions and SHALL log them with:
- action
- reason_code
- confidence
- affected_components
- timestamp

**GB-003:** GB SHALL support at least these supervisory actions:
- Continue
- Dampen
- Slow
- Stop / Safe-Mode

**GB-004:** GB SHALL detect and respond to the following conditions:
- High ΔH% drift
- Oscillation
- High IB population
- High contradiction level (messy input)

**GB-005:** GB SHALL maintain intervention frequency tracking.

**GB-006:** All GB operations SHALL be fully deterministic and replayable from seed + input.
