## 1. Purpose

This protocol defines the **controlled downward flow** from 50-series design work (including wave-level 50 insight) back into the 10.50 design requirements layer.

The 10.50 layer serves as the **realization-ready design requirements anchor** (see 30.00 and 50.05). 50 design specs are built on top of approved 10.50 + 30 evidence. However, 50 construction and insight frequently surface:

- Gaps or ambiguities in the 10.50 anchors.
- New or refined contracts, invariants, or realization details that belong at the 10.50 level.
- Traceability or evidence weaknesses that should be recorded as updates to 10.50 (with possible 30 follow-up).

This protocol ensures such insights are captured **downward** into 10.50 in a governed way, rather than remaining only inside 50 docs or ad-hoc notes. It complements the existing forward promotion (40 → 10.50/30 → 50) and the controlled backward flow from 20/10 (see promotion_protocol.md §12).

**Key principle:** 10.50 remains the authoritative "requirements" bridge. 50 is the "design" that realizes it. Flow down keeps 10.50 current as design work matures the understanding.

## 2. Triggers

This flow down is initiated when any of the following occur during 50 work:

- Wave 50 insight step (40.510 §4.2.2 step 3) produces findings that affect 10.50 anchors (e.g., "10.50.180 requires additional on-TP gate semantics from W3 evidence").
- During 50.05 construction or maintenance of a 50.xx design spec, the author identifies that the source 10.50 is incomplete, outdated, or insufficiently "realization-ready".
- 50 design decisions introduce new normative details (contracts, failure modes, determinism rules, observability) that should be promoted to the 10.50 level for future 50 work and traceability.
- Review of a 50 spec against its 10.50 anchor reveals drift (see 50.00 index or DESIGN_SYNC_LOGs).

**Not triggered by:** Purely internal 50 implementation details, 50-only design patterns, or exploratory speculation. Only items that should affect the canonical requirements anchor in 10.50.

## 3. Process Steps

1. **Identify affected 10.50 peers**  
   List the specific `10.50.xx_*.md` files (e.g., 10.50.180_tr_requirements.md). Reference the originating 50 doc(s) and/or wave insight memo.

2. **Draft the downward change**  
   Propose concrete updates to the 10.50 file(s):
   - New or revised HLR/LLR statements.
   - Additional realization details (e.g., exact field semantics, boundary behaviors, three-flow notes).
   - Updated source/evidence citations (40 capsules/artifacts + 30 peers where present).
   - Impact on traceability (cross-refs to 50.00, 30, 20).

   Include a short **Downward Flow Rationale** section in the proposal (or in the execution log).

3. **Synchronize 50.00 Design Traceability Index**  
   Update [50.00_design_traceability_index.md](../50_thought_simulator_design/50.00_design_traceability_index.md) for any changed mappings, added rows, or status notes. This is mandatory (see index maintenance rules).

4. **Create execution record**  
   Produce a log entry in `10_thought_simulator_req/docs/` (use or extend DESIGN_SYNC_LOG_*.md or a new `FLOW_DOWN_50_to_10.50_*.md` following the style of existing BACKFLOW/DESIGN_SYNC logs).
   - Date, triggering 50 artifact(s) or wave.
   - Before/after summary of 10.50 changes.
   - Three-flow attribution for the change (Forward from 20/40 evidence, Iterative from 50 design insight, any 30 verification impact).
   - Impact assessment (on other 50 specs, 30 peers, future waves).

5. **Human review and approval**  
   - At minimum: explicit sign-off by one of the core reviewers (CuriousOne23, CP, or delegated).
   - Record approver + date in the log and in the updated 10.50 file (Update History section).
   - For wave-related flow downs, also note in the 40.510 §4.2.4 wave log.

6. **Optional/conditional cascade**  
   - If the 10.50 change implies that prior 30 evidence is now insufficient, record a note for 30 follow-up (may trigger a 30 update or new verification scenario in the relevant 30 peer or wave note).
   - The 30.00 guide already requires 10.50 + 30 pairing for promoted status; flow down may cause a temporary "10.50 updated pending 30 refresh" state (document in 30.01 Notes).

7. **Verification of equivalence**  
   After the update, confirm no breakage in existing 50 specs that cite the 10.50 (similar to the "Forward-Equivalence State" checks in promotion_protocol backward flow). Record the result in the log.

## 4. Required Content in Updated 10.50 Files

When applying a flow-down change, the 10.50 file **SHALL** include:

- Clear citation of the originating 50 design doc and/or wave insight memo.
- A "Downward Flow Notes" or "50 Insight Integration" subsection (can be under Update History or a dedicated section).
- Updated three-flow breakdown if the change alters the balance of sources.
- Any new or changed "Not yet realized in 50" or "Realized in 50.xxx" markers.

## 5. Integration with Existing Processes

- **Waves (40.510):** After "50 insight" for a wave, any flow-down items are executed before the wave decision (continue / 50-note / etc.) is finalized in §4.2.4. The wave note may be updated with a "10.50 refinements applied" subsection.
- **50.05 construction:** 50.05 §3 now references this protocol for any identified 10.50 refinements discovered mid-construction.
- **promotion_protocol.md:** This is a specialized downward transaction. It inherits the general promotion rules (evidence package, review, no pollution of canonical layers) and the requirement for explicit direction confirmation if ambiguous.
- **30.00:** Flow down to 10.50 may require or recommend parallel 30 activity, but does not automatically invalidate existing 30 peers. 30.01 and wave notes should be consulted/updated as needed.
- **50.00 Index:** Always synchronized (see §51 of 50.00).

## 6. Anti-Drift and Completeness Rules

- 10.50 must not drift ahead of 50 without justification (50 is the realization; 10.50 is the anchor).
- Conversely, 50 specs must not claim realization of details that are not yet reflected in their 10.50 anchor (update 10.50 first via this protocol, or note as "target for next 10.50 flow down").
- Logs must be created; silent updates to 10.50 during 50 work are not permitted.

## 7. Examples

- W3 50 insight on TR on-TP identifies additional `TP.TR` field population rules → flow down updates 10.50.180 (FLOW section + new requirements), syncs 50.00, notes in W3 wave log, and records in a DESIGN_SYNC_LOG or FLOW_DOWN log. May prompt 30.180 refresh for the new details.
- During 50.150 TP design, discovery that commit_id boundary semantics need stronger determinism statement in 10.50.150 → direct 10.50 update + log + 50.00 sync.
