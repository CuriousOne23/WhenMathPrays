## Role in Development Flow
This document translates exploratory evidence from 40.130 and verification evidence from 30.130 into concrete **design requirements** for the Global Brain (GB) subsystem. It feeds into the final canonical specification in the 10-series.

## Purpose
Define the design-level requirements for the Governing Basin (GB) as a **non-mutating supervisory subsystem**, based on 20-series guidance and 40.130 prototype evidence.

**These requirements SHALL be normative and SHALL govern all GB implementations across platforms.**

## Key Design Requirements

**HLR-10.50.130-001:** GB SHALL operate exclusively as a supervisory layer and SHALL NOT perform direct meaning construction or mutate TP/MTP semantic content.

**HLR-10.50.130-002:** GB SHALL make deterministic supervisory decisions based on read-only global state snapshots.

**HLR-10.50.130-003:** GB SHALL support at minimum the following supervisory actions: Continue, Dampen, Slow, Stop/Safe-Mode.

**HLR-10.50.130-004:** GB SHALL detect and respond to high ΔH% drift, oscillation, high IB population, and high contradiction/messy input levels.

**HLR-10.50.130-005:** All GB decisions SHALL include a reason_code and confidence score for auditability.

**HLR-10.50.130-006:** GB SHALL maintain intervention frequency tracking and bounded resource awareness.

**HLR-10.50.130-007:** GB SHALL operate only at formally defined safe boundaries, and the 10-series SHALL define the complete set of allowable supervisory boundary operations.

**HLR-10.50.130-008:** All GB supervisory decisions SHALL be logged with deterministic structure, SHALL be replayable, and SHALL support full auditability.

## Design Decisions Informed by 40.130 Evidence

- Simple rule-based decision engine is viable as a starting point.
- Distinction between drift, oscillation, and contradiction is important and feasible.
- Contradiction_level is a useful supervisory signal.
- Deterministic logging and reason codes work effectively.

## Open Design Questions (to be resolved in 10-series)

- Exact threshold values for triggering supervisory actions.
- Optimal combination logic when multiple signals (drift, oscillation, contradiction, population) are present.
- Formal semantic definitions for drift, oscillation, and contradiction SHALL be finalized in the 10-series.
- Acceptable intervention frequency ranges and policies.
