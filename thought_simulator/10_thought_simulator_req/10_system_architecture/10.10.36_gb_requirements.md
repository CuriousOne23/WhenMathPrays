# 10.10.36 — Global Basin (GB) Requirements

## 1. Purpose

Define the canonical, normative requirements for the Global Basin (GB), the subsystem responsible for global supervisory evaluation, drift/oscillation detection, IB governance, OB decomposition governance, COP gating, and deterministic supervisory action selection within the Thought Simulator (TS).

GB is a **non‑mutating**, **asynchronous**, **deterministic** supervisory subsystem operating strictly at **safe boundaries**.

---

## 2. Scope

GB SHALL:

- evaluate global semantic stability  
- detect drift, oscillation, divergence, and convergence  
- supervise IB creation, evolution, merge/split, promotion, retirement  
- supervise OB decomposition and OB retirement  
- supervise COP proposal gating  
- issue bounded supervisory actions  
- operate asynchronously relative to TS  
- apply actions only at deterministic safe boundaries  
- maintain deterministic supervisory logs  
- operate within a bounded TCU envelope  
- degrade deterministically when over budget  

GB SHALL NOT:

- mutate TP or MTP meaning‑construction state  
- read OB/RB/TB/IB/InB/OuB internal state  
- read MTP internal state  
- block TS execution  
- perform unbounded or data‑dependent work  
- use wall‑clock precedence  
- bypass TS arbitration or safe‑boundary rules  
- perform semantic inference  

---

## 3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)

### 3.1 Supervisory Separation

- **HLR‑36‑001** — GB SHALL NOT mutate TP or MTP meaning‑construction state.  
- **HLR‑36‑002** — GB SHALL read only lane‑local TP snapshots and MPs.  
- **HLR‑36‑003** — GB SHALL NOT access OB/RB/TB/IB/InB/OuB internal state.

### 3.2 Safe‑Boundary Gating

- **HLR‑36‑010** — GB SHALL apply supervisory actions only at deterministic safe boundaries.  
- **HLR‑36‑011** — Unsafe‑boundary events SHALL defer supervisory action.

### 3.3 Supervisory Actions

- **HLR‑36‑020** — GB SHALL support the supervisory action taxonomy:  
  `Stop, Slow, Dampen, Escalate, SafeMode, ReduceDepth, SuspendInquiry, Crop, Prune, Reshape, Kill, Approve, Deny, None`.  
- **HLR‑36‑021** — GB SHALL select actions deterministically for identical inputs.

### 3.4 TCU Envelope

- **HLR‑36‑030** — GB SHALL operate within a bounded TCU envelope (min/typ/max).  
- **HLR‑36‑031** — GB SHALL degrade deterministically when exceeding TCU budget.  
- **HLR‑36‑032** — **TCU fallback SHALL override event‑type supervisory actions.**

### 3.5 IB Governance

- **HLR‑36‑040** — GB SHALL approve/deny IB creation deterministically.  
- **HLR‑36‑041** — GB SHALL supervise IB evolution.  
- **HLR‑36‑042** — GB SHALL supervise IB merge/split.  
- **HLR‑36‑043** — GB SHALL supervise IB promotion/retirement.

### 3.6 OB Governance

- **HLR‑36‑050** — GB SHALL supervise OB decomposition.  
- **HLR‑36‑051** — GB SHALL supervise OB retirement.

### 3.7 COP Gating

- **HLR‑36‑060** — GB SHALL evaluate COP proposals deterministically.  
- **HLR‑36‑061** — COP gating SHALL NOT mutate TP/MTP state.

### 3.8 Deterministic Logging

- **HLR‑36‑070** — GB SHALL maintain append‑only supervisory logs.  
- **HLR‑36‑071** — Logs SHALL include rationale, action, sequence, safe‑boundary flag, and TCU diagnostics.

### 3.9 Deterministic Replay

- **HLR‑36‑080** — Identical effective input SHALL produce identical supervisory output.  
- **HLR‑36‑081** — Ordering SHALL derive from sequence tokens, not timestamps.

## 5. Functional Boundaries

GB SHALL:

- classify supervisory requests  
- evaluate global semantic stability  
- detect drift/oscillation  
- supervise IB/OB/COP lifecycle events  
- issue bounded supervisory actions  
- apply actions only at safe boundaries  
- operate asynchronously  
- maintain deterministic logs  

GB SHALL NOT:

- mutate semantic state  
- block TS execution  
- bypass arbitration  
- perform inference  
- use wall‑clock ordering  

---

## 6. Supervisory Modes

GB SHALL support:

- Normal Mode  
- SafeMode (fallback)  
- SuspendInquiry  
- ReduceDepth  
- Prune/Crop/Reshape  
- Kill (hard supervisory stop)  

SafeMode SHALL be triggered by TCU fallback.

---

## 7. Deterministic Invariants

- identical input → identical output  
- deterministic TCU envelope  
- deterministic fallback  
- deterministic safe‑boundary gating  
- deterministic logs  
- deterministic replay
