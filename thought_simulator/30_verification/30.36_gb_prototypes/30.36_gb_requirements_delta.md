## Requirements Delta Summary

**Strongly Demonstrated:**
- Supervisory-only behavior (no semantic mutation)
- Deterministic decision making with reason codes
- Detection of high ΔH% drift, oscillation, and high IB population
- Basic contradiction/messy input response

**Partially Demonstrated:**
- Nuanced supervisory action selection
- Long-term stability under sustained load
- Intervention frequency management

**Not Covered in this Prototype:**
- Full IB lifecycle supervision (promotion/retirement)
- Learned component oversight
- Production performance optimizations

## Open Questions / Gaps for 10-series

- Exact thresholds for triggering supervisory actions
- Final taxonomy and semantics of reason codes
- Acceptable intervention frequency ranges
- Precise definition of "safe boundary" operations
- Rules for combining multiple signals (drift + contradiction + population)

## Verdict
**Good exploratory evidence gathered.**  
This prototype provides solid data to help mature the 10-series and 50-series design documents.
