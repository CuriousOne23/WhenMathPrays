# **20.33 — CIL Requirements (Conversation Integration Layer)**  
**Document ID:** 20.33_cil_requirements  
**Version:** 0.4 (TP‑Field Cleanup)  
**Date:** 2026‑07‑17  
**Status:** Draft — Requirements (CIL)

---

## **Purpose**

CIL is the Conversation Integration Layer.  
It serves as the deterministic, cycle‑boundary normalization and freezing layer between COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot and CEx’s rigid intake requirements.

CIL does **not** integrate new conversational material; COB performs all structural integration under CST signals.

CIL transforms:

- **COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot**, and  
- **TP.process + TP.metadata structural cues derived from IE**

into a **bounded, ordered, replay‑safe intake packet** for CEx.

CIL generates certainty, ambiguity, stability, and structural‑hint fields **without performing semantic interpretation**.  
CEx is the sole consumer of CIL output.

---

## **Position in Architecture**

```
COB → CIL → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1)
OuBA → CIL
CST → CIL
```

CIL is not part of any TS pipeline.  
It provides read‑only access to COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot and generates the intake packet for CEx.

CIL does not perform structural merges or splits; structural changes are handled exclusively by COB under CST signals.

# **Normative Requirements (TP‑Field Cleanup)**

### **HLR‑20.33‑001**  
CIL SHALL normalize and freeze **TP.process.structural_cues** and **TP.metadata.intake_metadata** together with COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot to produce the intake packet for CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑002**  
CIL SHALL generate referent, temporal, and discourse certainty flags, field‑importance hints, and ambiguity flags.

### **HLR‑20.33‑003**  
CIL SHALL provide read‑only access to COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot.

### **HLR‑20.33‑004**  
CIL SHALL NOT maintain **TP.semantic**, **TP.process**, **TP.metadata**, MTP state, lineage, commitments, referents, active objects, or conversation‑objects.

### **HLR‑20.33‑005**  
CIL SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, scoring, candidate generation, or pipeline participation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑006**  
CIL output SHALL be consumed exclusively by CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑007**  
CIL output SHALL be consumed exclusively by CEx for deterministic intake packet formation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑008**  
CIL SHALL maintain determinism and replay equivalence for all generated flags and packets.

### **HLR‑20.33‑010**  
CIL SHALL NOT own clarification FIFO or supervisory escalation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑011**  
CIL SHALL NOT embed semantic meaning or interpretation into any view consumed by CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑012**  
CIL SHALL NOT interact with ISc, Merge, TPU, IB, TB, or RBU except through approved supervisory wires.

# **CIL Intake Packet Structure (TP‑Field Cleanup)**

### **HLR‑20.33‑013**  
CIL SHALL produce an intake packet conforming to:

```json
CILIntakePacket {
  identity_selection: IdentitySelectionBlock,
  certainty: CertaintyBlock,
  ambiguity: AmbiguityBlock,
  stability: StabilityBlock,
  structural_hints: StructuralHintBlock,
  referent_mapping: ReferentMappingBlock,
  register_hint: string,
  timestamps: { generated_turn: TurnID }
}
```

### **HLR‑20.33‑020**  
CIL SHALL derive identity selection, certainty, ambiguity, stability, and structural hints solely from:

- **TP.process.structural_cues**,  
- **TP.metadata.intake_metadata**, and  
- **COB/CST snapshot**,  

and SHALL NOT modify or reinterpret COB’s structural content.

### **HLR‑20.33‑022**  
CIL SHALL guarantee that identical **TP.process.structural_cues**, **TP.metadata.intake_metadata**, and COB/CST snapshots produce identical intake packets.

# **TP‑Field Cleanup for Register Hint**

### **HLR‑20.33‑029**  
CIL SHALL include a register_hint derived from:

- short‑term cues in **TP.process.register_cues**, and  
- the COB register field.

### **HLR‑20.33‑031**  
CIL SHALL derive register_hint deterministically from **TP.process.register_cues** and COB register fields without semantic interpretation.

# **TP‑Field Cleanup for Ordering Metrics**

### **HLR‑20.33‑032**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer recency (`last_referred`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑033**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer frequency (`total_referrals`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑034**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer density (`recent_referrals`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑035**  
CIL SHALL include COB’s ordering score (`ordering_score`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑036**  
CIL SHALL expose recency, frequency, density, and ordering_score without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑037**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic ordering of identity layers using ordering_score provided by COB.

### **HLR‑20.33‑038**  
CIL SHALL NOT compute recency, frequency, density, or ordering_score; it SHALL only reflect COB’s values.

### **HLR‑20.33‑039**  
CIL SHALL include these fields in IdentitySelectionBlock:

```json
IdentitySelectionBlock {
  primary_layer_id: StableID | null,
  secondary_layer_ids: [StableID],
  layer_ranking: [{ layer_id: StableID, score: float }],
  last_referred: TurnID,
  total_referrals: int,
  recent_referrals: int,
  ordering_score: float
}
```

# **Clarifying‑Field Extraction (New)**

### **HLR‑20.33‑040**  
CIL **shall** extract clarifying fields, clarifying subfields, and hierarchical clarifying sub‑subfields (up to four levels) from COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot.

### **HLR‑20.33‑041**  
CIL **shall** include clarifying fields, clarifying subfields, and clarifying importance scores in the intake packet provided to CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑042**  
CIL **shall** preserve clarifying‑field topology when constructing the intake packet, including hierarchical relationships and bounded limits.

### **HLR‑20.33‑043**  
CIL **shall** enforce bounded limits on clarifying fields (maximum 10), clarifying subfields (maximum 100), and hierarchical depth (maximum 4 levels), dropping excess entries with extraction‑audit records.

### **HLR‑20.33‑044**  
CIL **shall** treat clarifying fields and clarifying subfields as read‑only and shall not modify their values, importance scores, or provenance.

### **HLR‑20.33‑045**  
CIL **shall** include clarifying‑field metadata in the following intake‑packet blocks:

```
CILIntakePacket {
  ...
  clarifying_fields: ClarifyingFieldBlock,
  clarifying_importance: ClarifyingImportanceBlock,
  clarifying_topology: ClarifyingTopologyBlock
}
```

### **HLR‑20.33‑046**  
CIL **shall** guarantee deterministic clarifying‑field extraction such that identical COB snapshots and identical TP structural cues produce identical clarifying‑field output.

### **HLR‑20.33‑047**  
CIL **shall** record all clarifying‑field drops, truncations, and topology compressions in **CE.metadata.extraction_audit**.

### **HLR‑20.33‑048**  
CIL **shall** preserve clarifying‑field continuity across turns by reflecting clarifying fields from the selected identity layer in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑049**  
CIL **shall** include clarifying‑field metadata in IdentitySelectionBlock when identity selection is ambiguous or indeterminate.

### **HLR‑20.33‑050**  
CIL **shall** include clarifying‑field metadata in fallback identity selection and mark continuity status as `"undetermined"` when applicable.

## **Next‑Turn Context Integration (TP.next_context_fields Cross‑Reference)**

**HLR‑20.33‑051**  
CIL SHALL read `TP.next_context{}` deterministically as defined in **20.105_tp_requirements.md**; CIL SHALL NOT read next‑turn context from any other TP fields.

**HLR‑20.33‑052**  
CIL SHALL treat all fields in `TP.next_context{}` as read‑only; CIL SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, or transform next‑turn context values.

**HLR‑20.33‑053**  
CIL SHALL place next‑turn context fields from `TP.next_context{}` into the intake packet for CEx exactly as defined in the CILIntakePacket schema, without renaming, omission, or structural modification.

**HLR‑20.33‑054**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields in the following intake‑packet blocks:

```
CILIntakePacket {
  ...
  next_context: NextContextBlock
}
```

**HLR‑20.33‑055**  
CIL SHALL include the following fields in `NextContextBlock` exactly as defined in 20.105:  
`topic`, `stance`, `intent`, `register`, `politeness`, `epistemic_shading`, `continuity`, `direction`, `coherence`, `shift_required`, `importance`.

**HLR‑20.33‑056**  
CIL SHALL NOT derive next‑turn context fields from clarifying fields, structural cues, or COB snapshots; next‑turn context SHALL originate exclusively from `TP.next_context{}`.

**HLR‑20.33‑057**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of next‑turn context extraction; identical `TP.next_context{}` values SHALL produce identical `NextContextBlock` output.

**HLR‑20.33‑058**  
CIL SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation when handling next‑turn context fields; next‑turn context SHALL be treated as structural metadata only.

**HLR‑20.33‑059**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields in the intake packet even when identity selection is ambiguous, indeterminate, or fallback‑selected.

**HLR‑20.33‑060**  
CIL SHALL preserve next‑turn context continuity across turns by reflecting `TP.next_context{}` values in the intake packet for CEx without modification.

**HLR‑20.33‑061**  
CIL SHALL NOT compute context coherence or context shift; it SHALL only reflect `TP.next_context.coherence` and `TP.next_context.shift_required` as provided by MCB.

**HLR‑20.33‑062**  
CIL SHALL NOT write next‑turn context fields into clarifying fields; CIL SHALL only place them into the intake packet for CEx.

**HLR‑20.33‑063**  
CIL SHALL NOT interact with truth/done fields, routing fields, structural geometry, ΔH%, lineage, or Path‑B semantics when processing next‑turn context.

**HLR‑20.33‑064**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields in the intake packet timestamps block only through `generated_turn`; no additional temporal metadata SHALL be added.

**HLR‑20.33‑065**  
CIL SHALL treat missing or partially populated `TP.next_context{}` fields as deterministic empty‑context cases and SHALL populate `NextContextBlock` with canonical empty values.

# **Inputs and Outputs (TP‑Field Cleanup)**

### **Inputs (read‑only):**

- Stabilized identity‑layer snapshot from COB  
- **TP.process.structural_cues**  
- **TP.metadata.intake_metadata**  
- Referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field‑importance hints  
- CST stability signals (reflected in COB snapshot)

### **Outputs:**

- Intake packet for CEx containing:
  - Identity‑layer selection  
  - Certainty flags  
  - Ambiguity flags  
  - Stability indicators  
  - Structural hints  
  - Referent mappings  
  - register_hint
