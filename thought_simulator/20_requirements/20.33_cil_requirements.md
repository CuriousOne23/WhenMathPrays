# 20.33 CIL Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic Conversation Integration Layer (CIL) behavior for integrating TP/MTP state with conversation-scoped context under GB supervisory boundaries.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes CIL obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including:
- 20.10 architectural ownership of deterministic TP/MTP conversation integration, FIFO-preserving intake, and deterministic ambiguity escalation.
- 20.10 interface, safe-boundary, profile-precedence, and reject-with-audit invariants for deterministic runtime governance.
- 20.30 functional ownership of CIL intake/routing/classification/escalation behavior and deterministic control-boundary behavior.
- 20.30 cross-module contract obligations for explicit, versioned, testable, and auditable interfaces.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.032-001: CIL SHALL process incoming TP state in deterministic FIFO sequence.
2. HLR-20.032-002: CIL SHALL operate on coherent snapshot sets pinned by deterministic snapshot identifiers.
3. HLR-20.032-003: CIL thread classification SHALL be deterministic and bounded by explicit confidence policy.
4. HLR-20.032-004: CIL SHALL escalate ambiguity to GB via deterministic request channels only.
5. HLR-20.032-005: CIL SHALL NOT directly instantiate inquiry channels without GB approval.
6. HLR-20.032-006: CIL SHALL apply deterministic timeout/default behavior by safety and operation class.
7. HLR-20.032-007: Late human approval SHALL follow deterministic re-entry semantics (ignore, queue, or compensate).
8. HLR-20.032-008: CIL SHALL apply deterministic parser/profile rules for canonicalized inputs.
9. HLR-20.032-009: CIL SHALL enforce deterministic tie-break behavior including deterministic collision fallback.
10. HLR-20.032-010: CIL SHALL preserve deterministic auditability of routing, fallback, and supervisory decisions.
11. HLR-20.032-011: CIL SHALL reject unsupported profile/policy/enum states deterministically with fixed audit reason codes.
12. HLR-20.032-012: CIL SHALL apply execution-signature profile precedence over environment defaults.
13. HLR-20.032-013: CIL SHALL remain platform-independent and deterministic under equivalent signature/profile state.
14. HLR-20.032-014: CIL ordering-critical decisions SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state and SHALL NOT depend on wall-clock precedence.
15. HLR-20.032-015: CIL transitions that alter routing, escalation state, replay/view mode, or integration policy SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with active TS-core cycles.
16. HLR-20.032-016: CIL request/response contracts with TS core and GB SHALL be explicit, versioned, deterministic, and auditable.
17. HLR-20.032-017: CIL SHALL NOT read or mutate OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, or MTP internal implementation state and SHALL operate through approved deterministic interfaces and canonicalized packet/state views.
18. HLR-20.032-018: CIL ambiguity and inquiry control SHALL follow deterministic TS->GB->TS supervisory flow, and CIL SHALL NOT bypass GB approval paths.
19. HLR-20.032-019: On profile/policy activation validation failure, CIL SHALL retain prior valid signature-bound state deterministically and emit fixed audit reason codes.
20. HLR-20.032-020: CIL enum, wire-map, and schema versions SHALL remain frozen per release and SHALL reject unsupported states with deterministic audit reason codes.
21. HLR-20.032-021: CIL reason-code dictionaries SHALL use immutable identifiers, deterministic deprecation rules, and versioned mapping behavior.
22. HLR-20.032-022: CIL SHALL preserve inherited FIFO order across ingestion, classification, and integration output and SHALL NOT reorder, skip, or speculate on sequence state.
23. HLR-20.032-023: CIL late-approval re-entry behavior SHALL apply deterministic policy outcomes and bounded deterministic queue/ignore/compensate behavior.
24. HLR-20.032-024: CIL SHALL log intake, classification, escalation requests, approvals/denials, timeout/default outcomes, and re-entry outcomes in deterministic append-only audit records.
25. HLR-20.032-025: CIL requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
26. HLR-20.032-026: CIL behavior SHALL remain fully testable via deterministic input/output fixtures, deterministic supervisory-event fixtures, and reproducible profile-bound test-oracle behavior.

## Functional Boundaries
- CIL owns TP/MTP conversation intake, deterministic integration, classification, and supervisory escalation request behavior.
- COB owns conversation-object identity, lineage, lifecycle state, and replay/export semantics.
- COP is optional and propose-only; CIL SHALL treat COP-visible outcomes as deterministic external proposals governed by approved supervisory/commit paths.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic FIFO-preserving intake and snapshot coherence under identical state.
- Deterministic classification/tie-break/escalation behavior under equivalent profile state.
- Deterministic timeout/default and late-approval re-entry outcomes.
- Deterministic reject-with-audit behavior for unsupported enum/profile/schema states.
- Deterministic append-only audit evidence for CIL supervisory lifecycle events.
