## Purpose

CIL is the Conversation Integration Layer. It serves as the deterministic, cycle‑boundary normalization and freezing layer between COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot and CEx’s rigid intake requirements. CIL does not integrate new conversational material; COB performs all structural integration under CST signals. CIL transforms the stabilized COB snapshot and TP/IE structural cues into a bounded, ordered, replay‑safe intake packet for CEx. CIL generates certainty, ambiguity, stability, and structural‑hint fields without performing semantic interpretation. CIL’s only consumer is CEx.

---

## Position in Architecture

COB → CIL → CEx → CE → Rest of Path A

OuBA → CIL
CST → CIL

CIL is not part of any TS pipeline. It provides read-only access to COB’s stabilized identity-layer snapshot and generates the intake packet for CEx.

CIL does not perform structural merges or splits of identity layers. Structural changes are handled exclusively by COB under CST signals.

---

## Normative Requirements

**HLR-20.33-001:** CIL SHALL normalize and freeze TP/IE structural cues together with COB’s stabilized identity-layer snapshot to produce the intake packet for CEx.  

**HLR-20.33-002:** CIL SHALL generate referent, temporal, and discourse certainty flags, field-importance hints, and ambiguity flags.  

**HLR-20.33-003:** CIL SHALL provide read-only access to COB’s stabilized identity-layer snapshot.  

**HLR-20.33-004:** CIL SHALL NOT maintain TP/MTP state, lineage, commitments, referents, active objects, or conversation-objects.  

**HLR-20.33-005:** CIL SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, scoring, candidate generation, or pipeline participation.  

**HLR-20.33-006:** CIL SHALL restrict its output to CEx as the sole consumer of the intake packet.  

**HLR-20.33-007:** CIL output SHALL be consumed exclusively by CEx for deterministic intake packet formation.  

**HLR-20.33-008:** CIL SHALL maintain determinism and replay equivalence for all generated flags and packets.  

**HLR-20.33-009:** CIL SHALL NOT write to USP or TP, initiate clarification, or generate candidate sets.  

**HLR-20.33-010:** CIL SHALL NOT own clarification FIFO or supervisory escalation.  

**HLR-20.33-011:** CIL SHALL NOT embed semantic meaning or interpretation into any view consumed by CEx.  

**HLR-20.33-012:** CIL SHALL NOT interact with ISc, Merge, TPU, IB, TB, or RBU except through approved supervisory wires.  

**HLR-20.33-013:** CIL SHALL produce an intake packet that conforms to the following structure:

```json
CILIntakePacket {
  identity_selection: IdentitySelectionBlock,
  certainty: CertaintyBlock,
  ambiguity: AmbiguityBlock,
  stability: StabilityBlock,
  structural_hints: StructuralHintBlock,
  referent_mapping: ReferentMappingBlock,
  register_hint: string,               // e.g. "formal", "casual", "technical", "east_la_lingo"
  timestamps: { generated_turn: TurnID }
}
```

**HLR-20.33-014:** The IdentitySelectionBlock SHALL contain:

```json
IdentitySelectionBlock {
  primary_layer_id: StableID | null,
  secondary_layer_ids: [StableID],
  layer_ranking: [{ layer_id: StableID, score: float }]
}
```

**HLR-20.33-015:** The CertaintyBlock SHALL contain:

```json
CertaintyBlock {
  primary_certainty: float,
  mapping_certainty: float,
  context_certainty: float
}
```

**HLR-20.33-016:** The AmbiguityBlock SHALL contain:

```json
AmbiguityBlock {
  ambiguous_mapping: bool,
  conflicting_cues: bool,
  ambiguity_score: float
}
```

**HLR-20.33-017:** The StabilityBlock SHALL contain:

```json
StabilityBlock {
  stable_context: bool,
  unstable_context: bool,
  collapse_risk: float
}
```

**HLR-20.33-018:** The StructuralHintBlock SHALL contain:

```json
StructuralHintBlock {
  local_cluster_hint: bool,
  local_relation_hint: bool,
  hint_details: {
    cluster_ids: [StableID],
    relation_types: [string]
  }
}
```

**HLR-20.33-019:** The ReferentMappingBlock SHALL contain:

```json
ReferentMappingBlock {
  mappings: [
    {
      referent_id: string,
      layer_id: StableID | null,
      mapping_certainty: float
    }
  ]
}
```

**HLR-20.33-020:** CIL SHALL derive identity selection, certainty, ambiguity, stability, and structural hints solely from TP/IE structural cues and the COB/CST snapshot and SHALL NOT modify or reinterpret COB’s structural content.  

**HLR-20.33-021:** CIL SHALL treat the intake packet as read-only context for CEx and SHALL NOT emit merge, split, or other structural change instructions.  

**HLR-20.33-022:** CIL SHALL guarantee that identical TP/IE cues and COB/CST snapshots produce identical intake packets.  

**HLR-20.33-023:** CIL SHALL NOT force layer creation, deletion, merge, or split.  

**HLR-20.33-024:** CIL SHALL NOT override CST signals.  

**HLR-20.33-025:** CIL SHALL NOT use external state, randomness, or wall-clock timing in intake packet generation.  

**HLR-20.33-026:** CIL SHALL reflect CST stability signals in the StabilityBlock without computing stability metrics itself.  

**HLR-20.33-027:** Structural hints produced by CIL SHALL be advisory only and SHALL NOT be treated as structural instructions by downstream components.  

**HLR-20.33-028:** CIL SHALL preserve determinism, ordering, and replay safety for all fields in the intake packet.  

**HLR-20.33-029:** CIL SHALL include a register_hint in the intake packet derived from short-term cues and the COB register field.  

**HLR-20.33-030:** The register_hint SHALL reflect the dominant subculture or communication style (e.g., "formal", "casual", "technical", "east_la_lingo") for the current turn.  

**HLR-20.33-031:** CIL SHALL derive the register_hint deterministically from TP/IE cues and the COB register field without performing semantic interpretation.

## Inputs and Outputs

**Inputs (read-only):**
- Stabilized identity-layer snapshot from COB
- TP/IE structural cues
- Referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field-importance hints
- CST stability signals (as reflected in the COB snapshot)

**Outputs:**
- Intake packet for CEx containing:
  - Identity-layer selection
  - Certainty flags
  - Ambiguity flags
  - Stability indicators
  - Structural hints
  - Referent mappings
  - register_hint

---

## Functional Boundaries

CIL is strictly non-semantic and reference-only. It does not participate in any TS pipeline and does not perform correction, scoring, or interpretation.

CIL is an integration lens. It does not store conversation objects, maintain lineage, or perform structural operations on identity layers. Structural changes remain the responsibility of COB under CST signals.
