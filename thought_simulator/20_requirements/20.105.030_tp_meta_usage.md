# TP Metadata Usage — Downstream Consumption, Meaning Refinement, and Structural Interpretation

This document defines how downstream primitives consume metadata fields within the Thought Packet (TP). Metadata usage ensures deterministic meaning refinement, stable routing, consistent truth-relation mapping, and coherent conversation-layer continuity.

## Scope

This specification covers downstream consumption rules, read-only constraints, usage boundaries, meaning-refinement dependencies, and structural and routing dependencies.

It does not redefine metadata fields or provenance rules.

## Metadata Usage Model

Downstream primitives consume metadata fields produced upstream. Consumption is read-only, except where a primitive is explicitly authorized to update a specific metadata field.

Metadata usage supports identity-conditioned meaning refinement, truth-relation mapping, structural interpretation, routing-conditioned semantics, entropy trajectory evaluation, and conversation-layer continuity.

## Important TP Layers (Usage Perspective)

- **Intake Layer** — Provides normalization and repair observations.
- **Structural Layer** — Supplies geometry and constraint residue for interpretation.
- **Routing Layer** — Informs arbitration and pathway decisions.
- **Meaning Signal Layer (MSL)** — Supplies qualifier, clarification, stance, shading, intent, direction, coherence, and subculture tokens.
- **Continuity Layer** — Supplies identity anchors, referent lineage, and clarifying metadata.
- **Entropy & Trajectory Layer** — Tracks stability and change over time.

These layers enable precise, traceable consumption by downstream primitives.

## Normative Requirements

Downstream primitives shall treat all metadata fields as read-only unless explicitly authorized to update a specific field. (HLR-20.105.030-001)

Downstream primitives shall preserve upstream metadata observations without modification. (HLR-20.105.030-002)

IdOB shall consume expressive_metadata, normalization_metadata, and structural_metadata when performing identity-conditioned meaning refinement. (HLR-20.105.030-003)

TR shall consume structural_metadata, routing_metadata, and expressive_metadata when mapping truth-relations. (HLR-20.105.030-004)

IdOB and TR shall consume routing_metadata when performing identity-conditioned or truth-conditioned interpretation. (HLR-20.105.030-005)

OuBA shall consume entropy_metadata when producing the SSR-A snapshot. (HLR-20.105.030-006)

COB, CIL, and CST shall consume continuity_metadata when maintaining identity continuity, long-horizon context, and stability evaluation. (HLR-20.105.030-007)

Downstream primitives shall not re-derive upstream observations already encoded in metadata fields. (HLR-20.105.030-008)

Metadata usage shall support deterministic replay, ensuring identical downstream behavior when the same TP is re-executed. (HLR-20.105.030-009)

## Clarifying-Field Consumption

IdOB shall consume clarifying-field metadata when performing identity-conditioned meaning refinement. (HLR-20.105.030-010)

TR shall consume clarifying-field metadata when mapping truth-relations that depend on identity continuity or referent stability. (HLR-20.105.030-011)

OuBA shall consume clarifying-field metadata when producing SSR-A snapshots requiring identity-layer continuity. (HLR-20.105.030-012)

COB, CIL, and CST shall consume clarifying-field metadata when maintaining identity continuity, long-horizon context, and stability evaluation. (HLR-20.105.030-013)

Downstream primitives shall treat clarifying-field metadata as read-only unless explicitly authorized to update clarifying importance or clarifying topology. (HLR-20.105.030-014)

Clarifying-field metadata usage shall support deterministic replay. (HLR-20.105.030-015)

## Downstream Consumption Responsibilities

### IdOB
Consumes expressive_metadata, normalization_metadata, structural_metadata, routing_metadata, and MSL tokens.

### TR
Consumes structural_metadata, routing_metadata, expressive_metadata, and clarifying-field metadata.

### OuBA
Consumes entropy_metadata, routing_metadata, structural_metadata, continuity_metadata, and clarifying-field metadata.

### COB / CIL / CST
Consume continuity_metadata, entropy_metadata, identity_anchors, referent_lineage, and clarifying-field metadata.

### MSL Usage
MSL tokens shall be consumed by CEx, RB, IdOB, and MCB to support qualifier-aware processing. (HLR-20.105.030-016)
