# 20.112 — LI‑prm (Local Inference Primitive)

## 1. Purpose

LI‑prm is the **Local Inference** primitive of Path B.  
It consumes representation‑layer fields, scoring‑layer fields, and continuity_fields produced by CoHI, applies **MLI‑prc**, and commits a meaning‑layer outcome for downstream realization.

LI performs **no** global inference, planning, routing, or surface generation.  
LI does **not** modify continuity_fields, lineage_fields, semantic_core, or any A‑side structure.

---

## 2. Architectural Position

LI is the **second** primitive in Path B:

**CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → ReB → OuBB**

Upstream:  
- CoHI  

Downstream:  
- REx  

LI is the **sole** primitive responsible for meaning commitment in Path B.

---

## 3. Inputs

LI reads only the following:

### 3.1 Representation‑layer fields  
Produced by TPTB.

### 3.2 Scoring‑layer fields  
Produced by TPSF.

### 3.3 continuity_fields  
Produced by CoHI:

- structural_history  
- referential_history  
- conversation_objects  
- topic_threads  
- continuity_markers  

### 3.4 truth_tags  
Produced by A‑side.

### 3.5 safety_flags  
Produced by A‑side.

No other fields may be accessed.

---

## 4. Transfer Function (informative)

MLI‑prc executes in three ordered stages.

---

### Stage 1 — Candidate Generation

Let $R$ be representation‑layer fields and $F$ be scoring‑layer fields.

$$
\mathcal{C} = \text{MLI-candidates}(R,\ F),\quad |\mathcal{C}| \ge 1
$$

Each candidate $c \in \mathcal{C}$ carries a score $\text{score}(c,\ F) \in [0,1]$.

---

### Stage 2 — Continuity Filtering

Let

$$
\Gamma = \\{ \gamma_{\text{sh}},\ \gamma_{\text{rh}},\ \gamma_{\text{co}},\ \gamma_{\text{tt}},\ \gamma_{\text{cm}} \\}
$$

A candidate is continuity‑consistent when:

$$
\text{consistent}(c,\ \Gamma)
= \forall\ \gamma \in \Gamma:\ \gamma = \emptyset\ \lor\ \text{compatible}(c,\ \gamma)
$$

Filtered set:

$$
\mathcal{C}^{\ast}
= \\{\ c \in \mathcal{C} \mid \text{consistent}(c,\ \Gamma) \ \\}
$$

If $\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \emptyset$, fallback to $\mathcal{C}$.

---

### Stage 3 — Meaning Commitment

Status:

$$
\sigma =
\begin{cases}
\texttt{RESOLVED} & |\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| = 1 \\
\texttt{CONFLICT} & |\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| > 1
\end{cases}
$$

Confidence:

$$
\kappa =
\begin{cases}
\text{score}(c^{\ast},\ F) & \sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED} \\
\max_{c \in \mathcal{C}^{\ast}} \text{score}(c,\ F) & \sigma = \texttt{CONFLICT}
\end{cases}
$$

Meaning and conflict set:

$$
(M,\ \Xi) =
\begin{cases}
(c^{\ast},\ \emptyset) & \sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED} \\
(\text{absent},\ \mathcal{C}^{\ast}) & \sigma = \texttt{CONFLICT}
\end{cases}
$$

Revision counter:

$$
\nu =
\begin{cases}
\nu_{\text{prior}} + 1 & \text{if re-applied within the same turn} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

---

## 5. Outputs

LI writes exactly six fields:

- `tp.mli_status`  
- `tp.meaning`  
- `tp.mli_confidence`  
- `tp.mli_conflict`  
- `tp.mli_reason_code`  
- `tp.mli_revision_cycles`  

All must be populated on every execution path.

---

## 6. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)

### Input and Access Control  
- **HLR‑20.112‑001** — LI SHALL apply MLI‑prc to representation‑layer fields, scoring‑layer fields, and continuity_fields.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑002** — LI SHALL read only the fields enumerated in the Input Contract.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑003** — LI SHALL NOT read semantic_core, prior‑turn meaning‑layer fields, COB, lineage_fields, or any field owned by REx, RPlan, RPU, or OuBB.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑004** — LI SHALL perform a pre‑condition check and SHALL commit `mli_status = CONFLICT` and `mli_confidence = 0` when any required field is absent or invalid.

### Candidate Generation  
- **HLR‑20.112‑005** — LI SHALL invoke MLI‑prc to generate a non‑empty candidate set $\mathcal{C}$.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑006** — LI SHALL NOT use probabilistic models, softmax, or projection matrices to generate $\mathcal{C}$.

### Continuity Filtering  
- **HLR‑20.112‑007** — LI SHALL apply continuity filtering using all non‑empty components of $\Gamma$.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑008** — When $\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \emptyset$, LI SHALL fall back to $\mathcal{C}$ and SHALL record `CONTINUITY_FALLBACK`.

### Meaning Commitment  
- **HLR‑20.112‑009** — LI SHALL set `mli_status = RESOLVED` when $|\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| = 1$ and `CONFLICT` otherwise.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑010** — LI SHALL write `mli_confidence = \kappa` where $\kappa \in [0,1]$.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑011** — LI SHALL populate `mli_conflict` with $\mathcal{C}^{\ast}$ when in conflict and an empty array when resolved.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑012** — LI SHALL increment `mli_revision_cycles` when re‑applied within the same turn and SHALL write `0` otherwise.

### Output Contract  
- **HLR‑20.112‑013** — LI SHALL populate all six output fields on every execution path.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑014** — LI SHALL NOT write to any field owned by TPTB, TPSF, CoHI, REx, RPlan, RPU, or OuBB.

### Ordering and Execution  
- **HLR‑20.112‑015** — LI SHALL NOT begin execution until CoHI has completed its writes for the current turn.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑016** — LI SHALL complete all meaning‑layer writes before REx begins execution.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑017** — LI SHALL preserve Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability by ensuring meaning is lineage‑stable before REx reads it.

### Invariant Preservation  
- **HLR‑20.112‑018** — LI SHALL NOT introduce semantic mutation relative to upstream meaning.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑019** — LI SHALL NOT introduce style or tone mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.112‑020** — LI SHALL propagate provenance_chain without modification.

---

## 7. Is / Is Not (informative)

LI **is**:
- the meaning‑commitment point of Path B  
- the sole executor of MLI‑prc  
- the producer of meaning‑layer fields consumed by REx  

LI **is not**:
- a global inference engine  
- a scorer  
- a planner  
- a router  
- a semantic_core builder  
- a continuity reasoner  
- a lineage integrator  
- a memory system  
- a surface generator  

---

## 8. Example (informative)

TPTB produces representation‑layer fields for:  
“Can you check that for me.”

TPSF assigns high plausibility to two candidates:

- *verify‑value* ($0.81$)  
- *inspect‑document* ($0.74$)

`conversation_objects` contains one referent: “the dataset.”

Continuity filtering eliminates *inspect‑document*.  
$\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \\{ \text{verify-value} \\}$.

LI writes:

- `mli_status = RESOLVED`  
- `meaning = verify-value`  
- `mli_confidence = 0.81`  
- `mli_conflict = []`  
- `mli_reason_code = ""`  
- `mli_revision_cycles = 0`  

REx begins substrate construction.
