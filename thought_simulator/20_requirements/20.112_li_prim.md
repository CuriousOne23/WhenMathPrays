# 20.112 LI — Latent Inference Primitive

## 1. Purpose

The **Latent Inference (LI)** primitive bridges coherence-scored thought structure with downstream reasoning execution. LI reads the `continuity_fields` bundle produced by CoHI and emits a ranked set of *inference candidates* — weighted hypotheses about what implicit structure the simulator may legitimately carry forward into REx.

The core operation is a projection from continuity space into inference space: each field in `continuity_fields` contributes a weighted vote for one or more latent propositions, and LI normalises those votes into a probability distribution over candidate inferences.

---

## 2. Focus

LI is focused exclusively on **latent structure recovery**. It does not generate new semantic content, evaluate the truth of propositions, or decide which inference will ultimately be acted upon. Those responsibilities belong to REx and RPlan respectively.

**[HLR-20.112-001]** The LI primitive shall accept the `continuity_fields` object emitted by CoHI as its sole mandatory input.

**[HLR-20.112-002]** The LI primitive shall produce an ordered `inference_candidates` list as its sole mandatory output.

**[HLR-20.112-003]** Each entry in `inference_candidates` shall carry at minimum: a candidate identifier, a confidence weight $w_i \in [0, 1]$, and a reference to the `continuity_field` index that contributed the largest activation.

---

## 3. When

LI executes on every Path B cycle, immediately after CoHI has finalised its `continuity_fields` bundle and before REx reads from the pipeline.

**[HLR-20.112-004]** The LI primitive shall not begin execution unless all three upstream primitives (TPTB, TPSF, CoHI) have reported a `COMPLETE` status for the current cycle.

**[HLR-20.112-005]** The LI primitive shall complete its execution and hand off `inference_candidates` before REx begins its execution phase for the same cycle.

---

## 4. Why

CoHI produces a rich multi-dimensional continuity signal, but that signal is not yet in a form that a reasoning engine can directly act on. The gap between *coherence scoring* and *reasoned action* is precisely the space LI is designed to fill.

Without LI, REx would need to interpret raw continuity weights directly — coupling reasoning logic tightly to the scoring schema of CoHI and making both harder to evolve independently. LI decouples those two concerns by translating CoHI's output language into a canonical inference vocabulary understood by REx and RPlan.

---

## 5. Is-Not

To prevent scope creep and misuse, the following are explicitly outside LI's responsibility:

- **Not a content generator.** LI does not synthesise new tokens, phrases, or propositions from scratch.
- **Not a truth evaluator.** LI assigns confidence weights to candidates; it does not assert that any candidate is factually correct.
- **Not a selector.** Final selection of which inference candidate is acted upon belongs to REx.
- **Not a memory store.** LI does not persist `inference_candidates` across cycles; each cycle's output is ephemeral until consumed.
- **Not a fallback handler.** If CoHI produces an empty or malformed `continuity_fields` bundle, LI surfaces an error rather than attempting recovery.

**[HLR-20.112-006]** The LI primitive shall not modify, overwrite, or re-score any field within the `continuity_fields` bundle it receives.

**[HLR-20.112-007]** The LI primitive shall not write directly to any data structure owned by REx, RPlan, RPU, or OuBB; all downstream communication shall pass exclusively through the defined pipeline handoff interface.

---

## 6. Formal Model

Let $\mathbf{c} = \langle c_1, c_2, \dots, c_n \rangle$ denote the vector of scalar values extracted from `continuity_fields`, where $n$ is the field count for the current cycle and each $c_k \in \mathbb{R}$.

LI applies a learned projection matrix $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and bias $\mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^m$ to produce a raw logit vector over $m$ latent inference classes:

$$
\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{W}\mathbf{c} + \mathbf{b}
$$

The confidence weight assigned to inference candidate $i$ is then the standard softmax:

$$
w_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} e^{z_j}}, \quad i = 1, \dots, m
$$

so that $\sum_{i=1}^{m} w_i = 1$ and each $w_i \in (0, 1)$.

**[HLR-20.112-008]** The LI primitive shall ensure that the confidence weights across all entries in `inference_candidates` sum to $1.0$ within a floating-point tolerance of $\varepsilon = 10^{-6}$.

**[HLR-20.112-009]** The LI primitive shall rank entries in `inference_candidates` in descending order of $w_i$ prior to handoff.

Candidates whose weight falls below a configurable pruning threshold $\theta$ are suppressed before handoff:

$$
\mathcal{I}_{\text{out}} = \{ i \mid w_i \geq \theta \}
$$

**[HLR-20.112-010]** The LI primitive shall expose $\theta$ as a configurable runtime parameter with a default value of $\theta = 0.05$.

**[HLR-20.112-011]** The LI primitive shall retain at least one candidate in `inference_candidates` even when all computed weights fall below $\theta$; in that case the single highest-weight candidate shall be passed through and the threshold suppression event shall be recorded in the cycle diagnostic log.

---

## 7. Example

*The following walkthrough is illustrative only and does not constitute a normative test case.*

Suppose CoHI emits a `continuity_fields` bundle with three scalar values:

$$
\mathbf{c} = \langle 0.82,\ 0.45,\ 0.61 \rangle
$$

After applying projection matrix $\mathbf{W}$ and bias $\mathbf{b}$, LI computes raw logits:

$$
\mathbf{z} = \langle 1.40,\ 0.30,\ 0.90 \rangle
$$

Applying softmax yields confidence weights:

$$
\mathbf{w} \approx \langle 0.516,\ 0.172,\ 0.313 \rangle
$$

With the default threshold $\theta = 0.05$, all three candidates survive pruning. The `inference_candidates` list handed off to REx is ordered: candidate 1 ($w \approx 0.516$), candidate 3 ($w \approx 0.313$), candidate 2 ($w \approx 0.172$). REx then selects among these; LI's role for this cycle is complete.
