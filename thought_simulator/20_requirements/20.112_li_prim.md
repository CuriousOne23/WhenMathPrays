# 20.112 LI — Local Inference Primitive

## Purpose

LI is the local inference primitive for Path B. It accepts the representation-layer
fields produced by TPTB, the scoring-layer fields produced by TPSF, and the
continuity fields produced by CoHI, applies **MLI-prc** to generate and
continuity-filter a candidate meaning set, and commits the resolved or conflicted
meaning into the six meaning-layer fields of the TP for consumption by REx. LI
performs no global inference, no planning, no routing, and no surface generation.

**[HLR-20.112-001]** The LI primitive SHALL apply MLI-prc to the TP snapshot
composed of representation-layer fields, scoring-layer fields, and continuity
fields, and SHALL commit all six meaning-layer output fields to the TP before REx
begins execution for the same turn.

## Input Contract

The table below defines all inputs LI reads. No other TP field, external store, or
runtime service may be accessed during LI execution. Representation-layer and
scoring-layer field names are as defined in the TPTB and TPSF output contracts
respectively.

| Field | Source Primitive | Required | Type | Validity Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Representation-layer fields | TPTB | Required | per TPTB output contract | all fields in TPTB output contract must be present and structurally valid |
| Scoring-layer fields | TPSF | Required | per TPSF output contract | all fields in TPSF output contract must be present and structurally valid |
| `tp.continuity_fields.structural_history` | CoHI | Required | structured object | must be present; may be empty |
| `tp.continuity_fields.referential_history` | CoHI | Required | structured object | must be present; may be empty |
| `tp.continuity_fields.conversation_objects` | CoHI | Required | array | must be present; may be empty |
| `tp.continuity_fields.topic_threads` | CoHI | Required | array | must be present; may be empty |
| `tp.continuity_fields.continuity_markers` | CoHI | Required | array | must be present; may be empty |
| `tp.truth_tags` | A-side | Required | array | may be empty |
| `tp.safety_flags` | A-side | Required | array | may be empty |

**[HLR-20.112-002]** The LI primitive SHALL read only the fields enumerated in the
Input Contract table; it SHALL NOT read from COB directly, from any meaning-layer
field written by a prior LI execution, or from any field owned by REx, RPlan, RPU,
or OuBB.

**[HLR-20.112-003]** The LI primitive SHALL perform a pre-condition check before
MLI-prc is applied: if any required representation-layer or scoring-layer field is
absent or structurally invalid, or if any `tp.continuity_fields` sub-field is
absent, LI SHALL set `tp.mli_status` to `CONFLICT`, SHALL write
`reason_code = PRE_CONDITION_FAULT`, SHALL set `tp.mli_confidence` to $0$, SHALL
leave `tp.meaning` absent, and SHALL halt without applying MLI-prc.

## Transfer Function

MLI-prc operates in three ordered stages: candidate generation, continuity
filtering, and meaning commitment.

### Stage 1 — Candidate Generation

Let $R$ denote the full set of representation-layer fields and $F$ denote the full
set of scoring-layer fields. MLI-prc derives a non-empty candidate set:

$$
\mathcal{C} = \text{MLI-candidates}(R,\ F), \quad |\mathcal{C}| \geq 1
$$

Each candidate $c \in \mathcal{C}$ carries a score $\text{score}(c, F) \in [0, 1]$
derived from $F$.

**[HLR-20.112-004]** The LI primitive SHALL invoke MLI-prc to generate candidate
set $\mathcal{C}$ from representation-layer fields $R$ and scoring-layer fields
$F$; it SHALL NOT produce an empty $\mathcal{C}$ and SHALL NOT use any
probabilistic model, softmax, or projection matrix to do so.

### Stage 2 — Continuity Filtering

Let $\Gamma = \\{\gamma_{\text{sh}},\ \gamma_{\text{rh}},\ \gamma_{\text{co}},\ \gamma_{\text{tt}},\ \gamma_{\text{cm}}\\}$
denote the five continuity field components from CoHI. A candidate $c$ is
continuity-consistent when it is compatible with all non-empty components of
$\Gamma$:

$$
\text{consistent}(c,\ \Gamma) = \forall\ \gamma \in \Gamma :
\gamma = \emptyset \ \lor\ \text{compatible}(c,\ \gamma)
$$

The continuity-filtered candidate set is:

$$
\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \\{\ c \in \mathcal{C} \mid \text{consistent}(c,\ \Gamma)\ \\}
$$

When $\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \emptyset$, MLI-prc falls back to
$\mathcal{C}^{\ast} := \mathcal{C}$ and records the fallback in
`tp.mli_reason_code`.

**[HLR-20.112-005]** The LI primitive SHALL apply continuity filtering to reduce
$\mathcal{C}$ to $\mathcal{C}^{\ast}$ using all non-empty components of $\Gamma$;
when $\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \emptyset$ it SHALL fall back to
$\mathcal{C}^{\ast} := \mathcal{C}$ and SHALL record
`reason_code = CONTINUITY_FALLBACK` in `tp.mli_reason_code`.

### Stage 3 — Meaning Commitment

The meaning status $\sigma$ is determined by the cardinality of $\mathcal{C}^{\ast}$:

$$
\sigma = \begin{cases}
\texttt{RESOLVED} & \text{if } |\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| = 1 \\
\texttt{CONFLICT}  & \text{if } |\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| > 1
\end{cases}
$$

The confidence $\kappa$ is derived from scoring-layer fields $F$:

$$
\kappa = \begin{cases}
\text{score}(c^{\ast},\ F) & \text{if } \sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED},\
\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \\{c^{\ast}\\} \\
\max_{c\ \in\ \mathcal{C}^{\ast}} \text{score}(c,\ F) & \text{if } \sigma = \texttt{CONFLICT}
\end{cases}, \quad \kappa \in [0, 1]
$$

The conflict set $\Xi$ and committed meaning $M$ are:

$$
(M,\ \Xi) = \begin{cases}
(c^{\ast},\ \emptyset) & \text{if } \sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED} \\
(\text{absent},\ \mathcal{C}^{\ast}) & \text{if } \sigma = \texttt{CONFLICT}
\end{cases}
$$

The revision cycle counter $\nu$ tracks re-applications of MLI-prc to the same TP:

$$
\nu = \begin{cases}
\nu_{\text{prior}} + 1 & \text{if the TP already carries } \nu_{\text{prior}} \geq 0
\text{ from a prior LI execution} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

**[HLR-20.112-006]** The LI primitive SHALL set `tp.mli_status` to `RESOLVED` when
$|\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| = 1$ and to `CONFLICT` when $|\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| > 1$; no
other value is permitted.

**[HLR-20.112-007]** The LI primitive SHALL write `tp.mli_confidence` = $\kappa$
where $\kappa \in [0, 1]$; it SHALL NOT write a value outside this range.

**[HLR-20.112-008]** The LI primitive SHALL populate `tp.mli_conflict` with
$\mathcal{C}^{\ast}$ when $\sigma = \texttt{CONFLICT}$ and SHALL write an empty
array when $\sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED}$; it SHALL NOT leave `tp.mli_conflict`
absent on either path.

**[HLR-20.112-009]** The LI primitive SHALL increment `tp.mli_revision_cycles` by
$1$ each time MLI-prc is re-applied to a TP that already carries a non-negative
`tp.mli_revision_cycles` value; it SHALL write $0$ when no prior value exists.

## Output Contract

The table below defines all fields LI writes. REx must validate presence and type
of every required field before beginning its own execution.

| Field | Type | Legal Values | Semantics |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tp.mli_status` | enum | `RESOLVED`, `CONFLICT` | Meaning commitment outcome; REx reads this first to determine whether `tp.meaning` is present |
| `tp.meaning` | structured object | present when `RESOLVED`; absent when `CONFLICT` | The single committed meaning candidate $c^{\ast}$; REx decomposes this via $\Phi$ |
| `tp.mli_confidence` | float | $\kappa \in [0, 1]$ | Confidence of committed or highest-scoring candidate; REx may use this to weight substep payloads |
| `tp.mli_conflict` | array | non-null; non-empty when `CONFLICT`; empty array when `RESOLVED` | The full filtered conflict set $\mathcal{C}^{\ast}$; REx evaluates resolvability against `tp.truth_tags` |
| `tp.mli_reason_code` | string | non-empty on fault or fallback; empty string on clean `RESOLVED` | Machine-readable code; REx propagates into `tp.rsub.reason_code` on abort |
| `tp.mli_revision_cycles` | integer | $\nu \geq 0$ | Count of MLI-prc re-applications on this TP; available to downstream primitives for audit tracing |

**[HLR-20.112-010]** The LI primitive SHALL populate all six fields in the Output
Contract table on every execution path; no output field SHALL be left absent or
null except where the table explicitly permits absence.

**[HLR-20.112-011]** The LI primitive SHALL NOT write to any representation-layer
field owned by TPTB, any scoring-layer field owned by TPSF, any continuity field
owned by CoHI, or any field owned by REx, RPlan, RPU, or OuBB.

## When

LI runs once per turn. Its position in the Path B execution order is fixed: after
TPTB, TPSF, and CoHI have each committed their respective fields, and before REx
reads any meaning-layer field.

**[HLR-20.112-012]** The LI primitive SHALL NOT begin execution until TPTB, TPSF,
and CoHI have each written their respective output fields to a completed state for
the current turn.

**[HLR-20.112-013]** The LI primitive SHALL complete all writes to the six
meaning-layer output fields before REx begins execution for the same turn.

## Why

TPTB and TPSF produce a rich representation and scoring of the input, but neither
commits to a single meaning. Without a dedicated local inference step, every
downstream primitive would need to independently derive and filter meaning from raw
representation fields, introducing inconsistency and making continuity-aware
reasoning fragile. LI provides a single, auditable commitment point where MLI-prc
resolves or surfaces conflict before any realization work begins.

## Is Not

LI is not a global inference engine.
LI is not a scorer; it consumes scores from TPSF but does not produce them.
LI is not a router.
LI is not a planner.
LI is not a semantic_core builder.
LI is not MLI itself; it invokes MLI-prc as a bounded primitive step.
LI is not a memory system.
LI is not a continuity reasoner; it consumes continuity_fields but does not compute
or update them.
LI does not read COB directly.
LI does not generate XP or surface expression.

## Example

TPTB has produced representation-layer fields encoding the utterance "Can you check
that for me." TPSF has written scoring-layer fields assigning high plausibility to
two candidates: *request to verify a value* ($0.81$) and *request to inspect a
document* ($0.74$). `tp.continuity_fields.conversation_objects` contains one entry
representing "the dataset" referenced two turns prior. `tp.truth_tags` asserts that
object as active. `tp.safety_flags` is empty.

Pre-condition check passes. MLI-prc begins.

**Stage 1.** $\mathcal{C} = \\{\text{verify-value},\ \text{inspect-document}\\}$.

**Stage 2.** Continuity filtering against `conversation_objects`: only
*verify-value* is compatible with the dataset referent.
$\mathcal{C}^{\ast} = \\{\text{verify-value}\\}$.

**Stage 3.** $|\mathcal{C}^{\ast}| = 1 \Rightarrow \sigma = \texttt{RESOLVED}$.
$\kappa = 0.81$. $M = \text{verify-value}$. $\Xi = \emptyset$. $\nu = 0$.

LI writes: `tp.mli_status = RESOLVED`, `tp.meaning = verify-value`,
`tp.mli_confidence = 0.81`, `tp.mli_conflict = []`,
`tp.mli_reason_code = ""`, `tp.mli_revision_cycles = 0`. REx reads
`tp.mli_status = RESOLVED`, confirms `tp.meaning` is present, and begins substrate
construction via $\Phi$ without any further meaning interpretation.
