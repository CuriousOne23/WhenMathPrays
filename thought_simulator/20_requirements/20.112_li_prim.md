# **LI (Local Inference Primitive) — 20.112**

**Purpose:**  
The Local Inference (LI) primitive applies **MLI‑prc** to the frozen Path A TP snapshot (including TPTB, TPSF, and CoHI‑produced continuity_fields) to commit the first layer of local meaning before any global inference occurs.  
**HLR‑20.112‑001:** LI SHALL apply **MLI‑prc** to resolve local meaning from the continuity‑augmented TP snapshot.

**Focus:**  
LI reads representation‑layer fields, A‑side metadata (`tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`), and **continuity_fields** imported by CoHI (structural_history, referential_history, conversation_objects, topic_threads, continuity_markers).  
**HLR‑20.112‑002:** LI SHALL read `tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`, and representation‑layer fields.  
**HLR‑20.112‑003:** LI SHALL read all continuity_fields produced by CoHI.  
LI writes meaning‑layer fields (`tp.meaning`, `tp.mli_status`, `tp.mli_confidence`, `tp.mli_conflict`, `tp.mli_reason_code`, `tp.mli_revision_cycles`) into the TP.  
**HLR‑20.112‑004:** LI SHALL write all meaning‑layer fields required for downstream Path B primitives.

**When:**  
LI runs after TPTB, after TPSF, and after CoHI has integrated continuity into the TP snapshot, and before REx, RPlan, or RPU.  
**HLR‑20.112‑005:** LI SHALL execute once per turn at the start of Path B inference, immediately after CoHI.

**Why:**  
LI provides deterministic, auditable, continuity‑aware local meaning commitment so that all downstream Path B primitives operate on a stable semantic substrate. This prevents semantic drift and ensures that global inference is grounded in resolved local meaning.

**Is not:**  
LI is not a global inference engine, a scorer, a router, a planner, a semantic_core builder, a memory system, or MLI itself. LI does not read COB directly and does not generate XP.

**Example:**  
Given an ambiguous utterance (“Can you check that for me”), LI reads representation fields, truth/safety metadata, and CoHI‑imported continuity (e.g., a conversation_object representing “the dataset we discussed earlier”). LI applies **MLI‑prc** to classify the utterance as a *request* and writes the resolved meaning and confidence into the TP for downstream primitives.

**Normative home:** 20.112.
