# 20.112 LI — Local Inference Primitive

## Purpose

LI is the primitive that applies **MLI-prc** to the current TP snapshot — including
representation-layer fields produced by TPTB, scoring fields produced by TPSF, and
`continuity_fields` produced by CoHI — to commit local meaning before any global
inference, planning, or routing occurs.

**[HLR-20.112-001]** The LI primitive SHALL apply MLI-prc to the current TP snapshot
and commit all meaning-layer fields to the TP before any downstream primitive in
Path B reads meaning-layer output.

## Focus

LI reads the following inputs from the TP:

- Representation-layer fields produced by TPTB
- A-side metadata: `tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`
- `continuity_fields` produced by CoHI

LI writes the following meaning-layer fields to the TP:

- `tp.meaning`
- `tp.mli_status`
- `tp.mli_confidence`
- `tp.mli_conflict`
- `tp.mli_reason_code`
- `tp.mli_revision_cycles`

**[HLR-20.112-002]** The LI primitive SHALL read `tp.continuity_fields` as produced by
CoHI before applying MLI-prc; it SHALL NOT read from COB directly.

**[HLR-20.112-003]** The LI primitive SHALL write `tp.meaning`, `tp.mli_status`,
`tp.mli_confidence`, `tp.mli_conflict`, `tp.mli_reason_code`, and
`tp.mli_revision_cycles` as its output; it SHALL NOT write to any field outside
the meaning layer.

**[HLR-20.112-006]** When MLI-prc detects competing meaning readings, the LI primitive
SHALL populate `tp.mli_conflict` with the set of conflicting candidate readings and
SHALL set `tp.mli_status` to `CONFLICT` before handoff to REx.

**[HLR-20.112-007]** The LI primitive SHALL increment `tp.mli_revision_cycles` by 1
each time MLI-prc is re-applied to a TP that already carries a prior `mli_status`
value from a previous LI execution in the same turn.

## When

LI runs once per turn at the start of Path B inference, after TPTB, TPSF, and CoHI
have each committed their contributions to the TP, and before REx, RPlan, or RPU
begin execution.

**[HLR-20.112-004]** The LI primitive SHALL NOT begin execution until TPTB, TPSF, and
CoHI have each written their respective TP fields to a completed state for the
current turn.

**[HLR-20.112-005]** The LI primitive SHALL complete all writes to meaning-layer TP
fields before REx, RPlan, or RPU begin execution for the same turn.

## Why

Without a dedicated local inference step, downstream primitives would each need to
re-derive meaning independently, producing inconsistency and making
continuity-aware reasoning fragile. LI ensures meaning commitment is deterministic,
auditable, and informed by CoHI's continuity signal at a single well-defined point
in the pipeline. This separation of local meaning commitment from global reasoning
makes Path B traceable and revision-safe.

## Is Not

LI is not a global inference engine.
LI is not a scorer.
LI is not a router.
LI is not a planner.
LI is not a semantic_core builder.
LI is not MLI itself; it invokes MLI-prc as one primitive step in the pipeline.
LI is not a memory system.
LI is not a continuity reasoner; it consumes `continuity_fields` but does not
compute or update them.

## Example

A user utterance arrives containing the phrase "run it again." Without continuity
context, the referent is ambiguous — it could apply to a test, a query, a report,
or a media clip. CoHI has populated `tp.continuity_fields` with a back-reference
to a prior TP whose `tp.meaning` was committed to a unit-test execution context.

LI reads those `continuity_fields`, applies MLI-prc, and resolves the referent:
`tp.meaning` is committed as *execute the most recent unit test*. `tp.mli_status`
is set to `RESOLVED`, `tp.mli_confidence` reflects the continuity match strength,
`tp.mli_conflict` is empty because no competing reading survived the continuity
constraint, and `tp.mli_reason_code` records the continuity rule that governed
resolution. REx receives a TP with a fully committed meaning layer.
