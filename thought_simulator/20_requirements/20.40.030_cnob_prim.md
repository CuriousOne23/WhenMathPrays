# **0. Purpose & Scope** *(Informative)*

The Constraint Object (CnOB) is the **third structural layer** in Path A.  
Its role is to extract **constraint‑level residue** from the refined structure produced by SROB (20.40.020).

CnOB encodes:

- constraint cues  
- missing‑slot signals  
- underspecification markers  
- conflict indicators  
- constraint‑level residue hashing  
- routing‑relevant constraint metadata  
- ΔH%‑derived constraint signals  

CnOB is **not** a semantic interpreter.  
It does **not** normalize structure, infer meaning, or reconstruct missing fields.  
It is a deterministic, monotonic, structural constraint layer that feeds directly into SmOB (20.40.040) and ultimately the Structural Signal Generator (SSG).

---

# **1. Inputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑010**  
CnOB SHALL accept the refined structural TP, refined residue fragments, and sharpened structural metadata produced by SROB.

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑011**  
CnOB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — and MAY annotate these structurally.  
CnOB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of these fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑012 — Structural Discourse‑Flag Normalization**  
CnOB SHALL normalize discourse‑context structural flags propagated from SROB — including recent‑entity continuity, temporal‑shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical constraint‑relevant structural metadata.

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑013 — Structural Discourse‑Constraint Encoding**  
CnOB SHALL encode normalized discourse‑context metadata strictly as **structural constraints**, without semantic interpretation, semantic role inference, or intent inference.

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑014 — Deterministic & Monotonic Discourse Constraints**  
CnOB SHALL ensure discourse‑context constraints are deterministic, bounded, monotonic, and reproducible for identical SROB inputs, consistent with constraint families C1–C7.

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑015 — Routing‑Eligible Discourse Constraints**  
CnOB SHALL expose discourse‑context constraints as part of constraint‑level residue and routing‑eligibility metadata, ensuring they are consumable by SmOB, STPX, and ISc without modification.

# **2. CnOB Definition (Normative)**

CnOB is a deterministic structural object containing:

- `constraint_families` — monotonic constraint sets (C1–C7)  
- `missing_slot_signals` — structural underspecification markers  
- `conflict_indicators` — structural conflict flags  
- `constraint_residue_hash` — deterministic hash of constraint‑level residue  
- `lineage_constraints` — constraints derived from structural ancestry  
- `delta_h_constraints` — constraints derived from ΔH%  
- `routing_constraints` — routing‑eligibility constraints  
- `policy_constraints` — constraints derived from policy signatures  

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑001**  
CnOB SHALL be constructed as a pure structural‑constraint object containing only monotonic, provably entailed constraints derived from SROB, with no semantic or supervisory content.

---

# **3. Deterministic Constraint Extraction**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑002**  
CnOB extraction SHALL be deterministic for identical SROB inputs, producing identical constraint sets across all executions.

---

# **4. Canonical Ordering of Constraints**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑003**  
CnOB SHALL apply canonical ordering to all constraint families and nested constraint structures to ensure deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

# **5. Structural Purity**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑004**  
CnOB SHALL contain only provable structural constraints and SHALL NOT infer, smooth, reconstruct, or approximate missing or noisy fields.

---

# **6. Constraint Families (C1–C7)**

CnOB supports the following monotonic structural constraint families:

- **C1:** Structural existence constraints  
- **C2:** Structural adjacency constraints  
- **C3:** Structural ordering constraints  
- **C4:** Structural boundary constraints  
- **C5:** Structural lineage constraints  
- **C6:** Structural change ($\Delta H\%$) constraints  
- **C7:** Routing‑eligibility constraints  

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑005**  
CnOB SHALL maintain monotonicity of all constraint families: constraints may be added but never removed.

---

# **7. Lineage‑Derived Constraints**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑006**  
CnOB SHALL derive lineage‑based constraints deterministically from SROB lineage and SHALL ensure these constraints are stable across replay.

---

# **8. ΔH%‑Derived Constraints**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑007**  
CnOB SHALL derive ΔH%‑based constraints deterministically from SROB and SHALL ensure ΔH% constraints are consistent with 20.47 and 20.12 invariants.

---

# **9. Constraint‑Residue Signals**

CnOB must extract:

- missing‑slot signals  
- underspecification markers  
- conflict indicators  
- constraint‑level residue hashing  
- missing‑mass attribution signals  

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑008**  
CnOB SHALL extract missing‑slot signals, underspecification markers, conflict indicators, and constraint‑level residue hashing as part of CnOB construction, ensuring deterministic residue for routing and ΔH% attribution.

---

# **10. CnOB as Input to SmOB**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑009**  
SmOB SHALL receive CnOB as a direct input and SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, or discard any CnOB constraints.

---

# **11. Mathematical Requirements (GitHub‑Safe KaTeX)**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑021**  
CnOB SHALL ensure each constraint‑level residue fragment $r\_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h\_i'$:

$$
h\_i' = hash(r\_i')
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑022**  
CnOB SHALL ensure the combined query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h\_1' \oplus h\_2' \oplus h\_3' \oplus \dots \oplus h\_n'
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑023**  
CnOB SHALL ensure refined constraint‑residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature‑invariance.

---

# **12. Outputs**

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑024**  
CnOB SHALL output:

- normalized constraint‑level residue  
- constraint families (C1–C7)  
- missing‑slot signals  
- underspecification markers  
- conflict indicators  
- constraint‑residue hash  
- lineage‑derived constraints  
- ΔH%‑derived constraints  
- routing‑eligibility constraints  
- policy‑derived constraints  

### **HLR‑20.40.030‑025**  
CnOB SHALL guarantee its output is valid input for SmOB.
