 **20.167 — Realization Plan Constructor (RPlan‑prm)**  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Primitive Class:** Path B — Realization Layer  
**Purpose:** Convert a Realization Extract (REx) into a **Realization Plan (RPlan)**: a structured, ordered, dependency‑aware plan describing *how* the system will realize the required output.

---

## **1. Overview**
The Realization Plan Constructor (RPlan‑prm) is the second primitive in the Path B pipeline.  
It consumes a **Realization Extract (REx)** and produces a **Realization Plan (RPlan)** — a structured, deterministic, machine‑actionable plan describing:

- The steps required to realize the meaning  
- The ordering and dependencies between steps  
- The constraints that govern each step  
- The invariants that must be preserved  
- The ambiguity‑resolution strategy (if needed)  
- The execution‑ready structure for RPU and ReB  

RPlan is the *procedural blueprint* for realization.

---

## **2. Inputs and Outputs**

### **2.1 Inputs**
RPlan‑prm SHALL read the following REx fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `REx.core_meaning` | Canonical semantic representation |
| `REx.intent` | Intent classification |
| `REx.constraints` | Normalized constraints |
| `REx.invariants` | Invariants to preserve |
| `REx.audience` | Target audience |
| `REx.channel` | Output channel |
| `REx.dependencies` | Required semantic dependencies |
| `REx.ambiguities` | Unresolved ambiguities |
| `REx.trace` | Provenance chain |

### **2.2 Outputs**
RPlan‑prm SHALL produce a **Realization Plan (RPlan)** with the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `RPlan.steps` | Ordered list of realization steps |
| `RPlan.dependencies` | Step‑level dependency graph |
| `RPlan.constraint_map` | Mapping of constraints to steps |
| `RPlan.invariant_map` | Mapping of invariants to steps |
| `RPlan.ambiguity_strategy` | Strategy for ambiguity handling |
| `RPlan.channel` | Carried from REx.channel |
| `RPlan.audience` | Carried from REx.audience |
| `RPlan.trace` | Extended provenance chain |

## **3. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)**  
All SHALL statements are bound to HLR‑20.167-xxx.

---

### **HLR‑20.167-001 — Plan Construction**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL construct a **deterministic, ordered sequence of realization steps** from `REx.core_meaning`.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-002 — Steps SHALL be atomic (no compound operations).  
- HLR‑20.167-003 — Steps SHALL be ordered according to semantic dependencies.  
- HLR‑20.167-004 — Steps SHALL be represented in a machine‑actionable internal format.  

**Example:**  
Meaning: *compose_reminder(team, meeting, 10am)*  
Steps:  
1. Identify target audience  
2. Identify event  
3. Construct reminder skeleton  
4. Insert required time  
5. Apply tone/style  
6. Finalize structure

---

### **HLR‑20.167-005 — Dependency Graph Construction**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL construct a **dependency graph** linking steps to required inputs.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-006 — Dependencies SHALL include semantic, structural, and constraint‑driven dependencies.  
- HLR‑20.167-007 — Cycles SHALL be detected and prohibited.  
- HLR‑20.167-008 — Missing dependencies SHALL be recorded for RPU.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167.009 — Constraint Mapping**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL map all constraints from `REx.constraints` to the steps they govern.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-010 — Length constraints SHALL apply to content‑generation steps only.  
- HLR‑20.167-011 — Format constraints SHALL apply to structural steps.  
- HLR‑20.167-012 — Safety constraints SHALL apply globally unless explicitly scoped.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167-013 — Invariant Mapping**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL map all invariants from `REx.invariants` to the steps they constrain.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-014 — Invariants SHALL be preserved verbatim.  
- HLR‑20.167-015 — Invariants SHALL override constraints in case of conflict.  
- HLR‑20.167-016 — Invariants SHALL be marked as non‑modifiable.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167-017 — Ambiguity Strategy Construction**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL construct an **ambiguity‑resolution strategy** for all items in `REx.ambiguities`.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-018 — Ambiguities SHALL be classified as lexical, structural, or intent‑level.  
- HLR‑20.167-019 — RPlan‑prm SHALL not resolve ambiguities; it SHALL only plan for their resolution.  
- HLR‑20.167-020 — Ambiguities SHALL be routed to RPU or ReB depending on type.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167-021 — Audience and Channel Propagation**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL propagate `REx.audience` and `REx.channel` into the RPlan.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-022 — Channel SHALL influence structural steps (e.g., email vs. chat).  
- HLR‑20.167-023 — Audience SHALL influence tone‑application steps.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167-024 — Provenance Extension**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL extend the provenance chain (`REx.trace`) into `RPlan.trace`.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-025 — The extension SHALL include a timestamped entry for RPlan‑prm.  
- HLR‑20.167-026 — The extension SHALL include the version of this primitive.  

---

### **HLR‑20.167-027 — No Realization or Updating**  
RPlan‑prm SHALL NOT perform realization, rewriting, updating, or content generation.

**For LLRs:**  
- HLR‑20.167-028 — RPlan‑prm SHALL not generate user‑visible text.  
- HLR‑20.167-029 — RPlan‑prm SHALL not modify meaning.  
- HLR‑20.167-030 — RPlan‑prm SHALL not execute the plan it constructs.  

---

## **4. RPlan Structure Specification**

```yaml
RPlan:
  steps:
    - <atomic step>
    - <atomic step>
  dependencies:
    <graph structure>
  constraint_map:
    <constraint → step mapping>
  invariant_map:
    <invariant → step mapping>
  ambiguity_strategy:
    <strategy object>
  channel: <from REx.channel>
  audience: <from REx.audience>
  trace: <extended provenance>
```

---

## **5. Invariants**

- HLR‑20.167-031 — RPlan SHALL be deterministic for identical REx inputs.  
- HLR‑20.167-032 — RPlan SHALL be complete: every part of `REx.core_meaning` must map to at least one step.  
- HLR‑20.167-033 — RPlan SHALL be realizable by RPU without modification.

## **6. Example End‑to‑End**

### **Input REx**
```yaml

  target: "team"
  event: "meeting"
  time: "tomorrow 10am"

  format: "email"
  length: "short"

REx.invariants:
  - "must mention time: 10am"

REx.audience: "internal team"
REx.channel: "email"

REx.dependencies: []
REx.ambiguities: []

REx.trace: [...]
```

### **Output RPlan**

```yaml
  - identify_target
  - identify_event
  - construct_email_skeleton
  - insert_required_time
  - apply_tone
  - finalize_structure

  construct_email_skeleton: [identify_event]
  insert_required_time: [construct_email_skeleton]

  finalize_structure: [apply_tone]

RPlan.constraint_map:
  length: [apply_tone, finalize_structure]
  format: [construct_email_skeleton]

RPlan.invariant_map:
  "must mention time: 10am": [insert_required_time]

RPlan.ambiguity_strategy: []

RPlan.channel: "email"
RPlan.audience: "internal team"

  - ...previous...

```
