# 20.167 RPlan — Realization Plan Constructor Primitive

## Purpose

RPlan is the primitive that transforms the realization substrate (`tp.rsub`) produced
by REx into an ordered realization plan (`tp.rplan`). It applies continuity-aware
sequencing and constraint logic to produce a deterministic, step-ordered plan that
RPU can execute without further interpretation.

**[HLR-20.167-001]** The RPlan primitive SHALL consume `tp.rsub` as produced by REx
and SHALL produce `tp.rplan` as an ordered sequence of realization steps before RPU
begins execution.

## Focus

RPlan reads the following inputs from the TP:

- `tp.rsub` — the realization substrate produced by REx
- `tp.continuity_fields` — produced by CoHI
- A-side metadata: `tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`

RPlan writes the following field to the TP:

- `tp.rplan` — ordered realization steps with per-step constraints, continuity
  weights, and plan status

**[HLR-20.167-002]** The RPlan primitive SHALL read `tp.rsub`, `tp.continuity_fields`,
and A-side metadata (`tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`) as its inputs; it SHALL NOT
read from COB directly.

**[HLR-20.167-003]** The RPlan primitive SHALL write `tp.rplan` as its sole output
field; it SHALL NOT modify any meaning-layer field written by LI or any
representation-layer field written by TPTB, TPSF, or CoHI.

**[HLR-20.167-004]** Each entry in `tp.rplan.ordered_steps` SHALL carry at minimum:
a step identifier, an ordered index $i$, a constraint set $C_i$, a continuity weight
$\omega_i \in [0, 1]$ derived from `tp.continuity_fields`, and a step status.

Let $S = \langle s_1, s_2, \dots, s_k \rangle$ denote the ordered step sequence in
`tp.rplan`. A step $s_j$ is blocked when any predecessor constraint is unmet:

$$
\text{blocked}(s_j) = \exists\, i < j \;:\; C_i \not\subseteq \text{resolved}(S)
$$

The plan is viable when no step is blocked:

$$
\text{viable}(S) = \forall\, j \in \{1, \dots, k\} \;:\; \neg\,\text{blocked}(s_j)
$$

**[HLR-20.167-005]** When `tp.rsub` is absent, empty, or structurally invalid, the
RPlan primitive SHALL set `tp.rplan.rplan_status` to `BLOCKED` and SHALL write a
`reason_code` identifying the failure condition before handoff to RPU.

## When

RPlan runs once per turn after REx has committed `tp.rsub` to the TP and before RPU
begins execution.

**[HLR-20.167-006]** The RPlan primitive SHALL NOT begin execution until REx has
written `tp.rsub` to a completed state for the current turn.

**[HLR-20.167-007]** The RPlan primitive SHALL complete all writes to `tp.rplan`
before RPU begins execution for the same turn.

## Why

REx produces a realization substrate that encodes what must be realized, but not the
order or constraints in which realization steps must execute. Without RPlan, RPU would
need to derive sequencing and continuity-aware constraints independently each turn,
making realization non-deterministic and difficult to audit. RPlan provides a single,
well-defined planning point that separates plan construction from plan execution and
ensures continuity decisions are baked into the plan before any surface generation
begins.

## Is Not

RPlan is not a meaning extractor.
RPlan is not a semantic interpreter.
RPlan is not a surface generator.
RPlan is not a scorer.
RPlan is not a router.
RPlan is not a memory system.
RPlan is not REx; it does not construct or evaluate the realization substrate.
RPlan is not RPU; it produces the plan that RPU executes but does not execute it.

## Example

REx has written `tp.rsub` containing three realization substeps: confirm referent
identity, apply formatting register, emit surface tokens. CoHI's
`tp.continuity_fields` carries a back-reference indicating the prior turn used a
formal register.

RPlan reads `tp.rsub` and `tp.continuity_fields`, applies its sequencing logic, and
produces `tp.rplan.ordered_steps` as $\langle s_1, s_2, s_3 \rangle$: first confirm
referent identity (constraint: `tp.meaning` resolved), then apply formal register
(continuity weight $\omega_2 = 0.91$ drawn from the CoHI back-reference), then emit
surface tokens (constraint: $s_1$ and $s_2$ complete). `tp.rplan.rplan_status` is
set to `VIABLE`. RPU receives a fully ordered, constraint-annotated plan and begins
execution without further interpretation.
