# 20.167 — RPlan‑prm (Realization Plan Constructor Primitive)

## 1. Purpose (informative)

RPlan is the **realization plan constructor** of Path B.  
It accepts:

- the realization substrate `tp.rsub` from REx  
- continuity_fields from CoHI  
- truth_tags and safety_flags from A‑side  

RPlan maps each substep to a typed realization step, assigns ordering, derives constraint sets, computes continuity weights, and produces the ordered realization plan `tp.rplan` for RPU.

RPlan performs:

- no meaning interpretation  
- no substrate construction  
- no execution  
- no scoring  
- no routing  
- no modification of COB  
- no modification of lineage_fields or semantic_core  

RPlan provides a **deterministic, auditable planning artifact** that separates “what to execute and in what order” from “executing it.”

**CoHI (1) → LI (2) → REx (3) → RPlan (4) → RPU (5) → OuBB (6) → ReB (7)**

## 3. Inputs (informative)

RPlan reads exactly the following:

### 3.1 Realization substrate (from REx)
- `tp.rsub`  
- `tp.rsub.rsub_status`  
- `tp.rsub.substeps`  
- `substep_id`, `substep_type`, `payload`, `depends_on`  

### 3.2 Continuity fields (from CoHI)
- structural_history  
- referential_history  
- conversation_objects  
- topic_threads  
- continuity_markers  

### 3.3 A‑side fields
- truth_tags  
- safety_flags  

No other fields may be accessed.

---

## 4. Transfer Function (informative)

RPlan executes one of two paths:

- **Abort Path** — when `rsub_status = BLOCKED`  
- **Planning Path** — when `rsub_status = READY`  

Let:

- $U = \langle u_1, \dots, u_k \rangle$ = substeps  
- $\Gamma$ = continuity_fields  
- $S = \langle s_1, \dots, s_k \rangle$ = realization steps  

---

### 4.1 Abort Path

When `tp.rsub.rsub_status = BLOCKED`, RPlan produces:

- `rplan_status = BLOCKED`  
- `reason_code = tp.rsub.reason_code`  
- empty `ordered_steps`  

---

### 4.2 Planning Path

When `rsub_status = READY`, RPlan constructs the plan.

---

### Step Mapping

Each substep $u_i$ maps to exactly one realization step $s_i$.

Mapping is injective:  
- no substep is dropped  
- no step is created without a substep  

---

### Ordering

Steps are topologically sorted by dependency graph:

- if $u_j \in \text{depends}\_{on}(u_i)$, then $s_j$ precedes $s_i$  
- ties are broken by source order in `tp.rsub.substeps`  

---

### Constraint Derivation

Each step $s_i$ receives a constraint set $C_i$ consisting of:

1. type‑required TP field refs  
2. dependency step_ids  
3. applicable safety_flags  

---

### Continuity Weight Computation

Let $\gamma_j$ be continuity scores and $\alpha_{ij}$ the relevance mask.

$$
\omega_i = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} \alpha_{ij}\ \gamma_j}
      {\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} \alpha_{ij}}
& \text{if } \displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} \alpha_{ij} > 0 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

---

### Viability Check

A constraint set $C_i$ is forward‑satisfiable when all refs are:

- available at construction time, or  
- produced by earlier steps  

The plan is viable when all steps satisfy this condition.

---

## 5. Outputs (informative)

RPlan writes exactly:

- `tp.rplan.rplan_status`  
- `tp.rplan.reason_code`  
- `tp.rplan.ordered_steps`  
- `step_id`, `index`, `step_type`, `payload`, `constraint_set`, `continuity_weight`, `step_status`  

All must be present on every execution path.

## 6. High‑Level Requirements (normative)

### Input and Access Control  
- **HLR‑20.167‑001** — RPlan SHALL read only the fields enumerated in the Input Contract.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑002** — RPlan SHALL NOT read COB, lineage_fields, semantic_core, or any field owned by LI, REx, RPU, OuBB, or ReB.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑003** — RPlan SHALL perform a pre‑condition check and SHALL block with `PRE_CONDITION_FAULT` when required fields are absent or invalid.

### Abort Path  
- **HLR‑20.167‑004** — When `rsub_status = BLOCKED`, RPlan SHALL block and propagate `tp.rsub.reason_code`.

### Step Mapping  
- **HLR‑20.167‑005** — RPlan SHALL map each substep to exactly one realization step.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑006** — RPlan SHALL NOT drop or duplicate substeps.

### Ordering  
- **HLR‑20.167‑007** — RPlan SHALL perform a deterministic topological sort.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑008** — RPlan SHALL detect dependency cycles and block with `DEPENDENCY_CYCLE`.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑009** — RPlan SHALL assign monotonically increasing indices.

### Constraint Derivation  
- **HLR‑20.167‑010** — RPlan SHALL derive constraint sets from type requirements, dependencies, and safety_flags.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑011** — RPlan SHALL NOT include inapplicable constraints.

### Continuity Weights  
- **HLR‑20.167‑012** — RPlan SHALL compute continuity weights using the specified formula.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑013** — RPlan SHALL NOT write continuity weights outside $[0, 1]$.

### Viability  
- **HLR‑20.167‑014** — RPlan SHALL evaluate plan viability using forward‑satisfiability.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑015** — When unsatisfiable, RPlan SHALL block with `UNSATISFIABLE_CONSTRAINT`.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑016** — When viable, RPlan SHALL set `rplan_status = VIABLE`.

### Invariant Preservation  
- **HLR‑20.167‑017** — RPlan SHALL preserve Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑018** — RPlan SHALL propagate provenance_chain without modification.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑019** — RPlan SHALL NOT introduce semantic mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑020** — RPlan SHALL maintain deterministic replay.

### Output Contract  
- **HLR‑20.167‑021** — RPlan SHALL populate all output fields on every execution path.  
- **HLR‑20.167‑022** — RPlan SHALL NOT write to any field owned by TPTB, TPSF, CoHI, LI, REx, RPU, OuBB, or ReB.

---

## 7. Is / Is Not (informative)

RPlan **is**:
- the constructor of realization plans  
- the bridge between substrate and execution  
- the producer of `tp.rplan`  

RPlan **is not**:
- a meaning extractor  
- a semantic interpreter  
- a substrate constructor  
- a scorer  
- a router  
- a memory system  
- an executor  

---

## 8. Example (informative)

REx commits three substeps:

- $u_1$: REFERENT_CONFIRM  
- $u_2$: REGISTER_APPLY  
- $u_3$: TOKEN_EMIT  

Dependencies: $u_1 \to u_2 \to u_3$.

RPlan:

- maps each substep to a step  
- topologically sorts them  
- derives constraints  
- computes continuity weights  
- verifies viability  

Result:  
`rplan_status = VIABLE` with steps indexed 1–3.
