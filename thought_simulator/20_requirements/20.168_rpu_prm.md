# **20.168 — Realization Plan Updater (RPU‑prm)**  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Primitive Class:** Path B — Realization Layer  
**Purpose:** Execute and update the Realization Plan (RPlan) step‑by‑step, resolving ambiguities, filling missing dependencies, and producing a fully realized intermediate artifact for ReB.

---

## **1. Overview**
The Realization Plan Updater (RPU‑prm) is the third primitive in the Path B pipeline.  
It consumes a **Realization Plan (RPlan)** and produces a **Realization Update (RPU)** — a partially or fully realized intermediate structure that:

- Executes RPlan steps in order  
- Resolves ambiguities  
- Fills missing dependencies  
- Applies constraints and invariants  
- Produces structured intermediate content  
- Prepares the output for final assembly by ReB  

RPU is the **execution engine** of Path B.

---

## **2. Inputs and Outputs**

### **2.1 Inputs**
RPU‑prm SHALL read the following RPlan fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `RPlan.steps` | Ordered atomic steps |
| `RPlan.dependencies` | Step dependency graph |
| `RPlan.constraint_map` | Constraint → step mapping |
| `RPlan.invariant_map` | Invariant → step mapping |
| `RPlan.ambiguity_strategy` | Strategy for resolving ambiguities |
| `RPlan.channel` | Output channel |
| `RPlan.audience` | Target audience |
| `RPlan.trace` | Provenance chain |

### **2.2 Outputs**
RPU‑prm SHALL produce a **Realization Update (RPU)** with the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `RPU.partial_realization` | Intermediate content produced by executing steps |
| `RPU.resolved_ambiguities` | Ambiguities resolved during execution |
| `RPU.filled_dependencies` | Dependencies resolved or retrieved |
| `RPU.constraint_state` | Constraint satisfaction status |
| `RPU.invariant_state` | Invariant preservation status |
| `RPU.channel` | Carried from RPlan.channel |
| `RPU.audience` | Carried from RPlan.audience |
| `RPU.trace` | Extended provenance chain |

## **3. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)**  
All SHALL statements are bound to HLR‑20.168.xxx.

---

### **HLR‑20.168.001 — Step Execution**  
RPU‑prm SHALL execute RPlan steps in deterministic order, respecting all dependencies.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Steps SHALL be executed only when all prerequisite steps are complete.  
- Execution SHALL be atomic and side‑effect‑free outside the RPU structure.  
- Execution SHALL produce structured intermediate content, not user‑visible text.

---

### **HLR‑20.168.002 — Ambiguity Resolution**  
RPU‑prm SHALL resolve ambiguities according to `RPlan.ambiguity_strategy`.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Ambiguities SHALL be classified before resolution.  
- Lexical ambiguities SHALL be resolved using contextual inference.  
- Structural ambiguities SHALL be resolved using dependency analysis.  
- Intent‑level ambiguities SHALL be resolved using audience + channel context.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.003 — Dependency Filling**  
RPU‑prm SHALL fill missing dependencies identified in `RPlan.dependencies`.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Missing semantic dependencies SHALL be retrieved or inferred.  
- Missing structural dependencies SHALL be synthesized.  
- Missing contextual dependencies SHALL be derived from audience + channel.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.004 — Constraint Enforcement**  
RPU‑prm SHALL enforce all constraints mapped in `RPlan.constraint_map`.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Length constraints SHALL be enforced during content assembly.  
- Format constraints SHALL be enforced during structural synthesis.  
- Safety constraints SHALL be enforced globally.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.005 — Invariant Preservation**  
RPU‑prm SHALL preserve all invariants mapped in `RPlan.invariant_map`.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Invariants SHALL override constraints in case of conflict.  
- Invariants SHALL be preserved verbatim.  
- Violated invariants SHALL halt execution and raise an internal error state.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.006 — Partial Realization Construction**  
RPU‑prm SHALL construct a structured `RPU.partial_realization` representing the intermediate output.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- Partial realization SHALL be structured, not user‑visible text.  
- Partial realization SHALL include placeholders for unresolved items.  
- Partial realization SHALL be compatible with ReB‑prm.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.007 — Provenance Extension**  
RPU‑prm SHALL extend the provenance chain (`RPlan.trace`) into `RPU.trace`.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- The extension SHALL include a timestamped entry for RPU‑prm.  
- The extension SHALL include the version of this primitive.  

---

### **HLR‑20.168.008 — No Final Realization**  
RPU‑prm SHALL NOT produce final user‑visible output.

**Mechanistic Notes:**  
- RPU‑prm SHALL not generate natural language text.  
- RPU‑prm SHALL not apply stylistic transformations.  
- RPU‑prm SHALL not finalize structure — that is the role of ReB‑prm.

    <status object>

---

## **5. Invariants**

- INV‑20.168.01 — RPU SHALL be deterministic for identical RPlan inputs.  
- INV‑20.168.02 — RPU SHALL preserve all invariants from RPlan.  
- INV‑20.168.03 — RPU SHALL not produce user‑visible text.  
- INV‑20.168.04 — RPU SHALL be fully consumable by ReB‑prm.

## **6. Example End‑to‑End**

### **Input RPlan**
```yaml
steps:
  - identify_target
  - identify_event
  - construct_email_skeleton
  - insert_required_time
  - apply_tone
  - finalize_structure

dependencies:
  identify_event: [identify_target]
  construct_email_skeleton: [identify_event]
  insert_required_time: [construct_email_skeleton]
  apply_tone: [insert_required_time]
  finalize_structure: [apply_tone]

constraint_map:
  length: [apply_tone, finalize_structure]
  format: [construct_email_skeleton]

invariant_map:
  "must mention time: 10am": [insert_required_time]

ambiguity_strategy: []
channel: "email"
audience: "internal team"
trace: [...]
```

### **Output RPU**
```yaml
RPU.partial_realization:
  target: "team"
  event: "meeting"
  skeleton: "<email_skeleton>"
  time_inserted: true
  tone_applied: true
  structure_ready: true

RPU.resolved_ambiguities: []
RPU.filled_dependencies: []
RPU.constraint_state:
  length: "satisfied"
  format: "satisfied"

RPU.invariant_state:
  "must mention time: 10am": "preserved"

RPU.channel: "email"
RPU.audience: "internal team"

RPU.trace:
  - ...previous...
  - {primitive: RPU-prm, version: 0.1, timestamp: ...}
```
