## Purpose

STPX is the Structured Token and Pattern Extractor for Path A. It extracts lexical, structural, and constraint cues from cleaned structural geometry and canonical tokens. It runs immediately after SSG in the Path A pipeline but does not operate on semantic geometry.

---

## Position in Path A

STPX is positioned after SSG in Path A and before RBU. It operates exclusively on structural geometry and canonical tokens produced by SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB → SSG.

## Cue Envelope Schema

The cue_envelope has the following deterministic structure:

$$
CE = \{ L, S, C, R \}
$$

where  
- $L$ = lexical surface cues  
- $S$ = structural cues  
- $C$ = constraint flags  
- $R$ = repair-region markers (optional)

---

## Mathematical Form

$$
CE = \text{Extract}(G_{\text{struct}}, T_{\text{clean}})
$$

where $G_{\text{struct}}$ is the structural geometry and $T_{\text{clean}}$ is the canonical token set.
