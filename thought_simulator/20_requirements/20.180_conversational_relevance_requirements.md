# 20.180 Conversational Relevance Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic cross-turn relevance behavior, including thread continuity, drift detection, and the treatment of CIL, COB, and COP artifacts as relevance context inputs.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes conversational relevance obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including cross-turn persistence, deterministic scoring, explicit rollback/safety rules, and supervisory use of CIL, COB, and COP artifacts where they contribute to continuity or update decisions.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.180-001: Cross-turn MTP persistence SHALL preserve thread identity and continuity anchors.
2. HLR-20.180-002: Relevance scoring SHALL be deterministic for fixed history and state.
3. HLR-20.180-003: Drift detection SHALL trigger IB pathways under explicit thresholds.
4. HLR-20.180-004: Conversational update rules SHALL include safety constraints for continuity and rollback.
5. HLR-20.180-005: CIL SHALL preserve deterministic cross-turn integration order and snapshot coherence for relevance evaluation inputs.
6. HLR-20.180-006: COB continuity anchors and lifecycle state SHALL be treated as deterministic relevance context inputs for cross-turn thread identity and persistence.
7. HLR-20.180-007: COP proposals, when committed through deterministic supervisory-approved paths, SHALL be eligible as relevance context inputs even when they are not derived from a CIL or COB event.
8. HLR-20.180-008: Optional COP proposals SHALL NOT directly change conversational relevance state outside deterministic supervisory-approved commit paths.
9. HLR-20.180-009: Conversational relevance audit artifacts SHALL include deterministic references to CIL integration events and COB continuity-object lineage used in scoring outcomes.
10. HLR-20.180-010: Conversational relevance audit artifacts SHALL also include deterministic references to COP-originated proposals and commit decisions when those artifacts affect relevance scoring or continuity updates.
11. HLR-20.180-011: Relevance scoring SHALL remain stable for identical history, state, and committed COP/CIL/COB artifact sets.
12. HLR-20.180-012: Drift detection SHALL not rely on nondeterministic sampling and SHALL be reproducible for identical inputs.
13. HLR-20.180-013: Relevance updates SHALL preserve safe-boundary ordering and SHALL not bypass supervisory approval paths.
14. HLR-20.180-014: Thread continuity decisions SHALL remain auditable with explicit reason codes and lineage markers for relevant CIL, COB, and COP inputs.
15. HLR-20.180-015: Cross-turn relevance state updates SHALL be versioned and SHALL require re-verification when update rules, commit rules, or scoring inputs change.
16. HLR-20.180-016: Conversational relevance requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

## Functional Boundaries
- Conversational relevance owns deterministic cross-turn scoring, continuity, and drift signaling.
- CIL and COB provide primary continuity and integration anchors.
- COP may contribute proposal and commit artifacts to relevance state only through deterministic supervisory-approved paths.
- Relevance state SHALL not be updated by raw COP proposals that have not been committed through the governing path.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic relevance scoring for identical history and committed artifact state.
- Deterministic inclusion of CIL, COB, and eligible COP inputs.
- Deterministic drift signaling and rollback behavior at safe boundaries.
- Auditability of continuity anchors, lineage markers, and commit decisions.
