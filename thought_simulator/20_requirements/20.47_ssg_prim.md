# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structural Signature Generator (SSG‑prm) is the final stage of the OB residue chain in Path‑A.  
Its role is to map the discrete SmOB structural graph into a **fixed‑length real‑valued vector** in $\mathbb{R}^d$ by extracting and normalizing a canonical set of **structural invariants**:

- arc patterns  
- binding depth  
- residue entropy  
- curvature  
- motif frequencies  

This vector, `tp.ssg_signature`, acts as a **coordinate chart** on the structural manifold: structurally similar graphs land geometrically close in $\mathbb{R}^d$, enabling RB‑prm to perform proximity‑aware relational routing without re‑reading OB layer outputs.

SSG‑prm encodes **how structure is arranged**, not **what it means**.  
It performs **no semantic interpretation**.

---

# **2. Scope** *(Informative)*

SSG‑prm:

- reads the TP after SmOB‑prm has populated the structural graph  
- extracts structural invariants  
- assembles a fixed‑length vector  
- L2‑normalizes the vector  
- writes four TP fields:  
  - `tp.ssg_signature`  
  - `tp.ssg_layer_bitmap`  
  - `tp.ssg_reason_code`  
  - `tp.ssg_status`  
- appends an audit record  
- yields control to RB‑prm  

SSG‑prm does **not**:

- interpret semantics  
- generate candidates  
- perform routing  
- compute ΔH%  
- modify TP.semantic, TP.metadata, TP.context, or TP.intake  

---

# **3. Pipeline Position** *(Informative)*

Single‑core:

```
SmOB‑prm → SSG‑prm → RB‑prm
```

Multi‑core:

```
{ SOB ∥ SROB ∥ CnOB ∥ SmOB } → SSG‑prm → RB‑prm
```

SSG‑prm always runs **after SmOB** and **before RB**.

# **4. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **4.1 Input Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.47‑001**  
SSG‑prm SHALL accept the SmOB‑prm structural graph as its sole structural input.

The SmOB structural graph is a labeled directed graph:

- $V$ — residue nodes  
- $E$ — directed arcs  
- $\lambda : V \cup E \to \mathcal{L}$ — structural labels  

SSG‑prm SHALL NOT read global state, CIL, CE, or external data sources.

---

## **4.2 Signature Computation**

### **HLR‑20.47‑002**  
SSG‑prm SHALL compute `tp.ssg_signature` as a deterministic, seed‑free, fixed‑length real‑valued vector $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}^d$:

$$
\sigma = \frac{\varphi(G)}{\lVert \varphi(G) \rVert_2}
$$

Where:

- $\varphi(G)$ extracts $d$ structural invariants  
- $\lVert \varphi(G) \rVert_2$ is the Euclidean norm  
- $\sigma = \mathbf{0}$ when $\varphi(G)$ is the zero vector  

$\varphi$ SHALL receive **no seed, clock, PID, or environmental input**.

---

### **4.2.1 Structural Invariant Families**

The feature vector $\varphi(G)$ is assembled from five invariant families:

| Index | Family | Description |
|---|---|---|
| $f_1$ | Arc patterns | Normalized frequency distribution over arc labels |
| $f_2$ | Binding depth | Maximum and mean depth of directed binding chains |
| $f_3$ | Residue entropy | Shannon entropy over residue‑address distribution |
| $f_4$ | Curvature | Cycle density and clustering coefficient |
| $f_5$ | Motif frequencies | Normalized counts of canonical structural motifs |

Formally:

$$
\varphi(G) = \bigl[ f_1(G) \ \|\  f_2(G) \ \|\  f_3(G) \ \|\  f_4(G) \ \|\  f_5(G) \bigr]
$$

Where $\|$ denotes vector concatenation.

---

## **4.3 Output Fields**

### **HLR‑20.47‑003**  
SSG‑prm SHALL write the following TP fields:

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `tp.ssg_signature` | `float[d]` | L2‑normalized structural‑invariant vector |
| `tp.ssg_layer_bitmap` | 4‑bit mask | Indicates which OB layers contributed invariants |
| `tp.ssg_reason_code` | enum | Classifies completeness of invariants |
| `tp.ssg_status` | enum | Execution status |

### **HLR‑20.47‑004**  
SSG‑prm SHALL write all SSG fields before RB‑prm is invoked.

SSG‑prm SHALL NOT modify any other TP fields.

---

## **4.4 Layer Bitmap**

Bitmap bits correspond to OB layers:

- $L_0$ = SOB  
- $L_1$ = SROB  
- $L_2$ = CnOB  
- $L_3$ = SmOB  

$$
\text{bitmap} = \sum_{i=0}^{3} b_i \cdot 2^i
$$

Where:

$$
b_i = 
\begin{cases}
1 & \varphi_i(G_{L_i}) \neq \mathbf{0} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

### **HLR‑20.47‑007**  
SSG‑prm SHALL produce a valid `tp.ssg_signature` for every TP.  
If no invariants are present, the signature SHALL be $\mathbf{0}$.

---

## **4.5 Reason Code**

| Code | Condition | Bitmap |
|---|---|---|
| `FULL` | All layers contributed invariants | `0b1111` |
| `PARTIAL` | Some layers contributed zero | `0b0001`–`0b1110` |
| `EMPTY` | No invariants present | `0b0000` |

---

## **4.6 Status Field**

### **HLR‑20.47‑015**  
SSG‑prm SHALL expose `tp.ssg_status ∈ {OK, MISSING_INPUT, DEGENERATE, PARTIAL}`.

---

## **4.7 Geometric Proximity (Informative)**

RB‑prm compares signatures using cosine similarity:

$$
\text{sim}(\sigma_1, \sigma_2)
= \frac{\sigma_1 \cdot \sigma_2}{\lVert \sigma_1 \rVert_2 \cdot \lVert \sigma_2 \rVert_2}
$$

SSG‑prm does **not** compute similarity.

---

## **4.8 Audit**

### **HLR‑20.47‑016**  
SSG‑prm SHALL append an `ssg_ref` record to `exec_trace` on every successful invocation.

# **5. Invariants (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.47‑005**  
SSG‑prm SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, truth evaluation, or candidate generation.

### **HLR‑20.47‑006**  
SSG‑prm SHALL NOT modify any TP field other than:

- `tp.ssg_signature`  
- `tp.ssg_layer_bitmap`  
- `tp.ssg_reason_code`  
- `tp.ssg_status`  

### **HLR‑20.47‑008**  
SSG‑prm SHALL be invoked exactly once per TP per OB‑chain cycle.

### **HLR‑20.47‑009**  
SSG‑prm SHALL NOT be re‑invoked without new SmOB output.

### **HLR‑20.47‑010**  
SSG‑prm SHALL produce identical signatures for identical structural graphs across all invocations.

### **HLR‑20.47‑011**  
SSG‑prm SHALL complete within one TCU of SmOB‑prm and before RB‑prm reads `tp.ssg_signature`.

---

# **6. Failure Modes (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.47‑012 — Missing Input**  
If SmOB output is absent or invalid, SSG‑prm SHALL:

- set `tp.ssg_status = MISSING_INPUT`  
- NOT write `tp.ssg_signature`  
- escalate to MB‑gov  

### **HLR‑20.47‑013 — Absent Layer**  
If any OB layer’s residue graph is absent, SSG‑prm SHALL:

- treat its invariant contribution as zero  
- clear its bitmap bit  
- set `tp.ssg_reason_code = PARTIAL`  

### **HLR‑20.47‑014 — Degenerate Case**  
If $\lVert \varphi(G) \rVert_2 = 0$ but $G$ is non‑empty, SSG‑prm SHALL:

- set `tp.ssg_status = DEGENERATE`  
- escalate to MB‑gov  
- write `tp.ssg_signature = \mathbf{0}`

# **7. Non‑Normative Notes** *(Informative)*

- Structural invariants ensure replay‑safe equivalence across OB layers.  
- Real‑valued vectors support graded structural similarity.  
- Cosine similarity is scale‑invariant and robust in high dimensions.  
- SSG‑prm is a coordinate chart on the structural manifold.  
- `EMPTY` is valid; `DEGENERATE` is anomalous.  
- Layer bitmap supports ΔH% interpretation downstream.
