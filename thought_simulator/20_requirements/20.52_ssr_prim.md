# 20.52_ssr_prim.md — Semantic Snapshot Reference Primitive

## Introduction  
The Semantic Snapshot Reference (SSR) is the immutable, sanitized meaning artifact produced at the Path‑A → Path‑B commit boundary. SSR provides Path‑B, governance, and replay with a deterministic, read‑only projection of the turn’s committed meaning, routing, and identity state. SSR is not a working memory object; it is a projection derived from TP and RRw under OuBA control.

## Purpose  
SSR exists to freeze committed meaning, remove construction‑time residue, expose only Path‑B‑visible fields, bind routing decisions, and support deterministic execution, replay, and audit.

**HLR‑20.52‑001:** SSR **shall** represent the committed meaning state for a single turn as a frozen, immutable object.  
**HLR‑20.52‑002:** SSR **shall** exclude all mutable TP fields, hypothesis‑layer deltas, and lane‑local residue.  
**HLR‑20.52‑003:** SSR **shall** be the sole meaning artifact consumed by Path‑B primitives.  
**HLR‑20.52‑004:** SSR **shall** be consumable by governance and replay without requiring access to TP.

## Input Specification  
SSR is constructed from a small, well‑defined set of inputs at the commit boundary. The mapping is expressed in GitHub‑friendly inline math as:  
$SSR = f\_{OuBA}(TP\_{committed}, RRw, policy\_{signature})$

### Inputs  
- $TP\_{\text{committed}}$: The TP at the moment OuBA declares meaning construction complete.  
- $RRw$: Routing Row primitive produced by RB/TR and validated by RBU.  
- $policy\_{signature}$: Governance and policy metadata.  
- $turn\_{id}$, $core\_{id}$, $orthogonality\_{signature}$: Identity and multi‑core separation metadata.  
- truth/safety/identity completion flags.

**HLR‑20.52‑005:** SSR **shall** be constructed only after TP has reached a committed state for the turn.  
**HLR‑20.52‑006:** SSR **shall** include RRw exactly as validated by RBU.  
**HLR‑20.52‑007:** SSR **shall** include $policy\_{signature}$ sufficient for governance and replay.  
**HLR‑20.52‑008:** SSR **shall** include $turn\_{id}$, $core\_{id}$, and $orthogonality\_{signature}$ sufficient for multi‑core separation.

## Process: Transfer From Input to Output  
OuBA transforms $TP\_{committed}$, RRw, and $policy\_{signature}$ into SSR. The transformation is expressed in GitHub‑friendly block math:

$$
SSR = sanitize(project(TP\_{committed})) \cup \\{RRw\\} \cup \\{policy\_{signature}\\}
$$

Where:

- $project(\cdot)$ selects only Path‑B‑visible fields.  
- $sanitize(\cdot)$ removes construction‑time artifacts and hypothesis‑layer deltas.  
- The union binds RRw and $policy\_signature$ into the immutable SSR.

### Process Steps (informative)  
1. Projection of $semantic\_{core}$, truth/safety/identity fields, continuity fields, discourse act, and identity metadata.  
2. Sanitization removing OB/TB/TPU residue, merge residue, hypothesis‑layer deltas, and lane‑local fields.  
3. Routing binding embedding RRw.  
4. Policy binding attaching $policy\_{signature}$.  
5. Freeze marking SSR immutable.

### High‑Level Requirements (Process)  
**HLR‑20.52‑009:** OuBA **shall** project only Path‑B‑visible fields from TP into SSR.  
**HLR‑20.52‑010:** OuBA **shall** embed RRw into SSR such that Path‑B can read routing decisions without accessing TP.  
**HLR‑20.52‑011:** OuBA **shall** attach $policy\_{signature}$ to SSR.  
**HLR‑20.52‑012:** OuBA **shall** mark SSR as immutable once constructed.  
**HLR‑20.52‑013:** OuBA **shall** ensure SSR contains continuity and identity metadata sufficient for replay and audit.

## Output Specification  
SSR is the committed meaning artifact consumed by Path‑B, governance, and replay.

### SSR Contents (informative)  
SSR contains:  
- $semantic\_{core}$  
- truth/safety/identity fields  
- continuity fields  
- discourse act  
- $orthogonality\_{signature}$  
- $core\_{id}$, $turn\_{id}$  
- conflict/underspec flags  
- RRw  
- $policy\_{signature}$

### High‑Level Requirements (Output)  
**HLR‑20.52‑014:** SSR **shall** contain $semantic\_{core}$ sufficient for Path‑B to realize meaning without TP.  
**HLR‑20.52‑015:** SSR **shall** contain committed truth, safety, and identity fields.  
**HLR‑20.52‑016:** SSR **shall** contain continuity fields sufficient for discourse continuity.  
**HLR‑20.52‑017:** SSR **shall** contain discourse act metadata sufficient for behavior selection.  
**HLR‑20.52‑018:** SSR **shall** contain $orthogonality\_{signature}$, $core\_{id}$, and $turn\_{id}$ sufficient for multi‑core separation.  
**HLR‑20.52‑019:** SSR **shall** contain conflict and underspecification flags that Path‑B must honor.  
**HLR‑20.52‑020:** SSR **shall** contain RRw as the authoritative routing record.  
**HLR‑20.52‑021:** SSR **shall** contain $policy\_{signature}$ sufficient for governance and replay.

## Examples  

### Example 1 — Clean Turn  
A factual question yields a committed TP with no conflicts. OuBA projects TP, sanitizes it, embeds RRw selecting an answer OpBeh, attaches $policy\_{signature}$, and freezes SSR. Path‑B reads SSR and assembles XP deterministically.

### Example 2 — Ambiguous Turn  
A question with two interpretations yields conflict flags. OuBA preserves these flags, embeds RRw selecting a clarification OpBeh, attaches a safety‑sensitive $policy\_{signature}$, and freezes SSR. Path‑B reads SSR and assembles XP that requests clarification.

### Example 3 — Replay  
Replay reads SSR, reconstructs committed meaning, routing, and identity metadata, and reproduces the turn without accessing TP.
