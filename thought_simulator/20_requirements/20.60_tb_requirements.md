# **20.60 — TB Requirements (v0.5, Fully Rewritten)**  
**Document ID:** 20.60  
**Version:** 0.5  
**Date:** 2026‑06‑24  
**Status:** Draft — Architecture‑Aligned Rewrite (Pipeline‑A Interpretive Basin)

---

# **0. Purpose**

The **Truth Basin (TB)** is the **Pipeline A interpretive basin** responsible for:

- interpreting OB evidence  
- producing structured **channel_interpretations{}**  
- generating **truth_hypothesis_records[]**  
- contributing to ΔH% and meaning construction  

TB is part of the **meaning‑construction pipeline**, not a post‑TS subsystem.  
TB operates **lane‑parallel**, writes to **TP**, and produces the structured interpretation inputs consumed by Truth/Done (20.140).

---

# **1. Scope**

TB covers:

- channel interpretation  
- evidence consolidation  
- contradiction and entailment detection  
- uncertainty and stance interpretation  
- structured hypothesis record generation  

TB does **not**:

- perform OB evidence extraction  
- perform Truth/Done evaluation  
- perform routing or arbitration  
- read or write any TS‑side snapshot  
- interact with Pipeline B  

TB operates **only on TP/OuBA fields** and produces **TP‑local outputs**.

---

# **2. Placement in the Pipeline**

TB runs inside **Pipeline A**, after:

1. OB evidence extraction  
2. initial meaning construction  
3. ΔH% updates  
4. lane‑local merges  

TB produces:

- `channel_interpretations{}`  
- `truth_hypothesis_records[]`  

These flow into:

- ΔH% normalization  
- Truth/Done evaluation (20.140)  
- OuBA finalization  

TB does **not** interact with MTP or any TS‑side commit mechanism.

---

# **3. Inputs**

TB consumes only explicit TP/OuBA fields:

- OB‑committed evidence  
- `propositions[]`  
- `semantic_tags[]`  
- `lineage[]`  
- `delta_h` / ΔH% fields  
- `messy_input_record` (read‑only)  
- `policy_signature`  
- `thought_router_fields` (read‑only)  

TB does **not** read:

- any TS‑side snapshot  
- `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`  
- `routing_epoch_id`, `routing_metadata`  
- Pipeline B artifacts  

---

# **4. Outputs**

TB produces two TP‑local structures:

### **4.1 channel_interpretations{}**

```
channel_interpretations {
  contradiction_markers?,
  entailment_markers?,
  uncertainty_markers?,
  affect_markers?,
  stance_markers?,
  logic_markers?
}
```

### **4.2 truth_hypothesis_records[]**

```
truth_hypothesis_record {
  hypothesis_id,
  proposition_ref,
  evidence_refs[],
  interpretation_tags[],
  channel_support_q32,
  channel_conflict_q32,
  last_updated_cycle
}
```

TB does **not** produce Truth/Done outputs (`truth_hypotheses`, `done_state`).

---

# **5. Interpretation Rules**

TB performs:

- contradiction detection  
- entailment detection  
- uncertainty extraction  
- stance and affect interpretation  
- logic‑structure extraction  
- evidence consolidation  

TB does **not**:

- perform probabilistic inference  
- perform semantic repair  
- invent propositions or evidence  
- perform routing or arbitration  

---

# **6. Messy‑Input Handling**

TB treats `messy_input_record` as **read‑only**:

- `MI_CONTRA` influences contradiction markers  
- `MI_VAGUE` influences uncertainty markers  
- `MI_INCOMP` influences missing‑evidence markers  

TB does not create, modify, or clear messy‑input tags.

---

# **7. Determinism & Replay**

TB outputs are reproducible from:

- TP/OuBA effective input state  
- `policy_signature`  

Seed does not influence TB.

Replay is defined over **TP → OuBA**, not over any TS‑side snapshot.

---

# **8. Functional Boundaries**

- **OB**: extracts evidence  
- **TB**: interprets evidence  
- **Truth/Done**: evaluates truth and completion  
- **RB/TR**: do not consume TB outputs for same‑cycle routing  
- **Pipeline B**: consumes OuBA; does not modify TB outputs  
- **TS snapshots**: not involved  

---

# **9. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**  
*(All SHALL statements appear only here.)*

### **Interpretation**

**HLR‑20.060‑001 — Deterministic interpretation**  
TB SHALL compute `channel_interpretations{}` and `truth_hypothesis_records[]` using deterministic field‑based logic over TP/OuBA inputs.

**HLR‑20.060‑002 — Evidence consolidation**  
TB SHALL consolidate OB‑committed evidence into structured interpretation markers.

**HLR‑20.060‑003 — No invented content**  
TB SHALL NOT invent propositions, hypotheses, or evidence references not present in approved inputs.

---

### **Channel Interpretations**

**HLR‑20.060‑004 — Contradiction detection**  
TB SHALL detect contradictions using explicit structured evidence and interpretation rules.

**HLR‑20.060‑005 — Entailment detection**  
TB SHALL detect entailment relationships using explicit structured evidence.

**HLR‑20.060‑006 — Uncertainty extraction**  
TB SHALL extract uncertainty markers using explicit structured cues and policy.

**HLR‑20.060‑007 — Stance and affect extraction**  
TB SHALL extract stance and affect markers using explicit structured cues.

---

### **Hypothesis Records**

**HLR‑20.060‑008 — Hypothesis record generation**  
TB SHALL generate `truth_hypothesis_records[]` conforming to §4.2 minimum schema.

**HLR‑20.060‑009 — Canonical ordering**  
TB SHALL apply canonical ordering to `truth_hypothesis_records[]` and nested lists before export or hashing.

---

### **Messy‑Input Handling**

**HLR‑20.060‑010 — Messy‑input read‑only**  
TB SHALL treat `messy_input_record` as read‑only and SHALL NOT create, modify, or clear messy‑input tags.

---

### **Approved Read Set**

**HLR‑20.060‑011 — Approved input enforcement**  
TB SHALL read only the approved field set defined in §3.

**HLR‑20.060‑012 — Forbidden reads**  
TB SHALL NOT read `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`, `routing_epoch_id`, `routing_metadata`, or any TS‑side snapshot.

---

### **Writes & Boundaries**

**HLR‑20.060‑013 — TP‑local writes only**  
TB SHALL write only to TP/OuBA fields defined in §4.

**HLR‑20.060‑014 — No routing writes**  
TB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`, routing flags, or routing metadata.

**HLR‑20.060‑015 — No Truth/Done writes**  
TB SHALL NOT write `truth_hypotheses`, `done_state`, or any Truth/Done fields.

---

### **Determinism & Replay**

**HLR‑20.060‑016 — Replay determinism**  
TB outputs SHALL be reproducible for identical TP/OuBA input state and `policy_signature`.

**HLR‑20.060‑017 — Seed independence**  
TB outputs SHALL NOT depend on seed.

**HLR‑20.060‑018 — Allocation independence**  
TB output derivation SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order, allocation sequence, or container address identity.

---

### **Verification & Policy**

**HLR‑20.060‑019 — Testability**  
TB SHALL be testable through deterministic fixtures covering nominal, boundary, ambiguous, and messy‑input‑gated cases.

**HLR‑20.060‑020 — Policy traceability**  
TB decisions SHALL be traceable to explicit policy references and reason codes.
