# 20.60 TB Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic Truth Basin (TB) behavior, interfaces, invariants, and observability requirements for semantic interpretation within the TS functional pipeline.
TB integrates structured evidence into explicit interpretation outputs that support truth-evaluation and completion stages while preserving deterministic lineage and auditability.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.060-001: TB SHALL produce structured truth interpretation fields.
2. HLR-20.060-002: TB SHALL operate over bounded, explicit inputs only and SHALL NOT rely on hidden or latent state.
3. HLR-20.060-003: TB SHALL NOT perform RB routing control, split arbitration, or merge arbitration authority.
4. HLR-20.060-004: TB execution ordering and channel-evaluation ordering SHALL be deterministic for identical effective state.
5. HLR-20.060-005: TB SHALL support stance, affect, intent, logic, and social interpretation channels as explicit deterministic outputs.
6. HLR-20.060-006: TB interpretations SHALL preserve lineage/provenance continuity across channels and across cycles.
7. HLR-20.060-007: TB outputs SHALL preserve compatibility with upstream ΔH% contribution and missing-mass accounting context.
8. HLR-20.060-008: TB SHALL support deterministic handling of noisy or ill-formed input effects by interpreting stabilized conceptual evidence rather than surface-form noise.
9. HLR-20.060-009: TB outputs SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical lane evidence input, MTP context, and seed-bounded system state.
10. HLR-20.060-010: TB SHALL NOT emit probabilistic latent-state artifacts as primitive outputs.
11. HLR-20.060-011: TB SHALL NOT perform hallucinated correction or unstated-content invention during interpretation.
12. HLR-20.060-012: TB SHALL expose deterministic test interfaces with explicit inputs, expected outputs, invariants, and failure modes.
13. HLR-20.060-013: TB SHALL expose test hooks sufficient for unit, integration, system, and property-based verification of interpretation behavior.
14. HLR-20.060-014: TB lifecycle transitions (entry, running, completion/exit) SHALL be explicitly logged and observable.
15. HLR-20.060-015: TB SHALL expose observability data required by MB pathways, including interpretation rationale fields, channel outputs, lane identifiers, and provenance context.
16. HLR-20.060-016: TB telemetry SHALL support deterministic diagnostic consumption by GB, OuB, and IB without mutating TB core semantics.
17. HLR-20.060-017: TB SHALL define measurable TCU budgets (min/typ/max) and per-tick TCU reporting fields.
18. HLR-20.060-018: TB overload/degradation behavior SHALL be deterministic, policy-bounded, and auditable.
19. HLR-20.060-019: TB behavior SHALL be platform-independent and SHALL NOT depend on hardware-, OS-, or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
20. HLR-20.060-020: TB contracts SHALL prioritize top-down thought-processing semantics over implementation-specific mechanics.
21. HLR-20.060-021: TB SHALL accept a deterministic input object containing structured OB evidence fields, lane identifier, RB routing context, lineage/provenance fields, ΔH% contribution and missing-mass context, and MTP snapshot fields required for interpretation.
22. HLR-20.060-022: TB SHALL emit a deterministic output object containing channel interpretation fields, truth_hypotheses-compatible interpretation records, updated lineage/provenance fields, TB execution diagnostics, and observability/telemetry fields required by MB, GB, OuB, and IB.
23. HLR-20.060-023: TB SHALL function as a deterministic semantic-interpretation basin that transforms structured evidence into explicit truth-evaluation inputs without assuming OB evidence-extraction authority or RB routing authority.

## TB Invariants
- Determinism invariant: identical effective input state yields identical TB interpretation outputs and channel semantics.
- Boundary invariant: TB SHALL not assume authority belonging to RB (routing/split/merge arbitration) or OB (evidence extraction).
- Auditability invariant: each TB interpretation field SHALL be attributable to explicit evidence inputs and deterministic processing steps.
