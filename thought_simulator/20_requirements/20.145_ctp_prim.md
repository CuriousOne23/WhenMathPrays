# **20.145 — CTP‑prm (Collect Thought Point Primitive)**

## **1. Purpose**

The purpose of **CTP‑prm** is to **collect, preserve, and package** the outputs of all OB‑prm instances routed by RB‑prm during an OB‑chain, and to produce a **single combined TP snapshot** for the next RB‑prm arbitration cycle.

CTP‑prm performs **no semantic work**.  
It does **not** merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or modify TP semantics.  
Its role is strictly **structural bookkeeping**.

CTP‑prm ensures that:

- all OB outputs are included,  
- provenance is preserved,  
- the TP snapshot is complete and immutable, and  
- RB‑prm receives the information it needs to route correctly,  
  **without knowing that CTP‑prm exists.**

CTP‑prm is the **barrier primitive** between OB‑chains in Pipeline A.

---

## **2. When and How CTP‑prm Is Used**

### **2.1 When CTP‑prm executes**

CTP‑prm executes:

- **after every OB‑chain**,  
- **before every RB‑prm arbitration except the first**,  
- **once all OB‑prm instances routed by RB‑prm have completed**,  
- **in both the corrected and no‑correction Path A flows**.

### **2.2 How CTP‑prm is used**

1. RB‑prm routes one or more OB‑prm instances.  
2. OB‑prm instances execute (sequentially or in parallel).  
3. TS tracks completion of all routed OB‑prm instances.  
4. TS invokes CTP‑prm.  
5. CTP‑prm:
   - collects all OB outputs,  
   - preserves provenance,  
   - performs no semantic merging,  
   - produces a combined TP snapshot,  
   - freezes the TP state.  
6. TS provides the combined TP snapshot to RB‑prm.  
7. RB‑prm arbitrates and may route another OB‑chain.

This produces the canonical Path A pattern:

```
OB → RB → {OB₁, OB₂, …, OBₙ} → CTP → RB → {OB₁a, OB₂a, …, OBₙa} → CTP → RB → …
```

CTP‑prm is the **only** mechanism for freezing and packaging TP state between OB‑chains.

---

## **3. Normative Requirements (SHALL statements)**

All SHALL statements below are normative.

---

### **3.1 Execution and Placement**

- **HLR‑20.145‑001**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** execute after every OB‑chain routed by RB‑prm.

- **HLR‑20.145‑002**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** execute before every RB‑prm arbitration except the first in the Path A cycle.

- **HLR‑20.145‑003**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** be bypassed when RB‑prm has routed one or more OB‑prm instances.

---

### **3.2 Collection and Combination**

- **HLR‑20.145‑004**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** collect the outputs of **all** OB‑prm instances routed by RB‑prm in the current OB‑chain.

- **HLR‑20.145‑005**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** wait until **all** routed OB‑prm instances have completed before producing its output.

- **HLR‑20.145‑006**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** combine all OB‑prm outputs into a **single TP snapshot** without semantic merging.

- **HLR‑20.145‑007**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** preserve provenance such that the contribution of each OB‑prm instance remains identifiable.

---

### **3.3 Freezing and Immutability**

- **HLR‑20.145‑008**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** freeze the TP snapshot such that no modifications occur until after the next RB‑prm arbitration.

- **HLR‑20.145‑009**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** produce an immutable TP snapshot that RB‑prm can safely read without risk of concurrent modification.

---

### **3.4 Interaction with TS, OB‑prm, and RB‑prm**

- **HLR‑20.145‑010**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** accept as input only the OB‑prm outputs provided by TS.

- **HLR‑20.145‑011**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** provide its TP snapshot exclusively to TS for delivery to RB‑prm.

- **HLR‑20.145‑012**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** modify TP semantics; only TPU‑prm is permitted to modify TP content.

- **HLR‑20.145‑013**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** ensure that the combined TP snapshot contains all routing‑relevant information required by RB‑prm to perform deterministic routing.

- **HLR‑20.145‑014**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** perform routing, scoring, arbitration, or semantic interpretation.

---

### **3.5 Parallel Execution Semantics**

- **HLR‑20.145‑015**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** support collection of OB‑prm outputs executed sequentially or in parallel.

- **HLR‑20.145‑016**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** treat all OB‑prm outputs routed by RB‑prm as belonging to a single OB‑chain, regardless of execution order or parallelism.

---

### **3.6 Error and Completeness Guarantees**

- **HLR‑20.145‑017**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** detect and reject incomplete OB‑chains (i.e., missing OB‑prm outputs).

- **HLR‑20.145‑018**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** fail safely if OB‑prm outputs are structurally invalid or inconsistent.

- **HLR‑20.145‑019**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** guarantee that RB‑prm receives exactly one TP snapshot per OB‑chain.

---

## **4. Summary**

CTP‑prm is a **lightweight, modular, non‑semantic** primitive whose sole responsibilities are:

- collecting OB outputs,  
- preserving provenance,  
- freezing TP state,  
- packaging a combined TP snapshot,  
- ensuring RB‑prm receives a complete routing substrate,  
- enabling repeated OB‑chains in Path A.

CTP‑prm does **not** merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or perform routing.  
It is a pure structural bookkeeping primitive essential for the determinism and auditability of Pipeline A.
