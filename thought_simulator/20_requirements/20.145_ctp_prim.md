# **20.145 — CTP‑prm (Collect Thought Point Primitive)**

## **1. Purpose**

The purpose of **CTP‑prm** is to **collect, preserve, and package** the outputs of RBU/TR and all IdOB  instances routed by RB‑prm during an IdOB‑chain, and to produce a **single combined TP snapshot** for the next RB‑prm arbitration cycle.

CTP‑prm performs **no semantic work**.  
It does **not** merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or modify TP semantics.  
Its role is strictly **structural bookkeeping**.

CTP‑prm ensures that:

- RBU/TR information is included,
- all IdOB outputs are included,  
- provenance is preserved,  
- the TP snapshot is complete and immutable, and  
- RB‑prm receives the information it needs to route correctly,  
  **without knowing that CTP‑prm exists.**

CTP‑prm is the **barrier primitive** between IdOB‑chains in Pipeline A.

CTP‑prm consolidates meaning‑layer deltas (IdOB output) and normalizes routing‑layer deltas (RBU/TR/RTU output). CTP‑prm SHALL preserve the separation between routing‑layer fields and meaning‑layer fields internally, while producing a unified TP snapshot that RB‑prm can route on deterministically.

The first appearance of CTP (after RBU + TR) is envelope initialization and TP snapshot stabilization. Subsequent appearances (after IdOB) are executions of the CTP primitive, which consolidate meaning‑layer deltas and normalize routing‑layer deltas for the next RB‑prm arbitration.

---

## **2. When and How CTP‑prm Is Used**

### **2.1 When CTP‑prm executes**

CTP‑prm executes:

- **after  RBU/TR primitives**
- **after every IdOB‑chain**,
- **before every RB‑prm arbitration except the first**,  
- **once all IdOB‑prm instances routed by RB‑prm have completed**,  
- **in both the corrected and no‑correction Path A flows**.

### **2.2 How CTP‑prm is used**

1. RB‑prm routes one or more IdOB‑prm instances.  
2. IdOB‑prm instances execute (sequentially or in parallel).  
3. TS tracks completion of all routed IdOB‑prm instances.  
4. TS invokes CTP‑prm.  
5. CTP‑prm:
   - collects all  RBU/TR and IdOB outputs,  
   - preserves provenance,  
   - performs no semantic merging,  
   - produces a combined TP snapshot,  
   - freezes the TP state.  
6. TS provides the combined TP snapshot to RB‑prm.  
7. RB‑prm arbitrates and may route another IdOB‑chain.

This produces the canonical Path A pattern:

RBU → TR → CTP(field) → RB → RTU → IdOB → CTP(primitive) → RB → RTU → IdOB → CTP(primitive) → RB → …

Where:
- CTP(field) is the initial TP‑snapshot stabilization after RBU → TR.
- CTP(primitive) is the consolidation of meaning‑layer deltas (IdOB output) and normalization of routing‑layer deltas (RTU output).

CTP‑prm is the only mechanism for freezing and packaging TP state between:
- routing‑layer initialization (RBU/TR), and
- meaning‑layer refinement cycles (IdOB → CTP → RB → RTU → IdOB …).

CTP‑prm ensures RB‑prm always receives a single unified TP snapshot for deterministic routing, while preserving the internal separation between routing‑layer fields and meaning‑layer fields.

---

### 2.3 CTP(field) vs. CTP(primitive)
CTP(field) appears immediately after RBU → TR and represents initialization of the consolidated‑TP envelope. It performs no collection, consolidation, or freezing; it only stabilizes the TP snapshot after routing‑layer initialization.

CTP(primitive) executes after IdOB and performs the full consolidation behavior defined in this document: collecting meaning‑layer deltas, normalizing routing‑layer deltas, preserving provenance, and producing a unified TP snapshot for RB‑prm.

## **3. Normative Requirements (SHALL statements)**

All SHALL statements below are normative.

---

### **3.1 Execution and Placement**

- **HLR‑20.145‑001**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** execute after every RBU/TR and IdOB route by RB‑prm.

- **HLR‑20.145‑002**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** execute before every RB‑prm arbitration except the first in the Path A cycle.

- **HLR‑20.145‑003**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** be bypassed when RB‑prm has routed one or more IdOB‑prm instances.

---

### **3.2 Collection and Combination**

- **HLR‑20.145‑004**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** collect the outputs of **all** IdOB‑prm instances routed by RB‑prm in the current IdOB‑chain.

- **HLR‑20.145‑005**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** wait until **all** routed IdOB‑prm instances have completed before producing its output.

- **HLR‑20.145‑006**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** combine all IdOB‑prm outputs into a **single TP snapshot** without semantic merging.

- **HLR‑20.145‑007**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** preserve provenance such that the contribution of each  RBU/TR and IdOB primitive instance remains identifiable.

---

### **3.3 Freezing and Immutability**

- **HLR‑20.145‑008**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** freeze the TP snapshot such that no modifications occur until after the next RB‑prm arbitration.

- **HLR‑20.145‑009**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** produce an immutable TP snapshot that RB‑prm can safely read without risk of concurrent modification.

---

### **3.4 Interaction with TS, RBU/TR and IdOB primitives, and RB‑prm**

- **HLR‑20.145‑010**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** accept as input only the  RBU/TR and IdOB outputs provided by TS.

- **HLR‑20.145‑011**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** provide its TP snapshot exclusively to TS for delivery to RB‑prm.

- **HLR‑20.145‑012**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** modify TP semantics; only TPU‑prm is permitted to modify TP content.

- **HLR‑20.145‑013**  
CTP‑prm SHALL ensure that the combined TP snapshot contains all routing‑relevant information required by RB‑prm to perform deterministic routing, while preserving the internal separation between routing‑layer fields (RBU/TR/RTU output) and meaning‑layer fields (IdOB output).

- **HLR‑20.145‑014**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL NOT** perform routing, scoring, arbitration, or semantic interpretation.

- **HLR‑20.145‑020**
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT merge routing‑layer fields into meaning‑layer fields or vice‑versa. CTP‑prm SHALL unify these fields only at the TP snapshot boundary for RB‑prm consumption.

---

### **3.5 Parallel Execution Semantics**

- **HLR‑20.145‑015**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** support collection of IdOB‑prm outputs executed sequentially or in parallel.

- **HLR‑20.145‑016**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** treat all IdOB‑prm outputs routed by RB‑prm as belonging to a single IdOB‑chain, regardless of execution order or parallelism.

---

### **3.6 Error and Completeness Guarantees**

- **HLR‑20.145‑017**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** detect and reject incomplete IdOB‑chains (i.e., missing IdOB‑prm outputs).

- **HLR‑20.145‑018**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** fail safely if IdOB‑prm outputs are structurally invalid or inconsistent.

- **HLR‑20.145‑019**  
  CTP‑prm **SHALL** guarantee that RB‑prm receives exactly one TP snapshot per IdOB‑chain.

---

## **4. Summary**

CTP‑prm is a **lightweight, modular, non‑semantic** primitive whose sole responsibilities are:

- collecting  RBU/TR and IdOB outputs,  
- preserving provenance,  
- freezing TP state,  
- packaging a combined TP snapshot,  
- ensuring RB‑prm receives a complete routing substrate,  
- enabling repeated IdOB‑chains in Path A.

CTP‑prm does **not** merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or perform routing.  
It is a pure structural bookkeeping primitive essential for the determinism and auditability of Pipeline A.
