# **20.30.080 — Response Generation Semantics**  
**Status:** Normative (HLR‑20.30.080‑xxx only)  
**Scope:** Expression‑layer semantics, seed isolation, realization mapping  
**Excludes:** Meaning construction (Pipeline A), merge semantics, truth/completion, RBU commit

---

# **0. Purpose**

This document defines the semantics of the **Response Generator (RG)**, the final stage of Pipeline B.  
It describes:

- how the RG converts the finalized TP(t) into surface‑form output  
- how expressive variability is introduced  
- how the random seed is isolated  
- how deterministic replay is preserved  
- how the RG interacts with the A/B boundary  

The RG **does not construct meaning**.  
It expresses meaning that has already been finalized by Pipeline A.

---

# **1. Response Generator Overview**

The RG is responsible for:

- realization planning  
- expression mapping  
- surface‑form generation  

It consumes:

- the finalized TP(t) at the A/B boundary  
- the `commit_id`  
- the response‑generator seed  

The RG is **deterministic given (TP(t), seed)**.

It does not:

- modify the MTP  
- alter meaning  
- reinterpret truth or completion  
- perform semantic inference  

It is an **expression‑only** subsystem.

---

# **2. Input to the Response Generator**

The RG receives:

- the finalized TP(t) as its semantic input  
- the `commit_id`  
- the response‑generator seed  
- realization metadata from Pipeline B  

The RG treats TP(t) as immutable.

## **2.1 Use of TPTB and TPSF in Response Generation**

The RG reads the TPTB (truth metadata) and TPSF (safety metadata) fields from the finalized TP(t).

- The RG does **not** perform truth evaluation or safety evaluation.  
- The RG expresses meaning **consistent** with the metadata provided by Path A.  
- TPTB guides truth‑aware expression (qualification, correction, clarification).  
- TPSF guides safety‑aware expression (refusal, redirection, safe‑completion).  
- The RG does **not** modify TPTB or TPSF.  
- The RG does **not** reinterpret or override truth or safety outcomes.

TPTB and TPSF are part of the immutable semantic snapshot at the A/B boundary.

---

# **2.2 Truth/Safety → Expression Mapping Table**

This table defines how **truth metadata (TPTB)** and **safety metadata (TPSF)** in the finalized TP(t) deterministically influence **expression‑layer behavior** in the Response Generator (RG).  
These mappings are **expression‑only** and **shall not** modify meaning, truth state, or safety state.

The RG performs **no evaluation**; it performs **expression‑layer realization** based solely on the metadata provided by Pipeline A.

---

## **2.2 Truth/Safety Expression Mapping Table**

| Metadata Field | Source (TPTB/TPSF) | Expression‑Layer Behavior (RG) | Notes |
|----------------|--------------------|--------------------------------|-------|
| **truth_conflict** | TPTB | Adds qualification markers (e.g., “may”, “appears”, “uncertain”) when conflict is present. | RG does not resolve conflict; it only expresses it. |
| **conflict_type** | TPTB | Selects appropriate clarification pattern (e.g., contradiction vs. insufficiency). | Deterministic mapping; no inference. |
| **alignment_class** | TPTB | Adjusts tone toward alignment, neutrality, or distancing. | Does not alter stance; only expresses it. |
| **truth_basis** | TPTB | Determines whether explanation includes evidence‑based, structural, or contextual phrasing. | RG does not generate new evidence. |
| **hypothesis_band** | TPTB | Controls level of certainty markers (e.g., “likely”, “unclear”, “incomplete”). | Band → deterministic linguistic shading. |
| **explanation** | TPTB | May be surfaced as a parenthetical or clarifying clause. | RG does not rewrite or reinterpret explanation. |
| **safety_violation_type** | TPSF | Determines refusal template (e.g., harmful request, disallowed content, protected class). | RG does not evaluate safety; TPSF is authoritative. |
| **safety_rationale** | TPSF | Controls the explanatory portion of a refusal or redirection. | RG expresses rationale verbatim or in mapped form. |
| **safe_completion_path** | TPSF | Selects redirection or alternative‑suggestion patterns. | Deterministic mapping; no semantic invention. |
| **allowed_scope** | TPSF | Constrains the expressive domain (e.g., general info only, high‑level only). | RG must not exceed allowed scope. |
| **disallowed_scope** | TPSF | Suppresses expression of prohibited content. | RG must not override TPSF. |
| **safety_conflict** | TPSF | Adds explicit disclaimers or safety‑aware framing. | RG does not resolve conflict; only expresses it. |

---

## **Interpretation Rules for the Table**

1. **Metadata → Expression Only**  
   The RG uses metadata to select *expression patterns*, not to modify meaning.

2. **No Truth or Safety Evaluation**  
   All fields are treated as **final**; the RG performs no inference.

3. **Deterministic Mapping**  
   For a fixed (TP(t), seed), the same metadata produces the same expression behavior.

4. **No Modification of Metadata**  
   TPTB and TPSF are read‑only; the RG does not rewrite, enrich, or reinterpret them.

5. **Seed‑Isolated Variability**  
   Variability applies only to *surface form*, not to metadata‑driven behavior.

---

# **3. Realization Planning**

Realization planning determines:

- the structure of the output  
- the ordering of expressed elements  
- the mapping from semantic fields to linguistic constructs  
- the level of expressive detail  

Realization planning is deterministic for a fixed seed.

---

# **4. Expression Mapping**

Expression mapping converts:

- propositions → sentences  
- stance/affect → tone  
- intent → directive/expressive forms  
- semantic tags → structural cues  

Expression mapping is deterministic for a fixed seed and does not modify meaning.

---

# **5. Variability and Seed Isolation**

Expressive variability is introduced **only** through the response‑generator seed.

The seed influences:

- lexical choice  
- phrasing  
- stylistic variation  
- ordering of equivalent expressions  

The seed does **not** influence:

- RTU/TR semantics  
- OB/TB semantics  
- ΔH%  
- truth evaluation  
- completion evaluation  
- merge behavior  
- RBU commit  
- MTP content  

Seed isolation ensures that TP(t) remains deterministic and immutable at the A/B boundary.

---

# **6. Replay Semantics**

Replay equivalence requires:

```
(TP(t), seed) → identical output
```

If the seed is unchanged, the RG produces identical surface‑form output.  
If the seed changes, meaning remains identical but expression may vary.

Replay determinism applies only to the expression layer; meaning is fixed by Pipeline A.

---

# **7. Output Structure**

The RG produces:

- a surface‑form response  
- optional structured metadata (e.g., tone, style, realization trace)  
- a deterministic mapping from semantic fields to output segments  

The output is a **pure expression** of the TP(t).

---

# **8. Normative Requirements (HLR‑20.30.080‑xxx)**

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑001 — Immutable TP(t)**  
The RG SHALL treat the finalized TP(t) as immutable and SHALL NOT modify meaning.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑002 — Deterministic Realization**  
The RG SHALL produce deterministic realization behavior for a fixed (TP(t), seed) tuple.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑003 — Seed‑Isolated Variability**  
Expressive variability SHALL be controlled exclusively by the response‑generator seed.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑004 — No Semantic Inference**  
The RG SHALL NOT perform semantic inference, reinterpret meaning, or alter truth/completion state.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑005 — Replay‑Safe Output**  
For identical (TP(t), seed) tuples, the RG SHALL produce identical surface‑form output.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑006 — Deterministic Mapping**  
The RG SHALL map semantic fields to surface‑form constructs using deterministic rules.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑007 — A/B Boundary Compliance**  
The RG SHALL operate strictly on the finalized TP(t) at the A/B boundary and SHALL NOT access pre‑commit semantic state.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑008 — No Write‑Back**  
The RG SHALL NOT write to TP, MTP, or any Pipeline A structure.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑009 — TPTB Consumption**  
The RG SHALL read TPTB metadata from the finalized TP(t) and SHALL NOT perform truth evaluation.

### **HLR‑20.30.080‑010 — TPSF Consumption**  
The RG SHALL read TPSF metadata from the finalized TP(t) and SHALL NOT perform safety evaluation.

---

# **9. Summary**

20.30.080 defines the TS’s expression layer:

- deterministic realization  
- deterministic expression mapping  
- seed‑isolated variability  
- immutable TP(t) at the A/B boundary  
- replay‑safe output  
- no semantic inference  
- strict A/B boundary compliance
