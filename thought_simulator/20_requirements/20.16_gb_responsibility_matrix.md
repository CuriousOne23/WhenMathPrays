# 20.16 GB Responsibility Matrix

**Document ID:** 20.16  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-03  
**Status:** Draft - For CP Review  
**Source:** 20.10 Architectural Principles (Section 1.14) + Grok-CP Dialogue

## Scope
This document defines the clear boundaries, responsibilities, and limitations of the Governing Basin (GB) supervisory layer. It operationalizes the GB Overload risk mitigation identified in 20.10.

## Non-Goals
- This document does not define GB algorithms or implementation details.
- It does not specify scoring formulas or execution logic (belongs in 30/40-series).
- It does not define basin-specific rules.

## 1. Purpose
To prevent GB from becoming a monolithic “god module” by explicitly defining:
- What GB **must** do
- What GB **must not** do
- How responsibilities are distributed

## 2. GB Responsibility Matrix

**HLR-20.016-001:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Supervisory Flow Control**  
- Approve/deny inquiries (IB instantiation)  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Mutate TP/MTP meaning state directly  
- Notes: Only at safe boundaries

**HLR-20.016-002:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Coherence & Stability**  
- Monitor ΔH% trajectory, detect drift/oscillation  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Perform meaning construction  
- Notes: Use explicit metrics

**HLR-20.016-003:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Arbitration**  
- Score and select between parallel candidate traces  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Make final meaning assertions  
- Notes: Must produce auditable rationale

**HLR-20.016-004:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Commitment Management**  
- Create, validate, decay commitments  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Override deterministic basin rules  
- Notes: Bounded depth

**HLR-20.016-005:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Learned Component Oversight**  
- Validate candidate primitives from learned modules  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Trust learned outputs without validation  
- Notes: Grammar + commitment check required

**HLR-20.016-006:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Cross-Basin Coordination**  
- Authorize and mediate basin transitions  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Directly execute basin-internal logic  
- Notes: Use explicit transition operators

**HLR-20.016-007:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Population Control**  
- Approve IB merge/split, OB decomposition  
- Must / May: **Must**  
- Must NOT: Perform merges/splits directly  
- Notes: Only at safe boundaries

**HLR-20.016-008:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Safety & Policy**  
- Enforce RB relationship safety policies  
- Must / May: **May**  
- Must NOT: Bypass RB safety checks  
- Notes: High priority

**HLR-20.016-009:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Human Oversight**  
- Request human approval for critical promotions  
- Must / May: **May**  
- Must NOT: Replace human judgment  
- Notes: Audit trail required

## 3. GB Limitations (Hard Constraints)

**HLR-20.016-010:** GB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction or primitive chaining.

**HLR-20.016-011:** GB SHALL NOT mutate core TS state directly (only through deterministic supervisory interface).

**HLR-20.016-012:** GB SHALL NOT make real-time decisions inside a TS execution cycle.

**HLR-20.016-013:** GB decisions SHALL remain auditable with fixed reason codes.

**HLR-20.016-014:** GB complexity SHALL be bounded (no unbounded recursion or search).

## 4. Responsibility Distribution Principle

**HLR-20.016-015:** Responsibilities SHALL be pushed downward wherever possible into:
- Basin-Local Grammar (BLG)
- Typed primitive operators
- ΔH% automatic regulation
- Deterministic basin rules

GB should act as **supervisor and arbitrator**, not primary executor.
