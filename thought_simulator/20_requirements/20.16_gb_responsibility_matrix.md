# 20.16 GB Responsibility Matrix

**Document ID:** 20.16  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-03  
**Status:** Draft - For CP Review  
**Source:** 20.10 Architectural Principles (Section 1.14) + Grok-CP Dialogue

## Scope
This document defines the clear boundaries, responsibilities, and limitations of the Global Brain (GB) supervisory layer. It operationalizes the GB Overload risk mitigation identified in 20.10.

## Non-Goals
- This document does not define GB algorithms or implementation details.
- It does not specify scoring formulas or execution logic (belongs in 30/40-series).
- It does not define basin-specific rules.

## 1. Purpose
To prevent GB from becoming a monolithic “god module” by explicitly defining:
- What GB **must** do
- What GB **must not** do
- How responsibilities are distributed

## 2. GB Responsibility Matrix

| Category                        | GB Responsibility                                      | Must / May | Must NOT                                      | Notes / Constraints |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|------------|-----------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Supervisory Flow Control**  | Approve/deny inquiries (IB instantiation)             | Must       | Mutate TP/MTP meaning state directly          | Only at safe boundaries |
| **Coherence & Stability**     | Monitor ΔH% trajectory, detect drift/oscillation      | Must       | Perform meaning construction                  | Use explicit metrics |
| **Arbitration**               | Score and select between parallel candidate traces    | Must       | Make final meaning assertions                 | Must produce auditable rationale |
| **Commitment Management**     | Create, validate, decay commitments                   | Must       | Override deterministic basin rules            | Bounded depth |
| **Learned Component Oversight**| Validate candidate primitives from learned modules    | Must       | Trust learned outputs without validation      | Grammar + commitment check required |
| **Cross-Basin Coordination**  | Authorize and mediate basin transitions               | Must       | Directly execute basin-internal logic         | Use explicit transition operators |
| **Population Control**        | Approve IB merge/split, OB decomposition              | Must       | Perform merges/splits directly                | Only at safe boundaries |
| **Safety & Policy**           | Enforce RB relationship safety policies               | May        | Bypass RB safety checks                       | High priority |
| **Human Oversight**           | Request human approval for critical promotions        | May        | Replace human judgment                        | Audit trail required |

## 3. GB Limitations (Hard Constraints)
1. GB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction or primitive chaining.
2. GB SHALL NOT mutate core TS state directly (only through deterministic supervisory interface).
3. GB SHALL NOT make real-time decisions inside a TS execution cycle.
4. GB decisions SHALL remain auditable with fixed reason codes.
5. GB complexity SHALL be bounded (no unbounded recursion or search).

## 4. Responsibility Distribution Principle
Wherever possible, responsibilities SHALL be pushed downward into:
- Basin-Local Grammar (BLG)
- Typed primitive operators
- ΔH% automatic regulation
- Deterministic basin rules

GB should act as **supervisor and arbitrator**, not primary executor.
