# **20.44.50 — ISc‑Semantic Requirements**  
**Subsystem:** TS  
**Primitive:** ISc‑Semantic (Semantic Projection Engine)  
**File:** `20.44.50_ts_isc_semantic.md`

---

# **1. Purpose**

The purpose of ISc‑Semantic is to provide a **deterministic, bounded, replay‑safe semantic projection mechanism** that proposes **candidate semantic repairs** when the system detects ambiguity, incompleteness, contradiction, referent uncertainty, or semantic drift within the Thought Packet (TP).

ISc‑Semantic does **not** decide meaning.  
It **proposes** meaning‑repair options.  
IB decides.

ISc‑Semantic is a **semantic projection engine**, not a scoring primitive.  
It is distinct from **20.44 ISc‑Scoring**, which evaluates finite candidate sets.

---

# **2. Scope**

ISc‑Semantic operates **inside Pipeline A**, after:

- IIInB  
- RB formation  
- OB stabilization  
- Detection of semantic issues  

ISc‑Semantic:

- receives a **semantic issue report** from Detection  
- generates **bounded semantic repair candidates**  
- projects each candidate into a **valid semantic_core state**  
- returns a **finite, deterministic set** of repair options to IB  

ISc‑Semantic **never**:

- mutates TP directly  
- writes to semantic_core  
- resolves meaning on its own  
- interacts with Pipeline B  
- performs scoring (that is 20.44)  

---

# **3. Definitions**

### **Semantic Issue**
A condition detected in TP that requires semantic repair, including:

- MI_VAGUE  
- MI_INCOMP  
- MI_CONTRA  
- MI_REF_UNRES  
- MI_DRIFT  

### **Semantic Projection**
A deterministic transformation from:

> **M₀ → $M_c$ → M′**

where:

- **M₀** = current semantic state  
- **$M_c$** = candidate projected state  
- **M′** = validated, envelope‑compliant candidate  

### **Repair Candidate**
A proposed semantic correction that:

- resolves the detected issue  
- preserves upstream constraints  
- respects envelope boundaries  
- is reversible and replay‑safe  

---

# **4. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **HLR-20.44.50.001 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL generate bounded semantic repair candidates**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL produce a **finite**, **deterministic**, **bounded** set of semantic repair candidates for each detected semantic issue.

The number of candidates SHALL be:

- ≥ 1  
- ≤ a system‑defined maximum  
- reproducible across identical inputs  

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.002 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT decide meaning**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT:

- select a final meaning  
- commit a semantic update  
- mutate semantic_core  
- override IB  

ISc‑Semantic only **proposes** candidates.  
IB decides.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.003 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL operate deterministically**

Given identical:

- TP  
- semantic_core  
- envelope state  
- detection report  

ISc‑Semantic SHALL produce identical candidate sets.

No randomness, sampling, or nondeterministic search is permitted.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.004 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL respect envelope constraints**

All candidate projections SHALL:

- comply with envelope rules  
- avoid violating long‑duration constraints  
- avoid introducing contradictions  
- maintain referential stability  

Candidates violating envelope constraints SHALL be discarded.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.005 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT mutate TP or semantic_core**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT:

- write to TP  
- write to semantic_core  
- write to MTP  
- write to any persistent store  

It operates on **copies** only.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.006 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL provide rationale for each candidate**

Each candidate SHALL include:

- the issue it resolves  
- the projection path (M₀ → $M_c$ → M′)  
- the envelope constraints applied  
- the justification for validity  

This rationale is required for IB decision‑making.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.007 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT expand the meaning space beyond envelope‑permitted regions**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT:

- invent new concepts  
- introduce new referents  
- hallucinate new entities  
- expand semantic_core beyond envelope bounds  

All projections must remain within the **existing semantic manifold**.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.008 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL handle referent resolution deterministically**

When resolving MI_REF_UNRES:

- referent candidates SHALL be finite  
- referent selection SHALL NOT occur  
- referent proposals SHALL be ranked only after scoring (20.44)  
- IB SHALL make the final decision  

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.009 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL be replay‑safe**

Given identical inputs, ISc‑Semantic SHALL produce:

- identical candidates  
- identical rationales  
- identical projection paths  

This ensures:

- auditability  
- reproducibility  
- deterministic inference  

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.010 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT interact with Pipeline B**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT:

- call Pipeline B  
- request external knowledge  
- perform retrieval  
- access coprocessors  

It operates **only** on:

- TP  
- semantic_core  
- envelope  
- detection report  

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.011 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL escalate unrepairable issues to IB**

If no valid candidate can be produced:

- ISc‑Semantic SHALL return an empty set  
- ISc‑Semantic SHALL provide a rationale  
- IB SHALL receive an escalation flag  

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT attempt fallback inference.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.012 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform scoring**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT:

- assign probabilities  
- compute confidence  
- compute entropy  
- rank candidates  

Scoring is exclusively handled by **20.44 ISc‑Scoring**.

---

# Section 5 — Forbidden Actions 

## **HLR‑20.44.50.013 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform intake repair**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform intake repair or any operation belonging to the IIInB domain.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.014 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform scoring**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform any scoring, ranking, probability assignment, or entropy computation.  
All scoring is exclusively handled by **20.44 ISc‑Scoring**.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.015 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT mutate semantic_core**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT write to, modify, or directly alter semantic_core under any circumstances.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.016 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT mutate TP**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT write to, modify, or directly alter any portion of the Thought Packet (TP).

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.017 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT generate new meaning outside envelope bounds**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT introduce new concepts, new referents, or any semantic content outside the envelope‑permitted meaning manifold.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.018 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT call UPI or IMR**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT invoke UPI, IMR, or any Pipeline B or external knowledge mechanism.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.019 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT block Pipeline A**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT introduce delays, waits, or blocking behavior in Pipeline A.  
It must complete deterministically within its bounded execution window.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.020 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT use nondeterministic search**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT use randomness, sampling, stochastic search, or any nondeterministic algorithm.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.021 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT perform generative inference**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL NOT generate new semantic structures, hypotheses, or meaning expansions beyond the bounded repair candidates required to resolve the detected issue.

---

# Section 6 — Outputs

## **HLR‑20.44.50.022 — ISc‑Semantic SHALL output a finite set of repair candidates**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL output a **finite**, **bounded**, and **deterministic** set of semantic repair candidates.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.023 — Each candidate SHALL include a projection path**

Each candidate output by ISc‑Semantic SHALL include a complete projection path:

> **M₀ → $M_c$ → M′**

sufficient for IB to evaluate the semantic transformation.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.024 — Each candidate SHALL include a rationale**

Each candidate SHALL include a rationale describing:

- the issue being repaired  
- the projection logic  
- the envelope constraints applied  
- the justification for validity  

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.025 — Each candidate SHALL be envelope‑validated**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL ensure that each candidate is fully validated against envelope constraints before output.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.026 — Each candidate SHALL be reversible**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL ensure that each candidate projection is reversible, enabling deterministic replay and auditability.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.027 — Each candidate SHALL be deterministic**

ISc‑Semantic SHALL ensure that each candidate is produced deterministically, with no stochastic variation across identical inputs.

---

## **HLR‑20.44.50.028 — ISc‑Semantic outputs SHALL be consumable by ISc‑Scoring and IB**

The output format SHALL be compatible with:

- **20.44 ISc‑Scoring** (for evaluation)  
- **20.90 IB** (for decision‑making)

---

# **7. Completion Criteria**

ISc‑Semantic is complete when:

- all detected semantic issues have been addressed  
- all candidates are envelope‑valid  
- all rationales are included  
- the candidate set is deterministic and bounded  
- IB has sufficient information to decide
