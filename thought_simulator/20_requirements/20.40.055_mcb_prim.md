# **20.40.055 — MCB‑prm (Meaning–Clarifying Bridge Primitive)**  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — Path A Meaning‑Layer Primitive  
**Purpose:** Relate meaning‑layer interpretation (IdOB output) to clarifying‑field statements (COB/CIL/CEx/CE/ISc/TPU output) without violating structural, routing, or commit‑layer boundaries.

---

## **1. Purpose**

MCB‑prm is the **Meaning–Clarifying Bridge primitive** in the OB‑family.  
It operates **after IdOB‑prm** and **before RBU‑prm**, and its role is to:

- relate meaning‑layer interpretation to clarifying‑field statements,  
- detect clarifying–meaning reinforcement or conflict,  
- produce meaning‑layer deltas only,  
- prepare the TP for RBU commit,  
- preserve strict separation between clarifying‑fields and meaning‑fields.

MCB‑prm performs **no structural extraction**, **no routing**, **no commit**, and **no clarifying‑field mutation**.  
It is a pure meaning‑layer refinement primitive.

---

## **2. Position in Path A**

MCB‑prm appears in the meaning‑layer loop:

```
RB → RTU → IdOB → MCB → RBU → CTP → RB → …
```

It executes:

- **after IdOB‑prm** has produced meaning‑layer semantics,  
- **before RBU‑prm** commits meaning into SSR,  
- **after clarifying‑fields have been finalized by TPU‑prm**,  
- **before OuBA‑prm** freezes semantic_core.

MCB‑prm is the **only primitive** in Path A permitted to read both:

- meaning‑layer fields (IdOB output), and  
- clarifying‑fields (COB/CIL/CEx/CE/ISc/TPU output).

---

## **3. Normative Requirements (SHALL statements)**

### **3.1 Nature of MCB‑prm**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑001** — MCB‑prm SHALL be an OB‑family meaning‑layer primitive that relates meaning‑layer interpretation to clarifying‑field statements.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑002** — MCB‑prm SHALL operate only after IdOB‑prm has produced meaning‑layer semantics.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑003** — MCB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑layer deltas without altering structural geometry or routing metadata.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑004** — MCB‑prm SHALL be serially chainable when additional clarifying‑meaning refinement is required.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑005** — MCB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose clarifying‑fields have been finalized by TPU‑prm.

---

### **3.2 What MCB‑prm is not**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑006** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify clarifying‑fields produced by COB, CIL, CEx, CE, ISc, or TPU.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑007** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT read or modify routing vectors, routing deltas, or TP.TR.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑008** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT trigger SSG, STPX, or any structural OB‑family primitive.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑009** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT participate in Path B realization or expression‑layer processing.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑010** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by SSG, RB‑prm, or RTU‑prm.

---

### **3.3 Invocation and position in the lineup**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑011** — MCB‑prm SHALL be invocable only after a routing cycle `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm → IdOB‑prm` has completed.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑012** — MCB‑prm SHALL be scheduled by TS when clarifying‑meaning reconciliation is required before RBU‑prm commit.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑013** — MCB‑prm SHALL execute after IdOB‑prm and before RBU‑prm in all Path A cycles.

---

### **3.4 Clarifying–Meaning Reconciliation**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑014** — MCB‑prm SHALL read clarifying‑fields in a strictly read‑only manner.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑015** — MCB‑prm SHALL detect clarifying–meaning reinforcement and produce positive meaning‑layer deltas.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑016** — MCB‑prm SHALL detect clarifying–meaning conflict and produce corrective meaning‑layer deltas.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑017** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify clarifying‑fields to resolve conflict; only meaning‑layer deltas are permitted.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑018** — MCB‑prm SHALL update `TP.semantic.meaning_semantics[]` with clarifying‑aligned meaning cues required by RBU‑prm and TR.

---

### **3.5 Meaning ΔH% and semantics tagging**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑019** — MCB‑prm SHALL compute and write a meaning‑delta field `TP.semantic.mcb_delta_h` representing the magnitude of clarifying‑meaning refinement.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑020** — MCB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.semantic.mcb_semantics[]` with clarifying‑aligned meaning semantics it resolved.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑021** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by structural or routing primitives.

---

### **3.6 Completion and next‑OB signaling**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑022** — MCB‑prm SHALL set `TP.mcb_complete = true` when clarifying‑meaning refinement is complete.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑023** — MCB‑prm SHALL set `TP.mcb_complete = false` when additional MCB‑prm passes are required.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑024** — MCB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.mcb_next_ob_candidates[]` with OB‑family primitives that may be required for further meaning‑layer refinement.

---

### **3.7 TP update request**

**HLR‑20.40.055‑025** — MCB‑prm SHALL issue a meaning‑layer TP update request `TPU.mcb_update` containing only:  
`mcb_delta_h`, `mcb_semantics[]`, `meaning_semantics[]`, `mcb_complete`, `mcb_next_ob_candidates[]`.  
**HLR‑20.40.055‑026** — MCB‑prm SHALL NOT request routing updates, structural updates, or TR updates.

---

## **4. Interfaces (Informative)**

### **Upstream**
MCB‑prm is scheduled by TS after IdOB‑prm completes and clarifying‑fields have been finalized by TPU‑prm.

### **Downstream**
After MCB‑prm completes and `TPU.mcb_update` is committed:

- RBU‑prm consumes MCB meaning‑layer deltas,  
- RB‑prm uses committed meaning to determine next routing cycle,  
- TR may use clarifying‑aligned meaning semantics indirectly via RBU commit.

### **Routing**
MCB‑prm does not read or modify routing vectors or routing deltas.

### **Truth and Path B**
MCB‑prm prepares meaning for RBU commit; it does not initiate Path B.

---

## **5. Summary**

MCB‑prm is a **meaning‑layer refinement primitive** that:

- relates meaning to clarifying‑fields,  
- detects reinforcement or conflict,  
- produces meaning‑layer deltas only,  
- preserves clarifying‑field immutability,  
- prepares TP for RBU commit,  
- fits cleanly between IdOB and RBU in Path A.

It does **not** perform structural extraction, routing, commit, or clarifying‑field mutation.
