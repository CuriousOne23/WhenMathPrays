# TP Metadata Fields — Provenance, Structure, and Upstream Annotation

This document defines the metadata fields carried by the Thought Packet (TP) within Path A. Metadata fields provide upstream-observed information that downstream primitives use for deterministic meaning refinement, routing, structural interpretation, and truth-relation mapping.

This document is a normative extension of 20.105_tp_requirements.md.

## Scope

This specification covers metadata field definitions, provenance rules, commit boundaries, immutability constraints, upstream annotation responsibilities, and downstream consumption expectations.

It does not define primitive behavior, defect classification logic, or meaning-refinement rules. Those remain in their respective primitive specifications.

## TP Metadata Field Categories

TP metadata fields are grouped into the following categories:

- TP.metadata.intake_metadata — observations from InB and IIInB
- TP.metadata.normalization_metadata — observations and repairs from IE
- TP.metadata.structural_metadata — structural geometry annotations from OB-Set
- TP.metadata.routing_metadata — routing arbitration and pathway information
- TP.metadata.expressive_metadata — expressive or stylistic observations
- TP.metadata.repair_metadata — deterministic repair provenance
- TP.metadata.provenance_metadata — commit lineage and replay-safety fields
- TP.metadata.entropy_metadata — ΔH% trajectory fields
- TP.metadata.continuity_metadata — identity and conversation-layer anchors
- TP.metadata.msl_metadata — Meaning Signal Layer tokens (new)

Each category contains one or more fields defined below.

## Normative Requirements

The TP shall contain a metadata section composed of zero or more metadata fields defined in this document. (HLR-20.105.010-001)

Metadata fields shall be immutable once committed by TPU, except where a primitive is explicitly authorized to update a specific field. (HLR-20.105.010-002)

Each metadata field shall record its provenance, identifying the primitive that produced or last updated the field. (HLR-20.105.010-003)

All metadata fields shall be accessible to downstream primitives without requiring re-inspection or re-derivation of upstream observations. (HLR-20.105.010-004)

Metadata fields shall preserve their values across TPU commits unless explicitly replaced by a new commit. (HLR-20.105.010-005)

Metadata fields shall support deterministic replay, meaning that re-executing the same TP through the same primitives yields identical metadata values. (HLR-20.105.010-006)

## Clarifying Metadata Requirements

The TP shall include clarifying-field metadata consisting of clarifying fields, clarifying subfields, and up to four levels of hierarchical clarifying sub-subfields. (HLR-20.105.010-010)

Clarifying metadata shall be bounded to a maximum of 10 clarifying fields, 100 total subfields, and 4 hierarchical levels of sub-subfields. (HLR-20.105.010-011)

Clarifying metadata shall include an importance score for each clarifying field and subfield. (HLR-20.105.010-012)

Clarifying metadata shall encode the hierarchical topology of clarifying fields, subfields, and sub-subfields to support deterministic replay and continuity analysis. (HLR-20.105.010-013)

Clarifying metadata shall record provenance identifying the primitive that produced or last updated each clarifying field or subfield. (HLR-20.105.010-014)

Clarifying metadata shall be immutable once committed by TPU, except where COB or CIL are explicitly authorized to update clarifying importance or topology. (HLR-20.105.010-015)

Clarifying metadata shall support deterministic replay such that identical sequences of OuBA packets, CST signals, and COB/CIL operations produce identical clarifying metadata. (HLR-20.105.010-016)

Clarifying metadata shall preserve continuity across freeze/thaw cycles, merge/split events, and identity-layer evolution without loss or mutation. (HLR-20.105.010-017)
