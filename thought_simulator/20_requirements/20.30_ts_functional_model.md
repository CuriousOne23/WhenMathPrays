# 20.30 TS Functional Model

## Purpose
Formalizes the end-to-end TS functional model derived from the current pre-refactor 20.03 source-of-truth document.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.030-001: TS SHALL implement deterministic routing, splitting, OB processing, TB interpretation, merging, truth evaluation, and done evaluation.
2. HLR-20.030-002: MTP updates SHALL be deterministic and field-explicit.
3. HLR-20.030-003: DeltaHPercent accounting SHALL preserve contribution and missing-mass semantics.
4. HLR-20.030-004: Response generation SHALL consume MTP and seed deterministically.
5. HLR-20.030-005: Seed input SHALL NOT influence meaning construction stages.

## Program Flow Integration
Per-tick lifecycle SHALL include: scheduling, creation/injection, entry checks, processing, routing/transitions, regulation, completion, logging, snapshot/checkpoint, and housekeeping.

## Deterministic Handling of Noisy or Ill-Formed Input
1. HLR-20.030-006: TS SHALL accept malformed, misspelled, grammatically broken, or otherwise ill-formed input without rejecting the turn solely due to surface-form defects.
2. HLR-20.030-007: InB SHALL deterministically map noisy surface forms to stable conceptual basin inputs using rule-bounded normalization and SHALL NOT use probabilistic inference for this mapping.
3. HLR-20.030-008: OB activation SHALL be concept-based and spelling-independent for semantically equivalent noisy variants under identical normalization inputs.
4. HLR-20.030-009: TB SHALL reconstruct intended structural interpretation from degraded syntax using deterministic interpretation rules bounded by available evidence.
5. HLR-20.030-010: RB SHALL stabilize meaning transitions under noisy input and SHALL NOT hallucinate corrections, infer unstated facts, or invent content.
6. HLR-20.030-011: DeltaHPercent (ΔH%) updates SHALL remain bounded, deterministic, and replayable under noisy or ill-formed input conditions.
7. HLR-20.030-012: End-to-end meaning construction SHALL remain stable, reproducible, and auditable despite surface-form degradation.
