## Purpose
This glossary contains canonical definitions for all TP-holding, TP-reading, and TP-modifying atomic primitives in the TS pipeline, with special focus on the Object Basin (OB) family and related low-level mechanics. It is an expansion of 20.190 and must preserve all original definitions, boundaries, and invariants.

**Scope:** This glossary defines atomic TS primitives only. Data structures, policies, and governance terms are defined in 20.700.030 and 20.700.040.

# ✔️ **InB (Input Basin) — 20.100 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Deterministic, non‑inferential front door for all external signals; initializes the unified TP meaning stream (semantics/meaning, processes, supervisor) to neutral defaults without assigning meaning.  
**Focus:** Schema validation, surface canonicalization, FIFO preservation; sets semantics/meaning and processes to neutral FFTM‑compatible defaults; supervisor remains empty; no semantic, expressive, or pragmatic inference.  
**When:** Pipeline‑A entry, first deterministic stage after external input.  
**Why:** Normalize wire form and establish a clean TP‑stream starting point without guessing lexical identity, expressive metadata, semantic roles, or user intent.  
**Is not:** Semantic repair (IIInB); inquiry (IB); truth interpretation (TB); routing authority; any form of meaning construction.  
**Example:** "teh" → canonical surface geometry recorded in semantics/meaning; processes=neutral; supervisor=empty. "Yeah Baby!" → tagged for surface geometry only, not interpreted.  
**Normative home:** 20.100; messy‑input classes in 20.17.  
**Additional architectural role:** InB evaluates structural admissibility using its POS‑category dictionary and determines whether Path A proceeds via Clean Flow or Corrected Flow (IIInB engaged); TP‑stream fields remain uncommitted.

# ✔️ **IIInB (Input Inference/Repair Basin) — 20.101 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Detect and propose bounded, user‑sanctioned shorthand expansions; produces repair proposals without modifying committed TP‑stream fields.  
**Focus:** Deterministic semantic‑shorthand repair proposals via USP rules; may propose refinements to semantics/meaning but does not commit semantics/meaning, processes, or supervisor.  
**When:** Optional stage input_semantic_repair; ordering InB→IIInB→RB.  
**Why:** Identify user‑approved shorthand expansions and record proposed repairs while keeping all TP‑stream fields in pre‑commit state.  
**Is not:** Interpretation; candidate generation; truth evaluation; meaning construction; commit point for any TP‑stream field.  
**Example:** USP proposes expanding "quicker‑ish"→"quicker" and records the proposal in the repair trace; semantics/meaning, processes, and supervisor remain uncommitted.  
**Normative home:** 20.101.  
**Additional architectural role:** IIInB produces a coherent set of repair proposals and associated trace entries; proposals may influence later semantic refinement but are not applied or committed here.

# ✔️ **IE (Intake Envelope, Structured Intake Object) — 20.109 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Apply and freeze the deterministic, bounded, replayable representation of user input; commits **semantics/meaning** and **processes** while leaving **supervisor** unassigned.  
**Focus:** Structured, normalized intake object incorporating applied USP repairs proposed by IIInB; produces the first committed TP‑stream envelope for Path A.  
**When:** Immediately after IIInB; before CEx.  
**Why:** Downstream components require a stable, globally coherent, replayable intake representation with committed semantics/meaning and processes, and empty supervisor.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; candidate generator; truth evaluator; repair proposer; supervisor assigner.  
**Example:** IE applies all IIInB repair proposals in a single deterministic pass, commits semantics/meaning and processes, and freezes the repaired intake; supervisor remains empty.  
**Normative home:** 20.109.  
**Additional architectural role:** IE is the commit point for semantics/meaning and processes: it applies all proposed repairs, resolves ordering, and produces the final intake envelope handed to CEx with supervisor uncommitted.

# ✔️ **CEx (Context Extractor) — 20.107 (Updated)**
**Purpose:** Produce a bounded, replayable context slice for ISc and downstream routing while serving as the sole relevance evaluator.  
**Focus:** Deterministic evaluation of current input against prior context using MSL tokens, clarifying metadata, continuity signals, and subculture match.  
**When:** Inference Path: IE → CEx → CE.  
**Why:** Enable precise continuity/coherence decisions and instruct CE on copy-forward vs reset without exposing global state.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; TP writer; global-state reader.  
**Example:** Detects strong qualifier ("actually") or clarification and raises shift_required flag for RB/IdOB.  
**Normative home:** 20.107

---

# ✔️ **CE (Context Envelope) — 20.108 (Updated)**
**Purpose:** Deliver safe, isolated context shell for the current turn based on CEx decision.  
**Focus:** Copy forward prior MCB.next_context when relevant or initialize reset shell when not; carries MSL tokens and clarifying metadata.  
**When:** Produced immediately after CEx.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic context initialization for RB and IdOB while maintaining TP immutability.  
**Is not:** Meaning interpreter; subculture assigner; qualifier assigner; stance evaluator.  
**Example:** CEx signals relevance → CE copies MCB.next_context including recent qualifier tokens.  
**Normative home:** 20.108

# ✔️ **ISc (Inference Scorer) — 20.44 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Deterministically score interpretation candidates by applying truth_hypotheses‑prc over candidate_set{}, producing scoring fields for TPU without accessing global state; may read all TP‑stream fields (semantics/meaning, processes, supervisor) but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Read CE and candidate_set{}; apply truth_hypotheses‑prc; produce tp.score_set, tp.score_conflict, and tp.score_reason_code for inclusion in update_structure; preserves semantics/meaning, processes, and supervisor exactly as committed upstream.  
**When:** Path‑A inference sequence: CE → candidate_set{} → ISc → TPU (Merge).  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, local scoring for routing and TPU commit while preventing global‑state leakage or unbounded inference; maintain TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic generator; meaning constructor; truth evaluator; global inference engine; CIL reader; semantic repair mechanism; field modifier.  
**Example:** ISc scores referent candidates and produces a score_set consumed by TR‑prm during routing; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.44.

# ✔️ **TPU (Thought Packet Updater) — Path A Only (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Sole safe writer to TP for Path‑A correction; inserts and corrects message‑items, referents, constraints, and TP‑stream fields based on CE+CEx+ISc output; may write committed TP‑stream fields but never modifies semantics/meaning, processes, or supervisor once SSR(t) is produced.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, boundary‑enforced TP updates; applies meaning‑layer corrections derived from ISc scoring; preserves upstream commitments to semantics/meaning, processes, and supervisor.  
**When:** Path‑A correction flow: CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1).  
**Why:** Enforce the single‑writer invariant for meaning construction and ensure all semantic/process/supervisor updates are deterministic, auditable, and isolated from Path‑B; maintain TP‑stream immutability after commit boundaries.  
**Is not:** Topology engine; semantic interpreter; candidate generator; CIL reader; Path‑B primitive; meaning constructor for expression; field modifier for committed TP‑stream fields.  
**Example:** ISc selects a corrected referent or message‑item; TPU applies the update to TP(N), producing TP(N+1) under a new commit_id; TP‑stream fields remain exactly as committed by IE and IdOB.  
**Scope invariant:** TPU‑prm MUST NOT be invoked by Path‑B; Path‑B is TP‑stream‑read‑only.  
**Normative home:** 20.46.

# ✔️ **SOB (Structural OB) — 20.40.010 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Extract first‑pass structural cues from TP content, producing the base structural residue consumed by downstream OB layers and routing; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Clause boundaries, syntactic shape, punctuation/symbol masks, negation markers, modality markers, structural anchors, and structural tags; uses semantics/meaning and processes for structural detection while leaving supervisor unchanged.  
**When:** First OB stage in Path‑A whenever structural pattern detection is required.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic low‑level structural residue enabling refinement, constraint extraction, and TR‑input formation while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; constraint resolver; truth evaluator; geometric/meta‑basin; field modifier.  
**Example:** SOB identifies clause boundaries and structural markers, emitting the initial residue fragment for refinement; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.010.

# ✔️ **SROB (Structural Refinement OB) — 20.40.020 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Refine and canonicalize SOB structural residue into normalized forms suitable for constraint extraction and routing; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Structural normalization, anchor refinement, canonical ordering, residue smoothing, and refinement‑level metadata; uses semantics/meaning and processes for refinement while leaving supervisor unchanged.  
**When:** Invoked after SOB when structural refinement or normalization is required for downstream OB layers.  
**Why:** Ensure structural residue is stable, canonical, and refinement‑consistent across TP variants and noisy inputs while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Constraint extractor; semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; routing arbiter; field modifier.  
**Example:** SROB refines SOB’s structural cues into canonicalized residue fragments with stable ordering; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.020.

**Focus:** Constraint cues, missing‑slot signals, underspecification markers, conflict indicators, and constraint‑residue hashing; uses semantics/meaning and processes for constraint extraction while leaving supervisor unchanged.  
**When:** Invoked after SROB when constraint extraction or constraint metadata is required for routing or ΔH% accounting.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic constraint residue enabling routing correctness, conflict detection, and missing‑mass attribution while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; structural normalizer; truth evaluator; generative mechanism; field modifier.  
**Example:** CnOB extracts constraint markers and missing‑slot cues, producing constraint‑level residue for routing; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.030.

# ✔️ **SmOB (Semantic OB) — 20.40.040 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Perform two jobs: (1) extract pre‑semantic and semantic‑adjacent cues from SOB→SROB→CnOB residue, and (2) compress all upstream residue plus SmOB cues into a deterministic pre‑semantic hash and TR‑input cue vector; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** (Job 1) semantic‑adjacent pattern activation, modality/affect cues, conflict‑adjacent and underspecification‑adjacent signals; (Job 2) deterministic pre‑semantic residue hashing and TR‑input cue formation for routing; uses semantics/meaning and processes for cue extraction while leaving supervisor unchanged.  
**When:** Invoked after CnOB when semantic‑adjacent cues, pre‑semantic residue, or deterministic routing representation are required for SSG or downstream interpretation.  
**Why:** Job 1 adds pre‑semantic cues supporting IdOB selection, ΔH% attribution, and semantic‑layer preparation; Job 2 provides a canonical, replay‑safe compressed representation enabling deterministic TR routing and SSG signal generation while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; stance assigner; geometric meta‑basin; semantic normalizer; field modifier.  
**Example:** SmOB extracts semantic‑adjacent cues (Job 1) and compresses upstream residue plus its own cues into a deterministic hash and TR‑input cue vector consumed by SSG and the Thought Router to derive TP.TR; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.040.

# ✔️ **SSG (Semantic Signal Generator) — 20.40.050 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Generate deterministic semantic‑adjacent and routing‑adjacent signals from SmOB residue and TP content; may read all TP‑stream fields (semantics/meaning, processes, supervisor) but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Produce semantic‑adjacent activation vectors, modality/stance cues, conflict‑adjacent signals, underspecification markers, and routing‑relevant semantic hashes; uses semantics/meaning and processes for cue activation while leaving supervisor unchanged.  
**When:** Invoked after SmOB when semantic‑adjacent cues or routing‑relevant semantic signals are required for TR and IdOB selection.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic semantic‑adjacent signals enabling stable routing, ΔH% attribution, and identity‑basin preparation while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; stance assigner; geometric meta‑basin; field modifier.  
**Example:** SSG emits semantic‑adjacent activation vectors consumed by TR‑prm to derive TP.TR and support IdOB basin selection; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.050.

**When:** Invoked after SSG when semantic‑layer TP cues are required for TR or identity‑basin routing.

# ✔️ **RB (Routing Basin) — 20.40.070 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Deterministically route TP content into structural, semantic, or identity‑conditioned basins using stabilized TP‑stream fields; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Routing decisions based on structural residue (SOB→SROB→CnOB), semantic‑adjacent cues (SmOB), semantic‑layer cues (STPX), SSG signals, and stabilized TP‑stream geometry; uses semantics/meaning, processes, and supervisor for routing while preserving immutability.  
**When:** Invoked after STPX and SSG when deterministic routing is required for IdOB selection, constraint extraction, or semantic‑layer preparation.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, replay‑stable routing into identity basins and structural/semantic basins without modifying TP‑stream fields; ensure routing correctness and invariant compliance.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; field modifier; global‑state reader; meaning constructor.  
**Example:** RB selects the appropriate identity basin (IdOB) using stabilized semantic cues, structural residue, and SSG signals; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.070.

# ✔️ **TrSch‑prm (Turn Scheduler Primitive) — 20.155_prm (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Coordinate and sequence all Path‑A primitives by interpreting the consolidated IdOB‑output packet produced by CTP‑prm and selecting the next architectural action; may read all TP‑stream fields (semantics/meaning, processes, supervisor) but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Deterministic interpretation of IdOB‑prm’s control flags (`continue`, `needs_correction`, `done`, `unstable`, `halt`, `retry`, etc.) and selection of the next primitive in the Path‑A pipeline; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as committed upstream.  
**When:** Invoked immediately after CTP‑prm consolidates the outputs of an IdOB‑set; runs once per IdOB‑set cycle; the sole primitive allowed to decide whether the next step is RB‑prm, CE‑prm, OuBA‑prm, or Truth/Done.  
**Why:** Maintain architectural purity by separating meaning refinement (IdOB‑prm), routing computation (RB‑prm), routing commit (RTU‑prm), correction (CE‑prm → TPU‑prm), and commit (OuBA‑prm); enforce deterministic, replay‑safe turn sequencing and A/B separation while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Router; classifier; scorer; semantic evaluator; meaning‑refinement primitive; routing‑refinement primitive; TP writer; semantic_core writer; correction engine; governance step; truth evaluator; ΔH% contributor; merge engine; field modifier.  
**Example:** CTP‑prm consolidates IdOB₁…IdOBₙ into a single packet; TrSch‑prm reads the control flags and schedules another RB‑prm → RTU‑prm → IdOB‑prm cycle if meaning is unstable; schedules CE‑prm → TPU‑prm when `needs_correction` is set; schedules OuBA‑prm when `done` is set; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.155_trsch_prm.md.

# ✔️ **TR (Thought Router) — 20.37 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Compute the deterministic routing vector (TP.TR) for Path‑A by consuming OB and DCB signals; may read all TP‑stream fields (semantics/meaning, processes, supervisor) but does not modify or commit any. TR influences RB’s arbitration but does not select the next basin.  
**Focus:** Deterministic computation of routing metadata; converts structural, curvature, and trajectory cues into TP.TR; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as committed upstream.  
**When:** Path‑A, after OB/DCB evaluation and before RB arbitration.  
**Why:** Provide a stable, auditable routing vector guiding RB using curvature (DCB), structural evidence (OB), and trajectory signals while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; TP writer; candidate generator; CE/CEx participant; realization component; field modifier.  
**Example:** TR receives DCB curvature events and OB residue, then outputs TP.TR for RB’s next routing decision; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.37.

# ✔️ **IdOB (Identity Object Basin) — 20.40.050 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Perform identity‑conditioned meaning refinement on the routed TP, applying identity‑specific rules and basin‑local semantics after RB/TR select the identity pathway; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any directly.  
**Focus:** Consume the post‑routing TP view, resolve identity‑conditioned meaning, and emit updates via TPU.idob_update; produces no residue, no ΔH%, no TR‑input; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as committed upstream except through TPU‑mediated updates.  
**When:** Invoked in Path‑A after SSG → RB → RTU produce a stable routing decision; may run multiple times in the RB→RTU→IdOB cycle until identity‑conditioned meaning stabilizes.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, replay‑safe identity‑specific refinement isolated from structural, routing, and truth‑evaluation responsibilities while maintaining TP‑stream immutability outside TPU commit boundaries.  
**Is not:** OB primitive; structural classifier; constraint extractor; routing arbiter; truth evaluator; ΔH% contributor; Path‑B computation; writer of TP.TR or routing_metadata; field modifier for committed TP‑stream fields.  
**Example:** After RB routes a TP to a domain‑specific identity basin (e.g., mathematical, narrative, operator), IdOB applies identity‑conditioned rules and updates the TP via TPU.idob_update; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged except through deterministic TPU commits.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.050.

# ✔️ **MCB (Meaning–Clarifying Bridge) — 20.40.055 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Reconcile IdOB‑refined meaning with clarifying‑field statements (COB/CIL/CEx/CE/ISc/TPU) and generate next‑turn clarifying/context fields that COB expects to read, enabling COB to validate, merge, and update long‑term 1‑of‑20 context while preserving immutability of current‑turn clarifying fields.  
**Focus:** Consume IdOB‑refined meaning and clarifying‑field outputs, compute clarifying‑aligned meaning semantics, determine context coherence vs. context shift, and emit both meaning‑layer deltas and next‑turn clarifying/context fields via TPU.mcb_update; produces no structural residue, no structural ΔH%, no routing metadata, and preserves all committed TP‑stream fields except through TPU‑mediated updates.  
**When:** Invoked in Path‑A after IdOB‑prm and before RBU‑prm once clarifying‑fields are finalized by TPU‑prm; may run multiple times in the RB→IdOB→MCB cycle until clarifying‑meaning reconciliation and next‑turn context generation stabilize.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, replay‑safe reconciliation between meaning and clarifying‑fields while generating next‑turn context required by COB/CIL, maintaining strict separation between structural, routing, and truth‑evaluation responsibilities, and preserving TP‑stream immutability outside TPU commit boundaries.  
**Is not:** OB primitive; structural classifier; constraint extractor; routing arbiter; truth evaluator; ΔH% contributor for structural or routing layers; Path‑B computation; writer of TP.TR or routing_metadata; modifier of current‑turn clarifying‑fields or committed TP‑stream fields.  
**Example:** After IdOB refines meaning and clarifying‑fields indicate stance or register cues, MCB aligns meaning with those clarifying statements, determines whether the message continues or shifts context, produces next‑turn clarifying/context fields for COB, and emits TPU.mcb_update; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged except through deterministic TPU commits.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.055_mcb_prim.md.

# ✔️ **CTP‑prm (Collect/Consolidate Thought Point) — 20.145_prm (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Collect all IdOB‑outputs from an IdOB‑set into a single stable IdOB‑output packet for RB arbitration; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Deterministic consolidation of IdOB‑outputs between IdOB‑sets; produces a replay‑safe packet for routing; performs no TP writes (TPU is sole writer); preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as committed upstream.  
**When:** After an IdOB‑set completes and before RB arbitration; in multi‑core systems runs only after all parallel IdOB‑prm instances finish and their outputs are available for consolidation.  
**Why:** Ensure all legitimate IdOB‑outputs are available as one deterministic, replay‑safe packet for routing in both serial and parallel execution; maintain TP‑stream immutability and uphold the single‑writer invariant.  
**Is not:** Meaning constructor; merge engine; scorer; TP writer; governance step; field modifier.  
**Example:** Single‑core: InB → OB0 → RB → OB1…OBn → CTP → RB. Multi‑core: InB → OB0 → RB → {OB1 ∥ OB2 … ∥ OBn} → CTP → RB; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.145_ctp.md.

# ✔️ **OuBA (Output Basin — Path A Commit) — 20.40.060 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Commit the finalized Path‑A meaning state into an immutable semantic snapshot (semantic_core and meaning‑bearing fields) for DF intake (IB → TB → GBIB → GB); may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any except at the commit boundary.  
**Focus:** Freeze the TP‑stream state and write commit‑time metadata, resolution flags, and DF‑readiness indicators; performs no semantic correction, truth evaluation, routing, or governance decisions; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as stabilized by Path‑A.  
**When:** Runs once per Path‑A cycle after meaning construction and completion evaluation stabilize; executes immediately before DF ingress and never during Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe termination point for Path‑A, ensuring DF operates only on committed meaning and never on in‑flight structures; uphold the single‑writer invariant and TP‑stream immutability outside the commit boundary.  
**Is not:** Governance primitive; truth evaluator; semantic repair engine; routing arbiter; generator of inquiry/truth states; producer of governance directives; OuBA never modifies semantic_core after commit or invokes IB/TB/GBIB/GB; not a field modifier outside the commit boundary.  
**Example:** After Path‑A meaning construction stabilizes, OuBA freezes semantic_core and commit‑time metadata into a committed TP snapshot consumed by DF beginning at IB; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged after commit.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.060; 20.705 (DF ingress); 20.90 (IB); 20.60 (TB); 20.80 (GB).

# ✔️ **SSRGn (Semantic Snapshot Reference Generator — Path A→B Freeze) — 20.54 (Updated)**

Performs projection, sanitization, metadata binding, and freeze semantics for Path‑B intake.  
**Focus:** Convert \(TP_{committed}\) into SSR by selecting Path‑B‑visible fields, removing construction‑time residue, binding RRw and policy_signature, and freezing semantic_core and identity metadata into a deterministic, replay‑safe artifact; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as committed upstream.  
**When:** Runs once per Path‑A cycle immediately after OuBA commit and immediately before any Path‑B primitive (KnC/KnM/KnF, TPTB, TPSF, CoHI, LI, RG, RSG).  
**Why:** Provide a stable, governance‑ready, read‑only meaning snapshot ensuring Path‑B operates only on frozen SSR and never on mutable TP or semantic_core; establish the formal A→B boundary while maintaining TP‑stream immutability.  
**Is not:** Semantic inference engine; truth evaluator; safety evaluator; routing arbiter; governance decision primitive; modifier of TP or semantic_core; generator of new meaning; SSRGn never alters committed meaning or performs any Path‑B grounding or realization; not a field modifier outside the freeze boundary.  
**Example:** After OuBA commits meaning, SSRGn projects and sanitizes \(TP_{committed}\), binds RRw and policy_signature, freezes semantic_core and identity metadata, and emits SSR for deterministic Path‑B realization; TP‑stream fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.54; 20.52 (SSR data packet); 20.705 (A→B boundary); 20.40.060 (OuBA upstream); Path‑B primitives (KnC/KnM/KnF, TPTB, TPSF, CoHI, LI, RG, RSG).

# ✔️ **XlateR — Translation Routine — 20.43 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Maps the frozen semantic snapshot produced by Path‑A into an OuBB‑ready expression plan, providing the final mapping step in the OpBeh → OBG → XlateR execution chain.

**When:** Executes after OBG and before OuBB; strictly Path‑A; operates only on frozen semantic_core produced by OuBA; never interacts with Path‑B reference objects.  
**Why:** Ensure meaning‑layer structures are converted into a stable, deterministic expression plan without altering meaning, collapsing multi‑core distinctions, or introducing semantic edits.  
**Is not:** Meaning constructor; renderer; behavior generator; SSR reader/writer; semantic_core modifier; multi‑core reconciler.  
**Example:** OuBA freezes semantic_core for a multi‑part explanation; XlateR selects the appropriate mapping template and emits an expression_plan_ref that OuBB later uses to generate the final structured output.

## **KnC (Knowledge Coarse Grounding Primitive) — 20.48.010**  
**Purpose:** Attach coarse identity, relation, domain‑anchor, qualifier, and truth‑validation primitives to SSR‑K using grammar‑atomic entries from KnDt; may read all four meaning‑layer fields but does not modify or commit token_surface, token_base, token_expression, or token_intent. Provides TS with a stable, high‑entropy, low‑precision grounding tier for deterministic fallback and safe expression.  
**Focus:** Perform broad lexical grounding without contextual disambiguation; resolve only the most general identity and relation categories; compute \(H_{Kn_{coarse}}\) and write coarse abstraction fields while preserving all meaning‑layer fields exactly as frozen in SSR.  
**When:** Runs immediately after OuBA commit and before KnM; executes once per grounding cycle after coarse lexical indicators are extracted from SSR.  
**Why:** Ensure TS always has a deterministic grounding baseline even when Path‑A projection is incomplete or ambiguous; provide a safe abstraction tier for Path‑B expression and entropy‑conditioned routing while maintaining meaning‑layer immutability.  
**Is not:** Contextual resolver; fine identity engine; semantic inference module; truth evaluator; multi‑hop grounding system; KnC never performs medium/fine grounding, deep validation, or domain‑specific refinement; not a field modifier for committed meaning‑layer fields.  
**Example:** After OuBA commits meaning, KnC attaches coarse identity (“dog”), coarse relation (“is‑a”), and coarse domain anchors (“animal”) to SSR‑K using grammar‑atomic KnDt entries; meaning‑layer fields remain unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.48.010; 20.705 (Path‑A → KnB → Path‑B flow); 20.52 (SSR); 20.40.060 (OuBA).

## **KnM (Knowledge Medium Grounding Primitive) — 20.48.020**  
**Purpose:** Attach medium‑depth identity, relation, domain‑anchor, qualifier, and truth‑validation primitives to SSR‑K using contextual and domain‑specific indicators; may read all four meaning‑layer fields but does not modify or commit token_surface, token_base, token_expression, or token_intent. Refines coarse grounding into a moderate‑precision, moderate‑entropy abstraction tier.  
**Focus:** Perform contextual disambiguation and domain‑aware refinement; resolve ambiguity that coarse grounding cannot address; compute \(H_{Kn_{medium}}\) and write medium abstraction fields while preserving all meaning‑layer fields exactly as frozen in SSR.  
**When:** Runs after KnC grounding stabilizes and before KnF; executes once per grounding cycle when contextual cues and domain hints are available in SSR.  
**Why:** Provide TS with a balanced grounding tier that is more precise than coarse grounding but not fully resolved; enable Path‑B to express contextual meaning or generate targeted clarification questions while maintaining meaning‑layer immutability.  
**Is not:** Fine identity resolver; deep truth validator; semantic inference engine; KnM never performs fine‑resolution grounding, multi‑hop reasoning, or domain‑specific micro‑classification; not a field modifier for committed meaning‑layer fields.
