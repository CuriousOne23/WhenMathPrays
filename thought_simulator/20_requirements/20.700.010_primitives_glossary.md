## Purpose
This glossary contains canonical definitions for all TP-holding, TP-reading, and TP-modifying atomic primitives in the TS pipeline, with special focus on the Object Basin (OB) family and related low-level mechanics. It is an expansion of 20.190 and must preserve all original definitions, boundaries, and invariants.

**Scope:** This glossary defines atomic TS primitives only. Data structures, policies, and governance terms are defined in 20.700.030 and 20.700.040.

## Entries

### InB (Input Basin) — 20.100
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Deterministic, non-inferential front door for all external signals.  
**Focus:** Schema validation, surface canonicalization, FIFO preservation; no meaning inference.  
**When:** Pipeline-A entry, first deterministic stage after external input.  
**Why:** Normalize wire form without guessing user intent.  
**Is not:** Semantic repair (IIInB); inquiry (IB); truth interpretation (TB); routing authority.  
**Example:** "teh" → canonical token; "Yeah Baby!" → tagged, not interpreted.  
**Upstream primitives:** External input.  
**Downstream primitives:** IIInB (if repair needed) or IE.  
**Normative home:** 20.100; messy-input classes in 20.17.

---

### IIInB (Input Inference/Repair Basin) — 20.101
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Apply bounded, user-sanctioned shorthand expansions before context extraction.  
**Focus:** Deterministic semantic-shorthand repair via USP rules; no meaning construction.  
**When:** Optional stage input_semantic_repair; ordering InB → IIInB → RB.  
**Why:** Apply user-approved shorthand expansions without inference.  
**Is not:** Interpretation; candidate generation; truth evaluation; meaning construction.  
**Example:** USP expands "quicker-ish" → canonical form.  
**Upstream primitives:** InB.  
**Downstream primitives:** IE.  
**Normative home:** 20.101.

---

### IE (Intake Envelope, Structured Intake Object)
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Provide a deterministic, bounded, replayable representation of user input.  
**Focus:** Structured, normalized, repaired intake object from InB → IIInB.  
**When:** Immediately after IIInB; before CEx.  
**Why:** Downstream components require deterministic, replayable intake.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpretation; candidate generator; truth evaluator.  
**Example:** Normalized tokens + USP repairs + structural tags.  
**Upstream primitives:** IIInB.  
**Downstream primitives:** CEx.  
**Normative home:** 20.109.

---

### CEx (ContextExtractor)
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Produce a bounded, replayable context slice (CE) for ISc without exposing global state.  
**Focus:** Deterministic extraction of only the context-relevant information from CIL needed for candidate generation and ISc scoring.  
**When:** Inference Path: IIInB → IE → CEx → CE → candidate_set{} → ISc.  
**Why:** ISc requires contextual information but cannot read CIL or any global state directly.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; TP writer; global-state reader; lineage exporter.  
**Example:** Extract active referents, local discourse entities, and bounded conversation-context cues from CIL to form CE.  
**Upstream primitives:** IE.  
**Downstream primitives:** CE.  
**Normative home:** 20.33 (CIL), 20.44 (ISc).

---

### CE (ContextEnvelope) — 20.44
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Deliver safe, isolated context to ISc without exposing global conversation state.  
**Focus:** Bounded, deterministic, replayable context object containing only the context-relevant information extracted by CEx from CIL.  
**When:** Produced immediately before ISc in the Inference Path.  
**Why:** Provide ISc with the minimal, safe context required for deterministic candidate generation and scoring.  
**Is not:** History; semantic object; TP writer; CIL proxy; lineage exporter; global-state container.  
**Example:** CE contains active referents, local discourse entities, and bounded commitment cues needed for candidate_set{} and ISc scoring.  
**Upstream primitives:** CEx.  
**Downstream primitives:** ISc.  
**Normative home:** 20.44.

---

### ISc (Inference Scorer) — 20.44
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Deterministically score interpretation candidates by applying truth_hypotheses-prc over candidate_set{}, producing scoring fields for TPU without accessing global state.  
**Focus:** Read CE and candidate_set{}; apply truth_hypotheses-prc; produce tp.score_set, tp.score_conflict, and tp.score_reason_code for inclusion in update_structure.  
**When:** Path-A inference sequence: CE → candidate_set{} → ISc → TPU (Merge).  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, local scoring for routing and TPU commit while preventing global-state leakage or unbounded inference.  
**Is not:** Semantic generator; meaning constructor; truth evaluator; global inference engine; CIL reader; semantic repair mechanism.  
**Example:** ISc scores referent candidates and produces a score_set consumed by TR-prm during routing.  
**Upstream primitives:** CE, candidate_set{}.  
**Downstream primitives:** TPU.  
**Normative home:** 20.44.

---

### TPU (Thought Packet Updater) — Path A Only
**Path:** A only (correction only)  
**Purpose:** Sole safe writer to TP for Path-A correction; inserts and corrects message-items, referents, constraints, and meaning fields based on CE+CEx+ISc output.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, boundary-enforced TP updates; no topology, routing, governance, or realization work; does not write semantic_core.  
**When:** Path-A correction flow: CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1).  
**Why:** Enforce the single-writer invariant for meaning construction and ensure all semantic updates are deterministic, auditable, and isolated from Path-B.  
**Is not:** Topology engine; semantic interpreter; candidate generator; CIL reader; Path-B primitive; meaning constructor for expression.  
**Example:** ISc selects a corrected referent or message-item; TPU applies the update to TP(N), producing TP(N+1) under a new commit_id.  
**Scope invariant:** TPU-prm MUST NOT be invoked by Path-B; Path-B is meaning-read-only.  
**Upstream primitives:** ISc.  
**Downstream primitives:** SOB (or next OB).  
**Normative home:** 20.105.

### SOB (Structural OB) — 20.40.010
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Extract first-pass structural cues from TP content, producing the base structural residue consumed by downstream OB layers and routing.  
**Focus:** Clause boundaries, syntactic shape, punctuation/symbol masks, negation markers, modality markers, structural anchors, and structural tags; no semantic interpretation.  
**When:** First OB stage in Path-A whenever structural pattern detection is required.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic low-level structural residue enabling refinement, constraint extraction, and TR-input formation.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; constraint resolver; truth evaluator; geometric/meta-basin.  
**Example:** SOB identifies clause boundaries and structural markers, emitting the initial residue fragment for refinement.  
**Upstream primitives:** TPU.  
**Downstream primitives:** SROB.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.010.

---

### SROB (Structural Refinement OB) — 20.40.020
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Refine and canonicalize SOB structural residue into normalized forms suitable for constraint extraction and routing.  
**Focus:** Structural normalization, anchor refinement, canonical ordering, residue smoothing, and refinement-level metadata; no semantic interpretation.  
**When:** Invoked after SOB when structural refinement or normalization is required for downstream OB layers.  
**Why:** Ensure structural residue is stable, canonical, and refinement-consistent across TP variants and noisy inputs.  
**Is not:** Constraint extractor; semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; routing arbiter.  
**Example:** SROB refines SOB’s structural cues into canonicalized residue fragments with stable ordering.  
**Upstream primitives:** SOB.  
**Downstream primitives:** CnOB.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.020.

---

### CnOB (Constraint OB) — 20.40.030
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Extract constraint-level residue and metadata from refined structure, enabling routing, ΔH% attribution, and downstream interpretation.  
**Focus:** Constraint cues, missing-slot signals, underspecification markers, conflict indicators, and constraint-residue hashing; no semantic inference.  
**When:** Invoked after SROB when constraint extraction or constraint metadata is required for routing or ΔH% accounting.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic constraint residue enabling routing correctness, conflict detection, and missing-mass attribution.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; structural normalizer; truth evaluator; generative mechanism.  
**Example:** CnOB extracts constraint markers and missing-slot cues, producing constraint-level residue for routing.  
**Upstream primitives:** SROB.  
**Downstream primitives:** SmOB.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.030.

---

### SmOB (Semantic OB) — 20.40.040
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Perform two jobs: (1) extract pre-semantic and semantic-adjacent cues from SOB→SROB→CnOB residue, and (2) compress all upstream residue plus SmOB cues into a deterministic pre-semantic hash and TR-input cue vector.  
**Focus:** (Job 1) semantic-adjacent pattern activation, modality/affect cues, conflict-adjacent and underspecification-adjacent signals; (Job 2) deterministic pre-semantic residue hashing and TR-input cue formation for routing.  
**When:** Invoked after CnOB when semantic-adjacent cues, pre-semantic residue, or deterministic routing representation are required for SSG or downstream interpretation.  
**Why:** Job 1 adds pre-semantic cues supporting IdOB selection, ΔH% attribution, and semantic-layer preparation; Job 2 provides a canonical, replay-safe compressed representation enabling deterministic TR routing and SSG signal generation.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; stance assigner; geometric meta-basin; semantic normalizer.  
**Example:** SmOB extracts semantic-adjacent cues (Job 1) and compresses upstream residue plus its own cues into a deterministic hash and TR-input cue vector consumed by SSG and the Thought Router to derive TP.TR.  
**Upstream primitives:** CnOB.  
**Downstream primitives:** SSG.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.040.

### SSG (Structural Signature Generator) — 20.47
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Convert SmOB structural output into a deterministic, fixed-length structural signature vector for routing and similarity operations.  
**Focus:** Extract and normalize structural invariants (arc patterns, residue entropy, curvature, binding depth, motif frequencies) into a coordinate-stable vector representation.  
**When:** Mandatory stage structural_signature_generation; ordering SmOB → SSG → RB.  
**Why:** Provide RB with a geometry-ready, token-free, replay-stable coordinate chart enabling structural proximity, lane selection, and routing decisions.  
**Is not:** Semantic embedding; tokenization; meaning construction; interpretation; hashing of semantic content; candidate generator.  
**Example:** A SmOB graph with high branching depth and low curvature yields a signature emphasizing depth-dominant structural geometry.  
**Upstream primitives:** SmOB.  
**Downstream primitives:** RB.  
**Normative home:** 20.47.

### RBU-prm (Routing Buffer Update Primitive)
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Enrich the TP with subculture, identity, register, and interpretive metadata required for RB-prm to perform correct routing.  
**Focus:** Deterministically writes interpretive metadata (culture, subculture, register, stance-relevant cues) into the TP snapshot before routing occurs.  
**When:** Invoked before RB-prm, ensuring RB-prm receives a fully enriched TP.  
**Why:** Maintain architectural purity by separating interpretive metadata enrichment (RBU-prm) from routing computation (RB-prm).  
**Is not:** A router, classifier, scorer, or semantic evaluator. RBU-prm does not compute routing deltas.  
**Example:** RBU-prm writes subculture and identity metadata into the TP. RB-prm then uses this metadata to select the correct manifold chart and routing lane.  
**Upstream primitives:** OB family.  
**Downstream primitives:** RB.  
**Normative home:** 20.51.

---

### RTU-prm (Routing TP Update Primitive) — 20.30.005
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Commit RB-prm’s routing metadata into the TP snapshot, including culture/subculture lanes, fallback lane, routing lineage, routing reasons, fan-out decisions, and all routing-relevant structural fields.  
**Focus:** Pure deterministic write-back of RB-prm output; validate the routing envelope; ensure RB-prm remains pure and side-effect-free; embed routing state for TS/TrSch scheduling and staleness evaluation.  
**When:** Invoked immediately after RB-prm and before TS schedules the next OB-chain; runs once per routing cycle and is never re-invoked for the same RB output.  
**Why:** Separate routing computation (RB-prm) from routing commit (RTU-prm); provide TS/TrSch with a stable, auditable routing state; ensure replay equivalence and multi-cycle determinism.  
**Is not:** Router; classifier; interpreter; scorer; semantic evaluator; modifier of OB outputs, semantic fields, truth fields, merge results, or completion data.  
**Example:** RB-prm selects culture and fallback lanes; RTU-prm validates the routing envelope and writes lane-selection fields, routing lineage, and routing reasons into the TP snapshot for TS/TrSch.  
**Upstream primitives:** RB.  
**Downstream primitives:** Next OB cycle or OuBA.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.005.

### RB (Relational Basin) — 20.50
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Perform deterministic routing arbitration in Path-A, selecting the next OB based on structural residue, ΔH%, lineage, and TR-input fields, without accessing any Path-B structures.  
**Focus:** Deterministic evaluation of routing metadata and relational cues to choose the next OB in the meaning-construction cycle; RB selects the basin but does not move the TP.  
**When:** Invoked during Path-A routing after OB and TR evaluation; runs in the RB→RTU→IdOB loop until Truth/Done stabilizes the turn-final TP.  
**Why:** Provide governed, replay-safe routing decisions that maintain A-side determinism and enforce the A/B separation required by 20.705.  
**Is not:** SRP; TrigRB; TR; geometric projector; transport mechanism; Path-B computation; reader of B-side routing tables or MTP.  
**Example:** RB evaluates residue and ΔH% changes, determines that structural cues require re-entering CnOB, and selects CnOB as the next basin for OBA-prm execution.  
**Upstream primitives:** TR, SSG.  
**Downstream primitives:** IdOB or next OB.  
**Normative home:** 20.50.

---

### TrSch-prm (Thought Scheduler Primitive)
**Path:** TS-internal  
**Purpose:** Schedules the next primitive execution according to the consolidated IdOB-set control flags and TrSch rules.  
**Focus:** Deterministic scheduling of the next primitive (OB, IdOB, RB, etc.) based on CTP control flags; never modifies TP or meaning.  
**When:** After RTU or CTP, before the next primitive execution.  
**Why:** Decouple primitive selection from execution; enable deterministic, replay-safe scheduling inside TS.  
**Is not:** A meaning primitive; a routing arbiter; a TP writer; a governance primitive; TrSch never performs meaning construction, routing decisions, or truth evaluation.  
**Example:** After RTU commits routing metadata, TrSch selects the next OB or IdOB and hands it to TS for execution.  
**TrSch is invisible to the user and never appears in user-facing Path-A diagrams. TP never exposes or reflects TrSch. Only TS mechanical diagrams show it.**  
**Normative home:** 20.155.

---

### TR (Thought Router) — 20.37
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Compute the deterministic routing vector (TP.TR) for Path-A by consuming OB and DCB signals; TR influences RB’s arbitration but does not select the next basin.  
**Focus:** Deterministic computation of routing metadata; TR does not interpret, merge, or modify meaning.  
**When:** Path-A, after OB/DCB evaluation and before RB arbitration.  
**Why:** Provide a stable, auditable routing vector guiding RB using curvature (DCB), structural evidence (OB), and trajectory signals.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; TP writer; candidate generator; CE/CEx participant; realization component.  
**Example:** TR receives DCB curvature events and OB residue, then outputs TP.TR for RB’s next routing decision.  
**Upstream primitives:** OB family, DCB.  
**Downstream primitives:** RB.  
**Normative home:** 20.37.

---

### IdOB (Identity Object Basin) — 20.40.050
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Perform identity-conditioned meaning refinement on the routed TP, applying identity-specific rules and basin-local semantics after RB/TR select the identity pathway.  
**Focus:** Consume the post-routing TP view, resolve identity-conditioned meaning, and emit updates via TPU.idob_update; produce no residue, ΔH%, or TR-input.  
**When:** Invoked in Path-A after SSG → RB → RTU produce a stable routing decision; may run multiple times in the RB→RTU→IdOB cycle until identity-conditioned meaning stabilizes.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, replay-safe identity-specific refinement isolated from structural, routing, and truth-evaluation responsibilities.  
**Is not:** OB primitive; structural classifier; constraint extractor; routing arbiter; truth evaluator; ΔH% contributor; Path-B computation; writer of TP.TR or routing_metadata.  
**Example:** After RB routes a TP to a domain-specific identity basin (e.g., mathematical, narrative, operator), IdOB applies identity-conditioned rules and updates the TP via TPU.idob_update.  
**Upstream primitives:** RB.  
**Downstream primitives:** OuBA (or next cycle).  
**Normative home:** 20.40.050.

### CTP-prm (Collect/Consolidate Thought Point) — 20.145_prm
**Path:** A  
**Purpose:** Collects all OB-outputs from an OB-set into a single stable OB-output packet for RB arbitration.  
**Focus:** Deterministic consolidation of OB-outputs between OB-sets; does not write or modify TP (TPU is sole writer).  
**When:** After an OB-set completes and before RB arbitration; in multi-core systems runs after all parallel OBs finish.  
**Why:** Ensures all legitimate OB-outputs are available as one replay-safe packet for routing in both serial and parallel execution.  
**Is not:** Meaning constructor; merge engine; scorer; TP writer; governance step.  
**Example:** Single-core: InB→OB0→RB→OB1…OBn→CTP→RB; Multi-core: InB→OB0→RB→{OB1∥OB2…∥OBn}→CTP→RB.  
**Upstream primitives:** OB-set.  
**Downstream primitives:** RB.  
**Normative home:** 20.145_ctp.md.

### OuBA (Output Basin — Path A Commit) — 20.40.060
**Path:** A only (commit boundary)  
**Purpose:** Commit the finalized Path-A meaning state into an immutable semantic snapshot (semantic_core and meaning-bearing fields) for DF intake (IB → TB → GBIB → GB).  
**Focus:** Freeze the meaning-layer state and write commit-time metadata, resolution flags, and DF-readiness indicators; perform no semantic correction, truth evaluation, routing, or governance decisions.  
**When:** Runs once per Path-A cycle after meaning construction and completion evaluation stabilize; executes immediately before DF ingress and never during Path-B realization.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay-safe termination point for Path-A, ensuring DF operates only on committed meaning and never on in-flight structures.  
**Is not:** Governance primitive; truth evaluator; semantic repair engine; routing arbiter; generator of inquiry/truth states; producer of governance directives; OuBA never modifies semantic_core after commit or invokes IB/TB/GBIB/GB.  
**Example:** After Path-A meaning construction stabilizes, OuBA freezes semantic_core and commit-time metadata into a committed TP snapshot consumed by DF beginning at IB.  
**Upstream primitives:** IdOB / final OB.  
**Downstream primitives:** CTP → Path B / Governance.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.060; 20.705 (DF ingress); 20.90 (IB); 20.60 (TB); 20.80 (GB).

## TrigRB (Trigger Relational Basin) — 20.57
**Purpose:** Detects realization‑relevant semantic triggers over the finalized meaning boundary (OuBA) before any B‑side planning or realization begins.  
**Focus:** B‑side semantic‑trigger detection over the stable, meaning‑complete OuBA state; identifies trigger codes that drive SRP selection and downstream realization behavior.  
**When:** First stage of Pipeline B: **OuBA → TrigRB → SRP → XP → REx → RPU → ReB → OuBB**.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, non‑interpretive trigger detection so that Path B can react to meaning without re‑entering Path A or modifying meaning.  
**Is not:** TR; RB; a semantic interpreter; a meaning constructor; an SRP compiler; TrigRB performs no inference, no repair, and no TP mutation.  
**Example:** OuBA meaning contains a directive‑like structure; TrigRB emits `trigger_codes{ directive_candidate }` for SRP lookup.  
**Normative home:** 20.57.

---

## SRP (Semantic Routing Planner) — 20.55
**Purpose:** Cold‑path compiler that builds B‑side routing tables and assigns routing_epoch_id; hot‑path components perform deterministic lookup only.  
**Focus:** Compile routing tables from B‑side schemas, operator registries, and policy registries; SRP does not read TP, MTP, TB, or meaning.  
**When:** Cold‑path execution triggered by schema or policy changes; not part of per‑turn Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Separate expensive routing‑table maintenance from hot‑path realization, ensuring deterministic, replay‑safe B‑side routing.  
**Is not:** RB; TR; IMR writer; meaning interpreter; Path‑A participant.  
**Example:** B‑side envelopes carry routing_epoch_id referencing the SRP‑compiled routing tables used by TrigRB/IMR/CE.  
**Normative home:** 20.55.

## XP / exec_plan / exec_trace — 20.500, 20.207
**Purpose:** Capture regenerable execution artifacts per commit_id, keeping B‑side audit separate from A‑side semantic state.  
**Focus:** Execution artifacts tied to a frozen meaning snapshot.  
  • **exec_plan (XP):** B‑side execution plan generated from frozen semantic_core.  
  • **exec_trace:** Append‑only audit log of OuB refs, IMR records, and realization events.  
**When:** Pipeline‑B, after meaning is frozen and committed.  
**Why:** Execution is regenerable; replay uses semantic_core only, ensuring A/B separation and deterministic meaning replay.  
**Is not:** TP; MTP; semantic_core; meaning‑layer state.  
**Example:** exec_trace records OuB refs and IMR correction events for audit and replay.  
**Normative home:** 20.500, 20.207.

# **TPTB (TP Truth Basin Tags) — 20.64 (Finalized)**
**Purpose:** Encodes the truth‑evaluation results produced by TB over the **frozen Path A TP snapshot** into a compact, auditable metadata structure (`tp.truth_tags`) that Path B reads at intake. This enables truth‑aware behavior in Path B without allowing Path B to perform truth evaluation, preserving strict A/B separation.
**Focus:** Deterministic, read‑only truth metadata: factual alignment, contradiction detection, evidential basis, stance coherence, referential consistency, and explanation codes.  
TPTB serializes TB’s `truth_hypotheses{}` into stable tags attached to the **A→B TP semantic snapshot**.
**When:** Produced **after OuBA freezes the TP snapshot** and **after TB interprets that snapshot**, during A‑cycle finalization.  
Delivered to Path B **before LI**, and available throughout REx, RPlan, and RPU.  
Never produced or modified during Path B.
**Why:** Allows Path B to express truth‑aware behavior (qualification, contradiction handling, corrective phrasing) without invoking TB, performing inference, or accessing global truth state. Ensures replay‑safe, non‑inferential truth behavior grounded in Path A’s authoritative interpretation.
**Is not:** A truth engine; a basin; a semantic interpreter; a scorer; a routing mechanism; a safety evaluator; a global truth model; or a Path B computation.  
TPTB never modifies TP and performs no evaluation itself.
**Example:** TB detects a factual contradiction in the TP snapshot.  
TPTB encodes `truth_conflict=true`, `conflict_type=contradiction`, and `basis=evidence_X`.  
Path B later reads these tags to generate a corrective or qualified response without performing truth evaluation.
**Normative home:** 20.64.

# **TPSF (TP Safety Flags) — 20.66 (Finalized)**
**Purpose:** Encodes the safety‑evaluation results produced by A‑side safety basins over the **frozen Path A TP snapshot** into a compact, auditable metadata structure (`tp.safety_flags`) that Path B reads at intake. This enables safety‑aware behavior in Path B without allowing Path B to perform safety evaluation. **Focus:** Deterministic, read‑only safety metadata: risk presence, risk category, severity, rationale codes, and policy hooks. TPSF serializes the safety basins’ `safety_assessment{}` into stable flags attached to the **A→B TP semantic snapshot**, co‑located with `tp.truth_tags`.
**When:** Produced **after OuBA freezes the TP snapshot** and **after A‑side safety evaluation**, during A‑cycle finalization. Delivered to Path B **before LI**, and available throughout REx, RPlan, and RPU.  
Never produced or modified during Path B.
**Why:** Allows Path B to express safety‑aware behavior (refusal, redirection, qualification, de‑escalation) without invoking safety basins, performing inference, or accessing global safety state. Ensures replay‑safe, non‑inferential safety behavior grounded in Path A’s authoritative safety evaluation.
**Is not:** A safety engine; a basin; a semantic interpreter; a scorer; a routing mechanism; a truth evaluator; a global safety model; or a Path B computation.  
TPSF never modifies TP and performs no evaluation itself.
**Example:** A‑side safety basins detect self‑harm risk in the TP snapshot.  
TPSF encodes `safety_risk=true`, `risk_type=self_harm`, `severity=high`, and a rationale code.  
Path B later reads these flags to refuse or redirect the request without performing safety evaluation.
**Normative home:** 20.66.

## **CoHI (Conversation History Integrator) — 20.113 (Revised, dual‑input)**  
**Purpose:** Imports **conversation‑level structural continuity and historical inference** from the COB/CIL loop into the **frozen Path A TP snapshot** so that Path B primitives operate on a TP enriched with both cross‑turn structure and turn‑local truth/safety metadata (TPTB, TPSF). CoHI merges conversation objects, referential chains, topic threads, continuity markers, and selected CIL outputs into stable `tp.continuity_fields` consumed by LI and downstream Path B primitives.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, read‑only integration over two inputs: (1) the A→B TP (including `tp.truth_tags` (TPTB) and `tp.safety_flags` (TPSF)) and (2) COB/CIL structures (structural_history, referential_history, conversation_objects, topic_threads, continuity_markers, and continuity‑relevant inference). CoHI performs no new inference; it only normalizes and projects these into a per‑turn continuity substrate attached to the TP.  
**When:** Runs at the **start of Path B**, after the A→B handoff TP (with TPTB/TPSF) is available and after the COB/CIL loop has been updated for the prior turn; executes once per turn, before LI, ensuring LI receives a continuity‑augmented TP. Never runs during Path A and never mutates COB or CIL state.  
**Why:** Enables LI, REx, RPlan, and RPU to operate on a TP that reflects both turn‑local truth/safety metadata and conversation‑level structure, ensuring referential stability, topic continuity, and coherent multi‑turn realization without forcing Path B primitives to read COB/CIL directly or perform memory operations.  
**Is not:** A memory system; a planner; an inference engine; a scorer; a router; a semantic interpreter; or a continuity reasoner. CoHI does not modify COB or CIL, does not perform global inference, and does not generate meaning.  
**Example:** COB/CIL maintain a conversation object for “the dataset we discussed earlier” plus continuity inference that it remains active; the A→B TP carries `truth_tags{ factual_conflict=false }` and `safety_flags{ safe=true }`. CoHI merges these into `tp.continuity_fields`, allowing LI to interpret “run it again” as referring to that dataset while respecting truth and safety metadata.  
**Normative home:** 20.113.

# **LI (Local Inference Primitive) — 20.112 (Revised Final)**  
**Purpose:** Begins **Path B** by applying the TS‑level concept **MLI** to the TP snapshot (including TPTB, TPSF, and CoHI‑produced continuity fields), committing the first layer of meaning before any global inference, scoring, routing, or B‑side planning occurs.  
**Focus:** Reads representation‑layer fields, A‑side metadata (`tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`), and **continuity_fields** injected by CoHI (structural_history, referential_history, conversation_objects, topic_threads, continuity_markers). Invokes **MLI‑prc** to resolve local meaning. Writes meaning‑layer fields (`tp.meaning`, `tp.mli_status`, `tp.mli_confidence`, `tp.mli_conflict`, `tp.mli_reason_code`, `tp.mli_revision_cycles`) into the TP.  
**When:** Runs **after CoHI** has integrated conversation‑level continuity into the TP snapshot and **before** any Path B global inference (REx, RPlan, RPU). Executes once per turn at the start of Path B inference.  
**Why:** Ensures deterministic, auditable, continuity‑aware local meaning commitment so all downstream Path B primitives operate on a stable semantic substrate enriched with cross‑turn structure. Prevents semantic drift and unstable global inference by forcing meaning resolution before any higher‑order reasoning.  
**Is not:** A global inference engine; a scorer; a router; a semantic_core builder; a B‑side planner; or MLI itself. Does not read from MTP or generate XP, and does not access COB directly.  
**Example:** Given an ambiguous utterance (“Can you check that for me”), LI reads the TP’s representation fields, A‑side metadata, and CoHI‑imported continuity (e.g., a conversation object representing “the dataset we discussed earlier”), applies MLI‑prc to classify the utterance as a *request*, and writes the resolved meaning and confidence into the TP for downstream primitives.  
**Normative home:** 20.112.

## REx (Realization Extractor) — Path B
**Purpose:** Extracts the realization‑relevant substrate from the finalized TP (read‑only) for downstream planning; does not read or modify MTP.  
**Focus:** Deterministic extraction of clause‑level structure, dependencies, discourse roles, referential anchors, and realization‑relevant constraints; no semantic interpretation or TP writes.  
**When:** First primitive in Path‑B: TP → REx → RPlan.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; TP writer; meaning constructor; truth/safety evaluator.

## RPlan (Realization Plan Construction) — Path B

**When:** After REx, before RPU.  
**Is not:** Merge engine; TP writer; meaning constructor; truth/safety evaluator.

## RPU (Realization Plan Updater) — Path B
**Purpose:** Executes the realization plan produced by RPlan, generating the realization substrate (style, pacing, channel, structure) without modifying TP or meaning.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, boundary‑enforced realization updates; no semantic merge, no TP writes, no meaning construction.  
**When:** Path‑B flow: TP→REx→RPlan→RPU→ReB.  
**Is not:** Semantic merge engine; TP writer; meaning constructor; CE/CEx participant; Path‑A primitive.

## ReB (Realization Basin — Path B Pre‑Builder Basin) — 20.169
**Purpose:** Validates the post‑RPU realization substrate before ; blocks malformed or inconsistent substrates and emits either reb_validated_ref or BLOCKED.

**When:** After RPU completes and before OuBB begins surface‑form assembly; runs exactly once per Path‑B substrate.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe validation boundary ensuring only structurally sound, invariant‑preserving substrates reach output construction.  
**Is not:** Generator; planner; semantic interpreter; repair mechanism; TP/semantic_core consumer; routing or truth evaluator; output builder.

## **Response Generator (RG) — Primitive**
**Purpose:** Converts the meaning substrate delivered inside SSR(t) into surface‑form output without altering meaning. 
**Focus:** Deterministic expression mapping over SSR(t), seed‑bounded variability, truth/safety‑aware realization using TPTB, TPSF, and RRw. 
**When:** Invoked on Path B after ReB prepares realization metadata and before OuBB commits final output. 
**Why:** Ensures expression is deterministic, replay‑safe, and consistent with Path A semantics while preserving meaning immutability. 
**Is not:** A semantic engine; a truth evaluator; a safety engine; a writer of TP or MTP; a modifier of SSR(t). 
**Example:** Given identical (SSR(t), seed), produces identical surface‑form output. 
**Normative home:** 20.30.080 Response Generator Primitive.

## **Random Seed Generator (RSG) — Primitive**
**Purpose:** Applies the turn’s seed to RG’s expression‑selection space, isolating nondeterminism and enforcing replay invariants. 
**Focus:** Seed‑bounded variant selection, deterministic mapping from (SSR(t), seed) to a single surface‑form realization. 
**When:** Invoked immediately after RG constructs the expression‑selection table and before OuBB commits final output. 
**Why:** Ensures identical SSR(t) and identical seed always yield identical surface‑form output, enabling deterministic replay and controlled stylistic variability. 
**Is not:** A semantic engine; a writer of TP or MTP; a modifier of SSR(t); a generator of meaning or realization metadata. 
**Example:** Given identical (SSR(t), seed), selects the same variant from RG’s expression table. 
**Normative home:** 20.30.085 Random Seed Boundary Primitive.

## OuBB (Output Basin — Path B Realization Commit) — 20.110
**Purpose:** Final Path‑B commit primitive that converts the realization‑ready TP into external‑facing output, writes output_commit and terminal markers, and finalizes the TP for TS emission; produces the turn‑final TP later ingested by HC‑Flow.  
**Focus:** Read‑only consumption of realization_semantics, response_plan, surface_form_spec, register, subculture; generation of realization_output{} and realization_metadata{}; writes output_commit=true and terminal=true without routing, truth evaluation, governance, or semantic interpretation.  
**When:** Runs exactly once after all Path‑B primitives complete and TP.realization_ready=true; terminates Path‑B and hands the committed TP to TS for emission.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe realization‑commit boundary isolated from meaning construction, routing, governance, and historical processing; ensure external emission and HC‑Flow ingestion derive solely from committed TP state.  
**Is not:** Routing arbiter; semantic interpreter; truth evaluator; governance primitive; ΔH% contributor; writer of TP.TR or routing_metadata; meaning generator; Path‑A primitive; producer of IB/TB/GB/COB/CIL/CoHI signals.  
**Example:** After RPU/ReB finalize the realization substrate, OuBB writes realization_output="…", realization_metadata{}, output_commit=true, terminal=true; TS emits the output and HC‑Flow later ingests the turn‑final TP.  
**Normative home:** 20.110.

## TP (Thought Packet) — 20.105

## SSR (Semantic Snapshot Reference) — 20.52  
**Purpose:** Frozen, immutable, sanitized projection of the TP produced by OuBA at the commit boundary; SSR is the sole meaning artifact consumed by Path‑B and governance.  
**Focus:** Holds committed semantic_core, truth/safety/identity fields, continuity fields, discourse act, orthogonality_signature, core_id, conflict/underspec flags, and RRw; provides Path‑B with a deterministic, replay‑safe, audit‑safe meaning substrate.  
**When:** Created once per turn by OuBA after TP meaning construction is complete; never mutated afterward.  
**Why:** Enforce strict A/B separation by preventing Path‑B from accessing TP; provide a stable, read‑only meaning construct for OpBeh, OBG, XlateR, XP, RPU, and governance.  
**Is not:** TP; semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; router; behavior selector; SSR never contains mutable TP fields or hypothesis‑layer deltas.  
**Example:** OuBA freezes TP into SSR containing committed meaning and RRw; Path‑B selects OpBeh deterministically from SSR without reading TP.  
**Normative home:** 20.52.

## RRw (Routing Row) — 20.53  
**Purpose:** Deterministic routing decision record produced by RB/TR and validated by RBU; included inside SSR and consumed by Path‑B primitives.  
**Focus:** Holds opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id, routing_epoch_id, rb_trigger_id, lineage additions, arbitration path, staleness metadata, and semantic‑deltaH routing signals; provides Path‑B with routing decisions without exposing TP internals.  
**When:** Generated during Path‑A routing; copied into SSR at OuBA commit; read by Path‑B during behavior/voice/mapping selection.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic routing for Path‑B while preserving A/B separation; ensure OpBeh/OBG/XlateR selection is stable, replay‑safe, and TP‑agnostic.  
**Is not:** TP field exposed to Path‑B; meaning constructor; semantic interpreter; standalone artifact; RRw is never delivered separately from SSR.  
**Example:** RB/TR produce RRw selecting opbeh_id and xlater_id; OuBA embeds RRw into SSR; OpBeh reads RRw from SSR to select behavior class.

# **Revised IB (Inquiry Basin) — 20.90 (Corrected)**  
**Purpose:** First stage of D‑Flow (DF), evaluating committed meaning from OuBA to determine inquiry‑side state, inquiry class, and governance‑relevant inquiry signals without modifying semantic_core or TP.  
**Focus:** Computes `inquiry_state{}` over the committed semantic snapshot, classifies inquiry‑relevant structures (commitment gaps, evidential posture, stance‑inquiry tension, referential uncertainty), and prepares inquiry‑side evaluation context for TB.  
**When:** Invoked **post‑OuBA commit** and only within **DF**: `IB → TB → GBIB → GB`. Runs exclusively on committed, immutable meaning; never runs during Path A construction or Path B realization.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, auditable inquiry evaluation over committed meaning, ensuring that governance‑side inquiry decisions occur only after Path A meaning is finalized and never influence meaning construction directly.  
**Is not:** A Path‑A primitive; a meaning constructor; a semantic repair engine; a truth evaluator; a generator of interpretation candidates; a TP writer; IB never modifies semantic_core, TP, or  and never performs semantic inference or reconstruction.  
**Example:** OuBA commits meaning containing a weak evidential posture and unresolved stance‑commitment tension; IB classifies the inquiry state as `inquiry_state{ evidential_weak, stance_tension }` and forwards this inquiry context to TB for truth‑evaluation alignment.  
**Normative home:** 20.90 (IB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.80 (GB).
