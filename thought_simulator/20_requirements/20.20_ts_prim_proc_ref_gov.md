### 20.20 TS primitives, processes, reference objects, and governing objects  

---

## Purpose  
Defines canonical TS primitive contracts, processes, reference objects, and governing objects, aligned with:

- **20.10** architectural principles  
- **20.12** canonical invariants  
- **20.30** functional‑model semantics  

This file is the **TS ontology**.

---

## I. TS primitives  
*(Meaning‑layer and realization‑layer primitives)*  

These are the actual TS primitives — entities that appear in pipelines, have deterministic interfaces, and participate in meaning construction or realization.

### Meaning‑layer primitives (Pipeline A)  
- TP — Thought Packet  
- MTP — Master Thought Packet  
- InB — Input Basin  
- OB — Object Basin (see 20.40 for OB meaning)  
- RB — Relational Basin  
- TB — Truth Basin  
- ΔH% — Contribution Metric  
- IB — Inquiry Basin  
- GB — Governing Basin  
- DCB — Directional Conversation Basin  
- OuB — Output Basin  
- ISc — Inference Scorer  
- COP — Coprocessor Interface Layer  
- TPU — Thought Packet Updater (sole writer)  

### Realization‑layer primitives (Pipeline B)
- CoHI‑prm — Continuity‑History Integrator  
- LI‑prm — Lineage Integrator  
- REx‑prm — Realization Extractor  
- RPlan‑prm — Realization Plan Constructor  
- RPU‑prm — Realization Plan Updater  
- ReB‑prm — Realization Basin  

*(These six are the canonical Path B primitives.  
Path B ordering: CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → ReB.)*

### HLRs that define primitive contracts and boundaries

- **HLR‑20.20‑001** — TS primitives SHALL have deterministic, observable interfaces.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑002** — TS primitives SHALL NOT overlap with TS processes.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑003** — TS primitives SHALL NOT overlap with TS reference objects.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑004** — TS primitives SHALL NOT overlap with TS governing objects.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑005** — TS primitives SHALL maintain meaning‑layer stability across all transformations.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑006** — TS primitives SHALL preserve referent stability when interacting with lineage structures.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑007** — TS primitives SHALL expose only deterministic composition rules.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑008** — TS primitives SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic branching.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑009** — TS primitives SHALL maintain platform‑independent behavior.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑010** — TS primitives SHALL NOT access TP or semantic_core unless explicitly defined.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑011** — TS primitives SHALL maintain observability for verification.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑012** — TS primitives SHALL NOT redefine or reinterpret upstream invariants.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑013** — TS primitives SHALL enforce the Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability invariant.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑014** — TS primitives SHALL maintain continuity‑stitch correctness when consuming continuity_fields.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑015** — TS primitives SHALL maintain lineage‑stability correctness when consuming lineage_fields.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑016** — TS primitives SHALL NOT alter canonical primitive semantics based on historical naming aliases.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑017** — TS primitives SHALL NOT be simultaneously classified as processes, reference objects, or governing objects.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑018** — TS primitives SHALL preserve the integrity of the primitive‑chain ordering.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑019** — TS primitives SHALL NOT bypass or skip required upstream primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑020** — TS primitives SHALL NOT introduce new referents outside the allowed referent‑stability set.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑021** — TS primitives SHALL maintain deterministic error‑reporting semantics.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑022** — TS primitives SHALL NOT modify reference objects.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑023** — TS primitives SHALL NOT modify governing objects.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑024** — TS primitives SHALL NOT alter the meaning‑layer → realization‑layer boundary.

(Additional HLR groups for Path B primitives appear in their respective files.)

## II. TS processes  
*(Processes performed inside primitives — not pipeline nodes)*  

These are not primitives and must never appear as nodes in flow diagrams.  
They are internal behaviors of primitives.

- Merge — Performed inside TPU  
- Scoring — Performed inside ISc  
- Normalization — Performed inside InB  
- Semantic Collapse — Performed inside OB  
- Routing Arbitration — Performed inside RB  
- Clarification Triggering — Performed inside IB  
- Truth Evaluation — Performed inside TB  
- Global Constraint Enforcement — Performed inside GB  

### HLRs that govern processes

- **HLR‑20.20‑025** — TS processes SHALL have non‑overlapping responsibilities.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑026** — TS processes SHALL expose deterministic interfaces.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑027** — TS processes SHALL maintain observability.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑028** — TS processes SHALL maintain platform independence.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑029** — TS processes SHALL maintain deterministic composition with their parent primitive.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑030** — Merge → TPU update envelopes SHALL follow deterministic replay rules.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑031** — ISc → COP → Merge → TPU sequences SHALL maintain replay equivalence.  

(Additional HLR groups for Path B primitives appear in 20.112–20.169.)

## III. TS reference objects  
*(Non‑pipeline, read‑only, global or contextual structures)*

These objects inform primitives but are not primitives themselves.

- CIL — Conversation Integration Layer  
- COB — Conversation Object Bank  
- USP — User State Profile  
- MTP slices — Referenced by CEx / TPU  
- Lineage identifiers  
- Commitment structures  
- Active referent sets  
- Thread geometry  
- continuity_fields — CoHI‑produced continuity descriptors  
- lineage_fields — LI‑produced lineage descriptors  
- continuity‑stitch maps — CoHI‑level continuity merge structures  
- referent‑stability sets — LI‑level referent stability guarantees  

### HLRs that govern reference objects

- **HLR‑20.20‑032** — Reference objects SHALL NOT overlap with primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑033** — Reference objects SHALL remain observable for verification.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑034** — Reference objects SHALL maintain platform independence.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑035** — Reference objects SHALL remain read‑only to all primitives.  

---

## IV. TS governing objects  
*(Supervisory, policy‑enforcing, post‑TS entities)*  

These objects govern correctness, truth, and global constraints.

- GB — Governing Basin  
- GBIB — Governance for IB/TB  
- TB — Truth Basin (post‑TS governance role)  
- IB — Inquiry Basin (post‑TS governance role)  

### HLRs that govern governing objects

- **HLR‑20.20‑036** — TB interpretation rules SHALL be applied deterministically.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑037** — GB constraints SHALL be enforced consistently across primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑038** — IB triggers SHALL follow deterministic governance rules.  
- **HLR‑20.20‑039** — Governing objects SHALL remain distinct from meaning‑layer basins and execution primitives.

## VI.
Primitive contract notes

- **Primitive boundaries** in this module are normative and govern both implementation and verification scope.  
- Historical naming aliases may be documented for lineage but do not alter canonical primitive semantics. 
- Path A and Path B primitives are **disjoint**: no object may be simultaneously a primitive and a process, reference object, or governing object.  
- **Invariant — Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability:**  
  Meaning‑layer output MUST be lineage‑stable before entering **CoHI**, and lineage‑stable output MUST be referent‑stable before entering **LI**.  
  This invariant governs the transition from Path A → Path B and ensures correctness of continuity and lineage integration.
