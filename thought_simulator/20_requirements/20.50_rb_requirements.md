# 20.50 RB Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.050-001: RB SHALL deterministically select next OB targets and lane projections.
2. HLR-20.050-002: RB SHALL operate over bounded, fixed TP fields.
3. HLR-20.050-003: RB SHALL NOT mutate truth evaluation state.
4. HLR-20.050-004: RB routing decisions SHALL be replayable from logs.
