# 20.110 OuB Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.110  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026-06-07  
**Status:** Approved v0.2 — Stage 4 GATE (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok; no edits required)

## Purpose

Defines normative **Output Basin (OuB)** expression semantics for Pipeline B: seed-bound surface-text realization from frozen committed meaning, incomplete-meaning handling, and presentation policy — without mutating `semantic_core` or Pipeline A state.

**This module owns:** expression selection determinism, seed boundary, incomplete-meaning detection semantics, and presentation rules under messy-input flags.  
**Integration wire contract:** [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) (`oub_artifact_ref`, XP/`exec_trace` binding, IMR handoff) — **not duplicated here** (OQ-004).

**Naming authority:** [20.500](20.500_refactoring_for_dual_TS_pipeline.md) §5 — OuB is Pipeline B only; no TP-B; no basin **OB** alias.

## 1. Pipeline and Input Boundary

OuB executes on Pipeline B only, after `exec_plan` commit for the active `commit_id` ([20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) HLR-20.058-001; [20.36](20.36_canonical_end_to_end_trace.md) HLR-20.036-005).

```text
frozen semantic_core (semantic_snapshot_ref)
  + exec_plan (opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id, expression_plan_ref)
  + seed_scope_ref
       → OuB expression selection
       → oub_artifact_ref (20.58)
```

1. **HLR-20.110-001:** OuB SHALL render output expressions from **frozen** `semantic_core` referenced by `semantic_snapshot_ref` at `mtp_update` for the active `commit_id` — not from live mutable MTP drafts or lane-local TP state.
2. **HLR-20.110-010:** OuB SHALL execute only on Pipeline B after `exec_plan_commit` for the bound (`commit_id`, `cycle_id`) / XP pass.
3. **HLR-20.110-011:** OuB SHALL NOT read or write `tp_id`, `lane_id`, or TP envelope fields in dual-pipeline mode ([20.105](20.105_tp_requirements.md), [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) HLR-20.058-006).
4. **HLR-20.110-012:** OuB SHALL consume `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, and `expression_plan_ref` from `exec_plan` only — not from TP or basin routing tables directly.

---

## 2. Expression Determinism and Seed Boundary

5. **HLR-20.110-002:** OuB SHALL consume seed-controlled variability **only** at expression selection (HLR-20.012-010).
6. **HLR-20.110-003:** OuB output mapping SHALL remain deterministic for fixed frozen `semantic_core`, fixed `exec_plan` content, and fixed `seed_scope_ref`.
7. **HLR-20.110-004:** OuB SHALL NOT alter upstream meaning-construction state: no writes to `semantic_core`, MTP pre-commit drafts, or Pipeline A envelopes.

**Invariants (retained):**

| Invariant | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Determinism | Identical inputs → identical `oub_artifact_ref` content hash (20.58 HLR-20.058-008) |
| Seed boundary | Seed affects expression/realization only; strip replay leaves `semantic_core` unchanged |

---

## 3. Writes and Artifacts

OuB expression outputs are realized as `oub_artifact_ref` records appended to `exec_trace` per [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) §2. This module defines **semantic content** of those artifacts; wire field names and XP denormalization are owned by 20.58 / 20.205.

8. **HLR-20.110-013:** OuB SHALL emit exactly one primary `oub_artifact_ref` per OuB invocation on a normal XP pass unless an approved IMR Type A retry schedules an additional expression pass on the same `xp_record` ([20.205](20.205_execution_packet_xp_requirements.md) §6).
9. **HLR-20.110-014:** OuB SHALL NOT emit `CorrectionTrigger` or IMR taxonomy classifications — IMR owns post-expression evaluation ([20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) HLR-20.058-012).

---

## 4. Messy-Input and Presentation

10. **HLR-20.110-015:** OuB SHALL NOT clear `messy_input_record` flags, smooth `conflict_flag`, or resolve `underspec_flag` in `semantic_core` ([20.17](20.17_messy_input_handling.md), [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) HLR-20.058-009/010).
11. Under `underspec_flag` or `conflict_flag`, OuB presentation SHALL follow OBG/register policy from `exec_plan` without inventing resolved meaning or suppressed contradiction evidence.

---

## 5. Incomplete-Meaning Handling (Legacy TP Path Reconciled)

Pre-dual-pipeline HLRs referenced TP tagging. **Dual-pipeline posture** per [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md) HLR-20.058-013/014:

12. **HLR-20.110-005:** OuB SHALL detect incomplete-meaning conditions when ΔH% / stability metrics exceed deployment-defined thresholds. OuB SHALL NOT externalize resolved meaning under incomplete-meaning conditions.
13. **HLR-20.110-006:** When incomplete-meaning is detected, OuB SHALL append an audit event to `exec_trace` with `oub_event_type = INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED` and SHALL schedule IB-Creation-Request pathways **only** through the supervisory queue — **not** via TP mutation.
14. **HLR-20.110-007:** OuB SHALL notify GB indirectly via the TS IB-Creation-Request pathway. OuB SHALL NOT communicate with GB directly.
15. **HLR-20.110-008:** Incomplete-meaning detection, `exec_trace` logging, and IB-Creation-Request scheduling SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries.
16. **HLR-20.110-009:** Incomplete-meaning events in `exec_trace` SHALL include at minimum:

```text
oub_incomplete_meaning_event {
  oub_event_type,           // INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED
  commit_id,
  cycle_id,
  xp_id,
  delta_h_q32,              // or deployment H% metric field
  threshold_q32,
  reason_code,
  policy_signature,
  audit_seq
}
```

Events SHALL be deterministic, append-only, and bounded. **Legacy:** pre-dual-pipeline exports that logged `tp_seq` on TP are deprecated; new fixtures SHALL use the `exec_trace` event shape above.

## 7. Verification Focus

- Class 1 ([20.36](20.36_canonical_end_to_end_trace.md)): OuB after `exec_plan_commit`; `oub_artifact_ref` in `exec_trace`; strip leaves `semantic_core` unchanged
- Seed variant: artifact hash differs; `semantic_core` strip-identical
- No `lane_id` / `tp_id` on OuB I/O
- XP `EXPRESSING` → `IMR_EVAL` requires `oub_artifact_ref` (20.58 HLR-20.058-018)
- Incomplete-meaning: event in `exec_trace`, not TP; no `semantic_core` smoothing
- `b_regeneration_equivalent`: OuB stage reproduces equivalent artifact (20.207 / 20.36 Class 1)
