# 20.12 TS Invariants

**Document ID:** 20.12  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Draft — canonical invariant registry

## Purpose

Defines the **canonical TS invariants** that MUST hold across all deployments — dual-pipeline (current) or unified (conditional future). Other 20-series modules SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken these invariants.

## State Envelopes

TS state is logically partitioned:

| Envelope | Contents (summary) | Writers |
|----------|-------------------|---------|
| `semantic_core` | Propositions, stance, goals, ΔH%, Thought Router fields | Pipeline A / Phase 1 only |
| `exec_plan` | `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, `routing_epoch_id`, planning refs | Pipeline B planning only |
| `exec_trace` | `exec_trigger_id`, OuB artifact refs, IMR records | Pipeline B expression/feedback only |
| `supervisory` | GB markers, policy logs, correction queues | GB at safe boundaries |

## Normative Requirements

1. **HLR-20.012-001 (Write-authority partition):** `semantic_core`, `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`, and `supervisory` writers SHALL be disjoint. No module SHALL write outside its designated envelope authority.

2. **HLR-20.012-002 (Meaning/realization separation):** Meaning construction and execution/realization SHALL remain **logically separated** regardless of deployment topology (dual-pipeline or unified). Realization SHALL NOT silently overwrite meaning.

3. **HLR-20.012-003 (semantic_core immutability by realization):** Pipeline B (or unified realization phases) SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`, stance, goals, epistemic structure, or MTP meaning fields.

4. **HLR-20.012-004 (exec_plan / exec_trace separation):** `exec_plan` (planning decisions) and `exec_trace` (expression artifacts and IMR records) SHALL remain distinct envelopes with distinct write authorities.

5. **HLR-20.012-005 (Phase immutability):** Within a cycle, interpretation/semantic construction SHALL complete before realization planning; realization planning SHALL complete before expression; expression evaluation (IMR) SHALL NOT mutate same-cycle `semantic_core`.

6. **HLR-20.012-006 (Cold-path SRP):** Semantic Routing Planner (SRP) compilation SHALL run off the per-turn hot path. Hot-path realization planning SHALL perform table lookup only against published `routing_epoch_id` tables.

7. **HLR-20.012-007 (Routing determinism):** All routing and planning decisions (basin routing, trigger detection, OpBeh/OBG/XlateR resolution) SHALL be deterministic functions of explicit state, published tables, and policy — not latent inference or hidden state.

8. **HLR-20.012-008 (Identity/behavior/mapping validity):** Every realized triple `(opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id)` SHALL be validated against registry definitions and an invalid-combination matrix before commit. Rejections SHALL emit fixed audit reason codes.

9. **HLR-20.012-009 (Bounded IMR):** Interpretation Mismatch Routine (IMR) feedback SHALL be trigger-only. IMR SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`. IMR SHALL enforce bounded correction depth, deduplication, and cooldown per cycle/conversation.

10. **HLR-20.012-010 (Seed boundary):** Pseudo-random seed variability SHALL affect expression/realization selection only. Seed SHALL NOT influence `semantic_core`, basin routing, Thought Router derivation, or SRP table lookup.

11. **HLR-20.012-011 (Replay equivalence):** Stripping `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` from any trace SHALL yield a `semantic_core` replay identical to Pipeline A (meaning-construction-only) replay for the same inputs, MTP state, and seed.

12. **HLR-20.012-012 (Trace reproducibility):** Full TS traces SHALL be reproducible from initial input, MTP state, seed, and `routing_epoch_id`. All material decisions SHALL be logged with deterministic ordering and rationale codes.

13. **HLR-20.012-013 (Epoch coherence):** All execution-layer fields SHALL carry `routing_epoch_id`. Stale-epoch consumption SHALL reject or apply deterministic fallback with audit evidence — never silent mixed-era routing.

14. **HLR-20.012-014 (Cross-pipeline visibility):** Pipeline B SHALL read Pipeline A state read-only. Pipeline B SHALL write only `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` (and append-only supervisory triggers derived from IMR). Pipeline A SHALL consume IMR correction signals only through explicit, bounded, auditable trigger pathways.

15. **HLR-20.012-015 (Namespace discipline):** Execution-layer symbols (OpBeh, OBG, XlateR, TrigRB, SRP, IMR) SHALL NOT alias meaning-layer basin symbols (Object Basin `OB`, Thought Router `TR`, Relational Basin `RB`).

## Verification Focus

- Envelope write-guard violations detected in replay diff
- Replay equivalence test (HLR-20.012-011) passes for all golden scenarios
- SRP hot-path execution absence in per-turn traces
- IMR oscillation bounds under mismatch injection
- Invalid triple rejection audit completeness
