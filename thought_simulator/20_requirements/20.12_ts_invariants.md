# 20.12 TS Invariants

**Document ID:** 20.12  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Draft — canonical invariant registry

## Purpose

Defines the **canonical TS invariants** that MUST hold across all deployments — dual-pipeline (current) or unified (conditional future). Other 20-series modules SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken these invariants.

## State Envelopes

TS state is logically partitioned:

| Envelope | Contents (summary) | Writers |
|----------|-------------------|---------|
| `semantic_core` | Propositions, stance, goals, ΔH%, Thought Router fields | Pipeline A / Phase 1 only |
| `exec_plan` | `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, `routing_epoch_id`, planning refs | Pipeline B planning only |
| `exec_trace` | `exec_trigger_id`, OuB artifact refs, IMR records | Pipeline B expression/feedback only |
| `supervisory` | GB markers, policy logs, correction queues | GB at safe boundaries |

## Normative Requirements

1. **HLR-20.012-001 (Write-authority partition):** `semantic_core`, `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`, and `supervisory` writers SHALL be disjoint. No module SHALL write outside its designated envelope authority.

2. **HLR-20.012-002 (Meaning/realization separation):** Meaning construction and execution/realization SHALL remain **logically separated** regardless of deployment topology (dual-pipeline or unified). Realization SHALL NOT silently overwrite meaning.

3. **HLR-20.012-003 (semantic_core immutability by realization):** Pipeline B (or unified realization phases) SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`, stance, goals, epistemic structure, or MTP meaning fields.

4. **HLR-20.012-004 (exec_plan / exec_trace separation):** `exec_plan` (planning decisions) and `exec_trace` (expression artifacts and IMR records) SHALL remain distinct envelopes with distinct write authorities.

5. **HLR-20.012-005 (Phase immutability):** Within a cycle, interpretation/semantic construction SHALL complete before realization planning; realization planning SHALL complete before expression; expression evaluation (IMR) SHALL NOT mutate same-cycle `semantic_core`.

6. **HLR-20.012-006 (Cold-path SRP):** Semantic Routing Planner (SRP) compilation SHALL run off the per-turn hot path. Hot-path realization planning SHALL perform table lookup only against published `routing_epoch_id` tables.

7. **HLR-20.012-007 (Routing determinism):** All routing and planning decisions (basin routing, trigger detection, OpBeh/OBG/XlateR resolution) SHALL be deterministic functions of explicit state, published tables, and policy — not latent inference or hidden state.

8. **HLR‑20.12‑022 (Structural‑signal routing inputs):**  
   The TS SHALL restrict routing‑relevant inputs to the fields defined by the Structural Signal Generator (20.47), including structural signature, lineage, ΔH%, routing metadata, and TR state. No other fields SHALL influence routing decisions.

   The routing equation is:

   $$
   RB\_route(TP) = f(TP.input\_fields,\ TP.TR,\ TP.tr\_needs\_update,\ TP.\Delta H,\ TP.lineage,\ TP.routing\_metadata,\ policy\_signature)
   $$

   All other fields — including seed, supervisory signals, and Pipeline B artifacts — are architecturally excluded from routing.

9. **HLR‑20.12‑023 (Pipeline A/B boundary discipline):**  
   The TS SHALL enforce strict Pipeline A/B separation as defined by the Structural Signal Generator (20.47), ensuring that no Pipeline B module may influence routing, TR updates, or structural‑transition eligibility.

   Pipeline A includes OB, RB, and TR.  
   Pipeline B includes IMR, OBG, SRP, and supervisory modules.  
   Pipeline B artifacts do not participate in routing and cannot modify routing‑relevant fields.

10. **HLR‑20.12‑024 (Canonical ordering):**  
    The TS SHALL apply canonical ordering to all nested maps, ID arrays, and exported routing structures to ensure deterministic replay, stable serialization, and consistent structural comparison across executions.

    Canonical ordering guarantees that identical inputs produce identical exported structures, independent of timing, scheduling, or core assignment.

11. **HLR‑20.12‑025 (Multi‑core routing isolation):**  
    The TS SHALL enforce multi‑core routing isolation, ensuring that no cross‑core merges, splits, consolidations, or routing‑metadata interactions occur.

    Each core operates as an independent routing domain.  
    Core identity is routing metadata only and carries no semantic meaning.

12. **HLR‑20.12‑026 (TR‑gating determinism):**  
    The TS SHALL enforce the TR‑gating contract, ensuring that TR updates occur only when `tr_needs_update = true`, and that no other module may write or modify TR state.

    The gating condition is:

    $$
    TR\_update(TP) \text{ occurs iff } TP.tr\_needs\_update = \text{true}
    $$

    This prevents semantic drift and ensures deterministic routing semantics.

13. **HLR‑20.12‑027 (Messy‑input invariance):**  
    The TS SHALL treat messy inputs as read‑only artifacts and SHALL NOT smooth, resolve, infer, or reinterpret missing or noisy fields during routing or semantic update.

    Messy inputs do not alter routing semantics, do not override TR‑gating behavior, and do not participate in structural‑transition logic.

14.  **HLR-20.012-008 (Identity/behavior/mapping validity):** Every realized triple `(opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id)` SHALL be validated against registry definitions and an invalid-combination matrix before commit. Rejections SHALL emit fixed audit reason codes.

15. **HLR-20.012-009 (Bounded IMR):** Interpretation Mismatch Routine (IMR) feedback SHALL be trigger-only. IMR SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`. IMR SHALL enforce bounded correction depth, deduplication, and cooldown per cycle/conversation.

16. **HLR-20.012-010 (Seed boundary):** Pseudo-random seed variability SHALL affect expression/realization selection only. Seed SHALL NOT influence `semantic_core`, basin routing, Thought Router derivation, or SRP table lookup.

17. **HLR-20.012-011 (Replay equivalence):** Stripping `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` from any trace SHALL yield a `semantic_core` replay identical to Pipeline A (meaning-construction-only) replay for the same inputs, MTP state, and seed.

18. **HLR-20.012-012 (Trace reproducibility):** Full TS traces SHALL be reproducible from initial input, MTP state, seed, and `routing_epoch_id`. All material decisions SHALL be logged with deterministic ordering and rationale codes.

19. **HLR-20.012-013 (Epoch coherence):** All execution-layer fields SHALL carry `routing_epoch_id`. Stale-epoch consumption SHALL reject or apply deterministic fallback with audit evidence — never silent mixed-era routing.

20. **HLR-20.012-014 (Cross-pipeline visibility):** Pipeline B SHALL read Pipeline A state read-only. Pipeline B SHALL write only `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` (and append-only supervisory triggers derived from IMR). Pipeline A SHALL consume IMR correction signals only through explicit, bounded, auditable trigger pathways.

21. **HLR-20.012-015 (Namespace discipline):** Execution-layer symbols (OpBeh, OBG, XlateR, TrigRB, SRP, IMR) SHALL NOT alias meaning-layer basin symbols (Object Basin `OB`, Thought Router `TR`, Relational Basin `RB`).

22. ### HLR‑20.012‑016 (ISc replay‑equivalence)
ISc scoring, including entropy, confidence, distributions, and rationale codes, SHALL be fully replay‑equivalent under fixed seed, TP(N), and candidate set. ISc SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic variation in meaning‑construction replay.
(Reference: 20.44 §9.5, §9.9)

23. ### HLR‑20.012‑017 (Coprocessor replay‑equivalence)
All COP‑refined distributions SHALL be deterministic functions of the proposal, seed, and execution signature. Stripping COP involvement SHALL yield a meaning‑construction replay identical to the ISc‑only replay for the same inputs and seed.
(Reference: 20.44 §9.5, §9.9; 20.34)

24. ### HLR‑20.012‑018 (TPU sole‑writer invariant)
TPU SHALL be the only subsystem permitted to mutate authoritative TP state (TP(N+1) and MTP).
Lane‑local TP instances MAY be mutated by basins during Pipeline A, but SHALL NOT constitute authoritative TP state.
(Reference: 20.46 HLR‑20.046‑001/003/004/005)

25. ## **HLR‑20.012‑019 (TPU deterministic commit boundary)**  
TPU commits SHALL occur only at deterministic GB‑approved safe boundaries.  
TPU SHALL apply updates atomically and SHALL NOT partially mutate TP.  
(Reference: 20.46 HLR‑20.046‑005/008)

26. ## **HLR‑20.012‑020 (Immutable update envelopes)**  
`tp_update_request` objects SHALL be immutable during validation and commit.  
TPU SHALL perform structural validation only and SHALL NOT interpret semantic content.  
(Reference: 20.46 HLR‑20.046‑011/013)

27. ## **HLR‑20.012‑021 (Deterministic fallback)**  
On any validation failure, TPU SHALL produce deterministic fallback behavior (retain TP(N) + audit) with no partial mutation.  
(Reference: 20.46 HLR‑20.046‑012)

## Verification Focus

- Envelope write-guard violations detected in replay diff
- Replay equivalence test (HLR-20.012-011) passes for all golden scenarios
- SRP hot-path execution absence in per-turn traces
- IMR oscillation bounds under mismatch injection
- Invalid triple rejection audit completeness
