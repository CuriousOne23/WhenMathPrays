# 20.012 — TS Invariants  
**Document ID:** 20.012  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026‑06‑06  
**Status:** Canonical Invariant Registry  

---

## 1. Purpose  
This module defines the **canonical TS invariants** that govern all meaning‑layer and realization‑layer behavior across all deployments.  
These invariants apply to both dual‑pipeline and unified topologies and define the global correctness conditions that all 20‑series modules must satisfy.

---

## 2. Scope  
This document governs:  
- meaning‑layer invariants  
- continuity and lineage invariants  
- realization‑layer invariants  
- state‑envelope invariants  
- cross‑pipeline ordering invariants  
- global stability and correctness invariants  

It does **not** define primitive‑specific behavior (see 20.112–20.169).

---

## 3. State Envelopes (informative)  
TS state is partitioned into four logical envelopes:

| Envelope        | Contents (summary)                                           | Writers                         |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| `semantic_core` | propositions, stance, goals, ΔH%, Thought Router fields       | Pipeline A only                 |
| `exec_plan`     | opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id, routing_epoch_id, plan metadata | RPlan (Pipeline B planning)     |
| `exec_trace`    | exec_trigger_id, OuB artifact refs, IMR records              | RPU / OuBB (Pipeline B output)  |
| `supervisory`   | GB markers, policy logs, correction queues                   | GB at safe boundaries           |

These envelopes define the global separation of meaning, planning, expression, and governance.

---

## 4. Invariant Definitions (informative)

### 4.1 Meaning‑Layer Stability  
Meaning construction must complete before realization begins.  
Meaning‑layer output must be lineage‑stable before entering CoHI.

### 4.2 Continuity‑Stitch Stability  
Continuity integration must preserve all continuity_fields and continuity‑stitch maps.

### 4.3 Lineage‑Stability  
Lineage integration must preserve lineage_fields and referent‑stability sets.

### 4.4 Realization‑Plan Fidelity  
Realization plans must reflect the meaning‑layer intent and continuity/lineage constraints.

### 4.5 Realization‑Update Fidelity  
Realization updates must preserve the realization plan and not introduce semantic mutation.

### 4.6 Provenance‑Chain Integrity  
All artifacts must carry a complete, ordered provenance_chain.

### 4.7 Execution‑Phase Ordering  
Interpretation → Planning → Expression must occur in strict order.

### 4.8 Deterministic Composition  
All transformations must be deterministic and replay‑equivalent.

### 4.9 No TP Access  
Realization‑layer primitives must not access TP or semantic_core.

## 5. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)

### 5.1 Envelope and Authority Requirements  
- **HLR‑20.012‑001** — Writers of `semantic_core`, `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`, and `supervisory` SHALL be disjoint.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑002** — No module SHALL write outside its designated envelope authority.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑003** — Pipeline B SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`, stance, goals, epistemic structure, or MTP meaning fields.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑004** — `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` SHALL remain distinct envelopes with distinct write authorities.

### 5.2 Meaning‑Layer and Realization‑Layer Separation  
- **HLR‑20.012‑005** — Meaning construction SHALL complete before realization planning begins.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑006** — Realization planning SHALL complete before expression begins.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑007** — Expression evaluation (IMR) SHALL NOT mutate same‑cycle `semantic_core`.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑008** — Realization SHALL NOT silently overwrite meaning.

### 5.3 Continuity and Lineage Invariants  
- **HLR‑20.012‑009** — Continuity_fields SHALL remain consistent across all continuity integrations.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑010** — Continuity‑stitch maps SHALL remain stable across CoHI operations.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑011** — Lineage_fields SHALL remain consistent across all lineage integrations.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑012** — Referent‑stability sets SHALL remain stable across LI operations.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑013** — Meaning‑layer output entering CoHI SHALL be lineage‑stable.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑014** — Lineage‑stable output entering LI SHALL be referent‑stable.

### 5.4 Realization‑Layer Invariants  
- **HLR‑20.012‑015** — Realization plans SHALL reflect meaning‑layer intent and continuity/lineage constraints.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑016** — Realization updates SHALL preserve realization‑plan fidelity.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑017** — Realization updates SHALL NOT introduce semantic mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑018** — Realization updates SHALL NOT introduce style or tone mutation.

### 5.5 Provenance and Ordering  
- **HLR‑20.012‑019** — All artifacts SHALL carry a complete, ordered provenance_chain.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑020** — No primitive SHALL bypass required upstream primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑021** — Execution phases SHALL occur in strict interpretation → planning → expression order.

### 5.6 Determinism and Replay  
- **HLR‑20.012‑022** — All transformations SHALL be deterministic.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑023** — All transformations SHALL be replay‑equivalent.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑024** — Hot‑path planning SHALL perform table lookup only against published routing_epoch_id tables.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑025** — SRP compilation SHALL run off the per‑turn hot path.

### 5.7 Global Constraints  
- **HLR‑20.012‑026** — Realization‑layer primitives SHALL NOT access TP or semantic_core.  
- **HLR‑20.012‑027** — Historical naming aliases SHALL NOT alter canonical invariant semantics.
