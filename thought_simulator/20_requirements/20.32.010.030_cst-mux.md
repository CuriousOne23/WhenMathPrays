# **20.32.010.030 — CST‑Mux Requirements**  
**Context Stability Tracking — Stability Signal Multiplexing Module**

---

## **0. Document Purpose (Informative)**  
This document defines the normative requirements for **CST‑Mux**, the Stability Signal Multiplexing Module in the CST pipeline. CST‑Mux receives synthesized stability signals from CST‑MS and packages them into the **Unified Stability Packet (USP)**. CST‑Mux does not modify stability signals; it organizes, aligns, indexes, and multiplexes them into a deterministic structure consumed by COB and CIL.  

CST‑Mux is a pure functional module:

- deterministic  
- replay‑safe  
- monotonic threshold behavior  
- no randomness  
- no external state  
- no wall‑clock time  

---

# **1. Inputs and Outputs**

## **1.1 Inputs (Informative)**  
CST‑Mux receives synthesized signals from CST‑MS for each identity layer *L* at turn *t*:  
- stability summary  
- instability summary  
- collapse risk  
- freeze risk  
- thaw readiness  
- ambiguity summary  
- drift summary  
- oscillation summary  

It also receives layer ordering, activation state, freeze/thaw state, and continuity restoration state.  

## **1.2 Input Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑001**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept all synthesized CST‑MS signals for each identity layer.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑002**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept deterministic layer ordering.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑003**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept layer activation, freeze, thaw, and continuity restoration states.

---

## **1.3 Outputs (Informative)**  
CST‑Mux produces the **Unified Stability Packet (USP)**, containing:  
- layer‑indexed stability signals  
- layer‑indexed instability signals  
- collapse/freeze/thaw signals  
- ambiguity/drift/oscillation summaries  
- activation flags  
- freeze/thaw flags  
- continuity flags  

USP is consumed by COB and CIL.  

## **1.4 Output Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑004**  
CST‑Mux SHALL produce a deterministic Unified Stability Packet (USP) for each turn *t*.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑005**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include all layer‑indexed stability and instability signals in USP.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑006**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include collapse, freeze, thaw, ambiguity, drift, and oscillation summaries in USP.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑007**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include activation, freeze, thaw, and continuity flags in USP.

---

# **2. Layer Indexing**

## **2.1 Layer Indexing (Informative)**  
CST‑Mux assigns each identity layer a deterministic index:

$$
L \in \\{0,1,2,3,4\\}
$$  

Typical layers include referent, temporal, discourse, lineage, and register.  

## **2.2 Layer Indexing Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑008**  
CST‑Mux SHALL assign each identity layer a deterministic index.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑009**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure layer indexing is stable across replay.

---

# **3. Signal Alignment**

## **3.1 Alignment (Informative)**  
CST‑Mux aligns all synthesized signals by layer:  

$$
\text{USP}(L,t) = (S, U, R_{\text{coll}}, R_{\text{freeze}}, R_{\text{thaw}}, A_{\text{sum}}, D_{\text{sum}}, O_{\text{sum}})
$$  

This ensures deterministic ordering and replay consistency.  

## **3.2 Alignment Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑010**  
CST‑Mux SHALL align all synthesized signals by identity layer.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑011**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure aligned signals maintain deterministic ordering.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑012**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure aligned signals are replay‑safe.

---

# **4. Activation Flags**

## **4.1 Activation (Informative)**  
Activation flag:  

$$
\text{active}(L,t) = 1[S(L,t) > \theta_{\text{active}}(L)]
$$  

Active layers participate in identity evolution; inactive layers are suppressed.  

## **4.2 Activation Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑013**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute activation flags using deterministic activation thresholds.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑014**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include activation flags in USP.

---

# **5. Freeze and Thaw Flags**

## **5.1 Freeze/Thaw (Informative)**  
Freeze flag:  

$$
\text{freeze}(L,t) = 1[R_{\text{freeze}}(L,t) > \theta_{\text{freeze}}(L)]
$$  

Thaw flag:  

$$
\text{thaw}(L,t) = 1[R_{\text{thaw}}(L,t) > \theta_{\text{thaw}}(L)]
$$  

Freeze/thaw flags determine whether COB or CIL may evolve or integrate a layer.

## **5.2 Freeze/Thaw Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑015**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute freeze flags using deterministic freeze thresholds.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑016**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute thaw flags using deterministic thaw thresholds.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑017**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include freeze and thaw flags in USP.

---

# **6. Continuity Flags**

## **6.1 Continuity (Informative)**  
Continuity flag:  

$$
\text{cont}(L,t) = 1[S(L,t) > \theta_{\text{cont}}(L)]
$$  

Indicates whether a layer is stable enough for thaw, identity evolution, or packet integration.  

## **6.2 Continuity Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑018**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute continuity flags using deterministic continuity thresholds.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑019**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include continuity flags in USP.

---

# **7. USP Construction**

## **7.1 USP (Informative)**  
USP is constructed as:  

$$
\text{USP}(t) = \\{(L,\ \text{USP}(L,t),\ \text{active},\ \text{freeze},\ \text{thaw},\ \text{cont})\\}_{L=0}^{4}
$$  

USP is deterministic, replay‑safe, layer‑indexed, and complete.  

## **7.2 USP Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑020**  
CST‑Mux SHALL construct USP as a deterministic, layer‑indexed packet.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑021**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include all aligned signals and flags in USP.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑022**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure USP is replay‑safe.

---

# **8. Determinism and Replay**

## **8.1 Determinism (Informative)**  
CST‑Mux is fully deterministic: pure functional multiplexing, no randomness, no external state, monotonic thresholds, replay‑safe behavior.  
Replay reconstructs all indexing, alignment, flags, and USP structure.  

## **8.2 Determinism Requirements (Normative)**  
**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑023**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute all outputs as pure functions of CST‑MS inputs and layer states.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑024**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure all threshold comparisons are deterministic and monotonic.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑025**  
CST‑Mux SHALL guarantee replay‑safe behavior for all outputs.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑026**  
CST‑Mux SHALL produce identical USP outputs under replay for identical inputs.
