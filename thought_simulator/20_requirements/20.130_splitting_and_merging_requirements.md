# 20.130 Splitting and Merging Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.130-001: Split operations SHALL preserve parent lineage and branch reason metadata.
2. HLR-20.130-002: Merge operations SHALL preserve deterministic parent reconciliation rules.
3. HLR-20.130-003: Split and merge limits per tick SHALL be configurable and enforced.
4. HLR-20.130-004: All split and merge events SHALL be logged for replay.
