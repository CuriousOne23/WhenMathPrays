# 20.130 Splitting and Merging Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic splitting and merging behavior for TP lane projection, evidence partitioning, and MTP recombination within the TS cycle. Execution of all split and merge operations defined in this module is normatively delegated to the Topology Executor (TE) defined in 20.131.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively defines splitting and merging obligations from 20.10 and 20.30; execution of these obligations is performed exclusively by TE (20.131) after RB arbitration.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.130-001: Split operations SHALL project a source TP into deterministic lane-specific outputs using explicit rules and SHALL preserve parent lineage metadata.
2. HLR-20.130-002: Merge operations SHALL recombine lane outputs into deterministic MTP state using explicit field-wise reconciliation rules.
3. HLR-20.130-003: Split and merge operations SHALL remain bounded by configurable per-tick limits and SHALL be enforced deterministically.
4. HLR-20.130-004: All split and merge events SHALL be logged by TE using deterministic lineage, reason, and sequence metadata.
5. HLR-20.130-005: Split operations SHALL preserve source TP identity, tp_seq ordering, and causal lineage markers in all derived lane outputs.
6. HLR-20.130-006: Split operations SHALL NOT introduce inference, hallucinated reconstruction, or meaning invention beyond the explicit source fields.
7. HLR-20.130-007: Split rules SHALL be versioned and SHALL remain reproducible for identical input state, profile state, and seed-bound system state.
8. HLR-20.130-008: Merge operations SHALL preserve deterministic parent reconciliation, including conflict resolution, field precedence, and explicit missing-field handling.
9. HLR-20.130-009: Merge operations SHALL preserve audit lineage from all contributing lane outputs and SHALL record contribution provenance.
10. HLR-20.130-010: Split and merge operations SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with active unsafeguarded TS state changes. Execution of split and merge operations SHALL be performed solely by TE as defined in 20.131; OB and RB SHALL NOT execute structural transitions. Safe-boundary execution is enforced by TE and SHALL follow the OB → RB → TE → RB pipeline ordering.
11. HLR-20.130-011: Split and merge operations SHALL not mutate finalized prior-state entries and SHALL instead create new deterministic state records. TE SHALL create new deterministic state records and SHALL NOT mutate finalized prior-state entries.
12. HLR-20.130-012: Split and merge limits per cycle SHALL include deterministic lane count, payload size, and evidence-depth bounds.
13. HLR-20.130-013: When split or merge limits are exceeded, TS SHALL apply deterministic reject, deferral, or bounded degradation behavior according to policy.
14. HLR-20.130-014: Split and merge operations SHALL preserve FIFO ordering and SHALL not reorder, skip, or speculate on sequence state.
15. HLR-20.130-015: Split output fields SHALL include visible semantics, suppressed semantics, derived evidence markers, and ΔH% contribution markers where applicable.
16. HLR-20.130-016: Merge output fields SHALL include combined evidence, remaining missing mass markers, delta-H-percent trajectory fields, and stability markers.
17. HLR-20.130-017: Split and merge behavior SHALL be deterministic and replayable for identical effective state. Deterministic execution is implemented by TE and SHALL be reproducible for identical effective input state.
18. HLR-20.130-018: Split and merge behavior SHALL be auditable, append-only, and time-indexed in the TP via TE‑produced topology_event_log entries.
19. HLR-20.130-019: Split and merge operations SHALL use explicit reason codes and policy signatures for each event.
20. HLR-20.130-020: Split and merge operations SHALL remain platform-independent and SHALL not depend on hardware- or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
21. HLR-20.130-021: Split and merge interfaces SHALL be explicit, versioned, testable, and consistent with MTP schema requirements.
22. HLR-20.130-022: Split and merge operations SHALL be consistent with GB supervisory constraints and SHALL not bypass approved supervisory pathways.
23. HLR-20.130-023: Split and merge transitions SHALL preserve deterministic traceability from source TP, through lane outputs, to recombined MTP state.
24. HLR-20.130-024: Split and merge artifacts SHALL remain bounded and SHALL not require unbounded scanning or inference to validate correctness.
25. HLR-20.130-025: Split and merge behavior SHALL be fully testable via deterministic fixtures covering nominal, boundary, overflow, and reject paths.
26. HLR-20.130-026: Splitting and merging requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

## Functional Boundaries
- Splitting: Defines deterministic projection rules; TE executes these rules.
- Merging: Defines deterministic recombination rules; TE executes these rules.
- OB: Detects identity conflict/complementarity; issues SplitRequest/MergeRequest.
- RB: Arbitrates requests; does not execute.
- TE: Executes topology transitions defined in this module (see 20.131).
- GB: Owns supervisory approvals for any split or merge path that affects global behavior.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic split projection for identical source state and profile state.
- Deterministic merge reconciliation for identical lane inputs and ordering state.
- Deterministic lineage preservation through split and merge cycles.
- Deterministic safe-boundary enforcement and append-only TP replay evidence.
- Deterministic TE execution of split and merge rules defined in this module.
