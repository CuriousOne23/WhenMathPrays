# **20.155_trsch_prm.md**  
# **TrSch‑prm (Turn Scheduler Primitive)**

---

## **1. Introduction**  
TrSch‑prm is the turn‑level sequencing primitive responsible for coordinating all Path‑A primitives after IdOB‑set consolidation. It interprets the control flags emitted by IdOB‑prm (via CTP‑prm) and selects the next architectural action in a deterministic, replay‑safe manner. TrSch‑prm does not modify TP, semantic_core, routing_metadata, or meaning fields. It performs no semantic evaluation, no routing computation, no correction, and no commit. TrSch‑prm is strictly a scheduler and control‑flow governor, ensuring that all downstream primitives operate in the correct order and under the correct conditions.

TrSch‑prm is an internal TS‑level scheduling primitive and is not part of the user‑facing conceptual Path‑A flow. User‑facing diagrams do not depict TrSch‑prm, and TP does not expose or indicate its presence to the user. TrSch‑prm appears only in TS mechanical execution diagrams, where it governs turn‑level control flow without participating in meaning refinement, routing semantics, correction semantics, or commit semantics.

---

## **2. Purpose**  
The purpose of TrSch‑prm is to enforce architectural purity by separating meaning refinement (IdOB‑prm), routing computation (RB‑prm), routing commit (RTU‑prm), correction (CE‑prm → TPU‑prm), and commit (OuBA‑prm). TrSch‑prm ensures that IdOB‑prm never routes itself, that RB‑prm never interprets meaning flags, and that OuBA‑prm is invoked only when meaning is stable and correction‑complete. TrSch‑prm is the sole arbiter of turn‑level control flow and the central enforcement point for A/B separation and single‑writer invariants.

---

## **3. Input Specification**  
TrSch‑prm consumes a single consolidated IdOB‑output packet produced by CTP‑prm. This packet contains:

- identity‑conditioned meaning refinements  
- stability indicators  
- correction indicators  
- completion indicators  
- IdOB‑prm control flags  
- structural metadata required for turn‑level decision making  

TrSch‑prm does not consume raw TP, semantic_core, routing_metadata, or intermediate OB/IdOB fragments. It operates exclusively on the consolidated packet produced by CTP‑prm.

---

## **4. Responsibility / Transfer Function**  
This section contains all normative SHALL statements for TrSch‑prm.  
Requirement identifiers follow the format **HLR‑20.155‑nnn**.

### **4.1 Turn‑Level Control Interpretation**

**HLR‑20.155‑001:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** interpret the control flags contained in the consolidated IdOB‑output packet produced by CTP‑prm.

**HLR‑20.155‑002:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** be the sole primitive authorized to interpret IdOB‑prm’s control flags.

**HLR‑20.155‑003:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** determine whether the next architectural action is RB‑prm, CE‑prm, OuBA‑prm, or Truth/Done.

### **4.2 Routing‑Cycle Continuation**

**HLR‑20.155‑004:**  
If the IdOB‑output packet indicates `continue`, TrSch‑prm **SHALL** schedule the next RB‑prm → RTU‑prm → IdOB‑prm cycle.

### **4.3 Correction Invocation**

**HLR‑20.155‑005:**  
If the IdOB‑output packet indicates `needs_correction`, TrSch‑prm **SHALL** schedule CE‑prm followed by TPU‑prm before resuming Path‑A.

### **4.4 Commit Invocation**

**HLR‑20.155‑006:**  
If the IdOB‑output packet indicates `done`, TrSch‑prm **SHALL** schedule OuBA‑prm as the next primitive.

### **4.5 Turn Finalization**

**HLR‑20.155‑007:**  
After OuBA‑prm completes, TrSch‑prm **SHALL** schedule Truth/Done as the final evaluator for the turn.

### **4.6 Prohibited Actions**

**HLR‑20.155‑008:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL NOT** modify TP, semantic_core, routing_metadata, or meaning fields.

**HLR‑20.155‑009:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL NOT** perform routing computation, semantic evaluation, correction, or commit.

**HLR‑20.155‑010:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL NOT** be invoked during RB‑prm, RTU‑prm, IdOB‑prm, or OuBA‑prm execution.

---

## **5. Output Specification**  
This section defines the normative outputs of TrSch‑prm.

**HLR‑20.155‑011:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** emit a single scheduling directive identifying the next primitive to execute.

**HLR‑20.155‑012:**  
The scheduling directive **SHALL** be one of the following:  
- `RB`  
- `CE`  
- `OuBA`  
- `TruthDone`  

**HLR‑20.155‑013:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** ensure that the scheduling directive is deterministic with respect to the IdOB‑output packet and replay‑safe across executions.

**HLR‑20.155‑014:**  
TrSch‑prm **SHALL** provide a stable, unambiguous transfer of control to the selected primitive.

---

## **6. Examples**

### **Example 1: Continue Routing Cycle**  
CTP‑prm consolidates IdOB₁…IdOBₙ into a single packet. The packet contains the `continue` flag. TrSch‑prm schedules:

$$
\text{RB} \rightarrow \text{RTU} \rightarrow \text{IdOB}
$$

### **Example 2: Correction Required**  
IdOB‑prm emits `needs_correction`. TrSch‑prm schedules:

$$
\text{CE} \rightarrow \text{TPU} \rightarrow \text{RB}
$$

### **Example 3: Meaning Stable**  
IdOB‑prm emits `done`. TrSch‑prm schedules:

$$
\text{OuBA} \rightarrow \text{Truth/Done}
$$

### **Example 4: Multi‑Core Consolidation**  
In a multi‑core system, IdOB₁…IdOBₙ run in parallel. CTP‑prm merges their outputs. TrSch‑prm reads the unified packet and schedules the next primitive based on the consolidated flags.
