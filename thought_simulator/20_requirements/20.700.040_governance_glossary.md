## Entries

### TPTB (Thought Packet Truth Boundary) — 20.64
**Purpose:** Encodes the truth-evaluation results produced by TB over the frozen Path A TP snapshot into a compact, auditable metadata structure that Path B reads at intake.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, read-only truth metadata: factual alignment, contradiction detection, evidential basis, stance coherence, referential consistency, and explanation codes.  
**When:** Produced after OuBA freezes the TP snapshot and after TB interprets that snapshot, during A-cycle finalization. Delivered to Path B before LI.  
**Why:** Allows Path B to express truth-aware behavior without invoking TB, performing inference, or accessing global truth state.  
**Is not:** A truth engine; a basin; a semantic interpreter; a scorer; a routing mechanism; a safety evaluator; a global truth model; or a Path B computation. TPTB never modifies TP and performs no evaluation itself.  
**Example:** TB detects a factual contradiction; TPTB encodes truth_conflict=true, conflict_type=contradiction. Path B later reads these tags to generate a corrective or qualified response.  
**Normative home:** 20.64.

### TPSF (Thought Packet Safety Flags) — 20.66
**Purpose:** Encodes the safety-evaluation results produced by A-side safety basins over the frozen Path A TP snapshot into a compact, auditable metadata structure that Path B reads at intake.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, read-only safety metadata: risk presence, risk category, severity, rationale codes, and policy hooks.  
**When:** Produced after OuBA freezes the TP snapshot and after A-side safety evaluation, during A-cycle finalization. Delivered to Path B before LI.  
**Why:** Allows Path B to express safety-aware behavior without invoking safety basins, performing inference, or accessing global safety state.  
**Is not:** A safety engine; a basin; a semantic interpreter; a scorer; a routing mechanism; a truth evaluator; a global safety model; or a Path B computation. TPSF never modifies TP and performs no evaluation itself.  
**Example:** A-side safety basins detect self-harm risk; TPSF encodes safety_risk=true, risk_type=self_harm, severity=high. Path B later reads these flags to refuse or redirect.  
**Normative home:** 20.66.

### GBIB (Governance Basin Input Buffer)
**Path:** Governance  
**Purpose:** Applies governance rules, USP constraints, truth-tags, safety-flags, and MTP-commit boundaries to filter or escalate proposals from IB and TB.  
**Focus:** Deterministic filtering and integration of inquiry, truth, and safety signals before final GB evaluation.  
**When:** After TB and IB in the Post-TS chain.  
**Why:** Prevent IB or TB from making unsupervised decisions; ensure all governance outcomes are policy-aligned.  
**Is not:** A meaning writer; a truth evaluator; a safety engine.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### GB (Governing Basin) — 20.80
**Path:** Governance  
**Purpose:** Perform final governance-side evaluation within D-Flow by consuming gbib_directives{} and determining whether committed meaning warrants promotion into Path-A (IdOB) or emission of governance inference into Path-B.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, rule-bound global evaluation of inquiry-truth alignment, governance-policy applicability, USP constraints, and safety-relevant governance rules; produce governance_outcome{} and log governance events.  
**When:** Post-OuBA commit, only within DF: IB → TB → GBIB → GB.  
**Why:** Provide a stable, auditable governance authority preventing IB or TB from making final decisions.  
**Is not:** Path-A primitive; Path-B primitive; semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; TP writer (except governance logs).  
**Example:** GBIB emits gbib_directives{ escalate_governance_review }; GB evaluates governance policy and USP constraints, logs the governance event, and either promotes the object to IdOB or emits governance inference to REx.  
**Normative home:** 20.80 (GB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.110 (OuBB), 20.60 (TB), 20.90 (IB).

### Clarification Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Determines when the system may issue a clarification request instead of producing a direct answer.  
**Focus:** Clarification allowed only when ambiguity is material, user-intent cannot be safely inferred, and governance permits inquiry.  
**When:** Evaluated during TB→IB→GBIB→GB after Path B realization.  
**Why:** Prevents unnecessary or unsafe clarifications and ensures all clarifications are governance-authorized.  
**Is not:** A semantic primitive; a meaning writer; an IMR mechanism; an automatic IB trigger.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### Suppression Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Enforces conditions under which clarification, interrogation, or inference proposals must be suppressed.  
**Focus:** Suppression triggered when proposals violate USP, conflict with truth/safety tags, exceed allowed inquiry depth, or would cause user-intent distortion.  
**When:** Applied in GBIB and finalized in GB.  
**Why:** Ensures the system does not escalate inquiry or inference beyond what governance permits.  
**Is not:** A routing rule; a Path A/B primitive; an IMR mismatch classification.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### Truth–Safety Precedence Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Establishes deterministic precedence between truth-evaluation outcomes and safety-evaluation outcomes when they conflict.  
**Focus:** Safety overrides truth when user harm or policy violation is possible; truth overrides ambiguity when meaning is determinate and safe.  
**When:** Evaluated in GBIB and GB.  
**Why:** Ensures safe, truthful, policy-aligned behavior under conflict conditions.  
**Is not:** A semantic inference rule; a TP writer; a Path A/B primitive.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### USP Precedence Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Ensures that User-Specified Preferences (USP) constrain governance decisions without overriding safety or truth requirements.  
**Focus:** USP applied after safety and truth evaluation but before final governance outcome.  
**When:** Applied in GBIB and finalized in GB.  
**Why:** Ensures user preferences shape expression while remaining subordinate to safety and truth.  
**Is not:** A safety override; a truth override; a meaning-layer inference.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### Governance Outcome Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Defines how GB produces the final governance_outcome{} that controls whether the system answers, clarifies, suppresses, or redirects.  
**Focus:** GB integrates TB truth-state, IB ambiguity proposals, GBIB governance filters, USP, TPTB, TPSF, and safety constraints.  
**When:** Final step of the Post-TS governance chain.  
**Why:** Ensures all outputs are policy-aligned, safe, truthful, and consistent with user intent.  
**Is not:** A meaning writer; a realization primitive; a neural inference.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

### Replay-Determinism Rules — Governance Block
**Purpose:** Ensure governance outcomes are fully deterministic and replay-safe given identical TP/MTP commits and identical seeds.  
**Focus:** Deterministic governance evaluation across TB→IB→GBIB→GB; nondeterminism confined to Path-B expression.  
**When:** Applied throughout DF and enforced before output emission.  
**Why:** Guarantee auditability, reproducibility, and compliance with TS determinism requirements.  
**Is not:** Randomness generator; meaning writer; Path-A/B primitive.  
**Normative home:** 20.80, 20.207.
