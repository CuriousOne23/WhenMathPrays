# 20.32 COB Requirements

**Status:** Approved — Track H Stage 3 USP snapshot versioning (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok)

## Purpose
Defines deterministic Conversation Object Basin (COB) behavior for conversation-scoped discourse state built from approved canonical semantics.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes COB obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including:
- 20.10 architectural ownership of deterministic conversation-object state and lifecycle governance.
- 20.10 deterministic replay/export, profile precedence, safe-boundary activation, and auditable interface invariants.
- 20.30 functional ownership of COB identity/lineage lifecycle, compaction/replay behavior, and deterministic export/redaction/empty-artifact behavior.
- 20.30 cross-module contract obligations for explicit, versioned, testable, and auditable interfaces.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.031-001: COB entries SHALL be derived from OB semantics only.
2. HLR-20.031-002: Provisional placeholders SHALL remain non-canonical until GB-approved IB->OB promotion completes.
3. HLR-20.031-003: COB identity SHALL preserve stable ID, lineage, merge/split history, deprecation state, and lifecycle state.
4. HLR-20.031-004: COB SHALL enforce deterministic single-winner promotion behavior per placeholder lineage.
5. HLR-20.031-005: Winner-proof lineage edges SHALL remain non-prunable in immutable audit lineage.
6. HLR-20.031-006: COB compaction SHALL use deterministic anchored summary proofs.
7. HLR-20.031-007: COB compaction cadence SHALL be deterministic from sequence/epoch state.
8. HLR-20.031-008: Replay-horizon mode SHALL be explicit and deterministic (full, bounded window, summary proof).
9. HLR-20.031-009: Replay-horizon mode changes SHALL activate only at deterministic safe boundaries.
10. HLR-20.031-010: Replay evidence export SHALL be deterministic and mode-specific.
11. HLR-20.031-011: Export redaction profile SHALL be deterministic, versioned, and execution-signature-bound.
12. HLR-20.031-012: Empty-artifact behavior for zero-event windows SHALL be deterministic and policy-bound.
13. HLR-20.031-013: Export container/compression/manifest behavior SHALL be deterministic and versioned.
14. HLR-20.031-014: COB ordering-critical behavior SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state and SHALL NOT depend on wall-clock precedence.
15. HLR-20.031-015: COB replay/export manifest serialization SHALL use canonical deterministic field ordering and key sorting.
16. HLR-20.031-016: COB profile overlaps SHALL resolve by execution-signature-bound profile precedence over environment defaults.
17. HLR-20.031-017: COB policy/profile mode changes SHALL activate atomically at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL rollback to prior valid signature state on activation failure.
18. HLR-20.031-018: Unsupported COB feature/profile/enum states SHALL follow deterministic reject-with-audit behavior using fixed reason codes.
19. HLR-20.031-019: COB interfaces and data contracts SHALL be explicit, versioned, and auditable, and SHALL preserve deterministic mapping behavior across compatibility windows.
20. HLR-20.031-020: COB state transitions, including creation, promotion, deprecation, merge/split, compaction, replay mode change, and export issuance, SHALL be logged in deterministic append-only audit records.
21. HLR-20.031-021: COB processing SHALL remain isolated from non-ordering side effects such that concurrent side effects SHALL NOT influence signature-visible artifacts.
22. HLR-20.031-022: COB requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
23. HLR-20.031-023: COB SHALL NOT read or mutate OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, or MTP internal state and SHALL operate exclusively on approved canonical conversation-object semantics.
24. HLR-20.031-024: COB lifecycle transitions (creation, promotion, deprecation, merge, split, compaction, replay-mode change) SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with active TS-core cycles.
25. HLR-20.031-025: COB enum, wire-map, and schema versions SHALL remain frozen per release and SHALL reject unsupported states with deterministic audit reason codes.
26. HLR-20.031-026: COB SHALL preserve FIFO ordering inherited from CIL and SHALL NOT reorder, skip, or speculate on conversation-object sequence state.
27. HLR-20.031-027: COB SHALL pin the active `usp_version_ref` for conversation scope as `cob_snapshot_pin` on each USP version transition per [20.102](20.102_usp_requirements.md) HLR-20.102-010.
28. HLR-20.031-028: COB SHALL retain immutable `usp_version_record` lineage including `usp_version_id`, `usp_version_ref`, `prior_version_ref`, and `cob_snapshot_pin` for replay and export.
29. HLR-20.031-029: COB USP snapshot pin transitions SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with active TS-core cycles.
30. HLR-20.031-030: COB replay/export manifests SHALL include pinned `usp_version_ref` evidence when Track H profile is enabled for the active execution signature.
31. HLR-20.031-031: COB SHALL NOT read or mutate USP rule content directly; COB SHALL govern versioning pins and audit visibility only.
32. HLR-20.031-032: COB SHALL NOT assign USP read or write authority to InB, IB, IMR, IIInB, UPI, or Pipeline B subsystems.

## Track H Integration — USP Snapshot Versioning (Normative Minimum)

COB governs conversation-scoped USP snapshot pins for [20.102](20.102_usp_requirements.md) ([20.510](20.510_refactoring_for_input_correction_track_h.md) Stage 3).

```text
cob_usp_snapshot_pin {
  cob_snapshot_pin,          // stable pin ID per conversation scope
  conversation_scope_id,
  usp_version_id,            // monotonic per conversation scope
  usp_version_ref,           // content hash of canonical active-rule snapshot
  prior_version_ref?,        // lineage
  pin_transition,            // CREATE | SUPERSEDE | REVOKE
  policy_signature,
  safe_boundary_marker,
  reason_codes[]
}
```

**Linkage:** every `usp_version_record` emitted by UPI commit SHALL carry `cob_snapshot_pin` populated by COB at the safe boundary of version transition.

**Authority:** UPI writes USP rules; COB pins `usp_version_ref` for replay continuity; IIInB reads pinned snapshots read-only on subsequent turns.

## Functional Boundaries
- COB owns conversation object identity, lineage, lifecycle state, replay/export semantics, and USP snapshot version pins (`cob_snapshot_pin`).
- CIL owns TP/MTP conversation intake, thread integration, and supervisory escalation behavior.
- USP ([20.102](20.102_usp_requirements.md)) rule content is written by UPI only; COB governs pin lineage and export visibility, not rule matching or repair.
- COP is optional and propose-only; COP proposals SHALL become COB-visible only through approved deterministic supervisory/commit paths.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic lifecycle transitions for identical input/signature state.
- Deterministic replay/export behavior across all replay-horizon modes.
- Deterministic profile precedence and unsupported-state reject behavior.
- Deterministic append-only audit evidence for COB state transitions.
- USP `cob_snapshot_pin` lineage across supersede/revoke transitions; export includes `usp_version_ref` when profile enabled.
