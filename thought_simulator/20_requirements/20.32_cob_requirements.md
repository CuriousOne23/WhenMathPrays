## Purpose

COB is the Conversation Object Basin. It maintains identity-layers, referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field-importance maps, and identity-layer lineage. COB applies CST correction signals and produces the stabilized identity-layer snapshot consumed by CIL.

COB is the long-horizon identity substrate of the Path-A context layer. It provides the stable, deterministic foundation that CST measures, CIL integrates with short-term cues, and CEx extracts from for TP metadata. COB does not ingest TP/IE structural cues; only CIL consumes TP/IE cues.

---

## Position in Architecture

COB sits between CST and CIL:

CST → COB → CIL → CEx → CE → TS → COB

COB receives structured meaning output from TS (via OuBA) and correction signals from CST. It does not interact with OB, IB, RB, TB, InB, OuB.

COB is the authoritative store of long-horizon conversational identity. Structural changes to identity layers occur only through deterministic lifecycle operations triggered by CST signals or internal capacity rules.

## Normative Requirements

**HLR-20.32-001:** COB maintains identity-layers, referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field-importance maps, and identity-layer lineage.  

**HLR-20.32-002:** COB applies CST correction signals to identity-layer structures.  

**HLR-20.32-003:** COB produces the stabilized identity-layer snapshot consumed by CIL.  

**HLR-20.32-004:** COB does not interact with OB, IB, RB, TB, InB, OuB.  

**HLR-20.32-005:** COB does not perform placeholder promotion, replay/export, compaction, or redaction.  

**HLR-20.32-006:** NA. 

**HLR-20.32-007:** COB maintains determinism and replay equivalence for all identity-layer operations.  

**HLR-20.32-008:** COB does not perform semantic interpretation or canonical semantics derivation.  

**HLR-20.32-009:** COB outputs are consumed exclusively by CIL for intake packet formation.  

**HLR-20.32-010:** COB SHALL NOT read or mutate OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, or MTP internal state.  

**HLR-20.32-011:** COB lifecycle transitions SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries.  

**HLR-20.32-012:** COB maintains versioning and schema stability for all identity-layer structures.  

**HLR-20.32-013:** COB SHALL maintain a maximum of 20 identity layers.  

**HLR-20.32-014:** Each identity layer SHALL conform to the following schema:

```json
IdentityLayer {
    layer_id: StableID,                    // deterministic across replay
    referent_map: ReferentMap,
    lineage: LineageStructure,
    strength: float,                       // centrality to user
    importance: float,                     // centrality to conversation
    ambiguity: float,                      // internal referent conflict
    decay_state: float,
    register: string,                      // e.g. "formal", "casual", "technical", "east_la_lingo", "science"
    timestamps: {
        created: TurnID,
        updated: TurnID
    }
}
```

**HLR-20.32-015:** Each referent map entry SHALL conform to the following schema:

```json
ReferentEntry {
    referent_id: StableID,
    surface_forms: [string],               // supports multi-word
    attributes: { key: value },
    strength: float,
    confidence: float,
    ambiguity: float,
    lineage_pointer: StableID,
    register: string,                      // subculture or communication style
    timestamps: {
        created: TurnID,
        updated: TurnID
    }
}
```

### **HLR‑20.32‑069: Clarifying Field Capacity**  
COB SHALL maintain a bounded set of clarifying fields for each identity layer, with a maximum of **10 clarifying fields**.

### **HLR‑20.32‑070: Clarifying Subfield Capacity**  
Each clarifying field SHALL contain a bounded set of clarifying subfields, with a maximum of **100 total subfields** distributed across no more than **4 hierarchical levels**.

### **HLR‑20.32‑071: Clarifying Importance Storage**  
Each clarifying field and subfield SHALL store an importance score provided initially by Path‑A and refined by COB using long‑horizon identity continuity.

### **HLR‑20.32‑072: Clarifying Metadata Exposure**  
COB SHALL expose clarifying fields, clarifying subfields, and clarifying importance scores in the stabilized identity‑layer snapshot consumed by CIL.

---

**HLR-20.32-016:** COB SHALL ingest new meaning exclusively through OuBA packets using deterministic merge logic.  

**HLR-20.32-017:** When merging new meaning, COB SHALL apply strength-weighted averaging of the form:

```math
new_{strength} = α * old_{strength} + (1 - α) * incoming_{strength}
```

**HLR-20.32-018:** COB SHALL resolve conflicts using confidence weighting, ambiguity penalties, lineage continuity, and CST override signals.  

---

### **HLR‑20.32‑073: Clarifying Importance Refinement**  
COB SHALL refine Path‑A’s clarifying‑importance estimate using referent continuity, lineage continuity, ambiguity/certainty history, and long‑horizon identity stability.

### **HLR‑20.32‑074: Clarifying Compression**  
COB SHALL compress clarifying fields and subfields by merging redundant clarifications, collapsing repeated clarifications, and summarizing importance while preserving deterministic replay.

### **HLR‑20.32‑075: Clarifying Pruning**  
COB SHALL prune clarifying fields and subfields when capacity limits are exceeded, preserving the highest‑importance clarifications and maintaining deterministic ordering.

**HLR-20.32-019:** COB SHALL create a new identity layer only when incoming referents do not match any existing layer above the creation threshold, lineage continuity cannot be established, ambiguity exceeds threshold, CST issues a new-identity signal, or referent cluster analysis indicates a distinct identity domain.  

**HLR-20.32-020:** COB SHALL split an identity layer when ambiguity exceeds threshold, referent clusters diverge, CST issues a split signal, or lineage continuity breaks.  

**HLR-20.32-021:** COB SHALL merge identity layers when referent clusters converge, ambiguity drops below threshold, CST issues a merge signal, or decay suggests consolidation.  

**HLR-20.32-022:** When capacity is exceeded (21st identity), COB SHALL compute an eviction score of the form:

```math
eviction_{score} = w1*(low strength) + w2*(low importance) + w3*(high decay_{state}) + w4*(low recency) + w5*(high ambiguity)
```

and SHALL evict the lowest-score layer (or merge first if ambiguity is high).  

**HLR-20.32-023:** COB SHALL apply decay each turn according to:

```math
decay_{state} = decay_{state} + β * time_{since_update}
strength = strength * (1 - γ * decay_{state})
importance = importance * (1 - γ * decay_{state})
```

**HLR-20.32-024:** COB SHALL prune referents, compress attributes, and summarize lineage when referent count, ambiguity, decay_state, or lineage depth exceed defined limits.  

**HLR-20.32-025:** COB SHALL retire a layer only when decay_state exceeds retirement threshold, both strength and importance fall below minimum, CST issues a retire signal, the referent map collapses, or lineage continuity breaks irrecoverably.  

**HLR-20.32-026:** COB SHALL respond to CST signals (split, merge, weaken, strengthen, freeze, thaw, retire, ambiguity, drift, collapse) using deterministic algorithms and SHALL log all signal applications for replay.  

**HLR-20.32-027:** While frozen, COB SHALL halt merges, splits, pruning, decay, and assignment, and SHALL queue incoming updates for deterministic replay upon thaw.  

**HLR-20.32-028:** COB SHALL detect identity collapse, referent collapse, lineage collapse, and continuity collapse, and SHALL enter recovery mode by freezing, applying deterministic recovery (split/merge/prune/retire/reassign), and thawing.  

**HLR-20.32-029:** COB SHALL guarantee that identical sequences of OuBA packets and CST signals produce identical identity-layer state, including layer IDs, referent maps, lineage, strength, importance, ambiguity, decay_state, and register.  

**HLR-20.32-030:** COB SHALL never allow spontaneous creation, deletion, or mutation of identity layers outside of the defined lifecycle rules and CST signals.  

**HLR-20.32-031:** COB SHALL archive lineage upon eviction or retirement rather than delete it.  

**HLR-20.32-032:** COB SHALL enforce deterministic ordering of all identity layers and referent map entries.  

**HLR-20.32-033:** COB SHALL bound the number of referents per identity layer, lineage depth, and ambiguity entries to prevent unbounded growth.  

**HLR-20.32-034:** COB SHALL produce a stabilized identity-layer snapshot after each update cycle for exclusive consumption by CIL.  

---

### **HLR‑20.32‑076: Clarifying Snapshot Inclusion**  
COB SHALL include clarifying fields, clarifying subfields, and clarifying importance scores in the stabilized identity‑layer snapshot provided to CIL.

### **HLR‑20.32‑077: Deterministic Clarifying Replay**  
COB SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of clarifying fields and subfields such that identical sequences of OuBA packets and CST signals produce identical clarifying metadata.

### **HLR‑20.32‑078: Clarifying Continuity Across Freeze/Thaw**  
COB SHALL maintain clarifying fields and subfields across freeze/thaw cycles without loss, mutation, or reordering.

**HLR-20.32-035:** COB SHALL maintain a register (subculture / communication style) field in every identity layer and referent entry.  

**HLR-20.32-036:** The register field SHALL be updated deterministically from OuBA packets and CST signals (e.g., strengthen “technical” register or shift toward “casual_friends”).  

**HLR-20.32-037:** CIL SHALL include a register_hint derived from short-term cues and the COB register field in its intake packet so that CEx can annotate TP metadata with the appropriate subculture.  

**HLR-20.32-038:** CST SHALL be able to issue strengthen_register and weaken_register signals to adjust the register field of identity layers and referents.  

**HLR‑20.32‑039:**  
COB SHALL maintain a `last_referred` timestamp for each identity layer, updated deterministically whenever the layer is selected by CEx.

**HLR‑20.32‑040:**  
COB SHALL maintain a `total_referrals` counter for each identity layer, incremented deterministically whenever the layer is selected by CEx.

**HLR‑20.32‑041:**  
COB SHALL maintain a `recent_referrals` counter for each identity layer, representing the number of times the layer has been selected within the last 10 Path‑A turns.

**HLR‑20.32‑042:**  
COB SHALL update `recent_referrals` using a deterministic sliding‑window mechanism over the last 10 Path‑A turns.

**HLR‑20.32‑043:**  
COB SHALL expose `last_referred`, `total_referrals`, and `recent_referrals` in the stabilized identity‑layer snapshot consumed by CIL.

**HLR‑20.32‑044:**  
COB SHALL incorporate recency, frequency, and density into deterministic ordering of identity layers.

**HLR‑20.32‑045:**  
COB SHALL compute a recency score of the form:

$$
recency\_{score} = \frac{1}{1 + (current\_{turn} - last\_{referred})}
$$

**HLR‑20.32‑046:**  
COB SHALL compute a frequency score of the form:

$$
frequency\_{score} = \log(1 + total\_{referrals})
$$

**HLR‑20.32‑047:**  
COB SHALL compute a density score of the form:

$$
density\_{score} = recent\_{referrals}
$$

**HLR‑20.32‑048:**  
COB SHALL combine recency, frequency, and density into a deterministic ordering score of the form:

$$
ordering\_{score} = u_1 \cdot recency\_{score} + u_2 \cdot frequency\_{score} + u_3 \cdot density\_{score}
$$

**HLR‑20.32‑049:**  
COB SHALL maintain deterministic ordering of identity layers using $ordering_{score}$ in addition to strength, importance, ambiguity, and decay.

**HLR‑20.32‑050:**  
COB SHALL update ordering deterministically each turn, regardless of whether identity‑layer structure changes.

**HLR‑20.32‑051:**  
COB SHALL ensure that identical sequences of OuBA packets, CST signals, and CEx selections produce identical recency, frequency, density, and ordering scores under replay.

**HLR‑20.32‑052:**  
COB SHALL include $last\_{referred}$, $total\_{referrals}$, $recent\_{referrals}$, and $ordering\_{score}$ in the stabilized snapshot provided to CIL.

**HLR‑20.32‑053:**  
COB SHALL maintain recency, frequency, and density counters as part of the identity‑layer schema:

```json
stats: {
    total_referrals: int,
    recent_referrals: int
},
timestamps: {
    created: TurnID,
    updated: TurnID,
    last_referred: TurnID
}
```

**HLR‑20.32‑054:**  
COB SHALL update `last_referred`, `total_referrals`, and `recent_referrals` only when CEx selects the identity layer for the present turn.

**HLR‑20.32‑055:**  
COB SHALL NOT modify recency, frequency, or density based on OuBA packets, CST signals, or TS output.

**HLR‑20.32‑056:**  
COB SHALL maintain recency, frequency, and density counters across freeze/thaw cycles without loss or mutation.

**HLR‑20.32‑057:**  
COB SHALL ensure recency, frequency, and density counters are unaffected by pruning, compaction, or lineage summarization.

**HLR‑20.32‑058:**  
COB SHALL archive recency, frequency, and density counters upon eviction or retirement of an identity layer.

## Functional Boundaries

COB is strictly non-semantic and focused on identity-layer maintenance. It does not perform semantic interpretation, placeholder promotion, replay/export, compaction, redaction. It does not interact with IB, RB, TB, InB.

COB never modifies TP, semantic_core, or Path-B state. Structural changes occur only through the defined deterministic lifecycle operations and CST signals.

## Split/Merge Structural Events (Informative)**  
This section describes how COB performs identity‑layer split and merge operations, what structural information is produced, and how CST consumes that information through TP lineage markers to maintain stability, continuity, and deterministic replay.

---

### **Overview**  
COB MAY perform structural identity‑layer transformations during a deterministic update cycle:

- **MERGE** — two or more identity layers consolidate into a single layer.  
- **SPLIT** — one identity layer diverges into multiple child layers.

These operations are structural, not semantic. They modify the topology of the identity‑layer basin and therefore affect CST’s computation of drift, collapse, oscillation, ambiguity, certainty, and lineage continuity. (HLR‑20.32‑059) COB SHALL provide explicit structural continuity information so CST does not misinterpret legitimate structural changes as instability.

---

### **Structural Information Produced by COB**  
After performing a split or merge, (HLR‑20.32‑060) COB SHALL produce the following information for CST:

#### **Updated Identity‑Layer Snapshot**  
(HLR‑20.32‑061) COB’s stabilized snapshot SHALL reflect the post‑operation state, including:

- referent_map  
- anchors  
- lineage  
- ambiguity  
- stability_metrics  
- ordering_metrics  
- strength, importance, decay_state  
- register  
- recency, frequency, density counters  

CST consumes this snapshot to compute structural stability metrics.

---

#### **Lineage Continuity Markers (TP.lineage_log[])**  
(HLR‑20.32‑062) COB SHALL append a structural event entry to **TP.lineage_log[]**, the normative TP field for identity‑layer provenance.  
Each entry SHALL include:

- **event_type** = MERGE | SPLIT  
- **parent_ref** — IDs of source layers  
- **child_refs** — IDs of resulting layers  
- **referent_map_before**  
- **referent_map_after**  
- **ordering_before**  
- **ordering_after**  
- **lineage_seq** — deterministic sequence number for replay alignment  

These markers allow CST to distinguish structural transformations from collapse or oscillation.

---

#### **Before/After Channel Continuity**  
(HLR‑20.32‑063) COB SHALL provide sufficient before/after information in lineage_log entries for CST to compute:

- referent continuity  
- ordering continuity  
- lineage continuity  
- ambiguity continuity  
- stability continuity  

This prevents CST from interpreting merge as disappearance or split as duplication.

---

#### **Replay‑Safe Provenance**  
(HLR‑20.32‑064) All lineage_log entries SHALL be deterministic and replay‑safe.  
CST SHALL use lineage_seq and structural markers to guarantee identical stability signals for identical COB snapshots during historical replay.

---

### **Why CST Needs This Information**  
CST operates strictly on structural identity‑layer fields.  
Without explicit split/merge markers:

- MERGE may appear as disappearance → false **collapse**  
- SPLIT may appear as duplication → false **oscillation**  
- referent‑map changes may appear as **ambiguity spikes**  
- ordering changes may appear as **drift**  
- lineage changes may appear as **lineage collapse**

Therefore, COB MUST provide structural continuity information so CST can:

- suppress false collapse/oscillation signals  
- adjust ambiguity/certainty appropriately  
- maintain lineage continuity  
- compute drift relative to the correct parent layer  
- maintain deterministic replay guarantees  

---

### **CST Interpretation Rules (Informative)**  
When CST encounters lineage_log entries:

#### **MERGE**  
(HLR‑20.32‑065) CST SHALL interpret:

- disappearance of parent layers as legitimate consolidation  
- ordering changes as structural, not drift  
- referent‑map union as structural, not ambiguity spike  
- lineage continuity preserved through MERGE marker  

---

#### **SPLIT**  
(HLR‑20.32‑066) CST SHALL interpret:

- appearance of child layers as legitimate divergence  
- ordering duplication as structural, not oscillation  
- referent‑map partition as structural, not drift  
- lineage continuity preserved through SPLIT marker  

---

### **TP Fields Used by CST**  
(HLR‑20.32‑067) CST SHALL read the following TP fields after a split or merge:

- **TP.lineage_log[]** — structural event markers  
- **TP.cob_state_snapshot** — updated identity‑layer snapshot  
- **TP.metadata.turn_index** — replay alignment  
- **TP.metadata.object_count** — structural topology  
- **TP.ordering_summary** — ordering continuity  
- **TP.stability_summary** — drift/collapse/oscillation continuity  
- **TP.ambiguity_summary** — ambiguity continuity  

No additional TP fields SHALL be used for structural event interpretation.

---

### **Historical Replay**  
(HLR‑20.32‑068) During replay, CST SHALL:

- reconstruct lineage continuity from TP.lineage_log[]  
- compare identity‑layer snapshots deterministically  
- produce identical stability signals for identical snapshots  
- treat MERGE/SPLIT markers as structural invariants  

This guarantees deterministic replay across all structural transformations.
