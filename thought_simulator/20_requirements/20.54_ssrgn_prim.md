## 1. Introduction

SSRGn is the dedicated primitive responsible for generating the immutable Semantic Snapshot Reference (SSR) at the Path A → Path B boundary. It operates under OuBA control after meaning commitment and performs the final sanitization, projection, binding, and freeze operations required to produce a deterministic, governance-ready SSR for downstream consumption.

---

## 2. Purpose

SSRGn ensures a clean, frozen projection of committed meaning that Path B, governance, and replay components can consume without access to mutable TP state. It enforces the boundary semantics between construction (Path A) and realization (Path B).

**HLR-20.54-001:** SSRGn SHALL produce exactly one SSR data packet per committed TP turn.  

**HLR-20.54-002:** SSRGn SHALL operate only after OuBA has declared the TP committed.  

**HLR-20.54-003:** SSRGn SHALL preserve all Path-B-visible committed meaning while removing construction-time artifacts.  

**HLR-20.54-004:** SSRGn SHALL bind validated RRw and policy_signature into the SSR.  

**HLR-20.54-005:** SSRGn SHALL mark the resulting SSR as immutable (frozen).  

**HLR-20.54-019:** SSRGn SHALL define the formal boundary between Path A and Path B and SHALL be the final primitive permitted to read TP state.  

---

## 3. Inputs

SSRGn accepts a tightly defined set of inputs from the OuBA commit boundary.

**HLR-20.54-006:** SSRGn SHALL accept the following inputs:  
- $TP_{committed}$ (from OuBA)  
- RRw (routing metadata)  
- $policy_{signature}$ (governance metadata)  
- $turn_{id}$  
- $core_{id}$  
- $orthogonality_{signature}$  
- truth/safety/identity completion flags  
- semantic_core (stabilized from upstream OB primitives)  
- discourse_act (from OuBA field reference)  

**HLR-20.54-007:** SSRGn SHALL reject inputs that violate completeness or commit invariants.  

---

## 4. Process / Transfer Function Description

SSRGn executes a deterministic sequence of operations on the committed inputs.

The high-level transfer function is:

$$
SSR = freeze\left( bind\left( sanitize\left( project(TP_{committed}) \right), RRw, policy_{signature} \right) \right)
$$

**HLR-20.54-008:** SSRGn SHALL perform projection to select only Path-B-visible fields from $TP_{committed}$.  

**HLR-20.54-009:** SSRGn SHALL perform sanitization to remove mutable, hypothesis-layer, and construction-time residue.  

**HLR-20.54-010:** SSRGn SHALL bind RRw exactly as provided and validated upstream.  

**HLR-20.54-011:** SSRGn SHALL attach $policy_{signature}$ for governance and replay.  

**HLR-20.54-012:** SSRGn SHALL enforce deterministic behavior: identical inputs SHALL yield identical SSR outputs.  

**HLR-20.54-013:** SSRGn SHALL not perform semantic inference, routing decisions, or any Path-A construction operations.  

**HLR-20.54-014:** SSRGn SHALL preserve continuity, identity, and completion metadata.  

**HLR-20.54-020:** SSRGn SHALL apply freeze semantics such that no downstream primitive can modify SSR fields.  

---

## 5. Output Specification (SSR)

The output of SSRGn is the SSR data packet as defined in 20.52_ssr_data_packet.md.

**HLR-20.54-015:** SSRGn SHALL emit an SSR that conforms exactly to the structure and invariants specified in 20.52.  

**HLR-20.54-016:** SSR SHALL contain: semantic_core, truth/safety/identity fields, continuity fields, discourse act metadata, orthogonality_signature, core_id, turn_id, RRw, policy_signature, and relevant flags.  

**HLR-20.54-017:** SSRGn SHALL ensure the emitted SSR is read-only and suitable for Path B consumption, governance, and deterministic replay.  

**HLR-20.54-018:** SSRGn SHALL not expose or retain any mutable TP fields, residue, or internal Path-A state in the SSR.  

**HLR-20.54-021:** SSRGn SHALL ensure SSR contains no TP lineage_log, hypothesis-layer deltas, or construction-time residue.
