# **20.30.060 — Truth & Completion Semantics**  
**Status:** Normative (HLR‑20.30.060‑xxx only)  
**Scope:** Truth evaluation, uncertainty modeling, completion evaluation  
**Excludes:** Merge semantics (20.30.050), TPU commit semantics (20.30.070), response generation (20.30.080), Safety evaluation and TPSF production (see 20.66_tpsf_prim.md and 20.170_safety_requirements.md).

---

# **0. Purpose**

This document defines the **truth‑evaluation** and **completion‑evaluation** semantics used by the Thought Simulator (TS).  
It describes:

- how truth hypotheses are represented  
- how uncertainty (H%) is evaluated  
- how completion (“done”) is determined  
- how truth and completion interact with the MTP lifecycle  

Truth and completion semantics provide the final interpretive layer of Pipeline A before `mtp_update`.

---

# **1. Truth Evaluation Overview**

Truth evaluation determines:

- the structured truth hypotheses associated with the current TP/MTP  
- the uncertainty level (H%)  
- the direction and magnitude of semantic change  
- whether additional reasoning is required  

Truth metadata (TPTB):  
Truth evaluation SHALL produce a structured truth‑metadata object (TPTB) as defined in 20.64_tptb_prim.md and attach it to the TP/MTP as a read‑only field.

Truth evaluation is **deterministic** and **structure‑based**, not probabilistic.  
It uses:

- TB interpretive outputs  
- ΔH% contributions  
- lineage context  
- prior MTP truth state  

Truth evaluation does not infer new meaning; it interprets the meaning already constructed.

---

## 1.1 TPTB Integration (New)
This subsection states:

- Truth evaluation SHALL populate the TPTB metadata structure.
- TPTB is the metadata representation of truth‑completion outcomes.
- TPTB is produced after truth evaluation and before MTP update.
- Path B SHALL NOT perform truth evaluation and SHALL rely exclusively on TPTB.

This is the architectural anchor.

---

# **2. Truth Hypothesis Structure**

A truth hypothesis is a structured object containing:

- the proposition under evaluation  
- supporting evidence  
- contradicting evidence  
- ΔH%‑derived uncertainty  
- stance/intent/affect modifiers (if relevant)  
- a deterministic truth state  

Truth states may include:

- supported  
- contradicted  
- unresolved  
- incomplete  
- inconsistent  

These states are descriptive here; detailed definitions appear in the 10‑series.

---

## 2.1 Mapping Truth Hypotheses → TPTB (New)

This describes how:

- truth_conflict
- conflict_type
- alignment_class
- truth_basis
- explanation
- hypothesis_band

…are derived from the truth hypothesis object.
This is the semantic mapping.

## 2.2 Truth Metadata (TPTB)

Truth evaluation produces a structured truth‑metadata object (TPTB) that encodes:
- evaluated truth hypotheses
- uncertainty (H%)
- supporting and contradicting evidence
- lineage context
- ΔH%‑derived adjustments

TPTB is read‑only for all downstream components (merge, TPU, RG).
TPTB is the only truth‑metadata structure produced by truth evaluation.

---

# **3. Uncertainty (H%) Semantics**

H% represents **structural uncertainty**, not probability.  
It is derived from:

- ΔH% contributions  
- missing‑mass indicators  
- TB interpretive signals  
- prior MTP uncertainty state  

H% is used to determine:

- whether truth hypotheses are stable  
- whether additional reasoning is required  
- whether completion can be evaluated  

H% is deterministic and replay‑equivalent.

---

# **4. Completion Evaluation Overview**

Completion evaluation determines whether the TS has:

- sufficient meaning  
- sufficient truth stability  
- sufficient uncertainty reduction  
- sufficient interpretive coverage  

Completion is a **semantic** determination, not a compute or time‑based one.

Completion states include:

- done  
- not done  
- done‑with‑uncertainty  
- incomplete  
- requires‑inquiry  

Completion evaluation occurs **after merge** and **before TPU commit**.

---

# **5. Truth/Completion Interaction**

Truth and completion semantics interact as follows:

- truth hypotheses inform completion  
- unresolved or unstable truth hypotheses may prevent completion  
- high H% may prevent completion  
- stable truth hypotheses may enable completion  
- TB interpretive outputs influence both truth and completion  

Truth and completion evaluation do not modify meaning; they interpret it.

---

# **6. Truth & Completion in the TS Cycle**

Truth and completion evaluation occur:

1. after merge  
2. before TPU commit  
3. within the same deterministic cycle  
4. using only the semantic state produced by Pipeline A  

The results are included in the TPU update envelope and committed at `mtp_update`.

---

# **7. Normative Requirements (HLR‑20.30.060‑xxx)**

These are the **only** SHALL‑bearing requirements in this file.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑001 — Deterministic Truth Evaluation**  
Truth evaluation SHALL interpret TB outputs, ΔH% contributions, and prior MTP truth state using deterministic rules.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑002 — Structured Truth Hypotheses**  
Truth hypotheses SHALL be represented as structured objects containing proposition, evidence, uncertainty, and truth state.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑003 — Deterministic Uncertainty Evaluation**  
H% SHALL be computed deterministically from ΔH%, missing‑mass indicators, TB outputs, and prior uncertainty state.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑004 — No Probabilistic Truth Inference**  
Truth evaluation SHALL NOT use probabilistic inference, latent‑space estimation, or stochastic sampling.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑005 — Deterministic Completion Evaluation**  
Completion evaluation SHALL determine done/not‑done states using deterministic rules based on truth stability and uncertainty.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑006 — Truth/Completion Ordering**  
Truth evaluation SHALL precede completion evaluation within the same cycle.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑007 — Replay‑Safe Truth/Completion Behavior**  
Truth and completion outcomes SHALL be identical for identical (input, MTP state, execution signature) tuples.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑008 — TPU Envelope Integration**  
Truth and completion results SHALL be included in the deterministic TPU update envelope.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑009**  
Truth completion SHALL populate the TPTB metadata structure defined in 20.64.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑010**  
TPTB SHALL be finalized after truth evaluation and before MTP update.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑011**  
Path B SHALL NOT perform truth evaluation and SHALL rely exclusively on TPTB.

### **HLR‑20.30.060‑012** 
Truth/completion evaluation SHALL produce TPTB metadata as defined in 20.64_tptb_prim.md and SHALL attach it to the TP/MTP as a read‑only structure for all downstream components.

---

# **8. Summary**

20.30.060 defines the TS’s interpretive layer:

- deterministic truth evaluation  
- structured truth hypotheses  
- deterministic uncertainty modeling  
- deterministic completion evaluation  
- no probabilistic inference  
- replay‑safe behavior  
- integration into the TPU update envelope  

This file completes the interpretive semantics of Pipeline A.
