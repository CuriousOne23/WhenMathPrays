# 20.80 GB Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.080-001: GB SHALL apply global contextual constraints deterministically.
2. HLR-20.080-002: GB MAY suppress or encourage branching under explicit policy rules.
3. HLR-20.080-003: GB context updates SHALL be bounded in cost and auditable.
4. HLR-20.080-004: GB SHALL NOT bypass TS arbitration controls.
