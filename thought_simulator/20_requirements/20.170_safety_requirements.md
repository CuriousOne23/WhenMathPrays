# 20.170 Safety Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.170-001: TS SHALL provide deterministic fail-safe termination sequencing.
2. HLR-20.170-002: Safety policy SHALL enforce bounded resource utilization.
3. HLR-20.170-003: Safety-relevant state transitions SHALL be fully logged.
4. HLR-20.170-004: Unsafe nondeterministic pathways SHALL be prohibited in core execution.
