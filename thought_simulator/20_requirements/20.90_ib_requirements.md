# 20.90 IB Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic Inquiry Basin (IB) behavior for ambiguity handling, bounded inquiry expansion, evidence requests, and supervised inquiry evolution within the TS pipeline.
IB provides structured inquiry-state evolution and recommendation outputs under GB governance without bypassing TS deterministic flow controls.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.090-001: IB SHALL detect unresolved ambiguity and missing structure conditions.
2. HLR-20.090-002: IB SHALL operate over bounded, explicit inquiry inputs only and SHALL NOT rely on hidden or latent state.
3. HLR-20.090-003: IB SHALL trigger deterministic branch or clarification proposals with explicit rationale.
4. HLR-20.090-004: IB execution SHALL remain bounded through deterministic configurable limits.
5. HLR-20.090-005: IB recommendations SHALL be traceable to source cues, lineage references, and TP identifiers.
6. HLR-20.090-006: IB SHALL be instantiated only after GB approval from an IB-Creation-Request flow.
7. HLR-20.090-007: IB SHALL receive inquiry evidence through OB<->IB interaction paths and SHALL NOT accept direct OuB routing.
8. HLR-20.090-008: IB SHALL preserve deterministic inquiry-state fields including hypotheses, pending evidence requests, partial interpretations, depth state, and branch state.
9. HLR-20.090-009: IB identity metadata SHALL include unique IB identifier, provisional operator state, and originating snapshot linkage.
10. HLR-20.090-010: IB origin metadata SHALL include triggering OB identifiers, incomplete-meaning origin tag, creation timestamp, and GB approval reference.
11. HLR-20.090-011: IB evolution metadata SHALL include ΔH% trajectory, stability impact, branching metrics, conflict rate, usage frequency, and TCU cost profile.
12. HLR-20.090-012: IB promotion metadata SHALL include stability thresholds, ΔH% plateau indicators, conflict-resolution stability, usage thresholds, human-approval marker, and GB promotion request reference.
13. HLR-20.090-013: IB decomposition and retirement metadata SHALL include decomposition-target status, decomposition-plan membership, merge/split markers, and retirement marker state.
14. HLR-20.090-014: IB metadata updates SHALL be deterministic, bounded, and logged with when, why, and how context.
15. HLR-20.090-015: IB outputs SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical inquiry input state, MTP context, and seed-bounded system state.
16. HLR-20.090-016: IB SHALL NOT perform probabilistic inference, hallucinated reconstruction, or unstated-content invention while generating inquiry recommendations.
17. HLR-20.090-017: IB SHALL expose deterministic test interfaces with explicit inputs, expected outputs, invariants, and failure modes.
18. HLR-20.090-018: IB SHALL expose test hooks sufficient for unit, integration, system, and property-based verification of inquiry behavior.
19. HLR-20.090-019: IB lifecycle transitions (create, evolve, suspend, reshape, merge, split, promote, retire) SHALL be explicitly logged and observable.
20. HLR-20.090-020: IB supervisory actions from GB (Crop, Prune, Dampen, Slow, Suspend, Reshape, Kill) SHALL be applied only at deterministic safe boundaries.
21. HLR-20.090-021: IB merge operations SHALL instantiate deterministic merged IB state, combine source metadata deterministically, and retire source IBs under GB-approved control.
22. HLR-20.090-022: IB split operations SHALL instantiate deterministic IB-X1..IB-Xn state, partition inquiry/evolution metadata deterministically, and retire or dampen parent IB state under GB-approved control.
23. HLR-20.090-023: IB promotion to OuB SHALL occur only after GB approval and SHALL NOT modify OB registry state. IB promotion SHALL produce deterministic OuB-ready meaning outputs and SHALL treat TP.TR as read-only semantic input metadata consistent with 20.37, without altering OB, RB, or TB conceptual structures.
24. HLR-20.090-024: IB retirement or decomposition-related transitions SHALL occur only under GB-approved supervisory policy and SHALL remain auditable.
25. HLR-20.090-025: IB SHALL expose supervisory-view outputs to GB via deterministic TP/MP interface pathways without requiring GB direct reads of IB internal implementation state.
26. HLR-20.090-026: IB observability outputs SHALL be consumable by MB, GB, and IB diagnostics pipelines without mutating IB core semantics.
27. HLR-20.090-027: IB SHALL define measurable TCU budgets (min/typ/max) and per-tick TCU reporting fields.
28. HLR-20.090-028: IB overload/degradation behavior SHALL be deterministic, policy-bounded, and auditable.
29. HLR-20.090-029: IB behavior SHALL be platform-independent and SHALL NOT depend on hardware-, OS-, or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
30. HLR-20.090-030: IB contracts SHALL prioritize top-down inquiry semantics and bounded supervisory evolution over implementation-specific mechanics.
31. HLR-20.090-031: IB documentation artifacts (inquiry contracts, metadata schema, lifecycle controls, and supervisory invariants) SHALL remain complete and version-aligned with 20.30 section 8.6 and 20.80 GB requirements.
32. HLR-20.090-032: IB creation lifecycle SHALL support asynchronous GB approval flow and SHALL preserve non-blocking TS execution while requests are pending.
33. HLR-20.090-033: IB instantiation SHALL consume approved pending inquiry snapshots only, and denied inquiry requests SHALL NOT produce IB state.
34. HLR-20.090-034: IB lifecycle and supervisory logs SHALL remain append-only, time-indexed, and consistent with GB supervisory logging obligations.
35. HLR-20.090-035: IB SHALL tag the TP on every lifecycle transition, supervisory action, or inquiry-state evolution. Each TP entry SHALL include:
  - IB identity metadata (ib_id, origin_tp, origin_snapshot, triggering_ob_ids, gb_approval_reference)
  - lifecycle event type (IB_CREATED, IB_EVOLVED, IB_BRANCH_PROPOSED, IB_CLARIFICATION_REQUESTED, IB_SPLIT, IB_MERGED, IB_PROMOTION_REQUESTED, IB_PROMOTED_TO_OUB, IB_RETIRED)
  - supervisory context (gb_action, gb_reference_id, safe_boundary_marker, supervisory_reason_code)
  - inquiry-state deltas (hypothesis_delta, evidence_request_delta, branch_state_delta, depth_state_delta, ΔH%_delta, stability_delta)
  - deterministic reason codes
  - TCU cost profile (tcu_min, tcu_typ, tcu_max)
  - deterministic replay markers (tp_seq, timestamp, policy_signature, profile_signature)
  IB TP tagging SHALL be append-only, deterministic, bounded, safe-boundary enforced, and reproducible under identical conditions. IB TP tagging SHALL NOT mutate prior TP entries.
36. HLR-20.090-036: As long as an IB exists, all IB lifecycle events, inquiry-state deltas, supervisory actions, and deterministic replay markers SHALL be logged into the TP at each safe boundary. IB SHALL NOT write logs to any location other than the TP. IB logging SHALL remain append-only, time-indexed, and consistent with TS deterministic replay requirements.
37. HLR-20.090-037: IB SHALL log its own internal evolution, inquiry-state changes, branch proposals, evidence requests, ΔH% changes, stability changes, and TCU usage into the TP. GB SHALL log supervisory actions applied to the IB into the TP, including approvals, denials, slow/dampen/crop/prune/reshape/kill actions, promotion approvals, and retirement approvals.
38. HLR-20.090-038: All IB TP tagging, supervisory actions, lifecycle transitions, and inquiry-state updates SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries. IB SHALL NOT update state or emit TP entries outside safe-boundary execution points.

## IB Invariants
- Determinism invariant: identical effective inquiry state yields identical IB recommendations, metadata progression, and lifecycle outcomes.
- Boundary invariant: IB SHALL not bypass GB supervisory approval gates, TS deterministic safe-boundary application rules, or OuB routing boundaries.
- Auditability invariant: each IB creation, evolution, merge, split, promotion, decomposition, or retirement event SHALL be attributable to explicit deterministic inputs and supervisory policy context.
- TP-Completeness invariant: IB SHALL emit complete, deterministic TP entries for all lifecycle transitions, supervisory actions, and inquiry-state deltas, ensuring full replayability and auditability.
