# **20.90 IB — Inquiry Basin Requirements**

---

## **1. Purpose**

The Inquiry Basin (IB) is the **deterministic semantic decision primitive** of Pipeline A.  
IB resolves ambiguity, selects meaning, commits semantic updates, and governs inquiry evolution under GB supervision.  
IB operates only on **bounded, explicit, deterministic inputs** and never performs semantic projection or scoring.

IB is the **final arbiter of meaning** before OuB promotion.

---

## **2. Scope**

This specification defines:

- IB inputs  
- IB outputs  
- IB lifecycle  
- IB metadata  
- IB supervisory interactions  
- IB deterministic replay rules  
- IB logging and auditability  
- IB integration with IIInB, ISc‑Semantic, ISc‑Scoring, Envelope, and GB  

This file is normative.

---

## **3. Inputs**

IB SHALL accept only:

- **semantic repair candidates** from ISc‑Semantic  
- **scoring vectors** from ISc‑Scoring  
- **inquiry‑state metadata**  
- **TP.TR** (read‑only)  
- **envelope‑validated candidate sets**  
- **GB supervisory directives**  
- **approved inquiry snapshots**  

IB SHALL NOT accept:

- raw TP mutations  
- semantic_core writes  
- external knowledge  
- nondeterministic sources  
- OuB direct routing  

---

## **4. Outputs**

IB SHALL output:

- the **selected meaning update**  
- deterministic inquiry‑state evolution  
- deterministic branch/clarification proposals  
- deterministic OuB‑ready meaning (when promoted)  
- TP tagging entries for all lifecycle transitions  
- GB‑visible supervisory‑view outputs  

All outputs SHALL be deterministic, bounded, and reproducible.

# **5. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)**  
*(All original HLRs preserved. New HLRs added with suffix letters.)*

---

## **5.1 Core Deterministic Behavior**

1. **HLR‑20.090‑001** — IB SHALL detect unresolved ambiguity and missing structure conditions.  
2. **HLR‑20.090‑002** — IB SHALL operate over bounded, explicit inquiry inputs only and SHALL NOT rely on hidden or latent state.  
3. **HLR‑20.090‑003** — IB SHALL trigger deterministic branch or clarification proposals with explicit rationale.  

### **NEW (ISc Integration)**  
4. **HLR‑20.090‑003A** — IB SHALL consume semantic repair candidates from ISc‑Semantic and scoring outputs from ISc‑Scoring as its exclusive decision inputs.  

5. **HLR‑20.090‑004** — IB execution SHALL remain bounded through deterministic configurable limits.  
6. **HLR‑20.090‑005** — IB recommendations SHALL be traceable to source cues, lineage references, and TP identifiers.  

---

## **5.2 Instantiation and Evidence Flow**

7. **HLR‑20.090‑006** — IB SHALL be instantiated only after GB approval from an IB‑Creation‑Request flow.  
8. **HLR‑20.090‑007** — IB SHALL receive inquiry evidence through OB↔IB interaction paths and SHALL NOT accept direct OuB routing.  

---

## **5.3 Deterministic Inquiry‑State Preservation**

9. **HLR‑20.090‑008** — IB SHALL preserve deterministic inquiry‑state fields including hypotheses, pending evidence requests, partial interpretations, depth state, and branch state.  

### **NEW (Envelope Integration)**  
10. **HLR‑20.090‑008A** — IB SHALL operate only on envelope‑validated candidate sets produced by ISc‑Semantic.  

11. **HLR‑20.090‑009** — IB identity metadata SHALL include unique IB identifier, provisional operator state, and originating snapshot linkage.  
12. **HLR‑20.090‑010** — IB origin metadata SHALL include triggering OB identifiers, incomplete‑meaning origin tag, creation timestamp, and GB approval reference.  

### **NEW (TP.TR immutability)**  
13. **HLR‑20.090‑010A** — IB SHALL treat TP.TR as immutable read‑only semantic metadata during all inquiry and decision operations.  

---

## **5.4 Evolution, Promotion, and Decomposition Metadata**

14. **HLR‑20.090‑011** — IB evolution metadata SHALL include ΔH% trajectory, stability impact, branching metrics, conflict rate, usage frequency, and TCU cost profile.  
15. **HLR‑20.090‑012** — IB promotion metadata SHALL include stability thresholds, ΔH% plateau indicators, conflict‑resolution stability, usage thresholds, human‑approval marker, and GB promotion request reference.  
16. **HLR‑20.090‑013** — IB decomposition and retirement metadata SHALL include decomposition‑target status, decomposition‑plan membership, merge/split markers, and retirement marker state.  
17. **HLR‑20.090‑014** — IB metadata updates SHALL be deterministic, bounded, and logged with when, why, and how context.  

---

## **5.5 Deterministic Outputs and Replay Safety**

18. **HLR‑20.090‑015** — IB outputs SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical inquiry input state, MTP context, and seed‑bounded system state.  

### **NEW (Replay Safety)**  
19. **HLR‑20.090‑015A** — IB SHALL produce identical decisions under deterministic replay for identical inquiry state and candidate inputs.  

### **NEW (Semantic Commit)**  
20. **HLR‑20.090‑015B** — IB SHALL commit the selected meaning update into semantic_core at a deterministic safe boundary.  

---

## **5.6 Forbidden Actions**

21. **HLR‑20.090‑016** — IB SHALL NOT perform probabilistic inference, hallucinated reconstruction, or unstated‑content invention.  

### **NEW (ISc Separation)**  
22. **HLR‑20.090‑016A** — IB SHALL NOT generate semantic repair candidates.  
23. **HLR‑20.090‑016B** — IB SHALL NOT perform scoring, ranking, or probability assignment.  

---

## **5.7 Verification Interfaces**

24. **HLR‑20.090‑017** — IB SHALL expose deterministic test interfaces with explicit inputs, expected outputs, invariants, and failure modes.  
25. **HLR‑20.090‑018** — IB SHALL expose test hooks sufficient for unit, integration, system, and property‑based verification.  

---

## **5.8 Lifecycle and Supervisory Control**

26. **HLR‑20.090‑019** — IB lifecycle transitions SHALL be explicitly logged and observable.  
27. **HLR‑20.090‑020** — GB supervisory actions SHALL be applied only at deterministic safe boundaries.  
28. **HLR‑20.090‑021** — IB merge operations SHALL be deterministic and GB‑approved.  

### **NEW (Envelope Enforcement)**  
29. **HLR‑20.090‑021A** — IB SHALL reject all candidates that violate envelope constraints and SHALL escalate when no envelope‑valid candidate exists.  

30. **HLR‑20.090‑022** — IB split operations SHALL be deterministic and GB‑approved.  
31. **HLR‑20.090‑023** — IB promotion to OuB SHALL treat TP.TR as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify OB, RB, or TB structures.  
32. **HLR‑20.090‑024** — IB retirement/decomposition SHALL be GB‑approved and auditable.  
33. **HLR‑20.090‑025** — IB SHALL expose supervisory‑view outputs via deterministic TP/MP pathways.  
34. **HLR‑20.090‑026** — IB observability outputs SHALL be consumable by MB, GB, and diagnostics pipelines.  
35. **HLR‑20.090‑027** — IB SHALL define measurable TCU budgets.  
36. **HLR‑20.090‑028** — IB overload/degradation behavior SHALL be deterministic and auditable.  
37. **HLR‑20.090‑029** — IB behavior SHALL be platform‑independent.  
38. **HLR‑20.090‑030** — IB contracts SHALL prioritize top‑down inquiry semantics.  
39. **HLR‑20.090‑031** — IB documentation SHALL remain version‑aligned with 20.30 and 20.80.  
40. **HLR‑20.090‑032** — IB creation SHALL support asynchronous GB approval.  
41. **HLR‑20.090‑033** — IB instantiation SHALL consume approved inquiry snapshots only.  
42. **HLR‑20.090‑034** — IB logs SHALL be append‑only and time‑indexed.  

---

## **5.9 TP Tagging and Logging**

43. **HLR‑20.090‑035** — IB SHALL tag the TP on every lifecycle transition with full metadata, supervisory context, inquiry deltas, deterministic reason codes, TCU profile, and replay markers.  
44. **HLR‑20.090‑036** — IB SHALL log all lifecycle events and SHALL NOT write logs outside the TP.  
45. **HLR‑20.090‑037** — IB SHALL log internal evolution; GB SHALL log supervisory actions.  
46. **HLR‑20.090‑038** — All IB updates SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries.  

---

- mutate TP.TR

- bypass GB supervisory gates  
- write logs outside TP
