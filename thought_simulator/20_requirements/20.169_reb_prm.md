# 20.169 — Realization Basin Primitive (ReB‑prm)

## 1. Purpose

ReB‑prm defines the Realization Basin primitive at the terminal end of Path B.  
ReB is a non‑semantic, non‑generative, non‑planning primitive whose function is to validate that a fully realized artifact—produced by the complete Path B pipeline—remains internally coherent, invariant‑preserving, and admissible for downstream delivery or logging.

ReB does not read or modify semantic_core, does not access TP, does not perform planning or generation, and does not repair or transform artifacts.  
ReB is the final internal gate for Path B artifacts.

---

## 2. Architectural Position

ReB occupies the final position in the Path B pipeline:

CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → OuBB → ReB

ReB receives the final artifact body and all associated metadata, including continuity_fields, lineage_fields, realization_plan, realization_update, provenance_chain, and channel‑level constraints.  
ReB does not re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.

---

## 3. Inputs

ReB consumes a single structured input consisting of:

### 3.1 Artifact Body  
The final channel‑ready artifact (text, markup, or structured output).

### 3.2 Metadata Bundle  
A metadata structure containing:
- continuity_fields  
- lineage_fields  
- realization_plan  
- realization_update  
- provenance_chain  
- channel_constraints  
- style/tone invariants  
- semantic invariants  
- constraint/invariant status from OuBB  
- exec_trace up to OuBB

ReB does not read TP, semantic_core, COB, CIL, or Pipeline A state.

---

## 4. Outputs

ReB emits one of three outcomes:

- reb_artifact_ref (artifact accepted)  
- REJECT (artifact invalid)  
- UPSTREAM_CORRECTION_REQUIRED (artifact contradicts upstream metadata)

ReB does not mutate artifacts.

---

## 5. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)

### Continuity and Lineage Validation
- **HLR‑20.169‑001** — ReB SHALL verify that continuity_fields are consistent with the artifact body.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑002** — ReB SHALL verify that lineage_fields accurately reflect the artifact’s provenance.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑003** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts whose continuity_fields contradict CoHI output.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑004** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts whose lineage_fields contradict LI output.

### Realization Consistency
- **HLR‑20.169‑005** — ReB SHALL verify consistency between the artifact and the realization_plan.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑006** — ReB SHALL verify consistency between the artifact and the realization_update envelope.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑007** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that contradict RPlan or RPU metadata.

### Upstream Invariant Preservation
- **HLR‑20.169‑008** — ReB SHALL ensure that all invariants declared by CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, and RPU remain intact.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑009** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that drop or weaken upstream invariants.

### Channel and Semantic Constraints
- **HLR‑20.169‑010** — ReB SHALL enforce channel_constraints.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑011** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that access or imply access to TP.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑012** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that mutate meaning relative to upstream intent.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑013** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that mutate style or tone invariants.

### Structural and Provenance Integrity
- **HLR‑20.169‑014** — ReB SHALL verify that provenance_chain is complete, ordered, and unbroken.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑015** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts with malformed or contradictory structure.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑016** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that introduce branching or alternative interpretations.

### Execution Boundaries
- **HLR‑20.169‑017** — ReB SHALL NOT re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑018** — ReB SHALL NOT generate, repair, or transform artifacts.

---

## 6. Violation Categories

ReB classifies violations into:
- continuity_violation  
- lineage_violation  
- realization_violation  
- provenance_violation  
- channel_violation  
- semantic_violation  
- style_violation  
- structural_violation  

Each violation identifies the upstream primitive responsible.

---

## 7. Example

Input artifact and metadata are evaluated for continuity consistency, lineage consistency, realization consistency, semantic and stylistic stability, and provenance completeness.  
If all checks pass, the artifact is accepted.

---

## 8. Is / Is Not

### ReB IS:
- the terminal invariant gate  
- the final validator of continuity, lineage, and realization correctness  
- the last opportunity to block an artifact  
- a pure validator

### ReB IS NOT:
- a meaning generator  
- a continuity or lineage constructor  
- a realization planner or updater  
- a repair mechanism  
- a semantic interpreter
