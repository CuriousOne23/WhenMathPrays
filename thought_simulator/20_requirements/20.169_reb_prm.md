# 20.169 — ReB‑prm  
Realization Basin Primitive

## 1. Purpose

ReB‑prm is the **terminal invariant gate** of Path B.  
It receives a fully realized artifact—after continuity integration (CoHI), lineage integration (LI), realization extraction (REx), realization planning (RPlan), realization updating (RPU), and output‑buffering (OuBB)—and determines whether the artifact is **admissible**, **rejectable**, or **requires upstream correction**.

ReB does **not** generate meaning, continuity, lineage, or realization structure.  
Its role is to **validate** that all upstream invariants remain intact and that the artifact is internally coherent with its declared metadata.

ReB is the final point where the system may block an artifact before it becomes externally visible.

---

## 2. Position in the Architecture

ReB sits at the end of the Path B pipeline:

**CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → OuBB → ReB**

ReB receives:

- the artifact body  
- continuity_fields (from CoHI)  
- lineage_fields (from LI)  
- realization_plan metadata (from RPlan)  
- realization_update envelope (from RPU)  
- output‑buffer metadata (from OuBB)

ReB MUST NOT re‑execute or re‑interpret upstream primitives.  
It validates **propagated invariants**, not meaning or realization construction.

---

## 3. Inputs

ReB receives a single structured input:

### 3.1 Artifact body
The final artifact text or structured output.

### 3.2 Metadata bundle
ReB requires the following metadata fields:

- **continuity_fields** — CoHI‑level continuity‑stitch descriptors  
- **lineage_fields** — LI‑level lineage descriptors  
- **realization_plan** — RPlan‑produced plan structure  
- **realization_update** — RPU‑produced update envelope  
- **provenance_chain** — ordered list:  
  `[CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, OuBB]`  
- **channel_constraints** — channel‑level restrictions (e.g., no TP access)  
- **style/tone invariants** — inherited from Path A  
- **semantic invariants** — inherited from Path A  

If any required field is missing, malformed, or contradictory, ReB MUST reject the artifact.

---

## 4. Outputs

ReB produces one of three outcomes:

### 4.1 ACCEPT
Artifact is valid, all invariants preserved.

### 4.2 REJECT
Artifact violates one or more invariants; rejection includes a structured violation report.

### 4.3 UPSTREAM_CORRECTION_REQUIRED
Artifact is internally coherent but contradicts upstream metadata; the violation report identifies which primitive must be re‑invoked.

ReB NEVER mutates the artifact.

---

## 5. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)

### HLR‑169‑001 — Continuity Consistency  
ReB SHALL verify that continuity_fields are consistent with the artifact body and do not contradict CoHI‑level stitching.

### HLR‑169‑002 — Lineage Consistency  
ReB SHALL verify that lineage_fields accurately reflect the artifact’s provenance and do not contradict LI‑level lineage stability.

### HLR‑169‑003 — Realization Consistency  
ReB SHALL verify that the artifact is consistent with the realization_plan and realization_update envelope.

### HLR‑169‑004 — Upstream Invariant Preservation  
ReB SHALL ensure that all invariants declared by CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, and RPU remain intact.

### HLR‑169‑005 — No Upstream Re‑execution  
ReB SHALL NOT re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.

### HLR‑169‑006 — Channel Constraint Enforcement  
ReB SHALL enforce channel‑level constraints (e.g., no TP access, no semantic mutation).

### HLR‑169‑007 — Provenance Integrity  
ReB SHALL verify that the provenance_chain is complete, ordered, and unbroken.

---

## 6. Violation Categories

ReB MUST classify violations into the following categories:

- **continuity_violation** — mismatch with continuity_fields  
- **lineage_violation** — mismatch with lineage_fields  
- **realization_violation** — mismatch with RPlan/RPU metadata  
- **provenance_violation** — broken or inconsistent provenance_chain  
- **channel_violation** — violation of channel constraints  
- **semantic_violation** — mutation of meaning  
- **style_violation** — mutation of tone/style  
- **structural_violation** — malformed artifact structure  

Each violation MUST identify the upstream primitive responsible.

---

## 7. Invariants

ReB enforces the following invariants:

### INV‑169‑A — Continuity‑Stitch Preservation  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts whose continuity_fields contradict CoHI output.

### INV‑169‑B — Lineage‑Stability Preservation  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts whose lineage_fields contradict LI output.

### INV‑169‑C — Realization‑Plan Fidelity  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that contradict RPlan or RPU metadata.

### INV‑169‑D — No Semantic Mutation  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that alter meaning relative to upstream intent.

### INV‑169‑E — No Style/Tone Mutation  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that alter style/tone invariants.

### INV‑169‑F — Provenance Completeness  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts with missing or reordered provenance entries.

### INV‑169‑G — No Branching  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that introduce new branches or alternative interpretations.

### INV‑169‑H — No TP Access  
ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that access or imply access to TP‑layer structures.

---

## 8. Example

**Input artifact:**  
A final answer text with no semantic or stylistic mutations.

**Metadata:**  
```
continuity_fields: {...}
lineage_fields: {...}
realization_plan: {...}
realization_update: {...}
provenance_chain: [CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, OuBB]
channel_constraints: {...}
```

**ReB evaluation:**  
- continuity_fields match artifact → OK  
- lineage_fields match provenance → OK  
- realization_plan/update envelope match artifact → OK  
- no semantic/style mutation → OK  
- provenance_chain complete → OK  

**Outcome:** ACCEPT.

---

## 9. Is / Is Not

### ReB IS:
- the terminal invariant gate  
- the final validator of continuity, lineage, and realization correctness  
- the last opportunity to block an artifact  
- a pure validator (no mutation, no generation)

### ReB IS NOT:
- a meaning generator  
- a continuity or lineage constructor  
- a realization planner or updater  
- a repair mechanism  
- a semantic interpreter
