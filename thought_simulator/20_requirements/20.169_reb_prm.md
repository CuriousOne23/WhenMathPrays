# 20.169 — Realization Basin Primitive (ReB‑prm)

## 1. Purpose

ReB‑prm defines the **Realization Basin** primitive at the terminal end of Path B.  
ReB is a **non‑semantic, non‑generative, non‑planning** primitive whose sole function is to validate that a fully realized artifact—produced by the complete Path B pipeline—remains internally coherent, invariant‑preserving, and admissible for downstream delivery or logging.

ReB does **not**:
- read or modify `semantic_core`
- read or modify TP
- perform planning, generation, or style selection
- resolve incomplete meaning
- interact with Pipeline A
- mutate or repair artifacts

ReB is the **final internal gate** for Path B artifacts.

---

## 2. Position in the Architecture

ReB sits at the end of the updated Path B pipeline:

**CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → OuBB → ReB**

ReB receives:
- the final artifact body
- continuity_fields (from CoHI)
- lineage_fields (from LI)
- realization_plan metadata (from RPlan)
- realization_update envelope (from RPU)
- output‑buffer metadata (from OuBB)
- provenance_chain covering all upstream primitives

ReB SHALL NOT re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.  
ReB validates **propagated invariants**, not meaning or realization construction.

---

## 3. Inputs

ReB consumes a single structured input:

### 3.1 Artifact Body
The final channel‑ready artifact (text, markup, or structured output).

### 3.2 Metadata Bundle
ReB requires the following metadata fields:

- **continuity_fields** — CoHI‑level continuity‑stitch descriptors  
- **lineage_fields** — LI‑level lineage descriptors  
- **realization_plan** — RPlan‑produced plan structure  
- **realization_update** — RPU‑produced update envelope  
- **provenance_chain** — ordered list:  
  `[CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, OuBB]`  
- **channel_constraints** — channel‑level restrictions  
- **style/tone invariants** — inherited from Path A  
- **semantic invariants** — inherited from Path A  
- **constraint/invariant status** as reported by OuBB  
- **exec_trace** up to OuBB

ReB SHALL NOT read:
- TP  
- `semantic_core`  
- COB/CIL  
- Pipeline A state  

If any required field is missing, malformed, or contradictory, ReB SHALL reject the artifact.

---

## 4. Outputs

ReB emits exactly one of the following:

### 4.1 `reb_artifact_ref` (ACCEPT)
Artifact is valid and all invariants are preserved.

### 4.2 REJECT
Artifact violates one or more invariants; ReB emits a structured violation report.

### 4.3 UPSTREAM_CORRECTION_REQUIRED
Artifact is internally coherent but contradicts upstream metadata; the violation report identifies which primitive must be re‑invoked.

ReB SHALL NOT mutate the artifact.

---

## 5. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)

### **Continuity and Lineage Validation**
- **HLR‑20.169‑001** — ReB SHALL verify that continuity_fields are consistent with the artifact body.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑002** — ReB SHALL verify that lineage_fields accurately reflect the artifact’s provenance.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑003** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts whose continuity_fields contradict CoHI output.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑004** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts whose lineage_fields contradict LI output.

### **Realization Consistency**
- **HLR‑20.169‑005** — ReB SHALL verify that the artifact is consistent with the realization_plan.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑006** — ReB SHALL verify that the artifact is consistent with the realization_update envelope.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑007** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that contradict RPlan or RPU metadata.

### **Upstream Invariant Preservation**
- **HLR‑20.169‑008** — ReB SHALL ensure that all invariants declared by CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, and RPU remain intact.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑009** — ReB SHALL NOT accept artifacts that drop or weaken upstream invariants.

### **Channel and Semantic Constraints**
- **HLR‑20.169‑010** — ReB SHALL enforce channel_constraints.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑011** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that access or imply access to TP.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑012** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that mutate meaning relative to upstream intent.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑013** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that mutate style or tone invariants.

### **Structural and Provenance Integrity**
- **HLR‑20.169‑014** — ReB SHALL verify that provenance_chain is complete, ordered, and unbroken.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑015** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts with malformed or contradictory structure.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑016** — ReB SHALL reject artifacts that introduce branching or alternative interpretations.

### **Execution Boundaries**
- **HLR‑20.169‑017** — ReB SHALL NOT re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑018** — ReB SHALL NOT generate, repair, or transform artifacts.

---

## 6. Violation Categories

ReB SHALL classify violations into the following categories:

- **continuity_violation** — mismatch with continuity_fields  
- **lineage_violation** — mismatch with lineage_fields  
- **realization_violation** — mismatch with RPlan/RPU metadata  
- **provenance_violation** — broken or inconsistent provenance_chain  
- **channel_violation** — violation of channel constraints  
- **semantic_violation** — mutation of meaning  
- **style_violation** — mutation of tone/style  
- **structural_violation** — malformed artifact structure  

Each violation SHALL identify the upstream primitive responsible.

---

## 7. Example

**Input artifact:**  
A final answer text with no semantic or stylistic mutations.

**Metadata:**  
```
continuity_fields: {...}
lineage_fields: {...}
realization_plan: {...}
realization_update: {...}
provenance_chain: [CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, OuBB]
channel_constraints: {...}
```

**ReB evaluation:**  
- continuity_fields match artifact → OK  
- lineage_fields match provenance → OK  
- realization_plan/update envelope match artifact → OK  
- no semantic/style mutation → OK  
- provenance_chain complete → OK  

**Outcome:** ACCEPT.

---

## 8. Is / Is Not

### ReB IS:
- the terminal invariant gate  
- the final validator of continuity, lineage, and realization correctness  
- the last opportunity to block an artifact  
- a pure validator (no mutation, no generation)

### ReB IS NOT:
- a meaning generator  
- a continuity or lineage constructor  
- a realization planner or updater  
- a repair mechanism  
- a semantic interpreter
