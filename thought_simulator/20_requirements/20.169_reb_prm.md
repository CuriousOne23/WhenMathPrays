# 20.169 — ReB‑prm (Realization Basin Primitive)

## 1. Purpose (informative)

ReB‑prm defines the **Realization Basin** primitive near the terminal end of Path B.  
ReB is a non‑semantic, non‑generative, non‑planning primitive whose function is to validate that a fully realized artifact—produced by the complete Path B pipeline up through RPU—remains internally coherent, invariant‑preserving, and admissible for downstream delivery or logging.

ReB does not read or modify semantic_core, does not access TP, does not perform planning or generation, and does not repair or transform artifacts.  
ReB is the final **internal invariant gate** for Path B artifacts before OuBB.

---

## 2. Architectural Position (informative)

ReB occupies the sixth position in the Path B pipeline:

**CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → ReB → OuBB**

Upstream:  
- RPU  

Downstream:  
- OuBB  

ReB receives the final artifact body and all associated metadata required to validate continuity, lineage, realization, and channel‑level constraints.  
ReB does not re‑execute or reinterpret upstream primitives.

---

## 3. Inputs (informative)

ReB consumes a single structured input consisting of:

### 3.1 Artifact Body  
The final channel‑ready artifact (text, markup, or structured output) as produced by RPU and routed through the realization pipeline.

### 3.2 Metadata Bundle  
A metadata structure containing:

- continuity_fields (as frozen by CoHI and propagated through Path B)  
- lineage_fields  
- realization_plan (from RPlan)  
- realization_update / exec_trace (from RPU)  
- provenance_chain  
- channel_constraints and policy flags  
- style/tone invariants  
- semantic invariants  
- constraint/invariant status as reported by upstream primitives  

ReB does **not** read TP, semantic_core, COB, CIL, or any Pipeline A state.

---

## 4. Outputs (informative)

ReB writes exactly:

- `tp.reb_status`  
- `tp.reb_reason_code`  
- `tp.reb_artifact_ref`  

with the following status values:

- `ACCEPTED` — artifact is valid and may proceed to OuBB  
- `REJECTED` — artifact is invalid and MUST NOT be delivered  
- `UPSTREAM_CORRECTION_REQUIRED` — artifact contradicts upstream metadata or invariants and requires upstream correction

ReB does not mutate artifacts and does not generate alternative artifacts.

---

## 5. High‑Level Requirements (normative)

### Continuity and Lineage Validation  

- **HLR‑20.169‑001** — ReB SHALL verify that continuity_fields are consistent with the artifact body.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑002** — ReB SHALL verify that lineage_fields accurately reflect the artifact’s provenance.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑003** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` when continuity_fields contradict CoHI‑frozen continuity.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑004** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` when lineage_fields contradict LI‑committed meaning or downstream lineage updates.

### Realization Consistency  

- **HLR‑20.169‑005** — ReB SHALL verify consistency between the artifact and the realization_plan.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑006** — ReB SHALL verify consistency between the artifact and the realization_update / exec_trace from RPU.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑007** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` when the artifact contradicts RPlan or RPU metadata.

### Upstream Invariant Preservation  

- **HLR‑20.169‑008** — ReB SHALL verify that all invariants declared by CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, and RPU remain intact at the artifact boundary.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑009** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = UPSTREAM_CORRECTION_REQUIRED` when an artifact drops, weakens, or violates an upstream invariant.

### Channel and Semantic Constraints  

- **HLR‑20.169‑010** — ReB SHALL enforce channel_constraints on the artifact body and metadata.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑011** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` for artifacts that access or imply access to TP or internal state.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑012** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` for artifacts that mutate meaning relative to upstream intent.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑013** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` for artifacts that mutate style or tone invariants.

### Structural and Provenance Integrity  

- **HLR‑20.169‑014** — ReB SHALL verify that provenance_chain is complete, ordered, and unbroken from CoHI through RPU.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑015** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` for artifacts with malformed or contradictory structure.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑016** — ReB SHALL set `reb_status = REJECTED` for artifacts that introduce branching, alternative interpretations, or ambiguous realization paths.

### Execution Boundaries  

- **HLR‑20.169‑017** — ReB SHALL NOT re‑execute or reinterpret CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, or RPU.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑018** — ReB SHALL NOT generate, repair, or transform artifacts.  
- **HLR‑20.169‑019** — ReB SHALL NOT write to any field owned by CoHI, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, OuBB, or any Pipeline A component.  

### Normative Home  

- **HLR‑20.169‑020** — Any implementation of ReB SHALL conform to this specification at revision `0.3.1` or a formally approved successor.

---

## 6. Violation Categories (informative)

ReB classifies violations into:

- continuity_violation  
- lineage_violation  
- realization_violation  
- provenance_violation  
- channel_violation  
- semantic_violation  
- style_violation  
- structural_violation  

Each violation is annotated with the upstream primitive(s) whose outputs are contradicted or whose invariants are violated.

---

## 7. Example (informative)

An artifact and its metadata bundle arrive from RPU:

- continuity_fields indicate a stable referent “the dataset”  
- lineage_fields trace meaning commitment to LI and realization to RPlan/RPU  
- realization_plan and realization_update indicate a three‑step emission sequence  
- channel_constraints prohibit disclosure of internal TP or model state  

ReB verifies:

- the artifact’s references to “the dataset” match continuity_fields  
- lineage_fields and provenance_chain form a complete, ordered path from CoHI through RPU  
- the artifact structure and content are consistent with the realization_plan and realization_update  
- no mention is made of TP, internal prompts, or hidden state  
- style and tone match the declared invariants  

If all checks pass, ReB writes:

- `tp.reb_status = ACCEPTED`  
- `tp.reb_reason_code = ""`  
- `tp.reb_artifact_ref` pointing to the validated artifact  

OuBB then consumes `tp.reb_status` and `tp.reb_artifact_ref` for channel‑specific delivery.
