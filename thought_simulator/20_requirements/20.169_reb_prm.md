# **20.169 — Realization Basin (ReB‑prm)**  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Primitive Class:** Path B — Realization Layer  
**Purpose:** Convert a Realization Update (RPU) into **final, user‑visible output**, assembling structure, applying style, and emitting the realized artifact to the appropriate channel.

---

## 1. Overview
The Realization Basin (ReB‑prm) is the **fourth and final primitive** in the Path B pipeline.  
It consumes a **Realization Update (RPU)** and produces a **final realization artifact** (ReB‑out), which is user‑visible and channel‑appropriate.

ReB‑prm:

- Assembles the intermediate structure from RPU  
- Applies audience‑ and channel‑appropriate formatting  
- Applies tone and style consistent with upstream hints  
- Ensures constraints and invariants remain satisfied  
- Emits final content suitable for delivery

---

## 2. Inputs and outputs

### 2.1 Inputs
ReB‑prm SHALL read the following RPU fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `RPU.partial_realization` | Structured intermediate content |
| `RPU.resolved_ambiguities` | Ambiguities resolved by RPU |
| `RPU.filled_dependencies` | Dependencies resolved or retrieved |
| `RPU.constraint_state` | Constraint satisfaction status |
| `RPU.invariant_state` | Invariant preservation status |
| `RPU.channel` | Output channel |
| `RPU.audience` | Target audience |
| `RPU.trace` | Provenance chain |

### 2.2 Outputs
ReB‑prm SHALL produce a **Realization Basin Output (ReB‑out)** with at least:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `ReB.content` | Final user‑visible content |
| `ReB.channel` | Channel used for realization |
| `ReB.audience` | Intended audience |
| `ReB.constraints_applied` | Summary of applied constraints |
| `ReB.invariants_preserved` | Summary of preserved invariants |
| `ReB.trace` | Extended provenance chain |

---

## 3. High‑level requirements (HLRs)

All SHALL statements are bound to **HLR‑20.169-xxx**.

---

### HLR‑20.169-001 — Final assembly from partial realization  
ReB‑prm SHALL assemble `RPU.partial_realization` into a coherent, final realization artifact.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- Assembly SHALL respect the structural organization implied by `RPU.partial_realization`.  
- Assembly SHALL not introduce new semantic content beyond what is implied by RPU.  
- Assembly SHALL ensure the final artifact is syntactically well‑formed for the target channel.

---

### HLR‑20.169-002 — Channel‑appropriate formatting  
ReB‑prm SHALL format `ReB.content` according to `RPU.channel`.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- For `channel = email`, ReB‑prm SHALL produce email‑appropriate structure (greeting, body, closing).  
- For `channel = chat`, ReB‑prm SHALL produce concise, conversational structure.  
- For other channels, ReB‑prm SHALL apply the minimal structure required for that channel’s conventions.

---

### HLR‑20.169-003 — Audience‑appropriate realization  
ReB‑prm SHALL adapt `ReB.content` to `RPU.audience` without altering core meaning.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- Audience adaptation SHALL affect tone, level of detail, and terminology, not semantics.  
- Audience adaptation SHALL remain consistent with upstream tone and constraints.  
- Audience adaptation SHALL not contradict any invariants.

---

### HLR‑20.169-004 — Constraint honoring in final output  
ReB‑prm SHALL honor all applicable constraints reflected in `RPU.constraint_state`.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- Length constraints SHALL be enforced on the final content (e.g., approximate sentence/word bounds).  
- Format constraints SHALL be enforced in the final structure (e.g., bullet lists, sections).  
- Safety constraints SHALL be enforced; if they cannot be satisfied, ReB‑prm SHALL avoid emitting unsafe content.

---

### HLR‑20.169-005 — Invariant preservation in final output  
ReB‑prm SHALL preserve all invariants indicated as preserved in `RPU.invariant_state`.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- Invariants SHALL appear in the final content when they require explicit mention (e.g., “must mention time: 10am”).  
- Invariants that constrain style or structure SHALL be reflected in the final artifact.  
- ReB‑prm SHALL not emit content that contradicts any preserved invariant.

---

### HLR‑20.169-006 — No upstream semantic mutation  
ReB‑prm SHALL NOT alter the core meaning established by REx, RPlan, and RPU.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- ReB‑prm SHALL not introduce new claims, entities, or commitments.  
- ReB‑prm SHALL not remove required semantic elements that RPU has marked as realized.  
- ReB‑prm SHALL restrict itself to realization, formatting, and style, not planning or reinterpretation.

---

### HLR‑20.169-007 — Provenance extension  
ReB‑prm SHALL extend the provenance chain (`RPU.trace`) into `ReB.trace`.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- The extension SHALL include a timestamped entry for ReB‑prm.  
- The extension SHALL include the version of this primitive.  

---

### HLR‑20.169-008 — Single‑artifact emission  
ReB‑prm SHALL emit a single, coherent `ReB.content` artifact per invocation.

**Mechanistic notes:**

- ReB‑prm SHALL not emit multiple competing realizations.  
- ReB‑prm SHALL ensure `ReB.content` is self‑contained and ready for delivery on the specified channel.

---

## 4. ReB structure specification

```yaml
ReB:
  content: <final user-visible artifact>
  channel: <from RPU.channel>
  audience: <from RPU.audience>
  constraints_applied:
    <summary of constraints enforced>
  invariants_preserved:
    <summary of invariants preserved>
  trace:
    <extended provenance>
```

---

## 5. Invariants

- **HLR‑20.169-009** — ReB‑prm SHALL only operate on RPU structures produced by a valid RPU‑prm.  
- **HLR‑20.169-010** — ReB‑prm SHALL not re‑enter planning or updating; it performs realization only.  
- **HLR‑20.169-011** — ReB‑prm SHALL produce content that is internally consistent with `RPU.partial_realization`.  
- **HLR‑20.169-012** — ReB‑prm SHALL not emit content that violates any invariant marked as preserved in `RPU.invariant_state`.

## 6. Example end‑to‑end

### Input RPU (simplified)
```yaml
RPU.partial_realization:
  target: "team"
  event: "meeting"
  time: "tomorrow 10am"
  skeleton: "<email_skeleton>"
  tone_applied: "friendly"
  structure_ready: true

RPU.resolved_ambiguities: []
RPU.filled_dependencies: []
RPU.constraint_state:
  length: "satisfied"
  format: "email"
RPU.invariant_state:
  "must mention time: 10am": "preserved"

RPU.channel: "email"
RPU.audience: "internal team"
RPU.trace: [...]
```

### Output ReB
```yaml
ReB.content: |
  Hi team,

  This is a friendly reminder about our meeting tomorrow at 10am.
  Please be on time and come prepared with your updates.

  Best,
  [Your Name]

ReB.channel: "email"
ReB.audience: "internal team"

ReB.constraints_applied:
  length: "short email"
  format: "email structure"

ReB.invariants_preserved:
  - "must mention time: 10am"

ReB.trace:
  - ...previous...
  - {primitive: ReB-prm, version: 0.1, timestamp: ...}
```
