# 20.165 DCB Stability Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.165  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-05  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Defines geometric stability requirements for the Directional Conversation Basin (DCB) feedback element and provides a qualitative stability argument under TS contraction rules.

Numeric bounds and algorithms for DCB stability belong in 20.95 or the 50-series; this module remains geometric and implementation-agnostic.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes DCB stability obligations from 20.106, 20.10 architectural principles, and 20.30 functional model contraction and boundedness semantics.

## Stability Requirements

1. HLR-20.165-001: DCB feedback SHALL NOT amplify curvature along the TP trajectory under equivalent persisted state.
2. HLR-20.165-002: DCB SHALL NOT create oscillation or runaway directional-change event sequences.
3. HLR-20.165-003: DCB SHALL NOT recursively modify its own geometric input state.
4. HLR-20.165-004: DCB SHALL preserve TS contraction properties defined in 20.10 and 20.30, including bounded routing influence and non-expansive feedback.
5. HLR-20.165-005: DCB stability requirements SHALL remain qualitative in this module and SHALL NOT specify numeric thresholds or procedural algorithms.
6. HLR-20.165-006: DCB stability violations SHALL be detectable through deterministic observability of directional-change event rates and trajectory geometry, without requiring semantic interpretation.

## Qualitative Stability Argument

7. HLR-20.165-007: Because DCB feedback is bounded and non-expansive (HLR-20.106-018), curvature along the TP trajectory cannot grow without bound under TS contraction rules in 20.10 and 20.30; directional-change influence remains a finite geometric perturbation on routing hints consumed by TR, not an unbounded semantic state expansion.
8. HLR-20.165-008: DCB stability SHALL be preserved when DCB events are consumed only under `tr_needs_update` gating, because geometric feedback cannot force unbounded TR execution cycles without concurrent semantic-writer stale signaling defined in 20.37.
