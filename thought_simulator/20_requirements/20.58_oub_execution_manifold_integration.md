# 20.58 OuB ↔ Execution Manifold Integration

**Document ID:** 20.58  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Draft — rough draft (Stage 2, **unapproved** per [20.500](20.500_refactoring_for_dual_TS_pipeline.md))

## Purpose

Defines the **integration contract** between **OuB** (Output Basin expression) and the Pipeline B execution manifold (`exec_plan`, XP, OpBeh/OBG/XlateR). This document **does not duplicate** [20.110](20.110_oub_requirements.md) — it hardens dual-pipeline boundaries and XP/`exec_trace` binding for the B-phase expression stage.

**OQ-004 resolution (draft):** 20.58 = integration layer; 20.110 body hardening deferred to **Stage 4** (re-review / v0.2 bump), not a parallel duplicate module.

## 1. Dual-Pipeline Expression Stage

```text
exec_plan (triple + expression_plan_ref) + frozen semantic_core
       → OuB (seed-bound expression selection)
       → oub_artifact_ref in exec_trace
       → IMR evaluation
```

1. **HLR-20.058-001:** OuB in Pipeline B SHALL execute only after `exec_plan` commit for the active `commit_id` / XP pass.
2. **HLR-20.058-002:** OuB SHALL read `semantic_core` only via `semantic_snapshot_ref` frozen at `mtp_update` — not live mutable MTP drafts.
3. **HLR-20.058-003:** OuB SHALL consume `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, and `expression_plan_ref` from `exec_plan` — not from TP.

---

## 2. Writes and Artifacts

```text
oub_artifact_ref {
  artifact_id,
  commit_id,
  cycle_id,
  xp_id,
  exec_plan_ref,
  seed_scope_ref,
  register_rendered?,
  content_hash,
  oub_reason_codes[]
}
```

4. **HLR-20.058-004:** OuB SHALL write expression artifacts only to `exec_trace` (append-only) and SHALL NOT write `semantic_core` or `exec_plan` body fields (except read).
5. **HLR-20.058-005:** Every OuB invocation SHALL append `oub_artifact_ref` to `exec_trace` and MAY denormalize latest ref on XP per 20.205 D7.
6. **HLR-20.058-006:** OuB SHALL NOT write TP fields in dual-pipeline mode; legacy 20.110 TP tagging paths SHALL be disabled or remapped to `exec_trace` audit in Stage 4 20.110 hardening.

---

## 3. Seed Boundary

7. **HLR-20.058-007:** Seed SHALL affect OuB expression selection only (HLR-20.012-010, 20.110-002).
8. **HLR-20.058-008:** Identical `exec_plan` + frozen `semantic_core` + seed → identical `oub_artifact_ref` content hash.

---

## 4. Messy-Input and Presentation

9. **HLR-20.058-009:** OuB SHALL NOT clear `messy_input_record` flags or smooth `conflict_flag` in `semantic_core` (20.17, 20.31-018).
10. **HLR-20.058-010:** OuB presentation under `underspec_flag` or `conflict_flag` SHALL follow OBG/register policy without inventing resolved meaning.

---

## 5. IMR Handoff

11. **HLR-20.058-011:** IMR SHALL consume `oub_artifact_ref` from `exec_trace` for the same `commit_id` / XP pass (20.45).
12. **HLR-20.058-012:** OuB SHALL NOT emit `CorrectionTrigger` directly — IMR owns taxonomy classification.

---

## 6. Incomplete-Meaning (Stage 4 Alignment Note)

Legacy 20.110 HLR-20.110-005–009 reference TP tagging and IB pathways. **Draft posture:**

13. **HLR-20.058-013:** In dual-pipeline PoC, incomplete-meaning detection SHALL log to `exec_trace` with `oub_event_type = INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED` and SHALL schedule GB/IB pathways only through supervisory queue — not TP mutation.
14. **HLR-20.058-014:** Stage 4 20.110 hardening SHALL reconcile legacy TP-centric HLRs with this integration contract without weakening 20.105 TP boundary.

---

## Verification

15. **HLR-20.058-015:** Class 1 fixture: OuB stage after `exec_plan_commit`; artifact in `exec_trace`; strip leaves `semantic_core` unchanged.
16. **HLR-20.058-016:** Seed variant changes artifact hash only; `semantic_core` strip-identical.
17. **HLR-20.058-017:** No `lane_id` on OuB inputs/outputs.
18. **HLR-20.058-018:** XP `EXPRESSING` → `IMR_EVAL` transition requires `oub_artifact_ref` present.
