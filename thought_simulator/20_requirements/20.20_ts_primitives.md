# 20.20 TS Primitives

## Purpose
Defines canonical TS primitive contracts, responsibilities, and invariants, aligned with 20.10 architectural principles and 20.30 functional-model semantics.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.020-001: TP (Thought Packet) SHALL be a structured state container and SHALL NOT execute autonomous inference logic.
2. HLR-20.020-002: MTP (Master Thought Packet) SHALL be the canonical global semantic state and SHALL accept only deterministic, field-explicit updates.
3. HLR-20.020-003: OB (Object Basin) SHALL process lane-local TP views, extract local evidence, and emit bounded ΔH% contributions.
4. HLR-20.020-004: RB (Relational Basin) SHALL perform deterministic routing, transition, split, and merge arbitration under explicit policy rules.
5. HLR-20.020-005: TB (Truth Basin) SHALL output structured interpretation fields (for stance, affect, intent, and logic) and SHALL NOT emit probabilistic latent-state artifacts as primitive outputs.
6. HLR-20.020-006: InB (Input Basin) SHALL normalize and map external/noisy input into stable conceptual basin inputs deterministically and without probabilistic inference.
7. HLR-20.020-007: OuB (Output Basin) SHALL realize expression outputs from finalized MTP state, and controlled variability SHALL be confined to this layer through seed-bounded realization selection.
8. HLR-20.020-008: MB (Monitoring Basin) SHALL provide deterministic, non-intrusive observability and SHALL NOT directly mutate TS core meaning-construction state.
9. HLR-20.020-009: GB (Governing Basin) SHALL apply global constraints/policies deterministically and SHALL NOT bypass primitive interface contracts.
10. HLR-20.020-010: IB (Inquiry Basin) SHALL detect ambiguity/gaps and issue deterministic branch or clarification proposals under explicit trigger thresholds.
11. HLR-20.020-011: ΔH% SHALL be a normalized primitive accounting metric for lane contribution/missing-mass semantics and SHALL remain deterministic and auditable.
12. HLR-20.020-012: Primitive responsibilities SHALL be semantically non-overlapping; no primitive SHALL duplicate another primitive's authority boundary.
13. HLR-20.020-013: Every primitive SHALL expose deterministic, testable interfaces with explicit inputs, outputs, invariants, and failure modes.
14. HLR-20.020-014: Every primitive SHALL expose observability fields sufficient for replay, audit, and deterministic diagnostics.
15. HLR-20.020-015: Every primitive SHALL define TCU (or equivalent) cost metrics that are measurable, deterministic, and comparable across runs.
16. HLR-20.020-016: Primitive behavior SHALL be platform-independent and SHALL NOT depend on hardware- or runtime-specific nondeterministic side effects.
17. HLR-20.020-017: Primitive composition across the 20.30 pipeline (routing, splitting, OB processing, TB interpretation, merging, truth/done, response) SHALL preserve deterministic end-to-end semantics.
18. HLR-20.020-018: Primitive contracts SHALL preserve meaning stability under malformed or ill-formed input, including concept-based OB activation and bounded ΔH% updates.
19. HLR-20.020-019: Directional Conversation Basin (DCB) SHALL be classified as a geometric meta-basin element that emits directional-change events from TP trajectory geometry; DCB SHALL NOT be a semantic basin and SHALL NOT overlap OB authority (per HLR-20.020-012); DCB SHALL NOT depend on implementation-specific mechanics beyond geometric invariants defined in 20.106.

## Primitive Contract Notes
- Primitive boundaries in this module are normative and govern both implementation and verification scope.
- Historical naming aliases MAY be documented for lineage but SHALL NOT alter canonical primitive semantics.
