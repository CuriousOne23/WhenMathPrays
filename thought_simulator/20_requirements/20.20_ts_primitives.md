# 20.20 TS Primitives

## Purpose
Defines canonical primitive semantics and responsibilities.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.020-001: TP SHALL hold state and SHALL NOT execute inference logic.
2. HLR-20.020-002: OB SHALL extract local evidence and DeltaHPercent contributions.
3. HLR-20.020-003: RB SHALL route lane flow deterministically.
4. HLR-20.020-004: TB SHALL perform truth interpretation and merge-policy recommendations.
5. HLR-20.020-005: Primitive responsibilities SHALL NOT overlap semantically.
