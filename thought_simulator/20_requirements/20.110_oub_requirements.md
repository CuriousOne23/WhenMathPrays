# 20.110 OuB Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.110-001: OuB SHALL render output expressions from finalized MTP state.
2. HLR-20.110-002: OuB SHALL consume seed-controlled variability only at expression selection.
3. HLR-20.110-003: OuB output mapping SHALL remain deterministic for fixed MTP and seed.
4. HLR-20.110-004: OuB SHALL NOT alter upstream meaning construction state.
5. HLR-20.110-005: OuB SHALL detect incomplete-meaning conditions when H% exceeds deployment-defined thresholds. OuB SHALL NOT externalize meaning under incomplete-meaning conditions.
6. HLR-20.110-006: OuB SHALL emit an "incomplete-meaning" tag into the TP when H% exceeds threshold. TS core SHALL convert this tag into an IB-Creation-Request for GB supervisory approval.
7. HLR-20.110-007: OuB SHALL notify GB indirectly via the TS IB-Creation-Request pathway. OuB SHALL NOT communicate with GB directly.
8. HLR-20.110-008: Incomplete-meaning detection, TP tagging, and IB-Creation-Request emission SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries.
9. HLR-20.110-009: OuB incomplete-meaning events SHALL be logged into the TP with:
  - oub_event_type = INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED
  - H% value
  - threshold value
  - reason_code
  - tp_seq
  - policy_signature
  OuB TP tagging SHALL be deterministic, append-only, and bounded.

## OuB Invariants
- Determinism invariant: For fixed MTP state and seed, OuB SHALL produce identical output expressions.
- Seed-Variability invariant: OuB SHALL consume seed-controlled variability only at output-expression selection and SHALL NOT influence upstream meaning-construction state.
