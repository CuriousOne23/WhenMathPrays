# 20.40.050 — IdOB‑prm (Identity Object Basin Primitive)

## Informative description (no SHALLs)

IdOB‑prm is the **identity‑conditioned Object Basin primitive** in the OB‑family. It operates on the **manifold chart already selected by RB‑prm** and refines the object’s **meaning** using identity, subculture, register, stance, and related interpretive cues that are already present in the TP. IdOB‑prm does not extract structure, does not produce residue, and does not modify routing metadata. It exists to perform **meaning‑level manifold interpretation** once structural OBs and SSG have completed and RB‑prm has determined that identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.

IdOB‑prm is invoked after a routing cycle (`RB‑prm → RTU‑prm`) has placed the TP in the appropriate manifold chart and scheduled IdOB‑prm as the next OB‑family primitive. IdOB‑prm may be invoked multiple times in a session (serial IdOB passes) when layered identity‑conditioned meaning refinements are needed. IdOB‑prm prepares the TP for truth routing (TR) and potential Path B realization by resolving meaning features that cannot be inferred from structural OBs, SSG, or routing metadata alone.

---

## Normative requirements

### 1. Nature of IdOB‑prm

**HLR‑20.40.50‑001** — IdOB‑prm SHALL be an OB‑family primitive that operates on the manifold to perform identity‑conditioned meaning interpretation.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑002** — IdOB‑prm SHALL operate only within the manifold chart previously selected by RB‑prm.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑003** — IdOB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑level updates without altering manifold topology or structural object representation.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑004** — IdOB‑prm SHALL be serially chainable, allowing multiple IdOB‑prm invocations when additional identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑005** — IdOB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose routing state has been committed by RTU‑prm.

### 2. What IdOB‑prm is not

**HLR‑20.40.50‑006** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT produce structural residue or structural deltas.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑007** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT read or modify routing vectors, routing deltas, or TP fields owned by RB‑prm or RTU‑prm.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑008** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT trigger SSG or depend on SSG execution after its own invocation.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑009** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT participate in Path B realization or expression‑layer processing.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑010** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by SSG, RB‑prm, or RTU‑prm for routing or structural interpretation.

### 3. Invocation and position in the lineup

**HLR‑20.40.50‑011** — IdOB‑prm SHALL be invocable only after a routing cycle `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` has completed.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑012** — IdOB‑prm SHALL be scheduled by TS as an OB‑family primitive following `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` when RB‑prm determines that identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑013** — IdOB‑prm SHALL execute after structural OB‑family primitives and SSG have completed for the current cycle, unless TS explicitly schedules IdOB‑prm earlier for a meaning‑only refinement pass that does not depend on additional structural extraction.

### 4. Meaning ΔH% and semantics tagging

**HLR‑20.40.50‑014** — IdOB‑prm SHALL compute and write a meaning‑delta field `TP.meaning_delta_h` representing the magnitude of meaning‑level refinement performed in the current IdOB‑prm invocation.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑015** — IdOB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.idob_semantics[]` with the identity‑conditioned semantics it resolved (e.g., subculture‑conditioned meaning, stance, register, actor‑specific semantics, cultural semantics, referent semantics).  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑016** — IdOB‑prm SHALL update `TP.meaning_semantics[]` with meaning‑adjacent cues required by TR and any subsequent OB‑family primitives that depend on meaning‑level interpretation.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑017** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by SSG, RB‑prm, or RTU‑prm.

### 5. Completion and next‑OB signaling

**HLR‑20.40.50‑018** — IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.idob_complete = true` when its meaning‑level refinement for the current cycle is complete.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑019** — IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.idob_complete = false` when additional IdOB‑prm passes are required for the same TP.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑020** — IdOB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.idob_next_ob_candidates[]` with OB‑family primitives that may be required for further meaning‑level refinement, without modifying routing vectors or routing eligibility fields.

### 6. Path B eligibility tagging

**HLR‑20.40.50‑021** — IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.path_b_eligible = true` when identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is sufficient for TR and potential Path B realization checks to proceed.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑022** — IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.path_b_eligible = false` when identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is incomplete or when the TP is not eligible for Path B realization.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑023** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT initiate Path B; it only signals eligibility via TP fields.

### 7. TP update request

**HLR‑20.40.50‑024** — IdOB‑prm SHALL issue a meaning‑level TP update request `TPU.idob_update` containing only the following fields: `meaning_delta_h`, `idob_semantics[]`, `meaning_semantics[]`, `idob_complete`, `path_b_eligible`, and `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`.  
**HLR‑20.40.50‑025** — IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT request routing updates, structural updates, or TR updates; those updates remain the responsibility of RB‑prm, RTU‑prm, TR, and their associated TP update primitives.

---

## Interfaces (informative)

- **Upstream:** IdOB‑prm is scheduled by TS after a routing cycle `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` has completed and RB‑prm has determined that identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.  
- **Downstream:** After IdOB‑prm completes and `TPU.idob_update` is committed, RB‑prm and RTU‑prm run in the next routing cycle to consume IdOB‑prm’s meaning‑level tags (`idob_complete`, `meaning_delta_h`, `idob_semantics[]`, `path_b_eligible`, `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`) and determine whether additional OB‑family primitives, TR, or Path B checks are required.  
- **Routing:** IdOB‑prm does not read or modify routing vectors or routing deltas. All routing computation and commit remain the responsibility of RB‑prm and RTU‑prm.  
- **Truth and Path B:** IdOB‑prm prepares the TP for TR and potential Path B realization by resolving identity‑conditioned meaning and tagging `path_b_eligible`. TR and RB‑prm use these tags to decide whether and how Path B may proceed.
