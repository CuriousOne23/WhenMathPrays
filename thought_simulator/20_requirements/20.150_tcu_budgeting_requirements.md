# 20.150 TCU Budgeting Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic TCU budgeting, measurement, overrun handling, and reporting requirements for TS subsystems.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes TCU budgeting obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including bounded resource accounting, deterministic degradation policy, auditability, profile-bound enforcement, and stable cross-subsystem comparability.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.150-001: Each TS subsystem SHALL have deterministic min/typ/max TCU budget ranges.
2. HLR-20.150-002: TCU budgets SHALL be explicit, versioned, and comparable across supported execution profiles.
3. HLR-20.150-003: Budget overrun handling SHALL follow deterministic degradation or reject policy and SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic recovery.
4. HLR-20.150-004: Per-tick TCU accounting SHALL be logged by subsystem in append-only, time-indexed records.
5. HLR-20.150-005: Calibration baseline computation SHALL be documented, reproducible, and bound to execution-signature/profile state.
6. HLR-20.150-006: TCU measurement SHALL be deterministic for identical effective workload, profile, and seed-bound state.
7. HLR-20.150-007: TCU reporting SHALL include subsystem usage, total cycle usage, overrun amount, and degradation actions taken.
8. HLR-20.150-008: TCU budget enforcement SHALL occur at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with unsafeguarded TS state changes.
9. HLR-20.150-009: TCU budgeting SHALL remain platform-independent and SHALL not depend on hardware-, OS-, or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
10. HLR-20.150-010: TCU budget decisions SHALL use explicit reason codes and policy signatures for any degrade, defer, or reject outcome.
11. HLR-20.150-011: TCU budgets SHALL be maintained for all bounded TS subsystems, including routing, splitting, merging, truth evaluation, intake, supervision, and output realization where applicable.
12. HLR-20.150-012: TCU overrun behavior SHALL preserve determinism, boundedness, and replayability for identical effective state.
13. HLR-20.150-013: TCU accounting SHALL preserve FIFO and tick ordering semantics and SHALL use deterministic sequence/token state as the ordering authority.
14. HLR-20.150-014: TCU budget updates and calibration changes SHALL be versioned and SHALL require explicit re-verification.
15. HLR-20.150-015: TCU budgeting SHALL be auditable per tick and SHALL remain consistent with TP, MTP, and supervisory logging requirements.
16. HLR-20.150-016: TCU budgeting requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

## Functional Boundaries
- TCU budgeting owns deterministic resource accounting, budget enforcement, and reporting.
- Subsystems own their local work but SHALL remain within configured TCU envelopes.
- GB supervision MAY consume TCU metrics for flow-control decisions but SHALL not mutate budgeting semantics directly.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic budget accounting for identical workload and profile state.
- Deterministic overrun/degrade/reject outcomes under equivalent boundary conditions.
- Deterministic calibration baseline behavior and versioned profile binding.
- Per-tick append-only TCU audit evidence and cross-subsystem comparability.
