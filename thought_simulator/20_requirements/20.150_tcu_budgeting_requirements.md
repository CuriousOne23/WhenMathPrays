# 20.150 TCU Budgeting Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.150-001: Each TS subsystem SHALL have min/typ/max TCU budget ranges.
2. HLR-20.150-002: Budget overrun handling SHALL follow deterministic degradation policy.
3. HLR-20.150-003: Per-tick TCU accounting SHALL be logged by subsystem.
4. HLR-20.150-004: Calibration baseline computation SHALL be documented and reproducible.
