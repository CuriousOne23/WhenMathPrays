# 20.131 Topology Executor (TE) Requirements

## Purpose
Defines the deterministic **Topology Executor (TE)** responsible for performing all split and merge operations requested by OB and approved by RB arbitration. TE executes topology transitions, updates TP lineage, writes topology event logs, and ensures deterministic, replayable structural state for downstream OB, TR, and RB cycles.

## Parent Scope
This module normatively decomposes the **execution** obligations implied by 20.130 (Split/Merge Requirements).  
OB detects → RB arbitrates → **TE executes** → RB routes.

## 1. Invocation and Execution Conditions

1. **HLR‑20.131‑001:** TE SHALL execute **only** when a TP carries an OB‑issued SplitRequest or MergeRequest.
2. **HLR‑20.131‑002:** TE SHALL execute **only after** RB arbitration approves the requested topology operation.
3. **HLR‑20.131‑003:** TE SHALL NOT execute when no split/merge request is present.
4. **HLR‑20.131‑004:** TE SHALL run in the deterministic order:  
   **OB → [TE if requested] → RB → next OB**.
5. **HLR‑20.131‑005:** TE SHALL NOT read or modify `TP.TR`, `tr_needs_update`, or any semantic_core field.

## 2. Split Execution Requirements

6. **HLR‑20.131‑006:** When executing a split, TE SHALL create one or more new lane TPs, each containing a deterministic partition of the original TP fields.
7. **HLR‑20.131‑007:** TE SHALL preserve parent TP lineage metadata in all derived lane TPs.
8. **HLR‑20.131‑008:** TE SHALL preserve `tp_seq` ordering and causal lineage markers.
9. **HLR‑20.131‑009:** TE SHALL NOT introduce inference, hallucinated reconstruction, or meaning invention during split.
10. **HLR‑20.131‑010:** TE SHALL apply the explicit split rules defined in 20.130 and SHALL remain reproducible for identical input state.

## 3. Merge Execution Requirements

11. **HLR‑20.131‑011:** When executing a merge, TE SHALL deterministically combine all contributing lane TPs into a single MTP draft.
12. **HLR‑20.131‑012:** TE SHALL reconcile fields using explicit field‑wise merge rules defined in 20.130.
13. **HLR‑20.131‑013:** TE SHALL preserve and consolidate lineage from all contributing lane TPs.
14. **HLR‑20.131‑014:** TE SHALL preserve ΔH% contribution and missing‑mass accounting during merge.
15. **HLR‑20.131‑015:** TE SHALL NOT introduce inference, smoothing, or semantic reconstruction during merge.

## 4. Topology Event Logging

16. **HLR‑20.131‑016:** TE SHALL append a deterministic `topology_event_log[]` entry to all affected TPs.
17. **HLR‑20.131‑017:** Each topology event log entry SHALL include:  
    - event_type (split or merge)  
    - parent_ids[]  
    - child_ids[]  
    - reason_code  
    - policy_signature  
    - sequence_id  
    - timestamp or cycle index  
18. **HLR‑20.131‑018:** TE SHALL ensure topology logs are append‑only and replay‑stable.
19. **HLR‑20.131‑019:** TE SHALL ensure topology logs are canonicalized before export and replay diff.

## 5. Determinism and Boundedness

20. **HLR‑20.131‑020:** TE SHALL be deterministic for identical effective input state.
21. **HLR‑20.131‑021:** TE SHALL enforce split/merge boundedness constraints defined in 20.130.
22. **HLR‑20.131‑022:** TE SHALL NOT reorder, skip, or speculate on sequence state.
23. **HLR‑20.131‑023:** TE SHALL NOT mutate finalized prior‑state entries; TE SHALL create new deterministic state records.

## 6. TP and MTP Consistency

24. **HLR‑20.131‑024:** TE SHALL ensure all resulting TPs are structurally valid for downstream OB processing.
25. **HLR‑20.131‑025:** TE SHALL ensure that merged outputs conform to MTP schema requirements in 20.115.
26. **HLR‑20.131‑026:** TE SHALL NOT commit semantic updates; TPU remains the sole semantic writer.

## 7. Safety and Governance

27. **HLR‑20.131‑027:** TE SHALL NOT bypass GB supervisory constraints defined in 20.30.
28. **HLR‑20.131‑028:** TE SHALL expose deterministic test hooks for unit, integration, and replay verification.

## Functional Boundaries
- **OB:** Detects identity conflict/complementarity; issues requests.  
- **RB:** Arbitrates split/merge; does not execute.  
- **TE:** Executes topology transitions; updates lineage; logs events.  
- **TR:** Semantic routing only; read‑only.  
- **TPU:** Semantic commit only; never performs topology.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic split/merge execution for identical inputs.  
- Deterministic lineage preservation.  
- Deterministic topology_event_log entries.  
- Replay stability under 20.36.  
- Conformance with 20.130 boundedness rules.
