# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Input Basin (InB) is the **first deterministic boundary** of Path‑A.  
Its role is to ingest raw external signals and convert them into **bounded, canonical, schema‑validated intake fields** without performing semantic interpretation.

InB ensures that every external input—regardless of noise, encoding, formatting, or transport artifacts—is transformed into a **stable, replay‑equivalent, pre‑semantic representation** that downstream modules can trust.

InB responsibilities include:

- deterministic ingestion of external signals into **TP.intake**  
- canonicalization under versioned parser/profile rules  
- schema validation and bounded reject/degrade behavior  
- FIFO‑preserving intake ordering  
- isolation of transport/session metadata  
- deterministic provenance recording  
- non‑inferential normalization of noisy or malformed input  
- strict separation between raw input handling and semantic repair  

InB is a **data‑integrity primitive**, not a semantic module.  
It ensures Path‑A begins from a **stable, deterministic, auditable foundation**.

---

# **2. Non‑Goals** *(Informative)*

InB does **not**:

- infer user intent  
- perform semantic error correction  
- choose between plausible meanings  
- apply user‑specific shorthand interpretation  
- construct meaning, routing, truth, or inquiry  

Semantic repair begins only after:

```
IIInB → IE → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU → TP.semantic
```

---

# **3. Input Correction Scope** *(Informative)*

| Term | InB Role |
|------|----------|
| **Structural / non‑inferential correction** | In scope — encoding normalization, schema validation, deterministic lexical canonicalization, bounded reject/degrade, MI_NOISE‑class surface handling. |
| **Semantic input error correction** | Out of scope — semantic repair begins only after IE; TP.semantic remains empty. |

---

# **4. Architectural Position** *(Informative)*

```
External Input → InB → IIInB → IE → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1)
```

InB is the **entry point** of the entire inference pipeline.

---

# **5. Inputs and Outputs** *(Informative)*

### **Inputs (read‑only)**  
- raw external signals  
- transport/session metadata  
- parser/profile configuration  
- execution signature  

### **Outputs**  
- **TP.intake** (canonicalized, bounded, schema‑validated)  
- **TP.metadata.intake_provenance**  
- **TP.metadata.intake_audit**  
- **TP.metadata.transport**  
- **TP.metadata.sequence**  
- **TP.metadata.profile_signature**

---

# **6. Functional Boundaries** *(Informative)*

InB owns:

- external‑signal normalization  
- deterministic canonicalization  
- schema validation  
- bounded intake acceptance/rejection behavior  

InB does **not** own:

- semantic repair  
- conversation/state integration  
- downstream deterministic processing  
- supervisory control decisions  

---

# **7. Determinism and Replay** *(Informative)*

InB guarantees:

- identical input → identical **TP.intake**  
- identical input → identical **TP.metadata**  
- deterministic canonicalization  
- deterministic provenance  
- deterministic reject/degrade behavior  
- deterministic FIFO ordering  

Replay systems must reconstruct identical intake state.

---

# **8. Error Behavior** *(Informative)*

Malformed input results in:

- deterministic reject or canonicalize  
- fixed audit reason codes  
- append‑only audit entries  
- deterministic provenance  
- bounded degrade behavior  

InB never halts the pipeline.

# **9. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**  
*(Reorganized, merged/split for clarity; all original information preserved)*

---

## **9.1 Deterministic Intake Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑001**  
InB SHALL ingest external signals into bounded **TP.intake** fields.

### **HLR‑20.100‑002**  
InB SHALL NOT perform inference or truth arbitration; **TP.semantic** SHALL NOT be constructed or modified by InB.

### **HLR‑20.100‑003**  
InB transformations SHALL be deterministic and replayable, producing identical **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata.intake_provenance** for identical inputs.

---

## **9.2 Schema Validation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑004**  
InB input mappings into **TP.intake** SHALL be schema‑validated.

### **HLR‑20.100‑005**  
InB canonicalization SHALL normalize equivalent surface forms deterministically under versioned parser/profile rules.

---

## **9.3 FIFO Ordering Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑006**  
InB SHALL preserve FIFO ordering metadata in **TP.metadata.sequence** and SHALL NOT reorder, skip, or speculate on sequence state.

---

## **9.4 Malformed Input Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑007**  
InB malformed‑input handling SHALL follow deterministic reject‑or‑canonicalize policy with fixed audit reason codes recorded in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

### **HLR‑20.100‑008**  
InB SHALL enforce deterministic limits on payload size, token count, and field cardinality using bounded reject/degrade behavior applied to **TP.intake**.

---

## **9.5 Transport Metadata Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑009**  
InB SHALL isolate transport/session metadata into **TP.metadata.transport** and SHALL prevent implicit meaning mutation of **TP.intake** by transport attributes.

---

## **9.6 Encoding Normalization Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑010**  
InB SHALL map source encodings to canonical internal representation deterministically, including Unicode and escape normalization rules applied to **TP.intake.surface**.

---

## **9.7 Provenance Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑011**  
InB SHALL emit deterministic provenance entries into **TP.metadata.intake_provenance** for intake source, normalization profile, schema version, and acceptance/rejection outcome.

### **HLR‑20.100‑012**  
InB acceptance/rejection outcomes SHALL be auditable, append‑only, and reproducible for identical input/signature state, recorded in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

---

## **9.8 Profile/Policy Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑013**  
InB SHALL apply execution‑signature‑bound profile precedence over environment defaults, reflected in **TP.metadata.profile_signature**.

### **HLR‑20.100‑014**  
InB profile/policy activation changes SHALL activate only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL update **TP.metadata.profile_signature** deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.100‑015**  
On profile/policy activation validation failure, InB SHALL retain prior valid signature state and emit fixed audit reason codes into **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

---

## **9.9 Unsupported State Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑016**  
InB SHALL reject unsupported enum, schema, or wire‑map states deterministically with fixed audit reason codes in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

### **HLR‑20.100‑017**  
InB reason‑code dictionaries SHALL use immutable identifiers and versioned deterministic mapping behavior referenced by **TP.metadata.intake_audit.reason_code**.

---

## **9.10 Platform‑Independent Determinism Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑018**  
InB SHALL preserve platform‑independent deterministic behavior under equivalent input/signature/profile state, producing identical **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

---

## **9.11 Isolation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑019**  
InB SHALL NOT read or mutate IdOB, RB, TB, IB, GB, CIL, COB, COP, or MTP internal state and SHALL operate only on intake‑bound fields and approved interfaces.

---

## **9.12 Downstream Handoff Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑020**  
InB handoff contracts to downstream intake/integration pathways SHALL be explicit, versioned, testable, and auditable, defining the structure of **IE.intake** derived from **TP.intake**.

---

## **9.13 Timestamp Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑021**  
InB SHALL treat timestamps as evidence metadata only; ordering‑critical behavior SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state in **TP.metadata.sequence**.

---

## **9.14 Diagnostic Export Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑022**  
InB diagnostic exports for intake events SHALL use deterministic flush boundaries, canonical field ordering, and canonical serialization of **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

---

## **9.15 Zero‑Event Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑023**  
InB zero‑event windows SHALL follow deterministic empty‑artifact semantics reflected in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

---

## **9.16 Parent Invariant Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑024**  
InB requirements SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

---

## **9.17 Verification Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑025**  
InB behavior SHALL remain fully testable through deterministic fixtures covering valid, malformed, oversized, and unsupported‑profile/schema inputs.

---

## **9.18 Semantic Isolation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.100‑026**  
InB SHALL hand off accepted canonicalized input without introducing semantic interpretation; **TP.semantic** SHALL remain empty until downstream TS/GB‑governed components construct it.

---

# **10. Conformance** *(Informative)*

InB conforms when:

- intake is deterministic  
- provenance is deterministic  
- audit behavior is deterministic  
- FIFO ordering is preserved  
- malformed input is handled deterministically  
- semantic isolation is preserved  
- replay produces identical **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**
