## **Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Input Basin (InB) is the **first deterministic boundary** of Path‑A.  
Its purpose is to ingest raw external signals and convert them into **bounded, canonical, schema‑validated intake fields** without performing any semantic interpretation or truth arbitration.

InB provides the foundation on which all downstream reasoning depends.  
It ensures that every external input—regardless of noise, encoding, formatting, or transport artifacts—is transformed into a **stable, replay‑equivalent, pre‑semantic representation** that downstream modules can trust.

InB’s responsibilities include:

- **Deterministic ingestion** of external signals into **TP.intake** fields  
- **Canonicalization** of surface forms under versioned parser/profile rules  
- **Schema validation** and bounded reject/degrade behavior  
- **FIFO‑preserving intake ordering**  
- **Isolation of transport/session metadata** from **TP.intake** payload fields  
- **Deterministic provenance recording** into **TP.metadata.intake_provenance**  
- **Non‑inferential normalization** of noisy or malformed input  
- **Strict separation** between raw input handling and semantic repair (semantic repair begins only after IE → CEx → CE)

InB is the **entry point** of the entire inference pipeline.  
It guarantees that all downstream components—IIInB, CEx, CE, ISc, TPU, and TP(N+1)—receive input that is **clean, bounded, canonical, and free of semantic assumptions**.

InB is a **data‑integrity primitive**, not a semantic module.  
It exists to ensure that Path‑A begins from a **stable, deterministic, and auditable foundation** before any semantic processing occurs.

---

## **Non‑Goals**

- **Semantic input error correction** — inferring user intent, choosing between plausible meanings, or applying user‑specific shorthand meaning.  
  This is **out of scope for InB** and begins only after IIInB (deterministic repair) and IE (pre‑semantic envelope).  
  All semantic interpretation occurs downstream in CEx → CE → ISc → TPU → TP.semantic.

- **Meaning construction, routing, truth, or inquiry** — delegated to downstream Pipeline‑A components and GB‑governed pathways per HLR‑20.100‑026.

---

## **Input Correction Scope (Terminology)**

| Term | InB role |
|------|----------|
| **Structural / non‑inferential correction** | **In scope** — encoding normalization, schema validation, deterministic lexical canonicalization, bounded reject/degrade, `MI_NOISE`‑class surface handling. |
| **Semantic input error correction** | **Out of scope** — InB SHALL NOT perform; semantic repair begins only after IE and is reflected in **TP.semantic** downstream. |

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes InB obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including:
- 20.10 architectural constraints on determinism, bounded interfaces, noisy-input handling, and auditable behavior.
- 20.10 FIFO-preserving intake and ordering invariants across downstream basin flow.
- 20.30 functional constraints on deterministic pipeline stage behavior and explicit schema-stable primitive contracts.
- 20.30 deterministic parser/profile governance and reject-with-audit runtime invariants.

## Normative Requirements
1. **HLR‑20.100‑001**  
   InB SHALL ingest external signals into bounded **TP.intake** fields.

2. **HLR‑20.100‑002**  
   InB SHALL NOT perform inference or truth arbitration; **TP.semantic** SHALL NOT be constructed or modified by InB.

3. **HLR‑20.100‑003**  
   InB transformations SHALL be deterministic and replayable, producing identical **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata.intake_provenance** for identical inputs.

4. **HLR‑20.100‑004**  
   InB input mappings into **TP.intake** SHALL be schema‑validated.

5. **HLR‑20.100‑005**  
   InB canonicalization SHALL normalize equivalent surface forms deterministically under versioned parser/profile rules.

6. **HLR‑20.100‑006**  
   InB SHALL preserve FIFO ordering metadata in **TP.metadata.sequence** and SHALL NOT reorder, skip, or speculate on sequence state.

7. **HLR‑20.100‑007**  
   InB malformed‑input handling SHALL follow deterministic reject‑or‑canonicalize policy with fixed audit reason codes recorded in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

8. **HLR‑20.100‑008**  
   InB SHALL enforce deterministic limits on payload size, token count, and field cardinality using bounded reject/degrade behavior applied to **TP.intake**.

9. **HLR‑20.100‑009**  
   InB SHALL isolate transport/session metadata into **TP.metadata.transport** and SHALL prevent implicit meaning mutation of **TP.intake** by transport attributes.

10. **HLR‑20.100‑010**  
    InB SHALL map source encodings to canonical internal representation deterministically, including Unicode and escape normalization rules applied to **TP.intake.surface**.

11. **HLR‑20.100‑011**  
    InB SHALL emit deterministic provenance entries into **TP.metadata.intake_provenance** for intake source, normalization profile, schema version, and acceptance/rejection outcome.

12. **HLR‑20.100‑012**  
    InB acceptance/rejection outcomes SHALL be auditable, append‑only, and reproducible for identical input/signature state, recorded in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

13. **HLR‑20.100‑013**  
    InB SHALL apply execution‑signature‑bound profile precedence over environment defaults, reflected in **TP.metadata.profile_signature**.

14. **HLR‑20.100‑014**  
    InB profile/policy activation changes SHALL activate only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL update **TP.metadata.profile_signature** deterministically.

15. **HLR‑20.100‑015**  
    On profile/policy activation validation failure, InB SHALL retain prior valid signature state and emit fixed audit reason codes into **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

16. **HLR‑20.100‑016**  
    InB SHALL reject unsupported enum, schema, or wire‑map states deterministically with fixed audit reason codes in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

17. **HLR‑20.100‑017**  
    InB reason‑code dictionaries SHALL use immutable identifiers and versioned deterministic mapping behavior referenced by **TP.metadata.intake_audit.reason_code**.

18. **HLR‑20.100‑018**  
    InB SHALL preserve platform‑independent deterministic behavior under equivalent input/signature/profile state, producing identical **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

19. **HLR‑20.100‑019**  
    InB SHALL NOT read or mutate IdOB, RB, TB, IB, GB, CIL, COB, COP, or MTP internal state and SHALL operate only on intake‑bound fields and approved interfaces, producing only **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

20. **HLR‑20.100‑020**  
    InB handoff contracts to downstream intake/integration pathways SHALL be explicit, versioned, testable, and auditable, defining the structure of **IE.intake** derived from **TP.intake**.

21. **HLR‑20.100‑021**  
    InB SHALL treat timestamps as evidence metadata only; ordering‑critical behavior SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state in **TP.metadata.sequence**.

22. **HLR‑20.100‑022**  
    InB diagnostic exports for intake events SHALL use deterministic flush boundaries, canonical field ordering, and canonical serialization of **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

23. **HLR‑20.100‑023**  
    InB zero‑event windows SHALL follow deterministic empty‑artifact semantics (canonical empty artifact or no artifact with fixed reason code) reflected in **TP.metadata.intake_audit**.

24. **HLR‑20.100‑024**  
    InB requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

25. **HLR‑20.100‑025**  
    InB behavior SHALL remain fully testable through deterministic fixtures covering valid, malformed, oversized, and unsupported‑profile/schema inputs, validating **TP.intake** and **TP.metadata**.

26. **HLR‑20.100‑026**  
    InB SHALL hand off accepted canonicalized input without introducing semantic interpretation; **TP.semantic** SHALL remain empty until downstream TS/GB‑governed components construct it.

## Functional Boundaries
- InB owns external-signal normalization, deterministic canonicalization, schema validation, and bounded intake acceptance/rejection behavior.
- InB **error correction** means **non-inferential intake normalization only**; semantic repair before Pipeline A is explicitly **not** an InB obligation.
- CIL and TS-core pathways own conversation/state integration and downstream deterministic processing.
- GB owns supervisory control decisions and SHALL NOT be bypassed by InB for global behavior changes.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic acceptance/rejection equivalence for identical input/signature/profile state.
- Deterministic canonicalization across equivalent noisy surface forms.
- Deterministic FIFO-preserving sequence metadata continuity at handoff boundaries.
- Deterministic reject-with-audit behavior for malformed/oversized/unsupported states.
- Deterministic provenance and append-only intake audit evidence.
