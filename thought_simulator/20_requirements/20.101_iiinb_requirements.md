# **20.101 IIInB Requirements (v0.2 — Architecture‑Aligned)**  
**Document ID:** 20.101  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026‑06‑12  
**Status:** Draft v0.2 — Architecture‑Aligned (CuriousOne23, Copilot)

---

# **Purpose**

Defines deterministic **Input Inference/Repair Basin (IIInB)** behavior as the profiled pre‑Pipeline stage **`input_semantic_repair`**, performing **bounded shorthand repair only**, using read‑only USP rules after InB normalization.

IIInB is the **only** primitive permitted to perform **pre‑semantic shorthand expansion**, and it is the **exclusive upstream producer** of the cleaned intake envelope consumed by **CEx**.

### **Updated Intake Ordering (HQ‑001):**

```
InB → IIInB → CEx → CE → ISc
```

IIInB is not part of semantic inference or scoring logic; it is strictly pre‑semantic repair upstream of CEx..

---

# **Non‑Goals (Updated)**

IIInB SHALL NOT:

- perform surface canonicalization (InB owns this)  
- perform silent gap‑fill or generative completion  
- resolve `MI_INCOMP` or `MI_VAGUE`  
- perform long‑term repair  
- read or consult **CIL**  
- extract context or construct **CE**  
- perform semantic scoring  
- generate candidate sets  
- participate in the inference pipeline beyond producing the cleaned envelope  
- write to `semantic_core`, `TP.TR`, MTP, or any semantic field  
- interact with IB, TB, GBIB, or GB  

---

# **Parent Scope (Updated)**

- **Program:** Track H (20.510)  
- **Reads:** InB canonicalized intake, USP snapshot  
- **Writes:** Intake‑bound repair tags on TP only  
- **Downstream Consumer:** **CEx** (explicit)  
- **Escalates:** Unknown shorthand → CIL  
- **Does not modify:** 20.10, 20.12, 20.20, 20.30  

---

# **Functional Boundaries (Updated)**

| Component | Relationship to IIInB |
|----------|------------------------|
| **InB** | Upstream surface normalization |
| **USP** | Read‑only rule snapshot |
| **CEx** | **Immediate downstream consumer** of IIInB output |
| **CE** | Not produced by IIInB |
| **ISc** | Downstream scoring primitive (IIInB does not interact) |
| **CIL** | **IIInB SHALL NOT read CIL** |
| **RB / OB / TR / TB** | IIInB SHALL NOT appear in these pipelines |
| **UPI / IMR** | No direct invocation |

# **Normative Requirements (Updated)**

## **Activation and Placement**

1. **HLR‑20.101‑001:** IIInB SHALL execute as stage wire `input_semantic_repair` only when `profile_enabled = true`.  
2. **HLR‑20.101‑002:** When `profile_enabled = false`, IIInB SHALL be skipped with zero semantic effect.  
3. **HLR‑20.101‑003:** IIInB SHALL run only after InB and before **CEx**:  
   ```
   InB → IIInB → CEx
   ```
4. **HLR‑20.101‑004 (NA):** *Old requirement referencing RB→OB→TR→TB chain.*  
   **Reason:** IIInB is no longer described relative to TB/IB pipelines.

### **Inserted Requirements (Placement Clarification)**

4. **HLR‑20.101‑003010:** IIInB SHALL NOT appear in the RB→OB→TR→TB chain.  
5. **HLR‑20.101‑003020:** IIInB SHALL NOT appear in the inference path beyond producing the cleaned envelope for CEx.  
6. **HLR‑20.101‑003030:** IIInB output SHALL be shaped for deterministic consumption by CEx.

---

## **USP Consumption**

5. **HLR‑20.101‑005:** Load USP via read‑only snapshot.  
6. **HLR‑20.101‑006:** Record `usp_version_ref` on every repair record.  
7. **HLR‑20.101‑007:** Apply only `ACTIVE` USP rules.  
8. **HLR‑20.101‑008:** IIInB SHALL NOT mutate USP.

---

## **Repair Semantics**

9. **HLR‑20.101‑009:** IIInB SHALL perform bounded pattern→expansion repair only.  
10. **HLR‑20.101‑010:** Deterministic segmentation into bounded spans.  
11. **HLR‑20.101‑011:** On rule match, emit `APPLIED`.  
12. **HLR‑20.101‑012:** On no match, emit `ESCALATED` → CIL.  
13. **HLR‑20.101‑013:** IIInB SHALL NOT resolve `MI_VAGUE` or `MI_INCOMP`.

### **Inserted Requirements (Context Boundaries)**

14. **HLR‑20.101‑013010:** IIInB SHALL NOT read CIL for any purpose.  
15. **HLR‑20.101‑013020:** IIInB SHALL NOT perform context extraction.  
16. **HLR‑20.101‑013030:** IIInB SHALL NOT construct or modify CE.  
17. **HLR‑20.101‑013040:** IIInB SHALL NOT perform semantic scoring or candidate generation.

---

## **Write Authority (TP Only)**

14. **HLR‑20.101‑014:** Writes confined to intake‑bound TP fields.  
15. **HLR‑20.101‑015:** IIInB SHALL NOT write semantic fields.  
16. **HLR‑20.101‑016:** Append one repair record per pass.

### **Inserted Requirement**

17. **HLR‑20.101‑016010:** IIInB SHALL NOT write any fields intended for CEx, CE, or ISc.
18. **HLR‑20.101‑016020:** — (No TP Reads), IIInB SHALL NOT read TP, MTP, semantic_core, TP.TR, or any meaning‑layer

---

## **Escalation**

17. **HLR‑20.101‑017:** Escalation emits `iiinb_escalation_ref` → CIL.  
18. **HLR‑20.101‑018:** IIInB SHALL NOT block Pipeline A.

---

## **Bounds, TCU, Determinism**

19. **HLR‑20.101‑019:** Enforce segment and apply caps.  
20. **HLR‑20.101‑020:** Report TCU usage.  
21. **HLR‑20.101‑021:** Deterministic and replayable.  
22. **HLR‑20.101‑022:** Use fixed reason codes.

---

## **Cross‑Program Separation**

23. **HLR‑20.101‑023:** IMR SHALL NOT invoke IIInB directly.  
24. **HLR‑20.101‑024:** IIInB SHALL NOT read/write Pipeline B envelopes.

### **Inserted Requirement**

25. **HLR‑20.101‑024010:** IIInB SHALL NOT interact with IB, TB, GBIB, or GB.

---

## **Parent Compliance and Testability**

25. **HLR‑20.101‑025:** Maintain parent invariants.  
26. **HLR‑20.101‑026:** Fully testable via fixtures.  
27. **HLR‑20.101‑027:** Canonical audit ordering.  
28. **HLR‑20.101‑028:** Diagnostics consumable by MB.

---

# **New Section: Downstream Handoff to CEx**

### **Inserted Requirements**

29. **HLR‑20.101‑029:** IIInB output SHALL be the sole upstream input to CEx.  
30. **HLR‑20.101‑030:** IIInB SHALL NOT read CIL; only CEx may.  
31. **HLR‑20.101‑031:** IIInB SHALL NOT embed global context into its output.  
32. **HLR‑20.101‑032:** IIInB SHALL produce a stable, bounded envelope suitable for CE construction by CEx.  
33. **HLR‑20.101‑033:** IIInB SHALL NOT attempt to infer or approximate CE fields.  
34. **HLR‑20.101‑034:** IIInB SHALL NOT perform long‑term repair; long‑term repair (if retained) SHALL use CE downstream.
