# 20.160 Randomness Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.160-001: Randomness SHALL be isolated to output-layer realization selection.
2. HLR-20.160-002: Seed SHALL be pseudo-random, documented, and reproducible.
3. HLR-20.160-003: Seed SHALL NOT affect OB, RB, TB, DeltaHPercent, truth, or MTP update logic.
4. HLR-20.160-004: Randomness-critical code changes SHALL require re-verification.
5. HLR-20.160-005: Any permitted stochastic behavior SHALL use a pinned PRNG algorithm version.
6. HLR-20.160-006: PRNG seed derivation and stream partitioning rules SHALL be deterministic and versioned.
7. HLR-20.160-007: PRNG profile version SHALL be bound to execution-signature deterministic profile state.
