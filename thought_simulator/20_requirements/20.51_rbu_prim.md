**20.51 — RBU‑prm (Routing Buffer Update Primitive)**  
**Status:** requirements  
**Source of truth:** this file  
**HLRs:** 001–020  

---

## **Purpose**  
Define the normative behavior of **RBU‑prm**, the post‑RB commit primitive responsible for writing RB‑prm’s routing deltas into the TP snapshot.  
RBU‑prm ensures:

- routing computation remains pure (RB‑prm)  
- routing commit is deterministic and auditable  
- TS and TR receive a stable routing state for the next cycle  

RBU‑prm performs **no routing**, **no classification**, and **no interpretation**.  
It is a **mechanical commit stage**.

## **Invocation Model (New Section)**

**HLR‑20.051‑010:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL be invoked exclusively by RB‑prm.**  
No other TS or semantic component may trigger RBU‑prm.

**HLR‑20.051‑011:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL NOT read or consume CIL metadata.**  
RBU‑prm is a non‑semantic commit primitive and SHALL NOT interpret any metadata produced by CIL, OB, GB, or semantic_core.

**HLR‑20.051‑012:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL commit only the routing envelope produced by RB‑prm.**  
RBU‑prm performs no routing, no arbitration, and no classification.

**HLR‑20.051‑013:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL be modeled as a conditional branch from RB‑prm, not a sequential pipeline stage.**  
RBU‑prm is invoked only when RB‑prm produces a routing delta requiring commit.

**HLR‑20.051‑014:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL be the sole writer of routing lineage and routing‑reason metadata into the TP.**  RB‑prm SHALL NOT write the TP directly.

**HLR‑20.051‑015:**  
**RBU‑prm SHALL NOT modify, enrich, or reinterpret the routing envelope.**  
It SHALL write the envelope exactly as provided by RB‑prm.

---

# **## 1. Definitions**

### **1.1 Routing Envelope**  
The structured output produced by RB‑prm containing lane selections, routing lineage, routing reasons, and staleness‑relevant metadata.

### **1.2 TP Routing Fields**  
The subset of TP fields reserved for routing metadata, including lane selection, lineage, arbitration notes, and routing deltas.

### **1.3 Commit Operation**  
A deterministic write of routing metadata into the TP snapshot, performed only by RBU‑prm.

---

# **## 2. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

### **HLR‑20.51.0001 — RBU‑prm SHALL Execute Immediately After RB‑prm**  
RBU‑prm SHALL run directly after RB‑prm completes and before TS schedules the next OB‑chain.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0002 — RBU‑prm SHALL Consume the Routing Envelope Produced by RB‑prm**  
RBU‑prm SHALL accept exactly one routing envelope from RB‑prm per cycle and SHALL reject malformed or incomplete envelopes.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0003 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Routing Metadata Into the TP Snapshot**  
RBU‑prm SHALL write all routing deltas, lane selections, lineage metadata, and arbitration results into the TP routing fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0004 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify Non‑Routing TP Fields**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter semantic fields, truth fields, merge fields, completion fields, or any TP content outside the routing namespace.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0005 — RBU‑prm SHALL Preserve RB‑prm’s Output Without Interpretation**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT reinterpret, recompute, or transform RB‑prm’s routing decisions.  
It SHALL commit them exactly as provided.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0006 — RBU‑prm SHALL Perform a Deterministic Commit**  
Given the same routing envelope and TP snapshot, RBU‑prm SHALL produce identical TP routing fields across all executions.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0007 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Trigger Any OBs or Semantic Primitives**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT invoke OBs, TR, TPU, or any semantic or routing computation.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0008 — RBU‑prm SHALL Validate Structural Integrity Before Commit**  
RBU‑prm SHALL verify that the routing envelope conforms to the RB‑prm schema and SHALL reject envelopes that violate structural constraints.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0009 — RBU‑prm SHALL Expose a Stable Routing State to TS**  
After commit, RBU‑prm SHALL ensure that TS receives a complete, stable routing snapshot for scheduling.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0010 — RBU‑prm SHALL Update Routing Lineage Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL write lineage fields (e.g., previous lane, arbitration path, fallback lane) into the TP routing namespace.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0011 — RBU‑prm SHALL Preserve Routing Reasons for TR**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit RB‑prm’s routing‑reason metadata so that TR can evaluate staleness and lane‑shift significance.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0012 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify TR State**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT update, reset, or recompute TR lineage or staleness fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0013 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify RB‑prm Output**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter routing deltas, lane selections, or arbitration results.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0014 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Exactly Once Per Cycle**  
RBU‑prm SHALL perform exactly one commit per RB‑prm execution and SHALL NOT re‑run for the same routing envelope.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0015 — RBU‑prm SHALL Maintain Auditability**  
RBU‑prm SHALL write routing metadata in a form that supports deterministic replay and audit of routing decisions.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0016 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Introduce New Routing Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT generate new routing fields; it SHALL only commit fields produced by RB‑prm.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0017 — RBU‑prm SHALL Maintain Backward‑Compatible Field Layout**  
RBU‑prm SHALL preserve the routing namespace layout defined in the TP specification and SHALL NOT reorder or repurpose fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0018 — RBU‑prm SHALL Reject Ambiguous or Multi‑Lane Routing Envelopes**  
If RB‑prm produces an envelope with unresolved or conflicting lane selections, RBU‑prm SHALL reject the commit.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0019 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify commit_id or Semantic Snapshots**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter commit_id, semantic_core, or any A/B synchronization metadata.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0020 — RBU‑prm SHALL Complete Before TS Scheduling Begins**  
RBU‑prm SHALL finish all commit operations before TS evaluates the routing state for the next OB‑chain.

---

### **HLR‑20.51.0021 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit All Routing Reasons** 
Including Those Derived From Subculture Metadata.

---

# **## 3. Is Not**  
RBU‑prm is **not**:

- a router  
- a classifier  
- a semantic interpreter  
- a scorer  
- a truth evaluator  
- a topology executor  
- a semantic writer  
- a TR updater  

RBU‑prm performs **only** deterministic commit of routing metadata.

---

# **## 4. Example**  
RB‑prm selects a cultural lane and fallback lane based on metadata in the TP.  
RBU‑prm receives the routing envelope, validates its structure, and commits:

- selected lane  
- fallback lane  
- routing lineage  
- arbitration notes  
- routing reasons  

TS then reads this updated TP to schedule the next OB‑chain.
