# **20.51 — RBU‑prm (Realization Buffer Update Primitive)**  
**Status:** Normative  
**Scope:** Commit‑layer primitive for authoritative MTP updates  
**Excludes:** Routing semantics (20.30.005), merge semantics (20.30.050), truth/completion semantics (20.30.060)

---

# **0. Purpose**

RBU‑prm is the **authoritative commit primitive** of Pipeline A.  
It performs the **atomic, deterministic write** of the update_structure into the MTP.

RBU‑prm:

- commits meaning  
- commits truth/completion metadata  
- commits ΔH% adjustments  
- commits lineage and semantic tags  
- commits TPTB and TPSF  
- generates the new `commit_id`  
- finalizes the A/B boundary  

RBU‑prm performs **no routing**, **no interpretation**, **no arbitration**, and **no semantic computation**.

Routing is handled exclusively by **RTU‑prm** (20.30.005) and **TR‑prm**.

---

# **1. Definitions**

### **1.1 update_structure**  
The deterministic structure produced by merge + truth/completion evaluation containing all fields required for MTP update.

### **1.2 Commit Operation**  
A deterministic, atomic write of update_structure fields into the MTP.

### **1.3 MTP**  
The global semantic state for the current thought, updated only by RBU‑prm.

---

# **2. Invocation Model**

- RBU‑prm is invoked **once per Pipeline A cycle**.  
- RBU‑prm is invoked **after merge** and **after truth/completion evaluation**.  
- RBU‑prm is the **final step** of Pipeline A before `mtp_update`.

RBU‑prm is **not** a conditional branch and does **not** depend on routing deltas.

---

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

### **HLR‑20.51‑001 — RBU‑prm SHALL Execute After Truth/Completion Evaluation**  
RBU‑prm SHALL run after merge and truth/completion evaluation and before `mtp_update`.

### **HLR‑20.51‑002 — RBU‑prm SHALL Consume Exactly One update_structure**  
RBU‑prm SHALL accept exactly one update_structure per cycle and SHALL reject malformed structures.

### **HLR‑20.51‑003 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit All Fields in update_structure**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit all fields provided in update_structure without omission.

### **HLR‑20.51‑004 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify update_structure**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT reinterpret, recompute, or transform update_structure fields.

### **HLR‑20.51‑005 — RBU‑prm SHALL Perform an Atomic Commit**  
RBU‑prm SHALL update the MTP atomically within a single deterministic commit step.

### **HLR‑20.51‑006 — RBU‑prm SHALL Generate a Deterministic commit_id**  
RBU‑prm SHALL generate a deterministic commit_id for each successful commit.

### **HLR‑20.51‑007 — RBU‑prm SHALL Validate Structural Integrity**  
RBU‑prm SHALL validate that update_structure conforms to the merge + truth/completion schema.

### **HLR‑20.51‑008 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Perform Routing**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT read, modify, or generate routing metadata.

### **HLR‑20.51‑009 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Invoke RTU‑prm or TR‑prm**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT trigger routing‑layer primitives.

### **HLR‑20.51‑010 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit TPTB Atomically**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit the TPTB metadata structure without modification.

### **HLR‑20.51‑011 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit TPSF Atomically**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit the TPSF metadata structure without modification.

### **HLR‑20.51‑012 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify Semantic Fields Outside update_structure**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter semantic_core, truth hypotheses, or completion state beyond what is provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑013 — RBU‑prm SHALL Preserve Field Ordering**  
RBU‑prm SHALL apply updates in a deterministic, canonical order.

### **HLR‑20.51‑014 — RBU‑prm SHALL Maintain Replay Equivalence**  
Given identical inputs, RBU‑prm SHALL produce identical MTP(N+1).

### **HLR‑20.51‑015 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Lineage and Semantic Tags**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit lineage and semantic tags exactly as provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑016 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Generate New Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT create new fields; it SHALL only commit fields present in update_structure.

### **HLR‑20.51‑017 — RBU‑prm SHALL Reject Ambiguous update_structure Inputs**  
If update_structure contains conflicting or undefined fields, RBU‑prm SHALL reject the commit.

### **HLR‑20.51‑018 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify A/B Boundary Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter synchronization metadata outside commit_id and the MTP write.

### **HLR‑20.51‑019 — RBU‑prm SHALL Expose Commit Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL expose commit_id and stability metadata to supervisory layers.

### **HLR‑20.51‑020 — RBU‑prm SHALL Complete Before Pipeline B Begins**  
RBU‑prm SHALL finish all commit operations before Pipeline B reads the MTP.

---

# **4. Is Not**

RBU‑prm is **not**:

- a router  
- a classifier  
- a semantic interpreter  
- a scorer  
- a truth evaluator  
- a routing updater  
- a merge engine  
- a TR updater  

RBU‑prm performs **only** deterministic commit of update_structure into the MTP.

---

# **5. Example**

Merge and truth/completion evaluation produce update_structure.  
RBU‑prm receives it, validates it, and commits:

- semantic fields  
- ΔH% adjustments  
- lineage  
- semantic tags  
- TPTB  
- TPSF  
- supervisory metadata  

RBU‑prm generates a new commit_id and finalizes the MTP for the cycle.

Pipeline B then reads the frozen MTP snapshot.
