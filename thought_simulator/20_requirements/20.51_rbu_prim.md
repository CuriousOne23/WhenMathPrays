# **20.51 — RBU‑prm (Realization Buffer Update Primitive)**  
**Status:** Normative  
**Scope:** Path‑A meaning‑side commit primitive  
**Excludes:** MTP updates, routing semantics, merge semantics, truth/completion semantics, global culture

---

# **0. Purpose**

RBU‑prm is the **meaning‑side commit primitive** of Path A.  
It performs the **local identity and semantic‑field commit** into the **TP**, not the MTP.

RBU‑prm:

- commits **TPTB** (Truth‑Pipeline‑To‑Belief metadata)  
- commits **TPSF** (Truth‑Pipeline Semantic Field)  
- commits **local subculture identity**  
- commits **local stance, register, tone, and semantic‑field cues**  
- commits **lineage and semantic tags**  
- finalizes the **meaning‑side identity state** for the current turn  
- prepares the TP for **CTP → RB → RTU** routing  

RBU‑prm performs **no routing**, **no MTP writes**, **no global culture computation**, and **no truth/completion evaluation**.

Global culture is Path B only and is **not** visible to RBU‑prm.

---

# **1. Definitions**

### **1.1 local_identity_structure**  
The structure containing local subculture, stance, register, tone, and semantic‑field cues extracted from the current turn only.

### **1.2 TPTB**  
Truth‑Pipeline‑To‑Belief metadata produced by TR and required for meaning‑side commitments.

### **1.3 TPSF**  
Semantic‑field metadata produced by SmOB/SSG and required for meaning‑side commitments.

### **1.4 TP (Thought Packet)**  
The meaning‑side working state for the current turn.  
**RBU‑prm writes to TP, not MTP.**

### **1.5 MTP**  
The global TS‑side snapshot.  
**RBU‑prm does not read or write MTP.**

---

# **2. Invocation Model**

- RBU‑prm is invoked **once per Path A cycle**, after SSG.  
- RBU‑prm receives:  
  - local_identity_structure  
  - TPTB  
  - TPSF  
  - lineage and semantic tags  
- RBU‑prm writes these into the **TP**.  
- RBU‑prm runs **before CTP → RB → RTU**.  
- RBU‑prm is **not** conditional and does **not** depend on routing deltas.  
- RBU‑prm does **not** interact with MTP or MTPU‑prm.

---

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

### **HLR‑20.51‑001 — RBU‑prm SHALL Execute After SSG**  
RBU‑prm SHALL run after semantic synthesis and before CTP.

### **HLR‑20.51‑002 — RBU‑prm SHALL Consume Exactly One local_identity_structure**  
RBU‑prm SHALL accept exactly one local_identity_structure per cycle.

### **HLR‑20.51‑003 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit TPTB Without Modification**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit TPTB exactly as provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑004 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit TPSF Without Modification**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit TPSF exactly as provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑005 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Local Subculture Identity**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit local subculture cues derived from the current turn only.

### **HLR‑20.51‑006 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Commit Global Culture**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT commit global culture, which is Path B only.

### **HLR‑20.51‑007 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Local Stance, Register, and Tone**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit stance, register, tone, and semantic‑field cues from the current turn.

### **HLR‑20.51‑008 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify TP Fields Outside Its Scope**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter truth hypotheses, merge results, or routing metadata.

### **HLR‑20.51‑009 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Perform Routing**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT read, modify, or generate routing metadata.

### **HLR‑20.51‑010 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Invoke CTP, RB, or RTU**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT trigger routing‑layer primitives.

### **HLR‑20.51‑011 — RBU‑prm SHALL Commit Lineage and Semantic Tags**  
RBU‑prm SHALL commit lineage and semantic tags exactly as provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑012 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Generate New Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT create new fields; it SHALL only commit fields provided.

### **HLR‑20.51‑013 — RBU‑prm SHALL Preserve Field Ordering**  
RBU‑prm SHALL apply updates in a deterministic, canonical order.

### **HLR‑20.51‑014 — RBU‑prm SHALL Maintain Replay Equivalence**  
Given identical inputs, RBU‑prm SHALL produce identical TP(N+1).

### **HLR‑20.51‑015 — RBU‑prm SHALL Reject Ambiguous Inputs**  
If local_identity_structure contains conflicting or undefined fields, RBU‑prm SHALL reject the commit.

### **HLR‑20.51‑016 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Read or Write MTP**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT interact with MTP or MTPU‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.51‑017 — RBU‑prm SHALL NOT Modify A/B Boundary Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT alter synchronization metadata.

### **HLR‑20.51‑018 — RBU‑prm SHALL Expose Local Identity Commit Metadata**  
RBU‑prm SHALL expose local identity commit metadata to supervisory layers.

### **HLR‑20.51‑019 — RBU‑prm SHALL Complete Before CTP**  
RBU‑prm SHALL finish all commit operations before CTP begins.

### **HLR‑20.51‑020 — RBU‑prm SHALL Operate Only on the Current Turn**  
RBU‑prm SHALL NOT access cross‑turn or global state.

---

# **4. Is Not**

RBU‑prm is **not**:

- a router  
- a classifier  
- a semantic interpreter  
- a scorer  
- a truth evaluator  
- a merge engine  
- a global culture engine  
- an MTP writer  
- a TS‑side primitive  

RBU‑prm performs **only** deterministic commit of local identity, TPTB, TPSF, and semantic tags into the **TP**.

---

# **5. Example**

SSG produces semantic synthesis.  
RBU‑prm receives:

- local subculture cues  
- stance, register, tone  
- TPTB  
- TPSF  
- lineage and semantic tags  

RBU‑prm commits these into the TP.  
RBU‑prm does **not** write MTP.  
RBU‑prm does **not** read global culture.  
RBU‑prm does **not** perform routing.

CTP → RB → RTU then operate on the updated TP.
