# 20.40.040 — SmOB Primitive (Semantic-Adjacent Object)

## 0. Purpose & Scope (Informative)

SmOB performs **two distinct and required jobs**, each simple, bounded, and architecturally separate. These two jobs must remain conceptually distinct to preserve routing determinism and prevent implementation drift.

### **Job 1 — Pre‑Semantic Cue Extraction (adds information into the TP)**  
SmOB extracts semantic‑adjacent cues from the structural and constraint residue produced by SOB, SROB, and CnOB.  
This includes:

- semantic‑adjacent pattern activations  
- modality cues  
- affect markers  
- conflict‑adjacent signals  
- underspecification‑adjacent signals  
- TR‑input cues  

These cues are **added into the TP** to support:

- identity OB selection  
- TR routing (TP.TR formation)  
- ΔH% semantic‑adjacent attribution  
- semantic‑layer preparation without interpretation  

This job is strictly **pre‑semantic**: SmOB does not interpret meaning, evaluate truth, assign stance, or perform semantic inference.

### **Job 2 — Pre‑Semantic Residue Compression (hashing)**  
SmOB compresses the combined upstream residue (SOB → SROB → CnOB) together with its own extracted pre‑semantic cues into a **deterministic representation** required by the Structural Signal Generator (20.47) and Routing Boundary (20.50).  
This representation includes:

- a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash  
- a TR‑input cue vector  
- semantic‑adjacent ΔH% signals  

This compression step provides SSG with a **fixed‑size, canonical, replay‑safe** representation of all upstream residue, enabling deterministic routing and identity OB selection.

SmOB is the **boundary object** between the OB pipeline (residue extraction) and the SSG pipeline (signal generation). Its two jobs are required and SHALL remain distinct in implementation.

## 0.1 SmOB’s Two Jobs (Normative Summary)

SmOB SHALL perform exactly two jobs:

- **Job 1 — Pre‑semantic cue extraction:** Extract semantic‑adjacent cues from upstream residue and add them into the TP.
- **Job 2 — Pre‑semantic residue compression:** Compress all upstream residue plus SmOB cues into a deterministic hash and TR‑input cue vector.

These two jobs SHALL remain conceptually and operationally distinct.

### HLR‑20.40.040‑011 
The TS SHALL implement SmOB Job 1 (pre‑semantic cue extraction) as a distinct phase that adds semantic‑adjacent cues into the TP.

### HLR‑20.40.040‑012
The TS SHALL implement SmOB Job 2 (pre‑semantic residue compression) as a distinct phase that produces a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash and TR‑input cue vector.


---

## 1. Inputs

**HLR‑20.40.040‑013**
The SmOB layer SHALL accept the refined structural TP, refined residue fragments, sharpened structural metadata, and constraint‑level residue produced by CnOB.

**HLR‑20.40.040‑014**
The SmOB layer MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — and MAY annotate these structurally for pre‑semantic cue extraction. The SmOB layer SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of these fields.

---

## 2. SmOB Definition (Normative)

SmOB is a deterministic object that performs two jobs: (1) extraction of pre‑semantic cues from upstream residue, and (2) compression of all upstream residue plus SmOB cues into a deterministic representation for routing.

SmOB is a deterministic object containing:

- `semantic_adjacent_cues` — pattern activations near semantic boundaries  
- `modality_cues` — structural indicators of modality (e.g., interrogative, imperative)  
- `affect_markers` — structural affective signals without semantic interpretation  
- `presemantic_residue_hash` — deterministic hash of pre‑semantic residue  
- `tr_input_cues` — cues required for TR update eligibility  
- `delta_h_semantic_adjacent` — ΔH% signals derived from semantic‑adjacent change  
- `routing_semantic_cues` — semantic‑adjacent routing metadata  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑001**  
The TS SHALL construct SmOB as a pure pre‑semantic object containing only semantic‑adjacent cues and residue derived from CnOB, with no semantic interpretation or stance assignment.

Fields related to cue extraction correspond to Job 1, while fields related to hashing and TR‑input cue formation correspond to Job 2.

---

## 3. Deterministic Pre‑Semantic Extraction (Normative)

This section specifies requirements for Job 1 (pre‑semantic cue extraction).

SmOB must be constructed deterministically from CnOB, ensuring:

- identical CnOB inputs → identical SmOB outputs  
- stable semantic‑adjacent cues  
- replay‑safe behavior  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑002**  
The TS SHALL ensure that SmOB extraction is deterministic for identical CnOB inputs, producing identical semantic‑adjacent cues across all executions.

---

## 4. Canonical Ordering (Normative)

All SmOB fields must be:

- canonically ordered  
- stable across replay  
- comparable across cycles  
- free of nondeterministic ordering artifacts  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑003**  
The TS SHALL apply canonical ordering to all SmOB fields, including nested cue structures, to ensure deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

## 5. Pre‑Semantic Residue Extraction (Normative)

SmOB must extract:

- pre‑semantic residue  
- semantic‑adjacent pattern activations  
- underspecification‑adjacent cues  
- conflict‑adjacent signals  
- modality‑adjacent cues  
- affect‑adjacent markers  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑004**  
The TS SHALL extract pre‑semantic residue and semantic‑adjacent cues from CnOB without performing semantic interpretation, smoothing, or reconstruction.

---

## 6. TR‑Input Cue Formation (Normative)

SmOB is responsible for forming the TR‑input cue vector used by the Thought Router (20.50) to compute TP.TR.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑005**  
The TS SHALL derive TR‑input cues deterministically from SmOB’s semantic‑adjacent fields and SHALL ensure these cues are stable across replay.

---

## 7. ΔH% Semantic‑Adjacent Integration (Normative)

SmOB must compute ΔH% signals that reflect **semantic‑adjacent structural change**, not semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑006**  
The TS SHALL compute ΔH% semantic‑adjacent signals deterministically from SmOB fields and SHALL ensure consistency with 20.12 invariants and 20.47 SSG requirements.

---

## 8. Pre‑Semantic Residue Hashing (Normative)

This section specifies requirements for Job 2 (pre‑semantic residue compression).

SmOB must produce a deterministic hash of pre‑semantic residue for routing and TR gating.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑007**  
The TS SHALL compute a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash from SmOB fields and SHALL ensure that identical inputs produce identical hashes across all executions.

---

## 9. Routing‑Relevant Semantic‑Adjacent Cues (Normative)

SmOB must expose semantic‑adjacent cues required by the routing equation in 20.47.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑008**  
The TS SHALL include routing‑relevant semantic‑adjacent cues in SmOB exactly as defined in 20.47 and SHALL NOT include any cues that require semantic interpretation.

---

## 10. SmOB as Sole Pre‑Semantic Input to SSG (Normative)

The Structural Signal Generator consumes **only** SmOB for pre‑semantic signals.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑009**  
The TS SHALL ensure that the Structural Signal Generator (20.47) receives SmOB as its sole pre‑semantic input and SHALL NOT accept additional semantic or supervisory fields.

---

## 11. SmOB Is Not (Informative)

SmOB is **not**:

- a semantic interpreter  
- a truth evaluator  
- a stance assigner  
- a geometric meta‑basin  
- a semantic normalizer  
- a generative mechanism  
- a smoothing or reconstruction layer  

---

## 12. Example (Informative)

Given a CnOB with constraint residue:

- a rising interrogative pattern produces a **modality cue**  
- a conflicting adjacency produces a **conflict‑adjacent marker**  
- an underspecified argument slot produces a **semantic‑adjacent cue**  
- the combined pre‑semantic residue produces a **pre‑semantic residue hash**  
- TR‑input cues are derived from these signals for routing and TP.TR formation
- SmOB then compresses the combined upstream residue and its own extracted cues into a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash and TR‑input cue vector, which SSG consumes for routing.

SmOB emits these deterministic pre‑semantic cues to the SSG.
