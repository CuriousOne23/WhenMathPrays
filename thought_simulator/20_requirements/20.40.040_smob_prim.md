# **0. Purpose & Scope** *(Informative)*

SmOB performs **two distinct, required jobs**, each simple, bounded, and architecturally separate.  
These jobs must remain conceptually distinct to preserve routing determinism and prevent implementation drift.

---

## **Job 1 — Pre‑Semantic Cue Extraction** *(Informative)*  
SmOB extracts semantic‑adjacent cues from upstream residue (SOB → SROB → CnOB), including:

- semantic‑adjacent pattern activations  
- modality cues  
- affect markers  
- conflict‑adjacent signals  
- underspecification‑adjacent signals  
- TR‑input cues  

These cues are **added into the TP** to support:

- identity OB selection  
- TR routing (TP.TR formation)  
- ΔH% semantic‑adjacent attribution  
- semantic‑layer preparation without interpretation  

SmOB does **not** interpret meaning, evaluate truth, assign stance, or perform semantic inference.

---

## **Job 2 — Pre‑Semantic Residue Compression** *(Informative)*  
SmOB compresses all upstream residue plus its own cues into:

- a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash  
- a TR‑input cue vector  
- semantic‑adjacent ΔH% signals  

This provides SSG with a **fixed‑size, canonical, replay‑safe** representation for deterministic routing.

SmOB is the **boundary object** between the OB pipeline (residue extraction) and the SSG pipeline (signal generation).

---

# **0.1 SmOB’s Two Jobs (Normative Summary)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑011**  
SmOB Job 1 (pre‑semantic cue extraction) SHALL be implemented as a distinct phase that adds semantic‑adjacent cues into the TP.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑012**  
SmOB Job 2 (pre‑semantic residue compression) SHALL be implemented as a distinct phase that produces a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash and TR‑input cue vector.

---

# **1. Inputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑013**  
SmOB SHALL accept refined structural TP, refined residue fragments, sharpened structural metadata, and constraint‑level residue produced by CnOB.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑014**  
SmOB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — and MAY annotate these structurally.  
SmOB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of these fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑015 — Structural Discourse‑Cue Normalization**  
SmOB SHALL normalize discourse‑context structural flags propagated from CnOB — including recent‑entity continuity, temporal‑shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical pre‑semantic cue metadata.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑016 — Structural Discourse‑Cue Extraction**  
SmOB SHALL encode normalized discourse‑context metadata strictly as **pre‑semantic cues** added into the TP, without inferring meaning, intent, stance, or semantic roles.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑017 — Deterministic & Bounded Discourse‑Adjacent Cues**  
SmOB SHALL ensure discourse‑adjacent cues are deterministic, bounded, reproducible for identical CnOB inputs, and compliant with canonical ordering requirements in HLR‑20.40.040‑003.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑018 — Routing‑Eligible Discourse‑Adjacent Cues**  
SmOB SHALL expose discourse‑adjacent cues as part of the TR‑input cue vector and pre‑semantic residue, ensuring they are consumable by SSG and RB without modification.

---

# **2. SmOB Definition (Normative)**

SmOB is a deterministic object performing:

- **Job 1:** extraction of pre‑semantic cues  
- **Job 2:** compression of upstream residue + SmOB cues  

SmOB contains:

- `semantic_adjacent_cues`  
- `modality_cues`  
- `affect_markers`  
- `presemantic_residue_hash`  
- `tr_input_cues`  
- `delta_h_semantic_adjacent`  
- `routing_semantic_cues`  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑001**  
SmOB SHALL contain only semantic‑adjacent cues and residue derived from CnOB, with no semantic interpretation or stance assignment.

---

# **3. Deterministic Pre‑Semantic Extraction (Job 1)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑002**  
SmOB extraction SHALL be deterministic for identical CnOB inputs, producing identical semantic‑adjacent cues across all executions.

---

# **4. Canonical Ordering**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑003**  
SmOB SHALL apply canonical ordering to all fields, including nested cue structures, ensuring deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

# **5. Pre‑Semantic Residue Extraction (Job 1)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑004**  
SmOB SHALL extract pre‑semantic residue and semantic‑adjacent cues from CnOB without semantic interpretation, smoothing, or reconstruction.

---

# **6. TR‑Input Cue Formation**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑005**  
SmOB SHALL derive TR‑input cues deterministically from semantic‑adjacent fields and SHALL ensure these cues are stable across replay.

---

# **7. ΔH% Semantic‑Adjacent Integration**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑006**  
SmOB SHALL compute ΔH% semantic‑adjacent signals deterministically from SmOB fields and SHALL ensure consistency with 20.12 invariants and 20.47 SSG requirements.

---

# **8. Pre‑Semantic Residue Hashing (Job 2)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑007**  
SmOB SHALL compute a deterministic pre‑semantic residue hash from SmOB fields and SHALL ensure identical inputs produce identical hashes.

---

# **9. Routing‑Relevant Semantic‑Adjacent Cues**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑008**  
SmOB SHALL include routing‑relevant semantic‑adjacent cues exactly as defined in 20.47 and SHALL NOT include cues requiring semantic interpretation.

---

# **10. SmOB as Sole Pre‑Semantic Input to SSG**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑009**  
SSG SHALL receive SmOB as its sole pre‑semantic input and SHALL NOT accept additional semantic or supervisory fields.

---

# **11. SmOB Is Not** *(Informative)*

SmOB is **not**:

- a semantic interpreter  
- a truth evaluator  
- a stance assigner  
- a semantic normalizer  
- a generative mechanism  
- a smoothing or reconstruction layer  

---

# **12. Mathematical Requirements (GitHub‑Safe KaTeX)**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑010**  
SmOB SHALL ensure each pre‑semantic residue fragment $r\_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h\_i'$:

$$
h\_i' = hash(r\_i')
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑011**  
SmOB SHALL ensure the combined pre‑semantic query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h\_1' \oplus h\_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h\_n'
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑012**  
SmOB SHALL ensure pre‑semantic residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature‑invariance.

---

# **13. Outputs**

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑013**  
SmOB SHALL output:

- semantic‑adjacent cues  
- modality cues  
- affect markers  
- conflict‑adjacent signals  
- underspecification‑adjacent cues  
- pre‑semantic residue hash  
- TR‑input cue vector  
- ΔH% semantic‑adjacent signals  
- routing‑semantic cues  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑014**  
SmOB output SHALL be valid input for SSG and RB.
