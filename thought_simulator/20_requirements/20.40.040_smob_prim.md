# 20.40.040 — SmOB Primitive (Structural Meaning Object)

## 0. Purpose & Scope (Informative)

The Structural Meaning Object (SmOB) is the canonical, deterministic, structural representation of a Thought Packet (TP) used by the Structural Signal Generator (20.47).  
SmOB provides the structural fields required for routing, structural comparison, lineage tracking, and ΔH% computation.

SmOB is **pure**, **deterministic**, and **side‑effect‑free**.  
It contains **no semantic inference**, **no smoothing**, and **no reconstruction** of missing fields.

SmOB is the *only* input to the Structural Signal Generator (SSG), and therefore must satisfy strict determinism, canonical ordering, and purity constraints.

---

## 1. SmOB Definition (Normative)

SmOB is a deterministic structural object with the following required fields:

- `structure` — the canonical structural representation of the TP  
- `lineage` — the structural ancestry chain  
- `delta_h` — the ΔH% structural‑change metric  
- `routing_metadata` — core ID, cycle index, and other routing‑relevant metadata  
- `policy_signature` — the policy‑level structural signature  
- `timestamp` — monotonic cycle timestamp (not wall‑clock time)

SmOB contains **no semantic fields**, **no supervisory fields**, and **no Pipeline B artifacts**.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑001**  
The TS SHALL construct SmOB as a pure structural object containing only the fields required by the Structural Signal Generator (20.47), with no semantic or supervisory content.

---

## 2. Deterministic Construction (Normative)

SmOB construction must be deterministic across:

- cores  
- cycles  
- execution environments  
- replay runs  
- scheduling variations  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑002**  
The TS SHALL ensure that SmOB construction is deterministic for identical TP inputs, producing identical SmOB outputs across all executions.

---

## 3. Canonical Ordering (Normative)

SmOB must be canonically ordered so that:

- nested maps  
- ID arrays  
- structural lists  
- lineage chains  

are stable and comparable across executions.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑003**  
The TS SHALL apply canonical ordering to all SmOB fields, including nested maps, arrays, and lineage structures, to ensure deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

## 4. Structural Purity (Normative)

SmOB must not include:

- inferred meaning  
- smoothed fields  
- reconstructed missing data  
- semantic approximations  
- Pipeline B artifacts  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑004**  
The TS SHALL ensure that SmOB contains only raw structural information and SHALL NOT infer, smooth, reconstruct, or approximate missing or noisy fields.

---

## 5. Lineage Tracking (Normative)

Lineage is a structural ancestry chain used for ΔH% computation and structural‑transition eligibility.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑005**  
The TS SHALL maintain a deterministic lineage chain within SmOB, ensuring that lineage is stable, canonical, and replay‑consistent.

---

## 6. ΔH% Structural Change Metric (Normative)

ΔH% measures structural change between cycles and is used by:

- SSG  
- RB  
- TR gating  
- structural‑transition logic  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑006**  
The TS SHALL compute ΔH% deterministically from SmOB structural fields and SHALL ensure that ΔH% is stable across replay.

---

## 7. Routing Metadata (Normative)

Routing metadata includes:

- core ID  
- cycle index  
- structural‑transition flags  
- routing‑relevant counters  

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑007**  
The TS SHALL include routing metadata in SmOB exactly as defined in 20.47 and SHALL NOT include any metadata that is not routing‑relevant.

---

## 8. Policy Signature (Normative)

The policy signature is a structural signature used by the routing equation.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑008**  
The TS SHALL compute and embed a deterministic policy signature in SmOB, ensuring that it is stable across replay and consistent with 20.47.

---

## 9. SmOB as the Sole SSG Input (Normative)

The Structural Signal Generator consumes **only** SmOB.  
No other object may feed into the SSG.

### **HLR‑20.40.040‑009**  
The TS SHALL ensure that the Structural Signal Generator (20.47) receives SmOB as its sole input and SHALL NOT accept any additional fields or objects.

---

## 10. Informative Notes (Informative)

- SmOB is the structural backbone of the TS.  
- SmOB purity ensures that routing remains deterministic and auditable.  
- SmOB canonical ordering ensures that structural comparisons are stable.  
- SmOB lineage and ΔH% support structural‑transition logic.  
- SmOB routing metadata ensures multi‑core isolation and deterministic routing.
