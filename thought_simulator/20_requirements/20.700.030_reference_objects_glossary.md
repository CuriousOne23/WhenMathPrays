## Entries

### CIL (Conversation Integration Layer) — 20.33
**Purpose:** Maintains ordered, auditable conversation intake separate from meaning construction.  
**Focus:** Deterministic conversation intake, FIFO preservation, and escalation to supervisory paths.  
**When:** Conversation layer; before TS intake boundaries.  
**Why:** Provide ordered, auditable integration separate from meaning construction.  
**Is not:** A context object; a semantic interpreter; a TP writer.  
**Example:** Clarification event delivered to UPI.  
**Normative home:** 20.33.

### COB (Conversation Object Basin) — 20.32
**Purpose:** Maintains long-horizon conversation object lifecycle and lineage separately from TP.  
**Focus:** Durable conversation-object lifecycle and lineage.  
**When:** Conversation layer.  
**Why:** Maintain long-horizon continuity separate from TP.  
**Is not:** A semantic interpreter; a TP writer; a context provider.  
**Example:** TP promotion to COB-visible object.  
**Normative home:** 20.32.

### USP (User Semantic Profile) — 20.101, 20.33
**Purpose:** Provide the authoritative, user-approved rule set governing all shorthand repair in IIInB and all context extraction in CEx.  
**Focus:** Persistent, user-approved mappings of shorthand forms, preferred expansions, and semantic commitments consumed by IIInB and CEx.  
**When:** Loaded at session initialization; read by IIInB during input_semantic_repair and by CEx during context extraction.  
**Why:** Without USP, IIInB would infer repairs, violating the non-inferential constraint on the input path.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; candidate generator; TP writer; live inference mechanism; truth evaluator.  
**Example:** USP maps "quicker-ish" → "reduce latency by ~20%"; IIInB applies the rule deterministically without constructing meaning.  
**Normative home:** 20.101 (repair rules); 20.33 (CEx consumption).

### candidate_set{} (Interpretation Candidates) — 20.44
**Purpose:** Present ISc with a finite, pre-generated set of structured interpretations so that scoring remains deterministic, bounded, and replay-safe.  
**Focus:** Finite set of structured interpretation candidates derived from the intake envelope plus CE via CEx.  
**When:** Produced immediately before ISc; consumed exactly once by ISc during the scoring step.  
**Why:** ISc requires a deterministic, closed candidate space to score; prevents free-form inference, unbounded search, or global-state-dependent candidate generation.  
**Is not:** Generated by TB; generated by IB; free inference; latent-space sampling; semantic construction.  
**Example:** Ambiguous pronoun references yield multiple structured referent candidates in candidate_set{}, which ISc scores deterministically using truth_hypotheses-prc.  
**Normative home:** 20.44 (ISc).

### commit_id / semantic_snapshot_ref — 20.120, 20.36
**Purpose:** Provide a stable, replay-deterministic identifier for each turn’s finalized TP and its corresponding historical MTP entry.  
**Focus:** Turn-level freeze markers for finalized meaning and realization, enabling deterministic replay, lineage tracking, and long-horizon inference.  
**When:** Generated once per turn by HC-Flow after Path B completes.  
**Why:** Ensure that all historical processing operates on authoritative, immutable snapshots.  
**Is not:** A Path-A commit; a Path-B commit; a mid-turn snapshot; a mutable reference.  
**Example:** After turn completion, HC-Flow assigns commit_id to TP(N) and archives it as MTP(N).  
**Normative home:** 20.120, 20.36, 20.115 (MTP), 20.705 (HC-Flow).

### DCB (Directional Conversation Basin) — 20.106
**Purpose:** Provide TR with geometric curvature signals indicating when the conversational trajectory bends.  
**Focus:** Compute TP position, direction vector, and curvature; emit directional-change events consumed by TR.  
**When:** Path-A, between OB output and TR routing.  
**Why:** TR uses curvature events to avoid misrouting during stance shifts, pivots, or reversals.  
**Is not:** OB; semantic analyzer; TP modifier; persistent writer; Path-B element.  
**Example:** A trajectory reversal produces a curvature spike consumed by TR.  
**Normative home:** 20.106.

### TB (Truth Basin) — 20.60
**Purpose:** Evaluate truth over the committed semantic snapshot from IB, producing structured truth_state{} for governance-side evaluation within D-Flow.  
**Focus:** Assess truth alignment, contradiction, evidential sufficiency, referential consistency, stance-truth coherence over committed meaning; emit truth_state{} without modifying semantic_core or TP.  
**When:** Post-OuBA commit, only within DF.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, auditable truth evaluation separating truth-interpretation from governance decision-making.  
**Is not:** Path-A primitive; meaning constructor; TP writer; scorer; semantic repair engine.  
**Example:** OuBA commits meaning with insufficient evidence; TB classifies truth_state and forwards it to GBIB.  
**Normative home:** 20.60 (TB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.80 (GB).

### MTP (Master Thought Packet) — 20.115
**Purpose:** Serves as the authoritative, immutable historical TP snapshot maintained by HC-Flow.  
**Focus:** Holds the finalized TP(N) state exactly as produced at turn completion.  
**When:** Updated once per turn by HC-Flow after Path B completes.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, auditable, replay-stable representation of each turn’s final TP.  
**Is not:** A Path-A object; a Path-B object; a DF object; a realization commit; a truth evaluator.  
**Example:** After turn completion, HC-Flow freezes TP(N) into MTP(N).  
**Normative home:** 20.115, 20.705 (HC-Flow), 20.112 (COB), 20.114 (CIL), 20.113 (CoHI).

### routing_epoch_id — 20.55, 20.45
**Purpose:** Binds B-side execution planning to a specific, immutable SRP routing table epoch.  
**Focus:** SRP table epoch identifier.  
**When:** B envelopes only.  
**Why:** Bind execution planning to a specific routing table; prevent epoch mismatch.  
**Is not:** A-cycle routing state; mutable by IMR.  
**Normative home:** 20.55, 20.45.

### TrigRB (Trigger Relational Basin) — 20.57
**Path:** B  
**Purpose:** Detects realization-relevant semantic triggers over the finalized meaning boundary (OuBA) before any B-side planning or realization begins.  
**Focus:** B-side semantic-trigger detection over the stable, meaning-complete OuBA state.  
**When:** First stage of Pipeline B.  
**Why:** Identify trigger codes that drive SRP selection and downstream realization behavior.  
**Is not:** RB; Path-A primitive.  
**Normative home:** 20.57.

### InputReference, Provenance, RulesetID, RB_RoutingPolicy, ExtensibleMetadata
**Purpose:** Supporting reference objects used across OB layers and routing.  
**Focus:** Static or versioned metadata ensuring determinism, pluggability, and traceability.  
**When:** Loaded or generated as needed by primitives.  
**Why:** Enable pluggable behavior and provenance without modifying core structures.  
**Is not:** TP-modifying; meaning-carrying.  
**Normative home:** 20.710_ob_data_strc.md.
