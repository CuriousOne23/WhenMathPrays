## **CIL (Conversation Integration Layer) — 20.33**
**Purpose:** Provide deterministic intake by merging short‑term TP/IE structural cues with long‑term COB/CST identity‑layer context.  
**Focus:** Identity‑layer selection, referent/temporal/discourse certainty flags, and field‑importance hints for CEx.  
**When:** Conversation layer; immediately before CEx in the Path‑A pipeline.  
**Why:** Deliver a stable, context‑aware intake packet enabling CEx to resolve referents and anchors deterministically.  
**Is not:** A conversation‑object store; a semantic interpreter; a TP writer; a stability integrator.  
**Example:** CIL selects identity‑layer 7 with weak certainty and marks temporal anchor as ambiguous for CEx resolution.  
**Input:** Long‑term identity‑layer snapshot from COB/CST and short‑term structural cues from TP/IE.  
**Output:** Intake packet containing identity selection, certainty flags, and structural hints for CEx.  
**Normative home:** 20.33.

---

## **COB (Conversation Object Basin) — 20.32**
**Purpose:** Maintain and evolve the 20 long‑horizon conversation‑objects, including identity‑layers, lineage, referent maps, and field‑importance structure.  
**Focus:** Durable conversation‑object continuity, identity‑layer stability, lineage maintenance, and long‑term structural coherence.  
**When:** Conversation layer; after TS meaning construction and before CIL intake.  
**Why:** Provide a stable long‑term conversational substrate that CIL and CEx can rely on for deterministic identity and referent resolution.  
**Is not:** A semantic interpreter; a TP writer; a meaning constructor; a drift detector (delegated to CST).  
**Example:** COB updates identity‑layer 7’s referent map after CE/TS produce a new stable referent for the conversation.  
**Input:** Structured meaning output from TS and correction signals from CST.  
**Output:** Stabilized 20‑layer identity snapshot for CIL intake.  
**Normative home:** 20.32.  
**Note:** COB is the authoritative keeper of long‑term conversational structure; CST modulates it, CIL consumes it.

## **CST (Conversation Stability Tracker) — 20.32.010**
**Purpose:** Detects long‑horizon drift, oscillation, collapse, and instability in COB’s 20 conversation‑objects and issues correction signals.  
**Focus:** Long‑term stability integration over multiple turns; modulation of COB’s identity‑layer structure.  
**When:** Conversation layer, parallel to COB, operating asynchronously between Path‑A cycles.  
**Why:** Maintain stable identity‑layer structure by smoothing noise, detecting slow drift, and preventing oscillatory or collapsing conversations.  
**Is not:** A conversation‑object store; a semantic interpreter; a TP resolver; a context provider; a meaning constructor.  
**Example:** CST detects oscillation between identity‑layers 4 and 9 and issues a merge‑recommendation signal to COB.  
**Input:** Read‑only snapshot of COB’s 20 identity‑layers, lineage, referent maps, and field‑importance structure.  
**Output:** Correction signals to COB (merge, split, promote, demote, stabilize, remove, weaken, strengthen).  
**Normative home:** 20.32.010.

## **UPI — User Preference Integrator — 20.103**  
**Purpose:** Integrates validated user‑preference clarifications into the USP by converting `clarification_event` packets into deterministic, versioned `usp_rule` entries under governance gating.  
**Focus:** Consumes `clarification_event` packets, performs requirement‑level validation, normalizes clarification outcomes into bounded rule fields, applies GB safety gating, and emits `upi_commit_record` plus `usp_version_record` for each accepted rule.  
**When:** Requirements layer; executes after conversation‑layer primitives (CIL/COB) and before TS intake (InB); not part of Path‑A, Path‑B, or DF.  
**Why:** Ensures user‑confirmed preferences enter the system as deterministic, auditable, versioned rules, preventing malformed or unsafe preference commits from reaching TS or meaning‑layer machinery.  
**Is not:** Not a TS primitive; not a meaning constructor; not a semantic interpreter; not a TP or SSR reader; not a basin; not a routing or behavior generator; never reads or writes meaning‑layer fields and never performs semantic inference.  
**Example:** A user says “When I say ‘summaries,’ I always mean bullet‑point summaries.” CIL emits a `clarification_event`; UPI validates it, applies GB gating, commits a new `usp_rule`, and produces a `upi_commit_record` plus `usp_version_record`.  
**Normative home:** 20.103.

## USP (User Semantic Profile) — 20.101, 20.33
**Purpose:** Provide the authoritative, user‑approved rule set governing all shorthand repair in IIInB and all context extraction in CEx, ensuring personalization is explicit, auditable, and consent‑bound.  
**Focus:** Persistent, user‑approved mappings of shorthand forms, preferred expansions, and semantic commitments consumed by IIInB and CEx.  
**When:** Loaded at session initialization; read by IIInB during input_semantic_repair and by CEx during context extraction.  
**Why:** Without USP, IIInB would infer repairs, violating the non‑inferential constraint on the input path.  
**Is not:** Semantic interpreter; candidate generator; TP writer; live inference mechanism; truth evaluator.  
**Example:** USP maps "quicker‑ish" → "reduce latency by ~20%"; IIInB applies the rule deterministically without constructing meaning.  
**Normative home:** 20.101 (repair rules); 20.33 (CEx consumption).

## candidate_set{} (Interpretation Candidates) — 20.44
**Purpose:** Present ISc with a finite, pre‑generated set of structured interpretations so that scoring remains deterministic, bounded, and replay‑safe.  
**Focus:** Finite set of structured interpretation candidates derived from the intake envelope plus CE via CEx; provides the complete search space for ISc scoring.  
**When:** Produced immediately before ISc; consumed exactly once by ISc during the scoring step.  
**Why:** ISc requires a deterministic, closed candidate space to score; prevents free‑form inference, unbounded search, or global‑state‑dependent candidate generation.  
**Is not:** Generated by TB; generated by IB; free inference; latent‑space sampling; semantic construction.  
**Example:** Ambiguous pronoun references yield multiple structured referent candidates in candidate_set{}, which ISc scores deterministically using truth_hypotheses‑prc.  
**Normative home:** 20.44 (ISc).

## commit_id / semantic_snapshot_ref — 20.120, 20.36
**Purpose:** Provide a stable, replay‑deterministic identifier for each turn’s finalized TP and its corresponding historical MTP entry. `commit_id` uniquely identifies the turn‑final TP(N); `semantic_snapshot_ref` points to the archived MTP(N) snapshot used by COB, CIL, and CoHI.  
**Focus:** Turn‑level freeze markers for finalized meaning and realization, enabling deterministic replay, lineage tracking, and long‑horizon inference over the historical record.  
**When:** Generated once per turn by HC‑Flow **after Path B completes** and the turn‑final TP is stable; `semantic_snapshot_ref` is created when TP(N) is archived into MTP(N).  
**Why:** Ensure that all historical processing (COB → CIL → CoHI) operates on authoritative, immutable snapshots, and that replay and audit can reconstruct the exact TP/MTP lineage for any turn.  
**Is not:** A Path‑A commit; a Path‑B commit; a mid‑turn snapshot; a mutable reference; a semantic_core pointer; a DF‑side commit.  
**Example:** After turn completion, HC‑Flow assigns `commit_id = c47a…` to TP(N) and archives it as MTP(N); downstream, CIL reads `semantic_snapshot_ref = MTP(N)` to derive long‑horizon inference for the next turn.  
**Normative home:** 20.120, 20.36, 20.115 (MTP), 20.705 (HC‑Flow).

## DCB (Directional Conversation Basin) — 20.106
**Purpose:** Provide TR with geometric curvature signals indicating when the conversational trajectory bends.  
**Focus:** Compute TP position, direction vector, and curvature; emit directional‑change events consumed by TR.  
**When:** Path‑A, between OB output and TR routing.  
**Why:** TR uses curvature events to avoid misrouting during stance shifts, pivots, or reversals.  
**Is not:** OB; semantic analyzer; TP modifier; persistent writer; Path‑B element.  
**Example:** A trajectory reversal (“I’m confident” → “Actually I’m not sure”) produces a curvature spike consumed by TR.  
**Normative home:** 20.106.

## TB (Truth Basin) — 20.60
**Purpose:** Evaluate truth over the committed semantic snapshot from IB, producing structured truth_state{} for governance‑side evaluation (GBIB → GB) within D‑Flow.  
**Focus:** Assess truth alignment, contradiction, evidential sufficiency, referential consistency, stance‑truth coherence, and factual plausibility over committed meaning; emit truth_state{} without modifying semantic_core or TP.  
**When:** Post‑OuBA commit, only within DF: IB → TB → GBIB → GB. Runs exclusively on committed, immutable meaning; never during Path‑A construction or Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic, auditable truth evaluation separating truth‑interpretation (TB) from governance decision‑making (GBIB/GB), ensuring Path‑B never performs truth computation and operates only on governance‑approved truth summaries.  
**Is not:** Path‑A primitive; meaning constructor; TP writer; scorer; semantic repair engine; generator of interpretation candidates; TB never modifies semantic_core, TP, or MTP and never performs semantic inference or reconstruction.  
**Example:** OuBA commits meaning with insufficient evidence; TB classifies truth_state{ evidential_weak, alignment_low } and forwards it to GBIB for supervisory integration.  
**Normative home:** 20.60 (TB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.80 (GB).
**Note:** Primitive presently is a passthru, will be worked on in the future

## MTP (Master Thought Packet) — 20.115
**Purpose:** Serves as the authoritative, immutable historical TP snapshot maintained by HC‑Flow, providing the stable, replay‑deterministic state used by COB and CIL to build conversation‑level structure and long‑horizon inference.  
**Focus:** Holds the finalized TP(N) state exactly as produced at turn completion: consolidated meaning metadata, object‑semantic fields, lineage roll‑ups, ΔH% accounting, and TP‑local tag families (`tptb{}`, `tpsf{}`), along with all committed TP metadata required for HC‑Flow processing (MTP → COB → CIL → CoHI). 
**When:** Updated once per turn by HC‑Flow **after Path B completes** and the turn‑final TP is stable. MTP is not produced by Path A or Path B; it is a TS‑side archival snapshot created after the turn’s realization is finished.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, auditable, replay‑stable representation of each turn’s final TP so that COB and CIL operate only on authoritative, finalized snapshots and never on in‑flight Path‑A or Path‑B structures.  
**Is not:** A Path‑A object; a Path‑B object; a DF object; a realization commit; a truth evaluator; a topology executor; a scorer; MTP is never modified by any Path‑A or Path‑B primitive and is never used as an operand in meaning construction.  
**Example:** After turn completion, HC‑Flow freezes TP(N) into MTP(N); COB updates conversation objects from this snapshot, and CIL derives long‑horizon inference that CoHI will merge into the next turn’s Path‑B continuity_fields.  
**Normative home:** 20.115, 20.705 (HC‑Flow), 20.112 (COB), 20.114 (CIL), 20.113 (CoHI).

## GBIB (Governance Basin — Inquiry Bridge) — 20.80
**Purpose:** Integrate inquiry evaluation (inquiry_state{}) and truth evaluation (truth_state{}) into a unified governance‑side context, producing gbib_directives{} for GB under governance policy.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, rule‑bound integration of inquiry_state{} and truth_state{}; evaluate inquiry‑truth coherence, governance‑policy applicability, USP constraints, and safety‑relevant governance rules.  
**When:** Post‑OuBA commit, only within DF: IB → TB → GBIB → GB. Runs exclusively on committed, immutable meaning; never during Path‑A construction or Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Provide a stable, auditable supervisory integration layer preventing IB or TB from directly influencing global governance; ensure governance decisions reflect aligned inquiry and truth evaluations under explicit policy constraints.  
**Is not:** Path‑A primitive; Path‑B primitive; semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; TP writer; scorer; generator of interpretation candidates; GBIB never modifies semantic_core, TP, or MTP and never performs semantic inference or reconstruction.  
**Example:** IB reports inquiry_state{ stance_tension } and TB reports truth_state{ evidential_weak }; GBIB integrates these into gbib_directives{ escalate_governance_review } for GB’s supervisory evaluation.  **Normative home:** 20.80 (GBIB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.60 (TB), 20.90 (IB).
**Note:** Primitive presently is a passthru, will be worked on in the future

## GB (Governing Basin) — 20.80
**Purpose:** Perform final governance‑side evaluation within D‑Flow by consuming gbib_directives{} and determining whether committed meaning warrants promotion into Path‑A (IdOB) or emission of governance inference into Path‑B.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, rule‑bound global evaluation of inquiry‑truth alignment, governance‑policy applicability, USP constraints, and safety‑relevant governance rules; produce governance_outcome{} and log governance events into the TP.  
**When:** Post‑OuBA commit, only within DF: IB → TB → GBIB → GB. Runs exclusively on committed, immutable meaning; never during Path‑A construction or Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Provide a stable, auditable governance authority preventing IB or TB from making final decisions; ensure promotion, suppression, or governance‑side routing occurs only under explicit policy constraints and is fully logged for replay.  
**Is not:** Path‑A primitive; Path‑B primitive; semantic interpreter; meaning constructor; TP writer (except governance logs); scorer; generator of interpretation candidates; GB never modifies semantic_core, TP content, or MTP.  
**Example:** GBIB emits gbib_directives{ escalate_governance_review }; GB evaluates governance policy and USP constraints, logs the governance event, and either promotes the object to IdOB (Path‑A) or emits governance inference to REx (Path‑B).  
**Normative home:** 20.80 (GB), 20.705 (DF), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.110 (OuBB), 20.60 (TB), 20.90 (IB).

## IMR — Intake Mismatch Resolver (20.45)
**Purpose:** Stuructual processing data and classify intake mismatches so downstream primitives (IE, CEx, and ISc) can adjust effort, retries, or supervision without granting IMR semantic authority.
**Focus:** Parallel, non‑semantic difficulty scoring plus mismatch tagging, feeding metadata into IE and CEx.
**Mode:** Runs **in parallel** with intake normalization (IIInB → IE), never blocking or owning correction. It emits **difficulty ratings** and **mismatch tags**, not repairs.
**Types (tags, not authorities):**

| Tag | Name                    | Meaning for downstream                                      |
|-----|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| A   | Low difficulty          | Normal semantic processing; no special handling            |
| B   | Medium difficulty       | May suggest extra structural checks or cautious CEx usage  |
| C   | High difficulty / risky | May suggest GB‑gated supervision or conservative semantics |

**Difficulty signal (illustrative):**  

$$
d_{\text{IMR}} = f(\text{fragmentation}, \text{ambiguity}, \text{repair density}, \text{markup complexity})
$$

**When:** Evaluated during intake, after IIInB repair application and while IE is being formed; IMR writes **metadata only**.
**Why:** Give CEx, CST, and downstream semantics a **difficulty map** of the input so they can decide whether to be cautious, escalate, or stay in normal mode—without IMR doing any semantic work itself.
**Is not:**  
- A semantic rewrite engine  
- A meaning constructor  
- A correction authority  
- A TP writer or CE participant  
**Precedence:** C > B > A for **difficulty flags**, but IMR does not own routing or correction; it only annotates.
**Normative home:** 20.45.
**Note:** Current implementation may be a simple passthrough with coarse difficulty flags; richer scoring and feature sets can be added later without changing IMR’s non‑semantic role.

## CorrectionTrigger — 20.45
**Purpose:** Schedule bounded corrective pathways via append‑only supervisory signal, preventing direct semantic_core writes and blocking chained correction loops.  
**Focus:** Append‑only supervisory signal consumed exactly once in the next cycle.  
**When:** Emitted by IMR Type B; consumed on the next Path‑A cycle only.  
**Why:** Correct semantic mismatches without allowing direct writes or recursive correction cascades.  
**Is not:** Direct semantic_core write; chained correction loop; semantic rewrite authority.  
**Normative home:** 20.45.

**When:** Read‑only across pipelines; never participates in Path‑A or Path‑B execution.

## Random Seed Boundary — 20.12, 20.207
**Purpose:** Isolate all nondeterminism to Path‑B expression selection so Path‑A meaning remains fully deterministic and replayable from TP(t).  
**Focus:** Seed‑bounded variation in RG; deterministic meaning‑layer replay under fixed TP(t).  
**When:** Applied at RG and OuBB under seed_scope during Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Enforce replay invariants: identical TP(t)+seed → identical surface form; different seeds → expression variation with identical meaning.  
**Is not:** A‑cycle planner; OpBeh identity selector; meaning‑layer contributor.  
**Example:** Changing the seed alters surface text but leaves semantic_core and TP(t) unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.12, 20.207.

## **KnDt (Knowledge Database — Grammar‑Atomic Grounding Store)**  
**Purpose:** Provide grammar‑atomic lexical, identity, relation, domain‑anchor, qualifier, and truth‑validation primitives to KnC, KnM, and KnF; serve as the deterministic, non‑probabilistic grounding substrate for TS meaning resolution.  
**Focus:** Store atomic grammatical units (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, determiners, conjunctions) and domain‑specific anchors; expose stable lookup primitives for identity resolution, relational grounding, domain classification, and qualifier attachment; maintain deterministic retrieval semantics without inference or embedding‑based similarity.  
**When:** Queried during every grounding cycle by KnC → KnM → KnF; accessed after OuBA commit and before Path‑B realization; used only during grounding and never during Path‑A meaning construction.  
**Why:** Enable TS to ground meaning deterministically using atomic lexical units rather than probabilistic embeddings; reduce database footprint by eliminating combinatorial storage; support entropy‑aware grounding by providing coarse, medium, and fine primitives for SSR‑K.  
**Is not:** Semantic inference engine; probabilistic vector store; embedding similarity system; multi‑hop reasoning module; KnDt never performs contextual interpretation, semantic repair, truth evaluation, or routing decisions.  
**Example:** KnC requests coarse identity anchors (“dog”), KnM requests contextual refinements (“retriever”), and KnF requests fine‑resolution entries (“Golden Retriever”), all retrieved as grammar‑atomic units from KnDt.  
**Normative home:** 20.48.010–030 (KnB primitives); 20.52 (SSR); 20.705 (Path‑A → KnB → Path‑B flow); ts_knowledge_structure.md (grounding architecture).
