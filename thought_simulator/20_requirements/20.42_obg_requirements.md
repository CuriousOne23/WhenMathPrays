# 20.42 OBG (Operational Behavior Group) Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.42  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Approved v0.1 — Stage 4 GATE (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok)

## Purpose

Defines **OBG** (Operational Behavior Group) — register/voice/register-identity primitive identified by `obg_id` on Pipeline B. OBG selects presentation register and voice identity for realization. 

Under multi-core Path A execution (20.115), OBG SHALL treat `core_id` and `orthogonality_signature` as read-only gating constraints and SHALL NOT collapse meaning-layer distinctions established by orthogonal Path A instances.


**Namespace:** OBG ≠ Object Basin **OB** (20.20 HLR-20.020-020).

## Parent Scope

- Second leg of exec triple with [20.41](20.41_opbeh_requirements.md) and [20.43](20.43_xlater_requirements.md)
- Bound in `exec_plan` per [20.205](20.205_execution_packet_xp_requirements.md)

---

## 1. Registry Model

```text
obg_registry_entry {
  obg_id,
  register_class,             // e.g. formal, pedagogical, conversational
  voice_identity_id,
  locale_tag?,
  policy_signature_scope,
  version,
  rationale_codes[]
}
```

1. **HLR-20.042-001:** Every `obg_id` in `exec_plan` SHALL resolve to exactly one OBG registry entry for the active `routing_epoch_id`.
2. **HLR-20.042-002:** Unknown `obg_id` SHALL reject commit with `OBG_UNKNOWN`.
3. **HLR-20.042-003:** OBG registry entries SHALL NOT encode meaning-layer stance or truth commitments — presentation only.

---

## 2. Selection and Gating

4. **HLR-20.042-004:** OBG selection SHALL be deterministic from routing row + frozen `semantic_core` gating fields (`done_state`, `messy_input_record` read-only) — not from seed.
   - OBG selection SHALL be deterministic **per core_id** when multi-core Path A execution is active.
   - OBG SHALL NOT select different registers for identical routing rows that differ only by core_id.

6. **HLR-20.042-005:** Under `MI_VAGUE` or `underspec_flag`, OBG selection MAY defer to policy-defined neutral register only — SHALL NOT invent missing semantic slots (20.17 HLR-20.017-015).
7. **HLR-20.042-006:** OBG SHALL NOT read or write TP / `lane_id`.

+ ### 2.1 Multi-Core Gating Constraints (New)
HLR-20.042-015: OBG SHALL respect multi-core Path A execution boundaries (20.115):

HLR-20.042-016:  - OBG SHALL read `core_id` from committed `semantic_core` as a **gating-only** field.
- OBG SHALL NOT reinterpret, modify, or override `core_id`.
- OBG SHALL NOT select different registers/voices for TPs belonging to different cores in a way that collapses or merges meaning-layer distinctions.

HLR-20.042-017: OBG SHALL read `orthogonality_signature` (read-only) when present:
- OBG SHALL NOT select a register/voice that contradicts or overrides the meaning-layer orthogonality domain established in Path A.
- OBG SHALL NOT fuse or reconcile meaning-layer distinctions implied by orthogonality_signature.

---

## 3. Triple and OuB Handoff

7. **HLR-20.042-007:** OBG SHALL participate in invalid-triple matrix validation (20.56).
8. **HLR-20.042-008:** OuB expression SHALL consume committed `obg_id` from `exec_plan` for register/voice rendering ([20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md)).

### 3.1 Core-Local Lineage Preservation (New)
HLR-20.042-018: OBG SHALL preserve `core_id` and `orthogonality_signature` lineage when participating in the OpBeh–OBG–XlateR triple:
- OBG SHALL NOT collapse or reinterpret multi-core lineage.
- OBG SHALL NOT introduce cross-core blending of register/voice identity.
- OBG SHALL pass through core_id unchanged to OuB realization.

---

## 4. Seed Boundary

9. **HLR-20.042-009:** `obg_id` selection SHALL NOT use seed; seed affects OuB expression artifact choice only downstream (HLR-20.012-010).
