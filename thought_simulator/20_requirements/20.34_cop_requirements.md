# 20.34 COP Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic constraints for optional asynchronous coprocessor behavior used by CIL/COB supervisory workflows.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes COP obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including:
- 20.10 architectural requirement that COP remain optional, deterministic, bounded, propose-only, and auditable.
- 20.10 safe-boundary activation, profile precedence, and deterministic reject-with-audit invariants.
- 20.30 functional requirement that COP SHALL NOT mutate authoritative decision state and SHALL preserve deterministic provenance and bounded queue/fairness behavior.
- 20.30 cross-module contract obligations for explicit, versioned, testable, and auditable interfaces.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.033-001: Coprocessor operation SHALL remain propose-only relative to TS core semantics.
2. HLR-20.033-002: Coprocessor proposals SHALL include deterministic provenance metadata (source, basis snapshot, deterministic input hash).
3. HLR-20.033-003: Coprocessor effects SHALL become visible only at deterministic safe-boundary commits.
4. HLR-20.033-004: Coprocessor queue behavior SHALL be bounded and deterministic.
5. HLR-20.033-005: Fairness policy and overload policy SHALL be execution-signature-bound.
6. HLR-20.033-006: Safety-critical proposals SHALL receive deterministic bounded-latency guarantees.
7. HLR-20.033-007: Overload handling SHALL follow deterministic policy (quota, preemption, or reject-with-audit).
8. HLR-20.033-008: Unsupported coprocessor policy states SHALL reject deterministically with fixed audit reason codes.
9. HLR-20.033-009: Coprocessor behavior SHALL NOT mutate authoritative decision state outside GB/TS policy-approved boundaries.
10. HLR-20.033-010: Coprocessor behavior SHALL be replayable and auditable under deterministic execution signature state.
11. HLR-20.033-011: COP ordering-critical behavior SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state and SHALL NOT depend on wall-clock precedence.
12. HLR-20.033-012: COP commit visibility SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with active TS-core decision cycles.
13. HLR-20.033-013: COP profile/policy overlaps SHALL resolve by execution-signature-bound profile precedence over environment defaults.
14. HLR-20.033-014: On profile/policy activation validation failure, COP SHALL retain prior valid signature-bound behavior deterministically and emit fixed audit reason codes.
15. HLR-20.033-015: COP enum, wire-map, and schema versions SHALL remain frozen per release and SHALL reject unsupported states with deterministic audit reason codes.
16. HLR-20.033-016: COP reason-code dictionaries SHALL use immutable identifiers, deterministic deprecation rules, and versioned deterministic mapping behavior.
17. HLR-20.033-017: COP SHALL NOT read or mutate OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, CIL, COB, or MTP internal implementation state and SHALL operate only on approved proposal interfaces and canonicalized metadata views.
18. HLR-20.033-018: COP queue admission, scheduling, and fairness behavior SHALL remain deterministic under equivalent input/signature state.
19. HLR-20.033-019: COP overload behavior SHALL be bounded and deterministic, including deterministic quota/preemption/reject outcomes and deterministic audit logging.
20. HLR-20.033-020: COP proposal lifecycle events, including proposal creation, queueing, scheduling, commit-eligible status, commit visibility, reject, and expiry, SHALL be logged in deterministic append-only audit records.
21. HLR-20.033-021: COP concurrent non-ordering side effects SHALL remain isolated from authoritative decision state and SHALL NOT influence signature-visible artifacts.
22. HLR-20.033-022: COP external interfaces SHALL be explicit, versioned, deterministic, and auditable across supported compatibility windows.
23. HLR-20.033-023: COP requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
24. HLR-20.033-024: COP behavior SHALL remain fully testable via deterministic proposal fixtures, deterministic queue/scheduling fixtures, deterministic overload fixtures, and reproducible profile-bound test-oracle behavior.

## Functional Boundaries
- COP is optional and propose-only and SHALL NOT mutate authoritative TS-core decision state.
- CIL owns conversation intake/integration and supervisory escalation paths.
- COB owns conversation-object lifecycle, lineage, replay/export semantics.
- COP-visible outcomes SHALL become system-visible only through approved deterministic supervisory/commit paths.

## COP Suitability Profile
COP is appropriate for work that is:
- bounded in scope and queueable;
- deterministic for equivalent input, signature, and policy state;
- expressible as proposal preparation, metadata transformation, commit staging, scheduling, or advisory analysis;
- provenance-rich and replayable;
- safe to reject, defer, or preempt without corrupting authoritative state.

COP is not appropriate for work that:
- mutates authoritative decision state directly;
- requires unbounded search, open-ended optimization, or speculative self-directed exploration;
- depends on wall-clock precedence or hidden mutable state;
- requires direct access to OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, CIL, COB, or MTP internals beyond approved proposal interfaces;
- cannot be deterministically bounded, audited, or re-verified at a safe boundary.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic provenance capture and proposal serialization.
- Deterministic queue/fairness/scheduling behavior for equivalent state.
- Deterministic safe-boundary commit visibility and reject-with-audit behavior.
- Deterministic overload handling outcomes and append-only lifecycle logging.
- Deterministic non-interference with authoritative decision-state artifacts.
