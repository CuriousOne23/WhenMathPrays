# 20.10 TS Architectural Principles

## Purpose
Defines the architectural principles that constrain all TS requirement modules.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.010-001: The TS core SHALL be deterministic for identical input, MTP state, and seed.
2. HLR-20.010-002: Meaning construction SHALL remain explicit, structured, and auditable.
3. HLR-20.010-003: Variability SHALL be isolated to the output layer through the randomness boundary.
4. HLR-20.010-004: Program flow SHALL follow fixed phase ordering and SHALL prohibit cross-phase mutation.
5. HLR-20.010-005: Observer influence SHALL remain external and SHALL NOT mutate TS core state directly.

## Program Flow Integration
This module incorporates the architectural ordering intent previously captured in legacy program-flow requirements.

## TS Architectural Principles (Expanded)
1. HLR-20.010-006: TS SHALL be structured for modifiability, debuggability, extendability, and human clarity through explicit, modular, and transparent architecture boundaries.
2. HLR-20.010-007: TS SHALL meet explicit and measurable execution-speed and memory-efficiency constraints for macro execution workloads.
3. HLR-20.010-008: TS SHALL provide deterministic and structured monitoring through MB, including GB, OuB, and IB observability pathways.
4. HLR-20.010-009: Every TS function, primitive, and macro SHALL define and expose deterministic, auditable, and comparable TCU (or equivalent) execution-speed metrics.
5. HLR-20.010-010: TS SHALL remain platform-independent and SHALL NOT rely on architecture assumptions tied to specific hardware, OS, or runtime implementations.
6. HLR-20.010-011: TS architecture SHALL prioritize top-down thought-processing structure, functions, and primitives over implementation-specific mechanics.
7. HLR-20.010-012: TS internal state and transitions SHALL be highly visible, inspectable, and explainable for diagnostics, research, architectural analysis, and stability investigations.
8. HLR-20.010-013: TS SHALL define bounded, measurable, and comparable efficiency metrics for size, speed, and cost, and SHALL introduce new metrics where existing measures are insufficient.
9. HLR-20.010-014: TS SHALL support controlled yet flexible architectural evolution under explicit governance rules that prevent architectural drift.
10. HLR-20.010-015: TS documentation SHALL remain complete, detailed, and continuously updated, explicitly recording architectural decisions, invariants, and constraints.
11. HLR-20.010-016: TS SHALL deterministically handle noisy or ill-formed input.
  - InB SHALL map noisy surface forms into stable conceptual basins without probabilistic inference.
  - OB activation SHALL be concept-based and spelling-independent.
  - TB SHALL reconstruct intended structure under degraded syntax without nondeterministic interpretation.
  - RB SHALL stabilize meaning without hallucinating corrections or inventing content.
  - ΔH% updates SHALL remain bounded and deterministic under noisy input.
  - Meaning construction SHALL remain stable and auditable despite surface-form degradation.
12. HLR-20.010-017: Testability SHALL be a first-class architectural requirement.
  - All basins (InB, OB, TB, RB, MB) SHALL expose deterministic and reproducible interfaces suitable for automated testing.
  - Every function, primitive, and macro SHALL define test hooks, expected outputs, invariants, and failure modes.
  - ΔH%, MTP evolution, and basin activations SHALL be observable, testable, and reproducible.
  - Testability SHALL be preserved during architecture evolution; no subsystem SHALL become opaque or untestable.
  - Determinism SHALL be treated as a mandatory testability condition: identical inputs SHALL produce identical outputs.

## 1.8 Supervisory Separation Principle
13. HLR-20.010-018: The TS core SHALL focus exclusively on deterministic execution of OB, RB, TB, and MTP logic.
14. HLR-20.010-019: The TS core SHALL NOT make global supervisory decisions, except simple local binary checks (including lane completeness checks, merge validity checks, and ΔH% normalization triggers).
15. HLR-20.010-020: GB SHALL perform global supervisory decisions, including inquiry gating, branching control, stability evaluation, drift/oscillation detection, and safety-mode transitions.
16. HLR-20.010-021: All TS service requests that affect global behavior SHALL be routed through GB.
17. HLR-20.010-022: TS->GB->TS supervisory flow SHALL be explicit, deterministic, and bounded.
18. HLR-20.010-023: OuB MAY tag incomplete meaning; TS core SHALL convert this into a supervisory request; GB SHALL approve or deny.
19. HLR-20.010-024: GB MAY be implemented on a separate processor, microcontroller, or safety controller to preserve isolation, determinism, and bounded supervisory overhead.
20. HLR-20.010-025: TS core and GB SHALL communicate through a deterministic supervisory interface with explicit directionality, timing, and rate limits.
21. HLR-20.010-026: GB SHALL NOT mutate TP/MTP meaning-construction state and SHALL influence only supervisory flow-control.

## 1.x FIFO Ordering and Sequential Interpretation Invariant
- HLR-20.010-061: TS SHALL process all incoming TPs in strict FIFO (first-in, first-out) order.
- HLR-20.010-062: FIFO ordering SHALL be preserved as part of TP metadata (including linear sequence index fields) and SHALL define the relative sequence of statements for all downstream basins.
- HLR-20.010-063: FIFO ordering SHALL be treated as an architectural invariant required for deterministic interpretation, reproducibility, and correct TS implementation.
- HLR-20.010-064: All basins (InB, OB, IB, TB, GB, OuB) SHALL rely on FIFO TP ordering as the authoritative chronological sequence for meaning construction and supervisory evaluation.
- HLR-20.010-065: TS code implementing TP ingestion, routing, and basin dispatch SHALL preserve FIFO ordering without reordering, skipping, or speculative lookahead.

## 1.9 GB Supervisory Duties and Inquiry Governance
22. HLR-20.010-027: GB SHALL operate asynchronously relative to TS execution.
23. HLR-20.010-028: TS SHALL NOT block or stall while waiting for GB decisions.
24. HLR-20.010-029: GB SHALL interrupt TS only at deterministic safe boundaries, including end-of-cycle, merge, or interpretation boundaries.
25. HLR-20.010-030: When OuB tags incomplete meaning, TS SHALL store the TP/MTP snapshot in a bounded pending inquiry buffer and SHALL continue execution without delay.
26. HLR-20.010-031: TS SHALL NOT instantiate an IB until GB approves the inquiry request.
27. HLR-20.010-032: OuB SHALL NOT route directly to IB.
28. HLR-20.010-033: Inquiry SHALL occur exclusively through OB<->IB interactions after IB instantiation.
29. HLR-20.010-034: TS SHALL update RB routing for inquiry paths only after GB approval.
30. HLR-20.010-035: GB SHALL supervise IB evolution continuously and MAY issue bounded actions including Crop, Prune, Dampen, Slow, Reshape, Suspend, or Kill.
31. HLR-20.010-036: GB SHALL ensure IB evolution remains within global stability, branching, and TCU envelopes.
32. HLR-20.010-037: GB SHALL evaluate IBs for promotion to OB based on stability, ΔH% reduction, usage metrics, conflict rate, and TCU efficiency.
33. HLR-20.010-038: GB MAY require human approval before IB-to-OB promotion.
34. HLR-20.010-039: TS SHALL collapse IB->OB only after GB approval.
35. HLR-20.010-040: GB SHALL monitor OBs for excessive complexity, semantic sprawl, high conflict rate, or degraded ΔH% profiles.
36. HLR-20.010-041: GB MAY request IB-based decomposition of an OB into smaller sub-OBs.
37. HLR-20.010-042: TS SHALL retire or deprecate an original OB only after GB approval.
38. HLR-20.010-043: All supervisory actions, including IB creation, IB evolution, IB promotion, OB decomposition, splits, and merges, SHALL be logged with when, why, and how context.
39. HLR-20.010-044: Supervisory logs SHALL be append-only, time-indexed, and auditable.
40. HLR-20.010-090: GB SHALL arbitrate whether COP-derived proposals become effective in CIL and COB state; such effects SHALL be applied only through GB-approved deterministic safe-boundary TS->GB->TS supervisory flow.

## 1.10 IB Metadata Requirements and GB Population Supervision
40. HLR-20.010-045: IB identity metadata SHALL include a unique IB identifier, provisional-operator flag, and link to the originating TP/MTP snapshot.
41. HLR-20.010-046: IB origin metadata SHALL include triggering OB identifiers, OuB incomplete-meaning tag, creation timestamp, and GB approval identifier.
42. HLR-20.010-047: IB evolution metadata SHALL include ΔH% trajectory, stability impact, branching metrics, conflict rate, usage frequency, and TCU cost profile.
43. HLR-20.010-048: IB inquiry-state metadata SHALL include current hypotheses, pending evidence requests, partial interpretations, inquiry depth, and inquiry branch state.
44. HLR-20.010-049: IB promotion metadata SHALL include stability thresholds, ΔH% plateau detection, conflict-resolution stability, usage thresholds, human-approval flag, and GB promotion request identifier.
45. HLR-20.010-050: IB decomposition/retirement metadata SHALL include decomposition-target status, decomposition-plan participation, merge/split markers, and retirement marker state.
46. HLR-20.010-051: IB metadata SHALL remain explicit, deterministic, bounded, and auditable throughout the IB lifecycle.
47. HLR-20.010-052: GB SHALL supervise IB population behavior, not only individual IB instances, using TP/MP supervisory views and without direct IB-internal reads.
48. HLR-20.010-053: GB SHALL detect converging IB sets and MAY request an IB-merge supervisory action.
49. HLR-20.010-054: GB SHALL detect divergent IB behavior and MAY request an IB-split supervisory action.
50. HLR-20.010-055: GB SHALL enforce IB population stability by preventing deterministic-equivalent duplication, unresolved drift, and runaway branching growth.
51. HLR-20.010-056: TS SHALL apply IB merge/split operations only at deterministic safe boundaries.
52. HLR-20.010-057: All IB merge/split events SHALL be logged with when, why, and how context.
53. HLR-20.010-058: GB supervisory duties in this subsection SHALL preserve asynchronous GB decision flow and TS non-blocking execution behavior.
54. HLR-20.010-059: OuB SHALL NOT route directly to IB, and inquiry exchange SHALL remain constrained to OB<->IB interaction paths after GB approval and routing updates.
55. HLR-20.010-060: GB governance for IB evolution, IB promotion, and OB decomposition SHALL remain deterministic, bounded, and recorded in append-only supervisory logs.

## 1.11 Deterministic Runtime and Governance Invariants
56. HLR-20.010-066: All ordering-critical decisions SHALL derive from deterministic sequence/token state and SHALL NOT depend on wall-clock precedence.
57. HLR-20.010-067: Timestamps SHALL be treated as evidence-only metadata; authoritative ordering SHALL derive exclusively from deterministic sequence tokens.
58. HLR-20.010-068: When deterministic profiles overlap, execution-signature-bound profiles SHALL take precedence over environment defaults.
59. HLR-20.010-069: Signature-bound policy changes SHALL activate only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT mix within a cycle.
60. HLR-20.010-070: On policy/signature activation validation failure, TS SHALL retain the prior signature deterministically and emit a fixed audit reason code.
61. HLR-20.010-071: Unsupported deterministic feature states SHALL trigger deterministic rejection paths with fixed audit reason codes.
62. HLR-20.010-072: Enum and wire-map encodings SHALL remain frozen by version and SHALL support deterministic mapping or reject-with-audit behavior.
63. HLR-20.010-073: Reason codes SHALL use globally unique immutable identifiers with reserved ranges, deterministic deprecation rules, and versioned dictionaries.
64. HLR-20.010-074: Hash construction SHALL enforce domain-separated preimage prefixes for execution signatures, audit chains, provenance artifacts, and compaction proofs.
65. HLR-20.010-075: Concurrent non-ordering side effects SHALL remain isolated from decision state and SHALL NOT influence signature-visible artifacts.
66. HLR-20.010-076: Diagnostic exports SHALL use deterministic flush boundaries, ordering, and inclusion/exclusion rules.
67. HLR-20.010-077: Diagnostic artifact retention and purge behavior SHALL follow deterministic windows, cadence, and purge ordering.
68. HLR-20.010-078: Zero-event windows SHALL follow deterministic empty-artifact semantics: either emit canonical empty artifacts or emit no artifact with fixed audit reason code.
69. HLR-20.010-079: Replay/export manifests SHALL use deterministic field ordering, key sorting, and canonical serialization.
70. HLR-20.010-080: Canonical parsers SHALL enforce strict grammar versions, strict parse mode, and deterministic malformed-input reject codes.
71. HLR-20.010-081: Parser profiles SHALL define deterministic lexical normalization for whitespace, Unicode escapes, and literal-versus-escaped character handling.
72. HLR-20.010-082: Replay-horizon mode changes SHALL activate only at deterministic safe boundaries under atomic policy activation rules.
73. HLR-20.010-083: Replay-evidence export behavior SHALL be deterministic and mode-specific, including full-stream, bounded-window+anchor, and summary-proof representations.

## 1.12 COB/CIL/COP Architectural Decomposition
74. HLR-20.010-084: TS architecture SHALL define CIL, COB, and optional COP as first-class deterministic sub-architectures governed by 20.30 functional invariants.
75. HLR-20.010-085: CIL architecture SHALL own deterministic TP/MTP conversation-integration duties, including FIFO-preserving intake, snapshot coherence, deterministic ambiguity escalation paths, and deterministic profile/policy enforcement.
76. HLR-20.010-086: COB architecture SHALL own deterministic conversation-object state, including identity lineage, promotion/deprecation lifecycle governance, compaction/replay-horizon policy behavior, and deterministic export/redaction semantics.
77. HLR-20.010-087: COP architecture SHALL remain optional and propose-only, and SHALL preserve deterministic bounded queueing, provenance, overload handling, and safe-boundary commit visibility.
78. HLR-20.010-088: CIL/COB/COP interface contracts SHALL be explicit, versioned, deterministic, and auditable, and SHALL include fixed reject/audit reason-code behavior for unsupported states.
79. HLR-20.010-089: 20.31 COB, 20.32 CIL, and 20.33 COP requirement modules SHALL normatively decompose this architecture and SHALL NOT weaken constraints defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
