# 20.10 TS Architectural Principles

**Document ID:** 20.10  
**Version:** 0.6  
**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Status:** Draft - For CP Review

## Purpose
Defines the architectural principles that constrain all TS requirement modules.

## Role in the Development Flow

This document serves as **foundational guidance** for the entire project. It defines the core architectural principles that all other 20-series documents, 40-series prototypes, and eventual 10-series canonical requirements must respect. 

Insights, challenges, and validation results from 40-series prototyping SHOULD be used to refine this document and mature the final normative definitions in the 10-series. This document is **not** intended as final executable specification.

## Normative Requirements

**HLR-20.010-001:** The TS core SHALL be deterministic for identical input, MTP state, and seed.

**HLR-20.010-002:** Meaning construction SHALL remain explicit, structured, and auditable.

**HLR-20.010-003:** Variability SHALL be isolated to the output layer through the randomness boundary.

**HLR-20.010-004:** Program flow SHALL follow fixed phase ordering and SHALL prohibit cross-phase mutation.

**HLR-20.010-005:** Observer influence SHALL remain external and SHALL NOT mutate TS core state directly.

## Expanded Architectural Principles

**HLR-20.010-006:** TS SHALL maintain strict separation between deterministic core computation and non-deterministic output generation.

**HLR-20.010-007:** All meaning-bearing structures (TP, MTP, OB, TB, etc.) SHALL use explicit, typed semantic fields.

**HLR-20.010-008:** ΔH% regulation SHALL act as the primary dynamic governor for trace stability, termination, and energy accounting.

**HLR-20.010-009:** Basin locality SHALL be enforced: computations SHALL prefer local grammar and operators unless explicitly authorized by GB at safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.010-010:** Commitments SHALL only be formed on traces that meet explicit stability thresholds.

**HLR-20.010-011:** All supervisory actions by GB SHALL be auditable with fixed reason codes.

**HLR-20.010-012:** TS SHALL prohibit unbounded recursion or search within any single execution cycle.

**HLR-20.010-013:** Input SHALL be mapped to the nearest stable conceptual structures without invention or hallucination.

**HLR-20.010-014:** Contradictions SHALL be preserved as parallel candidate traces rather than forcibly resolved at low levels.

**HLR-20.010-015:** Emotional and value-laden content SHALL be explicitly tagged and routed to appropriate basins.

**HLR-20.010-016:** Human oversight SHALL be requested for critical commitment promotions or safety boundary violations.

**HLR-20.010-017:** All state transitions SHALL remain replayable from initial seed and input.

## 1.8 Supervisory Separation Principle

**HLR-20.010-018:** GB SHALL act solely as supervisor and arbitrator, not as primary meaning constructor.

**HLR-20.010-019:** GB SHALL NOT perform direct primitive chaining or semantic composition.

**HLR-20.010-020:** Basin-internal logic SHALL remain independent of GB except at explicit safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.010-021:** GB decisions SHALL be limited to approve/deny, select-among-candidates, and request-clarification.

**HLR-20.010-022:** All GB actions SHALL route through deterministic supervisory interfaces.

**HLR-20.010-023:** GB complexity SHALL be strictly bounded.

**HLR-20.010-024:** GB SHALL delegate downward to basin-local grammar wherever possible.

**HLR-20.010-025:** GB intervention frequency SHALL be monitored and kept below defined thresholds.

**HLR-20.010-026:** GB SHALL maintain an explicit responsibility matrix (see 20.16).

## 1.x FIFO Ordering and Sequential Interpretation Invariant

**HLR-20.010-061:** All interpretation phases SHALL respect strict FIFO ordering of input tokens and prior TPs.

**HLR-20.010-062:** Sequential interpretation invariant SHALL be preserved across all basins.

**HLR-20.010-063:** No reordering or lookahead SHALL occur without explicit GB authorization at safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.010-064:** Each interpretation step SHALL be deterministic given prior state.

**HLR-20.010-065:** Full trace SHALL remain reproducible in original sequential order.

## 1.9 GB Supervisory Duties and Inquiry Governance

**HLR-20.010-027:** GB SHALL govern IB instantiation and population.

**HLR-20.010-028:** GB SHALL approve or deny new inquiry creation.

**HLR-20.010-029:** GB SHALL monitor and manage IB split/merge operations.

**HLR-20.010-030:** GB SHALL arbitrate between competing candidate traces.

**HLR-20.010-031:** GB SHALL validate candidate commitments before promotion.

**HLR-20.010-032:** GB SHALL detect and respond to ΔH% oscillations or drift.

**HLR-20.010-033:** GB SHALL enforce cross-basin coordination protocols.

**HLR-20.010-034:** GB SHALL request human oversight for high-stakes decisions.

**HLR-20.010-035:** GB SHALL maintain coherence across parallel traces.

**HLR-20.010-036:** GB SHALL enforce commitment lifecycle policies.

**HLR-20.010-037:** GB SHALL monitor population levels and trigger pruning when necessary.

**HLR-20.010-038:** GB SHALL validate learned component outputs against grammar and safety rules.

**HLR-20.010-039:** GB SHALL enforce safety policies on all relationship formations.

**HLR-20.010-040:** GB SHALL log all interventions with deterministic reason codes.

**HLR-20.010-041:** GB SHALL respect deterministic cycle boundaries.

**HLR-20.010-042:** GB SHALL NOT mutate TP/MTP semantic content directly.

**HLR-20.010-043:** GB SHALL operate only at explicit safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.010-044:** GB SHALL support auditable rollback of supervisory actions.

## 1.10 IB Metadata Requirements and GB Population Supervision

**HLR-20.010-045:** Every IB SHALL carry rich metadata including uncertainty, lineage, priority, and ΔH% trajectory.

**HLR-20.010-046:** GB SHALL use IB metadata for population control and scheduling.

**HLR-20.010-047:** IB metadata SHALL include creation timestamp, source, confidence, and conflict flags.

**HLR-20.010-048:** GB SHALL enforce maximum active IB limits per conversation.

**HLR-20.010-049:** GB SHALL prioritize IBs based on explicit priority and urgency metadata.

**HLR-20.010-050:** IB retirement SHALL occur only after explicit GB approval or timeout.

**HLR-20.010-051:** IB metadata SHALL track evidence accumulation and contradiction status.

**HLR-20.010-052:** GB SHALL use metadata to detect stalled or low-value inquiries.

**HLR-20.010-053:** All IB metadata fields SHALL be deterministic and auditable.

**HLR-20.010-054:** GB SHALL enforce fairness in IB scheduling.

**HLR-20.010-055:** IB population control SHALL prevent resource exhaustion.

**HLR-20.010-056:** IB metadata SHALL support deterministic priority-based selection.

**HLR-20.010-057:** GB SHALL enforce minimum and maximum population bounds per basin.

**HLR-20.010-058:** Metadata updates SHALL be atomic and traceable.

**HLR-20.010-059:** GB SHALL periodically review IB metadata for relevance and health.

**HLR-20.010-060:** All IB lifecycle actions SHALL require explicit GB authorization.

## 1.11 Deterministic Runtime and Governance Invariants

**HLR-20.010-066:** Runtime SHALL enforce fixed phase ordering with no cross-phase side effects.

**HLR-20.010-067:** All governance actions SHALL be deterministic and auditable.

**HLR-20.010-068:** No module SHALL mutate state outside its defined contract.

**HLR-20.010-069:** All cycles SHALL terminate with explicit done state or bounded degradation.

**HLR-20.010-070:** State mutation SHALL only occur through typed primitive operators.

**HLR-20.010-071:** Cycle boundaries SHALL be strictly enforced.

**HLR-20.010-072:** Governance invariants SHALL prevent race conditions.

**HLR-20.010-073:** All runtime behavior SHALL remain fully deterministic given the same input and seed.

**HLR-20.010-074:** Governance rules SHALL apply uniformly across all execution cycles.

**HLR-20.010-075:** Runtime invariants SHALL be enforced at every safe boundary.

**HLR-20.010-076:** No implicit state carry-over SHALL occur between cycles.

**HLR-20.010-077:** Governance SHALL ensure bounded resource usage per cycle.

**HLR-20.010-078:** All timing and ordering constraints SHALL be strictly respected.

**HLR-20.010-079:** Error states SHALL trigger deterministic fallback behavior.

**HLR-20.010-080:** Runtime SHALL support full replayability for debugging and verification.

**HLR-20.010-081:** Governance invariants SHALL take precedence over performance optimizations.

**HLR-20.010-082:** All modules SHALL respect the global determinism contract.

**HLR-20.010-083:** Runtime SHALL provide explicit logging of all governance decisions.

## 1.12 COB/CIL/COP Architectural Decomposition

**HLR-20.010-084:** Conversation state SHALL be decomposed into COB (identity), CIL (lifecycle), and COP (policy).

**HLR-20.010-085:** COB SHALL manage persistent conversation identity and history.

**HLR-20.010-086:** CIL SHALL manage conversation lifecycle states and transitions.

**HLR-20.010-087:** COP SHALL enforce conversation-level safety and relevance policies.

**HLR-20.010-088:** Decomposition SHALL enable independent scaling and auditing of each component.

**HLR-20.010-089:** Changes to one component SHALL not implicitly affect others.

**HLR-20.010-098:** COP SHALL enforce conversation-level safety and relevance policies.

## 1.13 RB Relationship Safety Policy Enforcement

**HLR-20.010-091:** All relationships between TPs SHALL be vetted against RB safety policies.

**HLR-20.010-092:** RB SHALL reject unsafe or prohibited relationships deterministically.

**HLR-20.010-093:** Safety violations SHALL trigger explicit alerts and logging.

**HLR-20.010-094:** RB policies SHALL cover harmful, unethical, or incoherent relationships.

**HLR-20.010-095:** Enforcement SHALL occur before commitment formation.

**HLR-20.010-096:** All safety decisions SHALL be auditable.

**HLR-20.010-097:** RB SHALL support dynamic policy updates under GB supervision.

## 1.14 Architectural Risk Mitigation and Refinements (Added 2026-06-03)

### Scope
This section defines architectural risks and mitigation strategies identified through rigorous external critique. It does not specify implementation details; those belong in the 30- and 40-series.

### Non-Goals
- This section does not define new primitives.
- It does not specify basin-specific grammar.
- It does not define GB algorithms.
- It does not prescribe ΔH% formulas or commitment decay rules. These belong in the 30- and 40-series.

### Strengths (Worth Protecting)
1. **Foundational Principles** — Determinism, auditability, explicit meaning construction, and supervisory control.  
2. **Layered Governance Model** — The 10/20/30/40/50 structure and promotion process.  
3. **Core Mechanisms** — Semantic operators + basin locality + ΔH% dynamic regulation + GB supervision.  
4. **Complexity Management** — Hierarchical grammar, commitments, typed operators, and basin locality.

### Major Architectural Risks

**HLR-20.010-099:** **GB Overload Risk** — The Global Brain (GB) may become a monolithic “god module” responsible for too many critical functions, creating a single point of failure and performance bottleneck.

**HLR-20.010-100:** **Messy Real-World Input Risk** — Insufficient explicit mechanisms for handling contradictory, vague, emotionally charged, or incomplete input, which could lead to unstable ΔH% trajectories or incoherent traces.

**HLR-20.010-101:** **Conceptual vs Validated Mechanisms Risk** — Many core mechanisms remain largely conceptual and have not yet been proven at scale or under realistic conditions.

**HLR-20.010-102:** **Theoretical Elegance Risk** — Mechanisms that appear elegant on paper may introduce hidden complexity, unexpected non-determinism, or poor performance when implemented.

**HLR-20.010-103:** **Trace Explosion & Resource Exhaustion Risk** — Uncontrolled growth in parallel traces, commitments, basin transitions, or IB population could exhaust memory and computation resources.

### Required Refinements (20-Layer Priority)

**HLR-20.010-104:** Define clear **GB Boundaries and Limitations** (see 20.16) to prevent overload and distribute responsibilities downward.

**HLR-20.010-105:** Develop **Explicit Messy Input Handling** rules (see 20.17) for contradictory, vague, emotional, and incomplete input.

**HLR-20.010-106:** Define **Success Criteria and Failure Modes** for all major mechanisms (see 20.18).

**HLR-20.010-107:** Establish **Explicit Complexity Limits** including maximum trace depth, parallel candidates, commitment depth, and population bounds.

**HLR-20.010-108:** Ensure all refinements maintain the core invariants of determinism, auditability, and explicit meaning construction.

### Mitigation Strategy
All risks and refinements identified in this section SHALL be addressed with concrete, traceable artifacts in the 20-series before major advancement in the 40_playground or 50_design layers.
