# 20.113 — CoHI‑prm (Conversation History Integrator Primitive)

## 1. Purpose (informative)

CoHI is the **continuity integration primitive** of Path B.  
It reads the five persistent cross‑turn structures from COB, projects them against the current‑turn TP snapshot from TPTB, computes continuity scores, and writes the resulting **continuity_fields** for consumption by LI.

CoHI performs:

- no inference  
- no meaning scoring  
- no planning  
- no routing  
- no modification of COB  
- no modification of lineage_fields  
- no modification of semantic_core  

CoHI provides a **turn‑scoped, deterministic, read‑only projection** of COB that freezes continuity context for the turn.

---

## 2. Architectural Position (informative)

CoHI is the **first** primitive in Path B:

**CoHI → LI → REx → RPlan → RPU → ReB → OuBB**

Upstream:  
- TPTB  
- TPSF  
- COB  

Downstream:  
- LI  

CoHI is the **sole** primitive permitted to read COB.

---

## 3. Inputs (informative)

CoHI reads exactly the following:

### 3.1 COB structures  
- structural_history  
- referential_history  
- conversation_objects  
- topic_threads  
- continuity_markers  

### 3.2 Representation‑layer fields  
Produced by TPTB.

### 3.3 Scoring‑layer fields  
Produced by TPSF.

### 3.4 truth_tags  
Produced by A‑side.

### 3.5 safety_flags  
Produced by A‑side.

No other fields may be accessed.

---

## 4. Transfer Function (informative)

Let:

$$
\mathcal{B} = \langle B_{\text{sh}},\ B_{\text{rh}},\ B_{\text{co}},\ B_{\text{tt}},\ B_{\text{cm}} \rangle
$$

and let $T$ denote the current‑turn TP snapshot.

CoHI applies the normalisation function $\Lambda$:

$$
\langle \gamma_{\text{sh}},\ \gamma_{\text{rh}},\ \gamma_{\text{co}},\ \gamma_{\text{tt}},\ \gamma_{\text{cm}} \rangle
= \Lambda(\mathcal{B},\ T)
$$

$\Lambda$ executes two ordered operations for each COB structure $B_k$.

---

### 4.1 Operation 1 — Turn‑Scoped Projection

Each entry $b_j \in B_k$ is retained when:

$$
\text{overlap}(b_j,\ T) > 0
$$

Define:

$$
\Pi(B_k,\ T) = \{\, b_j \in B_k \mid \text{overlap}(b_j,\ T) > 0 \,\}
$$

Fallback rule:

- If $\Pi(B_k,\ T) = \emptyset$ and $B_k \neq \emptyset$, CoHI retains the full $B_k$ and records `PROJECTION_FALLBACK`.  
- If $B_k = \emptyset$, the output sub‑field is empty.

---

### 4.2 Operation 2 — Continuity Score Computation

Each retained entry receives:

$$
\text{score}(b_j,\ T)
= \text{recency}(b_j)\ \ast\ \text{relevance}(b_j,\ T)
$$

with:

$$
0 \le \text{score}(b_j,\ T) \le 1
$$

Each output entry contains:

- `field_ref`  
- `continuity_score`  

---

## 5. Outputs (informative)

CoHI writes exactly six fields:

- `tp.continuity_fields.structural_history`  
- `tp.continuity_fields.referential_history`  
- `tp.continuity_fields.conversation_objects`  
- `tp.continuity_fields.topic_threads`  
- `tp.continuity_fields.continuity_markers`  
- `tp.continuity_fields.fault_code`  

All must be present on every execution path.

---

## 6. High‑Level Requirements (normative)

### Input and Access Control  
- **HLR‑20.113‑001** — CoHI SHALL read only the fields enumerated in the Input Contract.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑002** — CoHI SHALL NOT read any meaning‑layer field, lineage_fields, semantic_core, or any field owned by LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, ReB, or OuBB.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑003** — CoHI SHALL perform a pre‑condition check and SHALL write all continuity_fields as empty and SHALL write a non‑empty `fault_code` when any required input is absent.

### Projection  
- **HLR‑20.113‑004** — CoHI SHALL compute $\Pi(B_k,\ T)$ for each COB structure.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑005** — When $\Pi(B_k,\ T) = \emptyset$ and $B_k \neq \emptyset$, CoHI SHALL fall back to $B_k$ and SHALL record `PROJECTION_FALLBACK`.

### Scoring  
- **HLR‑20.113‑006** — CoHI SHALL compute $\text{score}(b_j,\ T) \in [0,1]$ for each retained entry.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑007** — CoHI SHALL NOT write any continuity_score outside $[0,1]$.

### Output Contract  
- **HLR‑20.113‑008** — CoHI SHALL populate all six continuity_fields on every execution path.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑009** — CoHI SHALL NOT leave any continuity_fields sub‑field absent or null.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑010** — CoHI SHALL NOT write to any field owned by TPTB, TPSF, LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, ReB, or OuBB.

### Ordering  
- **HLR‑20.113‑011** — CoHI SHALL NOT begin execution until TPTB and TPSF have committed all required fields.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑012** — CoHI SHALL complete all writes before LI begins execution.

### Invariant Preservation  
- **HLR‑20.113‑013** — CoHI SHALL preserve Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability by ensuring continuity_fields do not introduce referent mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑014** — CoHI SHALL propagate provenance_chain without modification.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑015** — CoHI SHALL NOT introduce semantic mutation relative to upstream meaning.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑016** — CoHI SHALL NOT introduce style or tone mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑017** — CoHI SHALL maintain deterministic replay: identical inputs SHALL produce identical outputs.

### COB Integrity  
- **HLR‑20.113‑018** — CoHI SHALL NOT modify any COB structure.  
- **HLR‑20.113‑019** — CoHI SHALL treat all COB reads as read‑only for the duration of the turn.

### Normative Home  
- **HLR‑20.113‑020** — Any implementation of CoHI SHALL conform to this specification at revision `0.3.1` or a formally approved successor.

---

## 7. Is / Is Not (informative)

CoHI **is**:
- the continuity integrator of Path B  
- the sole reader of COB  
- the producer of continuity_fields  

CoHI **is not**:
- an inference engine  
- a meaning scorer  
- a planner  
- a router  
- a continuity reasoner  
- a memory modifier  
- a surface generator  

---

## 8. Example (informative)

COB contains:

- conversation_objects: $b_1$ (“the dataset”), last referenced two turns prior  
- referential_history: $b_2$ (“it → the dataset”), last referenced one turn prior  
- topic_threads: $b_3$ (“data analysis”), last referenced this turn  

Other COB structures are empty.

Projection:

$$
\Pi(B_{\text{co}},\ T) = \{ b_1 \},\quad
\Pi(B_{\text{rh}},\ T) = \{ b_2 \},\quad
\Pi(B_{\text{tt}},\ T) = \{ b_3 \}
$$

Scoring:

- $\text{score}(b_1,\ T) = 0.70 \ast 0.95 = 0.665$  
- $\text{score}(b_2,\ T) = 0.85 \ast 0.98 = 0.833$  
- $\text{score}(b_3,\ T) = 1.00 \ast 1.00 = 1.000$  

CoHI writes all continuity_fields.  
LI reads them and resolves meaning without reading COB.
