# 20.113 CoHI — Conversation History Integrator Primitive

## Purpose

CoHI is the continuity integration primitive for Path B. It reads the five
persistent cross-turn structures from COB, projects them against the current-turn
TP snapshot from TPTB, computes a continuity score for each surviving entry, and
writes the five normalised continuity sub-fields into `tp.continuity_fields` for
consumption by LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, and OuBB. CoHI performs no inference, no
scoring of meaning candidates, no routing, and no planning. It does not modify COB.

**[HLR-20.113-001]** The CoHI primitive SHALL read the five COB structures
enumerated in the Input Contract, apply the normalisation function $\Lambda$ to
project each structure against the current-turn TP snapshot, and write all five
`tp.continuity_fields` sub-fields before LI begins execution for the same turn.

## Input Contract

The table below defines all inputs CoHI reads. No other field, external store, or
runtime service may be accessed during CoHI execution.

| Field | Source | Required | Type | Validity Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COB.structural_history` | COB | Required | structured object | must be present; may be empty; read-only — CoHI SHALL NOT modify |
| `COB.referential_history` | COB | Required | structured object | must be present; may be empty; read-only — CoHI SHALL NOT modify |
| `COB.conversation_objects` | COB | Required | array | must be present; may be empty; read-only — CoHI SHALL NOT modify |
| `COB.topic_threads` | COB | Required | array | must be present; may be empty; read-only — CoHI SHALL NOT modify |
| `COB.continuity_markers` | COB | Required | array | must be present; may be empty; read-only — CoHI SHALL NOT modify |
| Representation-layer fields | TPTB | Required | per TPTB output contract | all fields in TPTB output contract must be present; used as projection target $T$ |
| `tp.truth_tags` | A-side | Required | array | may be empty |
| `tp.safety_flags` | A-side | Required | array | may be empty |

**[HLR-20.113-002]** The CoHI primitive SHALL read only the fields enumerated in
the Input Contract table; it SHALL NOT read from any meaning-layer field owned by
LI, any realization substrate field owned by REx, or any field owned by RPlan,
RPU, or OuBB.

**[HLR-20.113-003]** The CoHI primitive SHALL perform a pre-condition check before
$\Lambda$ is applied: if any of the five COB structures is absent, or if any
required representation-layer field from TPTB is absent, CoHI SHALL write all five
`tp.continuity_fields` sub-fields as empty, SHALL write a non-empty
`tp.continuity_fields.fault_code` identifying the absent source, and SHALL halt
without applying $\Lambda$.

## Transfer Function

Let $\mathcal{B} = \langle B_{\text{sh}},\ B_{\text{rh}},\ B_{\text{co}},\ B_{\text{tt}},\ B_{\text{cm}} \rangle$
denote the five COB structures and let $T$ denote the current-turn TP snapshot
from TPTB. The normalisation function $\Lambda$ maps $\mathcal{B}$ and $T$ to the
five continuity sub-fields:

$$
\langle \gamma_{\text{sh}},\ \gamma_{\text{rh}},\ \gamma_{\text{co}},\ \gamma_{\text{tt}},\ \gamma_{\text{cm}} \rangle
= \Lambda(\mathcal{B},\ T)
$$

$\Lambda$ applies two operations to each COB structure $B_k$ in order.

### Operation 1 — Turn-Scoped Projection

Each entry $b_j \in B_k$ is tested for current-turn relevance against $T$.
An entry is projected (retained) when its referent overlaps with the
representation fields of $T$. Let $\Pi(B_k, T)$ denote the projected subset:

$$
\Pi(B_k,\ T) = \\{\ b_j \in B_k \mid \text{overlap}(b_j,\ T) > 0\ \\}
$$

When $\Pi(B_k, T) = \emptyset$ and $B_k \neq \emptyset$, CoHI retains the full
$B_k$ as a fallback and records the fallback in `tp.continuity_fields.fault_code`.
When $B_k = \emptyset$, the corresponding output sub-field is written as empty.

**[HLR-20.113-004]** The CoHI primitive SHALL project each COB structure $B_k$
against $T$ to produce $\Pi(B_k, T)$; when $\Pi(B_k, T) = \emptyset$ and
$B_k \neq \emptyset$ it SHALL fall back to retaining the full $B_k$ and SHALL
record `fault_code = PROJECTION_FALLBACK` in `tp.continuity_fields.fault_code`.

### Operation 2 — Continuity Score Computation

Each retained entry $b_j \in \Pi(B_k, T)$ receives a continuity score
$\text{score}(b_j, T) \in [0, 1]$ computed as the product of two factors:

$$
\text{score}(b_j,\ T) = \text{recency}(b_j) \cdot \text{relevance}(b_j,\ T)
$$

where $\text{recency}(b_j) \in [0, 1]$ decreases monotonically with turn distance
since $b_j$ was last referenced in COB, and $\text{relevance}(b_j, T) \in [0, 1]$
measures the degree of overlap between $b_j$'s referent and $T$'s representation
fields. Score bounds:

$$
0 \leq \text{score}(b_j,\ T) \leq 1
$$

**[HLR-20.113-005]** The CoHI primitive SHALL compute $\text{score}(b_j, T)$ for
every retained entry $b_j$ and SHALL write the result into the `continuity_score`
field of that entry in the corresponding `tp.continuity_fields` sub-field; it
SHALL NOT write a value outside $[0, 1]$.

**[HLR-20.113-006]** The CoHI primitive SHALL write an empty array or empty
structured object for any `tp.continuity_fields` sub-field whose corresponding
$B_k = \emptyset$; it SHALL NOT leave any sub-field absent regardless of whether
$B_k$ is empty or non-empty.

**[HLR-20.113-007]** The CoHI primitive SHALL NOT modify any COB structure during
the execution of $\Lambda$; all reads from COB are read-only for the duration of
the turn.

## Output Contract

The table below defines all fields CoHI writes. LI, REx, and RPlan must validate
presence of every sub-field before beginning their own execution.

| Field | Type | Legal Values | Semantics |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tp.continuity_fields.structural_history` | structured object | non-null; may be empty | Normalised projection of `COB.structural_history`; each entry carries `field_ref` and `continuity_score` |
| `tp.continuity_fields.referential_history` | structured object | non-null; may be empty | Normalised projection of `COB.referential_history`; each entry carries `field_ref` and `continuity_score` |
| `tp.continuity_fields.conversation_objects` | array | non-null; may be empty | Normalised projection of `COB.conversation_objects`; each entry carries `field_ref` and `continuity_score` |
| `tp.continuity_fields.topic_threads` | array | non-null; may be empty | Normalised projection of `COB.topic_threads`; each entry carries `field_ref` and `continuity_score` |
| `tp.continuity_fields.continuity_markers` | array | non-null; may be empty | Normalised projection of `COB.continuity_markers`; each entry carries `field_ref` and `continuity_score` |
| `tp.continuity_fields.fault_code` | string | non-empty on pre-condition fault or projection fallback; empty string otherwise | Machine-readable cause; LI and REx read this for diagnostic tracing |

Each entry written into any of the five sub-fields SHALL carry exactly two fields:

| Entry Field | Type | Legal Values |
|---|---|---|
| `field_ref` | string | non-empty; identifies the COB referent by its cross-turn TP field path |
| `continuity_score` | float | $\text{score}(b_j, T) \in [0, 1]$ |

**[HLR-20.113-008]** The CoHI primitive SHALL populate all six `tp.continuity_fields`
output fields on every execution path; no output field SHALL be left absent or null
except where the table explicitly permits an empty array or empty object.

**[HLR-20.113-009]** The CoHI primitive SHALL NOT write to any representation-layer
field owned by TPTB, any scoring-layer field owned by TPSF, or any field owned by
LI, REx, RPlan, RPU, or OuBB.

## When

CoHI runs once per turn. Its position in the Path B execution order is fixed: after
TPTB and TPSF have each committed their respective fields, and before LI reads any
`tp.continuity_fields` sub-field.

**[HLR-20.113-010]** The CoHI primitive SHALL NOT begin execution until TPTB has
committed all representation-layer fields and TPSF has committed all scoring-layer
fields to a completed state for the current turn.

**[HLR-20.113-011]** The CoHI primitive SHALL complete all writes to
`tp.continuity_fields` before LI begins execution for the same turn.

## Why

LI, REx, and RPlan all require cross-turn continuity context to produce correct
outputs, but none of them can read COB directly without becoming stateful and
non-deterministic with respect to the TP snapshot. CoHI provides a single,
turn-scoped, read-only projection of COB that freezes the relevant history into
the TP at a known point in the execution order. This ensures that every downstream
primitive sees the same continuity context for the same turn, and that COB is never
read from or written to at arbitrary points in the pipeline.

## Is Not

CoHI is not an inference engine.
CoHI is not a meaning extractor.
CoHI is not a planner.
CoHI is not a scorer of meaning candidates.
CoHI is not a router.
CoHI is not a continuity reasoner; it projects and scores history but does not
reason about what that history implies.
CoHI is not a memory modifier; it does not write to COB.
CoHI does not generate XP or surface expression.
CoHI does not perform global inference.

## Example

COB contains three relevant structures for the current turn:

- `COB.conversation_objects`: one entry $b_1$ representing "the dataset we
  discussed earlier," last referenced two turns prior.
- `COB.referential_history`: one entry $b_2$ representing a resolved pronoun
  "it → the dataset," last referenced one turn prior.
- `COB.topic_threads`: one entry $b_3$ representing the active topic thread
  "data analysis," last referenced this turn.

All other COB structures (`structural_history`, `continuity_markers`) are empty.

Pre-condition check passes. $\Lambda$ begins.

**Operation 1 — Projection.** $T$'s representation fields reference "the dataset"
and "it." All three entries overlap with $T$:
$\Pi(B_{\text{co}}, T) = \\{b_1\\}$,
$\Pi(B_{\text{rh}}, T) = \\{b_2\\}$,
$\Pi(B_{\text{tt}}, T) = \\{b_3\\}$.

**Operation 2 — Scoring.**

- $\text{score}(b_1, T) = 0.70 \cdot 0.95 = 0.665$
- $\text{score}(b_2, T) = 0.85 \cdot 0.98 = 0.833$
- $\text{score}(b_3, T) = 1.00 \cdot 1.00 = 1.000$

CoHI writes all six sub-fields. `tp.continuity_fields.fault_code = ""`. LI reads
all five sub-fields, uses $b_2$ to resolve the pronoun "it" and $b_1$ to anchor
"the dataset," and commits meaning without reading COB directly.
