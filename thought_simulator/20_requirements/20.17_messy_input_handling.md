# 20.17 Messy-Input Handling Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.17  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Draft — dual-pipeline hardening (Pipeline A only)

## Purpose

Defines explicit, deterministic rules for handling **contradictory, vague, emotional, incomplete, fragmentary, and noisy/malformed input** within **Pipeline A (meaning construction) only**. This module operationalizes HLR-20.010-100/105 and SHALL NOT weaken invariants in [20.12_ts_invariants.md](20.12_ts_invariants.md).

**Goal:** Messy-input semantics remain explicit in `semantic_core`; Pipeline B (realization) SHALL NOT smooth, invent, or resolve them.

## Parent Scope

- **Pipeline A only:** InB, Relational Basin, Object Basin, Thought Router, TB, Merge, MTP, IB proposals
- **Does not modify:** 20.10, 20.12, 20.20, 20.30 bootstrap documents
- **Pipeline B forbidden:** OuB, OpBeh/OBG/XlateR, IMR, SRP SHALL NOT alter messy-input semantics (HLR-20.017-030–032)

## Non-Goals

- Basin-specific implementation algorithms (see 20.35, 40-series)
- User interface copy or clarification UX
- Probabilistic or latent inference paths

---

## 1. Messy-Input Taxonomy (Mandatory)

Every detected messy-input condition SHALL be classified into one or more taxonomy classes using deterministic rules and fixed reason codes:

| Class | Code | Definition |
|-------|------|------------|
| **Contradiction** | `MI_CONTRA` | Mutually incompatible propositions, goals, or stance signals in the same or adjacent input scope |
| **Vagueness** | `MI_VAGUE` | Underspecified goals, referents, quantities, or constraints — multiple stable interpretations remain |
| **Emotional / value-laden** | `MI_AFFECT` | Strong affect, identity, or value signals requiring explicit tagging — not errors |
| **Incomplete / fragmentary** | `MI_INCOMP` | Missing required slots, truncated utterances, or absent context needed for commitment |
| **Noisy / malformed** | `MI_NOISE` | Spelling/syntax/surface-form degradation without stable semantic commitment |

1. **HLR-20.017-001:** Pipeline A SHALL classify messy-input conditions into the taxonomy classes above using deterministic detectors at InB, OB, TB, and Merge safe boundaries.
2. **HLR-20.017-002:** Multi-class input SHALL record all applicable classes with canonical sort order by class code; no class SHALL suppress logging of another.
3. **HLR-20.017-003:** Classification SHALL be reproducible from `(raw_input, prior_MTP, policy_signature)` with no latent inference.

## 2. Pipeline A Ownership Rules

Messy-input semantics SHALL be fully represented in `semantic_core` before Pipeline B executes.

### 2.1 Contradiction (`MI_CONTRA`)

4. **HLR-20.017-004:** Contradictions SHALL spawn **parallel candidate traces** with explicit `conflict_flag = true` and `conflict_group_id`; TS SHALL NOT force single-winner resolution at InB, OB, or low-level Merge.
5. **HLR-20.017-005:** Contradiction traces SHALL carry `contradiction_status` ∈ {`ACTIVE`, `PRESERVED`, `GB_ARBITRATED`} and SHALL log paired proposition IDs in conflict.
6. **HLR-20.017-006:** ΔH% for contradiction lanes SHALL record **volatility markers** and missing-mass attribution per conflicting suppression — not collapsed into a single lane total.

**Required TP/MTP tags:** `messy_input_class[]`, `conflict_flag`, `conflict_group_id`, `contradiction_status`, `parallel_trace_ids[]`

### 2.2 Vagueness (`MI_VAGUE`)

7. **HLR-20.017-007:** Vagueness SHALL produce `underspec_flag = true` and `underspec_slots[]` listing unresolved slot IDs (goal, referent, constraint, etc.).
8. **HLR-20.017-008:** Vague goals SHALL remain **high-uncertainty hypotheses**; commitment strength SHALL NOT exceed policy threshold until underspec slots are resolved or GB-approved.
9. **HLR-20.017-009:** ΔH% missing-mass SHALL increase for each unresolved `underspec_slot` using deterministic missing-mass rules — not probabilistic confidence.

**Required tags:** `underspec_flag`, `underspec_slots[]`, `goal_hypothesis_ids[]`, `commitment_strength_cap`

### 2.3 Emotional / value-laden (`MI_AFFECT`)

10. **HLR-20.017-010:** Emotional content SHALL receive explicit `affect_tags[]`, `value_tags[]`, and routing to appropriate TB modules (stance, affect, social) per 20.10-015.
11. **HLR-20.017-011:** Emotional intensity SHALL NOT cause unbounded ΔH% oscillation; deterministic damping markers SHALL apply when intensity exceeds policy threshold.
12. **HLR-20.017-012:** Affect tagging SHALL NOT substitute for propositional content — `MI_AFFECT` SHALL NOT auto-resolve `MI_VAGUE` or `MI_CONTRA`.

**Required tags:** `affect_tags[]`, `value_tags[]`, `affect_intensity`, `damping_applied`

### 2.4 Incomplete / fragmentary (`MI_INCOMP`)

13. **HLR-20.017-013:** Incomplete input SHALL set `incomplete_flag = true` and `missing_slots[]` with deterministic slot taxonomy.
14. **HLR-20.017-014:** When missing slots block commitment, Pipeline A SHALL emit an **IB-Creation-Request** (via OuB incomplete-meaning pathway or direct InB/TB trigger per 20.30) for GB approval — not silent gap-fill.
15. **HLR-20.017-015:** Incomplete input SHALL NOT trigger Pipeline B realization retry or OpBeh/OBG changes to mask missing semantics.

**Required tags:** `incomplete_flag`, `missing_slots[]`, `ib_request_pending`

### 2.5 Noisy / malformed (`MI_NOISE`)

16. **HLR-20.017-016:** InB SHALL map noisy surface forms to the **nearest stable conceptual structure** without inventing content (HLR-20.010-013).
17. **HLR-20.017-017:** Noisy mapping SHALL set `noise_normalized_flag = true` and `surface_form_hash` → `canonical_concept_id` audit pairs.
18. **HLR-20.017-018:** OB activation SHALL be spelling-independent and concept-based for equivalent noisy variants (per 20.40).

**Required tags:** `noise_normalized_flag`, `normalization_map[]`, `malformed_input_code` (if reject)

### 2.6 What Pipeline B must not do

19. **HLR-20.017-019:** Pipeline B SHALL NOT create, modify, or clear any messy-input tag in `semantic_core`.
20. **HLR-20.017-020:** Pipeline B SHALL NOT resolve contradictions, fill underspec slots, dampen affect representation, or complete fragmentary meaning in `semantic_core`.

---

## 3. Forbidden Behaviors

21. **HLR-20.017-021:** Pipeline A and B SHALL NOT use **latent inference** or probabilistic hallucination to handle messy input.
22. **HLR-20.017-022:** Pipeline A SHALL NOT **invent semantics** to fill gaps, resolve conflicts, or stabilize vague goals without explicit IB/GB-approved evidence.
23. **HLR-20.017-023:** Pipeline A SHALL NOT **force contradiction resolution** at InB, OB, or Merge except via GB-approved arbitration with logged `contradiction_status = GB_ARBITRATED`.
24. **HLR-20.017-024:** Pipeline B (OuB, XlateR, OpBeh, OBG, IMR) SHALL NOT perform **output-layer smoothing** that presents a single coherent stance when `semantic_core` contains active `conflict_flag` or `underspec_flag` unless TB commitment rules explicitly authorize a scoped presentation field.
25. **HLR-20.017-025:** Any OuB presentation of a single stance under active contradiction SHALL reference `presentation_scope_id` and `authorized_by_commitment_rule` in `exec_trace` — not alter `semantic_core`.

## 4. Deterministic Handling Algorithms (Reference)

Normative behavior summaries; detailed algorithms in 20.35.

### 4.1 Contradiction → parallel traces

26. **HLR-20.017-026:** On `MI_CONTRA` detection, Pipeline A SHALL fork deterministic parallel traces `T_a`, `T_b` (and further if needed) each carrying one conflicting proposition set; shared context SHALL be copied; conflicting fields SHALL diverge with linked `conflict_group_id`.

### 4.2 Vagueness → underspecification markers

27. **HLR-20.017-027:** On `MI_VAGUE` detection, Pipeline A SHALL insert `underspec_slots[]` entries with stable slot IDs and increment ΔH% missing-mass per unresolved slot; no default slot fill.

### 4.3 Emotional content → TB routing

28. **HLR-20.017-028:** On `MI_AFFECT` detection, Relational Basin SHALL route to affect/stance/social TB modules per fixed policy table; tags SHALL be written before Merge commits.

### 4.4 Incomplete → IB proposals

29. **HLR-20.017-029:** On `MI_INCOMP` with commitment blocked, Pipeline A SHALL queue IB-Creation-Request with `missing_slots[]` snapshot; TS continues without waiting (per 20.30 GB async model).

### 4.5 Noisy / malformed → normalization without hallucination

30. **HLR-20.017-030:** InB SHALL apply deterministic lexical normalization (per 20.30 profiles); unmapped tokens SHALL receive `malformed_input_code` and bounded degrade — not generative repair.

## 5. Overflow / Degradation Interaction

31. **HLR-20.017-031:** Under overflow (`overflow_flag = true`), messy-input tags SHALL NOT be silently dropped; truncation SHALL preserve `messy_input_class[]`, `conflict_flag`, and `underspec_flag` in priority order before optional field pruning.
32. **HLR-20.017-032:** Lane truncation under overflow SHALL NOT collapse active contradiction traces into one lane without `overflow_type = LANE_TRUNCATION` and `truncated_parallel_trace_ids[]` logged.
33. **HLR-20.017-033:** ΔH% normalization under overflow SHALL preserve missing-mass accounting for unresolved messy-input slots in `ΔH%_normalization_applied` per 20.30 §8.3.
34. **HLR-20.017-034:** Suppressed non-critical messy-input metadata SHALL be listed in `truncated_fields[]`; suppression SHALL NOT remove conflict or underspec primary flags.

---

## 6. Replay Guarantees

### Pipeline A replay

- Messy-input tags, parallel trace IDs, conflict groups, underspec slots, and normalization maps SHALL be fully serialized in TP/MTP audit records with canonical ordering.
- Identical `(raw_input, prior_MTP, policy_signature)` SHALL produce identical messy-input classification and tag sets.

### Dual-pipeline integrity

35. **HLR-20.017-035:** Stripping `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` from a trace SHALL preserve all messy-input semantics in `semantic_core` for replay equivalence (HLR-20.012-011).
36. **HLR-20.017-036:** Pipeline B realization SHALL be a pure function of read-only `semantic_core` (including messy tags) + `exec_plan` + seed; messy-input state visible to OuB SHALL NOT be altered by expression choices.
37. **HLR-20.017-037:** Hidden smoothing is forbidden: if `conflict_flag = true` or `underspec_flag = true` in `semantic_core`, replay diff MUST show those flags present before and after Pipeline B execution.

## Worked Example — Contradiction Preserved

| Step | Pipeline | Result |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | A / InB | User: "It is safe" + "It is dangerous" → `MI_CONTRA` |
| 2 | A / Merge | Parallel traces `T_safe`, `T_danger`; `conflict_flag = true` |
| 3 | B / OuB | Presents scoped output only if commitment rule authorizes; `semantic_core` unchanged |
| 4 | Replay | Strip exec envelopes → both traces and conflict flags reproduce |

## Verification Focus

- Contradiction never force-merged without GB audit
- Vagueness never auto-filled without evidence
- Pipeline B trace shows no messy-tag mutation
- Overflow scenarios preserve conflict/underspec primary flags
- Replay equivalence with messy-input golden fixtures
