**20.40.020 — SROB Requirements (Structural Refinement OB Layer)**  
**Status:** Normative  
**Parent:** 20.40_ob_requirements.md  
**Scope:** Requirements for the Structural Refinement OB (SROB) layer in Path A.  
**Purpose:** Define mandatory refinement behavior, structural normalization, and residue‑sharpening rules for the second OB layer.

---


## **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

The Structural Refinement OB (SROB) layer is the **second classification layer** in Path A.  
Its purpose is to **normalize, refine, and disambiguate** the structural output produced by SOB.

SROB performs:

- structural normalization  
- list/table/code/math block normalization  
- ambiguity resolution  
- metadata refinement  
- sharpening of operator, domain, tone, and constraint hints  

SROB does **not** interpret meaning, apply semantic logic, or enforce constraints.  
Its output must be structurally canonical and ready for constraint extraction by CnOB.

---

## 1. Inputs

**HLR‑20.40.020‑026**
The SROB layer SHALL accept the normalized structural TP, residue fragments, and structural metadata produced by SOB.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑027**
The SROB layer MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — and MAY annotate these structurally. The SROB layer SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of these fields.

---

## **2. Normative Requirements**

### **2.1 Structural Refinement**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑001**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** refine SOB segmentation into normalized structural units.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑002**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** normalize list structures, including depth, numbering, and indentation.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑003**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** normalize table structures, including headers, rows, and cell boundaries.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑004**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** normalize code blocks and math blocks without altering their content.

---

### **2.2 Ambiguity Resolution**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑005**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** resolve ambiguous segmentation boundaries, including sentence boundaries and nested list structures.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑006**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure consistent representation of structurally similar units across the TP.

---

### **2.3 Metadata Refinement**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑007**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** refine structural metadata from SOB into canonical normalized form.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑008**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** sharpen domain hints into more precise structural domain categories.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑009**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** sharpen operator hints into more precise structural operator categories.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑010**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** sharpen tone hints into more precise structural tone categories.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑011**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** sharpen constraint hints into more precise structural constraint categories.

---

### **2.4 Residue Formation**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑012**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** produce refined residue fragments representing normalized structure and sharpened hints.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑013**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure that refined residue fragments remain addressable by the routing block (RB).

**HLR‑20.40.020‑014**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure that structural entropy after SROB is strictly less than the entropy of the SOB output.

---

### **2.5 Prohibited Behavior**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑015**  
The SROB layer **SHALL NOT** perform semantic interpretation of any kind.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑016**  
The SROB layer **SHALL NOT** infer meaning, intent, or semantic roles.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑017**  
The SROB layer **SHALL NOT** apply or enforce constraint logic.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑018**  
The SROB layer **SHALL NOT** modify the original TP text.

---

### **2.6 Routing Requirements**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑019**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** produce output suitable for routing into CnOB as the next classification layer.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑020**  
The SROB layer **SHALL NOT** route directly into semantic OBs.

---

## **3. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

These requirements govern the geometric properties of residue fragments produced by SROB.

**HLR‑20.40.020‑021**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure that each refined residue fragment $r_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i'$.

Inline example:  
$h_i' = hash(r_i')$

**HLR‑20.40.020‑022**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure that the combined query address $q'$ formed by the routing block satisfies:

$$
q' = h_1' \oplus h_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h_n'
$$

**HLR‑20.40.020‑023**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** ensure that refined residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature‑invariance.

---

## **4. Outputs**

**HLR‑20.40.020‑024**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** output:

- a normalized structural TP  
- a refined residue set  
- refined structural metadata  
- sharpened operator‑hint metadata  
- sharpened domain‑hint metadata  
- sharpened tone‑hint metadata  
- sharpened constraint‑hint metadata  

**HLR‑20.40.020‑025**  
The SROB layer **SHALL** guarantee that its output is valid input for CnOB.
