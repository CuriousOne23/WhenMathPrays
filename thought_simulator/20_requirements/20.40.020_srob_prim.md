# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structural Refinement OB (SROB) layer is the **second classification layer** in Path A.  
Its role is to **normalize, refine, and disambiguate** the structural output produced by SOB.

SROB performs:

- structural normalization  
- list/table/code/math block normalization  
- ambiguity resolution  
- metadata refinement  
- sharpening of operator, domain, tone, and constraint hints  

SROB does **not** interpret meaning, apply semantic logic, or enforce constraints.  
Its output must be structurally canonical and ready for constraint extraction by CnOB.

---

# **2. Inputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑026**  
SROB SHALL accept the normalized structural TP, residue fragments, and structural metadata produced by SOB.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑027**  
SROB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — and MAY annotate these structurally.  
SROB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of these fields.

---

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑028 — Structural Discourse‑Flag Normalization**  
SROB SHALL normalize discourse‑context flags propagated from SOB — including recent‑entity continuity, temporal‑shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical structural metadata without semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑029 — Structural Discourse‑Residue Refinement**  
SROB SHALL refine discourse‑context residue fragments into deterministic, bounded, addressable structural fragments suitable for routing by RB and consumption by downstream OB layers (STPX, ISc).

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑030 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
SROB SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves structural normalization, refinement, or ambiguity resolution.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑031 — Read‑Only Discipline**
SROB SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑032 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
SROB SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to structural refinement and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑033 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
SROB SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑034 — Structural‑Only Interpretation**
SROB SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as structural or semantic‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑035 — Residue Integration**
SROB SHALL encode any consumed TP‑stream metadata as refined structural residue fragments when such metadata affects normalization or ambiguity resolution.

# **3. Normative Requirements**

---

## **3.1 Structural Refinement**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑001**  
SROB SHALL refine SOB segmentation into normalized structural units.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑002**  
SROB SHALL normalize list structures, including depth, numbering, and indentation.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑003**  
SROB SHALL normalize table structures, including headers, rows, and cell boundaries.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑004**  
SROB SHALL normalize code blocks and math blocks without altering their content.

---

## **3.2 Ambiguity Resolution**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑005**  
SROB SHALL resolve ambiguous segmentation boundaries, including sentence boundaries and nested list structures.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑006**  
SROB SHALL ensure consistent representation of structurally similar units across the TP.

---

## **3.3 Metadata Refinement**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑007**  
SROB SHALL refine structural metadata from SOB into canonical normalized form.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑008**  
SROB SHALL sharpen domain hints into more precise structural domain categories.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑009**  
SROB SHALL sharpen operator hints into more precise structural operator categories.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑010**  
SROB SHALL sharpen tone hints into more precise structural tone categories.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑011**  
SROB SHALL sharpen constraint hints into more precise structural constraint categories.

## **3.4 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

SROB may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic or semantic‑adjacent metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to structural refinement. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- residue_metadata (upstream SOB)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- identity_metadata (IdOB)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)

SROB treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
SROB ignores metadata that is not applicable to structural normalization or ambiguity resolution.  
SROB does not consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
SROB interprets TP‑stream metadata only as structural or semantic‑adjacent features.

---

## **3.5 Residue Formation**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑012**  
SROB SHALL produce refined residue fragments representing normalized structure and sharpened hints.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑013**  
SROB SHALL ensure refined residue fragments remain addressable by RB.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑014**  
SROB SHALL ensure structural entropy after SROB is strictly less than the entropy of the SOB output.

---

## **3.6 Prohibited Behavior**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑015**  
SROB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑016**  
SROB SHALL NOT infer meaning, intent, or semantic roles.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑017**  
SROB SHALL NOT apply or enforce constraint logic.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑018**  
SROB SHALL NOT modify original TP text.

---

## **3.7 Routing Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑019**  
SROB SHALL produce output suitable for routing into CnOB.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑020**  
SROB SHALL NOT route directly into semantic OBs.

---

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (GitHub‑Safe KaTeX)**

These requirements govern geometric properties of refined residue fragments.

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑021**  
SROB SHALL ensure each refined residue fragment $r\_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h\_i'$:

$$
h\_i' = hash(r\_i')
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑022**  
SROB SHALL ensure the combined query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h\_1' \oplus h\_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h\_n'
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑023**  
SROB SHALL ensure refined residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature‑invariance.

---

# **5. Outputs**

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑024**  
SROB SHALL output:

- normalized structural TP  
- refined residue set  
- refined structural metadata  
- sharpened operator‑hint metadata  
- sharpened domain‑hint metadata  
- sharpened tone‑hint metadata  
- sharpened constraint‑hint metadata  

### **HLR‑20.40.020‑025**  
SROB SHALL guarantee its output is valid input for CnOB.
