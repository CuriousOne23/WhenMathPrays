# **20.40.010 — SOB Requirements (Structural OB Layer)**  
**Status:** Normative  
**Parent:** 20.40_ob_requirements.md  
**Scope:** Requirements for the Structural OB (SOB) layer in Path A.  
**Purpose:** Define the mandatory behavior, invariants, and residue‑handling rules for the first OB layer.

---

## **1. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

The Structural OB (SOB) layer is the **first classification layer** in Path A.  
Its purpose is to extract **structure before meaning**, producing a structured TP and initial residue suitable for refinement by SROB.

SOB performs:

- segmentation  
- modality detection  
- operator‑hint extraction  
- domain‑hint extraction  
- tone‑hint extraction  
- constraint‑hint extraction  

SOB does **not** interpret meaning, resolve semantics, or apply constraints.  
It prepares the TP for deterministic routing deeper into Path A.

---

## **2. Normative Requirements**

### **2.1 Structural Segmentation**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑001**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** segment the input TP into structural units including sentences, clauses, lists, tables, code blocks, and mathematical blocks.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑002**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** preserve the order of structural units exactly as provided in the input.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑003**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** identify list structures (ordered, unordered, nested) and represent them explicitly in the TP structure.

---

### **2.2 Modality Extraction**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑004**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** classify each structural unit by modality, including but not limited to: declarative, interrogative, imperative, conditional, hypothetical.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑005**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** mark modality classifications in the TP structure without altering the original text.

---

### **2.3 Operator‑Hint Extraction**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑006**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** extract operator hints when present, including summarize, compare, classify, derive, plan, explain, rewrite, and transform.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑007**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** represent operator hints as residue fragments suitable for routing.

---

### **2.4 Domain‑Hint Extraction**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑008**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** extract domain hints when present, including math‑like, code‑like, narrative‑like, legal‑like, technical‑like, and conversational‑like.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑009**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** encode domain hints as residue fragments without interpreting domain semantics.

---

### **2.5 Tone‑Hint Extraction**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑010**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** extract tone hints when present, including formal, casual, technical, supportive, urgent, and neutral.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑011**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** encode tone hints as residue fragments without modifying the TP.

---

### **2.6 Constraint‑Hint Extraction**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑012**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** extract constraint hints when present, including precision, conciseness, politeness, safety, and formatting constraints.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑013**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** represent constraint hints as residue fragments for later processing by CnOB.

---

### **2.7 Structural Integrity Requirements**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑014**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** ensure that no structural unit is lost, merged incorrectly, or reordered.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑015**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** ensure that all extracted structural metadata is deterministic and reproducible for identical inputs.

---

### **2.8 Residue Formation Requirements**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑016**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** produce residue fragments representing unresolved structure, operator hints, domain hints, tone hints, and constraint hints.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑017**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** ensure that residue fragments are addressable by the routing block (RB) using the system’s geometric addressing scheme.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑018**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** ensure that residue entropy after SOB is strictly less than the entropy of the raw TP.

---

### **2.9 Routing Requirements**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑019**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** produce residue suitable for routing into SROB as the next classification layer.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑020**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** not route directly into semantic OBs or constraint OBs.

---

### **2.10 Prohibited Behavior**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑021**  
The SOB layer **SHALL NOT** perform semantic interpretation of any kind.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑022**  
The SOB layer **SHALL NOT** infer meaning, intent, or semantic roles.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑023**  
The SOB layer **SHALL NOT** apply constraints or enforce constraint logic.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑024**  
The SOB layer **SHALL NOT** modify the original text of the TP.

---

## **2.11 Discourse‑Context Propagation (Structural, Non‑Semantic)**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑030**  
The SOB layer **MAY** consume CE‑derived and CEx‑derived **discourse‑context metadata** in a **read‑only** manner, including but not limited to: recent‑entity continuity, temporal markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑031**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** encode discourse‑context metadata **only as structural flags** in the residue and **SHALL NOT** perform semantic interpretation, semantic role inference, or intent inference.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑032**  
Discourse‑context flags **SHALL** be deterministic, bounded, and reproducible for identical inputs, and **SHALL** comply with all structural‑integrity requirements in HLR‑20.40.010‑014 and HLR‑20.40.010‑015.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑033**  
The SOB layer **SHALL NOT** modify TP text, semantic_core, FFTM fields, or any meaning‑layer representation when propagating discourse‑context metadata.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑034**  
Discourse‑context flags **SHALL** be representable as addressable residue fragments suitable for routing by RB and refinement by SROB, STPX, and ISc.

---

## 3. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)

These requirements govern the geometric properties of residue fragments produced by SOB.

**HLR‑20.40.010‑025**  
The SOB layer SHALL ensure that each residue fragment $r_i$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i$ using the system’s approved hashing function.

Inline example:  
$h_i = hash(r_i)$

**HLR‑20.40.010‑026**  
The SOB layer SHALL ensure that the combined query address $q$ formed by the routing block satisfies:

$$
q = h_1 \oplus h_2 \oplus \dots \oplus h_n
$$

**HLR‑20.40.010‑027**  
The SOB layer SHALL ensure that residue fragments do not violate monotonicity, smoothness, or curvature‑invariance requirements defined in 20.40.

---

## **4. Outputs**

**HLR‑20.40.010‑028**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** output:

- a structured TP  
- a residue set  
- structural metadata  
- operator‑hint metadata  
- domain‑hint metadata  
- tone‑hint metadata  
- constraint‑hint metadata  

**HLR‑20.40.010‑029**  
The SOB layer **SHALL** guarantee that its output is valid input for SROB.
