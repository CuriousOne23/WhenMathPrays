# 20.105 TP Requirements (v0.6 — Architecture-Aligned)

**Document ID:** 20.105  
**Version:** 0.6  
**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Status:** Updated v0.6 — Architecture-Aligned (Pipeline A Only)

## Purpose

Defines deterministic **Thought Packet (TP)** structure, lifecycle, field authority, trajectory geometry persistence, and audit invariants for **lane‑local meaning‑construction projections** within **Pipeline A**.

**Normative definition (HLR‑20.105‑046):** TP is the **minimal deterministic state‑carrying artifact** exchanged across Pipeline A module boundaries — the per‑step, per‑lane working unit that records basin inputs/outputs, routing context, trajectory geometry, and **append‑only historical evidence** (`lineage_log[]`, `tp_audit_log[]`) without becoming a second authoritative meaning store.

TP projects MTP / `semantic_core` meaning into lane‑local form for OB, RB, TR, DCB, and Merge.  
**TB, IB, Truth/Done, and all Path‑B semantics are out of scope for Pipeline A and SHALL NOT appear on TP.**

Authoritative meaning commits only at Merge / `mtp_update`.

This module SHALL NOT weaken invariants in 20.12.
