# **20.105 TP Requirements**

This document defines the deterministic **Thought Packet (TP)** structure, lifecycle, field authority, trajectory geometry persistence, and audit invariants for lane‑local Pipeline‑A operations.

TP is the minimal deterministic state‑carrying artifact and primitive operand of Pipeline A. It serves as the per‑step, per‑lane working unit consumed and produced by each Pipeline‑A stage. It records basin inputs/outputs, routing context, trajectory geometry, and append‑only historical evidence (`lineage_log[]`, `tp_audit_log[]`) without functioning as a second authoritative meaning store.

Authoritative meaning commits at **Merge → TPU → semantic_core**.

TB, IB, Truth/Done, and all Path‑B semantics are out of scope for Pipeline A and SHALL not appear on TP.  
**HLR‑20.105‑001**

---

## **Evaluation‑Derived Metadata Fields**

TPs may contain two evaluation‑derived metadata fields:

- **TPTB** — Truth Metadata  
- **TPSF** — Safety Metadata  

These fields are produced exclusively by their respective evaluation stages and SHALL be read‑only for all downstream components.  
**HLR‑20.105‑002**

Lane‑local TPs SHALL NOT contain TPTB, TPSF, or any evaluation‑derived metadata prior to evaluation.  
**HLR‑20.105‑003**

---

## **Parent Scope**

This module normatively decomposes TP obligations from parent documents, including 20.10, 20.12, 20.30, and 20.31.

---

## **Non‑Goals**

- MTP authoritative aggregation  
- Detailed split/merge algorithms  
- DCB event semantics  
- TR derivation algorithms  
- Final truth/done authority (committed only in semantic_core post‑merge)  
- Pipeline‑B planning, expression, or IMR records on TP  

---

## **Dual‑Pipeline Boundary Discipline**

Pipeline A maintains strict separation from Path B.  
Pipeline B SHALL be TP‑stream read‑only and SHALL NOT invoke TPU or modify semantic_core.  
**HLR‑20.105‑004**

---

## **TP as Lane‑Local Projection**

TP SHALL represent the canonical per‑step meaning‑construction unit exchanged across deterministic Pipeline‑A boundaries.  
**HLR‑20.105‑005**

TP carries lane‑specific views of propositions, refs, ΔH%, suppression markers, routing context, and basin outputs.  
TP SHALL NOT duplicate authoritative semantic_core fields as a second source of truth.  
**HLR‑20.105‑006**

# **TP Field Model (Normative Minimum)**

## **Identity Block (immutable after creation)**

```json
tp_identity {
  tp_id,
  tp_seq,
  cycle_id,
  lane_id,
  parent_tp_id?,
  schema_version,
  profile_signature,
  wire_map_version,
  policy_signature,
  timestamp_evidence?
}
```

Identity block fields SHALL be immutable after TP creation.  
**HLR‑20.105‑007**

---

## **Lineage Log (append‑only)**

```json
lineage_log[] {
  lineage_seq,
  module_id,
  event_type,  // CREATE | SPLIT | MERGE | UPDATE | RETIRE
  parent_ref?,
  child_refs[],
  partition_rationale_ref?,
  merge_contribution_ref?
}
```

Lineage extensions SHALL append only.  
**HLR‑20.105‑008**

---

## **Working Projection Fields**

TP includes fields for:

- Thought Router (lane‑local)  
- Trajectory geometry  
- ΔH% lane fields  
- Basin outputs  
- Stability / overflow markers  
- Supervisory annotations (safe‑boundary only)  
- Intake text and repair proposals  
- Clarifying metadata hierarchy  
- Audit log  

---

## **Intake‑Bound Repair Fields**

IIInB produces repair proposals only.  
IE SHALL apply all proposed repairs deterministically when producing the final intake object.  
**HLR‑20.105‑009**

---

# **Meaning Signal Layer (MSL) and Clarifying Metadata**

TP SHALL carry a Meaning Signal Layer (MSL) containing short structured tokens for:

- qualifiers  
- clarifications  
- stance  
- shading  
- intent  
- direction  
- coherence  
- subculture  

**HLR‑20.105‑090**

Qualifiers and clarifications are the highest‑signal tokens for detecting intent and identity projection.

TP SHALL include clarifying metadata consisting of clarifying fields, subfields, and up to four levels of hierarchical sub‑subfields.  
This metadata SHALL be bounded:

- max 10 fields  
- max 100 total subfields  
- max 4 hierarchical levels  

**HLR‑20.105‑091**

Clarifying metadata SHALL record provenance and importance scores.  
**HLR‑20.105‑092**

MSL tokens and clarifying metadata SHALL be propagated deterministically through Path A (CEx → RB → IdOB → MCB).  
Propagation SHALL preserve ordering, provenance, and structural integrity.  
**HLR‑20.105‑093**

---

# **Metadata Immutability and Provenance**

All metadata fields SHALL be immutable once committed by TPU, except where a primitive is explicitly authorized to update a specific field.  
**HLR‑20.105‑010**

Each metadata field SHALL record its provenance.  
**HLR‑20.105‑011**

---

# **Safe Boundaries**

TP safe_boundary_marker and blocked_relation_log entries SHALL be written only at safe boundaries.  
**HLR‑20.105‑012**
