# 20.105 TP Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic Thought Packet (TP) structure, lifecycle, supervisory metadata, and audit invariants for per-step TS meaning-construction state.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes TP obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including deterministic identity, FIFO sequence integrity, bounded state, append-only audit behavior, and reproducible replay semantics.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.105-001: TP SHALL represent the canonical per-step meaning-construction unit exchanged across deterministic TS pipeline boundaries.
2. HLR-20.105-002: Every TP SHALL include stable identity fields including tp_id, tp_seq, timestamp metadata, lineage identifiers, and parent pointers.
3. HLR-20.105-003: TP tp_seq SHALL be monotonic within the configured deterministic sequence domain and SHALL preserve FIFO interpretation order.
4. HLR-20.105-004: TP identity and lineage fields SHALL be immutable after creation, except deterministic append-only lineage extension markers.
5. HLR-20.105-005: TP core fields SHALL include OB outputs, RB mappings, TB interpretations, delta-H-percent trajectory fields, and bounded stability metrics.
6. HLR-20.105-006: TP core-field updates SHALL be deterministic, schema-versioned, auditable, and reproducible for identical effective state.
7. HLR-20.105-007: TP supervisory fields SHALL include GB decision markers, safe-boundary markers, policy-enforcement markers, and blocked-relation logs.
8. HLR-20.105-008: TP blocked-relation logs SHALL record OBx class, OBy class, RBxy identifier, policy reason code, and timestamp/tp_seq evidence.
9. HLR-20.105-009: TP supervisory updates SHALL occur only through approved deterministic TS->GB->TS supervisory pathways at safe boundaries.
10. HLR-20.105-010: TP SHALL maintain append-only audit invariants; existing audit entries SHALL NOT be overwritten or deleted in-place.
11. HLR-20.105-011: TP audit records SHALL be deterministic, bounded, replayable, and serialized with canonical field ordering.
12. HLR-20.105-012: TP reason codes SHALL use immutable identifiers with deterministic versioned dictionary mapping behavior.
13. HLR-20.105-013: TP creation SHALL initialize all mandatory identity, core, and supervisory fields with deterministic defaults.
14. HLR-20.105-014: TP SHALL NOT permit nondeterministic mutation of previously finalized fields once a safe-boundary finalization marker is set.
15. HLR-20.105-015: TP promotion to COB-visible conversation-object semantics SHALL occur only through approved deterministic lifecycle pathways.
16. HLR-20.105-016: TP retention and compaction behavior SHALL be bounded by deterministic policy and replay-horizon mode semantics.
17. HLR-20.105-017: TP merge/split lineage markers SHALL preserve deterministic parent/child provenance continuity.
18. HLR-20.105-018: TP timestamps SHALL be treated as evidence metadata only; ordering authority SHALL derive from deterministic sequence tokens.
19. HLR-20.105-019: TP SHALL reject unsupported schema, enum, profile, or wire-map states with deterministic reject-with-audit behavior.
20. HLR-20.105-020: TP SHALL preserve platform-independent deterministic behavior under equivalent input, profile, and signature state.
21. HLR-20.105-021: TP processing SHALL remain bounded and SHALL NOT require unbounded scanning or inference to validate invariants.
22. HLR-20.105-022: TP zero-event windows SHALL follow deterministic empty-artifact semantics with canonical empty evidence or fixed no-artifact reason code.
23. HLR-20.105-023: TP export manifests SHALL use deterministic key ordering and canonical serialization.
24. HLR-20.105-024: TP lifecycle transitions (creation, active update, safe-boundary finalization, promotion visibility, retention, retirement) SHALL be logged in append-only audit records.
25. HLR-20.105-025: TP requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
26. HLR-20.105-026: TP invariants SHALL preserve determinism, reproducibility, boundedness, and auditability for all supported runtime profiles.

## Functional Boundaries
- TP owns packet-level identity, lineage, state, and supervisory/audit markers.
- MTP owns aggregated cross-packet state and long-range stability trajectory.
- GB supervisory decisions may annotate TP only through deterministic approved pathways.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic TP identity/lineage continuity across merge/split and replay.
- Deterministic FIFO-preserving tp_seq behavior under equivalent inputs.
- Deterministic append-only audit and blocked-relation evidence behavior.
- Deterministic safe-boundary finalization and reject-with-audit behavior.
