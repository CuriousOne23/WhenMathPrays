# 20.106 Directional Conversation Basin (DCB) Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.106  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-05  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Defines the Directional Conversation Basin (DCB) as a geometric meta-basin and bounded feedback element that observes TP trajectory geometry and emits directional-change events for TR consumption.

Trajectory geometry definitions are normative in this document; see `20.105_tp_requirements.md` for cross-reference only.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes DCB obligations from 20.10 architectural principles, 20.20 primitives, 20.30 functional model, and 20.37 TR specification.

## 1. Definition and Classification

1. HLR-20.106-001: DCB SHALL be classified as a geometric meta-basin element and SHALL NOT be classified as a semantic or stance basin.
2. HLR-20.106-002: DCB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction, semantic interpretation, or OB-class evidence extraction.
3. HLR-20.106-003: DCB SHALL NOT overlap OB authority boundaries defined in HLR-20.020-012.

## 2. Trajectory Geometry (Normative)

4. HLR-20.106-004: DCB SHALL depend on TP position as a geometric coordinate in trajectory space derived from persisted TP state.
5. HLR-20.106-005: DCB SHALL depend on TP direction vector as the geometric tangent of the TP trajectory at the observation step.
6. HLR-20.106-006: DCB SHALL depend on curvature as the rate of change of the TP direction vector along the trajectory.
7. HLR-20.106-007: Multi-step trajectory observation SHALL use consecutive persisted TP instances and MAY use MTP-held trajectory projection fields when required to relate steps across the conversation horizon.
8. HLR-20.106-008: Trajectory geometry definitions in this module SHALL remain implementation-agnostic and SHALL NOT prescribe numeric policy (see 20.95 for numeric bounds).

## 3. Role and Events

9. HLR-20.106-009: DCB SHALL observe the TP trajectory across steps within the deterministic TS cycle.
10. HLR-20.106-010: DCB SHALL emit directional-change events when curvature or directional deviation exceeds a bounded geometric invariant; the invariant bound SHALL be geometric and SHALL NOT be specified as numeric policy in this module.
11. HLR-20.106-011: Directional-change events SHALL be geometric signals only and SHALL NOT encode semantic labels, stance, or meaning categories.

## 4. Lifecycle and Interfaces

12. HLR-20.106-012: DCB SHALL execute after TP update and after OB processing, and before the TR routine for the same cycle.
13. HLR-20.106-013: DCB SHALL NOT write to TP fields, MTP authoritative semantic fields, or TP audit records.
14. HLR-20.106-014: DCB directional-change events SHALL be ephemeral TR inputs and SHALL NOT be persisted as TP fields.
15. HLR-20.106-015: RB SHALL NOT consume DCB directional-change events directly.
16. HLR-20.106-016: DCB directional-change events SHALL be consumed by the TR routine only on cycles where `tr_needs_update = true` and RB routes the TP to the TR routine.

## 5. Feedback and Determinism

17. HLR-20.106-017: DCB SHALL be a bounded feedback element because it uses prior trajectory direction to influence present routing through TR consumption.
18. HLR-20.106-018: DCB feedback SHALL be bounded, non-recursive, and non-expansive.
19. HLR-20.106-019: DCB SHALL NOT recursively modify its own geometric input state.
20. HLR-20.106-020: DCB SHALL NOT set `tr_needs_update`; trajectory-only change SHALL NOT by itself force TR execution.
21. HLR-20.106-021: DCB directional-change events SHALL be recomputable from persisted TP trajectory state (and permitted MTP trajectory projection fields) for deterministic replay.

## Functional Boundaries
- DCB owns geometric trajectory observation and directional-change event emission only.
- TR owns consumption of ephemeral DCB events when deriving semantic routing fields.
- OB owns semantic/stance local evidence extraction; DCB does not replace or extend OB semantics.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic DCB event emission for identical persisted trajectory state.
- Lifecycle ordering: post-OB, pre-TR, no TP writes.
- Ephemeral event contract and `tr_needs_update` gating alignment with 20.37.
- Replay recompute equivalence without persisting DCB events on TP.
