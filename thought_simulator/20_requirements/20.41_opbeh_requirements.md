# 20.41 OpBeh (Operational Behavior) Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.41  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Approved v0.1 — Stage 4 GATE (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok)

## Purpose

Defines **OpBeh** (Operational Behavior) — Pipeline B planning primitive identified by `opbeh_id`. OpBeh describes *how* realization behaves (structure, pacing, interaction pattern) at the execution layer.

Under multi-core Path A execution (20.115), OpBeh SHALL treat `core_id` and any meaning-layer orthogonality constraints as read-only gating inputs and SHALL NOT collapse distinctions established by orthogonal Path A instances.

**Namespace:** OpBeh ≠ Object Basin **OB** (HLR-20.012-015, 20.500 §1.5).

## Parent Scope

- Resolves `opbeh_id` from [20.56](20.56_routing_table_schema.md) rows into `exec_plan`
- Validated with OBG/XlateR as triple per HLR-20.012-008
- Writes planning refs only — not `semantic_core`

---

## 1. Registry Model

```text
opbeh_registry_entry {
  opbeh_id,
  display_name,
  behavior_class,
  allowed_obg_ids[],          // optional constraint hints
  allowed_xlater_ids[],
  policy_signature_scope,
  version,
  rationale_codes[]
}
```

1. **HLR-20.041-001:** Every `opbeh_id` in `exec_plan` SHALL resolve to exactly one registry entry in the active epoch package.
2. **HLR-20.041-002:** Unknown `opbeh_id` SHALL reject plan commit with `OPBEH_UNKNOWN`.
3. **HLR-20.041-003:** OpBeh registry SHALL be versioned per `routing_epoch_id` binding in 20.56 `registry_bindings`.

---

## 2. Planning Role

4. **HLR-20.041-004:** OpBeh selection SHALL be deterministic from `(routing_row, semantic_snapshot_ref, policy_signature)` — no seed influence on selection (HLR-20.012-010).
- Under multi-core Path A execution, OpBeh selection SHALL be deterministic per `core_id`; identical `(routing_row, semantic_snapshot_ref, policy_signature)` that differ only by `core_id` SHALL select the same `opbeh_id`.
6. **HLR-20.041-005:** OpBeh SHALL NOT read TP or `lane_id`.
7. **HLR-20.041-006:** OpBeh SHALL NOT write `semantic_core` or MTP fields.

### 2.1 Multi-Core Gating Constraints (New)
HLR-20.041-016: OpBeh SHALL respect multi-core Path A execution boundaries (20.115):
 
- OpBeh MAY read `core_id` and orthogonality-related gating fields from `semantic_snapshot_ref` as read-only inputs.
- OpBeh SHALL NOT reinterpret, modify, or override `core_id`.
- OpBeh SHALL NOT select behaviors that fuse or reconcile meaning-layer distinctions implied by orthogonal Path A instances.
- OpBeh SHALL NOT introduce cross-core coupling in behavior selection.


---

## 3. Triple Validation

7. **HLR-20.041-007:** OpBeh commit SHALL participate in `(opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id)` invalid-matrix check before `exec_plan` finalization (20.56 HLR-20.056-007).
8. **HLR-20.041-008:** Rejected triples SHALL log `opbeh_id` in `exec_trace` audit with `EXEC_TRIPLE_INVALID`.

### 3.1 Core-Local Triple Lineage (New)
HLR-20.041-017: When participating in the `(opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id)` triple, OpBeh SHALL preserve any `core_id` lineage present in the execution context:
- OpBeh SHALL NOT cause triples from different cores to be merged or conflated in audit or realization.
 - OpBeh-related audit records in `exec_trace` SHALL remain attributable to the originating `core_id` when present.


---

## 4. Expression Boundary

9. **HLR-20.041-009:** OpBeh defines planning intent only; surface text realization is [OuB](20.110_oub_requirements.md) / [20.58](20.58_oub_execution_manifold_integration.md).
10. **HLR-20.041-010:** OpBeh SHALL NOT alias basin pattern extraction semantics of OB (20.40).
