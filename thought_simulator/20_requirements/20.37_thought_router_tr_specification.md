# **20.37 — Thought Router (TR) Specification**  
**Document ID:** 20.37  
**Version:** 2.0 (Modernized Option‑C Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative — Path A Routing‑Vector Specification  
**Purpose:** Define TR meaning‑layer fields, lifecycle, producers, consumers, and deterministic routing behavior in Pipeline A.

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

The Thought Router (TR) is the **meaning‑layer routing vector** for a Thought Packet (TP) in Pipeline A.  
It is **not** a meaning‑layer primitive; it is a **field block** produced by the TR routine and consumed by RB.

TR:

- expresses stance, intent, affect, epistemic shading, tension, politeness, commitment, reservation, logical structure, epistemic delta‑H, lineage additions, and routing‑fields  
- is written **only** by the TR routine  
- is consumed **only** by RB (and read‑only by GB)  
- is committed into `SSR.thought_router_fields` by RBU during `mtp_update`  
- is never read or written by Pipeline B  
- is never read by IB, Merge, or Truth/Done  
- is recomputed only when `TP.tr_needs_update = true`  
- is deterministic, replay‑safe, and independent of seed, global mutable state, or TP allocation identity  

TR is the **canonical routing substrate** for RB in Path A.

---

# **1. Module Identity (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑001**  
TR SHALL be represented as `TP.TR` and SHALL be the canonical Path A routing‑vector block.

### **HLR‑20.037‑002**  
The TR routine SHALL be the exclusive writer of `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑003**  
Grammatical Structure primitives SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑052**  
TR SHALL operate exclusively in Pipeline A.

---

# **2. Canonical Locations (Normative)**

### **2.1 TP Snapshot**

Every TP SHALL contain:

```
TP.TR {
    stance,
    intent,
    affect,
    epistemic_shading,
    tension,
    politeness,
    commitment,
    reservation,
    logical_structure,
    epistemic_deltaH,
    lineage_additions,
    routing_fields
}
```

### **HLR‑20.037‑008**  
TR SHALL include all required fields listed above.

### **HLR‑20.037‑025**  
TR field ordering for serialization SHALL be deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.037‑053**  
Committed TR fields SHALL project into `SSR.thought_router_fields` at `mtp_update`.

### **2.2 Dirty‑Flag**

Every TP SHALL contain:

```
TP.tr_needs_update : boolean
```

### **HLR‑20.037‑037**  
TP SHALL include a boolean `tr_needs_update` indicating whether TR is stale.

### **HLR‑20.037‑038**  
Any meaning‑layer‑writer module that modifies TP meaning‑layer fields SHALL set `tr_needs_update = true`.

---

# **3. Producer Authority (Normative)**

### **3.1 TR Routine**

### **HLR‑20.037‑011**  
The TR routine SHALL derive TR only from explicit TP‑local inputs and permitted ephemeral DCB events.

### **HLR‑20.037‑006**  
TR writes SHALL be atomic within TR‑routine commit.

### **HLR‑20.037‑040**  
The TR routine SHALL clear `tr_needs_update = false` only after successful TR recompute and commit.

### **HLR‑20.037‑041**  
TR SHALL NOT execute when `tr_needs_update = false`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑048**  
TR routine inputs SHALL include permitted ephemeral DCB events and TP‑local inputs only.

### **HLR‑20.037‑063**  
TR SHALL NOT read `truth_hypotheses`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑055**  
TR SHALL NOT consume messy‑input tags for derivation.

### **HLR‑20.037‑060**  
TR derivation SHALL be reproducible from TP‑local inputs only; seed SHALL NOT influence outputs.

### **HLR‑20.037‑033**  
TR SHALL NOT include stochastic randomness state.

### **HLR‑20.037‑020**  
TR SHALL be independent of hidden mutable global state.

### **HLR‑20.037‑034**  
TR SHALL NOT be cross‑TP coupled except through explicit canonical TP fields.

---

# **4. TR‑Input Producers (Normative)**

### **4.1 Grammatical Structure Primitives**

### **HLR‑20.037‑010**  
Grammatical Structure primitives TR‑input fields SHALL be pre‑meaning‑layer only.

They write:

- syntactic shape  
- clause boundaries  
- negation markers  
- modality markers  
- pattern IDs  
- raw delta‑H  
- provenance  
- routing metadata  
- structural cues  

They may set `tr_needs_update = true`.

### **4.2 Messy‑Input Classifiers**

### **HLR‑20.037‑056**  
Modules that create or modify messy‑input tags SHALL set `tr_needs_update = true`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑055**  
TR SHALL NOT consume messy‑input tags.

### **4.3 DCB Geometric Overlay**

### **HLR‑20.037‑042**  
The TR routine MAY consume DCB directional‑change events as geometric hints.

### **HLR‑20.037‑043**  
DCB SHALL NOT populate `TP.TR` fields directly.

### **HLR‑20.037‑045**  
DCB SHALL NOT set `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑046**  
DCB events SHALL be consumed only when `tr_needs_update = true` and RB routes to TR.

### **HLR‑20.037‑047**  
DCB events SHALL be ephemeral and SHALL NOT be persisted.

---

# **5. TR → RB Relationship (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑004**  
RB SHALL consume `TP.TR` as a primary routing input.

### **HLR‑20.037‑005**  
RB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑039**  
RB SHALL route TP to the TR routine if and only if `tr_needs_update = true`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑019**  
RB routing SHALL remain a deterministic function of explicit inputs.

### **HLR‑20.037‑059**  
RB routing SHALL enforce bounded routing constraints.

### **HLR‑20.037‑030**  
TR SHALL remain compatible with RB bounded routing constraints.

---

# **6. TR Field Definitions (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑022**  
`epistemic_deltaH` SHALL be TR‑routine interpreted and auditable.

### **HLR‑20.037‑026**  
TR numeric subfields SHALL use canonical Q32.32.

### **HLR‑20.037‑064**  
`routing_fields` ordering SHALL be deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.037‑054**  
TR SHALL NOT encode execution‑layer hints.

### **HLR‑20.037‑023**  
`routing_fields` SHALL be explicit and consumable by RB without latent interpretation.

---

# **7. TR Lifecycle (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑027**  
TR lifecycle transitions SHALL be visible in deterministic logs.

### **HLR‑20.037‑012**  
TR SHALL be logged in deterministic replay artifacts.

### **HLR‑20.037‑061**  
Strip replay SHALL preserve `thought_router_fields`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑013**  
TR SHALL remain immutable after TR‑routine write until merge/retire.

### **HLR‑20.037‑035**  
TR conformance failures SHALL be treated as routing conformance failures.

---

# **8. Module Boundaries (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑014**  
IB SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑015**  
Merge SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑016**  
Truth/Done SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑062**  
GB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` or `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.037‑066**  
Pipeline B modules SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` or clear `tr_needs_update`.

---

# **9. Safety & Determinism (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.037‑007**  
TR fields SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical effective inputs.

### **HLR‑20.037‑028**  
TR consumers SHALL not infer write authority from read access.

### **HLR‑20.037‑029**  
TR SHALL support deterministic split/merge workflows.

### **HLR‑20.037‑065**  
TR derivation SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order or allocation identity.

---

# **10. Conformance (Normative)**

A system conforms when:

- `TP.TR` exists and is complete  
- `TP.tr_needs_update` exists  
- TR is written only by the TR routine  
- TR is consumed only by RB and GB  
- TR is deterministic, replay‑safe, and seed‑independent  
- TR respects all authority boundaries  
- TR respects all routing bounds  
- TR commits into SSR with canonical ordering  
- TR lifecycle is logged deterministically
