# 20.40 OB Requirements  
**Document ID:** 20.40  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** 2026‑06‑21  
**Status:** Parent‑Level Requirements for OB Primitives

---

# Purpose (Informative)

The Object Basin (**OB**) refers to the **generic class of Path A primitives** that operate on the manifold to:

- classify TP structure  
- refine or normalize structure  
- extract constraints  
- extract pre‑semantic cues  
- emit residue fragments  
- contribute to TR‑input formation  

OB is **not** a single primitive and **not** a semantic or routing module.  
OB primitives operate strictly within Path A and act only on the manifold representation.

Identity‑specific processing is **not part of OB**.  
Identity‑conditioned interpretation is defined separately in:

**→ 20.40.050_idob_prim.md (Identity OB Primitive Requirements)**

This document defines:

- parent‑level OB invariants  
- cross‑primitive rules  
- residue invariants  
- XOR and geometric invariants  
- global OB responsibilities  
- legacy HLR preservation  

No SHALL statements appear in informative sections.

---

# OB Terminology (Informative)

In all TS documents:

- “**OB**” refers to the **generic class of Object Basin primitives** in Path A.  
- OB primitives operate on the manifold and produce structural, constraint, and pre‑semantic residue.  
- OB does **not** include the Identity OB primitive.  
- Any document referring to “OB” without specifying a particular primitive SHALL be interpreted as referring to the **OB class as a whole**.

Identity OB is a **separate Path A primitive** and is defined exclusively in 20.40.050.

---

# Parent‑Level Architecture (Informative)

OB primitives produce:

- structured TP views  
- residue fragments  
- metadata fragments  
- ΔH% contributions  
- TR‑input cues  

Residue fragments must satisfy:

- addressability  
- XOR composability  
- geometric smoothness  
- monotonicity  
- curvature invariance  

OB primitives do **not** perform:

- semantic interpretation  
- stance assignment  
- truth evaluation  
- routing arbitration

## 1.  OB Boundary Discipline (Informative)

| Concern | OB posture |
|---------|------------|
| `semantic_core` | Writes lane-local evidence and TR-input projections; commits via Merge/`mtp_update` — not direct envelope bypass |
| `TP.TR`, `thought_router_fields` | **No write**; may set `tr_needs_update = true` |
| `exec_plan`, `exec_trace`, `supervisory` | **No read/write** |
| `routing_epoch_id` | **No read/write** |
| `truth_hypotheses` | **No read, no write**; truth evaluation is downstream (TB / 20.140) |
| `messy_input_record` | MAY append/classify at safe boundaries; **no resolve/smooth/clear** of contradiction or underspec |
| Seed | **SHALL NOT influence** OB activation or evidence outputs (HLR-20.012-010) |

### 1.1 Multi-Core OB Instance Model (New)
HLR-20.040-048: OB SHALL support **multi-instance execution** across CPU cores during multi-core Path A operation (20.115).

HLR-20.040-049: Each OB instance SHALL:
- operate independently,
- maintain no shared mutable state with other OB instances,
- bind to a single core_id,
- bind to a single orthogonality_signature.

HLR-20.040-050: OB instances SHALL NOT communicate, coordinate, or exchange intermediate evidence. All cross-core aggregation occurs only at MTP (20.115).

# Normative Requirements (HLRs)

All SHALL statements below correspond to the HLRs listed in the header.  
All legacy HLRs (001–051) are preserved.  
No HLR in this document refers to Identity OB.

## Normative Requirements

1. HLR-20.040-001: OB SHALL process lane-local TP views only.
2. HLR-20.040-002: OB activation SHALL be determined by deterministic routing, lane projection, and firing-order rules for identical TS state and inputs.
3. HLR-20.040-003: OB SHALL NOT perform merge or split arbitration.
4. HLR-20.040-004: OB execution ordering SHALL be deterministic under identical scheduler inputs.
5. HLR-20.040-005: OB SHALL emit structured local evidence fields and bounded ΔH% contribution records.
6. HLR-20.040-006: OB SHALL preserve ΔH% contribution and missing-mass accounting compatibility with downstream merge semantics.
7. HLR-20.040-007: OB SHALL treat semantically equivalent noisy surface forms as concept-equivalent for activation and evidence extraction.
8. HLR-20.040-008: OB SHALL NOT perform probabilistic inference, stochastic correction, or hallucinated content insertion.
9. HLR-20.040-009: OB outputs SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical lane input, MTP context, and `policy_signature`; seed SHALL NOT influence OB outputs.
10. HLR-20.040-010: OB SHALL expose deterministic test interfaces with explicit inputs, expected outputs, invariants, and failure modes.
11. HLR-20.040-011: OB SHALL expose test hooks sufficient for unit, integration, system, and property-based verification.
12. HLR-20.040-012: OB lifecycle transitions (entry, running, completion/exit) SHALL be explicitly logged and observable.
13. HLR-20.040-013: OB SHALL expose observability data required by MB pathways, including activation context, lane identifiers, evidence payloads, and ΔH% attribution.
14. HLR-20.040-014: OB telemetry SHALL support deterministic diagnostic consumption by GB, OuB, and IB without mutating OB core semantics.
15. HLR-20.040-015: OB SHALL define measurable TCU budgets (min/typ/max) and per-tick TCU reporting fields.
16. HLR-20.040-016: OB overload/degradation behavior SHALL be deterministic, policy-bounded, and auditable.
17. HLR-20.040-017: OB behavior SHALL be platform-independent and SHALL NOT depend on hardware-, OS-, or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
18. HLR-20.040-018: OB contracts SHALL prioritize top-down thought-processing semantics over implementation-specific mechanics.
19. HLR-20.040-019: OB interfaces and internal state representation SHALL remain modifiable, debuggable, and architecturally transparent.
20. HLR-20.040-020: OB documentation artifacts (contracts, invariants, and diagnostics fields) SHALL remain complete and version-aligned with TS architecture updates.
21. HLR-20.040-021: OB SHALL accept a deterministic lane-local TP input containing visible semantic fields, suppressed semantic fields, ΔH% potential, lane identifier, routing context, lineage/provenance fields, and MTP snapshot fields required for local pattern extraction. (OB reads semantic fields only as structural cues; OB does not perform semantic interpretation.)
22. HLR-20.040-022: OB SHALL emit a deterministic output object containing structured evidence fields, TR-input structural cues, ΔH% contribution, ΔH% missing-mass record, updated lineage/provenance fields, OB activation metadata, OB execution diagnostics, and observability/telemetry fields required by MB, GB, OuB, and IB.
23. HLR-20.040-023: OB SHALL function as a deterministic language-pattern detector, identifying pattern-family signatures in lane-local TP views and emitting structured evidence without performing semantic interpretation, inference, or correction.
24. HLR-20.040-024: OB SHALL enforce applicable global boundedness constraints defined in 20.30 section 8, including OB activation bounds, evidence payload bounds, and per-basin TCU envelopes.
25. HLR-20.040-025: When a global bound condition affects OB execution, OB SHALL emit deterministic overflow/degradation tags and stability telemetry conforming to the canonical overflow field schema in 20.30 section 8.3.
26. HLR-20.040-026: OB degradation behavior under global-bound exceedance SHALL follow deterministic, policy-bounded actions and SHALL NOT silently drop evidence fields without explicit tagging and logging.
27. HLR-20.040-027: OB SHALL NOT be a geometric meta‑basin and SHALL NOT perform semantic or stance evaluation.
28. Cross-Core Isolation (New)
HLR-20.040-051: OB SHALL NOT:
- read evidence produced by OB instances on other cores,
- incorporate cross-core TP views,
- perform cross-core normalization, fusion, reconciliation, or weighting.

OB execution is strictly **core-local**. All multi-core aggregation is performed exclusively by MTP using whole Path A artifacts (20.115).
29. HLR-20.040-028: DCB is a geometric meta-basin that MAY influence OB family selection through TR routing hints but is not an OB and SHALL NOT perform OB semantic extraction.
30. HLR-20.040-029: OB SHALL operate exclusively in Pipeline A; SHALL NOT read `exec_plan` or `exec_trace` (HLR-20.038-021).
31. HLR-20.040-030: OB SHALL write TR-input fields to `TP.input_fields` and SHALL set `tr_needs_update = true` when semantic-relevant outputs change; OB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` (20.37).
32. HLR-20.040-031: OB SHALL NOT read `TP.TR` for semantic derivation; optional read for multi-TP slot bookkeeping only (20.37 §2.2).
33. HLR-20.040-032: OB SHALL NOT write, read for derivation, or encode `routing_epoch_id`; SHALL NOT read or write `truth_hypotheses` (HLR-20.040-041).
34. HLR-20.040-033: OB messy-input classifiers SHALL follow 20.17 detector order and canonical export sort; OB SHALL NOT smooth contradiction, fill underspec slots, or clear messy-input tags.
35. HLR-20.040-034: OB ΔH% contribution and missing-mass fields SHALL use canonical Q32.32 per 20.95; comparisons and replay export SHALL use canonical integer form.
36. HLR-20.040-035: OB evidence and TR-input arrays SHALL use deterministic canonical ordering before export, hash, and replay diff per 20.95/20.39.
37. HLR-20.040-036: Strip replay removing Pipeline B envelopes SHALL preserve OB-committed evidence in `semantic_core` for Pipeline A-only equivalence (20.36 Class 1).
38. HLR-20.040-037: GB and OuB MAY consume OB telemetry read‑only; post‑TS consumers MAY read OB
telemetry but SHALL NOT mutate OB outputs or TP fields.
39. HLR-20.040-038: Under `overflow_flag`, OB SHALL preserve `conflict_flag` and `underspec_flag` primary markers before optional field pruning (20.17-031).
40. HLR-20.040-039: OB SHALL NOT perform merge/split, routing fan-out decisions, or inquiry instantiation — those remain RB/GB responsibilities.
41. HLR-20.040-040: OB output derivation SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order, allocation sequence, or container address identity; only explicit canonical lane inputs and stable IDs MAY influence outputs.
42. HLR-20.040-041: OB SHALL NOT read `truth_hypotheses` from MTP or `semantic_core` for pattern extraction or TR-input derivation; truth evaluation consumes OB evidence downstream via TB (20.140).
43. HLR-20.040-042: `activation_metadata{}` nested keys and ID arrays SHALL use deterministic canonical ordering before export, hash, and replay diff — lexicographic key sort; stable-ID array sort; numeric weights canonical Q32.32.
44. HLR-20.040-043: OB SHALL read only the approved MTP/`semantic_core` field set enumerated in §2.1; reads outside that set SHALL be treated as conformance failures. 
45. HLR-20.040-044: OB SHALL NOT read `truth_hypotheses`, `done_state`, `TP.TR`, or any execution/supervisory envelope fields.

### OB Orthogonality Signature
HLR-20.040-045: In multi-core Path A execution (20.115), OB SHALL expose a deterministic
**orthogonality_signature** describing its information domain:

- pattern-family domain
- probe/feature family domain
- structural cue domain

HLR-20.040-046: OB orthogonality_signature SHALL be:
- machine-checkable,
- stable across replay,
- sufficient for the scheduler to guarantee that OBs assigned to different cores do not overlap in information domain.

HLR-20.040-047: OB SHALL NOT emit overlapping orthogonality_signatures unless explicitly permitted by policy.
