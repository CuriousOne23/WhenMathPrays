# 20.30.500.010 — TS Operational Semantics (Semantic Table)

<details>
<summary>HLR‑20.30.010‑001 → 007</summary>

| HLR # | SHALL Text | Semantic Summary |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| 001 | Pipeline A SHALL execute the TS cycle in the canonical deterministic order defined in §7. | Cycle ordering is fixed and deterministic; Pipeline A must follow the exact sequence. |
| 002 | Lane creation, execution, and merge SHALL be deterministic and SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic expansion. | Lane semantics must be deterministic; no nondeterministic branching or expansion. |
| 003 | TPs SHALL be ephemeral lane‑local objects; MTP SHALL be the sole global semantic state. | TP is per‑lane/per‑cycle only; MTP is the only persistent semantic state. |
| 004 | Pipeline A SHALL guarantee replay equivalence for identical (input, MTP state) tuples. | Replay determinism: identical input + MTP → identical meaning. |
| 005 | `mtp_update` SHALL freeze semantic_core and SHALL be the sole handoff point to Pipeline B. | A/B boundary is fixed; only `mtp_update` commits meaning to B. |
| 006 | Pipeline A SHALL write meaning; Pipeline B SHALL express meaning without modifying it. | Strict separation: A constructs meaning; B expresses it but cannot change it. |
| 007 | Pipeline A SHALL detect, tag, and degrade deterministically under overflow conditions. | Overflow handling must be deterministic with tagging + bounded degradation. |
