# 20.70 MB Requirements

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.070-001: MB SHALL remain non-intrusive to core cognition by default.
2. HLR-20.070-002: MB SHALL support deterministic diagnostics and drift observation.
3. HLR-20.070-003: Any MB-triggered what-if split SHALL be explicitly flagged and logged.
4. HLR-20.070-004: MB controls SHALL be bounded and optional.
