# 20.43 XlateR (Translation Routine) Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.43  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** Approved v0.1 — Stage 4 GATE (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok)

## Purpose

Defines **XlateR** (Translation Routine) — Pipeline B mapping primitive identified by `xlater_id`. XlateR maps committed meaning structures into OuB-ready expression plans (not final surface text).

Under multi-core Path A execution (20.115), XlateR SHALL treat `core_id` and `orthogonality_signature` as read-only gating constraints and SHALL NOT collapse meaning-layer distinctions established by orthogonal Path A instances.

**Namespace:** XlateR ≠ Thought Router **TR** (HLR-20.012-015).

## Parent Scope

- Completes exec triple with [20.41](20.41_opbeh_requirements.md) / [20.42](20.42_obg_requirements.md)
- `exec_plan` field `xlater_id` per 20.39 sketch

---

## 1. Registry Model

```text
xlater_registry_entry {
  xlater_id,
  mapping_profile_id,
  supported_discourse_acts[],
  structural_template_refs[],
  policy_signature_scope,
  version,
  rationale_codes[]
}
```

1. **HLR-20.043-001:** Every `xlater_id` in `exec_plan` SHALL resolve to a XlateR registry entry for the active epoch.
2. **HLR-20.043-002:** Unknown `xlater_id` SHALL reject with `XLATER_UNKNOWN`.
3. **HLR-20.043-003:** XlateR templates SHALL NOT embed meaning-layer proposition edits — mapping/layout only.

---

## 2. Mapping Semantics

4. **HLR-20.043-004:** XlateR SHALL read frozen `semantic_core` fields explicitly listed in mapping profile — never TP.
5. **HLR-20.043-005:** XlateR SHALL preserve `conflict_flag` / `underspec_flag` visibility per 20.17 — no smoothing into single-voice presentation unless TB/Truth-Done authorized fields explicitly allow scoped presentation.
6. **HLR-20.043-006:** XlateR output SHALL be `expression_plan_ref` attached to `exec_plan` for OuB consumption (20.58).

### 2.1 Core-Local Mapping Constraints (New)
HLR-20.043-015: XlateR SHALL respect multi-core Path A execution boundaries:
- XlateR MAY read `core_id` and `orthogonality_signature` from the frozen `semantic_core` as read-only gating inputs.
- XlateR SHALL NOT reinterpret, modify, or override `core_id`.
- XlateR SHALL NOT fuse, reconcile, or smooth meaning-layer distinctions implied by orthogonal Path A instances.
- XlateR SHALL NOT select templates or mapping profiles that collapse multi-core meaning-layer divergence.


---

## 3. Determinism and Validation

7. **HLR-20.043-007:** XlateR mapping SHALL be deterministic for identical inputs and triple; seed SHALL NOT alter mapping profile selection.
- Under multi-core Path A execution, XlateR mapping SHALL be deterministic per `core_id`; identical mapping inputs that differ only by `core_id` SHALL select the same `xlater_id` and mapping profile.
8. **HLR-20.043-008:** XlateR SHALL participate in invalid-triple matrix validation before plan commit.
9. **HLR-20.043-009:** XlateR SHALL NOT write `semantic_core`.

---

## Verification

10. **HLR-20.043-010:** E2 regeneration reproduces equivalent `xlater_id` and plan body hash.
11. **HLR-20.043-011:** Negative: `semantic_tags[]` carrying `xlater_id` hints in meaning layer → 20.31-021 violation.
