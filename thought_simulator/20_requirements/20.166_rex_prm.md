# **REx (Realization Extractor Primitive) — 20.166**

**Purpose:**  
The Realization Extractor (REx) converts the committed meaning produced by LI into a structured realization substrate suitable for downstream planning and production. REx does not interpret meaning; it transforms meaning into realization‑ready components.  
**HLR‑20.166‑001:** REx SHALL consume the meaning‑layer fields produced by LI, including `tp.meaning`, `tp.mli_status`, `tp.mli_confidence`, `tp.mli_conflict`, and `tp.mli_reason_code`.

**Focus:**  
REx reads meaning‑layer fields, continuity_fields from CoHI, and A‑side metadata (`tp.truth_tags`, `tp.safety_flags`) to construct a realization substrate (`tp.rsub`).  
**HLR‑20.166‑002:** REx SHALL generate a realization substrate containing clause‑level structure, discourse roles, referential anchors, polarity, and realization‑relevant dependencies.  
**HLR‑20.166‑003:** REx SHALL incorporate continuity_fields (structural_history, referential_history, conversation_objects, topic_threads, continuity_markers) into the realization substrate.  
**HLR‑20.166‑004:** REx SHALL write the realization substrate into `tp.rsub` without modifying meaning‑layer fields.

**When:**  
REx runs after LI has committed local meaning and before RPlan performs realization planning. It executes once per turn in Path B.  
**HLR‑20.166‑005:** REx SHALL execute immediately after LI and before RPlan on every Path B turn.

**Why:**  
REx provides a deterministic bridge between meaning and realization by extracting the structural, referential, and discourse information required for planning and production. This ensures that RPlan and RPU operate on a stable, continuity‑aware realization substrate.

**Is not:**  
REx is not a meaning extractor, a planner, a scorer, a router, a semantic interpreter, a memory system, or a surface realization module. REx does not modify COB, does not generate expression, and does not perform global inference.

**Example:**  
If LI commits meaning indicating a request to “summarize the dataset,” and CoHI provides a conversation_object representing the dataset referenced earlier, REx incorporates this referent, assigns discourse roles, determines clause polarity, and constructs a realization substrate that RPlan can use to generate a coherent response.

**Normative home:** 20.166.
