# **20.166 — Realization Extractor (REx‑prm)**  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Primitive Class:** Path B — Realization Layer  
**Purpose:** Convert a *validated* Thought Packet (TP) into a **Realization Extract (REx)**: a structured, minimal, lossless representation of what the system must realize in the next step of reasoning or generation.

---

## **1. Overview**
The Realization Extractor (REx‑prm) is the first primitive in the Path B pipeline.  
It consumes a **validated TP** (post‑MTP, post‑CEX, post‑TE) and produces a **Realization Extract (REx)** — a normalized, canonical, machine‑actionable representation of:

- What must be realized  
- Why it must be realized  
- Under what constraints  
- With what invariants  
- With what dependencies  

REx is the *semantic substrate* for RPlan, RPU, and ReB.

---

## **2. Inputs and Outputs**

### **2.1 Inputs**
REx‑prm SHALL read the following TP fields (no others):

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `TP.message` | The semantic content to be realized |
| `TP.intent` | The user/system intent classification |
| `TP.topic` | Topic classification |
| `TP.tone_hint` | Tone or style constraints |
| `TP.constraints` | Hard constraints (length, safety, format) |
| `TP.audience` | Target audience |
| `TP.channel_hint` | Output channel (chat, email, code, etc.) |
| `TP.invariants` | Invariants from TE‑Split/Merge |
| `TP.trace` | Provenance chain |

### **2.2 Outputs**
REx‑prm SHALL produce a **Realization Extract (REx)** with the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `REx.core_meaning` | Canonical semantic representation of TP.message |
| `REx.intent` | Carried from TP.intent |
| `REx.constraints` | Normalized constraints |
| `REx.invariants` | Invariants preserved verbatim |
| `REx.audience` | Carried from TP.audience |
| `REx.channel` | Carried from TP.channel_hint |
| `REx.dependencies` | Any semantic dependencies extracted |
| `REx.ambiguities` | Any unresolved ambiguities requiring RPU or ReB |
| `REx.trace` | Extended provenance chain |

## **3. High‑Level Requirements (HLRs)**  
All SHALL statements are bound to HLR‑20.166-xxx.

---

### **HLR‑20.166-001 — Canonical Meaning Extraction**  
REx‑prm SHALL extract a **canonical, lossless semantic representation** of `TP.message` and store it in `REx.core_meaning`.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166.002 — The extractor SHALL remove surface‑form artifacts (phrasing, ordering, filler) while preserving meaning.  
- LLR‑20.166.003 — The extractor SHALL not introduce new meaning.  
- LLR‑20.166.004 — The extractor SHALL represent meaning in a normalized internal structure (not user‑visible).  

**Example:**  
TP.message: “Can you maybe write a short note thanking the team?”  
REx.core_meaning: *request: generate_thank_you_note(target=team, length=short)*

---

### **HLR‑20.166-005 — Constraint Normalization**  
REx‑prm SHALL normalize all constraints from `TP.constraints` into a machine‑actionable structure.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-006 — Length constraints SHALL be converted to numeric or symbolic bounds.  
- LLR‑20.166-007 — Safety constraints SHALL be preserved verbatim.  
- LLR‑20.166-008 — Format constraints SHALL be converted to structured tags (e.g., `format=email`).  

**Example:**  
TP.constraints: “keep it brief, like 3–4 sentences”  
REx.constraints.length: `{min_sentences: 3, max_sentences: 4}`

---

### **HLR‑20.166-009 — Invariant Preservation**  
REx‑prm SHALL preserve all invariants from `TP.invariants` without modification.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-010 — Invariants SHALL be copied verbatim.  
- LLR‑20.166-011 — Invariants SHALL be marked as non‑overridable.  

---

### **HLR‑20.166-012 — Dependency Extraction**  
REx‑prm SHALL identify and extract semantic dependencies required for realization.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-014 — Dependencies may include entities, facts, relationships, or required external knowledge.  
- LLR‑20.166-015 — Dependencies SHALL be represented as structured references.  

**Example:**  
TP.message: “Write a summary of the last meeting.”  
REx.dependencies: `{requires: meeting_notes}`

---

### **HLR‑20.166-016 — Ambiguity Detection**  
REx‑prm SHALL detect unresolved ambiguities and record them in `REx.ambiguities`.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-017 — Ambiguities SHALL be classified as lexical, structural, or intent‑level.  
- LLR‑20.166-018 — Ambiguities SHALL not be resolved by REx‑prm; they are deferred to RPU or ReB.  

---

### **HLR‑20.166-019 — Audience and Channel Propagation**  
REx‑prm SHALL propagate `TP.audience` and `TP.channel_hint` into `REx.audience` and `REx.channel`.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-020 — If `TP.channel_hint` is missing, REx‑prm SHALL set `REx.channel = default`.  
- LLR‑20.166-021 — Audience SHALL be preserved verbatim.  

---

### **HLR‑20.166-022 — Provenance Extension**  
REx‑prm SHALL extend the provenance chain (`TP.trace`) into `REx.trace`.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-023 — The extension SHALL include a timestamped entry for REx‑prm.  
- LLR‑20.166-024 — The extension SHALL include the version of this primitive.  

---

### **HLR‑20.166-025 — No Realization or Planning**  
REx‑prm SHALL NOT perform realization, planning, rewriting, or generation.

**LLRs:**  
- LLR‑20.166-026 — REx‑prm SHALL not modify tone, style, or content.  
- LLR‑20.166-027 — REx‑prm SHALL not generate text intended for user output.  
- LLR‑20.166.028 — REx‑prm SHALL not resolve ambiguities.  

---

## **4. REx Structure Specification**

```yaml
REx:
  core_meaning: <canonical semantic representation>
  intent: <from TP.intent>
  constraints: <normalized constraints>
  invariants: <verbatim from TP.invariants>
  audience: <from TP.audience>
  channel: <from TP.channel_hint or default>
  dependencies: <list>
  ambiguities: <list>
  trace: <extended provenance>
```

---

## **5. Invariants**

- INV‑20.166-029 — REx SHALL be lossless with respect to TP.message.  
- INV‑20.166-030 — REx SHALL not contain user‑visible text.  
- INV‑20.166-031 — REx SHALL be deterministic for identical inputs.

## **6. Example End‑to‑End**

### **Input TP**
```yaml
TP.message: "Write a friendly reminder email to the team about tomorrow's meeting."
TP.intent: "reminder"
TP.topic: "workplace"
TP.tone_hint: "friendly"
TP.constraints: "email format, short"
TP.audience: "internal team"
TP.channel_hint: "email"
TP.invariants: ["must mention time: 10am"]
TP.trace: [...]
```

### **Output REx**
```yaml
REx.core_meaning:
  action: "compose_reminder"
  target: "team"
  event: "meeting"
  time: "tomorrow 10am"

REx.intent: "reminder"
REx.constraints:
  format: "email"
  length: "short"

REx.invariants:
  - "must mention time: 10am"

REx.audience: "internal team"
REx.channel: "email"

REx.dependencies: []
REx.ambiguities: []

REx.trace:
  - ...previous...
  - {primitive: REx-prm, version: 0.1, timestamp: ...}
```
