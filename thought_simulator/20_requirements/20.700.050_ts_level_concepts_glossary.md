## Entries

### ΔH% (DeltaHPercent) — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the incremental structural-uncertainty contribution produced by each Path-A interpretive stage.  
**Focus:** Encode local uncertainty adjustments from structural gaps, unresolved branches, missing-mass indicators, and interpretive signals.  
**When:** Generated at each Path-A interpretive step and accumulated before truth/completion evaluation.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, non-probabilistic mechanism for tracking uncertainty evolution.  
**Is not:** Probability; confidence score; truth commitment; entropy functional; Path-B computation.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.030, 20.30.060.

### H% (UncertaintyPercent) — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the accumulated structural-uncertainty state of the TP/MTP after integrating ΔH% contributions.  
**Focus:** Encode the current structural-uncertainty level derived from unresolved branches, structural gaps, and interpretive signals.  
**When:** Updated during Path-A after merge and truth-hypotheses evaluation.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay-safe uncertainty measure that cleanly separates structural uncertainty from truth evaluation.  
**Is not:** Probability; confidence score; truth band; global uncertainty model; Path-B computation.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.060, 20.30.070.

### OB (Object Basin) — 20.40
**Purpose:** Defines the conceptual category for Path-A object-basin primitives.  
**Focus:** Refers collectively to the Path-A object-basin primitives (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB). IdOB is a separate identity-conditioned basin.  
**When:** Invoked during Path-A meaning construction prior to routing.  
**Why:** Provides deterministic, replay-safe structural interpretation that grounds routing, ΔH% evolution, and downstream meaning construction.  
**Is not:** A semantic basin; a truth evaluator; a routing arbiter; a geometric meta-basin; a single primitive.  
**Normative home:** 20.40 (parent-level OB requirements).

### Random Seed Boundary — 20.12, 20.207
**Purpose:** Isolate all nondeterminism to Path-B expression so Path-A meaning remains fully deterministic and replayable.  
**Focus:** Seed-bounded variation in RG; deterministic meaning-layer replay under fixed TP(t).  
**When:** Applied at RG and OuBB under seed_scope during Path-B realization.  
**Why:** Enforce replay invariants: identical TP(t)+seed → identical surface form.  
**Is not:** An A-cycle planner; OpBeh identity selector; meaning-layer contributor.  
**Example:** Changing the seed alters surface text but leaves semantic_core and TP(t) unchanged.  
**Normative home:** 20.12, 20.207.

### Four-Path Model (TS Processing Paths)
**Purpose:** Prevent architectural drift by enforcing four distinct, non-overlapping TS processing trajectories.  
**Focus:** Normal Path, Clarification Path, Inference Path, Discovery Path.  
**When:** Path chosen at InB using TB/IB ambiguity state and governance signals.  
**Why:** Ensure deterministic routing, prevent stage confusion, and isolate clarification, inference, and long-term analysis behaviors.  
**Normative home:** Architecture discussion.

### Single-Writer Invariant
**Purpose:** Enforce that only TPU may modify the Thought Packet (TP) during Path A.  
**Focus:** Strict write authority boundary.  
**Why:** Maintain determinism, auditability, and A/B separation.  
**Normative home:** 20.105 (TPU).

### A/B Separation Invariant
**Purpose:** Ensure Path A constructs meaning and Path B only realizes it without feedback into meaning.  
**Focus:** Strict read-only boundary for Path B on committed meaning.  
**Why:** Prevent meaning corruption and ensure replay-safety.  
**Normative home:** 20.705.
