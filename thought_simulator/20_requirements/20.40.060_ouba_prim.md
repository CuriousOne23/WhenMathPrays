## 1. Purpose

OuBA is the final commit primitive of Path A.  
It produces the immutable semantic snapshot that becomes the authoritative meaning state for governance, downstream DF intake, truth evaluation, safety enforcement, and Path‑B realization.  
OuBA is the last TP‑writing primitive in the system.

---

## 2. Scope

OuBA operates only on fully stabilized meaning state received from IdOB.  
It finalizes and freezes semantic_core, attaches provenance and lineage, and emits a Committed Thought Packet (CTP) for governance and Path B.  
OuBA is a commit boundary, not a meaning constructor, and does not perform inference, routing, or OB‑family recomputation.

---

## 3. Interfaces

### 3.1 Inputs

- `IdOB_Output` — stabilized meaning state, including:
  - semantic_core
  - referents and bindings
  - constraints and residue
  - OB lineage and routing metadata

### 3.2 Outputs

- `CTP` — Committed Thought Packet:
  - immutable representation of semantic_core and associated structures
  - governance‑ready and replayable
  - serialization‑ready

---

## 4. Normative requirements (HLRs)

**HLR-20.40.060-001:** OuBA SHALL accept as input only meaning states that have passed through IdOB in the Path‑A pipeline.  

**HLR-20.40.060-002:** OuBA SHALL produce exactly one CTP for each accepted IdOB output.  

**HLR-20.40.060-003:** OuBA SHALL freeze semantic_core such that no further primitive can modify its content after CTP emission.  

**HLR-20.40.060-004:** OuBA SHALL preserve all referents, bindings, and constraints present in the IdOB output without addition, removal, or reinterpretation.  

**HLR-20.40.060-005:** OuBA SHALL not introduce any new semantic content, referents, or constraints that were not present in the IdOB output.  

**HLR-20.40.060-006:** OuBA SHALL not perform any semantic inference, scoring, routing, or candidate generation.  

**HLR-20.40.060-007:** OuBA SHALL operate deterministically such that identical IdOB inputs yield identical CTP outputs.  

**HLR-20.40.060-008:** OuBA SHALL not invoke any OB layer (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB), SSG, TR, RB, or IdOB during its operation.  

**HLR-20.40.060-009:** OuBA SHALL not read or depend on global state beyond what is required to attach provenance and lineage metadata.  

**HLR-20.40.060-010:** OuBA SHALL attach complete OB lineage to the CTP, including identifiers for SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, IdOB, and routing decisions where available.  

**HLR-20.40.060-011:** OuBA SHALL attach a commit_timestamp and commit_hash to each CTP sufficient to support replay and audit.  

**HLR-20.40.060-012:** OuBA SHALL record the ruleset identifiers used along the Path‑A pipeline in the CTP metadata.  

**HLR-20.40.060-013:** OuBA SHALL record ΔH% history and structural signature lineage in the CTP metadata when available from upstream components.  

**HLR-20.40.060-014:** OuBA SHALL ensure that any remaining uncertainty or residue is explicitly represented in the CTP and not silently discarded.  

**HLR-20.40.060-015:** OuBA SHALL emit CTPs in a serialization‑ready form suitable for storage, governance inspection, and replay.  

**HLR-20.40.060-016:** OuBA SHALL enforce that no Path‑A primitive can modify TP after CTP emission.  

**HLR-20.40.060-017:** OuBA SHALL enforce that Path B and governance components treat the CTP as read‑only.  

**HLR-20.40.060-018:** OuBA SHALL not depend on nondeterministic sources such as random seeds, external services, or user‑profile‑dependent behavior.  

**HLR-20.40.060-019:** OuBA SHALL fail fast and report an error if the IdOB input is structurally incomplete or violates required invariants for commit.  

**HLR-20.40.060-020:** OuBA SHALL never drop, reorder, or duplicate semantic units (e.g., referents, constraints, message items) when constructing the CTP.

## 5. Metadata structure (informative)

The following CTP metadata shape is informative but consistent with the HLRs above:

```markdown
CTP {
  semantic_core: SemanticCore
  provenance: InputReference
  lineage: {
    sob_id,
    srob_id,
    cnob_id,
    smob_id,
    idob_id,
    routing_path,
    ruleset_ids: Array<RulesetID>
  }
  entropy_history: Array<DeltaHPercent>
  signature_history: Array<SignatureVectorID>
  commit_timestamp,
  commit_hash
}
```

## 6. Safety and governance boundary (informative)

OuBA marks the boundary between Path‑A meaning construction and governance/realization:

- After OuBA, TPTB/TPSF/GBIB operate only on CTPs.  
- Path B reads CTPs for realization and execution but does not modify them.  
- Replay and audit operate over CTPs and their recorded lineage and metadata.
