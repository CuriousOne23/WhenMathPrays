# **20.40.060 — OuBA‑prm (Output Basin Primitive)**  
**Version:** 2.1 (COB‑Complete Option‑C Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative — Path‑A Final Commit Primitive  
**Purpose:** Freeze and commit the authoritative semantic, metadata, context, and provenance state into a CTP.

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

OuBA‑prm is the **final TP‑writing primitive** in Path A.  
It receives the **fully stabilized meaning state** produced by IdOB and produces a **Committed Thought Packet (CTP)** — the immutable semantic snapshot used by:

- COB behavior engines  
- CST truth‑alignment engines  
- Path‑B truth and safety engines  
- governance, replay, and audit  

OuBA:

- performs **no inference**  
- performs **no routing**  
- performs **no OB‑family recomputation**  
- introduces **no new semantic content**  
- freezes semantic, metadata, context, identity, and lineage deterministically  
- emits a CTP that becomes the authoritative meaning envelope for downstream systems  

After OuBA, **no Path‑A primitive may modify TP**, and **all downstream components treat the CTP as read‑only**.

---

# **1. Scope (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑001**  
OuBA SHALL accept as input only meaning states that have passed through IdOB in the Path‑A pipeline.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑002**  
OuBA SHALL produce exactly one CTP for each accepted IdOB output. 

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑003**  
OuBA SHALL freeze `TP.semantic.semantic_core` such that no further primitive can modify its content.  

---

# **2. Semantic Preservation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑004**  
OuBA SHALL preserve all referents, bindings, and constraints present in the IdOB output without addition, removal, or reinterpretation.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑005**  
OuBA SHALL NOT introduce any new semantic content, referents, or constraints not present in the IdOB output.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑006**  
OuBA SHALL NOT perform semantic inference, scoring, routing, or candidate generation.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑007**  
OuBA SHALL operate deterministically such that identical IdOB inputs yield identical CTP outputs.   

---

# **3. Pipeline Boundaries (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑008**  
OuBA SHALL NOT invoke any OB layer (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB), SSG, TR, RB, or IdOB during its operation.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑009**  
OuBA SHALL NOT read or depend on global state beyond what is required to attach provenance, lineage, and metadata.   

---

# **4. Provenance & Lineage Commit (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑010**  
OuBA SHALL attach complete OB lineage to the CTP, including identifiers for SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, IdOB, and routing decisions where available.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑011**  
OuBA SHALL attach a `commit_timestamp` and `commit_hash` sufficient for replay and audit.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑012**  
OuBA SHALL record the ruleset identifiers used along Path A in the CTP metadata.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑013**  
OuBA SHALL record ΔH% history in `TP.metadata.entropy_history[]` and structural signature lineage in `TP.metadata.signature_history[]`.  

---

# **4A. COB‑Required Provenance Commit (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑013A**  
OuBA SHALL commit `TP.lineage_log[]` into the CTP such that COB and CST can perform lineage‑continuity analysis.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑013B**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP metadata fields required by COB, including:  
`contextual_alignment_record`, `identity_shift_record`, `topic_anchor_record`, `continuity_record`, and `intent_record`.

---

# **5. Residue & Uncertainty Handling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑014**  
OuBA SHALL ensure that any remaining uncertainty or residue is explicitly represented in the CTP and not silently discarded.   

---

# **6. Serialization & Replay (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑015**  
OuBA SHALL emit CTPs in a serialization‑ready form suitable for storage, governance inspection, and replay.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑018**  
OuBA SHALL NOT depend on nondeterministic sources such as random seeds, external services, or user‑profile‑dependent behavior.  

---

# **7. Commit Boundary Enforcement (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑016**  
OuBA SHALL enforce that no Path‑A primitive can modify TP after CTP emission.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑017**  
OuBA SHALL enforce that Path‑B, COB, CST, and governance components treat the CTP as read‑only.  

---

# **8. Error Handling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑019**  
OuBA SHALL fail fast and report an error if the IdOB input is structurally incomplete or violates required invariants for commit.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑020**  
OuBA SHALL never drop, reorder, or duplicate semantic units (referents, constraints, message items) when constructing the CTP. 

---

# **9. COB‑Required Commit of Semantic Envelope (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑021**  
OuBA SHALL commit the full semantic envelope required by COB, including:  
`proposition_set[]`, `truth_evidence[]`, `semantic_tags[]`, `messy_input_record`, and `lane_local_identity`.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑022**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP trace fields required by COB and CST, including:  
`ob_trace[]` and `tb_trace[]`.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑023**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP policy markers required for downstream routing and safety.

---

# **10. COB‑Required Commit of Context Envelope (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑024**  
OuBA SHALL commit the entire `TP.next_context.*` structure, including topic, stance, intent, register, politeness, epistemic shading, continuity, direction, coherence, shift_required, importance, and clarifying_fields[].

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑025**  
OuBA SHALL commit all context‑derived metadata required for COB continuity and CST alignment.

---

# **11. CST‑Required Identity Snapshot Commit (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑026**  
OuBA SHALL commit `TP.cob_state_snapshot` such that CST can perform identity‑layer truth alignment.

---

# **12. Informative Metadata Structure (No SHALLs)**

The following shape is consistent with all HLRs:

```
CTP {
  semantic_core
  semantic_envelope {
    proposition_set[]
    truth_evidence[]
    semantic_tags[]
    messy_input_record
    lane_local_identity
  }
  context_envelope {
    TP.next_context.*
  }
  provenance
  lineage {
    sob_id, srob_id, cnob_id, smob_id, idob_id
    routing_path
    ruleset_ids[]
    lineage_log[]
  }
  metadata {
    entropy_history[]
    signature_history[]
    contextual_alignment_record
    identity_shift_record
    topic_anchor_record
    continuity_record
    intent_record
    policy_markers[]
    ob_trace[]
    tb_trace[]
    cob_state_snapshot
  }
  commit_timestamp
  commit_hash
}
```

# **13. Governance Boundary (Informative)**

OuBA marks the transition from Path‑A meaning construction to governance and realization:

- COB and CST operate directly on CTPs  
- TPTB / TPSF / GBIB operate only on CTPs  
- Path B reads CTPs but does not modify them  
- Replay and audit operate over CTP lineage, metadata, and context
