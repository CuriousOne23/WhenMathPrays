## 1. Purpose

IIInB performs deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic surface‑level repair on the output of InB.  
It applies rule‑based, non‑inferential expansions that normalize user‑approved shorthand and correct deterministic surface irregularities without constructing meaning.  
IIInB is the second primitive in Path A.

---

## 2. Scope

IIInB operates only on the surface form produced by InB.  
It performs deterministic normalization, repair, and expansion of surface tokens.  
It does not interpret meaning, resolve ambiguity, or access global state.  
IIInB prepares input for IE and CEx while preserving all meaning.

---

## 3. Interfaces

### 3.1 Inputs

- `InB_Output` — canonicalized surface tokens, spans, punctuation, and metadata.

### 3.2 Outputs

- `IIInB_Output` — normalized, repaired surface form suitable for deterministic intake.

## 4. Normative Requirements (HLRs)

**HLR-20.101-001:** IIInB SHALL operate only on the output of InB in the Path‑A pipeline.  

**HLR-20.101-002:** IIInB SHALL apply only deterministic, rule‑based transformations that preserve meaning.  

**HLR-20.101-003:** IIInB SHALL not introduce new semantic content, referents, or constraints.  

**HLR-20.101-004:** IIInB SHALL not perform semantic inference, ambiguity resolution, or intent interpretation.  

**HLR-20.101-005:** IIInB SHALL not access TP history, CE, CIL, semantic_core, or any global state.  

**HLR-20.101-006:** IIInB SHALL expand only deterministic shorthand forms that have been explicitly approved for expansion.  

**HLR-20.101-007:** IIInB SHALL not expand shorthand forms whose interpretation depends on context, history, or semantics.  

**HLR-20.101-008:** IIInB SHALL preserve token order and SHALL not drop, reorder, or duplicate tokens.  

**HLR-20.101-009:** IIInB SHALL normalize surface irregularities such as spacing, punctuation, casing, and repeated characters only when such normalization is deterministic.  

**HLR-20.101-010:** IIInB SHALL not modify or reinterpret punctuation in ways that alter meaning.  

**HLR-20.101-011:** IIInB SHALL produce output that is fully deterministic and replayable for identical inputs.  

**HLR-20.101-012:** IIInB SHALL not invoke any OB layer (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB), SSG, TR, RB, or IdOB.  

**HLR-20.101-013:** IIInB SHALL not depend on nondeterministic sources such as randomness, user‑profile heuristics, or external services.  

**HLR-20.101-014:** IIInB SHALL annotate repair operations in metadata when required by the active ruleset.  

**HLR-20.101-015:** IIInB SHALL attach a ruleset identifier to its output sufficient to support replay and audit.  

**HLR-20.101-016:** IIInB SHALL fail fast and report an error if the input from InB is structurally invalid or cannot be deterministically repaired.  

**HLR-20.101-017:** IIInB SHALL emit output in a serialization‑ready form suitable for IE and CEx.  

---

## 5. Allowed Transformations (Informative)

The following operations are permitted when they satisfy all HLRs:

- deterministic shorthand expansion (e.g., “btw” → “by the way”)  
- deterministic contraction expansion  
- deterministic punctuation normalization  
- deterministic casing normalization  
- deterministic Unicode normalization  
- deterministic correction of repeated characters  

These operations do not alter meaning and do not require context.

---

## 6. Forbidden Transformations (Informative)

The following operations are not permitted:

- semantic inference of any kind  
- expansion requiring TP history or CE  
- ellipsis resolution  
- pronoun resolution  
- intent inference  
- command inference  
- creative rewriting  
- nondeterministic heuristics  

Examples of forbidden expansions include:

- “again” → “repeat the last action”  
- “same as before” → “repeat previous command”  
- “lights kitchen” → “lights in the kitchen”  

These require semantic interpretation and violate HLR‑20.101‑004 and HLR‑20.101‑007.

---

## 7. Determinism Requirements (Informative)

IIInB transformations are fully deterministic:

- same input → same output  
- no stochastic branching  
- no user‑dependent behavior  
- no environment‑dependent behavior  

Replay determinism is required for audit and governance.

---

## 8. Metadata Requirements (Informative)

IIInB output includes:

```markdown
metadata: {
  ruleset_id: RulesetID,
  timestamp,
  input_hash,
  repair_operations: Array<RepairOp>
}
```

This metadata supports replay, debugging, and provenance tracking.

---

## 9. Summary (Informative)

IIInB is a deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic repair primitive.  
It normalizes surface form without interpreting meaning and prepares input for IE and CEx.  
It enforces determinism, preserves meaning, and respects all pre‑semantic boundaries.
