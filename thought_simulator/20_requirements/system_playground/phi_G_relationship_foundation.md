# phi-G Relationship Foundation

## Purpose
Define the core phi-G relationship as a relational primitive that bridges SSG (single-responsibility transformation layer) to RB (Relational Basin) dynamics. This foundation enables subsequent simulation requirements and validation while preserving the overall manifold architecture (object-basin stability through relational transformation).

## Scope
- Technical focus only.
- Links from upstream object-basin variants (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB) through SSG into phi-G and onward to RB.
- RBU (subculture block) is explicitly out of scope.
- Emphasis on simulatable properties suitable for deterministic fixed-time-step execution on standard laptop hardware.

## Definitions and Primitives

**Phi (φ)**  
A generative or phase-like operator that acts on input state from SSG. It introduces dynamic variation or resonance modulation without collapsing into fixed objects. Key characteristics:
- Produces trajectories or deformations that can approach singularity-like dominance.
- Maintains relational openness (non-objectifying) in fuzzy regimes.

**G**  
A grounding or global relational operator that anchors or modulates phi outputs. It provides coherence and basin-compatible stability signals for downstream RB formation.

**phi-G Relationship**  
The composite mapping φ → G (or bidirectional under certain flows) that transforms SSG outputs into RB-compatible inputs. It operates as a relational bridge: phi generates exploratory variation; G ensures relational coherence and prevents unbounded drift.

## Integration with Lineup

- **Upstream (SOB/SROB/CnOB/SmOB → SSG)**: phi-G consumes stabilized or partially deformed states from object-basin variants via SSG's single job (transformation).
- **SSG Role**: Single responsibility — prepare and route state for phi-G application. No dual duties.
- **Downstream (phi-G → RB)**: Outputs feed relational basin dynamics (trajectory curvature, resonance, entropy terms compatible with the recurrence equation in GRP).

## Key Relational Properties

- **Approach to Singularity/Mystery**: As phi-G interaction intensifies, the relational field experiences dominance without full objectification. Observable as increased curvature or resonance amplification.
- **Fuzzy Space Handling**: Supports non-binary, experiential transitions rather than strict thresholds.
- **Stability Invariants**: Must preserve overall manifold coherence (basin integrity) while allowing transformation.
- **Simulability Constraints**: All operations defined for fixed-time-step updates. No recursive depth or high-memory structures that would exceed laptop resources.
