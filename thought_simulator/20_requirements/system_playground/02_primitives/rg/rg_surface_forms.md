# **rg_surface_forms — Symbolic Surface-Form Primitives**

## **1. Title and Purpose**

This document defines the symbolic surface‑form primitives used by RG.

RG surface‑form primitives provide connective and assembly templates for final OuBB text.

---

## **2. Architectural Context**

The pipeline is:  
**RSG → RG → OuBB**

RG surface‑form primitives are symbolic and deterministic.  
RG surface‑form primitives do not reinterpret SSR, manifold placement, or RSG projection logic.

---

## **3. Surface‑Form Primitive Definitions**

**rg_connective_link**  
- Symbolic template for linking clauses.  
- Reflects sequential clause‑shape alignment.

**rg_connective_merge**  
- Symbolic template for merging related clauses.  
- Reflects shared identity or relation anchors.

**rg_connective_transition**  
- Symbolic template for transitional connectives.  
- Reflects domain or flow shifts.

**rg_clause_join**  
- Symbolic assembly primitive for clause joining.  
- Reflects ordering metadata.

**rg_surface_join**  
- Symbolic assembly primitive for surface integration.  
- Reflects surface‑form compatibility.

**rg_ordered_join**  
- Symbolic assembly primitive for ordered combination.  
- Reflects full RSG ordering metadata.

Additional primitives follow the same pattern for all admissible combinations.  
Each primitive is symbolic and template‑based.  
Each primitive reflects clause‑shape alignment and ordering metadata.  
No geometric or numeric interpretation.

---

## **4. Surface‑Form Structure Rules**

- RG surface‑form primitives must encode symbolic connective and assembly behavior only.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not encode clause‑shape structure (RSG handles structure).  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not encode descriptive content (RSG provides content).  
- RG surface‑form primitives must remain stable across routing epochs.

---

## **5. Surface‑Form Admissibility Rules**

- Admissibility must depend on clause‑shape identity.  
- Admissibility must depend on ordering metadata.  
- Admissibility must respect compatibility between clause‑shape and surface‑form primitives.  
- No inference, no dynamic meaning, no probabilistic selection.

---

## **6. Surface‑Form Compatibility Rules**

- RG surface‑form primitives must be compatible with unified RSG output structures.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not override clause‑shape identity.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not modify surface‑form primitives.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not reinterpret domain anchors, coordinates, or mismatch indicators.

---

## **7. Output Requirements**

- RG surface‑form primitives must be symbolic templates ready for RG assembly.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must be deterministic and stable across routing epochs.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must be directly consumable by rg_assembly_rules.md.

---

## **8. Constraints to Avoid Drift**

- RG surface‑form primitives must remain symbolic.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not expand into geometric or numeric domains.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must not encode hidden logic or implicit meaning.  
- RG surface‑form primitives must preserve strict Path A → Path B separation.

This specification defines the deterministic symbolic surface‑form primitives for RG.
