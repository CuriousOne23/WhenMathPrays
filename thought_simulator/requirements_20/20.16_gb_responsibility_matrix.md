# 20.16 GB Responsibility Matrix and Quantitative Caps

**Document ID:** 20.16  
**Version:** 0.3  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Status:** CP-approved — dual-pipeline hardening (v0.2 refinements applied)

## Purpose

Defines GB **responsibility boundaries** and **explicit numeric/structural caps** that prevent GB overload (HLR-20.010-099/104). This module operationalizes HLR-20.010-023–026 and SHALL NOT weaken invariants in [20.12_ts_invariants.md](20.12_ts_invariants.md).

**Goal:** GB remains supervisor and arbitrator only — bounded, auditable, and incapable of becoming a monolithic god module.

## Parent Scope

- **GB supervisory envelope only:** reads TP lane-local state, MPs, and append-only supervisory triggers; writes `supervisory` markers and flow-control directives at safe boundaries
- **Does not modify:** 20.10, 20.12, 20.20, 20.30 bootstrap documents
- **Policy detail:** rate limits, priority models, safe-mode transitions, and external interface constraints are specified in [20.80_gb_requirements.md](20.80_gb_requirements.md)
- **IMR alignment:** IMR per-conversation caps in [20.45_imr_requirements.md](20.45_imr_requirements.md) are consumer-side; GB caps govern supervisory decisions on IMR requests

## Non-Goals

- GB algorithms, scoring formulas, or 40-series implementation
- Basin-specific grammar or meaning-construction rules
- User-facing clarification UX

---

## 1. GB Responsibility Matrix (Mandatory)

**HLR-20.016-001:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Supervisory Flow Control** — approve/deny inquiries (IB instantiation); **Must**; SHALL NOT mutate TP/MTP meaning state directly; only at safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.016-002:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Coherence & Stability** — monitor ΔH% trajectory, detect drift/oscillation; **Must**; SHALL NOT perform meaning construction; use explicit metrics.

**HLR-20.016-003:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Arbitration** — score and select between parallel candidate traces; **Must**; SHALL NOT make final meaning assertions; produce auditable rationale.

**HLR-20.016-004:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Commitment Management** — create, validate, decay commitments; **Must**; SHALL NOT override deterministic basin rules; bounded depth.

**HLR-20.016-005:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Learned Component Oversight** — validate candidate primitives from learned modules; **Must**; SHALL NOT trust learned outputs without validation; grammar + commitment check required.

**HLR-20.016-006:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Cross-Basin Coordination** — authorize and mediate basin transitions; **Must**; SHALL NOT directly execute basin-internal logic; use explicit transition operators.

**HLR-20.016-007:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Population Control** — approve IB merge/split, OB decomposition; **Must**; SHALL NOT perform merges/splits directly; only at safe boundaries.

**HLR-20.016-008:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Safety & Policy** — enforce RB relationship safety policies; **May**; SHALL NOT bypass RB safety checks; high priority.

**HLR-20.016-009:** GB SHALL be responsible for **Human Oversight** — request human approval for critical promotions; **May**; SHALL NOT replace human judgment; audit trail required.

### Hard limitations

10. **HLR-20.016-010:** GB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction or primitive chaining.

11. **HLR-20.016-011:** GB SHALL NOT mutate `semantic_core`, `exec_plan`, or `exec_trace` directly — only through deterministic supervisory interface actions that affect flow at safe boundaries (HLR-20.012-001).

12. **HLR-20.016-012:** GB SHALL NOT make real-time decisions inside a TS execution cycle except at enumerated safe-boundary application points.

13. **HLR-20.016-013:** GB decisions SHALL remain auditable with fixed reason codes.

14. **HLR-20.016-014:** GB complexity SHALL be bounded (no unbounded recursion or search).

15. **HLR-20.016-015:** Responsibilities SHALL be pushed downward wherever possible into BLG, typed primitive operators, ΔH% automatic regulation, and deterministic basin rules.

## 2. Quantitative Caps (Per-Cycle Defaults)

All caps below are **default policy values**; deployments MAY override via `policy_signature` but SHALL NOT remove cap enforcement entirely.

| Cap ID | Field | Default | Scope |
|--------|-------|---------|-------|
| `CAP_GB_ACTIONS` | `max_supervisory_actions_per_cycle` | **8** | Total issued supervisory commands (all classes) |
| `CAP_GB_IB_DECISIONS` | `max_ib_decisions_per_cycle` | **6** | IB approve + deny decisions combined |
| `CAP_GB_IB_APPROVE` | `max_ib_approvals_per_cycle` | **4** | IB instantiation approvals |
| `CAP_GB_IB_DENY` | `max_ib_denials_per_cycle` | **4** | IB instantiation denials |
| `CAP_GB_OSC_CHECK` | `max_oscillation_checks_per_cycle` | **2** | ΔH% oscillation/drift evaluations |
| `CAP_GB_XBASIN` | `max_cross_basin_coordination_per_cycle` | **3** | Authorized basin-transition events |
| `CAP_GB_IMR_CYCLE` | `max_imr_supervisory_decisions_per_cycle` | **2** | IMR-routed approve/deny/escalate decisions |
| `CAP_GB_ARBITRATE` | `max_arbitration_events_per_cycle` | **2** | Parallel-trace arbitration resolutions |
| `CAP_GB_INTERRUPT` | `max_supervisory_interrupts_per_cycle` | **4** | Async interrupts delivered to TS safe boundaries |

16. **HLR-20.016-016:** GB SHALL enforce `max_supervisory_actions_per_cycle` (default = 8) as a hard ceiling on all supervisory commands issued in one cycle, including flow-control, population, arbitration, and IMR decisions.

17. **HLR-20.016-017:** GB SHALL enforce `max_ib_decisions_per_cycle` (default = 6) on combined IB approve/deny decisions; `max_ib_approvals_per_cycle` (default = 4) and `max_ib_denials_per_cycle` (default = 4) SHALL be enforced as independent sub-caps.

18. **HLR-20.016-018:** GB SHALL enforce `max_oscillation_checks_per_cycle` (default = 2); additional drift/oscillation signals beyond the cap SHALL NOT trigger further evaluations in the same cycle.

19. **HLR-20.016-019:** GB SHALL enforce `max_cross_basin_coordination_per_cycle` (default = 3) on authorized basin-transition and cross-basin mediation events.

20. **HLR-20.016-020:** GB SHALL enforce `max_imr_supervisory_decisions_per_cycle` (default = 2) on IMR Type B cap-exceeded notifications, Type C approval/deny, and IMR-requested Safe-Mode/Halt decisions.

21. **HLR-20.016-021:** GB SHALL enforce `max_arbitration_events_per_cycle` (default = 2) on contradiction and parallel-trace arbitration (including `GB_ARBITRATED` outcomes per [20.17_messy_input_handling.md](20.17_messy_input_handling.md)).

22. **HLR-20.016-022:** GB SHALL enforce `max_supervisory_interrupts_per_cycle` (default = 4) on async interrupts delivered to TS; additional ready decisions SHALL queue deterministically for the next safe boundary.

## 3. Quantitative Caps (Per-Conversation and Structural)

| Cap ID | Field | Default | Scope |
|--------|-------|---------|-------|
| `CAP_GB_IMR_CONV` | `max_imr_supervisory_decisions_per_conversation` | **8** | Cumulative IMR-related GB decisions |
| `CAP_GB_CONV_ACTIONS` | `max_supervisory_actions_per_conversation` | **64** | Cumulative supervisory commands |
| `CAP_GB_PENDING_IB` | `max_pending_ib_requests` | **16** | Pending inquiry buffer depth |
| `CAP_GB_DEFER_QUEUE` | `max_deferred_supervisory_queue` | **32** | Cap-exceeded deferred actions |
| `CAP_GB_ARBIT_CAND` | `max_arbitration_candidates` | **4** | Parallel traces per `conflict_group_id` |
| `CAP_GB_TCU_SHARE` | `max_gb_tcu_share_percent` | **5** | GB TCU share ceiling (min typ = 3%) |
| `CAP_GB_DEFER_CYCLES` | `max_defer_cycles` | **8** | Max cycles a deferred action may remain queued before starvation handling |

23. **HLR-20.016-023:** GB SHALL enforce `max_imr_supervisory_decisions_per_conversation` (default = 8); when exceeded, GB SHALL deny further IMR supervisory approvals with `gb_reason_code = GB_CAP_IMR_CONV_EXCEEDED` and SHALL NOT retroactively alter prior decisions.

24. **HLR-20.016-024:** GB SHALL enforce `max_supervisory_actions_per_conversation` (default = 64); when exceeded, GB SHALL enter deterministic conversation-level degrade mode `GB_CONV_CAP_REACHED` (see §5).

25. **HLR-20.016-025:** GB SHALL enforce `max_pending_ib_requests` (default = 16) on the pending inquiry buffer; when full, new IB-Creation-Requests SHALL receive deterministic deny with `gb_reason_code = GB_CAP_PENDING_IB_FULL`.

26. **HLR-20.016-026:** GB SHALL enforce `max_deferred_supervisory_queue` (default = 32); when full, lowest-priority deferred actions SHALL be dropped with `gb_cap_status = DEFER_QUEUE_OVERFLOW` logged — never silently discarded.

27. **HLR-20.016-027:** GB SHALL enforce `max_arbitration_candidates` (default = 4) per `conflict_group_id`; additional parallel traces SHALL remain `ACTIVE` without GB arbitration until a subsequent cycle within cap.

28. **HLR-20.016-028:** GB SHALL operate within `max_gb_tcu_share_percent` (default = 5, minimum typical = 3 per 20.30 §8.4); TCU overrun SHALL trigger `GB_TCU_CAP_EXCEEDED` degradation per §5.4.

### Cap accounting rules

29. **HLR-20.016-029:** Per-cycle cap counters SHALL reset deterministically at cycle boundary; per-conversation counters SHALL persist across cycles until conversation close.

30. **HLR-20.016-030:** Cap accounting SHALL be reproducible from `(policy_signature, supervisory_inputs, cycle_id, conversation_id)` with no latent state.

31. **HLR-20.016-031:** All cap values and live counter snapshots SHALL be serializable in the `supervisory` envelope with canonical field ordering.

## 4. Dual-Pipeline Boundaries

32. **HLR-20.016-032:** GB SHALL read `semantic_core` only as **read-only** TP lane-local snapshots and MPs; GB SHALL NOT use `exec_plan` or `exec_trace` content to infer or alter meaning.

33. **HLR-20.016-033:** GB SHALL consume IMR supervisory triggers from append-only queues per HLR-20.012-014; GB SHALL NOT write `exec_trace` or schedule Pipeline B realization directly.

34. **HLR-20.016-034:** GB SHALL NOT create, modify, or clear messy-input tags (`messy_input_class[]`, `conflict_flag`, `underspec_flag`, etc.) in `semantic_core` per [20.17_messy_input_handling.md](20.17_messy_input_handling.md); GB MAY set `contradiction_status = GB_ARBITRATED` only via approved arbitration at safe boundaries without collapsing parallel traces.

35. **HLR-20.016-035:** GB supervisory outputs SHALL write only the `supervisory` envelope and safe-boundary flow-control directives; GB SHALL NOT bypass Pipeline A write authority for any meaning field.

36. **HLR-20.016-045:** GB SHALL NOT advance, modify, or publish `routing_epoch_id` or SRP routing tables (HLR-20.012-006, HLR-20.012-013); GB SHALL consume `routing_epoch_id` as read-only metadata on all supervisory inputs and audit records.

## 5. Deterministic Degradation When Caps Are Exceeded

When any cap is exceeded, GB SHALL apply exactly one degradation mode per cap class. Multiple simultaneous exceedances SHALL resolve by fixed precedence (§5.5).

### 5.1 Per-cap degradation modes

| Cap exceeded | Degrade mode | Deterministic behavior |
|--------------|--------------|------------------------|
| `CAP_GB_ACTIONS` | `GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_ACTIONS` | Defer lowest-priority actions to `deferred_supervisory_queue`; execute highest-priority within cap |
| `CAP_GB_IB_*` | `GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_IB` | Queue IB requests FIFO; if `CAP_GB_PENDING_IB` also full → deny with `GB_CAP_PENDING_IB_FULL` |
| `CAP_GB_OSC_CHECK` | `GB_DEGRADE_SKIP_OSC` | Skip additional oscillation checks; log `GB_OSC_CHECK_SKIPPED` with signal IDs suppressed |
| `CAP_GB_XBASIN` | `GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_XBASIN` | Defer coordination to next cycle; TS continues with last authorized routing epoch |
| `CAP_GB_IMR_*` | `GB_DEGRADE_DENY_IMR` | Deny IMR supervisory approval; emit `gb_approval_granted = false`; IMR receives fixed reason code |
| `CAP_GB_ARBITRATE` | `GB_DEGRADE_PRESERVE_PARALLEL` | Leave traces parallel (`contradiction_status = ACTIVE`); defer arbitration |
| `CAP_GB_INTERRUPT` | `GB_DEGRADE_BATCH_INTERRUPT` | Batch interrupts at next safe boundary only |
| `CAP_GB_TCU_SHARE` | `GB_DEGRADE_TCU_FALLBACK` | Stop new evaluations; apply pending decisions only; log `tcu_overrun_amount` per 20.30 §8.3 |
| `CAP_GB_CONV_ACTIONS` | `GB_DEGRADE_CONV_SAFE` | Enter Safe-Mode (per 20.80 §4); deny non-safety supervisory expansions for remainder of conversation |

37. **HLR-20.016-036:** GB SHALL apply the degradation mode in the table above for the matching cap class; degradation SHALL NOT invent semantics, force contradiction resolution, or fill underspec slots.

38. **HLR-20.016-037:** Every cap-exceeded event SHALL append a `gb_cap_record` with `gb_cap_status`, `gb_cap_code`, `cap_id`, `counter_value`, `cap_limit`, and `degrade_mode_applied`.

39. **HLR-20.016-038:** Cap degradation SHALL NOT mutate `semantic_core`, `exec_plan`, or `exec_trace`; flow effects SHALL apply only at safe boundaries through supervisory directives.

### 5.2 Deferred-action priority sort

When `GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_ACTIONS` applies, deferred actions SHALL sort by fixed priority (high → low) then FIFO within priority:

1. `P0_SAFETY_STOP` — external Stop, Type C safety halt
2. `P1_SAFETY_DENY` — IB deny on safety, Kill inquiry
3. `P2_DRIFT_RESPONSE` — oscillation/drift Dampen/Slow
4. `P3_IB_ROUTINE` — routine IB approve/deny
5. `P4_XBASIN` — cross-basin coordination
6. `P5_ARBITRATION` — parallel-trace arbitration
7. `P6_POPULATION` — Crop, Prune, merge/split

40. **HLR-20.016-039:** Deferred-action ordering SHALL use the priority list above; ties within the same priority SHALL break by `(enqueue_cycle, enqueue_seq)` ascending.

### 5.3 Deferred-action anti-starvation

Each queued deferred action SHALL carry `defer_cycle_count` (cycles since enqueue). Lower-priority caps SHALL NOT starve higher-priority work indefinitely, and higher-priority deferrals SHALL NOT block without bound.

41. **HLR-20.016-046:** GB SHALL enforce `max_defer_cycles` (default = 8) per deferred action; lower-priority deferred work SHALL NOT starve higher-priority caps indefinitely, and no deferred action SHALL remain queued beyond the limit. When `defer_cycle_count` reaches the limit:
- **P0–P2:** emit `gb_cap_code = DEFER_STARVATION_ALARM` and escalate to external supervisory interface per [20.80_gb_requirements.md](20.80_gb_requirements.md) §3
- **P3–P6:** remove from queue with `gb_cap_code = DEFER_STARVATION_DENY`; P6 population actions MAY trigger `GB_CONV_CAP_REACHED` if conversation action budget is also stressed
- Same-priority peers SHALL advance FIFO by `(enqueue_cycle, enqueue_seq)`; no peer SHALL outrank another beyond `max_defer_cycles`

### 5.4 Multi-cap precedence

43. **HLR-20.016-040:** When multiple caps are exceeded in the same evaluation pass, GB SHALL apply degradation in this order: **TCU → SAFETY (P0/P1) → ACTIONS → IB → IMR → OSC → XBASIN → ARBITRATE → INTERRUPT → CONVERSATION**.

### 5.5 Overflow telemetry

44. **HLR-20.016-041:** GB cap degradation SHALL emit overflow telemetry conforming to 20.30 §8.3 (`overflow_flag`, `overflow_type = GB_CAP_EXCEEDED`, `overflow_source_basin = GB`, `truncated_fields` listing deferred/dropped action IDs).

## 6. Replay Guarantees

45. **HLR-20.016-042:** Identical `(policy_signature, supervisory_inputs, prior_MTP, cycle_context)` SHALL produce identical cap accounting, degradation mode selection, deferred-queue ordering, and starvation outcomes.

46. **HLR-20.016-043:** Stripping `exec_plan` and `exec_trace` from a trace SHALL preserve all GB cap records and supervisory markers needed to replay GB decisions (HLR-20.012-011).

47. **HLR-20.016-044:** GB cap enforcement SHALL be a pure function of read-only inputs and `supervisory` state; GB SHALL NOT depend on Pipeline B expression artifacts for cap decisions.

## GB Cap Record Schema (minimum)

```text
gb_cap_record {
  cycle_id,
  conversation_id,
  cap_id,                    // e.g. CAP_GB_ACTIONS
  counter_value,
  cap_limit,
  gb_cap_status,             // OK | CAP_EXCEEDED | DEFER_QUEUE_OVERFLOW
  gb_cap_code,               // fixed reason code
  degrade_mode_applied?,     // e.g. GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_ACTIONS
  deferred_action_ids[],     // canonical sort
  defer_cycle_count?,        // per deferred action when applicable
  policy_signature
}
```

## Worked Example — IB Cap Exceeded

| Step | Event | GB behavior |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Cycle N: 6 IB-Creation-Requests arrive | First 4 approvals + 2 denials within `CAP_GB_IB_DECISIONS=6` |
| 2 | 7th request in same cycle | `GB_DEGRADE_DEFER_IB`: queue at `deferred_supervisory_queue` |
| 3 | Cycle N+1 safe boundary | Deferred IB decision executes if within cap |
| 4 | Pending buffer at 16 | 17th pending request → deny `GB_CAP_PENDING_IB_FULL` |
| 5 | Replay | Identical cap counters and defer ordering reproduced |

## Worked Example — Multi-Cap Precedence (Single Cycle)

Cycle **N** arrives with simultaneous pressure on TCU, actions, IMR, and arbitration. GB applies §5.4 precedence **TCU → … → ARBITRATE** and §5.2 priority **P0 → P6** within the actions budget.

| Order | Incoming signal | Cap hit | Precedence step | Outcome |
|-------|-----------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | GB TCU at 5% ceiling | `CAP_GB_TCU_SHARE` | TCU (first) | `GB_DEGRADE_TCU_FALLBACK`; no new evaluations this pass |
| 2 | IMR Type C safety halt | `CAP_GB_IMR_CYCLE` (2 already used) | SAFETY via P0 | P0 executes within remaining action budget; excess IMR → `GB_DEGRADE_DENY_IMR` |
| 3 | 9th supervisory command | `CAP_GB_ACTIONS` (8 max) | ACTIONS | P0/P1 retained; P6 Crop deferred; P5 arbitration deferred |
| 4 | 3rd arbitration request | `CAP_GB_ARBITRATE` | ARBITRATE (last) | `GB_DEGRADE_PRESERVE_PARALLEL`; `contradiction_status = ACTIVE` |
| 5 | Cycle N+1 | caps reset (cycle scope) | — | Deferred P5 arbitration runs if within cap; `defer_cycle_count` incremented |
| 6 | Cycle N+8 | P6 still deferred | `CAP_GB_DEFER_CYCLES` | `DEFER_STARVATION_DENY`; action removed with audit |
| 7 | Replay | — | — | Identical precedence, defer queue, and starvation codes |

## Verification Focus

- No cap may be bypassed under stress injection
- Cap exceed → deterministic degrade mode (never silent drop except logged `DEFER_QUEUE_OVERFLOW`)
- GB never writes `semantic_core` / `exec_plan` / `exec_trace` / `routing_epoch_id`
- IMR Type C approvals respect `CAP_GB_IMR_*`
- Multi-cap precedence ordering under combined stress
- Deferred actions hit `DEFER_STARVATION_*` at `max_defer_cycles` deterministically
- Replay equivalence for cap accounting golden fixtures
