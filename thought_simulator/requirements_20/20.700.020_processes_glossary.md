## Path A (Meaning Construction) — Process Block
**Purpose:** Constructs meaning deterministically from raw input, producing the authoritative TP for the turn.  
**Focus:** Path A hop string as written in 20.705 §2. CTP-prm is the 20.145 policy-freeze hop immediately before every RB (`TR→CTP→RB`), including the first RB and whether or not IdOB has run.  
**When:** Runs every input cycle until Truth/Done evaluation stabilizes and the turn‑final TP is produced; Path A ends at the meaning-commit freeze (OuBA), after which Path B begins.  
**Why:** Ensures meaning is built deterministically, replayably, and independently of realization, with all corrections and routing resolved before any B‑side processing.  
**Is not:** A realization pipeline; a truth evaluator; a safety engine; a generator of surface text; a writer of MTP; a post-IdOB consolidator named CTP.  
**Example:** `…→TR→CTP→RB→…` — CTP policy-freezes the current TP as-is and appends one `cognitive_history[]` entry; it does not merge multi-OB outputs or wait for IdOB. OuBA later meaning-freezes the committed snapshot.  
**Normative home:** 20.705 §2, 20.145

## Path B (Realization & Expression) — Process Block
**Purpose:** Realizes the finalized TP into surface‑form output without altering meaning, using deterministic planning and seed‑isolated expression mapping.  
**Focus:** LI‑prm→MLI→REx→RPlan→RPU→OuB→Response Generator; deterministic meaning‑layer inference (MLI) precedes realization planning; the Response Generator performs seed‑isolated expression mapping using TPTB/TPSF and Path‑B realization metadata.  
**When:** Runs after Path A completes and the meaning boundary (OuBA) is finalized; Path B begins at TrigRB and proceeds through SRP‑selected realization plans to final expression.  
**Why:** Ensure expression is deterministic, replay‑stable, truth/safety‑aware, and strictly consistent with Path‑A meaning, without re‑entering or modifying meaning construction.  
**Is not:** A meaning writer; a semantic interpreter; a truth evaluator; a safety engine; a generator of new meaning; a contributor to MTP.  
**Example:** Given identical (TP, seed), Path B produces identical surface text regardless of OB‑set routing or Path‑A correction structure.  
**Normative home:** 20.111, 20.705, 20.30.080

## **Supervisory Processes (Cross‑Pipeline Control) — Process Block**  
**Purpose:** Provides deterministic supervisory control for mismatch detection, correction gating, IB instantiation, and nondeterminism boundaries.  
**Focus:** IMR evaluation consumes evaluator_signal and TP/MTP deltas, classifies mismatches (A/B/C), emits CorrectionTrigger for Type B, and gates Type C through GB; IB‑Creation‑Request prevents automatic IB instantiation and requires GB evaluation; MB observes all supervisory signals; Random Seed Boundary confines nondeterminism to B‑side expression.  
**When:** Runs concurrently with or immediately after Path A/B events depending on trigger conditions.  
**Why:** Ensures bounded, auditable correction and prevents uncontrolled escalation or unsafe inquiry creation.  
**Is not:** A meaning writer; a realization primitive; a governance basin; a semantic interpreter.  
**Example:** MI_INCOMP triggers IB‑Creation‑Request, which forces GB evaluation before any IB is instantiated.  
**Normative home:** 20.17, 20.30, 20.70, 20.207 (not 20.45; live 20.45 is ISc scoring).

## **Post‑TS Processes (IB → TB → GBIB → GB) — Process Block**  
**Purpose:** Executes the inquiry-side DF / IBC over committed meaning. Live order after OuBA freeze is IB→TB→GBIB→GB (heading TB→IB was reversed). Path-B realization is not the required predecessor of IB.  
**Focus:** TB performs truth‑state evaluation; IB performs ambiguity analysis and generates clarification proposals; GBIB applies governance rules, truth‑tags, safety‑flags, and MTP‑commit boundaries to filter or escalate proposals; GB performs final global governance evaluation and produces `governance_outcome{}` for TS.  
**When:** Post-OuBA DF: IB→TB→GBIB→GB. Not titled TB→IB. Not required to wait for Path-B realization / IBC-after-B.  
**Why:** Ensures ambiguity handling, clarification decisions, and governance outcomes are deterministic, auditable, and policy‑aligned, preventing IB or Path B from making unsupervised decisions.  
**Is not:** A meaning writer; a realization primitive; a semantic interpreter; a TP or MTP modifier; a neural inference path.  
**Example:** IB proposes `ask_for_antecedent`; GBIB validates it under TPTB/TPSF; GB authorizes `issue_clarification` as the final governance outcome.  
**Normative home:** 20.80.

## Four‑Path Model (TS Processing Paths) — Process Block
**Purpose:** Prevent architectural drift by enforcing four distinct, non‑overlapping TS processing trajectories selected at InB based on ambiguity state, governance outcomes, and meaning‑layer requirements.  
**Focus:** Four deterministic, mutually exclusive paths: Normal Path, Clarification Path, Inference Path, Post‑TS Interrogation Path — each with its own entry conditions and isolated execution flow.  
**When:** Path chosen at InB using TB/IB ambiguity state, governance signals, and Path‑A/B readiness.  
**Why:** Ensure deterministic routing, prevent stage confusion, and isolate clarification, inference, and interrogation behaviors.  
**Is not:** Merged pipeline; semantic generator; meaning writer.  
**Example:** Ambiguous input triggers Clarification Path; MLI‑required interrogation triggers Inference Path.  
**Normative home:** 20.705 §2 / §3.6 (flow trackers). “Architecture discussion” is not a file; heading kept (T-022 empty-home).

## Truth/Done — 20.140
**Purpose:** Provides the explicit completion-detection gate that terminates a processing cycle based on truth hypothesis and done-state evaluation.
**Focus:** Terminal evaluation over `truth_hypotheses` and `done_state`.
**When:** Pipeline A `truth_done_evaluation` at the OuBA boundary.
**Why:** Explicit completion detection; prevent runaway processing cycles.
**Is not:** IMR satisfaction; IB resolution.
**Example:** `done_state` reached under clean rules.
**Normative home:** 20.140.

## truth_hypotheses‑prc — 20.45
**Purpose:** Provide a deterministic, bounded scoring procedure for ISc to evaluate interpretation candidates using local truth‑pattern cues without performing truth evaluation.  
**Focus:** Apply local truth‑pattern rules to candidate_set{} using CE; produce scoring signals (score_set, score_conflict, score_reason_code) for TPU.  
**When:** Invoked only inside ISc during Path‑A inference.  
**Why:** Enable deterministic, replay‑safe candidate scoring without accessing global state, TB truth evaluation, or governance truth.  
**Is not:** Truth evaluator; TB; Truth/Done; semantic generator; global inference engine; governance truth model.  
**Example:** truth_hypotheses‑prc detects that one referent candidate violates a local truth‑pattern constraint and assigns it a lower score in score_set.  
**Normative home:** 20.45_ts_isc_scoring.md (live ISc; 20.44 is WrdNm).

## truth_hypotheses — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the structured, local truth‑pattern hypotheses derived during Path‑A interpretation, used for candidate scoring (truth_hypotheses‑prc) and for Truth/Done completion evaluation.  
**Focus:** Encodes hypothesis‑level truth signals (stability, contradiction markers, structural truth cues) derived from merge_result and ΔH%/H% evolution; provides the substrate for local scoring and final completion evaluation.  
**When:** Produced during Path‑A after merge and before Truth/Done; included in update_structure and validated by RBU.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe representation of truth‑pattern hypotheses without performing truth evaluation (TB) or governance truth; enable ISc scoring and Truth/Done completion detection.  
**Is not:** TB truth; governance truth; semantic content; global inference; latent truth model; semantic_core.  
**Example:** merge_result stabilizes a referent binding, producing a strengthened truth_hypothesis; Truth/Done later evaluates these hypotheses to determine whether done_state is satisfied.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.030, 20.30.060, 20.140.

## **MLI (Meaning‑Layer Inference) — Process Block**  
**Purpose:** Performs deterministic, non‑neural reasoning over the meaning state to derive implications, commitments, and structural consequences before realization.  
**Focus:** Rule‑based meaning‑layer transformations executed *inside LI‑prm* at the start of Path B, after Path‑A populates the representation layer and before any realization planning or neural intuition.  
**When:** Invoked by LI‑prm immediately after TP/MTP commit and before REx/RPlan/RPU.  
**Why:** Ensures semantic reasoning is deterministic, auditable, and independent of neural inference; prevents ambiguity or implication resolution from being delegated to probabilistic models.  
**Is not:** A primitive; a basin; part of the Interpretive Basin Chain; a TP writer; a semantic_core builder.  
**Example:** If X implies Y and X is asserted, MLI deterministically adds Y to the meaning state before realization planning.  
**Normative home:** 20.111.

## IB‑Creation‑Request — 20.17, 20.30
**Purpose:** Trigger supervisory‑queue evaluation before any Inquiry Basin (IB) instantiation, preventing automatic inquiry escalation on MI_INCOMP.  
**Focus:** Supervisory‑queue wire requesting GB evaluation and approval prior to IB creation.  
**When:** Emitted on MI_INCOMP; may be scheduled by OuB.  
**Why:** Prevent automatic inquiry escalation; require governance‑side gating before IB instantiation.  
**Is not:** IB state; automatic inquiry; IMR trigger; meaning‑layer inference.  
**Normative home:** 20.17, 20.30.

## mtp_update — Removed (Obsolete Mechanism)
**Purpose:** (Removed) Previously represented a Path‑A–side atomic write of a semantic snapshot into the MTP. This mechanism no longer exists; MTP is now written exclusively by HC‑Flow after the entire turn completes.  
**Focus:** None. No Path‑A or Path‑B primitive writes to MTP, and no semantic snapshot is committed mid‑turn.  
**When:** Never invoked. Replaced by HC‑Flow’s once‑per‑turn archival snapshot of the finalized TP(N).  
**Why:** The architecture now enforces a strict separation: Path A and Path B construct meaning and realization, while HC‑Flow alone produces the immutable historical MTP snapshot. This eliminates mid‑turn commits, semantic_core dependencies, and RBU‑based updates.  
**Is not:** A Path‑A commit; a Path‑B commit; a semantic snapshot writer; an RBU operation; a truth evaluator; a routing step; a partial update.  
**Example:** (Removed) Any prior examples involving RBU validating update_structure and writing semantic snapshots into MTP are obsolete.  
**Normative home:** 20.115 (MTP), 20.705 (HC‑Flow).

## RRw (Routing Row) — 20.53  
**Purpose:** Deterministic routing decision record produced by RB/TR and validated by RBU; included inside SSR and consumed by Path‑B primitives.  
**Focus:** Holds opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id, routing_epoch_id, rb_trigger_id, lineage additions, arbitration path, staleness metadata, and semantic‑deltaH routing signals; provides Path‑B with routing decisions without exposing TP internals.  
**When:** Generated during Path‑A routing; copied into SSR at OuBA commit; read by Path‑B during behavior/voice/mapping selection.  
**Why:** Provide deterministic routing for Path‑B while preserving A/B separation; ensure OpBeh/OBG/XlateR selection is stable, replay‑safe, and TP‑agnostic.  
**Is not:** TP field exposed to Path‑B; meaning constructor; semantic interpreter; standalone artifact; RRw is never delivered separately from SSR.  
**Example:** RB/TR produce RRw selecting opbeh_id and xlater_id; OuBA embeds RRw into SSR; OpBeh reads RRw from SSR to select behavior class.  
**Normative home:** 20.53.

## **OpBeh — Operational Behavior Selector — 20.41**  
**Purpose:** Selects the operational behavior mode that TS must use for a given routing and realization context, choosing the correct behavior pathway before OBG generates the behavior object.  
**Focus:** Consumes routing‑layer and behavior‑layer signals, evaluates operational constraints, and deterministically selects the behavior mode that governs downstream behavior generation.  
**When:** Executes after routing primitives (RG/RSG/TrigRB) and before OBG; part of the TS behavior‑execution chain; not part of Path‑A or Path‑B meaning construction.  
**Why:** Ensures TS uses the correct operational behavior mode for the current request, preventing mismatches between routing intent and behavior generation, and guaranteeing deterministic behavior selection.  
**Is not:** Not a meaning constructor; not a routing basin; not a reference object; not a governance primitive; does not read or write TP or SSR; does not generate behavior objects (OBG does that).  
**Example:** Routing indicates a “structured explanation” mode; OpBeh selects the corresponding operational behavior profile, which OBG then uses to generate the final behavior object.  
**Normative home:** 20.41.
