## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **umbrella-level normative requirements** for the Object Basin (OB) family.  
It establishes the shared invariants, boundaries, and obligations that apply to all OB primitives from **20.40.010 through 20.40.060**.  
Each OB layer has its own dedicated primitive specification; this document governs the family as a whole.

---

## 2. Scope

The OB family consists of the following Path-A primitives:

- **SOB** — Structural Object Basin (20.40.010)  
- **SROB** — Structural Refinement OB (20.40.020)  
- **CnOB** — Constraint OB (20.40.030)  
- **SmOB** — Semantic Mapping OB (20.40.040)  
- **IdOB** — Identity-Conditioned OB (20.40.050)  
- **OuBA** — Output Basin Commit Primitive (20.40.060)  

This umbrella specification defines:

- OB terminology  
- OB family invariants  
- cross-layer constraints  
- ordering requirements  
- determinism requirements  
- provenance requirements  
- routing and residue requirements  

Individual layer-specific requirements appear in their respective documents.

---

## 3. OB Terminology (Informative)

**Object Basin (OB):**  
A deterministic Path-A subsystem that progressively reduces uncertainty and extracts structural, relational, constraint, and pre-semantic residue from TP content. IdOB is the family member that performs identity-conditioned meaning refinement; it is not a structural-residue layer.

**Residue:**  
The remaining structural or constraint-level information not yet resolved at a given OB stage.

**Entropy (H% / ΔH%):**  
A measure of uncertainty reduction across OB layers. Distinct from IdOB `meaning_delta_h`.

**Structural Signature:**  
A deterministic vector representation derived from SmOB output, used for routing.

**Routing:**  
The deterministic selection of the next OB layer based on structural signature, residue, and lineage.

**Commit Boundary:**  
The point at which meaning becomes immutable (OuBA). The frozen snapshot is a committed TP snapshot. It is not CTP. CTP denotes only CTP-prm (20.145), the Path A hop in 20.705 §2.

## 4. OB Family Normative Requirements

### 4.1 Ordering and Pipeline Integration

**HLR-20.40-001:** The OB family SHALL execute in the fixed order SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB unless routing explicitly selects IdOB or OuBA. When routing selects IdOB or OuBA, the live Path A neighborhood SHALL be the 20.705 §2 string: `TR → CTP → RB` immediately before the selected exit (`→ IdOB → MCB → RBU` or `→ OuBA`). IdOB is not required on the OuBA exit. `TR → RB → CTP` and `RB → RTU` as an IdOB invoke order are not live.

**HLR-20.40-002:** Each OB layer SHALL accept only the output of the immediately preceding OB layer or the routing decision from RB.

**HLR-20.40-003:** SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB SHALL NOT read or depend on global state, CE, CIL, or semantic_core as authority. IdOB-prm MAY read CE, CIL, or discourse fields only as identity-adjacent cues. IdOB-prm SHALL NOT treat those cues as a second meaning store and SHALL NOT write CE, CIL, or semantic_core.

---

### 4.2 Determinism and Replay

**HLR-20.40-004:** All OB layers SHALL operate deterministically such that identical inputs yield identical outputs.

**HLR-20.40-005:** OB layers SHALL not use nondeterministic sources, stochastic processes, or user-profile-dependent heuristics.

**HLR-20.40-006:** OB outputs SHALL be serialization-ready and replayable.

---

### 4.3 Pre-Semantic Boundary

**HLR-20.40-007:** SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, truth evaluation, or referent resolution, and SHALL NOT birth a meaning stand-in M.

**HLR-20.40-008:** SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB SHALL NOT modify semantic_core or introduce new meaning. IdOB-prm SHALL perform identity-conditioned meaning interpretation and SHALL write only the envelopes listed in 20.116.020 / 20.40.050 (crossing packet `TP.idob`, `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`, root completion flags, and optional authorized exports).

---

### 4.4 Structural and Constraint Integrity

**HLR-20.40-009:** OB layers SHALL preserve token order and SHALL not drop, reorder, or duplicate semantic units.

**HLR-20.40-010:** OB layers SHALL explicitly represent uncertainty, residue, and conflicts rather than removing or resolving them.

**HLR-20.40-011:** OB layers SHALL reduce entropy monotonically or record ΔH% when no reduction is possible.

---

### 4.5 Provenance and Lineage

**HLR-20.40-012:** Each OB layer SHALL attach provenance metadata sufficient for replay, audit, and lineage tracking.

**HLR-20.40-013:** Each OB layer SHALL record the ruleset identifier used during its execution.

**HLR-20.40-014:** Each OB layer SHALL record structural signatures, residue, and ΔH% where applicable.

---

### 4.6 Routing and Identity Conditioning

**HLR-20.40-015:** OB layers SHALL expose residue and structural signatures required for deterministic routing by RB.

**HLR-20.40-016:** IdOB SHALL be invoked only when routing explicitly selects identity-conditioned refinement. On that selection the invoke neighborhood SHALL be 20.705 §2: `TR → CTP → RB → IdOB`.

---

### 4.7 Commit Boundary

**HLR-20.40-017:** NA (2026-08-30). Retired. Prior text required OuBA to freeze meaning into a “Committed Thought Packet (CTP).” That acronym is reserved for CTP-prm (20.145 / 20.705 §2). Do not restore that wording.

**HLR-20.40-018:** After OuBA execution, no OB layer SHALL modify TP or semantic_core.

**HLR-20.40-019:** OuBA SHALL be the only OB-family primitive permitted to finalize and freeze meaning into a committed TP snapshot. That snapshot SHALL NOT be designated CTP.

## 6. Summary (Informative)

This document establishes the unified normative requirements governing the OB family.  
It defines the invariants, boundaries, and obligations that keep SOB–SmOB pre-semantic and provenance-rich, allow IdOB to refine meaning on the authorized envelopes, and keep OuBA as the freeze door.  
CTP appears in this family only as a pointer to 20.145; OuBA does not produce a CTP. Live IdOB/OuBA hops follow 20.705 §2.
