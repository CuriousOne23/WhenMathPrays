# **0. Informative Overview**

IdOB‑prm is the **identity‑conditioned meaning‑refinement primitive** in the OB‑family.  
It operates **after RB‑prm and RTU‑prm** have placed the TP into a specific manifold chart and committed routing state (or after an equivalent committed routing snapshot is provided for progressive isolation tests).

Official live order of that neighborhood (informative; isolation may start at IdOB):

```
WrdNm → ISc → RTU → TR → CTP → RB → IdOB → MCB → RBU
```

RTU **constructs** `routing_update`. TR / CTP / RB consume it. **RB is the last routing commit** IdOB treats as a read-only view. The compact historical sketch `RB → RTU → IdOB` is not the official Path A scheduling claim.

Isolation fixtures may present that snapshot as an **utterance** and/or a **structure card** (six IDs) plus optional packs and a CIE row. That does not move scheduling law: full-pipeline IdOB still follows committed routing when the lineup is live.

IdOB consumes structural semantic geometry (fields, roles, objects, gradients, universes, subfields) produced by upstream structural OBs, or assigned from an utterance by the structure-assignment tables, and maps those features to meaning-layer refinement. That interpretation is not structural mutation.

In foundation terms, IdOB realizes the first‑order generative operator:

$$
\mathcal{I} : (\mathbf{F},\ \text{IdOB}_{\text{prev}},\ \text{allowed TP identity/meaning fields}) \rightarrow \text{IdOB}
$$

IdOB‑prm:

- refines meaning using identity, subculture, stance, register, tone, and referent‑specific cues  
- forms and updates an **identity envelope** (roles, candidates, provenance, geometry markers, residue markers, stability, alignment, lineage markers)  
- applies **CIE** (defined in §0.6) as stance pressure on the meaning stand-in  
- may apply **regime‑conditioned** inherit vs reset behavior using the **shared regime table** (Stable, Refinement, Drift, Transition, Collapse)  
- does not mutate structural geometry (structural OBs / SSG own that domain) 
- does not produce **structural** residue (structural OBs / SSG own that domain)  
- does not modify **routing** metadata owned by RB‑prm or RTU‑prm  
- does not own DCB execution‑flow geometry ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$)  
- prepares the TP for TR and potential Path‑B realization  
- may run multiple times (serial IdOB passes) when layered meaning refinement is required  
- owns `tp.idob` and crossing-instrument `meaning_delta_h`; MCB reads those fields and must not overwrite them (20.40.055)

IdOB‑prm is a **meaning‑only OB primitive** relative to structure and routing: structural OBs and SSG have already completed for a normal cycle, and RB‑prm has determined that identity‑conditioned refinement is needed (or the progressive fixture supplies an equivalent committed routing context).

# 0.1 Meaning‑Layer Definitions (Informative)

IdOB‑prm operates over three distinct meaning layers:

1. **Lexical Meaning**  
   Meaning derived from the meaning_dictionary and attached to TP fields by WrdNm.  
   This meaning is static, dictionary-defined, and not identity-conditioned.

2. **Structural Meaning**  
   Meaning implied by structural semantic geometry, including semantic fields, roles,  
   objects, gradients, universes, and subfields.  
   This meaning is derived from structural OBs (or isolation assignment) and is not lexical.

3. **Identity‑Conditioned Meaning Refinement**  
   Meaning produced by IdOB‑prm through the integration of lexical meaning,  
   structural meaning, and identity geometry (including CIE).  
   This refinement produces meaning_delta_h and the identity envelope / crossing packet.

These definitions disambiguate the term “meaning” within this document.

Path A **meaning** for the crossing is the speaker’s **intended projection** stand-in (six named axes). It is not a claim that IdOB is a person’s inner object. The **utterance** is the carrier event (spoken or written string), not the meaning.

### **0.2 Layer separation** *(Informative)*

| Symbol | Layer | Owner |
|--------|-------|-------|
| $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$ | Path‑A execution‑flow accounting | DCB |
| $\kappa_{\text{route}}$ | Routing trajectory / RED | RB |
| $\kappa_{\text{id}}$ | Identity trajectory on IGM | IdOB diagnostics / geometry markers |

IdOB does not treat DCB curvature or RB adjacency_class as substitutes for identity geometry; it may **read** them only as identity‑adjacent context if present.

### **0.3 Shared regimes** *(Informative)*

Regime labels used in IdOB foundation behavior and progressive observation:

```
Stable | Refinement | Drift | Transition | Collapse
```

Thresholds are provisional in the relational model. IdOB does not invent a second regime vocabulary.

### **0.4 First‑order IdOB envelope** *(Informative)*

Foundation theory describes an envelope:

```
roles, candidates, provenance, geometry, residue, stability, alignment, lineage
```

Normative ownership of specific TP paths appears in the HLRs below (semantic meaning fields, completion flags, path‑B eligibility, identity‑importance, crossing packet `tp.idob`, and any declared envelope export path).

### **0.5 Progressive lineup** *(Informative)*

IdOB testbenches follow progressive_lineup dual modes (`testbench` / `general`), naming, import path, report format, optional structural foundation comparison, and foundation observability (§3.11–3.12). Category placement: `testbenches/path_a/identity/`.

Enabled isolation cases record the utterance with input and expected **packet** fields. Lifecycle formation…closure cases are archived and are not the S2M expected block.

### **0.6 CIE — Conversational Identity Envelope** *(Informative)*

**CIE** is the **conversational identity envelope** for one hop: the stance / hold under which the speaker puts the utterance forward.

CIE is **not**:

- the listener’s mood  
- a user persona store  
- routing  
- a second structure geometry  
- Path-B expression  
- MCB `next_context.stance` (that slot is a next-turn COB hint)

CIE **is**:

- a named row (`cie_id`) with `identity_importance` ($\alpha$) and an `identity_vector` $I$ aligned to the six meaning axes  
- the pressure applied **after** a meaning stand-in $M$ is born:  

$$
M' = \mathrm{clip}_{[0,1]^6}(M + \alpha I)
$$

Official this revision: register / kind-of-talk holds (examples: `physical_stance`, `scientific_stance`, `neutral`). Affect-as-pressure lists (fear, exasperation, …) are a later named table, not a substitute for CIE.

Swapping CIE must not change `structural_key` and must not invent map candidates. Tables: `primitives/idob/cie.examples.yaml`.

### **0.7 Two geometries of the crossing** *(Informative)*

IdOB’s structure-to-meaning hop uses **two** descriptive geometries (not a claim of a smooth manifold on the group list):

| Geometry | What it is | Machine form |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| Structure | Landscape / road the utterance is typed onto | Six IDs + `structural_key` (+ optional residue / feature tags) |
| Meaning | Speaker-projection stand-in | Six axes in $[0,1]$: physicality, sociality, temporality, intentionality, materiality, spatiality |

The **map** is a membership door (key → legal `group_id` set). **Rank** orders only those legal ids. Leftover `residue_code` is a tension signal, not an automatic next six-tuple (RB owns next-OB search).

# **1. Nature of IdOB‑prm** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑001**
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate as an OB‑family primitive performing identity‑conditioned meaning interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑002**
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate only within the manifold chart / routing context previously selected or committed by RB‑prm (or an equivalent committed routing snapshot supplied for progressive isolation testing).

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑003**
IdOB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑level updates without altering manifold topology, structural object representation, or RB‑owned routing filter fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑004**
IdOB‑prm SHALL support serial chaining, allowing multiple IdOB passes when additional meaning refinement is required.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑005**
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose routing state has been committed by RTU‑prm, or a progressive‑test fixture that explicitly presents a committed routing‑equivalent snapshot.

Clarification (does not repeal HLR-005): on the official live stretch, RTU constructs `routing_update` and RB is the last routing commit before IdOB. “Committed by RTU-prm” means the routing-update object has been produced and then consumed through `TR → CTP → RB`. Isolation fixtures remain authorized by HLR-053.

### HLR‑20.40.050‑050
IdOB‑prm SHALL interpret structural semantic geometry (fields, roles, objects, 
gradients, universes, subfields) as read-only inputs and SHALL map these structural 
features to identity-conditioned meaning refinement without performing structural 
mutation or altering structural OB-owned fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑037**
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat RB outputs (including `process.routing_filter` and optional RED fields) strictly as a read‑only view when present and SHALL NOT mutate RB‑owned fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑038**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT write DCB‑owned `geometric_state`, `geometric_history`, or `dcb_events` fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑039**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT treat DCB `geometric_state.curvature` ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$) as identity curvature ($\kappa_{\text{id}}$) or routing curvature ($\kappa_{\text{route}}$).

### **HLR-20.40.050-053**
A progressive isolation fixture SHALL be accepted as a routing-equivalent snapshot when it presents an utterance and/or a structure card (six IDs or `card_id` resolving to those IDs) plus optional `packs_loaded` and `cie_id`. Absence of a live RB/RTU pass SHALL NOT by itself forbid isolation execution.

### **HLR-20.40.050-054**
IdOB-prm SHALL record the utterance string on the crossing packet when an utterance is present. On assignment miss or partial six-ID fill, IdOB-prm SHALL keep that string and SHALL NOT birth a meaning stand-in from the miss.

IdOB‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as identity‑adjacent or semantic‑adjacent features.

# **4.1 Meaning Delta‑H Calculation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑043 — Meaning Delta‑H Definition**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute `meaning_delta_h` as a deterministic scalar representing the magnitude of meaning‑level refinement derived strictly from meaning‑layer fields and identity‑adjacent cues.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑044 — Meaning‑Field Dependency**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute `meaning_delta_h` exclusively from meaning‑layer features, including identity‑conditioned FFTM fields and permitted semantic‑adjacent cues, without consuming structural entropy fields or routing‑owned ΔH%.

### HLR‑20.40.050‑052
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat structural-derived meaning features as meaning-layer inputs 
for the purpose of computing meaning_delta_h, provided such features arise from 
structural semantic geometry and not from structural entropy or ΔH% fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑045 — Canonical Delta‑H Equation (family / export layout)**

$$
meaning\\_delta\\_h = \frac{\lVert m_{after} - m_{before} \rVert_1}{K}
$$

where $m_{before}$ and $m_{after}$ are identity‑conditioned meaning‑layer vectors, $\lVert\cdot\rVert_1$ is the L1 norm, and $K$ is a deterministic normalization constant ensuring $0 \le meaning\\_delta\\_h \le 1$.

This family formula remains authorized for TPU / FFTM export layouts that are not the six-axis crossing packet.

### **HLR-20.40.050-059 — Crossing-packet Delta-H (six-axis instrument)**
When the meaning stand-in is the six-axis crossing vector of §3A, IdOB-prm SHALL compute packet `meaning_delta_h` as

$$
meaning\\_delta\\_h = \lVert M' - M_{\text{before}} \rVert_2
$$

with $M_{\text{before}}$ the prior six-axis map when supplied, else the zero vector (HLR-048). IdOB-prm SHALL also write `meaning_cie_delta` $= \lVert M' - M \rVert_2$. This instrument formula does not repeal HLR-045 for other authorized layouts.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑046 — Replay‑Safe Delta‑H**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL guarantee that `meaning_delta_h` is deterministic under replay for identical TP snapshots, identity metadata, and meaning‑layer features.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑047 — No Structural Entropy Consumption**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT consume structural entropy $H$, entropy delta percentage ΔH%, or any scoring‑layer uncertainty metrics when computing `meaning_delta_h`.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑048 — Baseline Meaning Initialization**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set $m_{before} = 0$ when no prior meaning state exists (first IdOB pass or first meaning cycle after reset). IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT infer first‑cycle state from missing TP fields or uninitialized identity‑layer values.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑049 — Deterministic Normalization Constant (K)**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute the normalization constant $K$ deterministically from the fixed meaning‑layer feature layout.  
$K$ SHALL be defined as the sum of the maximum permitted magnitudes of all identity‑conditioned meaning‑layer fields:

$$
K = \sum_{i=1}^{N} max\\_range(m_i)
$$

where:

- $N$ is the number of meaning‑layer features in the identity‑conditioned meaning vector,  
- $max\\_range(m_i)$ is the architecture‑defined maximum absolute magnitude for feature $m_i$,  
- all ranges SHALL be declared in the meaning‑layer feature specification (semantic_core / FFTM meaning‑layer schema),  
- $K$ SHALL be constant across all TP snapshots, cycles, and IdOB passes.

IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT derive $K$ from runtime values of $m_{before}$ or $m_{after}$.

HLR-049 applies to the family formula (HLR-045). The six-axis instrument (HLR-059) does not use $K$.

# **9. Interfaces** *(Informative)*
