# **0. Informative Overview**

IdOB‑prm is the **identity‑conditioned meaning‑refinement primitive** in the OB‑family.  
It operates **after RB‑prm and RTU‑prm** have placed the TP into a specific manifold chart and committed routing state (or after an equivalent committed routing snapshot is provided for progressive isolation tests).

In foundation terms, IdOB realizes the first‑order generative operator:

$$
\mathcal{I} : (\mathbf{F},\ \text{IdOB}_{\text{prev}},\ \text{allowed TP identity/meaning fields}) \rightarrow \text{IdOB}
$$

IdOB‑prm:

- refines meaning using identity, subculture, stance, register, tone, and referent‑specific cues  
- forms and updates an **identity envelope** (roles, candidates, provenance, geometry markers, residue markers, stability, alignment, lineage markers)  
- may apply **regime‑conditioned** inherit vs reset behavior using the **shared regime table** (Stable, Refinement, Drift, Transition, Collapse)  
- does not extract structure  
- does not produce **structural** residue (structural OBs / SSG own that domain)  
- does not modify **routing** metadata owned by RB‑prm or RTU‑prm  
- does not own DCB execution‑flow geometry ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$)  
- prepares the TP for TR and potential Path‑B realization  
- may run multiple times (serial IdOB passes) when layered meaning refinement is required  

IdOB‑prm is a **meaning‑only OB primitive** relative to structure and routing: structural OBs and SSG have already completed for a normal cycle, and RB‑prm has determined that identity‑conditioned refinement is needed (or the progressive fixture supplies an equivalent committed routing context).

### **0.1 Layer separation** *(Informative)*

| Symbol | Layer | Owner |
|--------|-------|-------|
| $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$ | Path‑A execution‑flow accounting | DCB |
| $\kappa_{\text{route}}$ | Routing trajectory / RED | RB |
| $\kappa_{\text{id}}$ | Identity trajectory on IGM | IdOB diagnostics / geometry markers |

IdOB does not treat DCB curvature or RB adjacency_class as substitutes for identity geometry; it may **read** them only as identity‑adjacent context if present.

### **0.2 Shared regimes** *(Informative)*

Regime labels used in IdOB foundation behavior and progressive observation:

```
Stable | Refinement | Drift | Transition | Collapse
```

Thresholds are provisional in the relational model. IdOB does not invent a second regime vocabulary.

### **0.3 First‑order IdOB envelope** *(Informative)*

Foundation theory describes an envelope:

```
roles, candidates, provenance, geometry, residue, stability, alignment, lineage
```

Normative ownership of specific TP paths appears in the HLRs below (semantic meaning fields, completion flags, path‑B eligibility, identity‑importance, and any declared envelope export path).

### **0.4 Progressive lineup** *(Informative)*

IdOB testbenches follow progressive_lineup dual modes (`testbench` / `general`), naming, import path, report format, optional structural foundation comparison, and foundation observability (§3.11–3.12). Category placement: `testbenches/path_a/identity/`.

# **1. Nature of IdOB‑prm** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑001**
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate as an OB‑family primitive performing identity‑conditioned meaning interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑002**
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate only within the manifold chart / routing context previously selected or committed by RB‑prm (or an equivalent committed routing snapshot supplied for progressive isolation testing).

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑003**
IdOB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑level updates without altering manifold topology, structural object representation, or RB‑owned routing filter fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑004**
IdOB‑prm SHALL support serial chaining, allowing multiple IdOB passes when additional meaning refinement is required.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑005**
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose routing state has been committed by RTU‑prm, or a progressive‑test fixture that explicitly presents a committed routing‑equivalent snapshot.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑037**
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat RB outputs (including `process.routing_filter` and optional RED fields) strictly as a read‑only view when present and SHALL NOT mutate RB‑owned fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑038**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT write DCB‑owned `geometric_state`, `geometric_history`, or `dcb_events` fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑039**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT treat DCB `geometric_state.curvature` ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$) as identity curvature ($\kappa_{\text{id}}$) or routing curvature ($\kappa_{\text{route}}$).

## **1.1 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption** *(Informative)*

IdOB‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic or semantic‑adjacent metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)  
- clarifying_metadata  
- identity_metadata (IdOB fields, prior envelope)  
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)  
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)  
- normalization_metadata (IE)  
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX, RBU meaning commits when present)  
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB) as **identity‑adjacent** signals for residue markers — not structural mutation  
- next_context_metadata (MCB)  
- optional $\mathbf{F}$ approximations / foundation blocks for regime‑conditioned behavior  
- optional read‑only RB routing filter / RED view  
- optional read‑only DCB geometric_state as execution‑flow context only  
- lineage markers when used strictly as identity‑adjacent continuity features  

IdOB‑prm treats non‑owned TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
IdOB‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement.  
IdOB‑prm does not consume Pipeline‑B envelopes, truth/done fields, or RB/RTU fields as writable targets.  
IdOB‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as identity‑adjacent or semantic‑adjacent features.

# **4.1 Meaning Delta‑H Calculation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑043 — Meaning Delta‑H Definition**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute `meaning_delta_h` as a deterministic scalar representing the magnitude of meaning‑level refinement derived strictly from meaning‑layer fields and identity‑adjacent cues.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑044 — Meaning‑Field Dependency**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute `meaning_delta_h` exclusively from meaning‑layer features, including identity‑conditioned FFTM fields and permitted semantic‑adjacent cues, without consuming structural entropy fields or routing‑owned ΔH%.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑045 — Canonical Delta‑H Equation**

$$
meaning\_delta\_h = \frac{\lVert m_{after} - m_{before} \rVert_1}{K}
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑046 — Replay‑Safe Delta‑H**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL guarantee that `meaning_delta_h` is deterministic under replay for identical TP snapshots, identity metadata, and meaning‑layer features.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑047 — No Structural Entropy Consumption**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT consume structural entropy \(H\), entropy delta percentage ΔH%, or any scoring‑layer uncertainty metrics when computing `meaning_delta_h`.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑048 — Baseline Meaning Initialization**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set $m_{before} = 0$ when no prior meaning state exists (first IdOB pass or first meaning cycle after reset).

# **7. TP Update Request** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑024**
IdOB‑prm SHALL issue a meaning‑level TP update request `TPU.idob_update` containing only:

- `meaning_delta_h`  
- `idob_semantics[]`  
- `meaning_semantics[]`  
- `idob_complete`  
- `path_b_eligible`  
- `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`  
- and, when foundation envelope export is enabled, IdOB‑owned envelope fields required by the structural program  

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑025**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT request routing updates, structural updates, or TR updates.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑026 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves identity‑conditioned meaning refinement.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑027 — Read‑Only Discipline**
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat all non‑owned TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields outside the meaning‑layer and IdOB‑owned fields it is authorized to write.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑028 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑029 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT consume Pipeline‑B envelopes, truth/done fields, or routing_metadata / structural ΔH% as writable authority or as substitutes for identity‑conditioned meaning computation.

*(Clarification vs prior wording: lineage markers and read‑only RB/DCB views may be used as **identity‑adjacent context** when present; they remain non‑owned and must not be mutated. Prior absolute “no lineage fields” ban is corrected to this read‑only identity‑adjacent rule.)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑030 — Identity‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as identity‑adjacent or semantic‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform structural interpretation or routing interpretation.

*(Clarification: identity‑conditioned **meaning refinement** remains in scope; “semantic inference” in the prior phrasing is not a ban on IdOB’s meaning role.)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑031 — Identity‑Cue Integration**
IdOB‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into identity‑conditioned meaning refinement only when such metadata affects identity, subculture, stance, register, tone, or referent‑specific meaning.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑042**
IdOB‑prm SHALL produce identical authorized outputs for identical TP inputs (deterministic meaning refinement and, when enabled, deterministic foundation envelope updates).

# **8. Identity‑Importance** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑032**
IdOB‑prm SHALL refine semantic‑adjacent importance cues emitted by SmOB into **identity‑importance cues**, representing identity‑conditioned weighting of meaning‑relevant spans, referents, qualifiers, and subculture‑specific markers.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑033**
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute identity‑importance values using deterministic rules based solely on identity metadata, semantic‑adjacent cues, and meaning‑adjacent features, without performing structural interpretation or referent reconstruction beyond its meaning scope.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑034**
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues reduce meaning entropy and improve downstream TR and Path‑B eligibility checks without modifying TP text or structural fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑035**
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues remain addressable by RB‑prm as a **read‑only view** and consumable by TR, Path‑B eligibility logic, and subsequent IdOB passes.

### **HLR‑20.40.050‑036**
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues are monotonic and reproducible for identical TP snapshots.

# **9. Interfaces** *(Informative)*

**Upstream:**  
IdOB‑prm is scheduled after `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` when identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required, or under progressive isolation with a committed routing‑equivalent fixture.

**Downstream:**  
After `TPU.idob_update` is committed, RB‑prm and RTU‑prm may **read**:

- `idob_complete`  
- `meaning_delta_h`  
- `idob_semantics[]`  
- `path_b_eligible`  
- `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`  
- identity‑importance cues  
- foundation envelope view when exported  

to determine whether additional OB‑family primitives, TR, or Path‑B checks are required. RB does not write IdOB fields (20.50).

**Routing:**  
IdOB‑prm does not modify routing vectors, routing filters, or routing deltas. It may read RB/RED fields only as identity‑adjacent context.

**Truth & Path‑B:**  
IdOB‑prm prepares the TP for TR and potential Path‑B realization by resolving identity‑conditioned meaning and tagging eligibility; it does not initiate Path‑B.
