# **20.40.050 — IdOB‑prm Requirements (Updated for Path‑A Cognitive‑Field Model)**  
**Identity Object Basin Primitive (IdOB‑prm)**  
**Document ID:** 20.40.050  
**Version:** 2.1 (Cognitive‑Field Update)  
**Status:** Normative — OB‑Family Primitive  
**Purpose:** Identity‑conditioned meaning refinement after RB/RTU routing  

---

# **0. Informative Overview**

IdOB‑prm is the **identity‑conditioned meaning‑refinement primitive** in the OB‑family.  
It operates **after RB‑prm and RTU‑prm** have placed the TP into a specific manifold chart and committed routing state.

IdOB‑prm:

- refines meaning using identity, subculture, stance, register, tone, and referent‑specific cues  
- does not extract structure  
- does not produce residue  
- does not modify routing metadata  
- prepares the TP for TR and potential Path‑B realization  
- may run multiple times (serial IdOB passes) when layered meaning refinement is required  

IdOB‑prm is a **meaning‑only OB primitive**: structural OBs and SSG have already completed, and RB‑prm has determined that identity‑conditioned refinement is needed.

---

# **1. Nature of IdOB‑prm (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑001**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate as an OB‑family primitive performing identity‑conditioned meaning interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑002**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL operate only within the manifold chart previously selected by RB‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑003**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑level updates without altering manifold topology or structural object representation.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑004**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL support serial chaining, allowing multiple IdOB passes when additional meaning refinement is required.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑005**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose routing state has been committed by RTU‑prm.

---

## **1.1 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

IdOB‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic or semantic‑adjacent metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- clarifying_metadata
- identity_metadata (IdOB fields)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB)
- next_context_metadata (MCB)

IdOB‑prm treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
IdOB‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement.  
IdOB‑prm does not consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
IdOB‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as identity‑adjacent or semantic‑adjacent features.

---

# **2. What IdOB‑prm Is Not (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑006**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT produce structural residue or structural deltas.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑007**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT read or modify routing vectors, routing deltas, or TP fields owned by RB‑prm or RTU‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑008**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT trigger SSG or depend on SSG execution after its own invocation.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑009**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT participate in Path‑B realization or expression‑layer processing.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑010**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by SSG, RB‑prm, or RTU‑prm.

---

# **3. Invocation & Position in the Lineup (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑011**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL be invocable only after a routing cycle `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` has completed.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑012**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL be scheduled by TS as an OB‑family primitive when RB‑prm determines that identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑013**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL execute after structural OB‑family primitives and SSG have completed for the current cycle, unless TS schedules IdOB‑prm earlier for a meaning‑only refinement pass.

---

# **4. Meaning ΔH% and Semantics Tagging (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑014**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute and write `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`, representing the magnitude of meaning‑level refinement performed.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑015**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.semantic.idob_semantics[]` with identity‑conditioned semantics resolved during the pass.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑016**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL update `TP.semantic.meaning_semantics[]` with meaning‑adjacent cues required by TR and subsequent OB‑family primitives.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑017**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by SSG, RB‑prm, or RTU‑prm.

---

# **5. Completion & Next‑OB Signaling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑018**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.idob_complete = true` when meaning‑level refinement for the current cycle is complete.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑019**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.idob_complete = false` when additional IdOB passes are required.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑020**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.idob_next_ob_candidates[]` with OB‑family primitives that may be required for further meaning‑level refinement.

---

# **6. Path‑B Eligibility Tagging (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑021**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.path_b_eligible = true` when identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is sufficient for TR and potential Path‑B realization checks.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑022**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL set `TP.path_b_eligible = false` when meaning refinement is incomplete or the TP is not eligible for Path‑B.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑023**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT initiate Path‑B; it only signals eligibility.

---

# **7. TP Update Request (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑024**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL issue a meaning‑level TP update request `TPU.idob_update` containing only:

- `meaning_delta_h`  
- `idob_semantics[]`  
- `meaning_semantics[]`  
- `idob_complete`  
- `path_b_eligible`  
- `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`  

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑025**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT request routing updates, structural updates, or TR updates.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑026 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves identity‑conditioned meaning refinement.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑027 — Read‑Only Discipline**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields outside the meaning‑layer fields it owns.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑028 — Metadata Applicability Rule**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to identity‑conditioned meaning refinement and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑029 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑030 — Identity‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as identity‑adjacent or semantic‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform structural interpretation or semantic inference.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑031 — Identity‑Cue Integration**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into identity‑conditioned meaning refinement only when such metadata affects identity, subculture, stance, register, tone, or referent‑specific meaning.

---

# **8. New Requirements for Identity‑Importance (Added)**

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑032**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL refine semantic‑adjacent importance cues emitted by SmOB into **identity‑importance cues**, representing identity‑conditioned weighting of meaning‑relevant spans, referents, qualifiers, and subculture‑specific markers.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑033**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL compute identity‑importance values using deterministic rules based solely on identity metadata, semantic‑adjacent cues, and meaning‑adjacent features, without performing structural interpretation or referent reconstruction.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑034**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues reduce meaning entropy and improve downstream TR and Path‑B eligibility checks without modifying TP text or structural fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑035**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues remain addressable by RB‑prm and consumable by TR, Path‑B eligibility logic, and subsequent IdOB passes.

### **HLR‑20.40.50‑036**  
IdOB‑prm SHALL ensure identity‑importance cues are monotonic and reproducible for identical TP snapshots.

---

# **9. Interfaces (Informative)**

**Upstream:**  
IdOB‑prm is scheduled after `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm` when identity‑conditioned meaning refinement is required.

**Downstream:**  
After `TPU.idob_update` is committed, RB‑prm and RTU‑prm consume:

- `idob_complete`  
- `meaning_delta_h`  
- `idob_semantics[]`  
- `path_b_eligible`  
- `idob_next_ob_candidates[]`  

to determine whether additional OB‑family primitives, TR, or Path‑B checks are required.

**Routing:**  
IdOB‑prm does not read or modify routing vectors or routing deltas.

**Truth & Path‑B:**  
IdOB‑prm prepares the TP for TR and potential Path‑B realization by resolving identity‑conditioned meaning and tagging eligibility.
