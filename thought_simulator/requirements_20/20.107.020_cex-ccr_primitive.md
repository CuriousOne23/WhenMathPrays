# 20.107.020 — CEx‑CCR Primitive (TP‑Aligned, Version 1.0)  
### *Cross‑Correlation Layer for Conversation Identity Resolution*

---

## 1. Purpose *(Informative)*

CEx‑CCR is the **second internal module** of the CEx primitive.  
It receives:

- the **CEx‑IE envelope** (structural hints derived from IE tokens), and  
- the **CIL lineage + metrics** (identity, clarifying, context, continuity, topology, scalar metrics)

CEx‑CCR performs **deterministic cross‑correlation** between these two sources to determine:

- alignment strength across identity, clarifying, context, continuity, and reference‑back dimensions,  
- ambiguity, collapse risk, drift, and stability contributions,  
- the **conversation selection outcome**:  
  - one of the last 10 conversations,  
  - new conversation, or  
  - ambiguous → fallback.

CEx‑CCR does **not** perform repackaging (CEx‑IE) or downstream packaging (CEx‑Pck).

---

## 2. Inputs *(Informative)*

CEx‑CCR reads:

### 2.1 From CEx‑IE (`TP.cex.ie`)
- `tokens`  
- `token_flags`  
- `normalized_text`  
- `structural_phrases`  
- `topic_hint`  
- `intent_hint`  
- `continuity_hint`  
- `reference_hint`  
- `register_hint`  
- `politeness_hint`  
- `direction_hint`  
- `coherence_hint`  
- `importance_hint`

### 2.2 From CIL (`TP.cil`)
For each of the last ≤10 conversations:

- `identity_lineage`  
- `clarifying_lineage`  
- `context_lineage`  
- `continuity_lineage`  
- `topology`  
- `metrics`  
  - `primary_certainty`  
  - `ambiguity_score`  
  - `collapse_risk`  
  - `stability_score`  
  - `volatility_score`  
  - `drift_score`  
  - `lineage_confidence`  
- `semantic_residue`  
- `next_context`

---

## 3. Outputs *(Informative)*

CEx‑CCR emits a compact envelope under `TP.cex.ccr`:

```json
{
  "cex": {
    "ccr": {
      "alignment": {
        "identity": "CExAlign",
        "clarifying": "CExAlign",
        "context": "CExAlign",
        "continuity": "CExAlign",
        "reference": "CExAlign"
      },
      "scores": {
        "ambiguity": "number",
        "collapse": "number",
        "drift": "number",
        "stability": "number"
      },
      "decision": "CExDecision",
      "selected_conversation": "ConversationID | null"
    }
  }
}
```

Where:

- `CExAlign` is a compact enum: `none | weak | moderate | strong`  
- `CExDecision` is: `new | specific | fallback`

---

## 4. Field Definitions *(Informative)*

### 4.1 Alignment Fields
- **identity** — alignment between IE‑derived topic/intent and CIL identity lineage  
- **clarifying** — alignment between IE politeness/register and CIL clarifying lineage  
- **context** — alignment between IE topic/direction and CIL context lineage  
- **continuity** — alignment between IE continuity_hint and CIL continuity_prev  
- **reference** — alignment between IE reference_hint and CIL identity lineage

### 4.2 Score Fields
- **ambiguity** — derived from CIL ambiguity_score  
- **collapse** — derived from CIL collapse_risk  
- **drift** — derived from CIL drift_score  
- **stability** — derived from CIL stability_score

### 4.3 Decision Field
- **decision** — `new | specific | fallback`

### 4.4 Selected Conversation
- **selected_conversation** — conversation ID chosen by CCR, or `null` for new conversation

---

## 5. Normative Requirements — Inputs and Outputs

**HLR‑20.107.020‑001**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL read only `TP.cex.ie` and `TP.cil` fields.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑002**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL write only to `TP.cex.ccr` and SHALL NOT modify any upstream TP fields.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑003**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL emit alignment fields (`identity`, `clarifying`, `context`, `continuity`, `reference`) using the compact enum `CExAlign`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑004**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL emit score fields (`ambiguity`, `collapse`, `drift`, `stability`) as deterministic numeric values derived exclusively from CIL scalar metrics.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑005**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL emit a single `decision` field of type `CExDecision` with value `new`, `specific`, or `fallback`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑006**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL emit `selected_conversation` as the chosen conversation ID or `null` when the decision is `new`.

---

## 6. Normative Requirements — Alignment Computation

**HLR‑20.107.020‑007**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL compute identity alignment by comparing `topic_hint` and `intent_hint` against each conversation’s `identity_lineage`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑008**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL compute clarifying alignment by comparing `register_hint` and `politeness_hint` against each conversation’s `clarifying_lineage`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑009**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL compute context alignment by comparing `topic_hint` and `direction_hint` against each conversation’s `context_lineage`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑010**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL compute continuity alignment by comparing `continuity_hint` against each conversation’s `continuity_prev`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑011**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL compute reference alignment by comparing `reference_hint` against identity lineage and semantic residue.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑012**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL assign alignment values using deterministic rules mapping comparisons to `none | weak | moderate | strong`.

---

## 7. Normative Requirements — Score Computation

**HLR‑20.107.020‑013**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL derive ambiguity score directly from `TP.cil.metrics.ambiguity_score`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑014**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL derive collapse score directly from `TP.cil.metrics.collapse_risk`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑015**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL derive drift score directly from `TP.cil.metrics.drift_score`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑016**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL derive stability score directly from `TP.cil.metrics.stability_score`.

---

## 8. Path‑A COB/CIL Lineage & Metrics Support (Normative)**  
*(Paste directly into 20.107.020_cex_ccr.md)*

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑024**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest `TP.cil.identity_lineage` and treat it as the authoritative identity‑lineage structure for alignment computation.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑025**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest `TP.cil.continuity_lineage` and use it as the authoritative continuity‑lineage structure for continuity alignment.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑026**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest `TP.cil.topology` and use it as the authoritative identity‑layer topology for identity, context, and reference‑back alignment.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑027**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest all scalar fields in `TP.cil.metrics` and treat them as authoritative bounded‑semantic metrics for score computation:
- `ambiguity_score`  
- `collapse_risk`  
- `drift_score`  
- `stability_score`  
- `lineage_confidence`

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑028**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL treat all fields in `TP.cil.metrics` as read‑only and SHALL NOT compute, modify, or reinterpret any metric.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑029**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest `TP.cil.register_continuity` and incorporate it into clarifying‑alignment computation.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑030**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL ingest `TP.cil.importance_continuity` and incorporate it into context‑alignment and fallback‑selection logic.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑031**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of all lineage, topology, metric, and continuity fields consumed from `TP.cil`.

### **HLR‑20.107.020‑032**
CEx-CCR SHALL NOT emit pack_ids[], SHALL NOT load dictionaries, and SHALL NOT write TP.idob.

---

## 9. Normative Requirements — Decision Logic

**HLR‑20.107.020‑017**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL select **new conversation** when identity alignment is `none` AND ambiguity is high AND continuity_hint indicates reset.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑018**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL select **specific conversation** when identity alignment is `strong` AND ambiguity is low AND continuity alignment is `strong`.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑019**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL select **fallback** when identity alignment is `weak` OR ambiguity is moderate OR continuity alignment is unclear.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑020**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL select the fallback conversation as the most recent conversation with stability_score above a deterministic threshold.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑021**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL NOT use embeddings, semantic similarity, or global semantic inference in decision logic.

---

## 10. Normative Requirements — Determinism

**HLR‑20.107.020‑022**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL produce identical output for identical `TP.cex.ie` and `TP.cil` inputs.

**HLR‑20.107.020‑023**  
CEx‑CCR SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic fields.

---

## 11. Processing Summary *(Informative)*

CEx‑CCR:

1. Reads CEx‑IE structural hints and CIL lineage.  
2. Computes alignment across identity, clarifying, context, continuity, and reference dimensions.  
3. Computes ambiguity, collapse, drift, and stability scores.  
4. Applies deterministic decision rules to select a conversation.  
5. Emits a compact CCR envelope for CEx‑Pck.

This completes the definition of **20.107.020 — CEx‑CCR Primitive**.
