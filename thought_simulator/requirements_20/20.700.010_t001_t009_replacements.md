# ✔︎ **TPU (Thought Packet Updater) — Path A Only (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Path‑A content-mutation writer for correction; inserts and corrects message‑items, referents, constraints, and TP‑stream fields based on CE+CEx+ISc output; may write committed TP‑stream fields but never modifies semantics/meaning, processes, or supervisor once SSR(t) is produced.  
**Focus:** Deterministic, boundary‑enforced TP updates; applies meaning‑layer corrections derived from ISc scoring; preserves upstream commitments to semantics/meaning, processes, and supervisor.  
**When:** Path‑A correction flow: CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1).  
**Why:** Enforce TPU as the Path‑A content-mutation writer while leaving 20.116.020 envelope writes to IdOB-prm (Φ / TP.idob), CTP-prm (`cognitive_history[]`), and MCB (`next_context{}`); keep semantic/process/supervisor updates deterministic, auditable, and isolated from Path‑B.  
**Is not:** Topology engine; semantic interpreter; candidate generator; CIL reader; Path‑B primitive; meaning constructor for expression; field modifier for committed TP‑stream fields.  
**Example:** ISc selects a corrected referent or message‑item; TPU applies the update to TP(N), producing TP(N+1) under a new commit_id; TP‑stream fields remain exactly as committed by IE and IdOB.  
**Scope invariant:** TPU‑prm MUST NOT be invoked by Path‑B; Path‑B is TP‑stream‑read‑only.  
**Normative home:** 20.46.

# ✔︎ **TrSch‑prm (Turn Scheduler Primitive) — 20.155_prm (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Coordinate and sequence Path‑A primitives by reading the current TP (policy-frozen by CTP-prm immediately before RB) and selecting the next architectural action; may read all TP‑stream fields but does not modify or commit any.  
**Focus:** Deterministic interpretation of present control flags (`continue`, `needs_correction`, `done`, `unstable`, `halt`, `retry`, etc.) when those fields exist; preserves all TP‑stream fields exactly as present.  
**When:** After a CTP policy freeze on the live `TR→CTP→RB` spine; CTP does not wait for IdOB and does not emit a consolidated IdOB-set packet.  
**Why:** Keep scheduling separate from meaning refinement (IdOB-prm), routing (RB-prm), routing commit (RTU-prm), correction (CE-prm → TPU-prm), and meaning-commit freeze (OuBA-prm).  
**Is not:** Router; classifier; scorer; semantic evaluator; meaning‑refinement primitive; CTP consolidator; TP writer; semantic_core writer; correction engine; governance step; truth evaluator; ΔH% contributor; merge engine; field modifier.  
**Example:** CTP policy-freezes TP as-is (IdOB fields may be absent); TrSch reads whatever flags are present and does not treat CTP as an IdOB-output merger.  
**Normative home:** 20.155_trsch_prm.md; 20.145 (CTP before every RB).

# ✔︎ **IdOB (Identity Object Builder) — 20.40.050 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Sole Path A meaning-birth writer (INV-M0): identity‑conditioned interpretation that fills Φ (`selected_group_id`, six-axis `meaning_semantics`) and related TP.idob envelopes.  
**Focus:** Consume CE context shell, COB profile, CIL linkage, CEx‑CCR alignment signals, semantic‑adjacent residue, and MSL tokens to build identity objects, refine referents, interpret qualifiers, determine subculture, evaluate stance/shading/direction, and refine semantic‑importance entities/facts; writes identity‑conditioned meaning into TP metadata fields for COB/CIL/CST consumption.  
**When:** After RB routing decision; multiple cycles allowed within a turn for meaning shifts, qualifier‑driven reinterpretation, or identity‑basin refinement.  
**Why:** Enable precise, qualifier‑aware, identity‑conditioned meaning construction while respecting continuity and providing the meaning‑layer metadata required by COB, CIL, CST, and downstream primitives; ensure referent lineage, qualifier usage, subculture continuity, and semantic‑importance refinement are deterministically available for long‑horizon conversation identity.  
**Is not:** Cue OB; SmOB; context initializer; relevance evaluator; context writer; raw text processor; CIE-without-map birth; IdOB does not modify committed meaning outside its bounded Φ / TP.idob fields or perform global inference.  
**Example:** Strong qualifier cluster or subculture shift triggers additional IdOB cycles, producing refined referent lineage, updated qualifier metadata, updated subculture assignment, and refined semantic‑importance residues written into TP for COB/CIL continuity.  
**Normative home:** 20.40.050, 20.105.010, 20.105.020, 20.105.030

# ✔︎ **CTP‑prm (Collect Thought Point — policy freeze) — 20.145 (Updated)**  
**Purpose:** Policy-freeze the current TP immediately before every RB arbitration and append one `TP.metadata.cognitive_history[]` entry.  
**Focus:** Structural bookkeeping on the live spine `TR→CTP→RB`. Freezes TP as-is (IdOB may or may not have run). Allowed writes: `cognitive_history[]` (append-one), CTP provenance. No deep-copy snapshot, no required `TP.CTP` block, no freeze flag.  
**When:** Immediately before every RB-prm appearance, including the first RB of a cycle and segments where IdOB has not executed.  
**Why:** Give RB a stable TP view and an auditable history row without waiting for IdOB or combining multi-IdOB outputs. This is not the OuBA/SSRGn meaning-commit freeze.  
**Is not:** Post-IdOB consolidator; multi-IdOB merger; meaning constructor; scorer; content-mutation writer (TPU); OuBA meaning freeze; producer of `ready_for_ouba`.  
**Example:** `DCB → TR → CTP → RB → …` with Φ still ⊥ is legal: CTP still policy-freezes and appends history with nulls for missing IdOB sources.  
**Normative home:** 20.145_ctp_prim.md (HLR-20.145-028/029/030/008/022); 20.116.020 CTP row.
