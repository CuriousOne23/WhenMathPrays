# **20.116 — Path A Field Catalog (Authority)**
**Document ID:** 20.116  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** Normative — field-name / path / owner authority  
**Scope:** Canonical TP and conversation-layer field paths used by Path A structural programs, testbenches, and dual-mode validation

---

## 0. Authority rule

This series is the **20-series authority** for:

- canonical nested field **paths**
- **owner** of each envelope (who may write)
- **name-separation** (what two similar names must not be treated as the same object)

It is **not** the authority for primitive behavior. Behavior and SHALLs that define *what a primitive does* remain in the owning primitive document (20.105, 20.32, 20.40.050, 20.107.*, 20.108, CST children, etc.).

### Collision rule

| Collision type | Winner |
|----------------|--------|
| Path spelling, nesting, or owner of a named envelope | **20.116 series** |
| Primitive behavior, scheduling, formulas, thresholds | Owning primitive document |
| Playground dictionary vs 20-series | **20.116 series** |

`system_playground/design/pipeline/patha_field_names.md` remains a **derived working index**. It SHALL be updated to match this series; it SHALL NOT override it.

**HLR-20.116-001**  
Path A structural programs, testbenches, rulecheckers, and envelope-boundary guards SHALL resolve field paths against this series.

**HLR-20.116-002**  
No primitive SHALL invent a second envelope path for an object already named here.

**HLR-20.116-003**  
On a conflict between this series and a playground dictionary, this series SHALL prevail for names, paths, and write walls.

**HLR-20.116-004**  
On a conflict between this series and a primitive document over *behavior*, the primitive document SHALL prevail. The conflict SHALL be recorded as a catalog defect, not silently patched at runtime.

## 2. Non-goals

- No new field names invented in v0.1.
- No behavioral SHALLs copied from primitive law.
- No Path-B SSR field catalog (that remains 20.52 / 20.54 / 20.110).
- No flattening of nested TP paths into a second schema.

**HLR-20.116-005**  
All TP fields SHALL be emitted and consumed using canonical nested paths. Flattening is a primitive-local operation and SHALL NOT modify the TP envelope.

---

## 3. Source-of-truth stack (authoritative order)

When two 20-series files disagree on a *path or owner*:

1. This series (`20.116*`)
2. Envelope slot law: `20.105` / `20.105.010`
3. Owning primitive document
4. `20.15` (informative scaffold; no SHALLs)
5. Playground dictionaries and structural programs

**HLR-20.116-006**  
A structural program MAY list a subset of this catalog. It SHALL NOT list a path that this catalog forbids, and SHALL NOT claim ownership that this catalog assigns elsewhere.

## 5. How referencing documents SHALL point here

Existing primitive files keep their text. They receive a short **authority block** after frontmatter pointing at this series. That block does not replace their HLRs.

**HLR-20.116-007**  
Documents that name TP or conversation-layer envelopes listed in `20.116.010` SHALL treat this series as the path/owner authority and SHALL NOT introduce a parallel dictionary.

---

## 6. Change control

**HLR-20.116-008**  
Adding a field name, renaming a path, or changing an owner SHALL update this series in the same change set as the owning primitive document.

**HLR-20.116-009**  
V0.1 SHALL NOT invent names. New names require an explicit catalog amendment plus the owning primitive HLR.
