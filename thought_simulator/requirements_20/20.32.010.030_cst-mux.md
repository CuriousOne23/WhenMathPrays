# **20.32.010.030 — CST‑Mux Requirements**

**Context Stability Tracking — Stability Signal Multiplexing Module**

---

## **0. Document Purpose (Informative)**

This document defines the normative requirements for **CST‑Mux**, the Stability Signal Multiplexing Module in the CST pipeline. CST‑Mux receives stability signals from both CST‑Core and CST‑MS, organizes and aligns them, and packages them into the **Unified Stability Packet (USP)**.

CST‑Mux does not modify, reinterpret, or synthesize stability signals. It is a pure functional multiplexer whose sole purpose is to produce a deterministic, replay‑safe record of all CST activity for downstream consumption by CIL.

**Architectural Role (Informative)**  
- CST‑Mux receives signals from CST‑Core and CST‑MS only.  
- CST‑Mux does **not** receive any information from COB.  
- The USP is delivered **only to CIL** for replay reconstruction.  
- The USP is **never** sent to COB. COB receives its structural signals and commands directly from CST‑Core and CST‑MS.  
- This separation protects COB from any future packaging or compatibility changes that might be made for CIL or CEx.

CST‑Mux is a pure functional module:

- deterministic  
- replay‑safe  
- no randomness  
- no external state  
- no wall‑clock time  
- no structural authority  

---

# **1. Inputs and Outputs**

## **1.1 Inputs (Informative)**

CST‑Mux receives two classes of input:

**From CST‑Core**
- Raw metric signals: Drift, Oscillation, Ambiguity, Collapse  
- Structural signals: Freeze, Thaw, Continuity‑restoration  
- Layer ordering and activation state (when provided)

**From CST‑MS**
- Synthesized signals: stability summary, instability summary, collapse risk, freeze risk, thaw readiness, ambiguity summary, drift summary, oscillation summary  
- Structural command records (Freeze, Thaw, Collapse‑recovery, Create, Split, Merge) for logging  
- Synchronization‑mismatch diagnostic signals (when present)

CST‑Mux does not receive identity‑layer state, snapshots, or any other data from COB.

## **1.2 Input Requirements (Normative)**

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑001**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept all synthesized stability signals from CST‑MS for each identity layer.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑002**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept raw metric signals (Drift, Oscillation, Ambiguity, Collapse) from CST‑Core.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑003**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept structural signals (Freeze, Thaw, Continuity‑restoration) from CST‑Core.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑004**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept deterministic layer ordering and activation state.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑005**  
CST‑Mux SHALL accept structural command records and synchronization‑mismatch diagnostic signals from CST‑MS when present.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑006**  
CST‑Mux SHALL NOT accept any data, snapshots, or signals from COB.

---

## **1.3 Outputs (Informative)**

CST‑Mux produces a single output: the **Unified Stability Packet (USP)**.

The USP contains the aligned and indexed collection of all signals received from CST‑Core and CST‑MS for the current turn. The USP is delivered exclusively to CIL for deterministic replay reconstruction. The USP is never sent to COB.

## **1.4 Output Requirements (Normative)**

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑007**  
CST‑Mux SHALL produce a deterministic Unified Stability Packet (USP) for each turn *t*.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑008**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include in the USP all raw metric signals received from CST‑Core.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑009**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include in the USP all structural signals received from CST‑Core.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑010**  
CST‑Mux SHALL include in the USP all synthesized signals and command records received from CST‑MS.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑011**  
CST‑Mux SHALL deliver the USP exclusively to CIL.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑012**  
CST‑Mux SHALL NOT send the USP, or any derivative or modified form of the signals it contains, to COB.

---

# **2. Layer Indexing**

## **2.1 Layer Indexing (Informative)**

CST‑Mux assigns each identity layer a deterministic index so that all signals can be aligned and retrieved consistently during replay.

## **2.2 Layer Indexing Requirements (Normative)**

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑013**  
CST‑Mux SHALL assign each identity layer a deterministic index.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑014**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure layer indexing is stable across replay.

---

# **3. Signal Alignment and Packaging**

## **3.1 Alignment (Informative)**

CST‑Mux aligns every received signal by identity layer and packages them into the USP without alteration. Alignment exists solely to guarantee deterministic ordering and complete replay reconstruction.

## **3.2 Alignment Requirements (Normative)**

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑015**  
CST‑Mux SHALL align all received signals by identity layer.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑016**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure aligned signals maintain deterministic ordering.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑017**  
CST‑Mux SHALL ensure the packaged USP is replay‑safe.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑018**  
CST‑Mux SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, threshold, or synthesize any signal it receives.

---

# **4. Flags and Derived Indicators (Informative)**

CST‑Mux may include simple presence or status flags (activation, freeze, thaw, continuity) in the USP when those flags are supplied by CST‑Core or CST‑MS. These flags are recorded as received; CST‑Mux does not compute new control decisions from them.

---

# **5. Determinism and Replay**

## **5.1 Determinism (Informative)**

CST‑Mux is fully deterministic. Identical inputs from CST‑Core and CST‑MS always produce an identical USP. The USP exists so that CIL (and downstream replay) can reconstruct the exact stability context of any turn.

## **5.2 Determinism Requirements (Normative)**

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑019**  
CST‑Mux SHALL compute the USP as a pure function of the signals received from CST‑Core and CST‑MS.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑020**  
CST‑Mux SHALL guarantee replay‑safe behavior for the USP.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑021**  
CST‑Mux SHALL produce identical USP outputs under replay for identical inputs.

**HLR‑20.32.010.030‑022**  
CST‑Mux SHALL have no structural authority and SHALL NOT issue any commands to COB, CIL, or any other module.
