## TS-Level Invariants Summary
- **A/B Separation**: Path A constructs meaning deterministically; Path B performs realization only (read-only on committed meaning).
- **Envelope-Writer Rule**: TPU is the Path A content-mutation writer. Field-name authority 20.116.020 also authorizes bounded envelope writes: IdOB-prm (`TP.idob`, stand-in M / Φ), CTP-prm (`TP.metadata.cognitive_history[]`), MCB (`TP.next_context{}`). “TPU sole writer” does not erase those walls.
- **Determinism & Replay-Safety**: All Path A operations are fully deterministic and replayable. Nondeterminism is isolated to Path B expression via the Random Seed Boundary.
- **Monotonic Entropy Reduction**: Cue OB layers (SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB) reduce structural uncertainty; ΔH% tracks incremental changes (see 20.710).
- **Cue vs Birth (INV-M0)**: SmOB and prior OBs emit cues in E. Meaning-birth B and Φ writes (`selected_group_id`, `meaning_semantics`) are IdOB-prm only. Rich E is not birth. See the cut in 20.12.010 / 20.700.050.
- **Two freezes**: CTP-prm applies a policy freeze immediately before every RB (`TR→CTP→RB`). OuBA / SSRGn apply the Path A meaning-commit freeze. `ready_for_ouba` is not OuBA freeze. CTP is the 20.145 primitive only; OuBA commit is not called CTP.
- **Governance Gates**: TPTB, TPSF, and GBIB operate only on committed meaning from OuBA.
- **Provenance & Traceability**: All OB structures must carry complete provenance (see 20.710 normative structures).
- **Pluggability**: Rulesets and routing policies are swappable without violating structural invariants.
