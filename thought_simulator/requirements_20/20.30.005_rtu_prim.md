# **20.30.005 — RTU‑prm (Routing TP Update Primitive)**  
**Version:** 2.1 (IdOB neighbor hygiene + official Path A position)  
**Status:** Normative — Pipeline A Routing‑Layer Primitive  
**Purpose:** Construct deterministic routing_update metadata for TR‑prm and the scheduler.

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

RTU‑prm is the **first routing‑layer primitive** in Pipeline A.  
It constructs **routing_update**, a deterministic routing‑metadata object consumed by TR‑prm and the scheduler.

RTU‑prm:

- performs **no semantic work**  
- performs **no truth evaluation**  
- performs **no safety evaluation**  
- performs **no merge or commit work**  
- does **not** write to TP or MTP  
- does **not** interpret meaning  
- does **not** arbitrate lanes  
- does **not** modify lineage  
- does **not** invent `pack_ids[]`  
- does **not** write `tp.idob` or `meaning_delta_h`

RTU‑prm is a **pure routing‑signal constructor**.

Official Path A inner stretch that includes RTU (informative; does not change RTU’s job):

```
WrdNm → ISc → RTU → TR → CTP → RB → IdOB → MCB → RBU
```

RTU sits **before** the RB commit that IdOB treats as a read-only routing view. Isolation fixtures may start at IdOB without a live RTU pass; that does not authorize RTU to interpret meaning when the lineup is live.

IdOB may later **read** routing state as a view (20.40.050). RTU does not consume `tp.idob` to build `routing_update`.

---

# **1. Scope (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑001**  
RTU‑prm SHALL construct a routing_update object containing only routing‑layer fields required by TR‑prm and the scheduler.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑002**  
routing_update SHALL contain no semantic, truth, safety, merge, or commit‑layer fields.

---

# **2. Routing Metadata Construction (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑003**  
routing_update SHALL include lane_activation signals indicating which interpretive lanes are eligible for execution.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑004**  
routing_update SHALL include lane_suppression signals indicating which lanes must not be executed in the current cycle.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑005**  
routing_update SHALL include routing_reason codes describing why each lane was activated or suppressed.

---

# **3. Determinism & Replay Safety (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑006**  
RTU‑prm SHALL produce routing_update deterministically from the user request and prior `TP.routing_metadata`.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑007**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT depend on global state, stochastic processes, or latent‑space sampling.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑008**  
Identical inputs SHALL produce identical routing_update outputs.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑025 — TP Field Protection**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify:

- `TP.semantic`  
- `TP.process`  
- `TP.metadata`  
- `TP.context`  
- `TP.intake`  
- `TP.TR`  
- `TP.ssg_signature`  
- `TP.ssg_layer_bitmap`  
- `TP.ssg_reason_code`  
- `TP.ssg_status`  
- `TP.presemantic_cues`  
- `TP.tr_input_cues`  
- `tp.idob` / `TP.idob`  
- `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`  
- `pack_ids[]` / pack-load keys owned by COB / CEx / CIL  

RTU‑prm produces routing_update **only**; it does not write to TP.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑009**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify `semantic_core`.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑010**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify `truth_hypotheses`.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑011**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify TPTB or TPSF.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑012**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify ΔH% or H%.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑013**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify merge_result or update_structure.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑014**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT modify any MTP‑level field.

---

# **5. Routing Signal Semantics (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑015**  
lane_activation SHALL be computed using only routing‑relevant features of the user request and prior TP routing metadata.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑016**  
lane_suppression SHALL be computed when a lane is structurally incompatible with the request or violates routing constraints.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑017**  
If lane_activation and lane_suppression conflict, suppression SHALL take precedence.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑018**  
routing_update SHALL include a routing_confidence scalar in \([0,1]\) indicating routing stability.  
This value is informative to TR‑prm and is **not** a probability.

---

# **6. Interaction with TR‑prm & Scheduler (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑019**  
RTU‑prm SHALL expose routing_update to TR‑prm without requiring TR‑prm to recompute routing signals.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑020**  
routing_update SHALL be complete and self‑contained for scheduling decisions.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑021**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT invoke TR‑prm, OB‑prm, TB‑prm, RBU‑prm, or any interpretive or commit primitive.

---

# **7. Error & Constraint Handling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑022**  
RTU‑prm SHALL detect structurally invalid routing metadata and replace it with a deterministic fallback routing_update.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑023**  
RTU‑prm SHALL log routing_error codes when fallback routing is used.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑024**  
Fallback routing SHALL preserve safety by suppressing unsafe or undefined lanes.

---

# **8. Mathematical Notes (Informative)**

Routing confidence is a structural stability indicator:

\[
routing_{confidence} = 1 - \frac{\text{num_suppressed_lanes}}{\text{total_lanes}}
\]

It is a heuristic, not a probability, and has no semantic or truth‑evaluation meaning.

---

# **9. Outputs (Normative)**

RTU‑prm SHALL produce a **routing_update** object containing:

- lane_activation  
- lane_suppression  
- routing_reason codes  
- routing_confidence  
- routing_error (optional)

No other fields are permitted.

# **11. IdOB / pack neighbor hygiene (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑026 — Meaning-packet protection**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT write `tp.idob`, `TP.idob`, `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`, `TP.semantic.meaning_semantics[]`, CIE rows, or six structure IDs.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑027 — Pack keys**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT invent, load, or rewrite `pack_ids[]`. Pack keys are owned by the continuity / CEx-Pck contract (20.32 / 20.107.030 / 20.33). If `pack_ids[]` is present on the TP, RTU‑prm SHALL leave it unchanged.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑028 — Official Path A position**  
On a live Path A cycle that includes identity-conditioned meaning, RTU‑prm SHALL occupy the slot after ISc and before TR in the stretch `WrdNm → ISc → RTU → TR → CTP → RB → IdOB`. RTU‑prm SHALL NOT be specified as running after IdOB on that stretch.

### **HLR‑20.30.005‑029 — No IdOB interpretation**  
RTU‑prm SHALL NOT read `tp.idob` as an input required to construct `routing_update`. IdOB may later read committed routing as a view (20.40.050); that read does not authorize RTU to score meaning axes, apply CIE, or expand residue into a next six-tuple.
