# 20.110.010 OuBB ↔ Stack Manifold Integration

**Document ID:** 20.110.010  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026-06-25 
**Status:** Approved v0.3

## Purpose

Defines the **integration contract** between **OuBB** (Output Basin B expression) and the Pipeline B execution manifold (`exec_plan`, XP, OpBeh/OBG/XlateR). This document **does not duplicate** [20.110](20.110_oubb_requirements.md) — it hardens dual-pipeline boundaries and XP/`exec_trace` binding for the B-phase expression stage.

**OQ-004 resolution:** 20.110.010 = integration layer; [20.110](20.110_oubb_requirements.md) v0.2 (approved) holds expression semantics — this document hardens B-integration boundaries only, not a parallel duplicate module.

## 1. Dual-Pipeline Expression Stage

```text
exec_plan (triple + expression_plan_ref) + frozen SSR
       → OuBB (seed-bound expression selection)
       → oubb_artifact_ref in exec_trace
       → IMR evaluation
```

1. **HLR-20.110.010-001:** OuBB in Pipeline B SHALL execute only after `exec_plan` commit for the active `commit_id` / XP pass.
2. **HLR-20.110.010-002:** OuBB SHALL read `SSR` only via `semantic_snapshot_ref` frozen at the Path-A commit.
3. **HLR-20.110.010-003:** OuBB SHALL consume `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, and `expression_plan_ref` from `exec_plan` — not from any Pipeline-A structure.

---

## 2. Writes and Artifacts

```text
oubb_artifact_ref {
  artifact_id,
  commit_id,
  cycle_id,
  xp_id,
  exec_plan_ref,
  seed_scope_ref,
  register_rendered?,
  content_hash,
  oubb_reason_codes[]
}
```

4. **HLR-20.110.010-004:** OuBB SHALL write expression artifacts only to `exec_trace` (append-only) and SHALL NOT write `SSR` or `exec_plan` body fields (except read).
5. **HLR-20.110.010-005:** Every OuBB invocation SHALL append `oubb_artifact_ref` to `exec_trace` and MAY denormalize latest ref on XP per 20.205 D7.
6. **HLR-20.110.010-006:** OuBB SHALL NOT write Pipleline-A fields in dual-pipeline mode; legacy 20.110 TP tagging paths SHALL be disabled or remapped to `exec_trace` audit per [20.110](20.110_oubb_requirements.md) v0.2 dual-pipeline posture.

---

## 3. Seed Boundary

7. **HLR-20.110.010-007:** Seed SHALL affect OuBB expression selection only (HLR-20.012-010, 20.110-002).
8. **HLR-20.110.010-008:** Identical `exec_plan` + frozen `SSR` + seed → identical `oubb_artifact_ref` content hash.

---

## 4. Messy-Input and Presentation

9. **HLR-20.110.010-009:** OuBB SHALL NOT clear `messy_input_record` flags or smooth `conflict_flag` in `SSR` (20.17, 20.31-018).
10. **HLR-20.110.010-010:** OuBB presentation under `underspec_flag` or `conflict_flag` SHALL follow OBG/register policy without inventing resolved meaning.

---

## 5. IMR Handoff

11. **HLR-20.110.010-011:** IMR SHALL consume `oubb_artifact_ref` from `exec_trace` for the same `commit_id` / XP pass (20.45).
12. **HLR-20.110.010-012:** OuBB SHALL NOT emit `CorrectionTrigger` directly — IMR owns taxonomy classification.

---

## 6. Incomplete-Meaning (20.110 v0.2 Alignment)

Legacy 20.110 HLR-20.110-005–009 reference TP tagging and IB pathways. **Approved v0.2 posture:**

13. **HLR-20.110.010-013:** In dual-pipeline PoC, incomplete-meaning detection SHALL log to `exec_trace` with `oub_event_type = INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED` and SHALL schedule GB/IB pathways only through supervisory queue — not SSR mutation.
14. **HLR-20.110.010-014:** [20.110](20.110_oubb_requirements.md) v0.2 reconciles legacy TP-centric HLRs with this integration contract without weakening [20.105](20.105_tp_requirements.md) TP boundary.

---

## Verification

15. **HLR-20.110.010-015:** Class 1 fixture: OuBB stage after `exec_plan_commit`; artifact in `exec_trace`; strip leaves `SSR` unchanged.
16. **HLR-20.110.010-016:** Seed variant changes artifact hash only; `SSR` strip-identical.
17. **HLR-20.110.010-017:** No `lane_id` on OuBB inputs/outputs.
18. **HLR-20.110.010-018:** XP `EXPRESSING` → `IMR_EVAL` transition requires `oubb_artifact_ref` present.
