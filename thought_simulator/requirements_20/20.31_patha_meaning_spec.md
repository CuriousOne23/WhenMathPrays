# 20.31_patha_meaning_spec.md (v1.0 — Path A Meaning Specification)

---

## 0. Introduction

This document defines the **meaning structures and behaviors used inside Pipeline A** of the Thought Simulator (TS). Pipeline A is responsible for meaning construction, truth evaluation, epistemic change tracking, and meaning tagging. All meanings defined here are **TP‑local**, deterministic, and non‑global.

Pipeline B meanings are **explicitly out of scope**.

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of 20.31 is to specify:

- TP‑local meaning fields  
- OB/TB/IB meaning behavior  
- $\Delta H\%$ computation  
- messy‑input handling  
- completion meanings  
- deterministic replay invariants  

All meanings defined here exist **only inside TP and OuBA** and are **not stored globally**.

---

## 2. Scope

This specification defines **Pipeline A meaning structures only**:

- TP meaning fields  
- OB/TB/IB behavior  
- epistemic change ($\Delta H\%$) to H%
- truth‑evidence meanings  
- completion state meanings  
- messy‑input meanings  
- deterministic replay rules  

**Excluded:**

- Pipeline B meanings  
- SSR (Meaning Snapshot)  
- `meaning_core`  
- commit meanings  
- global meaning stores  
- Path‑A→B handoff  

---

## 3. TP Meaning Structures

Meaning in Pipeline A is represented through a **single unified TP meaning stream**.  
This stream is the sole meaning structure inside TP and is committed to OuBA at the end of the cycle.  
There is **no global meaning store**.

The unified TP meaning stream contains **three deterministic fields**:

- **semantics/meaning** — dictionary‑level identity, interpretation, and meaning construction  
- **processes** — flow‑contract operations applied during OB/TB/IB meaning behavior  
- **supervisor** — reference‑object and field‑reference‑table dependencies required for deterministic replay

These three fields replace all prior TP meaning subfields (propositions, truth evidence, messy input, tags, H%, completion, etc.).  
All meaning behaviors (OB, TB, IB, messy‑input handling, ΔH%, completion) operate **inside the unified meaning stream**, not as separate TP fields.

---

### 3.1 TP Unified Meaning Stream

Each TP contains **one unified meaning stream** with the following fields:

- **semantics/meaning**  
- **processes**  
- **supervisor**

These fields are **local to the cycle** and do not persist beyond TP → OuBA commit.  
All meaning behaviors update these fields deterministically.

---

## 4. OB Meanings (Observation Basis)

OB primitives operate exclusively on the **TP unified meaning stream**.  
OB behavior updates the **processes** field of the stream by contributing:

- observational interpretations  
- evidence transformations  
- epistemic‑change contributions (ΔH%)  
- messy‑input interpretations  
- OB‑specific semantic refinements  

All OB outputs are written **only** into the TP meaning stream and never into global state.

Crossing-packet meaning birth is not a general OB family write. IdOB-prm is the sole writer of stand-in M, `TP.idob`, `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`, and `TP.semantic.importance`. SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB may keep local envelope importance only; those local residues are not `TP.semantic.importance`.

**HLR‑20.31‑001 — OB Determinism**  
OB primitives **SHALL** produce identical updates to the TP meaning stream given identical TP input and seed.

**HLR‑20.31‑010 — IdOB-prm sole crossing-packet meaning writer**  
IdOB-prm **SHALL** be the sole writer of stand-in M, `TP.idob`, `TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h`, and `TP.semantic.importance`. SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB **SHALL NOT** write those crossing-packet fields. Local envelope importance retained by SOB / SROB / CnOB / SmOB **SHALL NOT** be treated as `TP.semantic.importance`.

## 5. TB Meanings (Truth Basis)

TB evaluates truth‑related aspects of the TP unified meaning stream by updating the **processes** field with:

- plausibility assessments  
- contradiction markers  
- truth‑evidence transformations  
- completion‑related signals  

TB does not write to any global store and does not create standalone TP fields.  
All TB behavior is expressed as deterministic updates to the meaning stream.

**HLR‑20.31‑002 — Truth Evaluation Determinism**  
Truth evaluation **SHALL** operate only on the TP unified meaning stream and **SHALL** be deterministic.

Inline update rule (applied inside the meaning‑stream `processes` field):

$$
T_{\text{new}} = T_{\text{old}} + \Delta T
$$

---

## 6. IB Meanings (Inquiry Basin)

IB is created only with governance approval and performs deeper inquiry by updating the **processes** field of the TP unified meaning stream.  
IB contributes:

- recursive evidence gathering  
- structured inquiry transformations  
- deeper semantic refinement  

IB operates strictly within the TP meaning stream and produces TP‑local updates only.

**HLR‑20.31‑003 — IB Isolation**  
IB **SHALL NOT** write to any global structure and **SHALL** update only the TP unified meaning stream.

---

## 7. H% Meanings

H% tracks epistemic change **inside the TP unified meaning stream**.  
OB and TB contribute ΔH% updates to the **processes** field, which maintains the epistemic‑change accumulator for the cycle.

**HLR‑20.31‑004 — H% Update Rule**  
H% **SHALL** be computed deterministically inside the TP meaning‑stream `processes` field:

$$
H\\%_{\text{final}} = H\\%_{\text{initial}} + \sum_i \Delta H\\%_{\text{OB}_i}
$$

No standalone `h_percent` field exists.

---

## 8. Completion Meanings

Completion is derived entirely from the TP unified meaning stream.  
TB contributes completion‑related signals to the **processes** field, which determines whether meaning construction is:

- incomplete  
- partially complete  
- complete  
- complete‑with‑uncertainty  

**HLR‑20.31‑005 — Completion Determinism**  
Completion **SHALL** be derived solely from updates inside the TP meaning‑stream `processes` field.

No standalone `completion_state` field exists.

---

## 9. Messy‑Input Meanings

Messy input is tracked only inside the TP unified meaning stream.  
OB contributes messy‑input interpretations to the **processes** field, which records:

- malformed structures  
- ambiguous phrasing  
- contradictory cues  
- incomplete evidence  

Messy input does not propagate beyond TP → OuBA commit.

**HLR‑20.31‑006 — Messy Input Record**  
Messy‑input tracking **SHALL** occur only inside the TP meaning‑stream `processes` field.

No standalone `messy_input_record` field exists.

---

## 10. Replay Invariants

Replay determinism applies to the entire TP unified meaning stream.  
Given identical TP input and identical seed, the **semantics**, **processes**, and **supervisor** fields MUST produce identical updates.

**HLR‑20.31‑007 — Replay Determinism**  

$$
x_1 = x_2 \ \land\ s_1 = s_2
\Rightarrow
\text{Replay}(x_1, s_1) = \text{Replay}(x_2, s_2)
$$

Replay does not operate on standalone meaning fields because none exist.

---

## 11. Policy Meanings

Policy markers operate exclusively inside the TP unified meaning stream.  
They update the **processes** field to enforce:

- safety  
- routing  
- inquiry limits  
- overflow behavior  

**HLR‑20.31‑008 — Policy Determinism**  
Policy evaluation **SHALL** be deterministic and **SHALL** update only the TP meaning‑stream `processes` field.

No standalone policy fields exist.

---

## 12. Overflow and Stability Meanings

Overflow behavior is expressed as deterministic updates to the TP unified meaning stream.  
Overflow triggers:

- bounded evidence truncation  
- deterministic degradation  
- overflow tagging  

All overflow effects modify the **processes** field only.

**HLR‑20.31‑009 — Overflow Determinism**  
Overflow **SHALL** produce deterministic, auditable updates inside the TP meaning‑stream `processes` field.

No standalone overflow fields exist.

---

## 13. Summary

Pipeline A meaning construction is expressed entirely through the **unified TP meaning stream**, which contains three deterministic fields:

- **semantics/meaning** — dictionary‑level identity and interpretation  
- **processes** — flow‑contract operations updated by OB/TB/IB, H%, completion, messy‑input handling, policy, and overflow behaviors  
- **supervisor** — reference‑object and field‑reference‑table dependencies required for deterministic replay  

TP is the **sole meaning container** in Pipeline A, and OuBA is the **only meaning output**.  
No global meaning store exists.  
All meaning behaviors are deterministic, TP‑local, and replay-stable.  
Pipeline B meanings remain explicitly out of scope.
