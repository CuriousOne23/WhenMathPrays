**Semantic-Adjacent Object Base (SmOB)**  
**Document ID:** 20.40.040  
**Version:** 2.0 (Bounded-Semantics Realization Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative  
**Parent:** 20.40_ob_requirements.md  
**Scope:** Requirements for the Semantic-Adjacent OB (SmOB) layer in Path A  
**Purpose:** Define mandatory behavior, invariants, owned-field writes, and bounded semantic-adjacent cue/compression rules for the fourth OB layer  
**Aligned with:** 20.40.010 (SOB), 20.40.020 (SROB), 20.40.030 (CnOB), 20.15, 20.105 / 20.105.010 / 20.105.020 / 20.105.030, tp_path_a_map.md, tp_context_layer.md

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

SmOB is the **fourth OB layer** in Path A and the **boundary object** between the OB residue pipeline and the SSG signal pipeline.  
It performs **two distinct, required jobs**, each simple, bounded, and architecturally separate so routing remains deterministic and implementation does not drift into meaning-layer work.

SmOB **may** perform **bounded, deterministic, domain-limited semantic-adjacent interpretation** of structural, constraint, and semantic-adjacent cues already present on the TP (especially CnOB fields, plus SROB/SOB when needed) when required to form pre-semantic cues, TR-input vectors, and compressed residue with high confidence for SSG, RB, and identity-conditioned selection.

SmOB does **not** evaluate truth, assign conversational stance as a meaning conclusion, resolve referent identity, write free-form meaning propositions, or act as a generative/smoothing reconstruction layer. SmOB writes only SmOB-owned fields.

---

## **Job 1 — Pre-Semantic Cue Extraction** *(Informative)*

SmOB extracts semantic-adjacent cues from upstream residue (SOB → SROB → CnOB), including:

- semantic-adjacent pattern activations  
- modality cues  
- affect markers  
- conflict-adjacent signals  
- underspecification-adjacent signals  
- constraint-importance-adjacent signals  
- TR-input cues  

These cues are **added into SmOB-owned TP fields** to support:

- identity OB selection  
- TR routing (TP.TR formation)  
- semantic-adjacent change attribution  
- semantic-layer **preparation** without deep meaning resolution  

---

## **Job 2 — Pre-Semantic Residue Compression** *(Informative)*

SmOB compresses upstream residue plus its own cues into:

- a deterministic pre-semantic residue hash  
- a TR-input cue vector  
- semantic-adjacent change / ΔH%-aligned signals derived from allowed SmOB fields (not forbidden routing envelopes)  

This provides SSG with a **fixed-size, canonical, replay-safe** representation for deterministic routing.

---

# **2. Functional Overview** *(Informative)*

SmOB:

- accepts CnOB constraint residue and related structural/semantic-adjacent metadata (preferring CnOB; may read SROB/SOB when progressive lineup requires)
- extracts pre-semantic cues under deterministic, bounded rules (Job 1)
- compresses residue into hash + TR-input vector (Job 2)
- may read applicable CE/CEx and TP-stream metadata read-only as structural or semantic-adjacent cues
- emits SmOB-owned fields consumable by SSG, RB, and IdOB
- may emit diagnostic-only metadata for developer playback
- should reduce pre-semantic ambiguity relative to raw CnOB consumption when measurable (visibility goal, not a hard HLR)

SmOB is part of the OB family whose purpose is to **digest its domain of the cue/residue space into rigid tags** so SSG and primary meaning stages are not overwhelmed.

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **3.1 Two Jobs (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-011**
SmOB Job 1 (pre-semantic cue extraction) SHALL be implemented as a distinct phase that adds semantic-adjacent cues into SmOB-owned TP fields.

### **HLR-20.40.040-012**
SmOB Job 2 (pre-semantic residue compression) SHALL be implemented as a distinct phase that produces a deterministic pre-semantic residue hash and TR-input cue vector.

---

## **3.2 Inputs and TP-Stream Discipline**

### **HLR-20.40.040-013**
SmOB SHALL accept refined structural TP, refined residue fragments, refined structural metadata, refined structural-importance metadata, and constraint-level residue produced by CnOB.

### **HLR-20.40.040-014** *(Rewritten — forbidden → bounded)*
SmOB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — in a read-only manner and MAY use them as structural or semantic-adjacent cues for cue extraction. SmOB SHALL NOT perform deep meaning resolution, intent inference, truth evaluation, or referent identity resolution on these fields.

### **HLR-20.40.040-015 — Structural Discourse-Cue Normalization**
SmOB SHALL normalize discourse-context structural flags propagated from CnOB — including recent-entity continuity, temporal-shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical pre-semantic cue metadata under bounded, deterministic rules.

### **HLR-20.40.040-016 — Structural Discourse-Cue Extraction** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL encode normalized discourse-context metadata strictly as **pre-semantic / semantic-adjacent cues** in SmOB-owned fields. SmOB SHALL NOT infer conversational stance as a meaning conclusion, assign semantic roles, resolve referent identity, or evaluate truth when encoding discourse cues. Bounded cue classification is permitted.

### **HLR-20.40.040-017 — Deterministic & Bounded Discourse-Adjacent Cues**
SmOB SHALL ensure discourse-adjacent cues are deterministic, bounded, reproducible for identical CnOB inputs, and compliant with canonical ordering requirements in HLR-20.40.040-003.

### **HLR-20.40.040-018 — Routing-Eligible Discourse-Adjacent Cues**
SmOB SHALL expose discourse-adjacent cues as part of the TR-input cue vector and pre-semantic residue, ensuring they are consumable by SSG and RB without modification of SmOB-owned content by those consumers.

### **HLR-20.40.040-019 — TP-Stream Metadata Consumption**
SmOB SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP-stream metadata when such metadata improves semantic-adjacent cue extraction or pre-semantic residue compression.

### **HLR-20.40.040-020 — Read-Only Discipline** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL treat upstream TP-stream metadata and non-SmOB-owned fields as read-only. SmOB SHALL NOT modify upstream fields; SmOB SHALL write only SmOB-owned fields as defined in HLR-20.40.040-030.

### **HLR-20.40.040-021 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
SmOB SHALL consume TP-stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to semantic-adjacent cue extraction or residue compression and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR-20.40.040-022 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
SmOB SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline-B envelopes. SmOB SHALL NOT consume ΔH% **routing envelopes**; semantic-adjacent change signals may still be computed from allowed SmOB/upstream structural and cue fields under deterministic rules.

### **HLR-20.40.040-023 — Bounded Interpretation of Consumed Metadata** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL treat all consumed TP-stream metadata as structural or semantic-adjacent features. SmOB MAY perform bounded, deterministic, semantic-adjacent-domain interpretation of those features. SmOB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, truth evaluation, or intent inference outside its defined domain. All SmOB interpretation of consumed metadata SHALL remain bounded, deterministic, and reproducible.

### **HLR-20.40.040-024 — Cue-Residue Integration**
SmOB SHALL encode any consumed TP-stream metadata as semantic-adjacent cues or pre-semantic residue fragments in SmOB-owned fields when such metadata affects cue extraction or residue compression.

---

## **3.2.1 TP-Stream Metadata Consumption** *(Informative)*

SmOB may consume deterministic, bounded, pre-semantic or semantic-adjacent metadata present in the TP-stream when applicable to cue extraction or residue compression. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX) when present and applicable as cues only
- identity_metadata (IdOB) when present and applicable as structural/cue inputs only
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST) when present and applicable
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)

SmOB treats non-owned TP-stream metadata as read-only.  
SmOB ignores metadata that is not applicable.  
SmOB does not consume routing_metadata, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or Pipeline-B envelopes.  
SmOB interprets TP-stream metadata only as structural or semantic-adjacent features under bounded rules.

---

## **3.3 SmOB Definition**

SmOB is a deterministic object performing:

- **Job 1:** extraction of pre-semantic / semantic-adjacent cues  
- **Job 2:** compression of upstream residue + SmOB cues  

SmOB contains (normative field families; wire schema may be refined in software architecture):

- `semantic_adjacent_cues`  
- `modality_cues`  
- `affect_markers`  
- `conflict_adjacent_signals`  
- `underspecification_adjacent_signals`  
- `constraint_importance_adjacent_signals`  
- `presemantic_residue_hash`  
- `tr_input_cues`  
- `delta_h_semantic_adjacent` (from allowed fields; not forbidden routing envelopes)  
- `routing_semantic_cues`  

### **HLR-20.40.040-001** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL contain only semantic-adjacent cues and pre-semantic residue derived from CnOB (and other allowed structural/semantic-adjacent inputs). SmOB SHALL NOT contain free-form meaning propositions, truth claims, or stance conclusions. Bounded semantic-adjacent cue classification within SmOB’s domain is permitted.

---

## **3.4 Deterministic Pre-Semantic Extraction (Job 1)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-002**
SmOB extraction SHALL be deterministic for identical CnOB inputs, producing identical semantic-adjacent cues across all executions.

---

## **3.5 Canonical Ordering**

### **HLR-20.40.040-003**
SmOB SHALL apply canonical ordering to all fields, including nested cue structures, ensuring deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

## **3.6 Pre-Semantic Residue Extraction (Job 1)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-004** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL extract pre-semantic residue and semantic-adjacent cues from CnOB under deterministic, bounded rules. SmOB SHALL NOT perform unconstrained semantic smoothing, free-form reconstruction of missing meaning, truth evaluation, or generative filling of content.

---

## **3.7 TR-Input Cue Formation**

### **HLR-20.40.040-005**
SmOB SHALL derive TR-input cues deterministically from semantic-adjacent fields and SHALL ensure these cues are stable across replay.

---

## **3.8 Semantic-Adjacent Change Integration**

### **HLR-20.40.040-006** *(Rewritten for envelope discipline)*
SmOB SHALL compute semantic-adjacent change signals deterministically from **allowed SmOB and upstream structural/cue fields** and SHALL ensure consistency with 20.12 invariants and 20.47 SSG requirements where applicable. SmOB SHALL NOT read forbidden routing_metadata or Pipeline-B ΔH% envelopes to do so (see HLR-20.40.040-022).

---

## **3.9 Pre-Semantic Residue Hashing (Job 2)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-007**
SmOB SHALL compute a deterministic pre-semantic residue hash from SmOB fields and SHALL ensure identical inputs produce identical hashes.

---

## **3.10 Routing-Relevant Semantic-Adjacent Cues**

### **HLR-20.40.040-008** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL include routing-relevant semantic-adjacent cues exactly as defined in 20.47. Those cues SHALL remain inside the semantic-adjacent domain and SHALL NOT require deep meaning resolution, truth evaluation, or intent inference outside SmOB’s defined rules.

---

## **3.11 SmOB as Sole Pre-Semantic Input to SSG**

### **HLR-20.40.040-009**
SSG SHALL receive SmOB as its sole pre-semantic input and SHALL NOT accept additional semantic or supervisory fields outside the Path-A contract. *(Normative consumer duty; retained here for Path-A chain clarity.)*

---

## **3.12 Bounded Behavior and Owned Fields** *(New / rewritten block)*

### **HLR-20.40.040-030** *(New — owned-field write discipline)*
SmOB SHALL write only SmOB-owned fields (including semantic-adjacent cues, TR-input cue vector, pre-semantic residue hash, semantic-adjacent importance cues, change signals, and SmOB audit/diagnostic records). SmOB SHALL NOT overwrite SOB-, SROB-, or CnOB-owned fields, meaning-layer fields, Context Frame fields, MSL, identity state, freeze signatures, or routing_metadata.

### **HLR-20.40.040-031** *(New — bounded semantic-adjacent domain)*
SmOB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, truth evaluation, intent inference, or referent identity resolution outside its defined semantic-adjacent domain. SmOB MAY perform bounded, deterministic structural and semantic-adjacent interpretation when required for Job 1 cue extraction, Job 2 compression, discourse-cue encoding, or semantic-adjacent importance refinement from CnOB-presented residue.

### **HLR-20.40.040-032** *(New — no meaning-layer writes)*
SmOB SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning-layer fields. Cues and compressed residue SHALL remain confined to SmOB-owned fields.

### **HLR-20.40.040-033** *(New — diagnostic metadata)*
SmOB MAY write diagnostic-only metadata intended solely for developer playback and analysis. Diagnostic-only metadata SHALL NOT be required reading for any other Path-A primitive and SHALL NOT affect routing or meaning decisions.

### **HLR-20.40.040-034** *(New — CnOB field preference)*
When both earlier OB fields and CnOB fields are present, SmOB SHALL prefer CnOB-owned constraint residue and related cues as the primary input for Job 1 and Job 2. Earlier OB fields MAY be used only when required by progressive-lineup isolation or explicit applicability rules.

---

## **3.13 SmOB Is Not** *(Informative)*

SmOB is **not**:

- a deep meaning / full semantic interpreter  
- a truth evaluator  
- a stance assigner (as a meaning conclusion)  
- a free-form semantic normalizer of content  
- a generative mechanism  
- a smoothing or reconstruction layer that invents missing meaning  

SmOB **is** a bounded semantic-adjacent cue and compression layer.

---

## **3.14 Semantic-Adjacent Importance**

### **HLR-20.40.040-025**
SmOB SHALL refine constraint-importance residues emitted by CnOB into **semantic-adjacent importance cues**, representing importance signals relevant to semantic-adjacent routing, change attribution, and identity-conditioned cue selection.

### **HLR-20.40.040-026** *(Rewritten)*
SmOB SHALL compute semantic-adjacent importance values using deterministic rules based solely on structural, constraint-adjacent, and semantic-adjacent cues. SmOB SHALL NOT perform semantic-role assignment or referent resolution when computing these values.

### **HLR-20.40.040-027** *(Rewritten — entropy demoted)*
SmOB SHALL ensure semantic-adjacent importance cues remain confined to SmOB-owned fields and SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning-layer fields. Entropy reduction for these cues is a development visibility goal, not a separate hard SHALL.

### **HLR-20.40.040-028**
SmOB SHALL ensure semantic-adjacent importance cues remain addressable by RB and consumable by SSG and IdOB.

### **HLR-20.40.040-029**
SmOB SHALL ensure semantic-adjacent importance cues are monotonic and reproducible for identical CnOB inputs.

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-010**
SmOB SHALL ensure each pre-semantic residue fragment $r_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i'$:

$$
h_i' = \mathrm{hash}(r_i')
$$

### **HLR-20.40.040-035** *(Math combined-address; was colliding number in prior draft)*
SmOB SHALL ensure the combined pre-semantic query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h_1' \oplus h_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h_n'
$$

### **HLR-20.40.040-036**
SmOB SHALL ensure pre-semantic residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature-invariance.

---

# **5. Outputs (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.040-037** *(Rewritten — owned fields; was number-colliding with input 013)*
SmOB SHALL output, in SmOB-owned fields:

- semantic-adjacent cues  
- modality cues  
- affect markers  
- conflict-adjacent signals  
- underspecification-adjacent cues  
- semantic-adjacent importance cues  
- pre-semantic residue hash  
- TR-input cue vector  
- semantic-adjacent change signals  
- routing-semantic cues  
- SmOB audit record (and optional diagnostic-only metadata per HLR-20.40.040-033)  

### **HLR-20.40.040-038**
SmOB output SHALL be valid input for SSG and RB.
