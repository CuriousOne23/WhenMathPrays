# **20.40.055 — MCB‑prm (Meaning–Clarifying Bridge Primitive)**  
**Document ID:** 20.40.055  
**Version:** 2.1 (Official Path A position + IdOB packet boundary)  
**Status:** Normative — OB‑Family Meaning‑Layer Primitive  
**Purpose:** Meaning–clarifying reconciliation and next‑turn context generation  

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

MCB‑prm is the **Meaning–Clarifying Bridge** in the OB‑family.  
It operates **after IdOB‑prm** and **before RBU‑prm**.

Official Path A inner stretch (informative):

```
WrdNm → ISc → RTU → TR → CTP → RB → IdOB → MCB → RBU
```

The predecessor list is **not** `RB → RTU → IdOB`. RTU and RB complete **before** IdOB; MCB consumes the committed IdOB hop.

Its purpose is to:

- relate meaning‑layer interpretation to clarifying‑field statements  
- detect reinforcement or conflict between meaning and clarifying fields  
- determine context coherence  
- determine whether the next message continues or shifts context  
- generate meaning‑layer deltas **owned by MCB** (`mcb_delta_h`, `mcb_semantics[]`)  
- **generate next‑turn clarifying/context fields** for COB  
- preserve immutability of current‑turn clarifying fields  
- prepare the TP for RBU commit  
- **read** IdOB’s crossing packet `tp.idob` (utterance, pack_ids, CIE, residue_code, meaning stand-in) without re-running map/rank or re-birthing $M$

MCB‑prm performs **no structural extraction**, **no routing**, **no commit**, and **no mutation of current‑turn clarifying fields**.

MCB‑prm is the **only primitive** allowed to read both:

- meaning‑layer fields (IdOB output, including `tp.idob`)  
- clarifying‑fields (COB/CIL/CEx/CE/ISc/TPU output)

MCB‑prm is the **only primitive** allowed to generate **next‑turn clarifying/context fields**.

**Name split (do not trade places):**

| Slot | Owner | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| CIE / `cie_id` / $\alpha I$ | IdOB this hop | Stance pressure on the speaker-projection stand-in $M$ |
| `TP.next_context.stance` / `next_context_stance` | MCB next turn | COB hint for the following message |
| `meaning_delta_h` / `tp.idob` | IdOB | Crossing instrument |
| `mcb_delta_h` / `mcb_semantics[]` | MCB | Clarifying–meaning refinement instrument |

Existing HLR numbers 046–047 appear twice in this document (metadata-interpretation pair and CIL lineage pair). Those historical numbers are left in place. New SHALLs in this revision begin at **HLR-20.40.055-055**.

# **1. Nature of MCB‑prm (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑001**  
MCB‑prm SHALL operate as an OB‑family meaning‑layer primitive relating meaning‑layer interpretation to clarifying‑field statements.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑002**  
MCB‑prm SHALL operate only after IdOB‑prm has produced meaning‑layer semantics.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑003**  
MCB‑prm SHALL produce meaning‑layer deltas without altering structural geometry or routing metadata.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑004**  
MCB‑prm SHALL support serial chaining when additional clarifying‑meaning refinement is required.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑005**  
MCB‑prm SHALL consume a TP snapshot whose clarifying‑fields have been finalized by TPU‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑042 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
MCB‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves clarifying–meaning reconciliation or next‑turn context generation.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑043 — Read‑Only Discipline**
MCB‑prm SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields outside the meaning‑layer and next‑turn context fields it owns.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑044 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
MCB‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to clarifying–meaning reconciliation or next‑turn context generation and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑045 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑046 — Clarifying‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**
MCB‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as clarifying‑adjacent or meaning‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform structural interpretation or semantic inference.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑047 — Clarifying–Meaning Integration**
MCB‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into clarifying–meaning reconciliation only when such metadata affects meaning, clarifying alignment, coherence, continuity, or next‑turn context generation.

## **1.1 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

MCB‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic or semantic‑adjacent metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to clarifying–meaning reconciliation or next‑turn context generation. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- clarifying_metadata (COB, CIL, CEx)
- identity_metadata (IdOB), including crossing packet `tp.idob`
- utterance carrier recorded by IdOB
- `pack_ids[]` as a continuity / dictionary-key cue (not a pack loader)
- CIE row as this-hop pressure already applied by IdOB (not re-applied by MCB)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB) and packet `residue_code` as a continuity hint only
- next_context_metadata (MCB from prior cycles)

MCB‑prm treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
MCB‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to clarifying–meaning reconciliation or next‑turn context generation.  
MCB‑prm does not consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
MCB‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as clarifying‑adjacent or meaning‑adjacent features.  
MCB‑prm does not re-run IdOB map/rank, does not re-birth $M$, and does not treat `residue_code` as a next six-tuple.

## **1.2 — Path‑A COB/CIL Lineage & Metrics Support (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑046**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.identity_lineage` as read‑only metadata for clarifying–meaning reconciliation and next‑turn context generation.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑047**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.continuity_lineage` and use it deterministically when computing coherence and shift_required.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑048**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.topology` and treat it as read‑only structural context for clarifying–meaning reconciliation.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑049**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest all scalar fields in `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.metrics` and treat them as read‑only bounded‑semantic indicators:
- `ambiguity_score`  
- `collapse_risk`  
- `drift_score`  
- `stability_score`  
- `lineage_confidence`

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑050**  
MCB‑prm SHALL incorporate `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.metrics` only when such metrics affect coherence, continuity, or next‑turn context generation.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑051**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.register_continuity` and use it deterministically when generating next‑turn register and politeness fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑052**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ingest `TP.metadata.cil_metadata.importance_continuity` and use it deterministically when generating next‑turn importance fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑053**  
MCB‑prm SHALL treat all lineage, topology, metric, and continuity fields as strictly read‑only and SHALL NOT modify or reinterpret them.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑054**  
MCB‑prm SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of all lineage, topology, metric, and continuity fields consumed from TP.metadata.cil_metadata.

# **2. What MCB‑prm Is Not (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑006**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify current‑turn clarifying‑fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑007**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT read or modify routing vectors, routing deltas, or `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑008**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT trigger SSG, STPX, or any structural OB‑family primitive.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑009**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT participate in Path‑B realization or expression‑layer processing.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑010**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by structural or routing primitives.

---

# **3. Invocation & Position in Path‑A (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑011**  
MCB‑prm SHALL be invocable only after the official Path A stretch through IdOB has completed:

`RTU‑prm → TR‑prm → CTP‑prm → RB‑prm → IdOB‑prm`

(or after an isolation fixture that already presents a completed IdOB crossing packet).  
The historical predecessor sketch `RB‑prm → RTU‑prm → IdOB‑prm` is withdrawn as a scheduling claim; it does not match the official Path A flow in 20.15.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑012**  
MCB‑prm SHALL be scheduled when clarifying–meaning reconciliation or next‑turn context generation is required before RBU‑prm commit.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑013**  
MCB‑prm SHALL execute after IdOB‑prm and before RBU‑prm in all Path‑A cycles.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑055 — Official stretch**  
On a live Path A cycle, MCB‑prm SHALL occupy the slot `IdOB → MCB → RBU`. Isolation tests that start at IdOB MAY omit live RTU/RB; they SHALL still present `tp.idob` (or an explicit empty-packet miss) before MCB runs.

---

# **4. Clarifying–Meaning Reconciliation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑014**  
MCB‑prm SHALL read clarifying‑fields in a strictly read‑only manner.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑015**  
MCB‑prm SHALL detect clarifying–meaning reinforcement and produce positive meaning‑layer deltas.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑016**  
MCB‑prm SHALL detect clarifying–meaning conflict and produce corrective meaning‑layer deltas.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑017**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify current‑turn clarifying‑fields to resolve conflict.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑018**  
MCB‑prm SHALL update `TP.semantic.meaning_semantics[]` with clarifying‑aligned meaning cues required by RBU‑prm and TR.  
Those cues SHALL be **append / alias** writes. They SHALL NOT replace IdOB’s six-axis stand-in on `tp.idob`, SHALL NOT rewrite `meaning_delta_h` computed by IdOB, and SHALL NOT change six structure IDs.

---

# **5. Next‑Turn Clarifying/Context Field Generation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑019**  
MCB‑prm SHALL generate next‑turn clarifying/context fields in the COB‑readable slots defined in the TP field model.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑020**  
MCB‑prm SHALL populate next‑turn context fields with:  
`next_context_topic`, `next_context_stance`, `next_context_intent`,  
`next_context_register`, `next_context_politeness`,  
`next_context_epistemic_shading`, `next_context_continuity`,  
`next_context_direction`, and any additional fields required by COB.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑021**  
MCB‑prm SHALL compute `TP.semantic.mcb_context_coherence` indicating whether the current message is coherent with the context placed by CEx.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑022**  
MCB‑prm SHALL compute `TP.semantic.mcb_context_shift_required` indicating whether the next message should continue or shift context.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑023**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify current‑turn context fields; it SHALL produce next‑turn context fields only.

---

# **6. Meaning ΔH% & Semantics Tagging (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑024**  
MCB‑prm SHALL compute and write `TP.semantic.mcb_delta_h`, representing the magnitude of clarifying‑meaning refinement.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑025**  
MCB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.semantic.mcb_semantics[]` with clarifying‑aligned meaning semantics.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑026**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT modify structural ΔH% or any ΔH% field used by structural or routing primitives.

---

# **7. Completion & Next‑OB Signaling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑027**  
MCB‑prm SHALL set `TP.mcb_complete = true` when clarifying‑meaning refinement and next‑turn context generation are complete.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑028**  
MCB‑prm SHALL set `TP.mcb_complete = false` when additional MCB passes are required.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑029**  
MCB‑prm SHALL populate `TP.mcb_next_ob_candidates[]` with OB‑family primitives that may be required for further meaning‑layer refinement.

---

# **8. TP Update Request (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑030**  
MCB‑prm SHALL issue a TP update request `TPU.mcb_update` containing only:

- `mcb_delta_h`  
- `mcb_semantics[]`  
- `meaning_semantics[]` (append / alias cues only; HLR-018)  
- all next‑turn context fields  
- `mcb_context_coherence`  
- `mcb_context_shift_required`  
- `mcb_complete`  
- `mcb_next_ob_candidates[]`

`TPU.mcb_update` SHALL NOT include writes to `tp.idob`, IdOB `meaning_delta_h`, CIE rows, or six structure IDs.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑031**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT request routing updates, structural updates, or TR updates.

---

# **9. Integration with TP.next_context_fields (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑032**  
MCB‑prm SHALL write all next‑turn context fields exclusively into `TP.next_context{}`.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑033**  
MCB‑prm SHALL populate the following `TP.next_context{}` fields deterministically:  
`topic`, `stance`, `intent`, `register`, `politeness`,  
`epistemic_shading`, `continuity`, `direction`,  
`coherence`, `shift_required`, `importance`.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑034**  
MCB‑prm SHALL treat `TP.next_context{}` as write‑only; it SHALL NOT read previously written next‑turn context fields during the same cycle.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑035**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT write next‑turn context fields into clarifying‑field locations.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑036**  
MCB‑prm SHALL ensure next‑turn context fields are fully deterministic under replay.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑037**  
MCB‑prm SHALL mirror `TP.semantic.mcb_context_coherence` and `TP.semantic.mcb_context_shift_required` into `TP.next_context.coherence` and `TP.next_context.shift_required`.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑038**  
MCB‑prm SHALL include the complete `TP.next_context{}` block in `TPU.mcb_update` exactly as defined in the TP field model.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑039**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT introduce schema‑external fields into `TP.next_context{}`.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑040**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT write next‑turn context fields during any safe‑boundary supervisory window.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑041**  
MCB‑prm SHALL treat `TP.next_context{}` as part of the TP field model and SHALL NOT interact with truth/done fields, routing fields, structural geometry, ΔH%, lineage, or Path‑B semantics when generating next‑turn context.

# **10. Interfaces (Informative)**

**Upstream:**  
MCB‑prm is scheduled after IdOB‑prm on the official stretch `RTU → TR → CTP → RB → IdOB → MCB → RBU`, and after TPU‑prm has finalized current-turn clarifying-fields (TPU’s early Path A pass remains the clarifying freeze; MCB does not re-open it).

**Downstream:**  
After `TPU.mcb_update` is committed:

- RBU‑prm consumes meaning‑layer deltas owned by MCB (`mcb_delta_h`) and may read IdOB `meaning_delta_h` as a separate instrument  
- COB consumes next‑turn context fields  
- COB merges next‑turn context into long‑term 1‑of‑20 context  
- CIL places next‑turn context into the next TP envelope  
- RB‑prm uses committed meaning for routing  
- TR may use clarifying‑aligned meaning semantics indirectly  
- OuBA may freeze a slim `TP.idob` plus `pack_ids[]` / utterance for COB ingest (20.40.060)

MCB‑prm does not read or modify routing vectors or routing deltas.

MCB‑prm prepares meaning and next‑turn context for RBU commit; it does not initiate Path‑B.

# **11. IdOB packet boundary (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑056 — Read the crossing packet**  
When `tp.idob` is present, MCB‑prm SHALL treat it as read-only input for clarifying–meaning reconciliation. Applicable packet fields include `utterance`, six structure IDs / `structural_key`, `selected_group_id`, `meaning_semantics` / `meaning_semantics_prime`, `meaning_delta_h`, CIE identifiers, `pack_ids` when copied onto the packet or TP, `residue_code`, and completion / eligibility flags.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑057 — Do not overwrite IdOB**  
MCB‑prm SHALL NOT write `tp.idob`, SHALL NOT recompute IdOB `meaning_delta_h`, SHALL NOT rewrite six structure IDs or `structural_key`, SHALL NOT apply CIE pressure a second time, and SHALL NOT invent map members or a next six-tuple.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑058 — Stance slots stay distinct**  
`TP.next_context.stance` / `next_context_stance` SHALL be treated as a next-turn COB hint. They SHALL NOT be written into CIE rows and SHALL NOT be used as $\alpha I$ on the current-hop stand-in $M$.

### **HLR‑20.40.055‑059 — Residue as continuity hint**  
When packet `residue_code` is present and non-empty, MCB‑prm MAY use it only as a deterministic input to `mcb_context_shift_required` / `next_context.shift_required`. MCB‑prm SHALL NOT treat `residue_code` as an RB next-OB six-tuple or as authority to re-run IdOB.
