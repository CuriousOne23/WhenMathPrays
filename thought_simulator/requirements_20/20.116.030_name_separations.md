# **20.116.030 — Name Separations**
**Document ID:** 20.116.030  
**Version:** 0.1

**Status:** Normative — names that must stay distinct

## 1. Purpose

Several Path A names look related and are not the same object. This file is the convictable list.

**HLR-20.116.030-001**  
Implementations, structural programs, and testbenches SHALL treat each pair in §2 as distinct objects. Equating a pair SHALL be a catalog defect.

## 3. Restated instruments (no new formulas)

**HLR-20.116.030-002**  
`TP.semantic.meaning_delta_h` and `TP.idob.meaning_delta_h` SHALL be the meaning instrument. They SHALL NOT be stored as `TP.metadata.entropy_metadata.delta_h`.

**HLR-20.116.030-003**  
`TP.next_context.stance` SHALL NOT be applied as CIE pressure on the current-hop stand-in.

**HLR-20.116.030-004**  
`pack_ids[]` SHALL NOT be derived from identity-layer `register` or from CEx-IE `register_hint`.

**HLR-20.116.030-005**  
Absence of `TP.metadata.identity.*` SHALL NOT make an S2M hop schema-illegal when `TP.idob` is present (`20.105.010-026`).
