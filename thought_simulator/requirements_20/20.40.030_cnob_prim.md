# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Constraint Object (CnOB) is the **third structural / constraint layer** in Path A.  
Its role is to extract **constraint-level residue** from the refined structure and tags produced by SROB (20.40.020)—including refinement of structural-importance residues into constraint-importance residues—so that SmOB and downstream scoring/routing receive bite-sized, rigidly defined constraint fields.

CnOB encodes:

- constraint cues and constraint families (C1–C7)  
- missing-slot signals  
- underspecification markers  
- conflict indicators  
- constraint-importance residues  
- constraint-level residue hashing  
- routing-eligible constraint metadata  
- structural-change / ΔH%-aligned constraint signals when derived from allowed structural inputs (not from forbidden routing envelopes)  

CnOB is **not** a general meaning interpreter and is **not** a structure normalizer (SROB owns normalization).  
CnOB **may** perform **bounded, deterministic, domain-limited constraint-oriented interpretation** of structural and semantic-adjacent cues already present on the TP (especially SROB fields and sharpened constraint hints) when required to form constraint families, missing-slot signals, conflict indicators, and constraint-importance residues with high confidence for downstream consumption.

CnOB does **not** reconstruct free-form missing content as meaning, resolve referent identity, assign semantic roles, or write meaning-layer fields. CnOB writes only CnOB-owned fields.

---

# **2. Functional Overview** *(Informative)*

CnOB:

- accepts SROB refined structural map, residue, and related structural/constraint-hint metadata (preferring SROB when present; SOB only if SROB absent in progressive-lineup toggles)
- extracts monotonic constraint families C1–C7 under deterministic rules
- detects missing-slot, underspecification, and conflict signals from structure and constraint tags
- refines structural-importance into constraint-importance residues
- may read applicable CE/CEx and TP-stream metadata read-only as structural or semantic-adjacent cues
- emits constraint residue addressable by RB and consumable by SmOB, STPX, and ISc
- may emit diagnostic-only metadata for developer playback (not required for other primitives)
- should reduce constraint-space ambiguity relative to SROB-only consumption when measurable (visibility goal, not a hard HLR)

CnOB is part of the OB family whose purpose is to **digest its domain of the meaning/constraint space into rigid tags** so primary meaning and routing primitives are not overwhelmed.

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **3.1 Inputs and TP-Stream Discipline**

### **HLR-20.40.030-010**
CnOB SHALL accept the refined structural TP, refined residue fragments, refined structural metadata, and refined structural-importance metadata produced by SROB.

### **HLR-20.40.030-011** *(Rewritten — forbidden → bounded)*
CnOB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — in a read-only manner and MAY use them as structural or semantic-adjacent cues for constraint extraction. CnOB SHALL NOT perform deep meaning resolution, intent inference, or referent identity resolution on these fields.

### **HLR-20.40.030-012 — Structural Discourse-Flag Normalization**
CnOB SHALL normalize discourse-context structural flags propagated from SROB — including recent-entity continuity, temporal-shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical constraint-relevant structural metadata under bounded, deterministic rules.

### **HLR-20.40.030-013 — Structural Discourse-Constraint Encoding** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL encode normalized discourse-context metadata strictly as **structural / constraint-level residue**. CnOB SHALL NOT perform semantic-role inference, intent inference, or referent identity resolution when encoding discourse constraints. Bounded constraint-oriented classification of discourse flags is permitted.

### **HLR-20.40.030-014 — Deterministic & Monotonic Discourse Constraints**
CnOB SHALL ensure discourse-context constraints are deterministic, bounded, monotonic, and reproducible for identical SROB inputs, consistent with constraint families C1–C7.

### **HLR-20.40.030-015 — Routing-Eligible Discourse Constraints**
CnOB SHALL expose discourse-context constraints as part of constraint-level residue and routing-eligibility metadata, ensuring they are consumable by SmOB, STPX, and ISc without modification of CnOB-owned content by those consumers.

### **HLR-20.40.030-026 — TP-Stream Metadata Consumption**
CnOB SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP-stream metadata when such metadata improves constraint extraction, constraint sharpening, or constraint-residue formation.

### **HLR-20.40.030-027 — Read-Only Discipline** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL treat upstream TP-stream metadata and non-CnOB-owned fields as read-only. CnOB SHALL NOT modify upstream fields; CnOB SHALL write only CnOB-owned fields as defined in HLR-20.40.030-037.

### **HLR-20.40.030-028 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
CnOB SHALL consume TP-stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to constraint extraction and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR-20.40.030-029 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
CnOB SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline-B envelopes. CnOB SHALL NOT consume ΔH% **routing envelopes**; structural-change cues already present on allowed structural residue may still inform C6-style constraints under deterministic rules without reading forbidden envelopes.

### **HLR-20.40.030-030 — Bounded Interpretation of Consumed Metadata** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL treat all consumed TP-stream metadata as structural or semantic-adjacent features. CnOB MAY perform bounded, deterministic, constraint-domain interpretation of those features. CnOB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, or intent inference outside its defined constraint domain. All CnOB interpretation of consumed metadata SHALL remain bounded, deterministic, and reproducible.

### **HLR-20.40.030-031 — Constraint-Residue Integration**
CnOB SHALL encode any consumed TP-stream metadata as constraint-level residue fragments in CnOB-owned fields when such metadata affects constraint extraction or constraint sharpening.

---

## **3.1.1 TP-Stream Metadata Consumption** *(Informative)*

CnOB may consume deterministic, bounded, pre-semantic or semantic-adjacent metadata present in the TP-stream when such metadata is applicable to constraint extraction. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX) when present and applicable as cues only
- identity_metadata (IdOB) when present and applicable as structural cues only
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST) when present and applicable
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)

CnOB treats non-owned TP-stream metadata as read-only.  
CnOB ignores metadata that is not applicable to constraint extraction or constraint-residue formation.  
CnOB does not consume routing_metadata, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or Pipeline-B envelopes.  
CnOB interprets TP-stream metadata only as structural or semantic-adjacent features under bounded constraint-domain rules.

---

## **3.2 CnOB Definition**

CnOB is a deterministic constraint object containing (normative field families; wire schema may be refined in software architecture):

- `constraint_families` — monotonic constraint sets (C1–C7)  
- `missing_slot_signals` — structural underspecification markers  
- `conflict_indicators` — structural conflict flags  
- `constraint_importance` — constraint-importance residues  
- `constraint_residue_hash` — deterministic hash of constraint-level residue  
- `lineage_constraints` — constraints derived from structural ancestry cues on allowed inputs  
- `delta_h_constraints` — constraints aligned with structural-change cues (C6), not forbidden routing ΔH% envelopes  
- `routing_constraints` — routing-eligibility constraints  
- `policy_constraints` — constraints derived from policy signatures when present and applicable  

### **HLR-20.40.030-001** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL be constructed as a pure **constraint-domain** object containing only monotonic, deterministically entailed constraints derived from SROB (and other allowed structural/semantic-adjacent inputs). CnOB SHALL NOT contain supervisory content, free-form meaning propositions, or intent conclusions. Bounded constraint-oriented interpretation within CnOB’s domain is permitted.

---

## **3.3 Deterministic Constraint Extraction**

### **HLR-20.40.030-002**
CnOB extraction SHALL be deterministic for identical SROB inputs, producing identical constraint sets across all executions.

---

## **3.4 Canonical Ordering of Constraints**

### **HLR-20.40.030-003**
CnOB SHALL apply canonical ordering to all constraint families and nested constraint structures to ensure deterministic replay and structural comparability.

---

## **3.5 Structural / Constraint Purity**

### **HLR-20.40.030-004** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL contain only constraints that are deterministically entailed under defined CnOB rules from structural and semantic-adjacent inputs. CnOB SHALL NOT invent unconstrained free-form fields, smooth over conflicts by semantic guessing, or reconstruct missing textual content as meaning. Missing-slot and underspecification **signals** are permitted as constraint residue, not as filled meaning.

---

## **3.6 Constraint Families (C1–C7)**

CnOB supports the following monotonic structural constraint families:

- **C1:** Structural existence constraints  
- **C2:** Structural adjacency constraints  
- **C3:** Structural ordering constraints  
- **C4:** Structural boundary constraints  
- **C5:** Structural lineage constraints  
- **C6:** Structural change constraints (aligned with measurable structural-change cues)  
- **C7:** Routing-eligibility constraints  

### **HLR-20.40.030-005**
CnOB SHALL maintain monotonicity of all constraint families: constraints may be added but never removed within a single CnOB construction for a given TP cycle.

---

## **3.7 Lineage-Derived Constraints**

### **HLR-20.40.030-006**
CnOB SHALL derive lineage-based constraints deterministically from structural ancestry cues on allowed inputs (e.g., SROB structure and provenance) and SHALL ensure these constraints are stable across replay.

---

## **3.8 Structural-Change (ΔH%-Aligned) Constraints**

### **HLR-20.40.030-007** *(Rewritten for envelope discipline)*
CnOB SHALL derive C6-style structural-change constraints deterministically from **allowed structural inputs** and SHALL ensure consistency with 20.47 and 20.12 invariants where applicable. CnOB SHALL NOT read forbidden routing_metadata or Pipeline-B ΔH% envelopes to do so (see HLR-20.40.030-029).

---

## **3.9 Constraint-Residue Signals**

### **HLR-20.40.030-008**
CnOB SHALL extract missing-slot signals, underspecification markers, conflict indicators, and constraint-level residue hashing as part of CnOB construction, ensuring deterministic residue for routing and change attribution.

---

## **3.10 CnOB as Input to SmOB**

### **HLR-20.40.030-009**
SmOB SHALL receive CnOB as a direct input and SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, or discard any CnOB-owned constraints when consuming them as input. *(Normative consumer duty; retained here for Path-A chain clarity.)*

---

## **3.11 Bounded Behavior and Owned Fields** *(New / rewritten block)*

### **HLR-20.40.030-037** *(New — owned-field write discipline)*
CnOB SHALL write only CnOB-owned fields (including constraint families, constraint residue, constraint-importance metadata, constraint-residue hash, and CnOB audit/diagnostic records). CnOB SHALL NOT overwrite SOB-owned fields, SROB-owned fields, meaning-layer fields, Context Frame fields, MSL, identity state, freeze signatures, or routing_metadata.

### **HLR-20.40.030-038** *(New — bounded constraint-domain semantics)*
CnOB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, intent inference, or referent identity resolution outside its defined constraint domain. CnOB MAY perform bounded, deterministic, structural and semantic-adjacent interpretation when required to extract constraint families, missing-slot signals, conflict indicators, discourse-constraint encoding, or constraint-importance residues from SROB-presented structure and tags.

### **HLR-20.40.030-039** *(New — no meaning-layer writes)*
CnOB SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning-layer fields. Constraint-importance and constraint residues SHALL remain confined to CnOB-owned fields.

### **HLR-20.40.030-040** *(New — diagnostic metadata)*
CnOB MAY write diagnostic-only metadata intended solely for developer playback and analysis. Diagnostic-only metadata SHALL NOT be required reading for any other Path-A primitive and SHALL NOT affect routing or meaning decisions.

### **HLR-20.40.030-041** *(New — SROB field preference)*
When both SOB and SROB structural/constraint-hint fields are present, CnOB SHALL prefer SROB-owned refined fields as the primary input for constraint extraction. SOB fields MAY be used only when SROB fields are absent (e.g., progressive-lineup isolation).

---

## **3.12 Constraint-Importance**

### **HLR-20.40.030-032**
CnOB SHALL refine structural-importance residues emitted by SROB into **constraint-importance residues**, representing constraint-relevant importance signals such as missing-slot importance, conflict-importance, and underspecification-importance.

### **HLR-20.40.030-033** *(Rewritten)*
CnOB SHALL compute constraint-importance values using deterministic rules based solely on structural and semantic-adjacent cues. CnOB SHALL NOT perform semantic-role assignment or referent resolution when computing constraint-importance.

### **HLR-20.40.030-034** *(Rewritten — entropy demoted)*
CnOB SHALL ensure constraint-importance residues remain confined to CnOB-owned fields and SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning-layer fields. Entropy reduction for these residues is a development visibility goal, not a separate hard SHALL.

### **HLR-20.40.030-035**
CnOB SHALL ensure constraint-importance residues remain addressable by RB and consumable by SmOB and STPX.

### **HLR-20.40.030-036**
CnOB SHALL ensure constraint-importance residues are monotonic and reproducible for identical SROB inputs.

---

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.030-021**
CnOB SHALL ensure each constraint-level residue fragment $r_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i'$:

$$
h_i' = \mathrm{hash}(r_i')
$$

### **HLR-20.40.030-022**
CnOB SHALL ensure the combined query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h_1' \oplus h_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h_n'
$$

---

# **5. Outputs (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.030-024** *(Rewritten — owned fields)*
CnOB SHALL output, in CnOB-owned fields:

- normalized constraint-level residue  
- constraint families (C1–C7)  
- missing-slot signals  
- underspecification markers  
- conflict indicators  
- constraint-importance residues  
- constraint-residue hash  
- lineage-derived constraints  
- structural-change-aligned constraints (C6)  
- routing-eligibility constraints  
- policy-derived constraints when applicable  
- CnOB audit record (and optional diagnostic-only metadata per HLR-20.40.030-040)  

### **HLR-20.40.030-025**
CnOB SHALL guarantee its output is valid input for SmOB.
