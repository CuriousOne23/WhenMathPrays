# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structural Refinement OB (SROB) layer is the **second classification layer** in Path A.
Its role is to **normalize, refine, disambiguate, and sharpen** the structural tags and residue produced by SOB so that downstream primitives (CnOB, SmOB, routing, and meaning stages) receive bite-sized, rigidly defined structural fields rather than raw or only coarsely tagged structure.

SROB performs:

- structural normalization (lists, tables, code/math blocks, unit consistency)
- structural ambiguity resolution (boundaries, nested structure)
- refinement of SOB structural metadata into canonical form
- sharpening of operator, domain, tone, and constraint hints **within the field space SOB already presented**
- refinement of structural-importance residues into positional/structural labels
- production of refined residue addressable by RB and consumable by CnOB and SmOB

SROB does **not** invent new hint *types* outside SOB’s field space, does **not** enforce constraints, does **not** resolve referent identity or entity meaning, and does **not** write meaning-layer fields. SROB **may** perform **bounded, deterministic, domain-limited semantic-adjacent interpretation** when required to normalize structure or sharpen SOB-presented tags with high confidence for downstream consumption.

SROB writes only SROB-owned fields. Upstream envelopes, TP text, and meaning-layer representations remain read-only.

---

# **2. Functional Overview** *(Informative)*

SROB:

- accepts SOB structural map, residue, and related structural metadata
- normalizes structural units and block types without altering original text content
- resolves structural boundary ambiguity under deterministic rules
- sharpens SOB hints (operator / domain / tone / constraint) into more precise categories in the same type space
- refines structural-importance labels as positional/structural cues (e.g., header-like, anchor-like, subject-like, object-like) — not semantic role labeling
- may read applicable CE/CEx discourse and TP-stream metadata read-only to support normalization
- emits refined residue for RB, CnOB, and SmOB
- may emit diagnostic-only metadata for developer playback and analysis (not for other primitives)
- should reduce structural ambiguity relative to SOB output when measurable (visibility goal, not a hard HLR)

SROB is part of the OB family whose purpose is to **digest structural and structural-adjacent space into rigid tags** so primary meaning and routing primitives are not overwhelmed.

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **3.1 Structural Refinement**

### **HLR-20.40.020-001**
SROB SHALL refine SOB segmentation into normalized structural units.

### **HLR-20.40.020-002**
SROB SHALL normalize list structures, including depth, numbering, and indentation, without altering list item text content.

### **HLR-20.40.020-003**
SROB SHALL normalize table structures, including headers, rows, and cell boundaries, without altering cell text content.

### **HLR-20.40.020-004**
SROB SHALL normalize code blocks and math blocks without altering their content.

---

## **3.2 Ambiguity Resolution**

### **HLR-20.40.020-005**
SROB SHALL resolve ambiguous segmentation boundaries, including sentence boundaries and nested list structures, using deterministic structural rules.

### **HLR-20.40.020-006**
SROB SHALL ensure consistent representation of structurally similar units across the TP.

---

## **3.3 Metadata and Hint Sharpening**

### **HLR-20.40.020-007**
SROB SHALL refine structural metadata from SOB into canonical normalized form in SROB-owned fields.

### **HLR-20.40.020-008**
SROB SHALL sharpen domain hints present in SOB output into more precise domain categories **within the same domain-hint type space**. SROB SHALL NOT introduce domain-hint types that SOB did not present and that are outside the agreed domain vocabulary used by the OB family.

### **HLR-20.40.020-009**
SROB SHALL sharpen operator hints present in SOB output into more precise operator categories **within the same operator-hint type space**. SROB SHALL NOT introduce operator-hint types outside that space.

### **HLR-20.40.020-010**
SROB SHALL sharpen tone hints present in SOB output into more precise tone categories **within the same tone-hint type space**. SROB SHALL NOT introduce tone-hint types outside that space.

### **HLR-20.40.020-011**
SROB SHALL sharpen constraint hints present in SOB output into more precise constraint categories **within the same constraint-hint type space**. SROB SHALL NOT introduce constraint-hint types outside that space. SROB SHALL NOT apply or enforce constraint logic.

### **HLR-20.40.020-041** *(New)*
Sharpening SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical inputs. Sharpening MAY use bounded, high-confidence structural and semantic-adjacent rules (including dictionaries and fixed refinement maps) to add greater detail required to assist downstream meaning and routing. Sharpening SHALL remain inside SROB’s domain and SHALL NOT perform deep meaning resolution, intent inference, or referent identity resolution.

---

## **3.4 Residue Formation**

### **HLR-20.40.020-012**
SROB SHALL produce refined residue fragments representing normalized structure and sharpened hints in SROB-owned fields.

### **HLR-20.40.020-013**
SROB SHALL ensure refined residue fragments remain addressable by RB.

### **HLR-20.40.020-014** *(Rewritten — entropy guidance demoted)*
**N/A as SHALL.** Structural entropy reduction relative to SOB output is a development visibility goal, not a hard requirement.  
*Informative:* SROB **should** reduce structural ambiguity or measurable structural entropy relative to SOB output when a defined metric is available; failure to reduce is a diagnostic signal, not an automatic normative violation.

---

## **3.5 Bounded Behavior and Prohibitions**

### **HLR-20.40.020-015** *(Rewritten — forbidden → bounded)*
SROB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, or intent inference outside its defined structural-refinement domain. SROB MAY perform bounded, deterministic, structural and semantic-adjacent interpretation when required for normalization, boundary disambiguation, hint sharpening within SOB’s field space, or structural-importance labeling.

### **HLR-20.40.020-016** *(Rewritten)*
SROB SHALL NOT infer user intent, task goals, referent identity, entity meaning, or meaning-layer relationships. Structural-importance labels such as subject-like, object-like, header-like, and anchor-like SHALL be treated as positional/structural cues only, not as semantic role assignments.

### **HLR-20.40.020-017**
SROB SHALL NOT apply or enforce constraint logic.

### **HLR-20.40.020-018**
SROB SHALL NOT modify the original text of the TP.

### **HLR-20.40.020-042** *(New — owned-field write discipline)*
SROB SHALL write only SROB-owned fields (including refined structural map, refined residue, refined structural-importance metadata, and SROB audit/diagnostic records). SROB SHALL NOT overwrite SOB-owned fields, meaning-layer fields, Context Frame fields, MSL, identity state, freeze signatures, or routing_metadata.

### **HLR-20.40.020-043** *(New — diagnostic metadata)*
SROB MAY write diagnostic-only metadata intended solely for developer playback and analysis. Diagnostic-only metadata SHALL NOT be required reading for any other Path-A primitive and SHALL NOT affect routing or meaning decisions.

---

## **3.6 Routing Requirements**

### **HLR-20.40.020-019**
SROB SHALL produce output suitable for routing into CnOB as the next classification layer in the progressive lineup.

### **HLR-20.40.020-020**
SROB SHALL NOT route directly into semantic OBs.

---

## **3.7 Inputs and TP-Stream Discipline**

### **HLR-20.40.020-026**
SROB SHALL accept the structural TP tags, residue fragments, structural metadata, and structural-importance metadata produced by SOB (SOB-owned fields and related structural envelopes).

### **HLR-20.40.020-027** *(Rewritten)*
SROB MAY read context fields extracted by CEx/CE — including thread summary, recent entities, prior references, temporal markers, causal markers, and repair history — in a read-only manner and MAY use them as structural or semantic-adjacent cues for normalization and disambiguation. SROB SHALL NOT perform deep meaning resolution or intent inference on these fields.

### **HLR-20.40.020-028 — Structural Discourse-Flag Normalization**
SROB SHALL normalize discourse-context flags propagated from SOB — including recent-entity continuity, temporal-shift markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers — into canonical structural metadata under bounded, deterministic rules.

### **HLR-20.40.020-029 — Structural Discourse-Residue Refinement**
SROB SHALL refine discourse-context residue fragments into deterministic, bounded, addressable structural fragments suitable for routing by RB and consumption by downstream layers (including CnOB, SmOB, STPX, ISc as applicable).

### **HLR-20.40.020-030 — TP-Stream Metadata Consumption**
SROB SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP-stream metadata when such metadata improves structural normalization, refinement, or ambiguity resolution.

### **HLR-20.40.020-031 — Read-Only Discipline** *(Rewritten)*
SROB SHALL treat upstream TP-stream metadata and non-SROB-owned fields as read-only. SROB SHALL NOT modify upstream fields; SROB SHALL write only SROB-owned fields as defined in HLR-20.40.020-042.

### **HLR-20.40.020-032 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
SROB SHALL consume TP-stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to structural refinement and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR-20.40.020-033 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
SROB SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline-B envelopes.

### **HLR-20.40.020-034 — Bounded Interpretation of Consumed Metadata** *(Rewritten)*
SROB SHALL treat all consumed TP-stream metadata as structural or semantic-adjacent features. SROB SHALL NOT perform deep semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, or intent inference when consuming TP-stream metadata. All SROB interpretation of consumed metadata SHALL remain bounded, deterministic, and reproducible.

### **HLR-20.40.020-035 — Residue Integration**
SROB SHALL encode any consumed TP-stream metadata as refined structural residue fragments in SROB-owned fields when such metadata affects normalization or ambiguity resolution.

---

## **3.8 Structural-Importance Refinement**

### **HLR-20.40.020-036**
SROB SHALL refine structural-importance residue fragments emitted by SOB into canonical, deterministic structural-importance metadata suitable for constraint extraction and semantic-adjacent processing by downstream consumers.

### **HLR-20.40.020-037** *(Rewritten)*
SROB SHALL normalize structural-importance labels (e.g., subject-like, object-like, header-like, anchor-like) using deterministic rules based on structural and semantic-adjacent cues. These labels SHALL be positional/structural cues only and SHALL NOT constitute semantic role labeling or entity-meaning assignment.

### **HLR-20.40.020-038**
SROB SHALL NOT assign semantic roles, interpret entity meaning, or resolve referent identity when refining structural-importance residues.

### **HLR-20.40.020-039** *(Rewritten)*
SROB SHALL ensure refined structural-importance residues remain confined to SROB-owned fields and SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning-layer fields. Entropy reduction for these residues is a development visibility goal (see HLR-20.40.020-014 informative note), not a separate hard SHALL.

### **HLR-20.40.020-040**
SROB SHALL ensure refined structural-importance residues remain addressable by RB and consumable by CnOB and SmOB.

---

## **3.9 TP-Stream Metadata Consumption** *(Informative)*

SROB may consume deterministic, bounded, pre-semantic or semantic-adjacent metadata present in the TP-stream when such metadata is applicable to structural refinement. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- residue_metadata (upstream SOB)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX) when present and applicable
- identity_metadata (IdOB) when present and applicable as structural cues only
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST) when present and applicable
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)

SROB treats non-owned TP-stream metadata as read-only.  
SROB ignores metadata that is not applicable to structural normalization or ambiguity resolution.  
SROB does not consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline-B envelopes.  
SROB interprets TP-stream metadata only as structural or semantic-adjacent features under bounded rules.

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.020-021**
SROB SHALL ensure each refined residue fragment $r_i'$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i'$:

$$
h_i' = \mathrm{hash}(r_i')
$$

### **HLR-20.40.020-022**
SROB SHALL ensure the combined query address $q'$ satisfies:

$$
q' = h_1' \oplus h_2' \oplus \dots \oplus h_n'
$$

### **HLR-20.40.020-023**
SROB SHALL ensure refined residue fragments do not violate geometric invariants defined in 20.40, including smoothness, monotonicity, and curvature-invariance.

---

# **5. Outputs (Normative)**

### **HLR-20.40.020-024** *(Rewritten — owned fields)*
SROB SHALL output, in SROB-owned fields:

- refined structural map (normalized units, block structure, consistent unit representation)
- refined residue set
- refined structural metadata
- sharpened operator-hint metadata (within SOB type space)
- sharpened domain-hint metadata (within SOB type space)
- sharpened tone-hint metadata (within SOB type space)
- sharpened constraint-hint metadata (within SOB type space)
- refined structural-importance metadata
- SROB audit record (and optional diagnostic-only metadata per HLR-20.40.020-043)

### **HLR-20.40.020-025**
SROB SHALL guarantee its output is valid input for CnOB.
