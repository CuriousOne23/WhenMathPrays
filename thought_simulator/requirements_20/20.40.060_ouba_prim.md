# **20.40.060 — OuBA‑prm (Output Basin Primitive)**  
**Version:** 2.1 (COB‑Complete Option‑C Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative — Path‑A Final Commit Primitive  
**Purpose:** Freeze and commit the authoritative semantic, metadata, context, and provenance state into a CMTP.

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

OuBA‑prm is the **final TP‑writing primitive** in Path A.  
It receives the **fully stabilized meaning state** produced by IdOB and produces a **Committed Meaning Thought Packet (CMTP)** — the immutable semantic snapshot used by:

- COB behavior engines  
- CST truth‑alignment engines  
- Path‑B truth and safety engines  
- governance, replay, and audit  

OuBA:

- performs **no inference**  
- performs **no routing**  
- performs **no OB‑family recomputation**  
- introduces **no new semantic content**  
- freezes semantic, metadata, context, identity, and lineage deterministically  
- emits a CMTP that becomes the authoritative meaning envelope for downstream systems  

After OuBA, **no Path‑A primitive may modify TP**, and **all downstream components treat the CMTP as read‑only**.

---

# **1. Scope (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑001**  
OuBA SHALL accept as input only meaning states that have passed through IdOB in the Path‑A pipeline.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑002**  
OuBA SHALL produce exactly one CMTP for each accepted IdOB output. 

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑003**  
OuBA SHALL freeze `TP.semantic.semantic_core` such that no further primitive can modify its content.  

## **1.1 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

OuBA‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic, semantic‑adjacent, and meaning‑layer metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is required to freeze, commit, or serialize the authoritative semantic state into a CMTP. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- clarifying_metadata (COB, CIL, CEx)
- identity_metadata (IdOB)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB)
- next_context_metadata (MCB)
- provenance_metadata (TP.metadata.*)
- lineage_metadata (TP.lineage_log[])
- entropy and signature histories

OuBA‑prm treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
OuBA‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to commit, provenance, lineage, or semantic freezing.  
OuBA‑prm does not consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields owned by Path‑B, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
OuBA‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as commit‑adjacent or provenance‑adjacent features.

---

# **2. Semantic Preservation (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑004**  
OuBA SHALL preserve all referents, bindings, and constraints present in the IdOB output without addition, removal, or reinterpretation.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑005**  
OuBA SHALL NOT introduce any new semantic content, referents, or constraints not present in the IdOB output.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑006**  
OuBA SHALL NOT perform semantic inference, scoring, routing, or candidate generation.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑007**  
OuBA SHALL operate deterministically such that identical IdOB inputs yield identical CMTP outputs.   

---

# **3. Pipeline Boundaries (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑008**  
OuBA SHALL NOT invoke any OB layer (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB), SSG, TR, RB, or IdOB during its operation.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑009**  
OuBA SHALL NOT read or depend on global state beyond what is required to attach provenance, lineage, and metadata.   

---

# **4. Provenance & Lineage Commit (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑010**  
OuBA SHALL attach complete OB lineage to the CMTP, including identifiers for SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, IdOB, and routing decisions where available.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑011**  
OuBA SHALL attach a `commit_timestamp` and `commit_hash` sufficient for replay and audit.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑012**  
OuBA SHALL record the ruleset identifiers used along Path A in the CMTP metadata.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑013**  
OuBA SHALL record ΔH% history in `TP.metadata.entropy_history[]` and structural signature lineage in `TP.metadata.signature_history[]`.  

---

# **4A. COB‑Required Provenance Commit (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑033**  
OuBA SHALL commit `TP.lineage_log[]` into the CMTP such that COB and CST can perform lineage‑continuity analysis.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑034**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP metadata fields required by COB, including:  
`contextual_alignment_record`, `identity_shift_record`, `topic_anchor_record`, `continuity_record`, and `intent_record`.

---

# **5. Residue & Uncertainty Handling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑014**  
OuBA SHALL ensure that any remaining uncertainty or residue is explicitly represented in the CMTP and not silently discarded.   

---

# **6. Serialization & Replay (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑015**  
OuBA SHALL emit CMTPs in a serialization‑ready form suitable for storage, governance inspection, and replay.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑018**  
OuBA SHALL NOT depend on nondeterministic sources such as random seeds, external services, or user‑profile‑dependent behavior.  

---

# **7. Commit Boundary Enforcement (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑016**  
OuBA SHALL enforce that no Path‑A primitive can modify TP after CMTP emission.   

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑017**  
OuBA SHALL enforce that Path‑B, COB, CST, and governance components treat the CMTP as read‑only.  

---

# **8. Error Handling (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑019**  
OuBA SHALL fail fast and report an error if the IdOB input is structurally incomplete or violates required invariants for commit.  

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑020**  
OuBA SHALL never drop, reorder, or duplicate semantic units (referents, constraints, message items) when constructing the CMTP. 

---

# **9. COB‑Required Commit of Semantic Envelope (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑021**  
OuBA SHALL commit the full semantic envelope required by COB, including:  
`proposition_set[]`, `truth_evidence[]`, `semantic_tags[]`, `messy_input_record`, and `lane_local_identity`.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑022**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP trace fields required by COB and CST, including:  
`ob_trace[]` and `tb_trace[]`.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑023**  
OuBA SHALL commit all TP policy markers required for downstream routing and safety.

---

# **10. COB‑Required Commit of Context Envelope (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑024**  
OuBA SHALL commit the entire `TP.next_context.*` structure, including topic, stance, intent, register, politeness, epistemic shading, continuity, direction, coherence, shift_required, importance, and clarifying_fields[].

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑025**  
OuBA SHALL commit all context‑derived metadata required for COB continuity and CST alignment.

---

# **11. CST‑Required Identity Snapshot Commit (Normative)**  
*(New section)*

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑026**  
OuBA SHALL commit `TP.cob_state_snapshot` such that CST can perform identity‑layer truth alignment.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑027 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
OuBA‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata is required to freeze, commit, serialize, or preserve the authoritative semantic, context, provenance, or lineage state.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑028 — Read‑Only Discipline**
OuBA‑prm SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields outside the commit‑layer fields it owns.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑029 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
OuBA‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to commit, provenance, lineage, or semantic freezing and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑030 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
OuBA‑prm SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, structural ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields owned by Path‑B, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑031 — Commit‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**
OuBA‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as commit‑adjacent or provenance‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform structural interpretation, semantic inference, or routing logic.

### **HLR‑20.40.060‑032 — Commit‑Layer Integration**
OuBA‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into the CMTP only when such metadata affects semantic freezing, context commit, provenance commit, lineage commit, or replay determinism.

# **13. Governance Boundary (Informative)**

OuBA marks the transition from Path‑A meaning construction to governance and realization:

- COB and CST operate directly on CMTPs  
- TPTB / TPSF / GBIB operate only on CMTPs  
- Path B reads CMTPs but does not modify them  
- Replay and audit operate over CMTP lineage, metadata, and context
