### **3.2 Ambiguous identity markers**

### **3.3 Unbounded identity claims**
Identity claims that exceed the geometry domain.

### **3.4 Identity contradictions without context**
Contradictions lacking continuity or pressure context.

### **3.5 Identity noise**
Random or incoherent identity signals.

### **3.6 Identity overload**
Too many identity markers in a single turn.

### **3.7 Identity collapse signals**
Signals that would destroy identity geometry.
