# **3. Why Referent Continuity Exists**

Referent continuity exists because:

1. Meaning requires stable referents.  
2. Ambiguous referents destabilize meaning.  
3. Shifting referents destabilize continuity.  
4. Collapsing referents destabilize identity.  
5. Routing must detect referent instability.  
6. Commit must be blocked during referent instability.  
7. Replay determinism requires referent tracking.

Referent continuity is the **semantic anchor** for meaning.
