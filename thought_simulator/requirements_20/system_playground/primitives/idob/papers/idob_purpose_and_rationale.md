# IdOB purpose and rationale

**Live purpose:** [idob_s2m_overview.md](idob_s2m_overview.md)

IdOB exists to make speaker-projected meaning **visible** as a frozen hop: structure key, legal groups, six-axis stand-in, CIE pressure, Δh, leftover. It is a research vehicle. It is not a claim that the machine is a person’s meaning-object.
