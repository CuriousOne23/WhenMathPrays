$$

- $M_t$ is meaning at turn t

# **5. How IdOB Uses A × B to Maintain Continuity**

- IdOB stabilizes topic.

If A shifts topic and B indicates drift:

- IdOB updates topic.

If A contradicts topic and B indicates conflict:

- IdOB resolves topic.
