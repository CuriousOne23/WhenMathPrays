## The sky is blue.

Heard as a told claim: a theme, a state. That pairing is the human skip. It is not input.

Illegal reopenings:

- SROB emits a span SOB did not cut, so that "blue" can count as a segment without a coverage check.
- CnOB writes `semantic_core` from `struct_roles`. A positional label is not the packet dict.
- SmOB re-segments so the state word sits in a nicer span, and calls that a smoothing operation. Smoothing may stabilize residue. It may not recut.
- IdOB drops or redraws the theme span because the packet would be cleaner without it. The sum may activate a catalog family over the frozen cut. It may not reopen the cut.
- The sky, or blue, is called an IdOB object. The deliverable is one `idob_packet`.
