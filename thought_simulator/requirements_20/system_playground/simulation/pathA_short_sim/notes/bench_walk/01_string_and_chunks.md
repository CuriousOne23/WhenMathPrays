# Bench walk 1 — string and chunks

**Look at:** the traveler, then pencil circles.  
**Writer:** Intake, then Structural Observation Block (SOB).  
**Next:** [02_hats_and_fits.md](02_hats_and_fits.md)

## Utterance

False image: a fact about the world.  
On the bench: the sentence as a string, before any job is assigned.  
Nearest picture: the sound still in the air.

*The book is on the table.* Nothing has been decided. Not a meaning. Not a claim.

## Segment

False image: "the book" is a thing you can pick up.  
On the bench: a slice of the string that SOB was willing to keep together.  
Nearest picture: a clerk circling a phrase with a pencil. The circle is not the book.

| Utterance | Circles you should be able to point at |
|---|---|
| *The sky is blue.* | *the sky*; *blue* |
| *The book is on the table.* | *the book*; *on the table* |
| *Where is the book that is on the table?* | question word; *the book*; relative material; *on the table* |

If you cannot circle it, you are not looking at an Identity Observation Block (IdOB) problem yet. The first desk has not drawn.

Official field names live with SOB (`struct_segments`, `segment_tokens`). This page does not invent them.
