# Bench walk 2 — hats and fits

**Look at:** a coat-check tag, a slot that closes, guests at one wedding.  
**Writers:** Structural Refinement Observation Block (SROB), Constraint Observation Block (CnOB), Semantic Observation Block (SmOB).  
**Next:** [03_signed_form.md](03_signed_form.md)

## Structural label

False image: Actor, Place, a kind of object.  
On the bench: the job of a chunk. SROB puts a hat on a circle.  
Nearest picture: a coat-check tag, "owner" or "bag," tied to a coat. The tag is not a person.

*The book is on the table.* *the book* gets the thing-talked-about hat. *on the table* gets the place hat. Those hats are not IdOBObjects.

## Constraint

False image: grammar class, or a ban on meaning.  
On the bench: a yes/no CnOB already answered. This combination may close, or it left a scrap.  
Nearest picture: "these two forms fit the slot; these two do not."

*Is* plus *on* can close as location of a thing. It must not close as "the book is the table." A scrap that did not close is `constraint_residue`, not a catalog entry.

## Basin cue

False image: meaning has started, because the word semantic appeared.  
On the bench: a note that two pieces belong in one picture even if they are not neighbors. SmOB writes it.  
Nearest picture: two guests at a wedding who are not standing together and are still in the same wedding.

Book and table stay one scene. That is not a definition of book.

## Two adjacencies, not the third

1. String adjacency: circles that sit next to each other. SOB, CnOB. *book* next to *that is on the table*.
2. Basin adjacency: pieces in one picture. SmOB.

The third adjacency is between stamp cards. It waits until [07_two_clerks.md](07_two_clerks.md). Say which one you mean.
