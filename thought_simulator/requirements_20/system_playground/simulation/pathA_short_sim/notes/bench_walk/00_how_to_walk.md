You are not missing a detail. You are looking at a workshop and naming it with kitchen words. The first name is usually the wrong room.

This track is a new language lesson. Each page tells you what to look at, the false image, the thing on the bench, and the nearest honest picture from ordinary life.

## What you think you are entering

You think you are entering a dictionary of meanings, or a map of ideas, or the place where *book* lives. You are entering a bench where a heard sentence is taken apart, the parts are written down, a few tools are allowed to stamp claim-tags, and the stamps are added into one card. The card is as far as this path goes.

Nearest picture: a customs desk, not a library. The sentence is the traveler. The tools do not contain the traveler's life. They stamp a card.

## Question that stays in bounds

Do not start from "what does this sentence mean?" That skips the workshop.

Start from: what am I allowed to point at right now?
