# Bench walk 4 — the stamp card

**Look at:** a visa stamp and its instruction card.  
**Token:** IdOBObject. Step 7.  
**Authority:** [idob_object_space.md](../idob_object_space/idob_object_space.md)  
**Next:** [05_on_switch.md](05_on_switch.md)

False image: the book, a region of meaning, or the finished idea academia will own.  
On the bench: one named rubber stamp in a drawer. *Locative* is a stamp. It does not contain the table. It may, if switched on, press claim-words onto a card.  
Nearest picture: a visa stamp with a written instruction card. The card says when it may be used, what words it may print, and which other stamps it is willing to share a passport with.

## Why this is the unit

An IdOBObject is the improvable unit of the IdOB meaning hop. It is a summand. It is not the hop, not the packet, and not Structure-to-Meaning (S2M).

A paper pressures fields a diff can show:

- Coverage of this stamp: which signed forms set `act=1`.
- Resolution of this stamp: which claim keys it may print, and which segment shapes it claims.
- Efficiency: priority, near versus far, merge versus suppress versus coexist, and when residual must not win.

`registry_digest` changes when the card changes. Eight cards live under `support/idob_objects/`. The "when" line is still blank in YAML (`activation: {}`). The habit of when to stamp still lives in `idob.legacy`. That blank line is why the stamp feels fake. It is not evidence that the stamp is the meaning.

## What you may not say

The book is not an IdOBObject. The packet is not an IdOBObject. Actor is not an IdOBObject. Finishing the sum does not promote the stamp into the card.
