## **1. How Ages Define Human Self‑Description**

Humanity has passed through several conceptual Ages, each of which quietly rewrote how humans describe themselves.

### **Agricultural Age**
Humans understood themselves as:
- workers of land  
- beings embedded in cycles  
- extensions of soil and season  

### **Industrial Age**
Humans understood themselves as:
- machines  
- cogs  
- producers  
- efficiency units  

### **Information Age**
Humans understood themselves as:
- processors  
- nodes  
- knowledge workers  
- interpreters of data  

Each Age installs a new “innate self‑description” — a way people think of themselves without realizing the Age is doing the thinking.

The AI Age is no exception.
