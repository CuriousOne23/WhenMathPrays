## Purpose of the closeout

Record that Category Selection has reached the present structural-confidence threshold for continued downstream TS development, with explicit deferred characterization remaining open. This is progression, not finalization.

## Accepted evidence base

- Stage 7 Batch 1 (accepted): ordered source-composition response for affective / `cold`.
- Stage 7 Batch 2 (accepted): same local ordering for physical / `table` and perspectival / `place`.
- Stage 7 Batch 3 (accepted): limited construction robustness and first-order pairwise competition.
- Stage 7 Batch 4A (accepted): clean local mapped/unmapped representability boundary.
- Stage 6 local calibration (PASS): operational expressibility of Palette v1.
- Palette v1 configuration implementation without Category Selection mechanism change.

Historical EXP1–EXP4 records remain historical implementation/reference evidence with their original limitations. They are not re-interpreted as Palette v1 formal characterization.

## What has earned structural confidence

- Local ordered source-composition response under the frozen contract.
- Replication of that response across three structurally distinct category identities.
- Limited construction robustness on tested alternate scaffolds.
- Interpretable first-order competition without collapse.
- Clean mapped/unmapped distinction with the authorized measurement surface.
- Provenance preservation.
- Successful Palette v1 expansion via research/configuration content only.

These properties have received independent controlled challenge and can be carried forward as part of the working TS structure.

## What remains only locally established

- Source-composition ordering is local to the three tested majors and the source-weight configuration used.
- Construction robustness is limited to the tested scaffolds.
- Competition evidence covers only one source direction per pair.
- Mapped/unmapped distinction is limited to the clean control cases of Batch 4A.

## Explicit deferred work

- Batch 4B: deeper edge pressure (weak, ambiguous, qualification-limited, or inventory-inadequate conditions).
- Inventory composition.
- Reciprocal competition directions.
- Additional categories beyond the Palette v1 triad.
- Improved measurements (entropy, top-versus-second margin) and fine interior geometry.
- Multi-turn or sequential stability.

These are deferred characterization items, not progression blockers.

## Measurement qualifications

Authorized surface (distribution, top category, confidence, rationale, provenance) was sufficient for the progression judgment. Entropy and margin remain unavailable; interior-geometry and ambiguity-separation claims are not authorized and were not required for the present decision.

## Controlled-evolution / scalability finding

Palette v1 was designed, implemented as configuration, calibrated, and successfully exercised without requiring Category Selection mechanism reconstruction. This is relevant evidence that ordinary research growth can proceed by qualifying probes and attaching them to established experimental contracts. It is one successful expansion, not proof of unlimited scalability.

### Content/mechanism separation evidence

Palette v1 provides first-order controlled evidence that Category Selection behavior is not limited to a single convenient lexical probe or category identity. The same local source-composition relationship was observed across the qualified `cold`/affective, `table`/physical, and `place`/perspectival realizations without category-specific changes to the Category Selection mechanism.

This supports treating Category Selection, within the tested Palette v1 region, as a reusable selection structure over qualified category evidence rather than as a collection of probe-specific special cases. It also supports the architectural use of the Category Selection result as a comparatively stable interface for downstream TS work while research content such as probes and categories evolves.

This finding remains bounded. It does not establish arbitrary-category generalization, unlimited scalability, category-independence under all conditions, or that future category expansion will never require mechanism refinement. Each future expansion remains subject to qualification, characterization, and the controlled-evolution / revalidation discipline.

## Section 13 decision

**B. Proceed with explicit reservations/deferred work**

The accumulated controlled evidence indicates that remaining and reasonably anticipated limitations are more consistent with bounded extension, parameterization, measurement improvement, or local refinement than with the need for wholesale reconstruction of the Category Selection process.

## Progression statement

Category Selection may be carried forward into the next stage of TS development as a credible working structure. Downstream TS components may therefore treat the current Category Selection result structure as a working interface under the explicit reservations and return conditions recorded here. Later refinement, expansion, remeasurement, or bounded refactoring remains expected and compatible with this progression.

## Return conditions

Category Selection should be reopened when downstream TS work produces evidence that:

- the basic structural model is no longer adequate for the new demands, or
- a previously deferred question (edge pressure, inventory composition, additional categories, improved measurements, etc.) has become consequential for continued development.

Reopening should follow the controlled-evolution discipline in Section 8B: explicit motivation, scope, affected evidence, preserved invariants, and required revalidation.

## Non-claims

This closeout does not claim that Category Selection is complete, globally characterized, proven, exhaustive, or frozen against later refactoring. It records only that the present structural-confidence threshold has been met for continued downstream work.
