# Packet now, mapping later

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Sum contract, still in force:** [../../architecture/idob_meaning_lock.md](../../architecture/idob_meaning_lock.md)

## Today

Identity Observation Block (IdOB) sums contributions from activated catalog objects. The `idob_packet` is that sum. `semantic_core` is a dict. The debugger observes the packet and does not sum again.

On this path, today, meaning is that packet. It is not a feeling, not a dictionary entry, not Meaning Signal Layer (MSL) stance, and not the Structure-to-Meaning (S2M) vector \(M\).

No binding, no packet. The unknown string is the check. If no precommitted family binds, inventing a packet would be pretending the machine understood the utterance.

## Later, not this pass

The intended job of IdOB is to map the composed floor to meaning. The packet is the legal composition that mapping must consume. It is not, today, already that mapped output.

The mapping may not reopen `struct_segments`, `struct_roles`, or constraints. CIE, MSL, and \(M\) stay out of the sum until a later pass says otherwise, and that pass is after the ready line.
