# Category Selection Characterization Program Closeout

**Document:** notes/program/category_selection_characterization_program_closeout.md  
**Status:** Program-level closeout record (non-normative).  
**Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Purpose:** Synthesize the Category Selection characterization program. Record what was undertaken, what was established, what was not established, the downstream S2M repercussion, why active characterization is placed on hold, and the conditions under which it may be reopened. This document does not claim global completeness or scalability.

## Strongest status statement

The present Category Selection characterization cycle is closed at its characterized boundary. Category Selection is available as a bounded working interface for downstream TS use. This closeout does not establish global completeness or scalability. Deferred characterization remains available for demand-driven reopening, while scaling beyond the present characterized environment requires a separate progression and expansion-assurance strategy.

## Why the characterization program was undertaken

Category Selection produces a committed `TP.semantic.isc_category_result` envelope that downstream consumers may read. Before carrying that result into S2M as a working interface, the program sought structural confidence that the mechanism exhibited credible, bounded behavior under controlled conditions, and that its result could affect which available candidates proceed without itself writing meaning or collapsing into legitimation.

## Sequence performed

1. Stage 1 readiness / capability map.  
2. Stage 2 axis and coverage reference (minimum meaningful coverage, palette requirements).  
3. Controlled Palette v1 design and implementation.  
4. Stage 7 formal campaign (Batches 1–4A).  
5. Palette v1 characterization closeout (progression decision, deferred items, return conditions).  
6. Downstream contextual-candidate-admission load (four qualified baseline observations).  
7. Supporting S2M realization and terminology clarification (treated as context, not a new Category Selection claim).

## What was established

- Under the Palette v1 controlled environment, Category Selection exhibited structural confidence sufficient for progression.  
- The result is usable as a working interface: a usable `top_category` or matching cue values can narrow `selected_space`.  
- Downstream, that narrowing controls contextual candidate admission (`category_supported`). Only admitted candidates proceed to structural legitimation. Category support does not itself legitimize or write meaning.  
- The four qualified admission baselines confirmed local adequacy of this handoff under clear, single-result conditions. No deferred Category Selection question was forced open.  
- Operational S2M path under the characterized conditions: Category Selection evidence → contextual candidate admission → structural legitimation/refusal → legitimized extension or null. Finite customized composite is represented by default meaning co-present with any legitimized extensions.

## What was not established

- Global completeness of Category Selection.  
- S2M adequacy beyond the tested handoff.  
- Behavior under competing evidence, inventory mismatch, weak confidence, high-entropy distributions, category-with-no-candidate, or multi-turn accumulation.  
- Scalability beyond the present bounded category/candidate environment.  
- That the present organization will continue to provide useful discrimination as inventories or context grow.

## What remains deferred

- Deeper edge pressure.  
- Inventory composition.  
- Reciprocal competition directions.  
- Additional categories beyond the Palette v1 triad.  
- Improved measurements / interior geometry.  
- Multi-turn or sequential stability.  

These remain open. They are not automatically scheduled.

## Downstream S2M repercussion

Category Selection has a concrete functional place in the present S2M realization. Its result determines which available candidates are considered for structural assessment. Structural legitimation remains a distinct subsequent decision. Write materializes only the legitimized set. The resulting S2M record (default meaning + legitimized extensions) is the finite customized composite. This relationship was demonstrated under the qualified admission conditions and does not require an additional post-legitimation selection stage or a separately constructed composite object.

## Why active characterization stops here

No observation accumulated since the Palette v1 closeout has made a deferred item consequential. The admission load used clear, non-competing, inventory-matching results and confirmed the authorized working-interface role. Further characterization is not forced by present evidence. Active work is therefore placed on HOLD at the characterized boundary.

## Return / reopening conditions

Resume Category Selection characterization only if a concrete observation or explicit downstream demand shows that the existing result cannot support continued use while preserving the distinctions already demonstrated (for example, competing evidence, inventory mismatch, or weak confidence producing an outcome the present handoff cannot process without distorting the admission/legitimation boundary). Until such an observation appears, further characterization is premature.

## Scalability and expansion-assurance handoff

The characterized mechanism is supported within a bounded category/candidate environment. Scaling beyond that environment is not established by this cycle. A separate scalability / expansion-assurance program is required before larger category spaces or expanded YAML/reference structures are treated as evidence of scalable Category Selection behavior. That program must distinguish regression (prior characterized behavior still holds) from coverage/progression (new combinations created by expansion have been qualified) and must not allow expansion to outrun structural support or characterization. This closeout does not design that program.

## Program status

Available for bounded downstream use under the conditions already established. Not globally complete. Not demonstrated to scale. Deferred characterization remains demand-driven.
