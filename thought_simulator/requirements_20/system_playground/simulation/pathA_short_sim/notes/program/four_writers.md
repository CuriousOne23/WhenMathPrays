# Four writers

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Official names:** Structural Observation Block (SOB), Structural Refinement Observation Block (SROB), Constraint Observation Block (CnOB), Semantic Observation Block (SmOB).
**Normative homes:** 20.40.010–20.40.040. This page does not thin those requirements.

These four are the structure floor. Identity Observation Block (IdOB) is not a fifth structural cut.

| Writer | May record | May not |
|---|---|---|
| SOB | Spans and token coverage. A span that does not cover its tokens is not a segment. | The packet. A semantic role. A repair of an unbound stream. |
| SROB | A positional label on a span SOB already wrote. Teaching alias: `struct_roles`. | A new segment. `semantic_core`. Semantic role labeling. |
| CnOB | Which precommitted rules hold, which fail, and what residue remains. | A span invented to satisfy a rule. `struct_roles` treated as `semantic_core`. |
| SmOB | Which unresolved neighborhood stabilizes into an adjacent cue, which smoothing operations ran, and what basin residue remains. Smoothing is an operation, not the name of the stage. | A new segmentation. The packet. |

Order rule: a later binding may not invent an earlier one the structure did not allow, and may not reopen a binding already written. Residue is forwarded. Compatibility aliases exist. Later stages may consume, label, constrain, cue, or sum. They may not invent the prior cut.

IdOB may activate catalog families over the frozen cut. It may not re-segment, re-label, or reopen constraints to make a packet come out clean.
