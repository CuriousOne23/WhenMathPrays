**Status:** Executed characterization closeout / present-realization boundary record (non-normative).  
**Purpose:** Consolidate where the current IdOB traversal characterization legitimately stops, what is safely deferred, what remains unavailable/TBD, and what should trigger reopening.

## 2. Established current boundary

- **S_t:** bounded realized seed/written-relation surface exists.
- **door:** proposal-defined door legality is unrealized.
- **C_t:** finite endpoint-association candidate surface is observable but remains surrogate (not door-legal C_t).
- **door structural seam:** C1-C6 characterization supports `SAFE_TO_DEFER` for local candidate-producer substitution while shape remains stable.
- **E_t:** aggregate candidate support is observable; generic explicit evidence-specific correspondence is not established.
- **L_t:** narrow duplicate linkage is observable; generic lineage and affirmative independence are not established.
- **D_t/Q_t:** presently blocked/unavailable for legitimate reconstruction.
- **B_t:** blocked by missing D_t prerequisites.
- **P_t:** current category softmax is not traversal-candidate P_t.
- **H_t:** traversal H_t is not established.
- **continue/OuBA:** traversal sufficiency rule is not established.

## 3. Why work stops here

Later stages cannot presently be characterized legitimately without one or more invalid moves:

- manufacturing unavailable correspondence/dependency facts;
- treating absence as opposition or independence;
- promoting aggregate/reconstructed correspondence into generic explicit correspondence;
- promoting surrogate candidates into door-legal C_t;
- promoting category normalization into traversal P_t.

Stopping here is a positive characterization result: current realizable and bounded-unavailable states are now explicit.

## 4. Structural health and safe deferral

- **Door seam:** bounded structural characterization supports `SAFE_TO_DEFER` for producer substitution at the current candidate seam.
- **Evidence->cue attachment:** known as a future local extension topic in correspondence records, but not generically realized.
- **Opposition/lineage/independence:** remain unavailable/TBD; no final extension design is claimed.

## 5. Surrogate boundary

Preserved boundaries:

- endpoint-association candidates != proposal-defined door-legal C_t
- aggregate candidate support != generic evidence-specific correspondence
- category-scoring distribution != traversal P_t
- current OuBA path-commit behavior != traversal sufficiency rule

## 6. Present validity boundary

The present traversal characterization is valid only over the bounded surfaces actually represented, executed, and observed in this cycle.

No global or numerical completeness boundary is claimed.

This closeout applies [20.740_ts_realization_qualification_guidance.md](../../../../../20.740_ts_realization_qualification_guidance.md): bounded utility now, explicit boundary now, and future extension should be re-characterized rather than presumed.

## 7. Reopen conditions

Traversal characterization should reopen when at least one condition is met:

1. A deliberately unavailable prerequisite is authorized for realization.
2. New represented evidence changes prerequisite availability.
3. A current surrogate boundary becomes insufficient for the next useful capability.
4. Structural-health evidence changes an existing `SAFE_TO_DEFER` decision.
5. A new bounded experiment can clear additional fog without fabrication.

This closeout does not authorize implementation by itself.
