# Grammar and structure

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Does not define tokens.** The seam remains the name authority: [../../architecture/idob_seam.md](../../architecture/idob_seam.md).

This page is for the difficulty that keeps returning: ordinary grammar against the structural cut. Full understanding of structure space is not required before the floor can be checked. The distinction is required, because grammar repairs and this path must not.

## Grammar starts after a claim is heard

*The sky is blue.* arrives as an assertion about the sky. Subject, copula, and predicate then explain that assertion. If the sentence is broken, ordinary grammar leans toward repair: a missing word, a typo, a likely intended claim. The hearing supplies the answer. The parse is judged by whether it recovers that answer.

Grammar says: I know what this means. Now let me parse it.

## Structure starts where the hearing is not allowed to count

The input is a token stream. The unknown string makes the same rule obvious, because there is nothing to re-hear:

```
LLhfds pw Ppen qqoubx&
```

You can still write pieces, order, coverage, what place a precommitted label will bind, what rule holds, and what stays odd. You cannot write a claim the stream did not bind. A miss stays a miss. Nothing bound is not an error to repair into a sentence.

Structure says: I do not know what this means. Let me see what survives.

## Why the stages are writers

SOB, SROB, CnOB, and SmOB are not four pictures of one object, and they are not a grammar pipeline with new names. They are writers in a fixed order. Detail: [four_writers.md](four_writers.md).

A later writer may consume what an earlier writer recorded. It may not go back and cut a better sentence because the heard claim would prefer one. That prohibition is the difference from grammar. Theory entrance: [../../debug/pathA_structure_theory.md](../../debug/pathA_structure_theory.md).

## What this page does not settle

It does not settle the ultimate nature of structure space. That difficulty can remain while the runner either prints a form or names it as unsupported. Silent repair is the failure this page is here to prevent.
