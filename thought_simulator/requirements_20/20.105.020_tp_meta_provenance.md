# TP Metadata Provenance — Origin, Commit Lineage, and Primitive Responsibilities

This document defines the provenance rules governing metadata fields within the Thought Packet (TP). Provenance ensures that each metadata field records its origin, commit lineage, and primitive-level responsibility, enabling deterministic replay, stable routing, and consistent downstream meaning refinement.

## Scope

This specification covers provenance structure, primitive-origin rules, commit lineage rules, update authorization rules, and replay-safety constraints.

It does not redefine metadata fields or primitive behavior.

## Important TP Layers

The TP organizes information into distinct layers that support clear provenance tracking:

- **Intake Layer** — Raw and normalized input observations (InB, IIInB, IE).
- **Structural Layer** — Geometric and constraint-based residue (OB-Set).
- **Routing Layer** — Arbitration and pathway decisions (RBU, RB, RTU, CTP).
- **Meaning Signal Layer (MSL)** — Short structured tokens for qualifiers, clarifications, stance, shading, intent, direction, coherence, and subculture.
- **Continuity Layer** — Identity, referent lineage, and clarifying metadata (COB, CIL, CST).
- **Provenance & Audit Layer** — Commit history and replay metadata (TPU).
- **Entropy & Trajectory Layer** — ΔH% and stability tracking (TR, OuBA).

These layers help maintain separation of concerns while ensuring full traceability.

## Provenance Structure

Each metadata field carries provenance information describing:
- the primitive that produced the field
- the primitive that last updated the field
- the TPU commit in which the field was written
- the lineage of commits affecting the field

Provenance is stored under `TP.metadata.provenance`.

## Normative Requirements

Each metadata field shall record the identity of the primitive that produced it. (HLR-20.105.020-001)

Each metadata field shall record the identity of the primitive that last updated it. (HLR-20.105.020-002)

Each metadata field shall record the TPU commit identifier associated with its most recent update. (HLR-20.105.020-003)

Metadata fields shall preserve a lineage of commit identifiers representing all commits that modified the field. (HLR-20.105.020-004)

Metadata fields shall only be updated by primitives explicitly authorized to modify those fields. (HLR-20.105.020-005)

Provenance history shall be immutable once recorded, except when extended by authorized updates. (HLR-20.105.020-006)

Provenance information shall support deterministic replay, ensuring identical provenance when the same TP is re-executed through the same primitive sequence. (HLR-20.105.020-007)

All provenance information shall be visible to downstream primitives without requiring re-inspection of upstream behavior. (HLR-20.105.020-008)

## Clarifying-Field Provenance

Clarifying-field metadata (including hierarchical subfields) shall record the primitive that produced each clarifying field, clarifying subfield, and hierarchical clarifying sub-subfield. (HLR-20.105.020-009)

Clarifying-field metadata shall record the primitive that last updated each clarifying field, clarifying subfield, and hierarchical clarifying sub-subfield. (HLR-20.105.020-010)

Clarifying-field metadata shall record the TPU commit identifier associated with the most recent update to each clarifying field or subfield. (HLR-20.105.020-011)

Clarifying-field metadata shall preserve a commit-lineage history for each clarifying field and subfield. (HLR-20.105.020-012)

Clarifying-field metadata shall only be updated by primitives explicitly authorized to modify clarifying fields or clarifying importance. (HLR-20.105.020-013)

Clarifying-field provenance history shall be immutable once recorded, except when extended by authorized updates. (HLR-20.105.020-014)

Clarifying-field provenance shall support deterministic replay. (HLR-20.105.020-015)

Clarifying-field provenance shall be visible to downstream primitives. (HLR-20.105.020-016)

## Primitive Responsibilities

### CEx / CE
Record provenance for all context_metadata fields, including relevance_flags, copy_forward_flags, reset_flags, context_fields, and context_provenance.  
CEx SHALL record origin and last_update for all CE fields.  
CE SHALL preserve provenance across cycles and SHALL NOT overwrite meaning-layer provenance.

### OB-Set (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB)
Record provenance for all residue_metadata fields, including structural_residue, refinement_residue, constraint_residue, semantic_adjacent_residue, presemantic_hash, and residue_provenance.  
OB-Set SHALL record origin and last_update for each residue slice.  
Residue provenance SHALL remain immutable once committed by TPU.

### SSG / STPX
Record provenance for semantic_layer_metadata fields, including semantic_adjacent_signals, semantic_layer_hash, referent_adjacent_signals, modality_stance_cues, and semantic_layer_provenance.  
SSG and STPX SHALL record origin and last_update for all semantic-layer fields.  
Semantic-layer provenance SHALL support deterministic replay across routing cycles.

### ISc
Record provenance for scoring_metadata fields, including score_set, score_conflict, score_reason_code, and scoring_provenance.  
ISc SHALL record origin and last_update for all scoring fields.  
Scoring provenance SHALL remain immutable after TPU commit.

### IdOB
Record provenance for identity_metadata fields, including identity_basin, identity_shift_flags, subculture_assignment, qualifier_cluster, and identity_provenance.  
IdOB SHALL record origin for identity-layer signals; TPU SHALL record commit provenance.  
Identity provenance SHALL support deterministic replay across multi-cycle IdOB refinement.

### MCB
Record provenance for next_context_metadata fields, including next_context, direction, coherence, stance, subculture, and next_context_provenance.  
MCB SHALL record origin and last_update for next_context fields.  
Next-context provenance SHALL remain immutable after TPU commit.

### OuBA / SSRGn
Record provenance for freeze_metadata fields, including freeze_signature, rrw_binding, policy_signature, ssr_projection_map, and freeze_provenance.  
OuBA SHALL record commit-time provenance; SSRGn SHALL record freeze-time provenance.  
Freeze provenance SHALL support deterministic replay across Path-A → Path-B boundaries.

### InB / IIInB
Record provenance for intake-related fields.

### IE
Record provenance for normalization and repair fields.

### OB-Set
Record provenance for structural metadata.

### Routing Primitives (RBU, RB, RTU, CTP)
Record provenance for routing metadata.

### IdOB
Signals meaning updates (provenance recorded via TPU).

### TPU
Authoritative writer for commit_id, commit_sequence, primitive_origin, and commit_timestamp.

### MSL Fields
Provenance shall track origin of all qualifier, clarification, and subculture tokens.

## Provenance Equations

### Commit Lineage Representation

$$
\text{lineage} = \\{ c_1, c_2, \ldots, c_n \\}
$$

### Provenance Update Rule

When a primitive $P$ updates a metadata field:

$$
\text{provenance.last}_{update} = P
$$

and the commit lineage is extended accordingly.
