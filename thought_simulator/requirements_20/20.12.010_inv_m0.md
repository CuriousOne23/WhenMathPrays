## 0. What this document is for

This module names **INV-M0**: meaning-birth is an IdOB-only writer × field pair. It exists so Path A testbenches, lineup guards, and later Catalog / Harness bots can convict a leak without re-reading every primitive file.

It is **not** a primitive. It does not schedule hops. It does not replace 20.40 or 20.116. Those files own behavior and write walls. This file owns the **invariant name**, the **variable dictionary**, the **preservation argument**, the **perturbation-closure argument**, and the **cut** recorded in §1.1.

Source notes folded in:

1. Deterministic preservation against CE / CIL / SmOB / `next_context` attempts to midwife $M$.
2. Closure of all *legal* perturbations of those envelopes (individually legal, jointly meaning-adjacent).
3. The cut: a TP can be full in $E$ while $M$ remains $\bot$. Richness is not birth. That refusal is easy to miss and is not the same fact as “write walls exist.”

---

## 1. What INV-M0 means

Context can be arbitrarily meaning-adjacent. Birth is not that adjacency. Birth is only:

- the hop writer is IdOB-prm, and
- the crossing packet carries a selected group and a six-axis stand-in $M$.

CIE pressure $M' = \mathrm{clip}(M + \alpha I)$ is **after** birth. CE context, CIL intake, SmOB importance, and MCB `next_context` are **cues**. Folding cues into $M$ is a catalog defect, not a successful birth.

---

## 1.1 The cut (informative field observation)

Ordinary pipeline talk already has walls: each module writes its envelope. That is not the observation.

The observation is a **place** that talk keeps covering over.

Language, and most semantic architectures, treat a dense envelope as if it were already a stand-in. If the packet holds stance, conversation, importance, and a next-turn hint, a reader is invited to say “meaning has begun.” INV-M0 refuses that invitation.

**Plain statement.** A thought packet can be *full* and $M$ can still be absent. Fullness lives in $E$. Birth lives in $\Phi$. Those are not two names for one occupancy.

$$
E(T) \text{ rich} \;\not\Longrightarrow\; \Phi(T) \neq \bot
$$

Equivalently: occupancy of the cue union does not entail occupancy of the birth coordinates.

**Why this is easy to miss.** The cues are *about* the same utterance that will later support $M$. They are meaning-adjacent in ordinary English. The adjacency is real as *talk*. It is not real as *birth*. The system that does not name the cut will keep counting $E$ as $M$ because that is how prose works.

**What is not being claimed.** TS did not invent richness, context, or conversation. It named a refusal: richness does not count as $M$. That refusal is its own geometry — an emptiness that can sit next to a crowded packet.

**Working name.** Call that emptiness **the cut**: the first-class gap between “the TP already looks semantic” and “IdOB has stood on $\Phi$.” Standing in the cut is seeing a legal $T$ with $E(T) \neq \emptyset$ and $\Phi(T) = \bot$ as a *correct* state, not as a machine that forgot to interpret.

**Relation to the proofs.** §6 shows attempted midwives fail. §7 shows every legal product of those midwives leaves $\Phi$ fixed. The cut is what those proofs *are about*: the identity action of $\mathcal{L}^*$ on $\Phi$ is not a bookkeeping trick. It is the geometry in which a full packet is still pre-birth.

## 2. Implications

- SOB–SmOB can refine structure and emit signatures; they cannot satisfy $B$.
- Isolation IdOB fixtures remain legal with an empty $E$ or a fat $E$. Fat $E$ does not skip the map door.
- A lineup that runs CE → CIL → SmOB → MCB and then checks $\Phi = \bot$ is the harness form of this invariant. The present identity isolation bench does not yet run those four attackers.
- 20.50 typed destinations are unrelated. INV-M0 does not wait on RB tables.
- Replay of a legal attacker string cannot acquire birth on a second pass: those hops do not own $\Phi$.
- An implementation that writes $\Phi$ from rich $E$ is outside the legal hop set $\mathcal{L}$. INV-M0 classifies it as defective; it does not enlarge $\mathcal{L}$.
- A test or narrative that treats “context/conversation/importance/stance are populated” as proof that meaning has begun is reading $E$ as $\Phi$. That reading is the thing the cut forbids.

## 4. Normative requirements

**HLR-20.12.010-001**  
INV-M0 SHALL hold on every legal Path A snapshot: meaning-birth $B(T, w)$ is true only when $w$ is IdOB-prm.

**HLR-20.12.010-002**  
$B(T, w)$ SHALL be evaluated only as the conjunction: $w$ is IdOB-prm, $T.\mathrm{idob}.\mathrm{selected\_group\_id} \neq \bot$, and $T.\mathrm{idob}.\mathrm{meaning\_semantics} = M \in [0,1]^6$. CIE formation of $M'$ SHALL NOT by itself satisfy $B$.

**HLR-20.12.010-003**  
Every hop $w \in \mathcal{L}$ SHALL leave $\Phi$ unchanged. Those hops SHALL write only inside their $E_w \subset E$.

**HLR-20.12.010-004**  
Every finite product $W \in \mathcal{L}^*$ SHALL satisfy $W(T)|_\Phi = T|_\Phi$. Joint meaning-adjacency of fields in $E$ SHALL NOT authorize a write into $\Phi$.

**HLR-20.12.010-005**  
Equating any field in $E$ with any field in $\Phi$ SHALL be treated as a catalog defect under 20.116.030.

**HLR-20.12.010-006**  
Replay of a fixed $W \in \mathcal{L}^*$ on identical $T_0$ SHALL yield identical $W(T_0)$ and SHALL NOT produce $B$.

**HLR-20.12.010-007**  
A machine that writes $\Phi$ from $E$ SHALL be classified as outside $\mathcal{L}$, not as a legal perturbation of INV-M0.

**HLR-20.12.010-008**  
Occupancy or richness of $E$ SHALL NOT be treated as occupancy of $\Phi$ and SHALL NOT satisfy $B$. A snapshot with nonempty $E$ and $\Phi = \bot$ SHALL be treated as a legal pre-birth state.

Behavior of IdOB birth (map $\supseteq$ rank $\to$ prototype $M$ $\to$ CIE) remains 20.40.050. Write-ownership remains 20.116.020.

## 5. Birth predicate (informative restatement)

$$
B(T, w) \iff
\begin{cases}
w = \text{IdOB-prm} \\
T.\mathrm{idob}.\mathrm{selected\_group\_id} \neq \bot \\
T.\mathrm{idob}.\mathrm{meaning\_semantics} = M \in [0,1]^6
\end{cases}
$$

CIE may then form

$$
M' = \mathrm{clip}_{[0,1]^6}(M + \alpha I)
$$

INV-M0:

$$
\forall\, w \in \{\mathrm{SOB},\mathrm{SROB},\mathrm{CnOB},\mathrm{SmOB},\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{CIL},\mathrm{MCB}\},\ \forall\, T_k:\quad \neg B(T_{k+1}, w)
$$

Equivalently: if $B(T_{k+1}, w^*)$ then $w^*$ is IdOB-prm.

Catalog law forces $E \cap \Phi = \emptyset$.

The cut:

$$
E(T) \text{ rich} \;\not\Longrightarrow\; \Phi(T) \neq \bot
$$

## 6. Preservation under adversarial birth attempts (informative)

This section is the first source note. Cited walls: 20.40-003 / 007 / 008, 20.116.020-001 / 003 / 004, 20.116.030-001 / 003, 20.40.050-057 / 058, 20.40-004.

### 6.1 Fixture $T_0$

Pre-IdOB, isolation-legal:

- utterance: `The project deadline is Friday.`
- CE: `TP.metadata.context.stance = confirm`
- CIL: `TP.cil.intake_packet.selected_conversation = C9`
- SmOB-shaped: `TP.semantic.importance.deadline = 0.91`
- MCB-shaped: `TP.next_context.stance = scientific_stance`
- $\Phi = \bot$

This $T_0$ is an instance of the cut: $E$ is occupied, $\Phi$ is empty, and that pair is correct.

### 6.2 Attack CE

Assume $B(T_C, \mathrm{CE})$. Then CE wrote $\Phi$. CE owns only $E_{\mathrm{CE}}$. Contradiction. $\neg B(T_C, \mathrm{CE})$. Rich context remains. Richness is not birth.

### 6.3 Attack CIL

Assume $B(T_L, \mathrm{CIL})$. Then CIL wrote the crossing packet. `TP.cil.intake_packet` $\neq$ `TP.idob`. Contradiction. $\neg B(T_L, \mathrm{CIL})$.

### 6.4 Attack SmOB

Assume $B(T_S, \mathrm{SmOB})$. SmOB must not birth $M$ and must not take CE/CIL as authority for that write. Legal $M$ is the prototype of a map winner after a structural key. SmOB may emit the key. It does not run door + rank + load. Contradiction. $\neg B(T_S, \mathrm{SmOB})$.

Importance-only writes stay in $E_{\mathrm{Sm}}$ and still $\neg B$.

### 6.5 Attack next_context / MCB

Assume $B(T_N, \mathrm{MCB})$ or CIE-birth without IdOB. Birth requires $M$ already. $T_0$ has no $M$. Applying `next_context.stance` as $\alpha I$ is not a map winner and is not CIE. MCB must not write `TP.idob`. Contradiction. $\neg B(T_N, \mathrm{MCB})$.

### 6.6 Joint adversary

$$
T_\star = \mathrm{MCB} \circ \mathrm{SmOB} \circ \mathrm{CIL} \circ \mathrm{CE}(T_0)
$$

Each factor is $\neg B$. Composition of four $\neg B$ hops cannot satisfy $B$: $B$ requires the authorized writer and the $\Phi$ fields only that writer may set. Union of legal cue envelopes is still $E$. Folding $E$ into $M$ is forbidden.

Replay of $T_0$ through the same four hops yields the same empty $\Phi$. There is no “stronger context on the second run” clause.

### 6.7 Only legal birth

IdOB on $T_\star$ with a structure card and `cie_id = neutral` (isolation fixture):

1. card / assignment $\to$ six IDs $\to$ `structural_key`
2. map $\to$ candidates $\to$ rank $\subseteq$ map
3. winner $\to$ prototype $M$
4. CIE $\alpha = 0$ $\to$ $M' = M$
5. writer = IdOB-prm $\to$ $B$ holds

Cues on $T_\star$ may be read. They must not replace steps 1–4.

### 6.8 Contradiction schema

$$
\begin{align*}
&\text{Assume } B(T, w) \land w \in \mathcal{L} \\
&\implies w \text{ wrote } \Phi \\
&\implies w \text{ owns } \Phi \\
&\implies w = \text{IdOB-prm} \\
&\implies \bot
\end{align*}
$$

INV-M0 is the negation of that assumption.

## 7. Closure under legal perturbation (informative)

This section is the second source note. Axioms are not restated; only $\Phi$-identity is used.

### 7.1 Support lemma

If $w \in \mathcal{L}$ and $T' = w(T)$, then $T'|_\Phi = T|_\Phi$, because $\mathrm{supp}(w) \subseteq E_w \subset E$ and $E \cap \Phi = \emptyset$.

### 7.2 Finite composition

Let $W = w_n \circ \cdots \circ w_1$ with each $w_i \in \mathcal{L}$. Then $W(T)|_\Phi = T|_\Phi$ by induction on $n$. $\mathcal{L}^*$ acts as the identity on $\Phi$.

Joint meaning-adjacency lives entirely in $E$. It never enters $\Phi$.

### 7.3 Maximal legal payloads

| Hop | Legal mutation still in $E$ |
|-----|-----------------------------|
| CE | `context.intent` names the deadline; candidate_set uses axis-like tags |
| CIL | intake carries a prior-turn six-axis copy under a CIL-local key |
| SmOB | `importance.deadline = 0.91`; signature that would map to a group *if* IdOB ran |
| MCB | `next_context.stance = scientific_stance`, `mcb_delta_h = 0.22` |

After all four, $\Phi$ remains $\bot$. An informal story assembled from $E(T_\star)$ is a function of $E$, not of $\Phi$. No legal hop in $\mathcal{L}$ has the output rule “if $E$ is rich, write $\Phi$.”

### 7.4 Contradiction without inspecting $E$

Assume some $W \in \mathcal{L}^*$ satisfies $B(W(T_0), w^*)$ for $w^* \in \mathcal{L}$. Then $W(T_0)|_\Phi \neq \bot$. Closure gives $W(T_0)|_\Phi = T_0|_\Phi = \bot$. Impossible. Richness of $E$ is not a premise and cannot discharge the assumption.

### 7.5 Replay-equivalence

For any legal strings $W, W'$:

$$
W(T_0)|_\Phi = W'(T_0)|_\Phi = T_0|_\Phi = \bot
$$

All legal perturbation histories are equivalent on $\Phi$. Determinism of each $w \in \mathcal{L}$ gives full-TP equality on a *fixed* $W$. Two replays cannot diverge into birth. INV-M0 is constant on $[T_0]_\Phi$. IdOB is the first map that can leave that class.
