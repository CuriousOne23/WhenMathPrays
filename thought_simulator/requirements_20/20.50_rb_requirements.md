# **1. Purpose & Scope** *(Informative)*

The Relational Basin (RB‑prm) is the **relational topology and routing‑proposal authority** for Pipeline A.

RB determines how Thought Packets / Thought Particles (TPs):

- traverse basins  
- form lanes  
- split  
- merge  
- escalate inquiries  
- trigger TR updates  
- express first‑order **local vs non‑local** adjacency under Routing–Entropy Dynamics (RED)

In foundation terms, RB realizes the first‑order operator:

$$
\mathcal{R} : (\mathbf{F},\ \text{IdOB}_{view},\ \text{context}_{allowed}) \rightarrow \text{RB}\_{out}
$$

where $\mathbf{F}$ is the invariant vector from the relational model, and RB_out includes routing‑filter content plus first‑order fields such as adjacency class, displacement scale, and regime hint.

RB consumes **committed `TP.TR`** in a **read‑only** manner.  
RB does **not** derive TR, modify TR, interpret semantics, resolve identity (IdOB owns that), index execution‑flow geometry (DCB owns $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$), or perform any Pipeline‑B responsibilities.

RB enforces:

- deterministic routing  
- TR gating  
- multi‑core routing discipline  
- split/merge arbitration  
- routing bounds  
- messy‑input routing  
- replay determinism  
- canonical routing‑filter construction  
- first‑order local / non‑local adjacency classification consistent with RED  

RB performs **no semantic interpretation**, **no OB extraction**, **no TB truth evaluation**, and **no SSR behavior**.

### **1.1 Curvature and layer separation** *(Informative)*

| Symbol | Layer | Owner / use |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$ | Path‑A execution‑flow | DCB `geometric_state.curvature` — accounting only |
| $\kappa_{\text{id}}$ | Identity trajectory on IGM | IdOB / identity geometry diagnostics |
| $\kappa_{\text{route}}$ | Routing trajectory | RB / RED diagnostics |

RB does not treat DCB $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$ as identity or routing curvature.

### **1.2 Shared regimes** *(Informative)*

Cycle regime labels (Stable, Refinement, Drift, Transition, Collapse) follow the **shared regime table** in `ts_invariant_relational_model.md`. Thresholds there are provisional for examination. RB may emit a `regime_hint` consistent with that table when $\mathbf{F}$ components (or TP approximations) are available; RB does not invent a second regime system.

### **1.3 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption** *(Informative)*

RB‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded TP‑stream metadata when such metadata is applicable to routing‑filter formation, relational‑topology evaluation, TR‑gating, or first‑order RED adjacency classification. Applicable metadata includes:

- structural_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, SSG)  
- cue_envelope metadata (STPX)  
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)  
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)  
- normalization_metadata (IE)  
- semantic_layer_metadata (IdOB, MCB) — **read‑only IdOB view**  
- provenance_metadata (`TP.metadata.*`) — produced by TPU, CEx‑Pck, MSL, CIL, IdOB, OuBA, SSRGn, DCB  
- lineage_metadata (`TP.lineage_log[]`) — produced by TPU  
- entropy and signature histories — produced by TR, OuBA, SSRGn, TPU  
- DCB `geometric_state` / history — **execution‑flow context only**  
- invariant approximations for $\mathbf{F}$ components when logged ($I_{\text{stab}}$, $R_{\text{res}}$, $P_{\text{cont}}$, $L_{\text{depth}}$, $Rt_{\text{adj}}$, $\Delta H$, $E_{\text{dens}}$, $C_{\text{coh}}$)

RB‑prm treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
RB‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to routing‑filter formation, relational‑topology evaluation, TR‑gating, or RED adjacency classification.  
RB‑prm does not consume Pipeline‑B envelopes, truth/done fields, semantic $\Delta H\%$, or any supervisory fields.  
RB‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as routing‑adjacent features.

### **1.4 First‑order RB_out fields** *(Informative)*

In addition to the canonical routing filter (§5), foundation theory describes RB_out fields useful for progressive observation:

- `route_proposal` — next neighborhood / role‑family target (symbolic or ordinal proxy)  
- `adjacency_class` — `local` or `non_local`  
- `rt_adj` — scalar or structured routing‑adjacency observation  
- `regime_hint` — shared regime label when computable  
- `displacement_scale` — small / medium / large per RED interaction law  

These fields may be embedded in the routing filter or exported alongside it; normative export rules are in the HLRs below.

# **2. Pipeline‑A Boundary Discipline** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑031**
RB SHALL operate exclusively in Pipeline A and SHALL NOT read `exec_plan` or `exec_trace`.

### **HLR‑20.050‑032**
RB SHALL NOT read `truth_hypotheses` or `routing_epoch_id`.

### **HLR‑20.050‑048**
Pipeline‑B modules SHALL NOT alter RB routing decisions or mutate routing metadata owned by RB.

### **HLR‑20.012‑010** *(cross‑document; retained)*
Seed SHALL NOT influence routing outcomes; RB SHALL exclude seed from routing state.

### **HLR‑20.050‑068**
RB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`, `tr_needs_update`, semantic_core, `TP.context`, IdOB envelope fields, DCB‑owned geometric_state / geometric_history / dcb_events ownership, `TP.intake`, or `TP.process` fields other than RB routing‑filter export and RB_out fields defined for RB ownership.

### **HLR‑20.050‑069**
RB SHALL treat IdOB outputs strictly as a read‑only view and SHALL NOT mutate identity roles, candidates, identity provenance, or IdOB geometry fields.

### **HLR‑20.050‑070**
RB SHALL NOT treat DCB `geometric_state.curvature` ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$) as identity curvature ($\kappa_{\text{id}}$) or routing curvature ($\kappa_{\text{route}}$).

RB is strictly **read‑only** except for writing the routing filter and RB‑owned RB_out export fields.

# **3. TR‑Gating Contract** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑027**
RB SHALL route a TP to TR **iff** `TP.tr_needs_update = true`.

### **HLR‑20.050‑028**
RB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` or modify `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.050‑004**
RB routing decisions SHALL be replayable from deterministic logs.

### **HLR‑20.050‑005**
RB SHALL enforce deterministic firing‑order and transition‑order computation.

### **Routing Equation (SSG‑Aligned + foundation inputs)** *(Normative equation contract)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑071**
RB SHALL implement routing as a deterministic function of read‑only inputs:

$$
RB\\_{route(TP)} = f(\text{TP.input\\_fields},\ \text{TP.TR},\ \text{TP.tr\\_needs\\_update},
\ \text{TP.process.}\Delta H,\ \text{TP.semantic.lineage},\ \text{TP.process.routing\\_metadata},
\ \text{policy\\_signature},\ \text{IdOB\\_view?},\ \mathbf{F}\text{‑approx?})
$$

Optional IdOB view and $\mathbf{F}$ approximations, when present, remain read‑only and SHALL NOT relax determinism or replay requirements.

# **4. Multi‑Core Routing** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑049**
RB SHALL route each TP only to OBs whose `orthogonality_signature` matches the TP’s assigned core domain.

### **HLR‑20.050‑050**
RB SHALL enforce core‑local firing‑order computation.

### **HLR‑20.050‑059**
RB SHALL maintain strict routing‑domain isolation across `core_id` boundaries; no cross‑core lane consolidation SHALL occur.

### **HLR‑20.050‑060**
RB SHALL treat `core_id` and `orthogonality_signature` strictly as routing‑domain metadata and SHALL NOT interpret them semantically.

# **5. Deterministic Routing Filter** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑021**
RB SHALL compute a deterministic routing filter each cycle.

### **HLR‑20.050‑022**
The routing filter SHALL be representable, inspectable, logged, and replayable.

### **HLR‑20.050‑023**
RB SHALL apply deterministic relational‑topology rules governing traversal, lane formation, and OB selection.

### **HLR‑20.050‑051**
The routing filter SHALL be exportable in canonical form with stable ordering.

### **HLR‑20.050‑038**
All nested maps and ID arrays SHALL use canonical ordering.

### **Canonical Routing Filter Structure** *(Informative shape; required fields enforced by HLRs)*

```
routing_filter {
    selected_ob_ids[],        // canonical sort by ob_id
    lane_projections[],
    delta_h_routing_context,  // Q32.32
    firing_order[],           // deterministic sequence
    transition_rationale[],
    policy_justification{},
    inquiry_escalation?,
    merge_eligibility?,
    split_directive?,
    // first-order foundation fields (when enabled in implementation)
    adjacency_class?,         // local | non_local
    rt_adj?,
    regime_hint?,             // Stable | Refinement | Drift | Transition | Collapse
    displacement_scale?,      // small | medium | large
    route_proposal?
}
```

### **HLR‑20.050‑072**
When foundation RED fields are enabled for a build, RB SHALL include `adjacency_class` as either `local` or `non_local` in the exported routing filter or paired RB_out structure.

### **HLR‑20.050‑073**
When foundation RED fields are enabled, RB SHALL compute `displacement_scale` deterministically from available $Rt_{\text{adj}}$ and $\|\Delta H\|$ (or documented TP proxies) in accordance with the first‑order RED interaction law (local/small → small; local/large or non‑local/small → medium; non‑local/large → large).

### **HLR‑20.050‑074**
When $\mathbf{F}$ approximations sufficient for the shared regime table are available, RB SHALL emit `regime_hint` using only the shared regime labels: Stable, Refinement, Drift, Transition, Collapse.

# **12. First‑Order RED / Adjacency Behavior** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑082**
When foundation RED classification is enabled and sufficient inputs exist, RB SHALL classify adjacency as `local` when routing remains within the defined prior neighborhood and $\|\Delta H\|$ is below the small‑entropy placeholder, and as `non_local` when neighborhood exit or critical entropy conditions are met, using the shared placeholder thresholds from the relational model unless a build‑specific policy signature overrides them deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.050‑083**
When regime is Stable or Refinement (shared table) and RED fields are enabled, RB SHALL prefer local route proposals unless TR‑gating or hard topology constraints require otherwise.

### **HLR‑20.050‑084**
When regime is Transition or Collapse (shared table) and RED fields are enabled, RB SHALL NOT force a false `local` adjacency_class to mask non‑local or collapse conditions.

### **HLR‑20.050‑085**
RB SHALL remain deterministic: identical TP inputs, policy signature, and optional foundation inputs SHALL produce identical routing filters and RB_out foundation fields.

# **13. RB Invariants** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑047**
RB SHALL maintain:

1. **Deterministic Routing Invariant**  
   Identical inputs → identical routing filters (and identical enabled RB_out foundation fields).

2. **TR‑Gating Invariant**  
   TR updates occur only when `tr_needs_update = true`.

3. **Core‑Isolation Invariant**  
   No cross‑core merges, splits, or lane consolidations.

4. **Read‑Only TR Invariant**  
   RB SHALL NOT modify `TP.TR`.

5. **Canonical Ordering Invariant**  
   All routing‑filter arrays and maps SHALL be canonically ordered.

6. **Layer‑Separation Invariant**  
   RB SHALL NOT conflate $\kappa_{\text{exec}}$, $\kappa_{\text{id}}$, and $\kappa_{\text{route}}$.

7. **IdOB Non‑Mutation Invariant**  
   RB SHALL NOT mutate IdOB‑owned identity fields.

# **14. Conformance Requirements** *(Normative)*

### **HLR‑20.050‑061**
A system is RB‑conformant only if all routing decisions, routing‑filter exports, TR‑gating behaviors, multi‑core routing constraints, and enabled foundation RED fields satisfy all applicable HLRs in this document.

### **HLR‑20.050‑062 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
RB‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves routing‑filter formation, relational‑topology evaluation, TR‑gating, or enabled RED classification.

### **HLR‑20.050‑063 — Read‑Only Discipline**
RB‑prm SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields outside routing‑filter export and RB‑owned RB_out fields.

### **HLR‑20.050‑064 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
RB‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to routing‑filter formation, relational‑topology evaluation, TR‑gating, or enabled RED classification, and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.050‑065 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
RB‑prm SHALL NOT consume Pipeline‑B metadata, semantic $\Delta H\%$, truth/done fields, supervisory fields, or any metadata owned by Path‑B realization.

### **HLR‑20.050‑066 — Routing‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**
RB‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata strictly as routing‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, stance inference, identity resolution, or meaning reconstruction.

### **HLR‑20.050‑067 — Routing‑Layer Integration**
RB‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into routing‑filter formation only when such metadata affects routing completeness, routing determinism, replay safety, or enabled RED classification completeness.

### **HLR‑20.050‑086**
When foundation RED fields are enabled, RB‑prm SHALL integrate IdOB view and $\mathbf{F}$ approximations only as routing‑adjacent features consistent with operator $\mathcal{R}$ and SHALL NOT assume IdOB responsibilities.

# **15. Verification Focus** *(Informative)*

Verification ensures:

- deterministic routing  
- canonical ordering  
- replay equivalence  
- correct TR gating  
- correct multi‑core isolation  
- correct messy‑input handling  
- layer separation ($\kappa_{\text{exec}}$ vs $\kappa_{\text{route}}$ / $\kappa_{\text{id}}$)  
- IdOB non‑mutation  
- when RED enabled: local/non‑local classification, displacement_scale directionality, regime_hint consistency with the shared table  
- progressive‑test observability of routing filter + RB_out foundation fields  

**First‑order observation questions** (from foundation papers; non‑normative):

1. When $I_{\text{stab}}$ is high, does RB keep `adjacency_class = local`?  
2. When $\|\Delta H\|$ crosses critical threshold, does RB emit non‑local without masking?  
3. Does RB over‑stabilize under Drift?  
4. How does RB behave if IdOB view is missing vs present?  
5. Are $Rt_{\text{adj}}$ logs consistent with DCB execution order without conflating layers?

# **16. Must‑Prove vs Defer (Foundation Alignment)** *(Informative)*

**Must prove for RB foundation confidence**

- Deterministic $\mathcal{R}$‑compatible routing for fixed inputs  
- TR gating correctness  
- Core isolation  
- Canonical export  
- Write boundary (no IdOB / DCB ownership / TR mutation)  
- When RED enabled: adjacency_class and displacement_scale deterministic behavior  

**Defer**

- Multi‑lane rich continuous routing geometry  
- Learned adjacency metrics  
- Full attractor discovery  
- Binding RB proposals to final TR policy beyond gating  
- Freezing provisional regime thresholds as permanent constants
