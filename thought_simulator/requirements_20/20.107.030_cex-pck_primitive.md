# 20.107.030 — CEx‑Pck Primitive (TP‑Aligned, Version 1.0)  
### *Packaging Layer for CEx Conversation Identity Resolution*

---

## 1. Purpose *(Informative)*

CEx‑Pck is the **third and final internal module** of the CEx primitive.  
It receives:

- the **CEx‑CCR envelope** (alignment results, scores, decision, selected conversation), and  
- the **CIL lineage metadata** for the selected conversation.

CEx‑Pck produces a **compact, deterministic TP.cex.pck envelope** containing:

- the chosen conversation identity,  
- continuity status,  
- updated context and clarifying fields,  
- provenance and audit entries.

This envelope is consumed by **COB/CST** to update lineage for the next turn.

CEx‑Pck performs **no cross‑correlation** and **no IE repackaging**.

---

## 2. Inputs *(Informative)*

CEx‑Pck reads:

### 2.1 From CEx‑CCR (`TP.cex.ccr`)
- `decision`  
- `selected_conversation`  
- `alignment.identity`  
- `alignment.clarifying`  
- `alignment.context`  
- `alignment.continuity`  
- `alignment.reference`  
- `scores.ambiguity`  
- `scores.collapse`  
- `scores.drift`  
- `scores.stability`

### 2.2 From CIL (`TP.cil`)
For the selected conversation (or null for new):

- `identity_lineage`  
- `clarifying_lineage`  
- `context_lineage`  
- `continuity_lineage`  
- `topology`  
- `metrics`  
- `semantic_residue`  
- `next_context`

---

## 3. Outputs *(Informative)*

CEx‑Pck emits a compact envelope under `TP.cex.pck`:

```json
{
  "cex": {
    "pck": {
      "identity_layer_id": "string",
      "continuity_status": "CExContinuityStatus",
      "context_fields": "CExContextFields",
      "clarifying_fields": "CExClarifyingFields",
      "provenance": "CExProvenance",
      "audit": "CExAudit"
    }
  }
}
```

Where:

- `identity_layer_id` — chosen conversation identity  
- `continuity_status` — `continue | reset | shift | fallback`  
- `context_fields` — compact context structure  
- `clarifying_fields` — compact clarifying structure  
- `provenance` — deterministic record of CCR → Pck transition  
- `audit` — compact audit entry for replay

All fields must comply with TS‑20.105 compactness and typing rules.

---

## 4. Field Definitions *(Informative)*

### 4.1 Output Fields

- **identity_layer_id**  
  Compact identifier of the selected conversation’s identity layer.

- **continuity_status** (`CExContinuityStatus`)  
  Finite enum: `continue | reset | shift | fallback`.

- **context_fields** (`CExContextFields`)  
  Compact structure containing context lineage values relevant to the selected conversation.

- **clarifying_fields** (`CExClarifyingFields`)  
  Compact structure containing clarifying lineage values relevant to the selected conversation.

- **provenance** (`CExProvenance`)  
  Deterministic record of CCR decision inputs and packaging outcome.

- **audit** (`CExAudit`)  
  Compact audit entry for replay and debugging.

---

## 5. Normative Requirements — Inputs and Outputs

**HLR‑20.107.030‑001**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL read only `TP.cex.ccr` and `TP.cil` fields.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑002**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL write only to `TP.cex.pck` and SHALL NOT modify any upstream TP fields.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑003**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `identity_layer_id` using the conversation ID selected by CEx‑CCR, or a newly generated ID when `decision = new`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑004**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `continuity_status` using a compact `CExContinuityStatus` value derived from CCR’s decision and continuity alignment.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑005**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `context_fields` using compact lineage values from the selected conversation’s `context_lineage`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑006**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `clarifying_fields` using compact lineage values from the selected conversation’s `clarifying_lineage`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑007**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `provenance` containing deterministic CCR decision inputs and packaging metadata.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑008**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `audit` containing compact replay‑safe audit information.

---

## 6. Normative Requirements — Continuity Status

**HLR‑20.107.030‑009**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `continuity_status = reset` when CEx‑CCR decision is `new`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑010**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `continuity_status = continue` when CEx‑CCR decision is `specific`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑011**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `continuity_status = fallback` when CEx‑CCR decision is `fallback`.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑012**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL emit `continuity_status = shift` when CCR continuity alignment indicates a structural shift.

---

## 7. Normative Requirements — Provenance and Audit

**HLR‑20.107.030‑013**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL encode provenance entries describing CCR alignment values, scores, and decision.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑014**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL encode audit entries sufficient for deterministic replay of the CCR → Pck transition.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑015**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL NOT include nondeterministic values in provenance or audit.

---

## 8. Normative Requirements — Determinism

**HLR‑20.107.030‑016**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL produce identical output for identical `TP.cex.ccr` and `TP.cil` inputs.

**HLR‑20.107.030‑017**  
CEx‑Pck SHALL NOT introduce nondeterministic fields.

---

## 9. Processing Summary *(Informative)*

CEx‑Pck:

1. Reads CCR decision and selected conversation ID.  
2. Retrieves lineage metadata for the selected conversation.  
3. Derives continuity status from CCR decision and continuity alignment.  
4. Packages identity, context, clarifying, provenance, and audit fields.  
5. Emits a complete `TP.cex.pck` envelope for COB/CST lineage update.

This completes the definition of **20.107.030 — CEx‑Pck Primitive**.
