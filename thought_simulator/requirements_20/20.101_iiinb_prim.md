# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Input Inference/Repair Basin (IIInB) is the **second deterministic boundary** of Path‑A and the **final purely pre‑semantic primitive**.  
IIInB is a **proposal‑only, non‑mutating intake inspector**.

IIInB:

- analyzes the canonicalized surface form produced by InB  
- detects pre‑semantic anomalies  
- identifies deterministic shorthand expansions  
- identifies deterministic normalization opportunities  
- produces **repair_proposals**  
- produces **anomaly_flags**  
- does **not** apply repairs  
- does **not** mutate tokens  
- does **not** normalize surface  
- does **not** produce structure  
- does **not** produce normalized_text  
- does **not** produce token_flags  
- does **not** interpret meaning  

IIInB is the **last primitive that sees the raw intake** before IE constructs the committed TP envelope.

---

# **2. Scope** *(Informative)*

IIInB operates only on:

- `TP.intake.surface`  
- `TP.intake.tokens`

IIInB produces:

- `TP.repair_proposals`  
- `TP.anomaly_flags`  
- `TP.intake.metadata.iiinb`

IIInB does **not**:

- apply repairs  
- mutate surface  
- mutate tokens  
- produce structure  
- produce normalized_text  
- produce token_flags  
- interpret meaning

### **3.3 TP Fields Used and Produced by IIInB (Normative)**

#### **HLR‑20.101‑034 — TP Fields Read by IIInB**  
IIInB SHALL read only:
- TP.intake.surface  
- TP.intake.tokens  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑035 — TP Fields Written by IIInB**  
IIInB SHALL write only:
- TP.repair_proposals  
- TP.anomaly_flags  
- TP.intake.metadata.iiinb  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑036 — Prohibition on Writing Committed Fields**  
IIInB SHALL NOT modify:
- TP.intake.normalized_text  
- TP.intake.tokens  
- TP.structure  
- TP.semantic  
- TP.process  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑037 — Required Structure of TP.repair_proposals**  
Each repair proposal SHALL include:
- deterministic rule identifier  
- deterministic token span  
- deterministic replacement parameters  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑038 — Required Structure of TP.anomaly_flags**  
Each anomaly flag SHALL include:
- deterministic token span  
- anomaly type  
- anomaly location  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑039 — IE Intake Contract**  
IIInB output SHALL provide sufficient information for IE to construct IE.intake.

#### **HLR‑20.101‑040 — Repair Non‑Commitment**  
IIInB SHALL NOT apply repairs.

#### **HLR‑20.101‑041 — Token and Surface Non‑Mutation**  
IIInB SHALL NOT mutate surface or tokens.

# ⭐ **4.9 New Model‑A Requirements (New HLRs 042–050)**

These are the required updates to synchronize IIInB with:

- IE v3.1  
- token_flags architecture  
- rule‑driven IE behavior  
- progressive lineup testing  
- replay determinism  
- pipeline determinism  

### **HLR‑20.101‑042 — No Normalized Text Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.intake.normalized_text.

### **HLR‑20.101‑043 — No Structural Metadata Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.structure.tags, TP.structure.spans, or TP.structure.markup.

### **HLR‑20.101‑044 — No Token Flags Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.intake.token_flags.

### **HLR‑20.101‑045 — Proposal‑Only Pipeline Contract**  
IIInB SHALL produce repair proposals and anomaly flags only; all committed intake construction SHALL be performed exclusively by IE.

### **HLR‑20.101‑046 — Replay Determinism Contract**  
IIInB SHALL ensure that repair_proposals and anomaly_flags are sufficient for IE to reconstruct deterministic replay metadata.

### **HLR‑20.101‑047 — Pipeline Determinism Contract**  
IIInB SHALL ensure that downstream primitives (IE → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU) receive stable, deterministic, non‑mutated intake.

### **HLR‑20.101‑048 — No Surface Normalization**  
IIInB SHALL NOT normalize whitespace, punctuation, casing, Unicode, or repetition.

### **HLR‑20.101‑049 — No Token Re‑Segmentation**  
IIInB SHALL NOT re‑tokenize or reinterpret token boundaries.

### **HLR‑20.101‑050 — Pre‑Semantic Boundary Enforcement**  
IIInB SHALL enforce that all meaning‑adjacent corrections (spelling, shorthand, repetition, punctuation, Unicode) remain proposals only and SHALL NOT be applied until IE.

# 5. Allowed Transformations (Informative)

The following transformations may be identified and proposed by IIInB when they satisfy all normative requirements and operate only on TP.intake.surface and TP.intake.tokens:

- deterministic shorthand expansion proposals (e.g., “btw” → “by the way”),
- deterministic contraction expansion proposals,
- deterministic punctuation normalization proposals,
- deterministic casing normalization proposals,
- deterministic Unicode normalization proposals,
- deterministic repeated‑character correction proposals.

These transformations are expressed only as repair proposals.  
IIInB does not apply any transformation; IE determines whether to apply proposed repairs.

---

# **6. Forbidden Transformations** *(Informative)*

Forbidden operations include any that require **TP.semantic**, **TP.process**, CE, or context:

- semantic inference  
- expansion requiring TP history  
- ellipsis resolution  
- pronoun resolution  
- intent inference  
- command inference  
- creative rewriting  
- nondeterministic heuristics  

Examples:

- “again” → “repeat the last action”  
- “same as before” → “repeat previous command”  
- “lights kitchen” → “lights in the kitchen”  

These violate semantic‑isolation HLRs.

---

# **7. Determinism Requirements** *(Informative)*

IIInB transformations are fully deterministic:

- same **TP.intake** → same **IIInB_Output**  
- no stochastic branching  
- no user‑dependent behavior  
- no environment‑dependent behavior  

Replay determinism is required for audit and governance.

---

# **8. Metadata Structure** *(Informative)*

IIInB output includes:

```json
metadata: {
  ruleset_id: RulesetID,
  timestamp: Timestamp,
  input_hash: Hash,
  repair_operations: [RepairOp]
}
```

This metadata supports replay, debugging, and provenance tracking.

---

# 9. Summary (Informative)

IIInB is a deterministic, pre‑semantic repair‑proposal primitive.  
It identifies surface‑level defects, shorthand forms, and normalization opportunities that can be proposed without interpreting meaning.  
IIInB produces repair proposals and anomaly indicators only; it does not apply repairs or modify committed TP fields.  
IE applies approved repairs and produces the committed normalized surface, token sequence, and structural interpretation for downstream primitives.
