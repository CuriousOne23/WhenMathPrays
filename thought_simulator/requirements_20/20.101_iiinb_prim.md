# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Input Inference/Repair Basin (IIInB) is the **second deterministic boundary** of Path‑A and the **first meaning‑adjacent, proposal‑only primitive** in the intake pipeline.

IIInB is a **non‑mutating intake inspector** that performs **bounded semantic operations** in the **repair‑proposal and anomaly‑detection domain**, without constructing committed meaning or modifying TP.semantic.

IIInB:

- analyzes the canonicalized surface form produced by InB  
- detects intake‑level anomalies and shorthand/normalization opportunities  
- performs **bounded meaning‑adjacent interpretation** sufficient to propose deterministic repairs  
- produces **repair_proposals**  
- produces **anomaly_flags**  
- does **not** apply repairs  
- does **not** mutate tokens  
- does **not** normalize surface  
- does **not** produce structure  
- does **not** produce normalized_text  
- does **not** produce token_flags  
- does **not** construct TP.semantic or user intent  

IIInB is the **last primitive that sees the raw intake** before IE constructs the committed TP envelope and applies approved repairs.

---

# **2. Scope** *(Informative)*

IIInB operates only on:

- `TP.intake.surface`  
- `TP.intake.tokens`

IIInB produces:

- `TP.repair_proposals`  
- `TP.anomaly_flags`  
- `TP.intake.metadata.iiinb`

IIInB does **not**:

- apply repairs  
- mutate surface  
- mutate tokens  
- produce structure  
- produce normalized_text  
- produce token_flags  
- construct TP.semantic or user intent  

IIInB’s semantic domain is **bounded to meaning‑adjacent repair proposals and anomaly detection**; committed meaning and semantic interpretation are performed downstream by IE and later primitives.

### **3.3 TP Fields Used and Produced by IIInB (Normative)**

#### **HLR‑20.101‑034 — TP Fields Read by IIInB**  
IIInB SHALL read only:
- TP.intake.surface  
- TP.intake.tokens  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑035 — TP Fields Written by IIInB**  
IIInB SHALL write only:
- TP.repair_proposals  
- TP.anomaly_flags  
- TP.intake.metadata.iiinb  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑036 — Prohibition on Writing Committed Fields**  
IIInB SHALL NOT modify:
- TP.intake.normalized_text  
- TP.intake.tokens  
- TP.structure  
- TP.semantic  
- TP.process  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑037 — Required Structure of TP.repair_proposals**  
Each repair proposal SHALL include:
- deterministic rule identifier  
- deterministic token span  
- deterministic replacement parameters  

#### **HLR‑20.101‑038 — Required Structure of TP.anomaly_flags**  
Each anomaly flag SHALL include:
- deterministic token span  
- anomaly type  
- anomaly location

### HLR‑20.101‑065 — Local Semantic Anomaly Types
IIInB SHALL emit local semantic anomaly types, including but not limited to:

- illegal_character.*
- malformed_token
- unicode_anomaly
- punctuation_anomaly
- repetition_pattern
- no_entry   # token has no dictionary entry as a standalone form

These anomaly types are local, bounded, and non‑composite.

### HLR‑20.101‑039 — IE Intake Contract (Corrected)
IIInB output SHALL provide sufficient information for IE to:

- apply deterministic repairs
- perform composite merges
- validate dictionary entries
- normalize surface
- construct committed intake

IIInB SHALL NOT perform these operations.


#### **HLR‑20.101‑040 — Repair Non‑Commitment**  
IIInB SHALL NOT apply repairs.

#### **HLR‑20.101‑041 — Token and Surface Non‑Mutation**  
IIInB SHALL NOT mutate surface or tokens.

## **3.4 Tokenization Rules (Normative)**

IIInB SHALL perform deterministic, language‑agnostic segmentation of `TP.intake.surface` into `TP.intake.tokens` according to the following rules.

### **HLR‑20.101‑051 — Whitespace Skipping**  
IIInB SHALL skip all whitespace characters and SHALL NOT emit whitespace tokens.

### **HLR‑20.101‑052 — Structural Marker Recognition**  
IIInB SHALL recognize the literal string `<broken>` as a single token when encountered in the surface.

### **HLR‑20.101‑053 — Repeating‑Letter Run Tokens**  
IIInB SHALL emit any run of three or more identical alphabetic characters as a standalone token.

### **HLR‑20.101‑054 — Word Token Construction**  
IIInB SHALL emit a word token consisting only of contiguous alphanumeric characters.

### **HLR‑20.101‑055 — Illegal Characters as Standalone Tokens**  
IIInB SHALL emit all illegal, unreadable, or non‑alphanumeric characters as standalone single‑character tokens.  
Illegal characters SHALL NOT be embedded inside word tokens.

### **HLR‑20.101‑056 — Trailing Comma Attachment**  
IIInB SHALL attach any sequence of trailing commas immediately following a word token to that word token.

### **HLR‑20.101‑057 — Punctuation Run Tokens**  
IIInB SHALL emit runs of identical punctuation characters from the set `{".", "!", "?", ","}` as grouped tokens.

### **HLR‑20.101‑058 — Standalone Symbol Tokens**  
Any character not covered by HLR‑20.101‑052 through HLR‑20.101‑057 SHALL be emitted as a standalone single‑character token.

### **HLR‑20.101‑059 — Deterministic Ordering**  
IIInB SHALL emit tokens in the exact order they appear in the surface string.

### **HLR‑20.101‑060 — No Tokenization Beyond Defined Rules**  
IIInB SHALL NOT merge, split, reinterpret, or normalize tokens beyond the rules defined in HLR‑20.101‑051 through HLR‑20.101‑058.

### **HLR‑20.101‑061 — Deterministic Tokenization Contract**  
IIInB tokenization SHALL be fully deterministic: identical surface input SHALL produce identical token sequences.

# **4. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**  

---

## **4.1 Deterministic Operation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑001**  
IIInB SHALL operate only on TP.intake.

### **HLR‑20.101‑011**  
IIInB output SHALL be fully deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.101‑013**  
IIInB SHALL NOT depend on nondeterministic sources.

---

## **4.2 Semantic Domain and Isolation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑002**  
IIInB SHALL produce deterministic repair proposals and anomaly flags in a bounded meaning‑adjacent domain, without constructing TP.semantic.

### **HLR‑20.101‑003**  
IIInB SHALL NOT introduce committed semantic content in TP.semantic or TP.process.

### HLR‑20.101‑004 — No Composite Semantic Inference (Corrected)
IIInB SHALL NOT infer composite meaning across multiple tokens, including:

- “Th” + “e” → “The”
- “caf” + “é” → “café”

IIInB MAY propose deterministic single‑token normalization, including:

- “cccaaattt” → “cat”   # deterministic repetition‑collapse rule

Composite reasoning belongs exclusively to IE.

### **HLR‑20.101‑007**  
IIInB SHALL NOT expand shorthand or contractions using TP history, user profile, or external context.

### **HLR‑20.101‑026**  
IIInB SHALL apply rules in deterministic order.

### **HLR‑20.101‑027**  
IIInB SHALL interpret tokens deterministically within its bounded meaning‑adjacent repair domain.

### **HLR‑20.101‑028**  
IIInB SHALL propose normalization only when deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.101‑029**  
IIInB SHALL detect invalid Unicode deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.101‑030**  
IIInB SHALL detect malformed tokens deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.101‑031**  
IIInB SHALL operate deterministically on mixed‑granularity intake.

### **HLR‑20.101‑032**  
IIInB SHALL represent multi‑span repairs deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.101‑033**  
IIInB SHALL handle overlapping rule matches deterministically.

---

## **4.3 Isolation From Global State**

### **HLR‑20.101‑005**  
IIInB SHALL NOT access TP.process or global state.

### **HLR‑20.101‑012**  
IIInB SHALL NOT invoke OB layers or downstream semantic primitives.

---

## **4.4 Shorthand and Normalization Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑006**  
IIInB SHALL propose deterministic shorthand expansions only within its bounded domain.

### **HLR‑20.101‑009**  
IIInB SHALL propose deterministic normalization only.

### **HLR‑20.101‑010**  
IIInB SHALL NOT reinterpret punctuation semantically beyond anomaly detection and repair proposal.

---

## **4.5 Token Preservation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑008**  
IIInB SHALL preserve token order.

### **HLR‑20.101‑020**  
IIInB SHALL express repairs using token spans only.

### **HLR‑20.101‑021**  
IIInB SHALL propose Unicode normalization only when deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.101‑022** — Annotation of Unrepairable Defects (Corrected)
IIInB SHALL annotate unrepairable defects using anomaly types such as:

- no_entry
- malformed_token
- illegal_character.*
- repetition_pattern

IIInB SHALL NOT attempt to repair or merge unrepairable tokens.

### **HLR‑20.101‑023**  
IIInB SHALL express repair operations deterministically.

---

## **4.6 Metadata Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑014**  
IIInB SHALL emit metadata sufficient for deterministic replay.

### **HLR‑20.101‑019**  
IIInB SHALL annotate all unrepairable defects.

### **HLR‑20.101‑015**  
IIInB SHALL attach a deterministic ruleset identifier.

### **HLR‑20.101‑025**  
IIInB ruleset evolution SHALL be deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.101‑017**  
IIInB output SHALL be serialization‑ready.

---

## **4.7 Error Handling Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.101‑016**  
IIInB SHALL fail fast on invalid intake.

### **HLR‑20.101‑024**  
IIInB failure behavior SHALL be deterministic.

IIInB SHALL read only surface and tokens.

IIInB SHALL write only proposals, anomalies, metadata.iiinb.

### **HLR‑20.101‑036**  
IIInB SHALL NOT modify committed fields.

### **HLR‑20.101‑037**  
IIInB SHALL express repair proposals deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.101‑038**  
IIInB SHALL express anomaly flags deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.101‑040**  
IIInB SHALL NOT apply repairs.

### **HLR‑20.101‑041**

---

## **4.9 Model‑A Requirements (HLR‑20.101‑042–HLR‑20.101‑050)**

### **HLR‑20.101‑042 — No Normalized Text Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.intake.normalized_text.

### **HLR‑20.101‑043 — No Structural Metadata Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.structure.tags, TP.structure.spans, or TP.structure.markup.

### **HLR‑20.101‑044 — No Token Flags Production**  
IIInB SHALL NOT produce TP.intake.token_flags.

### **HLR‑20.101‑045 — Proposal‑Only Pipeline Contract**  
IIInB SHALL produce repair proposals and anomaly flags only; all committed intake construction SHALL be performed exclusively by IE.

### **HLR‑20.101‑046 — Replay Determinism Contract**  
IIInB SHALL ensure that repair_proposals and anomaly_flags are sufficient for IE to reconstruct deterministic replay metadata.

### **HLR‑20.101‑047 — Pipeline Determinism Contract**  
IIInB SHALL ensure that downstream primitives (IE → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU) receive stable, deterministic, non‑mutated intake.

### **HLR‑20.101‑048 — No Surface Normalization**  
IIInB SHALL NOT normalize whitespace, punctuation, casing, Unicode, or repetition; such normalization SHALL be expressed only as repair proposals.

### **HLR‑20.101‑049 — No Token Re‑Segmentation**  
IIInB SHALL NOT re‑tokenize or reinterpret token boundaries beyond its deterministic tokenization rules.

### **HLR‑20.101‑050 — Proposal‑Only Meaning‑Adjacent Corrections**  
IIInB SHALL enforce that all meaning‑adjacent corrections (spelling, shorthand, repetition, punctuation, Unicode) remain proposals only and SHALL NOT be applied until IE.

---

## **4.10 Bounded Semantic Domain Requirements (New HLRs 062–064)**

### HLR‑20.101‑062 — Local Semantic Judgments (Corrected)
IIInB SHALL restrict semantic operations to local, token‑level anomaly detection, including:

- dictionary‑absence (no_entry)
- malformed tokens
- repetition anomalies
- illegal characters

IIInB SHALL NOT perform composite merges or semantic normalization.

### HLR‑20.101‑063 — Alignment With IE Testbench Contract (Corrected)
IIInB SHALL emit anomaly flags for all locally anomalous tokens, including no_entry,
so that IE can perform composite merges and dictionary validation.

### HLR‑20.101‑064 — Pre‑Commit Intake Boundary (Corrected)
IIInB SHALL NOT perform composite merges or committed normalization.
IE SHALL perform all composite merges, dictionary validation, and committed normalization.

# 5. Allowed Transformations *(Informative)*

The following transformations may be identified and proposed by IIInB when they satisfy all normative requirements and operate only on TP.intake.surface and TP.intake.tokens:

- deterministic shorthand expansion proposals  
- deterministic contraction expansion proposals  
- deterministic punctuation normalization proposals  
- deterministic casing normalization proposals  
- deterministic Unicode normalization proposals  
- deterministic repeated‑character correction proposals  

These transformations are expressed only as repair proposals.  
IIInB does not apply any transformation; IE determines whether to apply proposed repairs.

---

# **6. Forbidden Transformations** *(Informative)*

Forbidden operations include any that require **TP.semantic**, **TP.process**, CE, or broader context:

- semantic inference  
- expansion requiring TP history  
- ellipsis resolution  
- pronoun resolution  
- intent inference  
- command inference  
- creative rewriting  
- nondeterministic heuristics  

Examples:

- “again” → “repeat the last action”  
- “same as before” → “repeat previous command”  
- “lights kitchen” → “lights in the kitchen”  

These violate semantic‑isolation and bounded‑domain HLRs.

---

# **7. Determinism Requirements** *(Informative)*

IIInB transformations are fully deterministic:

- same **TP.intake** → same **IIInB_Output**  
- no stochastic branching  
- no user‑dependent behavior  
- no environment‑dependent behavior  

Replay determinism is required for audit and governance.

---

# **8. Metadata Structure** *(Informative)*

IIInB output includes:

```json
metadata: {
  ruleset_id: RulesetID,
  timestamp: Timestamp,
  input_hash: Hash,
  repair_operations: [RepairOp]
}
```

This metadata supports replay, debugging, and provenance tracking.

---

# **9. Summary** *(Informative)*

IIInB is a deterministic, **bounded meaning‑adjacent repair‑proposal primitive**.  
It identifies surface‑level defects, shorthand forms, and normalization opportunities that can be proposed without constructing committed meaning or TP.semantic.  
IIInB produces repair proposals and anomaly indicators only; it does not apply repairs or modify committed TP fields.  
IE applies approved repairs and produces the committed normalized surface, token sequence, and structural interpretation for downstream primitives.
