# ⭐ 20.108.010 CE Candidate‑Set Specification  
**Document ID:** 20.108.010  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026‑08‑15  
**Status:** Draft — CE Candidate‑Set Aligned with ISc and TP.ce

---

## 1. Purpose *(Informative)*

This document defines the **candidate_set envelope** produced by the Context Engine (CE) in Path‑A.  
The candidate_set is the finite set of interpretations or state transitions that CE generates for downstream scoring by the TS Inference Scorer (ISc).  
This specification focuses on:

- the structure of `TP.ce.candidate_set[]`  
- deterministic construction and ordering  
- provenance and replay requirements  
- integration with upstream features (IE, CEx, semantic.importance, TP metadata)  
- compatibility with ISc scoring requirements (20.45)  

---

## 2. Scope *(Informative)*

This document covers:

- CE’s responsibility for generating `TP.ce.candidate_set[]`  
- the internal structure of each candidate object  
- deterministic candidate construction and ordering rules  
- provenance and replay constraints for candidate_set  
- the relationship between CE, IE, CEx, and ISc  

It does **not** define:

- ISc scoring behavior (see 20.45)  
- IE normalization behavior (see 20.107.010)  
- CEx‑CCR alignment behavior (see 20.107.020)  
- TP metadata usage rules (see 20.105.030)

## 4. Definitions *(Informative)*

- **Candidate Set** — the finite array `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` containing CE‑generated interpretations or transitions.  
- **Candidate Object** — a single element of `TP.ce.candidate_set[]`, identified by `candidate_id` and associated feature fields.  
- **FFTM Fields** — four‑field token meaning features used by ISc for semantic scoring (see 20.45).  
- **Structural Features** — bounded, deterministic structural cues (e.g., WrdNm encodings, continuity IDs, constraint families).  
- **Semantic‑Adjacent Features** — structural or residue features that stabilize meaning without generating new semantics.  
- **Next‑Context Features** — pre‑semantic continuity signals used to maintain turn‑to‑turn coherence.

## 5. Candidate‑Set Structure Requirements *(Normative)*

### 5.1 Candidate‑Set Envelope

**HLR‑20.108.010‑001**  
CE SHALL emit a finite candidate_set array at `TP.ce.candidate_set[]`.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑002**  
Each element of `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` SHALL be a candidate object with the following fields:

```text
TP.ce.candidate_set[]: {
    candidate_id: number,
    fftm_fields: {...},
    structural_features: {...},
    semantic_adjacent_features: {...},
    next_context: {...},
    provenance: {...}
}
```

**HLR‑20.108.010‑003**  
Each candidate_id in `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` SHALL be unique within the array.

---

### 5.2 FFTM Fields

**HLR‑20.108.010‑004**  
CE SHALL populate `fftm_fields` for each candidate using the canonical FFTM meaning fields required by ISc (see 20.45).
CE SHALL populate fftm_fields with the canonical FFTM meaning fields required by ISc:
- token_surface
- token_base
- token_expression
- token_intent  

These four fields constitute the complete FFTM meaning-layer feature set consumed by ISc (see 20.45).

**HLR‑20.108.010‑005**  
FFTM fields in `fftm_fields` SHALL be deterministic and SHALL not depend on nondeterministic sampling or external state.

---

### 5.3 Structural Features

**HLR‑20.108.010‑006**  
CE SHALL populate `structural_features` for each candidate using bounded, deterministic structural cues derived from IE, CEx, and TP metadata.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑007**  
Structural features in `structural_features` SHALL be interpretable and SHALL support deterministic replay.
The structural_features object SHALL contain at minimum:
- surface_id
- lemma_id
- expression_id
- ordering_id
- constraint_family_id
- next_context_id  

Additional bounded structural cues (e.g., continuity_id, entity_id, adjacency, constraint_importance) MAY be included when available.

---

### 5.4 Semantic‑Adjacent Features

**HLR‑20.108.010‑008**  
CE SHALL populate `semantic_adjacent_features` for each candidate using semantic residues and adjacency cues (e.g., from OB‑Set, CEx‑CCR, semantic.importance) without generating new semantic propositions.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑009**  
Semantic‑adjacent features in `semantic_adjacent_features` SHALL be treated as structural or residue features and SHALL not modify semantic_core or FFTM fields.
The semantic_adjacent_features object SHALL contain at minimum:
- semantic_residue (from CEx‑CCR)
- structural_residue (from OB‑Set)
  Optional fields MAY include: referent_adjacent_signals, modality_stance_cues, or other bounded residue metadata.

---

### 5.5 Next‑Context Features

**HLR‑20.108.010‑010**  
CE SHALL populate `next_context` for each candidate using pre‑semantic continuity signals consistent with CE.context_fields and MCB next_context_metadata.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑011**  
Next‑context features in `next_context` SHALL be deterministic and SHALL support replay across TP cycles.

---

### 5.6 Provenance

**HLR‑20.108.010‑012**  
CE SHALL populate `provenance` for each candidate with sufficient information to trace:

- upstream primitives contributing to the candidate  
- key feature sources (IE, CEx, OB‑Set, TP metadata)  
- CE internal decision paths  

**HLR‑20.108.010‑013**  
Candidate provenance in `provenance` SHALL comply with TP provenance requirements defined in 20.105.020.

---

## 6. Candidate‑Set Construction and Ordering Requirements *(Normative)*

**HLR‑20.108.010‑014**  
CE SHALL construct `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` using only upstream features from IE, CEx, semantic.importance, and TP metadata; CE SHALL not consume Pipeline‑B envelopes or truth/done fields.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑015**  
CE SHALL generate the candidate_set deterministically for a given TP input, state, and seed.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑016**  
CE SHALL apply canonical candidate ordering rules so that `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` has a stable, reproducible ordering across replay runs.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑017**  
CE SHALL ensure that candidate_set construction does not expand the TP beyond replay‑safe bounds defined in 20.705_patha_pathb_flow.md.

CE will generate one candidate per primary semantic.importance entity.
CE MAY generate additional candidates when structural ambiguity is detected (e.g., multiple valid ordering_id or constraint_family_id interpretations).
CE will always generate at least one candidate, even when semantic.importance is empty; this will be the “default interpretation candidate”.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑027**  
CE SHALL generate the candidate_set according to the deterministic construction rule defined in §6.

---

## 7. Integration with ISc *(Normative)*

**HLR‑20.108.010‑018**  
CE SHALL produce `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` in a form directly consumable by ISc, including all FFTM, structural, semantic‑adjacent, next‑context, and provenance fields required by 20.45.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑019**  
CE SHALL not perform scoring; CE SHALL only generate candidates and associated features, leaving all scoring behavior to ISc.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑020**  
CE SHALL guarantee that `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` remains immutable after TPU commit and that ISc operates on a read‑only candidate_set.

---

## 8. Replay and Immutability Requirements *(Normative)*

**HLR‑20.108.010‑021**  
CE SHALL ensure that candidate_set generation satisfies Path‑A replay invariants: identical TP inputs, state, and seed SHALL yield identical `TP.ce.candidate_set[]`.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑022**  
CE SHALL treat `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` as immutable after TPU commit; no primitive SHALL modify candidate_set once committed.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑023**  
CE SHALL record sufficient replay metadata in `provenance` to allow reconstruction and audit of candidate_set generation during verification.

---

## 9. Forbidden Actions *(Normative)*

**HLR‑20.108.010‑024**  
CE SHALL NOT generate candidates using nondeterministic sampling, external services, or opaque heuristics that cannot be replayed.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑025**  
CE SHALL NOT modify semantic_core or FFTM fields after candidate_set construction; all semantic meaning changes SHALL occur upstream of CE.

**HLR‑20.108.010‑026**  
CE SHALL NOT write candidate_set fields outside `TP.ce.candidate_set[]` or bypass TP schema constraints defined in 20.105.010.
