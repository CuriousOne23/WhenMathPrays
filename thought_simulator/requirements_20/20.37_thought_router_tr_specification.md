# **20.37 — Thought Router (TR‑prm) Specification (Option‑C Rewrite)**  
**Document ID:** 20.37  
**Version:** 3.0 (Full Option‑C Rewrite + TP‑Field Model Alignment)**  
**Status:** Normative — Path‑A Meaning‑Layer Routing‑Vector Specification  
**Purpose:** Define TR meaning‑layer fields, lifecycle, producers, consumers, and deterministic routing behavior in Pipeline A.

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

The Thought Router (TR‑prm) is the **meaning‑layer routing‑vector constructor** for Path‑A.  
TR is **not** an OB primitive; it is a **meaning‑layer computation routine** that produces the canonical routing‑vector block `TP.TR`.

TR:

- expresses stance, intent, affect, epistemic shading, tension, politeness, commitment, reservation, logical structure, epistemic ΔH, lineage additions, and routing‑fields  
- is written **only** by the TR routine  
- is consumed **only** by RB (and read‑only by GB)  
- is committed into `SSR.thought_router_fields` by TPU during `mtp_update`  
- is recomputed only when `TP.tr_needs_update = true`  
- is deterministic, replay‑safe, seed‑independent, and allocation‑independent  
- consumes **TP‑local meaning‑layer fields**, **structural cues**, and **ephemeral DCB events**  
- never reads Pipeline‑B fields, truth hypotheses, or messy‑input tags  

TR is the **canonical routing substrate** for RB in Path‑A.

---

# **1. Module Identity (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑001 — Canonical Representation**  
TR SHALL be represented as `TP.TR` and SHALL be the canonical Path‑A routing‑vector block.

### **HLR‑20.37‑002 — Exclusive Writer**  
The TR routine SHALL be the exclusive writer of `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑003 — OB Prohibition**  
OB‑family primitives SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑004 — Pipeline‑A Only**  
TR SHALL operate exclusively in Pipeline‑A.

---

# **2. Canonical Locations (Normative)**

### **2.1 TR Block**

Every TP SHALL contain:

```
TP.TR {
    stance,
    intent,
    affect,
    epistemic_shading,
    tension,
    politeness,
    commitment,
    reservation,
    logical_structure,
    epistemic_delta_h,
    lineage_additions[],
    routing_fields{}
}
```

### **HLR‑20.37‑008 — Required Fields**  
TR SHALL include all fields listed above.

### **HLR‑20.37‑025 — Deterministic Ordering**  
TR field ordering SHALL be deterministic for serialization and replay.

### **HLR‑20.37‑053 — SSR Projection**  
Committed TR fields SHALL project into `SSR.thought_router_fields` during TPU commit.

---

# **3. Dirty‑Flag (Normative)**

Every TP SHALL contain:

```
TP.tr_needs_update : boolean
```

### **HLR‑20.37‑037 — Dirty‑Flag Presence**  
TP SHALL include a boolean `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑038 — Dirty‑Flag Setting**  
Any meaning‑layer writer that modifies meaning‑layer fields SHALL set `tr_needs_update = true`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑040 — Dirty‑Flag Clearing**  
The TR routine SHALL clear `tr_needs_update = false` only after successful TR recompute and commit.

### **HLR‑20.37‑041 — No‑Op When Clean**  
TR SHALL NOT execute when `tr_needs_update = false`.

---

# **4. TR Routine Inputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑011 — TP‑Local Inputs Only**  
TR SHALL derive routing‑vector fields only from:

- TP.semantic meaning‑layer fields  
- TP.semantic meaning‑semantics[]  
- TP.semantic idob_semantics[]  
- TP.process.routing_metadata  
- TP.semantic.lineage  
- structural cues from STPX  
- permitted ephemeral DCB events  

### **HLR‑20.37‑048 — Ephemeral DCB Events**  
TR MAY consume ephemeral DCB directional‑change events.

### **HLR‑20.37‑056 — Messy‑Input Prohibition**  
TR SHALL NOT consume messy‑input tags.

### **HLR‑20.37‑063 — Truth‑Hypothesis Prohibition**  
TR SHALL NOT read truth hypotheses or any Pipeline‑B fields.

### **HLR‑20.37‑060 — Seed Independence**  
TR derivation SHALL be seed‑independent.

### **HLR‑20.37‑020 — No Hidden Global State**  
TR SHALL NOT depend on hidden mutable global state.

### **HLR‑20.37‑034 — No Cross‑TP Coupling**  
TR SHALL NOT depend on other TPs except through canonical lineage fields.

### **HLR‑20.37‑0XX — Residue‑Topology Prohibition** 
TR SHALL NOT consume semantic‑residue topology fields (TP.metadata.semantic_residue, TP.semantic.importance, residue clusters, residue drift, residue curvature).
These fields are owned by IdOB and RB.

---

# **5. TR‑Input Producers (Normative)**

## **5.1 Meaning‑Layer Writers (IdOB, MCB, RBU)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑070 — Meaning‑Layer Inputs**  
Meaning‑layer writers SHALL set `tr_needs_update = true` whenever meaning‑layer fields change.

## **5.2 Structural Geometry Producers (STPX)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑010 — Structural‑Only Inputs**  
STPX SHALL provide structural cues (lexical, structural, constraint, discourse‑context) as TR‑input fields.

## **5.3 DCB Geometric Overlay**

### **HLR‑20.37‑042 — Geometric Hints**  
TR MAY consume DCB directional‑change events.

### **HLR‑20.37‑043 — No Direct Writes**  
DCB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑045 — No Dirty‑Flag Mutation**  
DCB SHALL NOT set `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑046 — Gated Consumption**  
DCB events SHALL be consumed only when:

- `tr_needs_update = true`, and  
- RB routes the TP to TR  

### **HLR‑20.37‑047 — Ephemeral**  
DCB events SHALL NOT persist.

---

# **6. TR → RB Relationship (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑004 — RB Consumption**  
RB SHALL consume `TP.TR` as a primary routing input.

### **HLR‑20.37‑005 — RB Read‑Only**  
RB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑039 — TR‑Gating**  
RB SHALL route TP to TR iff `tr_needs_update = true`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑019 — Deterministic Routing**  
RB routing SHALL remain deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.37‑030 — Compatibility**  
TR SHALL remain compatible with RB bounded routing constraints.

---

# **7. TR Field Definitions (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑022 — Epistemic ΔH**  
`epistemic_delta_h` SHALL be TR‑interpreted and auditable.

### **HLR‑20.37‑026 — Numeric Format**  
TR numeric subfields SHALL use canonical Q32.32.

### **HLR‑20.37‑064 — Routing‑Fields Ordering**  
`routing_fields{}` ordering SHALL be deterministic.

### **HLR‑20.37‑054 — No Execution‑Layer Hints**  
TR SHALL NOT encode execution‑layer hints.

### **HLR‑20.37‑023 — Explicit Routing Fields**  
`routing_fields{}` SHALL be explicit and consumable by RB without latent interpretation.

---

# **8. TR Lifecycle (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑027 — Lifecycle Logging**  
TR lifecycle transitions SHALL be visible in deterministic logs.

### **HLR‑20.37‑012 — Replay Logging**  
TR SHALL be logged in deterministic replay artifacts.

### **HLR‑20.37‑061 — SSR Preservation**  
Strip replay SHALL preserve `SSR.thought_router_fields`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑013 — Immutability**  
TR SHALL remain immutable after TR‑routine write until TPU commit.

### **HLR‑20.37‑035 — Conformance Failures**  
TR conformance failures SHALL be treated as routing conformance failures.

---

# **9. Module Boundaries (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑014 — IB Prohibition**  
IB SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑015 — Merge Prohibition**  
Merge SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑016 — Truth/Done Prohibition**  
Truth/Done SHALL NOT read `TP.TR`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑062 — GB Prohibition**  
GB SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` or `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.37‑066 — Pipeline‑B Prohibition**  
Pipeline‑B modules SHALL NOT write `TP.TR` or clear `tr_needs_update`.

---

# **10. Safety & Determinism (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.37‑007 — Deterministic Outputs**  
TR fields SHALL be deterministic and reproducible for identical inputs.

### **HLR‑20.37‑028 — No Write‑Authority Inference**  
TR consumers SHALL NOT infer write authority from read access.

### **HLR‑20.37‑029 — Split/Merge Compatibility**  
TR SHALL support deterministic split/merge workflows.

### **HLR‑20.37‑065 — Allocation Independence**  
TR derivation SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order or allocation identity.

---

# **11. Conformance (Normative)**

A system conforms when:

- `TP.TR` exists and is complete  
- `TP.tr_needs_update` exists  
- TR is written only by the TR routine  
- TR is consumed only by RB and GB  
- TR is deterministic, replay‑safe, seed‑independent  
- TR respects all authority boundaries  
- TR respects all routing bounds  
- TR commits into SSR with canonical ordering  
- TR lifecycle is logged deterministically
