# 20.160 Randomness Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic control of seed-based variability so that randomness is isolated to output realization and does not affect core meaning construction, supervision, or lifecycle semantics.

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes randomness obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including output-only variability, reproducible seed handling, profile-bound PRNG behavior, and explicit reject/re-verification constraints.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.160-001: Randomness SHALL be isolated to output-layer realization selection.
2. HLR-20.160-002: Seed SHALL be pseudo-random, documented, and reproducible.
3. HLR-20.160-003: Seed SHALL NOT affect OB, RB, TB, ΔH%, truth, or MTP update logic.
4. HLR-20.160-004: Randomness-critical code changes SHALL require re-verification.
5. HLR-20.160-005: Any permitted stochastic behavior SHALL use a pinned PRNG algorithm version.
6. HLR-20.160-006: PRNG seed derivation and stream partitioning rules SHALL be deterministic and versioned.
7. HLR-20.160-007: PRNG profile version SHALL be bound to execution-signature deterministic profile state.
8. HLR-20.160-008: Seed/PRNG state SHALL NOT influence CIL, COB, or COP deterministic decision semantics, including CIL integration outcomes, COB lifecycle/replay semantics, or COP queue/scheduling decisions.
9. HLR-20.160-009: Any COP-local randomization feature state SHALL be disabled by default and SHALL reject unless explicitly signature-bound to deterministic profile behavior.
10. HLR-20.160-010: Randomness SHALL only be consumed at explicit expression-selection boundaries and SHALL NOT be consumed during meaning construction, routing, splitting, merging, truth evaluation, or supervision.
11. HLR-20.160-011: Randomness boundaries SHALL be deterministic, documented, and safe-boundary enforced.
12. HLR-20.160-012: PRNG state SHALL be reproducible for identical input, seed, and execution-signature/profile state.
13. HLR-20.160-013: Output-layer variation SHALL remain bounded and SHALL not create nondeterminism in upstream TP, MTP, or GB-governed state.
14. HLR-20.160-014: Randomness handling SHALL be auditable with explicit reason codes, algorithm version identifiers, and seed provenance markers.
15. HLR-20.160-015: Randomness profile updates SHALL be versioned and SHALL require deterministic re-verification before release.
16. HLR-20.160-016: Randomness requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.

## Functional Boundaries
- Randomness owns output-layer realization selection only.
- Meaning construction, routing, supervision, lifecycle, and audit semantics SHALL remain deterministic and seed-independent.
- Any stochastic behavior outside output realization SHALL be rejected unless explicitly re-specified and re-verified.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic output variation for identical MTP and seed state.
- No seed influence on OB, RB, TB, CIL, COB, COP, MTP, or GB decision semantics.
- Deterministic PRNG version/seed provenance and re-verification gating.
- Output-only randomness consumption at explicit safe boundaries.
