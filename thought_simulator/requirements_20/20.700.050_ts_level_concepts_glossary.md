### MSL (Meaning Signal Layer) — tsc
**Purpose:** Compact, structured token set that carries signals about how meaning is framed or adjusted.  
**Focus:** Qualifiers, clarifications, stance, shading, intent, direction, coherence, and subculture.  
**When:** Throughout Path A (ingested by CEx, RB, IdOB, MCB) and carried in TP.metadata.msl_metadata.  
**Why:** Provides high-signal cues for relevance, routing, identity projection, and context updates without storing raw text.  
**Is not:** Full semantic content; core meaning payload; unstructured text.  
**Example:** "actually" token triggers CEx relevance re-evaluation and potential additional IdOB cycle.  
**Normative home:** 20.105.010, 20.105.030, effectivity_of_ts_context.md

## ΔH% (DeltaHPercent) — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the incremental structural‑uncertainty contribution produced by each Path‑A interpretive stage (OB, merge, truth‑hypotheses evaluation), ensuring uncertainty evolution is deterministic, auditable, and replay‑safe.  
**Focus:** Encode local uncertainty adjustments from structural gaps, unresolved branches, missing‑mass indicators, and interpretive signals; contribute to the deterministic computation of H%.  
**When:** Generated at each Path‑A interpretive step and accumulated before truth/completion evaluation; included in the update_structure validated and committed by RBU.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, non‑probabilistic mechanism for tracking uncertainty evolution without latent‑space inference or stochastic estimation.  
**Is not:** Probability; confidence score; truth commitment; entropy functional; Path‑B computation.  
**Example:** Merge detects unresolved semantic branches and increases ΔH%; truth‑hypotheses evaluation stabilizes a hypothesis and decreases ΔH%, lowering H% in the next cycle.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.030, 20.30.060.

## **FFTM (Four‑Field Token Model) — 20.31**
**Purpose:** Canonical Path‑A meaning representation; the minimal deterministic token‑level structure required for meaning construction before any basin, routing, behavior, or expression mapping occurs.  
**Focus:** Owns the four token meaning fields (token_surface, token_base, token_expression, token_intent) and guarantees lane‑local, non‑inferential meaning formation.  
**When:** Executes strictly in Path‑A during meaning construction, before semantic_core aggregation and before any basin or Path‑B primitive.  
**Why:** Provides a stable, sufficient, and deterministic foundation for meaning; prevents premature inference, cross‑token contamination, and meaning collapse; ensures Path‑A meaning is structurally complete prior to downstream processing.  
**Is not:** Not a basin; not a routing primitive; not a behavior selector; not an expression mapper; does not read or write SSR; does not perform inference; does not modify semantic_core; not part of Path‑B.  
**Example:** The token “run” is represented as FFTM: token_surface=“run”, token_base=lemma(run), token_expression=verb/action frame, token_intent=neutral‑directive; these four fields form the atomic unit later aggregated into semantic_core.  
**Normative home:** 20.31_patha_meaning_spec.md.

## H% (UncertaintyPercent) — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the accumulated structural‑uncertainty state of the TP/MTP after integrating ΔH% contributions across Path‑A interpretive stages.  
**Focus:** Encode the current structural‑uncertainty level derived from unresolved branches, structural gaps, and interpretive signals; used by truth‑hypotheses and completion evaluation to determine stability and readiness for commit.  
**When:** Updated during Path‑A after merge and truth‑hypotheses evaluation; included in the update_structure validated and committed by RBU.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe uncertainty measure that cleanly separates structural uncertainty from truth evaluation, confidence, or probabilistic reasoning.  
**Is not:** Probability; confidence score; truth band; global uncertainty model; Path‑B computation.  
**Example:** If ΔH% accumulates due to unresolved semantic branches, H% increases; if truth‑hypotheses evaluation stabilizes a hypothesis, H% decreases, signaling readiness for completion.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.060, 20.30.070.

## **Grounding Entropy (H\_{Kn_{coarse}}, H\_{Kn_{medium}}, H\_{Kn_{fine}})**  
**Purpose:** Quantify grounding certainty at each abstraction tier (coarse, medium, fine) during KnB processing; provide Path‑B with deterministic entropy signals for tier selection, expression safety, and surgical‑question triggers.  
**Focus:** Measure precision of grounded knowledge relative to maximum attainable precision; reflect ambiguity, contextual stability, and domain‑specific resolution; expose entropy values as explicit SSR fields for downstream routing and realization.  
**When:** Computed during each grounding stage: KnC computes $H\_{Kn_{coarse}}$ after coarse grounding; KnM computes $H\_{Kn_{medium}}$ after medium grounding; KnF computes $H\_{Kn_{fine}}$ after fine grounding; all values are written into SSR before Path‑B executes.  
**Why:** Enable TS to select the correct abstraction tier without inference or probabilistic reasoning; ensure deterministic behavior by making uncertainty explicit and measurable; prevent Path‑B from expressing overly precise meaning when grounding entropy is high.  
**Is not:** A truth metric; semantic confidence score; probabilistic estimate; grounding entropy never performs inference, semantic repair, or truth evaluation and never modifies grounded fields.  
**Example:** KnC attaches coarse identity (“dog”) and computes $H\_{Kn_{coarse}}$; KnM refines identity (“retriever”) and computes $H\_{Kn_{medium}}$; KnF resolves identity (“Golden Retriever”) and computes $H\_{Kn_{fine}}$, allowing Path‑B to select coarse/medium/fine expression based on total entropy $H_{total} = H_{SSR} + H\_{Kn_{tier}}$.  
**Normative home:** 20.48.010–030 (KnC/KnM/KnF); 20.52 (SSR); 20.705 (Path‑A → KnB → Path‑B flow); ts_numeric_policy.md (entropy definitions).

## OB (Object Basin) — 20.40
**Purpose:** Defines the generic class of Path‑A object‑basin primitives (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, IdOB) that classify structure, extract constraints, and emit residue and TR‑input cues for routing.  
**Focus:** Encodes structural, constraint, and pattern signatures from lane‑local TP views; produces residue fragments, ΔH% contributions, and TR‑input fields without performing semantic interpretation or truth evaluation.  
**When:** Invoked during Path‑A meaning construction prior to routing; activated deterministically based on lane‑local TP structure, routing context, and orthogonality_signature; contributes evidence and residue before TR and RB execution.  
**Why:** Provides deterministic, replay‑safe structural interpretation that grounds routing, ΔH% evolution, and downstream meaning construction without semantic inference or identity‑conditioned processing.  
**Is not:** A semantic basin; a truth evaluator; a routing arbiter; a geometric meta‑basin; an identity‑conditioned primitive; a Path‑B computation; a generator of TP.TR or truth_hypotheses.  
**Example:** SOB/SROB/CnOB extract structural and constraint residue; SmOB emits pre‑semantic residue; OB sets `tr_needs_update` when structural cues change, enabling TR to recompute routing.  
**Normative home:** 20.40 (parent‑level OB requirements).

## TCU (Thought Compute Unit) — 20.30
**Purpose:** Normalizes compute cost reporting per primitive so that deterministic cost envelopes can be enforced and compared across the architecture.
**Focus:** Normalized compute-cost unit reported per tick / per primitive.
**When:** Reported per tick / per primitive.
**Why:** Deterministic cost envelopes; comparable cost reporting across primitives.
**Is not:** Wall-clock time; token count.
**Example:** OpBeh vs OuB cost recorded separately.
**Normative home:** 20.30.

## Messy-Input Taxonomy (MI_*) — 20.17
**Purpose:** Classifies all malformed or ambiguous input conditions so that routing, repair, and escalation decisions are made deterministically rather than ad hoc.
**Focus:** Structured classification of problematic input conditions with deterministic downstream routing implications.
**When:** Applied at InB / IIInB during input canonicalization.
**Why:** Prevent ad hoc handling of malformed input; ensure deterministic routing per class.
**Is not:** Semantic interpretation; truth evaluation; a repair mechanism.

| Class | Focus | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `MI_CONTRA` | Mutually incompatible propositions | "It is safe" + "It is dangerous" |
| `MI_VAGUE` | Underspecified referents | "Make it better" |
| `MI_AFFECT` | Affect tagging | Tone marker only |
| `MI_INCOMP` | Commitment-blocked incompleteness | Missing slots → IB-Creation-Request |
| `MI_NOISE` | Surface noise | Typos / encoding glitches |
**Normative home:** 20.17.

## MCS (Meaningful Cognitive Step) — 20.30.040
**Purpose:** Defines the atomic, auditable unit of cognitive work in TS, enabling deterministic accounting of interpretive, structural, and evaluative operations across Path A.
**Focus:** Counts only meaningful, user‑relevant cognitive transformations (OB/TB interpretation, merge decisions, truth‑hypothesis evaluation, completion evaluation, routing decisions) while excluding zero‑cost metadata updates and mechanical bookkeeping.
**When:** Incremented during Path A whenever TS performs a qualifying interpretive or evaluative action; never incremented during Path B or for non‑cognitive operations.
**Why:** Ensures TS reasoning is measurable, bounded, replay‑safe, and free of hidden computation; provides a stable basis for invariants, performance guarantees, and correctness proofs.
**Is not:** A token count; a FLOP measure; a latency metric; a routing hop; a metadata write; a safety or truth tag; a Path B operation.
**Example:** OB interpretation that resolves a referent counts as an MCS; writing TPTB or TPSF does not; updating lane lineage does not; merge reconciliation of conflicting semantic cores does.
**Normative home:** 20.30.040.

## semantic_core — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the immutable, governance‑ready semantic snapshot produced at commit, capturing the finalized meaning of the turn for archival, replay, and downstream governance evaluation.  
**Focus:** Encodes the committed meaning‑bearing structures (propositions, roles, referents, frames, relations) extracted from the finalized TP(N) at commit; serves as the stable substrate for DF‑side truth, governance, and historical replay.  
**When:** Created exactly once per turn by OuBA at commit; never exists during Path‑A construction or Path‑B realization.  
**Why:** Provide a deterministic, replay‑safe semantic substrate that isolates meaning construction (Path‑A) and realization (Path‑B) from governance, archival, and historical evaluation.  
**Is not:** Path‑A structure; routing structure; safety model; truth‑metadata object; latent embedding; Path‑B construct; stylistic or expressive representation.  
**Example:** OuBA freezes the finalized TP(N) into semantic_core, which DF then uses for truth evaluation (TB), governance integration (GBIB), and governance decision (GB).  
**Normative home:** 20.30.010, 20.30.050.

## update_structure — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represent the deterministic, fully‑specified envelope of all Path‑A outputs (truth_hypotheses, TPTB, TPSF, ΔH%, H%, merge_result, routing metadata) that RBU validates before commit.  
**Focus:** Encode the complete, replay‑safe Path‑A state transition, including structural updates, metadata fields, and commit‑relevant deltas; ensure no implicit or hidden computation occurs during RBU validation.  
**When:** Constructed after merge, truth‑hypotheses evaluation, completion evaluation, and uncertainty updates; passed to RBU as the authoritative input for validation.  
**Why:** Provide a single, auditable, deterministic object capturing the entire Path‑A evolution of the TP, enabling RBU to validate commit‑readiness without re‑running computation.  
**Is not:** semantic_core; semantic object; truth model; routing decision; latent representation; Path‑B construct; partial update; incremental diff.  
**Example:** After merge produces merge_result and truth‑hypotheses evaluation produces TPTB/TPSF, update_structure aggregates truth_hypotheses, TPTB, TPSF, ΔH%, H%, merge_result, and routing metadata into a single validation envelope for RBU.  
**Normative home:** 20.30.070.

## merge_result — 20.30
**Purpose:** Represents the deterministic, lane‑reconciled semantic output produced by merge after integrating multiple lane‑local semantic_cores, truth hypotheses, and structural signals.
**Focus:** Encodes the unified semantic structure (propositions, roles, referents, frames, relations) plus merge‑level decisions (conflict resolution, branch selection, structural reconciliation) that feed truth and completion evaluation.
**When:** Produced after OB/TB interpretation and before truth/completion evaluation; included in update_structure and validated by RBU during commit.
**Why:** Provides a single, authoritative semantic representation for downstream evaluation, ensuring deterministic reasoning and preventing lane‑specific divergence from leaking into commit.
**Is not:** A truth‑metadata object; a routing decision; a latent embedding; a Path B construct; a stylistic or expressive representation.
**Example:** Multiple lane‑local semantic_cores propose different referent bindings; merge_result reconciles them into a single referent structure used by truth evaluation.
**Normative home:** 20.30.050.

## **TP.TR — semantic‑routing fields**  
**Purpose:** Structured semantic‑routing state used by TR‑prm, RB‑prm, and SSG for staleness evaluation, semantic‑transition eligibility, and routing‑vector construction.  
**Focus:** Tracks semantic‑adjacent routing metadata (stance, intent, affect, epistemic shading, tension, politeness, commitment, reservation, logical structure, semantic‑deltaH, lineage additions, routing semantics). Contains routing‑relevant semantics, not meaning.  
**When:** Updated only when `tr_needs_update = true` under the TR‑gating contract.  
**Why:** Maintain deterministic, auditable semantic‑routing behavior across cycles; ensure semantic transitions occur only under explicit architectural conditions.  
**Is not:** A meaning field, truth field, stance interpreter, classifier, or semantic evaluator; does not contain semantic content, only semantic‑routing metadata.  
**Example:** TR‑prm updates stance and commitment fields after a semantic‑routing change; RB‑prm reads the updated TP.TR to determine semantic‑transition eligibility.  
**Normative home:** 20.30 (TP Message Contract), 20.40.040 (SmOB), 20.47 (SSG), 20.50 (RB), 20.10 (TR‑gating).

## DF — Inquiry‑side evaluation flow
**Purpose:** Perform governance‑side evaluation of committed meaning produced by OuBA, determining inquiry, truth, and governance outcomes for the turn.  
**Focus:** Process committed semantic_core snapshots through IB, TB, and GBIB into GB for inquiry evaluation, truth assessment, supervisory integration, and promotion or governance‑side routing decisions.  
**When:** Invoked only after Path‑A commits meaning via OuBA; operates exclusively on committed, immutable meaning.  
**Why:** Maintain deterministic, auditable governance evaluation; ensure inquiry, truth, and supervisory decisions occur only on committed meaning under explicit architectural conditions.  
**Is not:** Meaning‑construction flow; semantic writer; Path‑A participant; DF never modifies semantic_core, truth relations, or any Path‑A structure.  
**Example:** OuBA commits meaning; IB evaluates inquiry structure; TB evaluates truth; GBIB integrates supervisory context; GB logs governance events and either promotes to IdOB or emits governance inference to REx.  
**Normative home:** 20.705 (TS Flow Diagrams), 20.90 (IB), 20.80 (GB), 20.40.060 (OuBA), 20.110 (OuBB).

## HCF — Historical Conversation Flow
**Purpose:** Provide TS and Path‑B with long‑horizon conversational perspective by transforming turn‑final TP snapshots (committed by OuBB) into structured conversation‑level continuity and inference.  
**Focus:** Process historical TP sequences through MTP, COB, and CIL to derive conversation objects, referential history, topic threads, and long‑range conversational inference; outputs are consumed by CoHI and reinjected into Path‑B as continuity‑relevant structure.  
**When:** Invoked after Path‑B completes a turn and OuBB finalizes the turn‑final TP. Operates as a slow‑reader subsystem independent of real‑time execution, maintaining its own historical loop (MTP → COB → CIL) while exchanging information with Path‑B through CoHI.  
**Why:** Maintain deterministic, auditable long‑term conversational context; ensure continuity, referential stability, and multi‑turn inference derive only from committed TP history; provide Path‑B with structured, policy‑safe continuity without requiring Path‑B primitives to read or manipulate historical memory directly.  
**Is not:** Meaning‑construction flow; truth evaluator; governance subsystem; HCF does not modify TP, semantic_core, or Path‑A state; does not participate in real‑time realization.  
**Example:** OuBB commits a turn‑final TP; HCF ingests it and updates MTP; COB constructs or updates conversation objects; CIL derives long‑horizon inference; CoHI merges these historical structures with the next turn’s Path‑B TP to provide continuity‑aware realization.  
**Normative home:** 20.705 (TS Flow Diagrams), 20.190 (Glossary), 20.110 (OuBB), 20.113 (CoHI), 20.112 (COB), 20.114 (CIL).

## RSG Manifold — Expression Manifold Mapping Layer
**Purpose:** Provide TS with a geometric intermediate representation by projecting atomic SSR fields into an n‑dimensional expression manifold, enabling deterministic collapse and pattern selection for surface‑form realization.  
**Focus:** Convert SSR fields (identity, relation, domain, qualifier, time, truth/safety, tone, continuity) into manifold coordinates using projection operators; apply priority gradients (truth > safety > clarity > brevity > tone > continuity) to collapse the manifold to a single realizable point; output the selected manifold address for RSG pattern lookup.  
**When:** Invoked after Path‑A and Path‑B produce finalized SSR; operates during RSG’s forward‑projection and gradient‑collapse phases before table-driven realization; participates only in meaning-to-geometry mapping, not in TP extraction or grounding.  
**Why:** Ensure deterministic, bounded, non-generative translation from SSR to surface form; enforce truth/safety dominance through geometric constraint surfaces; provide a structured IR that supports reproducible realization and eliminates stochastic behavior; guarantee that RSG selects only pre-defined patterns from the offline table.  
**Is not:** A generative model; semantic inference engine; grammar constructor; RSG Manifold does not synthesize language, modify SSR, alter grounding metadata, or participate in TP formation; it is not a runtime memory system or historical continuity mechanism.  
**Example:** SSR contains identity=User, relation=Location, domain=Current, qualifier=Immediate; projection operators map fields to manifold coordinates; gradients collapse the manifold to a direct declarative region; RSG selects the corresponding table entry and realizes “You are here now.”  
**Normative home:** 20.30.085 (RSG Primitive), 20.30.090 (RSG Mapping Rules), 20.705 (TS Flow Diagrams), [why_ts_uses_manifold_model.md](system_playground/why_ts_uses_manifold_model.md)
