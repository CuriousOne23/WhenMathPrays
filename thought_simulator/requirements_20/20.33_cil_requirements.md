# **20.33 — CIL Requirements (Updated for Path‑A Cognitive‑Field Model)**  
**Conversation Integration Layer (CIL)**  
**Document ID:** 20.33_cil_requirements  
**Version:** 1.2 (Importance Integration Update)  
**Status:** Normative — Requirements (CIL)

---

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

CIL is the deterministic, cycle‑boundary normalization and freezing layer between:

- **COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot**,  
- **the Unified Stability Packet (USP)** from CST‑Mux, and  
- **CEx’s rigid intake requirements**.

CIL does **not** integrate new conversational material; structural integration is performed exclusively by COB.

CIL transforms:

- **TP.process.structural_cues**  
- **TP.metadata.intake_metadata**  
- **COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot**  
- **USP from CST‑Mux**  
- **importance signals** (new)  
  - structural‑importance  
  - constraint‑importance  
  - semantic‑adjacent importance  
  - identity‑importance  
  - long‑horizon importance  

into a **bounded, ordered, replay‑safe intake packet** for CEx.

CIL performs **no semantic interpretation**.

---

# **2. Architectural Position**

```
CST-Core ──┐
           ├──→ CST-Mux (USP) ──┐
CST-MS ────┘                    │
                                ├──→ CIL → CEx → CE → ISc → TPU → TP(N+1)
COB ────────────────────────────┘
OuBA → COB
```

CIL is not part of TS.  
CIL provides read‑only access to:

- COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot  
- USP from CST‑Mux  

CIL produces the intake packet consumed exclusively by CEx.

---

# **3. Inputs (Read‑Only)**

CIL reads:

- COB stabilized identity‑layer snapshot  
- USP from CST‑Mux  
- TP.process.structural_cues  
- TP.metadata.intake_metadata  
- TP.process.register_cues  
- TP.next_context{}  
- referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors  
- clarifying‑importance  
- structural‑importance (new)  
- constraint‑importance (new)  
- semantic‑adjacent importance (new)  
- identity‑importance (new)  
- long‑horizon importance (new)  
- conversation_count_complete (new)  
- initial_state_complete (new)

All inputs are read‑only.

---

# **4. Outputs**

CIL produces a deterministic intake packet containing:

- identity selection  
- certainty flags  
- ambiguity flags  
- stability indicators  
- structural hints  
- ordering metrics  
- clarifying‑field metadata  
- **all importance signals** (new)  
- referent mappings  
- register_hint  
- next‑turn context fields  
- completeness flags (new)  
- timestamps

---

  next_context: NextContextBlock,
  timestamps: { generated_turn: TurnID }

---

# **6. Identity Selection & Ordering Metrics**

Identity selection is derived solely from:

- COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot  
- TP.process.structural_cues  
- TP.metadata.intake_metadata  

CIL reflects COB’s ordering metrics without modification.

### **IdentitySelectionBlock**

```json
IdentitySelectionBlock {
  primary_layer_id: StableID | null,
  secondary_layer_ids: [StableID],
  layer_ranking: [{ layer_id: StableID, score: float }],
  last_referred: TurnID,
  total_referrals: int,
  recent_referrals: int,
  ordering_score: float
}
```

---

# **7. Clarifying‑Field Extraction**

CIL extracts clarifying fields, subfields, and hierarchical sub‑subfields (up to four levels) from COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot.

CIL preserves clarifying‑field topology, importance scores, provenance, and continuity across turns.

Bounded limits:

- max 10 clarifying fields  
- max 100 clarifying subfields  
- max depth 4  

Drops and compressions are recorded in **CE.metadata.extraction_audit**.

---

# **8. Register Hint**

CIL derives register_hint deterministically from:

- **TP.process.register_cues**, and  
- COB register fields  

without semantic interpretation.

---

# **9. Next‑Turn Context Integration (20.105 Alignment)**

CIL reads **TP.next_context{}** deterministically and reflects it into the intake packet without modification, renaming, or reinterpretation.

CIL treats next‑turn context as structural metadata only.

CIL preserves next‑turn context continuity across turns and handles missing fields as canonical empty‑context cases.

---

# **10. Deterministic Replay Guarantees**

CIL guarantees:

- identical inputs → identical intake packets  
- deterministic extraction of structural cues  
- deterministic clarifying‑field extraction  
- deterministic ordering metrics  
- deterministic next‑turn context reflection  
- deterministic reflection of USP stability content  

Replay equivalence is required across all fields.

# **11. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**  

---

## **11.1 Core Architectural Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.33‑001**  
CIL SHALL normalize and freeze **TP.process.structural_cues** and **TP.metadata.intake_metadata** together with COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot and the USP from CST‑Mux to produce the intake packet for CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑002**  
CIL SHALL generate referent certainty, temporal certainty, discourse certainty, field‑importance hints, and ambiguity flags.

### **HLR‑20.33‑003**  
CIL SHALL provide read‑only access to COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot.

### **HLR‑20.33‑004**  
CIL SHALL NOT maintain TP.semantic, TP.process, TP.metadata, MTP state, lineage, commitments, referents, active objects, or conversation‑objects.

### **HLR‑20.33‑005**  
CIL SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, scoring, candidate generation, or pipeline participation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑006**  
CIL output SHALL be consumed exclusively by CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑007**  
CIL output SHALL be consumed exclusively by CEx for deterministic intake packet formation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑008**  
CIL SHALL maintain determinism and replay equivalence for all generated flags and packets.

### **HLR‑20.33‑009**  
CIL SHALL NOT own clarification FIFO or supervisory escalation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑010**  
CIL SHALL NOT embed semantic meaning or interpretation into any view consumed by CEx.

### **HLR‑20.33‑011**  
CIL SHALL NOT interact with ISc, Merge, TPU, IB, TB, or RBU except through approved supervisory wires.

### **HLR‑20.33‑066**  
CIL SHALL accept the Unified Stability Packet (USP) from CST‑Mux as a distinct read‑only input.

### **HLR‑20.33‑067**  
CIL SHALL treat the USP as replay and packaging material only and SHALL NOT use it to exercise structural authority or to issue commands.

### **HLR‑20.33‑068**  
CIL SHALL NOT modify the content of the USP for control purposes.

### **HLR‑20.33‑069**  
CIL SHALL NOT send the USP, any derivative of the USP, or any stability control signal back to COB.

### **HLR‑20.33‑070**  
CIL SHALL reflect stability indicators in the intake packet from the USP and the COB snapshot without inventing new freeze, thaw, collapse, or topology decisions.

---

## **11.2 New Requirements — Importance Integration**

### **HLR‑20.33‑013**  
CIL SHALL produce an intake packet conforming to the CILIntakePacket schema.

### **HLR‑20.33‑020**  
CIL SHALL derive identity selection, certainty, ambiguity, stability, and structural hints solely from TP.process.structural_cues, TP.metadata.intake_metadata, COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot, and the USP from CST‑Mux.

### **HLR‑20.33‑022**  
CIL SHALL guarantee that identical structural cues, COB snapshots, and USP content produce identical intake packets.

### **HLR‑20.33‑071**  
CIL SHALL ingest structural‑importance from SOB/SROB (via COB snapshot) and reflect it into the intake packet without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑072**  
CIL SHALL ingest constraint‑importance from CnOB (via COB snapshot) and reflect it into the intake packet without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑073**  
CIL SHALL ingest semantic‑adjacent importance from SmOB (via COB snapshot) and reflect it into the intake packet without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑074**  
CIL SHALL ingest identity‑importance from IdOB (via COB snapshot) and reflect it into the intake packet without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑075**  
CIL SHALL ingest long‑horizon importance from COB and reflect it into the intake packet without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑076**  
CIL SHALL treat all importance signals as read‑only metadata and SHALL NOT compute, modify, or reinterpret importance values.

### **HLR‑20.33‑077**  
CIL SHALL expose all importance signals in the intake packet for CEx alignment.

### **HLR‑20.33‑078**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of importance extraction.

---

## **11.3 New Requirements — COB Completeness Signaling**

### **HLR‑20.33‑029**  
CIL SHALL include a register_hint derived from TP.process.register_cues and COB register fields.

### **HLR‑20.33‑031**  
CIL SHALL derive register_hint deterministically without semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.33‑079**  
CIL SHALL ingest `conversation_count_complete` from COB and reflect it into the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑080**  
CIL SHALL ingest `initial_state_complete` from COB and reflect it into the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑081**  
CIL SHALL treat completeness flags as read‑only and SHALL NOT compute or modify them.

### **HLR‑20.33‑082**  
CIL SHALL expose completeness flags to CEx for alignment and fallback logic.

---

## **11.4 New Requirements — Importance‑Weighted Identity Selection**

### **HLR‑20.33‑032**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer recency (`last_referred`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑033**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer frequency (`total_referrals`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑034**  
CIL SHALL include identity‑layer density (`recent_referrals`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑035**  
CIL SHALL include COB’s ordering score (`ordering_score`) in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑036**  
CIL SHALL expose recency, frequency, density, and ordering_score without modification.

### **HLR‑20.33‑037**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic ordering of identity layers using ordering_score provided by COB.

### **HLR‑20.33‑038**  
CIL SHALL NOT compute recency, frequency, density, or ordering_score.

### **HLR‑20.33‑039**  
CIL SHALL include ordering metrics in IdentitySelectionBlock.

### **HLR‑20.33‑083**  
CIL SHALL incorporate importance signals into identity‑layer ranking deterministically, using COB’s ordering_score + importance continuity.

### **HLR‑20.33‑084**  
CIL SHALL NOT compute new importance values; it SHALL only reflect COB’s importance‑weighted ordering.

### **HLR‑20.33‑085**  
CIL SHALL expose importance‑weighted identity selection in IdentitySelectionBlock.

## **11.5 Clarifying‑Field Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.33‑040**  
CIL SHALL extract clarifying fields, subfields, and hierarchical sub‑subfields (up to four levels) from COB’s stabilized identity‑layer snapshot.

### **HLR‑20.33‑041**  
CIL SHALL include clarifying fields, subfields, and importance scores in the intake packet.

### **HLR‑20.33‑042**  
CIL SHALL preserve clarifying‑field topology, including hierarchical relationships and bounded limits.

### **HLR‑20.33‑043**  
CIL SHALL enforce bounded limits on clarifying fields (10), subfields (100), and depth (4), dropping excess entries with extraction‑audit records.

### **HLR‑20.33‑044**  
CIL SHALL treat clarifying fields and subfields as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify their values, importance scores, or provenance.

### **HLR‑20.33‑045**  
CIL SHALL include clarifying‑field metadata in ClarifyingFieldBlock, ClarifyingImportanceBlock, and ClarifyingTopologyBlock.

### **HLR‑20.33‑046**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic clarifying‑field extraction.

### **HLR‑20.33‑047**  
CIL SHALL record clarifying‑field drops, truncations, and topology compressions in CE.metadata.extraction_audit.

### **HLR‑20.33‑048**  
CIL SHALL preserve clarifying‑field continuity across turns.

### **HLR‑20.33‑049**  
CIL SHALL include clarifying‑field metadata when identity selection is ambiguous or indeterminate.

### **HLR‑20.33‑050**  
CIL SHALL include clarifying‑field metadata in fallback identity selection and mark continuity status as `"undetermined"` when applicable.

## **11.6 Next‑Turn Context Requirements (20.105 Alignment)**

### **HLR‑20.33‑051**  
CIL SHALL read `TP.next_context{}` deterministically as defined in 20.105.

### **HLR‑20.33‑052**  
CIL SHALL treat all fields in `TP.next_context{}` as read‑only.

### **HLR‑20.33‑053**  
CIL SHALL place next‑turn context fields into the intake packet exactly as defined in the schema.

### **HLR‑20.33‑054**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields in NextContextBlock.

### **HLR‑20.33‑055**  
CIL SHALL include all next‑turn context fields defined in 20.105.

### **HLR‑20.33‑056**  
CIL SHALL NOT derive next‑turn context fields from clarifying fields, structural cues, or COB snapshots.

### **HLR‑20.33‑057**  
CIL SHALL guarantee deterministic replay of next‑turn context extraction.

### **HLR‑20.33‑058**  
CIL SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation when handling next‑turn context fields.

### **HLR‑20.33‑059**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields even when identity selection is ambiguous or fallback‑selected.

### **HLR‑20.33‑060**  
CIL SHALL preserve next‑turn context continuity across turns.

### **HLR‑20.33‑061**  
CIL SHALL NOT compute context coherence or context shift.

### **HLR‑20.33‑062**  
CIL SHALL NOT write next‑turn context fields into clarifying fields.

### **HLR‑20.33‑063**  
CIL SHALL NOT interact with truth/done fields, routing fields, structural geometry, ΔH%, lineage, or Path‑B semantics when processing next‑turn context.

### **HLR‑20.33‑064**  
CIL SHALL include next‑turn context fields in timestamps only through `generated_turn`.

### **HLR‑20.33‑065**  
CIL SHALL treat missing or partially populated `TP.next_context{}` fields as deterministic empty‑context cases.
