## Purpose

IMR is the Intake Mismatch Resolver. It is a parallel, non-semantic intake-side classifier that evaluates mismatch conditions, computes difficulty rating, emits deterministic mismatch tags, and writes an append-only audit record. IMR never blocks intake, never performs repairs, never constructs meaning, and never mutates any meaning-layer or intake-layer fields. IMR is metadata-only.

---

## Position in Architecture

IMR operates **in parallel** with early intake processing. It evaluates intake-side structural signals and produces metadata consumed by `exec_trace`, IE formation, and CEx. IMR does not participate in the main Path A pipeline and does not interact with semantic or identity layers.

---

## Inputs and Outputs

**Inputs (read-only):**
- IIInB output (normalized tokens, structural spans, markup boundaries, repair annotations)
- Evaluator signals and evaluator codes
- Intake metadata (fragmentation, ambiguity, repair density, markup complexity, structural anomalies)

**Outputs:**
- IMR record (append-only audit block)
- Difficulty rating (consumed by IE and CEx)
- Mismatch tags (consumed by IE)

---

## Functional Boundaries

IMR is strictly non-semantic and metadata-only. It does not perform repairs, semantic interpretation, meaning construction, or field mutation. It does not read or modify CE semantic output, identity-layers, referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field-importance maps, COB snapshots, or CIL packets.

---

## Normative Requirements

**HLR-20.45-001:** IMR SHALL evaluate mismatch conditions on intake-side structural signals.  
**HLR-20.45-002:** IMR SHALL compute difficulty rating from fragmentation, ambiguity, repair density, markup complexity, and structural anomalies.  
**HLR-20.45-003:** IMR SHALL emit deterministic mismatch tags.  
**HLR-20.45-004:** IMR SHALL write an append-only audit record.  
**HLR-20.45-005:** IMR SHALL NOT block intake.  
**HLR-20.45-006:** IMR SHALL NOT perform repairs.  
**HLR-20.45-007:** IMR SHALL NOT construct meaning.  
**HLR-20.45-008:** IMR SHALL NOT mutate any meaning-layer or intake-layer fields.  
**HLR-20.45-009:** IMR SHALL read only intake-side structural signals and evaluator codes.  
**HLR-20.45-010:** IMR SHALL NOT read CE semantic output, identity-layers, referent maps, temporal anchors, discourse anchors, field-importance maps, COB snapshots, or CIL packets.  
**HLR-20.45-011:** IMR SHALL maintain determinism and replay equivalence for all classification and audit records.  
**HLR-20.45-012:** IMR SHALL maintain stable, versioned schema and classification behavior.  
**HLR-20.45-013:** IMR SHALL produce metadata-only outputs and SHALL NOT generate semantic or intake-layer artifacts.

## IMR Data Model

IMR record contains:
- mismatch_type
- difficulty_rating
- mismatch_tags
- structural_anomalies
- evaluator_signal_code
- audit_timestamp
