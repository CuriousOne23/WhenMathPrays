# **20.145 — CTP‑prm (Collect Thought Point Primitive)**
**Document ID:** 20.145  
**Version:** 3.0 (Path‑A Placement Lock + Operational Freeze Semantics)  
**Status:** Normative — Pipeline‑A Structural Bookkeeping Primitive  
**Purpose:** Policy‑freeze the current TP immediately before RB arbitration and append one cognitive‑history entry  

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

CTP‑prm is the **Collect Thought Point** primitive.  
It performs **no semantic work** and **no routing work**.  
Its role is strictly **structural bookkeeping**.

Under the corrected Path‑A routing spine:

```
DCB → TR → CTP → RB → WrdNm → ISc → RTU → TR → CTP → RB → …
```

CTP‑prm runs **immediately before every RB‑prm** appearance.  
It freezes whatever TP exists at that moment (IdOB may or may not have run).

CTP‑prm:

- freezes the current TP as a **policy freeze** (no deep‑copy snapshot object, no `TP.CTP` block, no freeze flag required in v1)
- preserves routing‑layer fields written by RBU/TR/RTU exactly as present
- preserves meaning‑layer fields written by IdOB (when present) exactly as present
- appends exactly one cognitive‑history entry per invocation
- writes CTP provenance
- ensures RB‑prm receives a stable TP view without concurrent CTP mutation of semantics or routing
- maintains separation between meaning‑layer and routing‑layer fields (does not merge layers)

Historical framing (retained for architecture continuity):

- **CTP(field)** — TP stabilization after RBU/TR in earlier diagrams  
- **CTP(primitive)** — consolidation after IdOB‑prm in earlier diagrams  

In **v1 Path‑A**, these are **not separate implementation modes**.  
A single CTP invocation always‑before‑RB covers both situations by freezing the TP as‑is.

CTP‑prm does **not** merge meaning, interpret content, wait for IdOB, combine multi‑IdOB outputs, or modify TP semantics.  
It freezes policy‑state and packages history for audit and RB consumption.

# **1. Execution & Placement (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑001**  
**NA (v1 Path‑A).**  
Superseded by HLR‑20.145‑028.  
Prior text required execution after every RBU/TR and IdOB route by RB‑prm.  
Under corrected Path A, CTP runs immediately before RB and does not require IdOB to have executed.

### **HLR‑20.145‑002**  
**NA (v1 Path‑A).**  
Superseded by HLR‑20.145‑028.  
Prior text required CTP before every RB arbitration except the first in the Path‑A cycle.  
Under corrected Path A, CTP runs immediately before **every** RB appearance, including the first.

### **HLR‑20.145‑003**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT be bypassed on any Path‑A segment that ends in RB‑prm arbitration.

### **HLR‑20.145‑028 — Always Before RB**  
CTP‑prm SHALL execute immediately before every RB‑prm arbitration on Path A.  
CTP‑prm SHALL run even when IdOB‑prm has not executed in the current cycle segment.

### **HLR‑20.145‑029 — Path‑A Adjacency**  
On the Path‑A routing spine, CTP‑prm SHALL occupy the position between TR‑prm and RB‑prm:

```
… → DCB → TR → CTP → RB → …
```

# **2. Collection & Combination (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑004**  
**NA (v1 Path‑A).**  
Superseded by HLR‑20.145‑030.  
Prior text required collection of TP snapshots after all IdOB‑prm instances wrote meaning‑layer deltas.  
v1 freezes the current TP as‑is; IdOB need not have run.

### **HLR‑20.145‑005**  
**NA (v1 Path‑A).**  
Superseded by HLR‑20.145‑030.  
Prior text required waiting until all routed IdOB‑prm instances completed.  
v1 CTP does not wait for IdOB.

### **HLR‑20.145‑006**  
**NA (v1 Path‑A).**  
Superseded by HLR‑20.145‑030.  
Prior text required combining all IdOB‑prm outputs into a single TP without semantic merging.  
v1 does not combine multi‑IdOB outputs; any IdOB deltas already resident in TP are left unchanged.

### **HLR‑20.145‑007**  
CTP‑prm SHALL preserve provenance such that contributions already recorded by upstream primitives (including RBU/TR and IdOB when present) remain identifiable.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT erase or rewrite upstream provenance entries.

### **HLR‑20.145‑030 — Freeze TP As‑Is**  
CTP‑prm SHALL freeze the current TP state as received.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT wait for IdOB‑prm.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT collect or combine multi‑IdOB output sets.  
If IdOB fields are present on the TP, CTP‑prm SHALL leave them unchanged.  
If IdOB fields are absent, CTP‑prm SHALL still complete successfully.

# **3. Freezing & Immutability (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑008**  
CTP‑prm SHALL apply a **policy freeze**: after CTP completes, CTP‑prm itself SHALL NOT further modify the TP until a later CTP invocation.  
Policy freeze does **not** require a deep‑copy snapshot object, a `TP.CTP` block, or a dedicated freeze flag in v1.

### **HLR‑20.145‑009**  
CTP‑prm SHALL leave all non‑CTP‑owned fields unchanged so that RB‑prm can read the TP without concurrent CTP mutation of semantics, routing vectors, or geometric ownership fields.

### **HLR‑20.145‑031 — Freeze Is Policy‑Only (v1)**  
In v1, freeze SHALL be policy‑only.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT create a deep‑copy snapshot store.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT create a `TP.CTP` block as a required write target.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT require a freeze‑flag field for correctness.

---

# **4. Interaction with TS, RBU/TR, IdOB, and RB‑prm (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑010**  
CTP‑prm SHALL accept as input the current TP at the CTP execution point (after upstream DCB/TR and any earlier primitives that have already written).  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT require IdOB fields to be present.

### **HLR‑20.145‑011**  
CTP‑prm SHALL provide the post‑CTP TP to TS for delivery to RB‑prm (or to the progressive runner equivalent).

### **HLR‑20.145‑012**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT modify TP semantics; only TPU‑prm may modify TP content in the normative content‑mutation sense.  
CTP‑prm’s allowed writes are limited to cognitive‑history, CTP provenance, and optional non‑semantic audit side channels defined in this document.

### **HLR‑20.145‑013**  
CTP‑prm SHALL preserve all routing‑relevant information already present on the TP for RB‑prm while preserving separation between routing‑layer fields and meaning‑layer fields.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT invent missing routing or meaning fields.

### **HLR‑20.145‑014**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT perform routing, scoring, arbitration, or semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.145‑020**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT merge routing‑layer fields into meaning‑layer fields or vice‑versa.  
Any unification for RB consumption is by TP boundary identity only (same TP object), not by field fusion.

### **HLR‑20.145‑021 — Invariant/Residue/Geometry Prohibition**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT interpret, merge, transform, or recompute invariant‑layer fields (`semantic.importance`, `metadata.semantic_residue`, identity geometry markers, residue topology markers, invariant vector approximations).  
These fields SHALL be preserved exactly as written by upstream primitives when present.

## **4.1 Cognitive‑History Logging (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑022 — Cognitive‑History Append**
CTP‑prm SHALL append exactly one cognitive‑history entry to:

```
metadata.cognitive_history[]
```

for every CTP‑prm invocation.

### **HLR‑20.145‑023 — Cognitive‑History Content**
Each cognitive‑history entry SHALL contain the following keys (schema‑stable):

```
cycle_id
timestamp
invariants: {
    I_stab,
    R_res,
    P_cont,
    L_depth,
    Rt_adj,
    delta_H,    # ΔH
    E_dens,
    C_coh
}
idob_geometry: {
    neighborhood,
    k_id
}
idob_roles
idob_residue
idob_stability
rb_adjacency_class
rb_displacement_scale
rb_regime_hint
rb_route_proposal
```

Values SHALL be copied exactly from upstream TP locations when present.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, merge, or recompute any invariant‑layer, identity‑geometry, or residue‑topology values.

### **HLR‑20.145‑032 — Missing Field Policy**  
If a cognitive‑history source field is absent or unavailable, CTP‑prm SHALL write JSON `null` / language `None` for that key.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT omit required keys from the entry schema.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT invent substitute values.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT fail solely because foundation or IdOB fields are missing.

### **HLR‑20.145‑024 — Cognitive‑History Append‑Only**
`metadata.cognitive_history[]` SHALL be append‑only.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT delete, reorder, or modify prior entries.

### **HLR‑20.145‑025 — Cognitive‑History Determinism**
Given identical TP inputs and identical cycle_id/timestamp inputs, CTP‑prm SHALL produce identical cognitive‑history entries.  
Cognitive‑history logging SHALL support deterministic replay.

### **HLR‑20.145‑026 — Cognitive‑History Write Boundary**
CTP‑prm SHALL write only:

- `metadata.cognitive_history[]` (append one entry)
- provenance fields explicitly assigned to CTP‑prm (`metadata.provenance.ctp_last_update`)
- optional diagnostic audit side channel (`exec_trace` CTP ref), if used

CTP‑prm SHALL NOT write or modify:

- IdOB envelope fields  
- RB routing‑filter fields  
- TR fields  
- DCB `geometric_state`, `geometric_history`, or `dcb_events`  
- semantic_core  
- intake fields  
- context metadata  
- routing_metadata  
- a required `TP.CTP` snapshot block (v1)

### **HLR‑20.145‑027 — Cognitive‑History Timing**
CTP‑prm SHALL append the cognitive‑history entry as part of the CTP invocation that implements the policy freeze, and SHALL complete that append before the TP is considered ready for RB‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.145‑033 — Cycle Context Sources**  
CTP‑prm SHALL obtain `cycle_id` from DCB provenance when available (`metadata.provenance` / DCB cycle markers as present on TP), else from runner‑supplied cycle context for isolation tests.  
CTP‑prm SHALL obtain `timestamp` from runner‑supplied deterministic timestamp in testbench mode, or system/runner clock in general execution.  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT require a separate `_ctp_cycle_context` object beyond optional testbench convenience.

CTP‑prm SHALL complete with cognitive‑history nulls for missing sources.

# **7. Informative Summary (No SHALLs)**

CTP‑prm is a **structural bookkeeping primitive** that:

- runs always immediately before RB on Path A  
- policy‑freezes the current TP (no deep‑copy snapshot, no required `TP.CTP` block)  
- appends one schema‑stable cognitive‑history entry (missing → null)  
- writes CTP provenance  
- preserves upstream provenance and layer separation  
- does not wait for, collect, combine, or validate IdOB  
- does not merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or perform routing  

It is essential for determinism, auditability, and a stable TP view for RB on Pipeline‑A.
