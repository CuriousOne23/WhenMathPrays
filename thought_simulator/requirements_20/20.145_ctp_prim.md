# **20.145 — CTP‑prm (Collect Thought Point Primitive)**  
**Document ID:** 20.145  
**Version:** 2.0 (Structural Optimization + TP‑Field Update)  
**Status:** Normative — Pipeline‑A Structural Bookkeeping Primitive  
**Purpose:** Freeze, consolidate, and package TP snapshots between IdOB‑chains  

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

CTP‑prm is the **Collect Thought Point** primitive.  
It performs **no semantic work** and **no routing work**.  
Its role is strictly **structural bookkeeping**.

CTP‑prm:

- collects all meaning‑layer outputs written by IdOB‑prm  
- preserves routing‑layer fields written by RBU/TR/RTU  
- freezes the TP snapshot  
- packages a unified, immutable TP for RB‑prm arbitration  
- preserves provenance of all contributing primitives  
- ensures RB‑prm receives a complete, stable TP snapshot  
- maintains separation between meaning‑layer and routing‑layer fields  

CTP‑prm is the **barrier primitive** between IdOB‑chains in Pipeline‑A.

There are two appearances:

- **CTP(field)** — initial TP‑snapshot stabilization after RBU/TR  
- **CTP(primitive)** — consolidation after IdOB‑prm, producing the unified TP snapshot for RB‑prm

CTP‑prm does **not** merge meaning, interpret content, or modify TP semantics.  
It simply freezes and packages the TP snapshot.

---

# **1. Execution & Placement (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑001**  
CTP‑prm SHALL execute after every RBU/TR and IdOB route by RB‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.145‑002**  
CTP‑prm SHALL execute before every RB‑prm arbitration except the first in the Path‑A cycle.

### **HLR‑20.145‑003**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT be bypassed when RB‑prm has routed one or more IdOB‑prm instances.

---

# **2. Collection & Combination (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑004**  
CTP‑prm SHALL collect TP snapshots produced after all IdOB‑prm instances have written meaning‑layer deltas.

### **HLR‑20.145‑005**  
CTP‑prm SHALL wait until all routed IdOB‑prm instances have completed before producing its output.

### **HLR‑20.145‑006**  
CTP‑prm SHALL combine all IdOB‑prm outputs into a single TP snapshot without semantic merging.

### **HLR‑20.145‑007**  
CTP‑prm SHALL preserve provenance such that contributions of each RBU/TR and IdOB instance remain identifiable.

---

# **3. Freezing & Immutability (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑008**  
CTP‑prm SHALL freeze the TP snapshot such that no modifications occur until after the next RB‑prm arbitration.

### **HLR‑20.145‑009**  
CTP‑prm SHALL produce an immutable TP snapshot that RB‑prm can safely read without risk of concurrent modification.

---

# **4. Interaction with TS, RBU/TR, IdOB, and RB‑prm (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑010**  
CTP‑prm SHALL accept as input only the TP snapshot produced after RBU/TR and IdOB have written their fields.

### **HLR‑20.145‑011**  
CTP‑prm SHALL provide its TP snapshot exclusively to TS for delivery to RB‑prm.

### **HLR‑20.145‑012**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT modify TP semantics; only TPU‑prm may modify TP content.

### **HLR‑20.145‑013**  
CTP‑prm SHALL ensure the combined TP snapshot contains all routing‑relevant information required by RB‑prm while preserving separation between routing‑layer fields and meaning‑layer fields.

### **HLR‑20.145‑014**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT perform routing, scoring, arbitration, or semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.145‑020**  
CTP‑prm SHALL NOT merge routing‑layer fields into meaning‑layer fields or vice‑versa; it SHALL unify them only at the TP snapshot boundary for RB‑prm consumption.

---

# **5. Parallel Execution Semantics (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑015**  
CTP‑prm SHALL support collection of IdOB‑prm outputs executed sequentially or in parallel.

### **HLR‑20.145‑016**  
CTP‑prm SHALL treat all IdOB‑prm outputs routed by RB‑prm as belonging to a single IdOB‑chain, regardless of execution order or parallelism.

---

# **6. Error & Completeness Guarantees (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.145‑017**  
CTP‑prm SHALL detect and reject incomplete IdOB‑chains.

### **HLR‑20.145‑018**  
CTP‑prm SHALL fail safely if IdOB‑prm outputs are structurally invalid or inconsistent.

### **HLR‑20.145‑019**  
CTP‑prm SHALL guarantee that RB‑prm receives exactly one TP snapshot per IdOB‑chain.

---

# **7. Informative Summary (No SHALLs)**

CTP‑prm is a **structural bookkeeping primitive** that:

- collects RBU/TR and IdOB outputs  
- preserves provenance  
- freezes TP state  
- packages a unified TP snapshot  
- ensures RB‑prm receives a complete routing substrate  
- enables repeated IdOB‑chains in Path‑A  

CTP‑prm does **not** merge meaning, resolve conflicts, interpret content, or perform routing.  
It is essential for determinism, auditability, and correctness of Pipeline‑A.
