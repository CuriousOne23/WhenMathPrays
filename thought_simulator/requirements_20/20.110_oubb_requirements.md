# 20.110 OuBB Requirements

**Document ID:** 20.110  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 2026-06-07  
**Status:** Approved v0.2 — Stage 4 GATE (2026-06-07; CuriousOne23, Copilot, Grok; no edits required)

## Purpose

Defines normative **Output Basin (OuBB)** expression semantics for Pipeline B: seed-bound surface-text realization from frozen committed meaning, incomplete-meaning handling, and presentation policy — without mutating `SSR` or Pipeline A state.

**This module owns:** expression selection determinism, seed boundary, incomplete-meaning detection semantics, and presentation rules under messy-input flags.  
**Integration wire contract:** [20.110.010](20.110.010_oubb_stack.md) (`OuBB_artifact_ref`, XP/`exec_trace` binding, IMR handoff) — **not duplicated here** (OQ-004).

**Naming authority:** [20.500](20.500_refactoring_for_dual_TS_pipeline.md) §5 — OuBB is Pipeline B only; no TP-B; no basin **OB** alias.

For Path B, OuBB consumes artifacts that have passed invariant validation by ReB‑prm (20.169). ReB performs the final internal correctness and invariant checks before OuBB executes expression selection.

## 1. Pipeline and Input Boundary

OuBB executes on Pipeline B only, after `exec_plan` commit for the active `commit_id` ([20.110.010](20.110.010_oubb_stack.md) HLR-20.110.010-001; [20.36](20.36_canonical_end_to_end_trace.md) HLR-20.036-005).

```text
frozen SSR (semantic_snapshot_ref)
  + exec_plan (opbeh_id, obg_id, xlater_id, expression_plan_ref)
  + seed_scope_ref
       → OuBB expression selection
       → OuBB_artifact_ref (20.110.010)
```

1. **HLR-20.110-001:** OuBB SHALL render output expressions from **frozen** `SSR` referenced by `semantic_snapshot_ref` at `the Path‑A commit for the active commit_id` for the active `commit_id` — not from live mutable MTP drafts or lane-local TP state.
2. **HLR-20.110-010:** OuBB SHALL execute only on Pipeline B after `exec_plan_commit` for the bound (`commit_id`, `cycle_id`) / XP pass.
3. **HLR-20.110-011:** OuBB SHALL NOT read or write tp_id, lane_id, or any Pipeline‑A fields. OuBB SHALL operate exclusively on SSR and Pipeline‑B metadata.
4. **HLR-20.110-012:** OuBB SHALL consume `opbeh_id`, `obg_id`, `xlater_id`, and `expression_plan_ref` from `exec_plan` only — not from TP or basin routing tables directly.

---

## 2. Expression Determinism and Seed Boundary

5. **HLR-20.110-002:** OuBB SHALL consume seed-controlled variability **only** at expression selection (HLR-20.012-010).
6. **HLR-20.110-003:** OuBB output mapping SHALL remain deterministic for fixed frozen `SSR`, fixed `exec_plan` content, and fixed `seed_scope_ref`.
7. **HLR-20.110-004:** OuBB SHALL NOT alter upstream meaning-construction state: no writes to `SSR`, MTP pre-commit drafts, or Pipeline A envelopes.

**Invariants (retained):**

| Invariant | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Determinism | Identical inputs → identical `OuBB_artifact_ref` content hash (20.110.010 HLR-20.110.010-008) |
| Seed boundary | Seed affects expression/realization only; strip replay leaves `SSR` unchanged |

---

## 3. Writes and Artifacts

OuBB expression outputs are realized as `OuBB_artifact_ref` records appended to `exec_trace` per [20.110.010](20.110.010_oubb_stack.md) §2. This module defines **semantic content** of those artifacts; wire field names and XP denormalization are owned by 20.110.010 / 20.205.

8. **HLR-20.110-013:** OuBB SHALL emit exactly one primary `OuBB_artifact_ref` per OuBB invocation on a normal XP pass unless an approved IMR Type A retry schedules an additional expression pass on the same `xp_record` ([20.205](20.205_execution_packet_xp_requirements.md) §6).
9. **HLR-20.110-014:** OuBB SHALL NOT emit `CorrectionTrigger` or IMR taxonomy classifications — IMR owns post-expression evaluation ([20.110](20.110.010_oubb_stack.md) HLR-20.110.010-012).

---

## 4. Messy-Input and Presentation

10. **HLR-20.110-015:** OuBB SHALL NOT clear messy_input_record, smooth conflict_flag, or resolve underspec_flag in SSR..
11. Under `underspec_flag` or `conflict_flag`, OuBB presentation SHALL follow OBG/register policy from `exec_plan` without inventing resolved meaning or suppressed contradiction evidence.

---

## 5. Incomplete-Meaning Handling (Legacy TP Path Reconciled)

Pre-dual-pipeline HLRs referenced TP tagging. **Dual-pipeline posture** per [20.110.010](20.110.010_oubb_stack.md) HLR-20.110.010-013/014:

12. **HLR-20.110-005:** OuBB SHALL detect incomplete-meaning conditions when ΔH% / stability metrics exceed deployment-defined thresholds. OuBB SHALL NOT externalize resolved meaning under incomplete-meaning conditions.
13. **HLR-20.110-006:** When incomplete-meaning is detected, OuBB SHALL append an audit event to `exec_trace` with `OuBB_event_type = INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED` and SHALL schedule IB-Creation-Request pathways **only** through the supervisory queue — **never** via SSR mutation.
14. **HLR-20.110-007:** OuBB SHALL notify GB indirectly via the TS IB-Creation-Request pathway. OuBB SHALL NOT communicate with GB directly.
15. **HLR-20.110-008:** Incomplete-meaning detection, `exec_trace` logging, and IB-Creation-Request scheduling SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries.
16. **HLR-20.110-009:** Incomplete-meaning events in `exec_trace` SHALL include at minimum:

```text
OuBB_incomplete_meaning_event {
  OuBB_event_type,           // INCOMPLETE_MEANING_DETECTED
  commit_id,
  cycle_id,
  xp_id,
  delta_h_q32,              // or deployment H% metric field
  threshold_q32,
  reason_code,
  policy_signature,
  audit_seq
}
```

Events SHALL be deterministic, append-only, and bounded. **Legacy:** pre-dual-pipeline exports that logged `tp_seq` on TP are deprecated; new fixtures SHALL use the `exec_trace` event shape above.

| Owner | Responsibility |
|-------|----------------|
| **OuBB (this module)** | Expression selection; seed-bound realization; incomplete-meaning detection semantics |
| **ReB‑prm (20.169)** | Performs invariant‑basin validation before OuBB; OuBB does not replace or duplicate ReB checks |
| **20.110.010** | Integration wire; `OuBB_artifact_ref` schema; XP/`exec_trace` binding |
| **OpBeh/OBG/XlateR** | Planning and register policy inputs on `exec_plan` |
| **IMR** | Post-OuBB evaluation; `CorrectionTrigger` taxonomy |
| **SSR** | Frozen semantic snapshot — not an OuBB write target — **not** an OuBB write target in dual-pipeline mode |

## 7. Verification Focus

- Class 1 ([20.36](20.36_canonical_end_to_end_trace.md)): OuBB after `exec_plan_commit`; `OuBB_artifact_ref` in `exec_trace`; strip leaves `SSR` unchanged
- Seed variant: artifact hash differs; `SSR` strip-identical
- No `lane_id` / `tp_id` on OuBB I/O
- XP `EXPRESSING` → `IMR_EVAL` requires `OuBB_artifact_ref` (20.110.010 HLR-20.110.010-018)
- Incomplete-meaning: event in `exec_trace`, not TP; no `SSR` smoothing
- `b_regeneration_equivalent`: OuBB stage reproduces equivalent artifact (20.207 / 20.36 Class 1)
