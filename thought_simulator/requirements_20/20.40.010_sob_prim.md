# **20.40.010 — SOB Requirements (Rewritten)**  
**Structural OB Layer (SOB)**  
**Document ID:** 20.40.010  
**Version:** 1.0 (Structural Optimization Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative  
**Parent:** 20.40_ob_requirements.md  
**Scope:** Requirements for the Structural OB (SOB) layer in Path A  
**Purpose:** Define mandatory behavior, invariants, and residue‑handling rules for the first OB layer  

---

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structural OB (SOB) layer is the **first classification layer** in Path A.  
Its role is to extract **structure before meaning**, producing:

- a structured TP  
- structural metadata  
- residue fragments  

SOB performs:

- segmentation  
- modality detection  
- operator‑hint extraction  
- domain‑hint extraction  
- tone‑hint extraction  
- constraint‑hint extraction  

SOB does **not** interpret meaning, resolve semantics, infer intent, or apply constraints.  
Its output prepares the TP for deterministic routing deeper into Path A, beginning with SROB.

---

# **2. Functional Overview** *(Informative)*

SOB:

- segments TP into structural units  
- extracts structural hints  
- produces residue fragments  
- preserves ordering and structural integrity  
- ensures deterministic, reproducible metadata  
- prepares residue for routing by RB  
- ensures entropy reduction before SROB  

SOB is strictly **pre‑semantic** and operates only on structural properties.

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**  
*(Reorganized, merged/split for clarity; all original information preserved)*

---

## **3.1 Structural Segmentation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑001**  
SOB SHALL segment the input TP into structural units including sentences, clauses, lists, tables, code blocks, and mathematical blocks.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑002**  
SOB SHALL preserve the order of structural units exactly as provided in the input.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑003**  
SOB SHALL identify list structures (ordered, unordered, nested) and represent them explicitly in the TP structure.

---

## **3.2 Modality Extraction Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑004**  
SOB SHALL classify each structural unit by modality, including declarative, interrogative, imperative, conditional, and hypothetical.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑005**  
SOB SHALL mark modality classifications in the TP structure without altering the original text.

---

## **3.3 Operator‑Hint Extraction Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑006**  
SOB SHALL extract operator hints when present, including summarize, compare, classify, derive, plan, explain, rewrite, and transform.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑007**  
SOB SHALL represent operator hints as residue fragments suitable for routing.

---

## **3.4 Domain‑Hint Extraction Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑008**  
SOB SHALL extract domain hints when present, including math‑like, code‑like, narrative‑like, legal‑like, technical‑like, and conversational‑like.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑009**  
SOB SHALL encode domain hints as residue fragments without interpreting domain semantics.

---

## **3.5 Tone‑Hint Extraction Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑010**  
SOB SHALL extract tone hints when present, including formal, casual, technical, supportive, urgent, and neutral.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑011**  
SOB SHALL encode tone hints as residue fragments without modifying the TP.

---

## **3.6 Constraint‑Hint Extraction Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑012**  
SOB SHALL extract constraint hints when present, including precision, conciseness, politeness, safety, and formatting constraints.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑013**  
SOB SHALL represent constraint hints as residue fragments for later processing by CnOB.

---

## **3.7 Structural Integrity Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑014**  
SOB SHALL ensure that no structural unit is lost, merged incorrectly, or reordered.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑015**  
SOB SHALL ensure that all extracted structural metadata is deterministic and reproducible for identical inputs.

---

## **3.8 Residue Formation Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑016**  
SOB SHALL produce residue fragments representing unresolved structure, operator hints, domain hints, tone hints, and constraint hints.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑017**  
SOB SHALL ensure that residue fragments are addressable by the routing block (RB) using the system’s geometric addressing scheme.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑018**  
SOB SHALL ensure that residue entropy after SOB is strictly less than the entropy of the raw TP.

---

## **3.9 Routing Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑019**  
SOB SHALL produce residue suitable for routing into SROB as the next classification layer.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑020**  
SOB SHALL NOT route directly into semantic OBs or constraint OBs.

---

## **3.10 Prohibited Behavior Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑021**  
SOB SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation of any kind.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑022**  
SOB SHALL NOT infer meaning, intent, or semantic roles.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑023**  
SOB SHALL NOT apply constraints or enforce constraint logic.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑024**  
SOB SHALL NOT modify the original text of the TP.

---

## **3.11 Discourse‑Context Propagation Requirements (Structural Only)**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑030**  
SOB MAY consume CE‑derived and CEx‑derived discourse‑context metadata in a read‑only manner, including recent‑entity continuity, temporal markers, contrastive structural markers, and causal structural markers.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑031**  
SOB SHALL encode discourse‑context metadata only as structural flags in residue and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation or intent inference.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑032**  
Discourse‑context flags SHALL be deterministic, bounded, and reproducible for identical inputs.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑033**  
SOB SHALL NOT modify TP text, semantic_core, FFTM fields, or any meaning‑layer representation when propagating discourse‑context metadata.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑034**  
Discourse‑context flags SHALL be representable as addressable residue fragments suitable for routing by RB and refinement by SROB, STPX, and ISc.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑035 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
SOB SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves structural segmentation or hint extraction.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑036 — Read‑Only Discipline**
SOB SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑037 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
SOB SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to structural classification and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑038 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
SOB SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑039 — Structural‑Only Interpretation**
SOB SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as structural or semantic‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation.

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑040 — Residue Integration**
SOB SHALL encode any consumed TP‑stream metadata as structural residue fragments when such metadata affects segmentation or hint extraction.

## **3.12 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

SOB may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic or semantic‑adjacent metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to structural classification. Applicable metadata includes:

- context_metadata (CE)
- residue_metadata (upstream OB‑Set)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- identity_metadata (IdOB)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)

SOB treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
SOB ignores metadata that is not applicable to structural segmentation or hint extraction.  
SOB does not consume routing_metadata, ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
SOB interprets TP‑stream metadata only as structural or semantic‑adjacent features.

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑025**  
SOB SHALL ensure that each residue fragment $r_i$ is hashable into an address fragment $h_i$ using the system’s approved hashing function:  

$$
h_i = hash(r_i)
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑026**  
SOB SHALL ensure that the combined query address $q$ formed by the routing block satisfies:  

$$
q = h_1 \oplus h_2 \oplus \dots \oplus h_n
$$

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑027**  
SOB SHALL ensure that residue fragments do not violate monotonicity, smoothness, or curvature‑invariance requirements defined in 20.40.

---

# **5. Outputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑028**  
SOB SHALL output:

- structured TP  
- residue set  
- structural metadata  
- operator‑hint metadata  
- domain‑hint metadata  
- tone‑hint metadata  
- constraint‑hint metadata  

### **HLR‑20.40.010‑029**  
SOB SHALL guarantee that its output is valid input for SROB.
