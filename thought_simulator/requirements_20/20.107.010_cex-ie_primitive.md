# 20.107.010 — CEx‑IE Primitive (TP‑Aligned, Version 1.0)  
### *IE Repackaging Layer for CEx Cross‑Correlation*

---

## 1. Purpose *(Informative)*

CEx‑IE is the **first internal module** of the CEx primitive.  
It reads the **IE‑emitted TP substrate** and produces a **compact, CEx‑specific structural envelope** suitable for cross‑correlation with CIL in CEx‑CCR.

CEx‑IE:

- consumes IE’s committed intake fields (`ie_tokens`, `token_flags`, `normalized_text`, `structure`, `repair_annotations`),  
- applies **bounded, rule‑driven structural interpretation** (no global semantics),  
- emits a **CEx‑IE envelope** containing structural hints and repackaged features,  
- does not perform conversation selection, lineage alignment, or packaging for downstream primitives.

All conversation relevance decisions are performed by CEx‑CCR and CEx‑Pck.

---

## 2. TP Inputs to CEx‑IE *(Informative)*

CEx‑IE operates on the **IE‑emitted TP envelope** and reads only the following fields:

- `TP.intake.ie_tokens`  
- `TP.intake.token_flags`  
- `TP.intake.normalized_text`  
- `TP.structure.tags`  
- `TP.structure.spans`  
- `TP.structure.markup`  
- `TP.metadata.repair_annotations`  

These fields are produced by IE per **20.109 — IE Primitive (TP‑Aligned, Version 3.3)** and form the **machine‑efficient substrate** for all CEx processing.

---

## 3. CEx‑IE Output Envelope *(Informative)*

CEx‑IE produces a **CEx‑specific structural envelope** under `TP.cex.ie`:

```json
{
  "cex": {
    "ie": {
      "tokens": ["string"],
      "token_flags": ["TokenFlag"],
      "normalized_text": "string",
      "structural_phrases": ["CExStructuralPhrase"],
      "topic_hint": "CExTopicHint",
      "intent_hint": "CExIntentHint",
      "continuity_hint": "CExContinuityHint",
      "reference_hint": "CExReferenceHint",
      "register_hint": "CExRegisterHint",
      "politeness_hint": "CExPolitenessHint",
      "direction_hint": "CExDirectionHint",
      "coherence_hint": "CExCoherenceHint",
      "importance_hint": "CExImportanceHint"
    }
  }
}
```

All fields are **compact**, **finite**, and **bounded‑semantic**, and must comply with the structural field requirements defined in 20.105.

---

## 4. Field Definitions *(Informative)*

### 4.1 Input Fields (from IE)

- **`TP.intake.ie_tokens`**  
  Committed token sequence emitted by IE after repair integration and normalization.

- **`TP.intake.token_flags`**  
  One entry per token, containing bounded semantic classification (e.g., normative, repaired, anomalous) and structural flags (e.g., punctuation, whitespace, anomaly markers).

- **`TP.intake.normalized_text`**  
  Normalized surface text constructed by IE using rule‑driven spacing and repair integration.

- **`TP.structure.tags`**  
  Structural tags emitted by IE (e.g., sentence boundaries, clause markers, structural separators).

- **`TP.structure.spans`**  
  Spans describing structural regions (e.g., repaired segments, anomaly regions, structural blocks).

- **`TP.structure.markup`**  
  Markup indicators for structural emphasis or formatting (e.g., bold, italics, code regions).

- **`TP.metadata.repair_annotations`**  
  Repair provenance entries describing applied IIInB repairs and anomaly resolutions.

---

### 4.2 Output Fields (CEx‑IE Envelope)

All output fields are **CEx‑specific** and live under `TP.cex.ie`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.tokens`**  
  Copy of `TP.intake.ie_tokens` for CEx‑internal use.

- **`TP.cex.ie.token_flags`**  
  Copy of `TP.intake.token_flags` for CEx‑internal use.

- **`TP.cex.ie.normalized_text`**  
  Copy of `TP.intake.normalized_text` for CEx‑internal use.

- **`TP.cex.ie.structural_phrases`**  
  Compact list of detected **explicit structural cue‑phrases** (e.g., “start fresh”, “new topic”, “as I was saying”, “please help”), each encoded as `CExStructuralPhrase` per 20.105.

- **`TP.cex.ie.topic_hint`** (`CExTopicHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `greeting | assistance | system | misc | noise | other`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.intent_hint`** (`CExIntentHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `inform | request | begin | none`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.continuity_hint`** (`CExContinuityHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `continue | reset | shift | unknown`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.reference_hint`** (`CExReferenceHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `none | previous | specific_previous | ambiguous_previous`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.register_hint`** (`CExRegisterHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `casual | formal | informal | none`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.politeness_hint`** (`CExPolitenessHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `high | normal | none`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.direction_hint`** (`CExDirectionHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `forward | backward | none`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.coherence_hint`** (`CExCoherenceHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `stable | unstable | none`.

- **`TP.cex.ie.importance_hint`** (`CExImportanceHint`)  
  Finite structural bucket: `low | medium | high`.

All enumerations must be defined as **compact, finite types** in the TS‑20.105 type registry.

## 5. Normative Requirements — Inputs and Outputs

**HLR‑20.107.010‑001**  
CEx‑IE SHALL read only the following TP fields:

- `TP.intake.ie_tokens`  
- `TP.intake.token_flags`  
- `TP.intake.normalized_text`  
- `TP.structure.tags`  
- `TP.structure.spans`  
- `TP.structure.markup`  
- `TP.metadata.repair_annotations`  

**HLR‑20.107.010‑002**  
CEx‑IE SHALL write only to `TP.cex.ie` and SHALL NOT modify any upstream TP fields produced by IE or IIInB.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑003**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.tokens` as a deterministic copy of `TP.intake.ie_tokens`.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑004**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.token_flags` as a deterministic copy of `TP.intake.token_flags`.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑005**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.normalized_text` as a deterministic copy of `TP.intake.normalized_text`.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑006**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.structural_phrases` using a compact representation of detected structural cue‑phrases, encoded as `CExStructuralPhrase` per 20.105.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑007**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.topic_hint` as a single `CExTopicHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to `ie_tokens`, `token_flags`, and `normalized_text`.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑008**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.intent_hint` as a single `CExIntentHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to explicit request‑like cue‑phrases and token patterns.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑009**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.continuity_hint` as a single `CExContinuityHint` value derived from explicit continuity cue‑phrases (e.g., “start fresh”, “new topic”, “again”, “continue”, “start over”, “switching gears”).

**HLR‑20.107.010‑010**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.reference_hint` as a single `CExReferenceHint` value derived from explicit reference‑back cue‑phrases (e.g., “as I was saying”, “back to the earlier point”, “returning to the first question”, “about that other thing”, “let’s go back to X”).

**HLR‑20.107.010‑011**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.register_hint` as a single `CExRegisterHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to token_flags and normalized_text (e.g., slang markers, formal markers).

**HLR‑20.107.010‑012**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.politeness_hint` as a single `CExPolitenessHint` value derived from explicit politeness cue‑phrases (e.g., “please”, “thanks”, “sorry”).

**HLR‑20.107.010‑013**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.direction_hint` as a single `CExDirectionHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to structural phrases indicating forward or backward reference (e.g., “moving on”, “back to…”).

**HLR‑20.107.010‑014**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.coherence_hint` as a single `CExCoherenceHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to structural tags, spans, and anomaly‑related token_flags.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑015**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit `TP.cex.ie.importance_hint` as a single `CExImportanceHint` value derived from bounded structural rules applied to emphasis‑related patterns (e.g., repeated tokens, emphasis phrases) without performing global semantic interpretation.

## 6. Normative Requirements — Processing Behavior

**HLR‑20.107.010‑016**  
CEx‑IE SHALL perform only bounded structural operations over `ie_tokens`, `token_flags`, `normalized_text`, `structure.tags`, `structure.spans`, and `repair_annotations`, operating on individual tokens or fixed windows of tokens (≤5) as defined in its ruleset.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑017**  
CEx‑IE SHALL detect structural cue‑phrases (e.g., continuity, reference‑back, intent, politeness, shift phrases) using deterministic pattern rules and SHALL encode them in `TP.cex.ie.structural_phrases`.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑018**  
CEx‑IE SHALL derive all hint fields (`topic_hint`, `intent_hint`, `continuity_hint`, `reference_hint`, `register_hint`, `politeness_hint`, `direction_hint`, `coherence_hint`, `importance_hint`) exclusively from IE‑emitted fields and SHALL NOT use any external context, embeddings, or global semantic inference.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑019**  
CEx‑IE SHALL be deterministically reproducible: given identical IE‑emitted TP input, CEx‑IE SHALL produce identical `TP.cex.ie` output.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑020**  
CEx‑IE SHALL NOT perform conversation selection, lineage alignment, or fallback decisions; these operations are reserved for CEx‑CCR and CEx‑Pck.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑021**  
CEx‑IE SHALL comply with all structural field naming, typing, and compactness requirements defined in TS‑20.105 for `TP.cex.ie` fields.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑022**  
CEx‑IE SHALL emit all hint fields even when their value is `none` or `unknown`, ensuring a complete and predictable `TP.cex.ie` envelope for downstream consumption.

**HLR‑20.107.010‑023**  
CEx‑IE SHALL NOT modify `TP.intake`, `TP.structure`, `TP.metadata.repair_annotations`, or any other upstream fields; it SHALL only read these fields and write to `TP.cex.ie`.

---

## 7. Processing Summary *(Informative)*

CEx‑IE:

1. Reads IE’s committed intake substrate (`ie_tokens`, `token_flags`, `normalized_text`, `structure`, `repair_annotations`).  
2. Detects explicit structural cue‑phrases and encodes them as `structural_phrases`.  
3. Derives compact structural hint fields (`topic_hint`, `intent_hint`, `continuity_hint`, `reference_hint`, `register_hint`, `politeness_hint`, `direction_hint`, `coherence_hint`, `importance_hint`) using bounded, deterministic rules.  
4. Emits a complete `TP.cex.ie` envelope for CEx‑CCR to use in cross‑correlation with CIL.

This completes the definition of **20.107.010 — CEx‑IE Primitive**.
