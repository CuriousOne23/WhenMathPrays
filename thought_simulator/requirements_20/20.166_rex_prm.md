# 20.166 — REx‑prm (Realization Extractor Primitive)

## 1. Purpose (informative)

REx is the **realization extraction primitive** of Path B.  
It accepts:

- meaning‑layer fields committed by LI  
- continuity\_fields committed by CoHI  
- truth\_tags and safety\_flags from A‑side  

REx applies the **substrate construction function** $\Phi$ to decompose committed meaning into a typed, dependency‑ordered sequence of **realization substeps**, producing the realization substrate `ssr.rsub` for RPlan.

REx performs:

- no meaning interpretation  
- no planning  
- no scoring  
- no routing  
- no surface generation  
- no modification of COB  
- no modification of lineage\_fields or SSR  

REx provides a **deterministic, typed handoff** between meaning and planning.

---

## 2. Architectural Position (informative)

REx is the **third** primitive in Path B:

**CoHI (1) → LI (2) → REx (3) → RPlan (4) → RPU (5) → ReB (6) → OuBB (7)**

Upstream:  
- TPTB  
- TPSF  
- CoHI  
- LI  

Downstream:  
- RPlan  

REx is the **sole** primitive permitted to construct realization substeps.
REx operates on the SSR(t) snapshot produced by Path‑A finalization and updated by CoHI and LI.

---

## 3. Inputs (informative)

REx reads exactly the following:

### 3.1 Meaning‑layer fields (from LI)
- `ssr.meaning`  
- `ssr.mli_status`  
- `ssr.mli_confidence`  
- `ssr.mli_conflict`  
- `ssr.mli_reason_code`  
- `ssr.mli_revision_cycles`  

### 3.2 Continuity fields (from CoHI)
- structural\_history  
- referential\_history  
- conversation\_objects  
- topic\_threads  
- continuity\_markers  

### 3.3 A‑side fields
- truth\_tags  
- safety\_flags  

No other fields may be accessed.

---

## 4. Transfer Function (informative)

REx executes one of two paths:

- **Abort Path** — when meaning is in conflict and unresolvable  
- **Construction Path** — when meaning is resolved or partially resolvable  

Let:

- $M$ = committed meaning  
- $\Gamma$ = continuity\_fields  
- $\Phi$ = substrate construction function  

---

### 4.1 Abort Path

A conflict entry $c$ is resolvable when:

- at least one truth\_tag asserts its referent  
- no safety\_flag prohibits acting on its referent  

The conflict set is **fully unresolvable** when no entry satisfies both conditions.

When unresolvable, REx produces:

- `rsub_status = BLOCKED`  
- `reason_code = UNRESOLVABLE_CONFLICT`  
- no substeps  

---

### 4.2 Construction Path

When meaning is resolved or partially resolvable, REx applies $\Phi$:

$$
\langle u_1,\ u_2,\ \dots,\ u_k \rangle = \Phi(M,\ \Gamma)
$$

Each substep $u\_i$ is a 4‑tuple:

$$
u_i = \bigl(\text{substep}\_{id_i},\ \text{substep}\_{type_i},\ p_i,\ D_i\bigr)
$$

Where:

- $\text{substep}\_{id_i}$ — unique identifier  
- $\text{substep}\_{type_i}$ — enum value  
- $p_i$ — payload (continuity‑augmented)  
- $D_i$ — dependency set referencing earlier substeps  

$\Phi$ must be deterministic: identical inputs produce identical substep sequences.

---

## 5. Outputs (informative)

REx writes exactly:

- `ssr.rsub.rsub_status`  
- `ssr.rsub.reason_code`  
- `ssr.rsub.substeps`  
- `ssr.rsub.substeps[i].substep_id`  
- `ssr.rsub.substeps[i].substep_type`  
- `ssr.rsub.substeps[i].payload`  
- `ssr.rsub.substeps[i].depends_on`  

All must be present on every execution path.

## 6. High‑Level Requirements (normative)

### Input and Access Control  
- **HLR‑20.166‑001** — REx SHALL read only the fields enumerated in the Input Contract.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑002** — REx SHALL NOT read COB, lineage_fields, or any A‑side meaning fields outside SSR.
- **HLR‑20.166‑003** — REx SHALL perform a pre‑condition check and SHALL block with `PRE_CONDITION_FAULT` when required fields are absent or invalid.

### Conflict Handling  
- **HLR‑20.166‑004** — REx SHALL evaluate resolvability of each conflict entry using truth\_tags and safety\_flags.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑005** — When the conflict set is fully unresolvable, REx SHALL block with `UNRESOLVABLE_CONFLICT`.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑006** — When at least one conflict entry is resolvable, REx SHALL proceed to construction.

### Substrate Construction  
- **HLR‑20.166‑007** — REx SHALL apply $\Phi$ to produce a deterministic ordered sequence of substeps.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑008** — REx SHALL produce at least one substep when `ssr.mli_status = RESOLVED`.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑009** — REx SHALL reject any substep whose `substep_type` is not a recognised enum value.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑010** — REx SHALL incorporate applicable continuity\_fields entries into substep payloads.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑011** — REx SHALL NOT incorporate inapplicable continuity entries into any payload.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑012** — REx SHALL ensure all dependency references in `depends_on` refer to earlier substeps.

### Invariant Preservation  
- **HLR‑20.166‑013** — REx SHALL preserve Meaning‑to‑Lineage Stability by not introducing referent mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑014** — REx SHALL propagate provenance\_chain without modification.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑015** — REx SHALL NOT introduce semantic mutation relative to LI.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑016** — REx SHALL NOT introduce style or tone mutation.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑017** — REx SHALL maintain deterministic replay: identical inputs SHALL produce identical outputs.

### Output Contract  
- **HLR‑20.166‑018** — REx SHALL populate all fields in the Output Contract on every execution path.  
- **HLR‑20.166‑019** — REx SHALL NOT write to any field owned by TPTB, TPSF, CoHI, LI, RPlan, RPU, ReB, or OuBB.

### Normative Home  
- **HLR‑20.166‑020** — Any implementation of REx SHALL conform to this specification at revision `0.3.1` or a formally approved successor.

---

## 7. Is / Is Not (informative)

REx **is**:
- the extractor of realization substeps  
- the bridge between meaning and planning  
- the producer of `ssr.rsub`  

REx **is not**:
- a semantic interpreter  
- a planner  
- a scorer  
- a router  
- a memory system  
- a surface generator  
- a modifier of COB  

---

## 8. Example (informative)

LI commits meaning for “summarize the dataset.”  
Continuity\_fields include a conversation object representing the dataset.  
truth\_tags assert it; safety\_flags are empty.

REx applies $\Phi$ and produces:

- $u\_1$: REFERENT\_RESOLVE  
- $u\_2$: DISCOURSE\_FRAME  
- $u\_3$: POLARITY\_SET  

All substeps are valid.  
`rsub_status = READY`.  
RPlan begins planning.
