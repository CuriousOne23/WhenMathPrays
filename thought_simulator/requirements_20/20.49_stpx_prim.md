# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structured Token & Pattern Extractor (STPX‑prm) is the **post‑SSG structural cue extractor** in Path‑A.  
Its role is to produce a **deterministic cue_envelope** containing:

- lexical surface cues  
- structural geometry cues  
- constraint cues  
- repair‑region markers  
- discourse‑context structural cues  

STPX operates strictly on **structural geometry**, not semantic geometry.  
It prepares a bounded, deterministic cue_envelope for consumption by:

- ISc (scoring)  
- RB (routing)  
- IdOB (identity‑conditioned refinement)  
- downstream routing primitives  

STPX performs no semantic interpretation, no meaning reconstruction, and no identity resolution.

---

# **2. Scope** *(Informative)*

This document defines:

- STPX inputs  
- STPX outputs  
- metadata boundaries  
- provenance obligations  
- deterministic cue extraction rules  
- cue_envelope schema  
- HLRs governing STPX behavior  

It does not define semantic interpretation, routing logic, identity refinement, or meaning‑layer behavior.

---

# **3. Position in Path‑A** *(Informative)*

STPX runs immediately after SSG‑prm in the canonical Path‑A pipeline defined in 20.15:

```
SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB → ISc → SSG → STPX → RBU → DCB → TR → CTP → ISc → RTU → RB → IdOB → MCB → (cycles) → OuBA
```

STPX consumes:

- structural geometry from OB‑Set  
- structural invariants from SSG  
- canonical tokens  
- structural‑adjacent metadata  

STPX writes only its cue_envelope into `TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata`.

---

# **4. Inputs** *(Informative)*

STPX consumes the following **read‑only** TP fields:

### **4.1 Structural Geometry**
- `TP.metadata.structural_metadata.*`  
- `TP.metadata.residue_metadata.*`  
- SmOB structural graph  
- constraint surfaces  
- segment boundaries  

### **4.2 SSG Output**
- `tp.ssg_signature`  
- `tp.ssg_layer_bitmap`  
- `tp.ssg_reason_code`  
- `tp.ssg_status`  

### **4.3 Canonical Tokens**
- normalized tokens from IE  
- expressive metadata  
- repair metadata  

### **4.4 Structural‑Adjacent Metadata**
- continuity_metadata  
- expressive_metadata  
- normalization_metadata  
- provenance_metadata  
- lineage_metadata  
- entropy/signature histories  

### **4.5 Forbidden Inputs**
STPX does not consume:

- semantic envelope  
- context envelope  
- routing_metadata  
- identity_metadata  
- truth/done fields  
- Pipeline‑B envelopes  

---

# **5. Outputs** *(Informative)*

STPX writes **only**:

```
TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata.stpx_cues
TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata.semantic_layer_provenance
```

No other TP fields are modified.

---

# **6. Metadata Boundaries** *(Informative)*

STPX treats all metadata as **read‑only**, except for its own envelope.  
STPX does not re‑derive upstream observations already encoded in metadata fields.  
STPX interprets metadata strictly as **structural‑adjacent features**.

---

# **7. Provenance Rules** *(Informative)*

STPX records provenance under:

```
TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata.semantic_layer_provenance
```

Provenance includes:

- origin primitive = STPX  
- last_update primitive = STPX  
- TPU commit identifier  
- full commit lineage  

Provenance is immutable after commit except for authorized extensions.

---

# **8. Cue Envelope Schema** *(Informative)*

The cue_envelope has deterministic structure:

```
CE = { L, S, C, R }
```

Where:

- **L** — lexical surface cues  
- **S** — structural cues (including discourse‑context cues)  
- **C** — constraint cues  
- **R** — repair‑region markers  

All categories are bounded, deterministic, and replay‑safe.

---

# **9. Deterministic Rules** *(Informative)*

Cue extraction is deterministic:

```
CE = Extract(G_struct, T_clean, M_struct_adjacent)
```

Where:

- `G_struct` = structural geometry  
- `T_clean` = canonical tokens  
- `M_struct_adjacent` = structural‑adjacent metadata  

Identical inputs produce identical cue_envelopes.

# **10. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

Below are the updated HLRs.  
Obsolete HLRs are marked **NA**.  
New HLRs begin at **HLR‑20.49‑021**.

---

## **10.1 Deterministic Cue Envelope**

### **HLR‑20.49‑001 — Deterministic Cue Envelope**  
STPX SHALL produce a deterministic cue_envelope for every TP.

### **HLR‑20.49‑009 — Replay‑Safe Cue Envelope**  
STPX cue_envelope SHALL be bounded and replay‑safe.

### **HLR‑20.49‑010 — Downstream Compatibility**  
STPX cue_envelope schema SHALL be deterministic and suitable for consumption by ISc, RB, IdOB, and downstream routing primitives.

---

## **10.2 Structural Geometry Input**

### **HLR‑20.49‑002 — Structural‑Only Operation**  
STPX SHALL operate only on cleaned structural geometry produced by SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, and SSG.

### **HLR‑20.49‑002A (NEW)**  
STPX SHALL consume `tp.ssg_signature`, `tp.ssg_layer_bitmap`, `tp.ssg_reason_code`, and `tp.ssg_status` as structural‑adjacent metadata.

### **HLR‑20.49‑002B (NEW)**  
STPX SHALL consume `TP.metadata.structural_metadata.*` and `TP.metadata.residue_metadata.*` as structural geometry inputs.

### **HLR‑20.49‑002C (NEW)**  
STPX SHALL NOT consume semantic, context, routing, identity, or truth‑evaluation envelopes.

---

## **10.3 Cue Categories**

### **HLR‑20.49‑003 — Lexical Surface Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit lexical surface cues extracted from canonical tokens.

### **HLR‑20.49‑004 — Structural Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit structural cues derived from structural geometry and invariant patterns.

### **HLR‑20.49‑005 — Constraint Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit constraint cues derived from constraint‑level residue and structural constraint metadata.

### **HLR‑20.49‑006 — Repair‑Region Markers**  
STPX SHALL emit repair‑region markers when present in upstream geometry.

---

## **10.4 Prohibited Behavior**

### **HLR‑20.49‑007 — Prohibited Meaning‑Layer Behavior**  
STPX SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, identity resolution, routing, entropy scoring, or meaning refinement.

### **HLR‑20.49‑008 — Prohibited TP Modification**  
STPX SHALL NOT modify TPU, meaning geometry, identity fields, routing fields, or truth‑evaluation fields.

---

## **10.5 Discourse‑Context Cue Handling**

### **HLR‑20.49‑011 — Discourse‑Context Cue Consumption**  
STPX SHALL consume normalized discourse‑context structural cues propagated from SmOB.

### **HLR‑20.49‑012 — Structural‑Only Interpretation**  
STPX SHALL treat discourse‑context cues strictly as structural geometry and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, stance inference, identity resolution, or meaning reconstruction.

### **HLR‑20.49‑013 — Deterministic Emission**  
STPX SHALL emit discourse‑context cues deterministically.

### **HLR‑20.49‑014 — Structural Cue Integration**  
STPX SHALL integrate discourse‑context cues into the structural cue category (S).

---

## **10.6 Metadata Consumption Rules**

### **HLR‑20.49‑015 — Applicable Metadata Consumption**  
STPX SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves cue extraction or cue_envelope formation.

### **HLR‑20.49‑016 — Read‑Only Discipline**  
STPX SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields except its own envelope.

### **HLR‑20.49‑017 — Metadata Applicability Rule**  
STPX SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when relevant to cue extraction or cue_envelope formation.

### **HLR‑20.49‑018 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**  
STPX SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, semantic ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields owned by Path‑B, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.49‑019 — Structural‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**  
STPX SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as structural‑adjacent features.

### **HLR‑20.49‑020 — Metadata Integration Rule**  
STPX SHALL integrate consumed metadata only when such metadata affects cue completeness, cue determinism, or replay safety.

---

## **10.7 New HLRs (Version 4.0)**

### **HLR‑20.49‑021 — Semantic Layer Metadata Write**  
STPX SHALL write its cue_envelope to `TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata.stpx_cues`.

### **HLR‑20.49‑022 — Provenance Write**  
STPX SHALL write provenance for its cue_envelope under `TP.metadata.semantic_layer_metadata.semantic_layer_provenance`.

### **HLR‑20.49‑023 — Provenance Determinism**  
STPX provenance SHALL be deterministic and SHALL support deterministic replay.

### **HLR‑20.49‑024 — Canonical TP Paths**  
STPX SHALL emit all metadata using canonical nested TP paths.

### **HLR‑20.49‑025 — No Upstream Re‑Derivation**  
STPX SHALL NOT re‑derive upstream observations already encoded in TP metadata fields.

### **HLR‑20.49‑026 — SSG Contract Compliance**  
STPX SHALL treat SSG outputs as authoritative structural‑adjacent metadata.
