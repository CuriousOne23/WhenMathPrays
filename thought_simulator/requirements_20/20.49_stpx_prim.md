# **20.49 — STPX Primitive (Structured Token & Pattern Extractor)**  
**Document ID:** 20.49  
**Version:** 2.0 (Structural Optimization Rewrite)  
**Status:** Normative — Path‑A Primitive  
**Parent:** 20.40 OB‑Requirements  
**Upstream:** SSG‑prm (20.47)  
**Downstream:** RBU‑prm, ISc (20.44), RB‑prm (20.50), IdOB  

---

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

The Structured Token & Pattern Extractor (STPX‑prm) is the **post‑SSG structural cue extractor** in Path‑A.  
Its role is to produce a **deterministic cue_envelope** containing:

- lexical surface cues  
- structural geometry cues  
- constraint cues  
- repair‑region markers  
- discourse‑context structural cues  

STPX operates **strictly on structural geometry**, not semantic geometry.  
It prepares a bounded, deterministic cue envelope for consumption by:

- ISc (scoring)  
- RB‑prm (routing)  
- IdOB (identity selection)  
- downstream routing primitives  

STPX performs **no semantic interpretation**, **no meaning reconstruction**, and **no identity resolution**.

---

# **2. Position in Path‑A** *(Informative)*

STPX runs immediately after SSG‑prm:

```
SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB → SSG → STPX → RBU → RB → IdOB
```

STPX consumes:

- cleaned structural geometry  
- canonical tokens  
- structural invariants  
- discourse‑context structural cues  

STPX does **not** read:

- semantic_core  
- FFTM meaning fields  
- TP.semantic  
- TP.context  
- TP.metadata  
- TP.intake  

STPX writes **only** the cue_envelope.

## **2.1 TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption (Informative)**

STPX‑prm may consume deterministic, bounded, pre‑semantic, semantic‑adjacent, and structural metadata present in the TP‑stream when such metadata is applicable to cue extraction or cue_envelope formation. Applicable metadata includes:

- residue_metadata (SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB)
- semantic_adjacent_metadata (SmOB)
- structural_metadata (SmOB structural graph, SSG invariants)
- continuity_metadata (COB, CIL, CST)
- expressive_metadata (IIInB, IE)
- normalization_metadata (IE)
- semantic_layer_metadata (SSG, STPX)
- provenance_metadata (TP.metadata.*)
- lineage_metadata (TP.lineage_log[])
- entropy and signature histories

STPX‑prm treats all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only.  
STPX‑prm ignores metadata that is not applicable to cue extraction or cue_envelope formation.  
STPX‑prm does not consume routing_metadata, semantic ΔH%, truth/done fields, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.  
STPX‑prm interprets TP‑stream metadata only as structural‑adjacent features.

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **3.1 Deterministic Cue Envelope**

### **HLR‑20.49‑001**  
STPX SHALL produce a deterministic cue_envelope for every TP.

### **HLR‑20.49‑009**  
STPX cue_envelope SHALL be bounded and replay‑safe.

### **HLR‑20.49‑010**  
STPX cue_envelope schema SHALL be deterministic and suitable for consumption by ISc, RB, IdOB, and downstream routing primitives.

---

## **3.2 Structural Geometry Input**

### **HLR‑20.49‑002**  
STPX SHALL operate only on cleaned structural geometry produced by:

```
SOB → SROB → CnOB → SmOB → SSG
```

STPX SHALL NOT read semantic geometry, meaning fields, identity fields, routing fields, or truth‑evaluation fields.

---

## **3.3 Cue Categories**

### **HLR‑20.49‑003 — Lexical Surface Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit lexical surface cues extracted from canonical tokens.

### **HLR‑20.49‑004 — Structural Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit structural cues derived from structural geometry and invariant patterns.

### **HLR‑20.49‑005 — Constraint Cues**  
STPX SHALL emit constraint cues derived from constraint‑level residue and structural constraint metadata.

### **HLR‑20.49‑006 — Repair‑Region Markers**  
STPX SHALL emit repair‑region markers when present in upstream geometry.

---

## **3.4 Prohibited Behavior**

### **HLR‑20.49‑007**  
STPX SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, identity resolution, routing, entropy scoring, or meaning refinement.

### **HLR‑20.49‑008**  
STPX SHALL NOT modify TPU, meaning geometry, identity fields, routing fields, or truth‑evaluation fields.

---

## **3.5 Discourse‑Context Cue Handling**

### **HLR‑20.49‑011 — Discourse‑Context Cue Consumption**  
STPX SHALL consume normalized discourse‑context structural cues propagated from SmOB, including:

- recent‑entity continuity  
- temporal‑shift markers  
- contrastive structural markers  
- causal structural markers  

STPX SHALL emit these cues as part of the structural cue set.

### **HLR‑20.49‑012 — Structural‑Only Interpretation**  
STPX SHALL treat discourse‑context cues strictly as structural geometry and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, stance inference, identity resolution, or meaning reconstruction.

### **HLR‑20.49‑013 — Deterministic Emission**  
STPX SHALL emit discourse‑context cues deterministically such that identical structural geometry and SmOB inputs produce identical cue_envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.49‑014 — Cue Envelope Integration**  
STPX SHALL integrate discourse‑context cues into the cue_envelope under the structural cue category ($S$), ensuring availability to ISc, RB, IdOB, and downstream routing primitives.

### **HLR‑20.49‑015 — TP‑Stream Metadata Consumption**
STPX‑prm SHALL consume applicable deterministic TP‑stream metadata when such metadata improves cue extraction or cue_envelope formation.

### **HLR‑20.49‑016 — Read‑Only Discipline**
STPX‑prm SHALL treat all TP‑stream metadata as read‑only and SHALL NOT modify any TP fields.

### **HLR‑20.49‑017 — Metadata Applicability Rule**
STPX‑prm SHALL consume TP‑stream metadata only when such metadata is relevant to cue extraction or cue_envelope formation and SHALL ignore metadata that is not applicable.

### **HLR‑20.49‑018 — Forbidden Metadata Consumption**
STPX‑prm SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, semantic ΔH%, truth/done fields, lineage fields owned by Path‑B, or any Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.49‑019 — Structural‑Adjacent Interpretation Only**
STPX‑prm SHALL treat all consumed TP‑stream metadata as structural‑adjacent features and SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, stance inference, identity resolution, or meaning reconstruction.

### **HLR‑20.49‑020 — Cue‑Layer Integration**
STPX‑prm SHALL integrate consumed TP‑stream metadata into cue extraction only when such metadata affects cue completeness, cue determinism, or replay safety.

# **4. Cue Envelope Schema (Normative)**

The cue_envelope has deterministic structure:

$$
CE = \\{ L, S, C, R \\}
$$

Where:

- $L$ — lexical surface cues  
- $S$ — structural cues (including discourse‑context cues)  
- $C$ — constraint cues  
- $R$ — repair‑region markers  

All categories SHALL be bounded, deterministic, and replay‑safe.

---

# **5. Mathematical Form (Normative)**

STPX SHALL compute the cue_envelope using:

$$
CE = \text{Extract}(G_{\text{struct}}, T_{\text{clean}})
$$

Where:

- $G_{\text{struct}}$ — structural geometry from SSG  
- $T_{\text{clean}}$ — canonical token set from upstream OB layers  

Extraction SHALL be deterministic.

---

# **6. Outputs (Normative)**

STPX SHALL output:

- cue_envelope.L — lexical cues  
- cue_envelope.S — structural cues  
- cue_envelope.C — constraint cues  
- cue_envelope.R — repair markers  

STPX SHALL NOT write to any TP field.

---
