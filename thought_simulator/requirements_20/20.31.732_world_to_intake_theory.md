## 1. Purpose

Path A already has structure → meaning.
730 already names world-talk intake as interface furniture.
731 already locks Seed `place()` and three gold utterances.

What was missing is the named mile *before* 730: how a human world-grouping becomes an Intake object without minting structure.

That missing mile is this paper.

Intake is the pre-structure object. Intake is not IdOB structure.

$$
u \mapsto \{F?,\, A?,\, H\}
$$

where $u$ is an utterance, $F?$ is a family candidate, $A?$ is an about candidate, and $H$ is hole-required.

$H = 1$ whenever $F?$ is unresolved, $A?$ is unresolved, line-kinds are mixed, or the utterance is not a Seed gold replay.

730 consumes that triple. 731 places it. 700 / 710 / `20.40.050` are not opened by this file.

## 2. Two observers

Humans group the world by domains (science, arts, politics, sports, family, nature, history, work, body). They encode those groups with grammar, dictionary, and language.

TS does not see domains. TS sees talk-shape and an about-anchor, or it sees a hole.

| Observer | Sees | Does not see |
|---|---|---|
| Human | domain groups, topics, stories, “what this is about” as meaning | talk-family inventory, Seed gold list, Hole Ledger |
| TS | line-kind geometry + about_id candidates + holes | science / arts / politics as ontology |

The front door is the translation between those two observers. It is non-semantic. It does not guess meaning. It does not invent a fourth gold.

CuriousOne23’s continuity claim, stated as a path splice rather than a new primitive:

```
world grouping          (human domains; observer A)
  → utterance           (grammar + dictionary; communication)
  → Intake              (this file)   { F?, A?, H }
  → 730 interface law
  → 731 Seed place()
  → hole | known card
  → reviewer / Door     (later)
  → IdOB structure      (20.40.050)
  → structure → meaning (existing IdOB bench)
```

The named object before structure is **Intake**.

$$
\text{Intake} \neq \text{structure}, \qquad A \neq F, \qquad \text{placement} \neq \text{proposal}
$$

## 3. About catalog as non-semantic grouping

`about_id` is an anchor, not a topic and not a meaning axis.

Seed About Index (normative allow-list for this revision; do not grow it here):

- `material_event_anchor`
- `timed_obligation_anchor`
- `person_load_anchor`

A world category may *suggest* one of those anchors as $A?$. It may never force an `about_id`. It may never mint a fourth about.

World words stay in the utterance column. They do not become:

- `structural_key`
- `$M$`
- family inventory
- six IDs
- meaning-axis names (physicality / sociality / temporality or any sibling)

If the utterance does not land on a Seed about, $A?$ is unresolved and $H = 1$.

## 4. Talk families as grammar-shaped line-kinds

`family_id` is a line-kind, not a speech-act ontology and not a six-ID.

Seed families (this revision):

- `event_talk`
- `state_talk`
- `task_talk`
- `claim_talk`
- `ask_talk`
- `update_talk`

Omitted from Seed: `relation_talk`. This file does not restore it.

Grammar cues (`If` / `But` / `And`, interrogative mark, imperative shape, copula + time word) emit $F?$ only. Two cues that pull two families in one line are not resolved by priority. They set $H = 1$.

A cue table is not a parser. 733 owns the cue table. This file only states the ethic: line-kind is geometry of the line, not meaning of the world.

## 5. Why TS refuses to guess (hole-first)

Seed `place()` recognizes three gold utterances. Everything else is unseen.

Guessing meaning to close an unseen line would write world words toward structure. That is the contamination 730 forbids.

Hole-first is therefore not a bug report. It is the mapping ethic:

$$
H = 1 \;\Longleftarrow\; (F?\ \text{unresolved}) \lor (A?\ \text{unresolved}) \lor (\text{mixed line-kinds}) \lor (u \notin \text{Seed gold})
$$

Gold 3 already teaches the pattern. Utterance `"I can't keep doing this."` may carry `person_load_anchor` and still must keep `family_id: null`, `card_id: null`, and an open hole. This file does not close that hole.

## 6. Failure modes → hole

| Failure | $F?$ | $A?$ | $H$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambiguous family (two cues) | unresolved | whatever | $1$ |
| Unknown anchor (world group not in Seed About) | whatever | unresolved | $1$ |
| Mixed line-kinds in one $u$ | unresolved | whatever | $1$ |
| Off-gold utterance under Seed lookup | candidate only | candidate only | $1$ |
| Gold 3 (U03) | unresolved | Seed about allowed | $1$ |

No failure mode in this table mints a family, an about, a card, or a six-ID.

## 7. Schematic examples (cap: 5)

These lines show observer split. They are not a catalog. They do not grow Seed.

**S1 — gold event (human: nature/material; TS: event + material anchor)**

- $u$ = `"The rock burst open."`
- Human read: a thing in the world changed.
- TS Intake: $F?=\texttt{event\_talk}$, $A?=\texttt{material\_event\_anchor}$, $H=0$ only because this $u$ is Gold 1.

**S2 — gold state (human: work/calendar; TS: state + timed-obligation anchor)**

- $u$ = `"The project deadline is Friday."`
- Human read: a scheduled condition.
- TS Intake: $F?=\texttt{state\_talk}$, $A?=\texttt{timed\_obligation\_anchor}$, $H=0$ only because this $u$ is Gold 2.

**S3 — gold hole (human: personal load; TS: about candidate + refused family)**

- $u$ = `"I can't keep doing this."`
- Human read: a person under load.
- TS Intake: $A?=\texttt{person\_load\_anchor}$ allowed, $F?$ unresolved, $H=1$.

**S4 — mixed cues (human: request plus condition; TS: hole)**

- $u$ = `"If the deadline is Friday, can we move it?"`
- Human read: calendar talk plus a question.
- TS Intake: `If` + interrogative pull two line-kinds. $H=1$. No family minted.

**S5 — world group outside Seed About (human: nature/sky; TS: hole)**

- $u$ = `"The sky is blue."`
- Human read: nature fact.
- TS Intake: state-shaped cue may exist; no Seed about accepts “sky.” $A?$ unresolved. $H=1$.
