## 3. `place()` contract

Contract shape:

`place(utterance) -> Placement Record` and zero or more Hole Ledger rows.

May write:
- about_id
- family_id
- optional pattern_id
- card_id only when a live card already exists
- hole rows

Must not write:
- structural_key
- Door Table
- $M$
- CIE
- new live cards

Replay requirement:
- same utterance -> same placement fields under test.
