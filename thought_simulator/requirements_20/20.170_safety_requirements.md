# 20.170 Safety Requirements

## Purpose
Defines deterministic safety constraints for fail-safe termination, bounded resource behavior, emergency control, and prohibited unsafe execution pathways across the TS cycle.

## Excludes: 
Truth evaluation and TPTB production (see 20.30.060_truth_completion.md and 20.64_tptb_prim.md).

## Parent Decomposition Scope
This module normatively decomposes safety obligations from 20.10 and 20.30, including deterministic fail-safe sequencing, safe-boundary enforcement, bounded resource utilization, and explicit auditability of all safety-relevant transitions.

This module normatively delegates TP/MTP‑level safety metadata representation to TPSF (20.66), which Path A SHALL populate during safety evaluation.

## **HLR‑20.170‑018 - Safety Metadata (TPSF)**
Safety evaluation produces a structured safety‑metadata object (TPSF) as defined in 20.66_tpsf_prim.md. TPSF encodes the evaluated safety state, boundary conditions, reason codes, and deterministic safety outcomes. TPSF is attached to the TP/MTP as a read‑only metadata structure and SHALL NOT be modified by downstream components (merge, TPU, RG) or upstream components (TB, OB, routing).

TPSF is the only safety‑metadata structure produced by safety evaluation.

## Normative Requirements
1. HLR-20.170-001: TS SHALL provide deterministic fail-safe termination sequencing.
2. HLR-20.170-002: Safety policy SHALL enforce bounded resource utilization across all bounded TS subsystems.
3. HLR-20.170-003: Safety-relevant state transitions SHALL be fully logged in append-only, time-indexed records.
4. HLR-20.170-004: Unsafe nondeterministic pathways SHALL be prohibited in core execution.
5. HLR-20.170-005: Emergency overrides SHALL be forbidden unless explicitly signature-bound, safe-boundary-activated, and audited with fixed reason codes.
6. HLR-20.170-006: Safety-class fail-open/fail-closed behavior SHALL remain deterministic and policy-governed.
7. HLR-20.170-007: Safety enforcement SHALL occur only at deterministic safe boundaries and SHALL NOT interleave with unsafeguarded TS state changes.
8. HLR-20.170-008: Safety policy SHALL be explicit, versioned, and reproducible for identical effective state and profile state.
9. HLR-20.170-009: Unsafe execution pathways SHALL be blocked before they can mutate OB, RB, TB, IB, InB, OuB, CIL, COB, COP, MTP, or TP state.
10. HLR-20.170-010: Safety events SHALL emit deterministic audit records containing reason codes, policy signatures, affected subsystems, and boundary markers.
11. HLR-20.170-011: Safety monitoring SHALL be bounded and SHALL not rely on probabilistic inference, unbounded scanning, or latent-state interpretation.
12. HLR-20.170-012: Safety controls SHALL preserve FIFO and tick ordering semantics and SHALL use deterministic sequence/token state as the ordering authority.
13. HLR-20.170-013: Safety-relevant overrun, degradation, deny, or shutdown behavior SHALL be deterministic and replayable for identical effective state.
14. HLR-20.170-014: Safety profile updates and calibration changes SHALL require explicit re-verification before release.
15. HLR-20.170-015: Safety requirements in this module SHALL remain platform-independent and SHALL not depend on hardware-, OS-, or runtime-specific nondeterministic effects.
16. HLR-20.170-016: Safety requirements in this module SHALL satisfy and SHALL NOT weaken parent invariants defined by 20.10 and 20.30.
17. HLR-20.170-017: Safety evaluation SHALL produce TPSF metadata as defined in 20.66_tpsf_prim.md and SHALL attach it to the TP/MTP as a read‑only structure for all downstream components.

## Functional Boundaries
- Safety owns deterministic gating, blocking, termination, and emergency control policy enforcement.
- Subsystems SHALL remain within safety-defined envelopes and SHALL not bypass safety controls.
- GB MAY consume safety telemetry for supervisory decisions but SHALL not mutate safety semantics directly.

## Verification Focus
- Deterministic fail-safe termination for identical effective state.
- Deterministic block/deny behavior for unsafe pathways at safe boundaries.
- Deterministic append-only audit of all safety-relevant transitions and emergency actions.
- Deterministic bounded-resource enforcement with versioned policy behavior.
