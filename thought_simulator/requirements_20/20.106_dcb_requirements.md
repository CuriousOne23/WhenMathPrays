### **20.106 — Directional Conversation Basin (DCB‑prm)**  
**Document ID:** 20.106  
**Version:** 4.0 (Path‑A Loop Alignment + Geometric Overlay Update)  
**Status:** Normative — Geometric Meta‑Basin  
**Scope:** Ephemeral geometric observer for TR consumption only  
**Parent:** 20.15 Path‑A Pipeline  

---

## 0. Informative overview (no SHALLs)

The Directional Conversation Basin (DCB‑prm) is a **geometric meta‑basin** in **Path‑A’s geometric overlay**.  
It observes **trajectory geometry** of Thought Packets (TPs) and emits **ephemeral directional‑change events** for consumption by the Thought Router (TR).

DCB:

- provides **geometric routing hints**, not semantic meaning  
- performs no OB extraction, no RB routing, no TR derivation, no Path‑B interpretation  
- produces no TP writes, no persistent fields, no semantic content  
- emits events that are ephemeral, bounded, non‑recursive, and recomputable  

DCB’s influence occurs only through TR, and only when TR’s gating conditions are satisfied.

---

## 1. Purpose (informative)

DCB’s purpose is to:

- observe geometric trajectory state from `TP.metadata.geometric_state.*`  
- detect curvature and directional deviation in Path‑A’s geometric overlay  
- emit ephemeral directional‑change events for TR  
- support routing refresh without altering TP or semantic_core  
- preserve Path‑A determinism and replay equivalence  

DCB does not interpret meaning, stance, identity, or semantics, and does not participate in Path‑B.

---

## 2. Position in Path‑A (informative)

In the updated Path‑A loop (20.15, tp_path_a_map.md v4.0), DCB appears **after STPX and RBU** and **before TR** in each identity‑conditioned cycle:

```text
… → SSG → STPX → RBU → DCB → TR → CTP → ISc → RTU → RB → IdOB → MCB → RBU → DCB → TR → …
```

DCB:

- runs in every identity‑conditioned cycle where geometric overlay is active  
- does not run only once per turn  
- never modifies TP; it only observes geometric state and emits ephemeral events for TR.

---

## 3. Non‑goals (informative)

DCB does not:

- interpret semantics or stance  
- extract OB evidence or structural cues  
- derive `TP.TR` or routing vectors  
- classify messy input or apply semantic thresholds  
- persist events on TP or semantic_core  
- interact with Path‑B, truth‑to‑belief, or safety fields.

---

## 4. Dual‑pipeline boundary discipline (normative)

### **HLR‑20.106‑001 — Geometric meta‑basin classification**  
DCB SHALL be classified as a geometric meta‑basin and SHALL NOT be classified as a semantic, stance, or identity basin.

### **HLR‑20.106‑002 — No semantic interpretation**  
DCB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction, semantic interpretation, stance inference, or OB‑class evidence extraction.

### **HLR‑20.106‑003 — No OB authority overlap**  
DCB SHALL NOT overlap OB‑family authority boundaries and SHALL NOT read OB residues or structural cue envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.106‑013 — No TP writes**  
DCB SHALL NOT write to any TP field, semantic_core, metadata, routing fields, or truth‑evaluation fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑014 — Ephemeral events only**  
DCB directional‑change events SHALL be ephemeral and SHALL NOT persist on TP or semantic_core.

### **HLR‑20.106‑020 — No TR dirty‑flag mutation**  
DCB SHALL NOT set or modify any TR dirty‑flag (including `tr_needs_update`).

### **HLR‑20.106‑022 — Pipeline‑A only**  
DCB SHALL operate only in Path‑A’s geometric overlay and SHALL NOT read execution envelopes or Path‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.106‑030 — Pipeline‑B forbidden**  
Pipeline‑B modules SHALL NOT inject, modify, or consume DCB events.

---

## 4A. Numeric‑policy alignment (normative)

### **HLR‑20.106‑038 — Numeric‑policy compliance**  
DCB SHALL treat curvature, deviation, and geometric invariants as numeric‑policy‑governed values defined in 20.95.

### **HLR‑20.106‑039 — No threshold definition**  
DCB SHALL NOT define numeric thresholds; invariant thresholds SHALL be derived exclusively from `policy_signature`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑040 — Replay‑safe numeric behavior**  
DCB SHALL ensure numeric‑policy compliance during strip replay and deterministic recomputation.

---

## 5. Trajectory geometry (normative)

DCB observes only **persisted geometric state** in the TP metadata.

### **HLR‑20.106‑004 — Position**  
DCB SHALL depend on TP position as a geometric coordinate in trajectory space, read from `TP.metadata.geometric_state.position`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑005 — Direction**  
DCB SHALL depend on the geometric tangent of the TP trajectory, read from `TP.metadata.geometric_state.direction`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑006 — Curvature**  
DCB SHALL depend on curvature as the rate of change of the direction vector, read from `TP.metadata.geometric_state.curvature`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑007 — Multi‑step observation**  
DCB SHALL use consecutive persisted TP instances and MAY use permitted MTP trajectory‑projection fields when defined by 20.95.

### **HLR‑20.106‑008 — Implementation‑agnostic geometry**  
DCB SHALL NOT prescribe numeric encoding policy; numeric encoding SHALL follow 20.95 and `policy_signature`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑025 — Approved read set**  
DCB SHALL read only:

- `TP.metadata.geometric_state.position`  
- `TP.metadata.geometric_state.direction`  
- `TP.metadata.geometric_state.curvature`  
- `TP.metadata.geometric_state.step_index`  
- `TP.metadata.geometric_state.lane_id`  
- permitted MTP trajectory‑projection fields  
- TP identifiers required for event correlation (`tp_id`, `cycle_id`)

### **HLR‑20.106‑035 — Geometry‑only dependency**  
DCB SHALL NOT read any meaning‑layer, identity‑layer, or execution‑layer fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑037 — Forbidden reads**  
DCB SHALL NOT read:

- propositions  
- messy_input_record  
- thought_router_fields  
- routing_metadata  
- truth_hypotheses  
- semantic_core  
- Path‑B envelopes  

Any such read SHALL be a conformance failure.

### **HLR‑20.106‑048 — Upstream ordering compliance**  
DCB SHALL execute after STPX and RBU in the Path‑A loop and SHALL NOT execute before meaning‑side commit.

### **HLR‑20.106‑049 — STPX cue prohibition**  
DCB SHALL NOT read any STPX cue_envelope fields (lexical, structural, constraint, or repair cues).

### **HLR‑20.106‑050 — Meaning‑side commit prohibition**  
DCB SHALL NOT read any meaning‑side commit fields produced by RBU, including identity, stance, register, tone, semantic tags, or lineage markers.

---

## 6. Directional‑change events (normative)

### 6.1 Emission rules

### **HLR‑20.106‑009 — Cycle observation**  
DCB SHALL observe trajectory geometry within the deterministic Path‑A cycle defined in 20.15.

### **HLR‑20.106‑010 — Invariant‑bound emission**  
DCB SHALL emit directional‑change events when curvature or directional deviation exceeds a bounded geometric invariant defined by numeric policy.

### **HLR‑20.106‑011 — Geometric‑only events**  
DCB events SHALL encode geometric signals only and SHALL NOT include semantic labels, stance, or identity content.

### **HLR‑20.106‑036 — Finite emission**  
DCB SHALL NOT emit an unbounded number of events per TP per cycle; emission SHALL be finite and bounded.

---

### 6.2 Event schema (normative)

DCB SHALL emit events with at least the following schema:

```text
directional_change_event {
  event_id,
  tp_id,
  cycle_id,
  step_index,
  deviation_class,   // CURVATURE | DIRECTION
  invariant_id,
  tangent_ref{},
  curvature_ref{},
  ephemeral: true
}
```

### **HLR‑20.106‑028 — Deterministic ordering**  
Off‑TP diagnostic batches SHALL sort events by `(cycle_id, step_index, event_id)`.

---

### 6.3 Consumption rules

### **HLR‑20.106‑012 — Ordering**  
DCB SHALL execute after STPX and RBU and before TR in the same Path‑A cycle.

### **HLR‑20.106‑015 — RB forbidden**  
RB SHALL NOT consume DCB events directly.

### **HLR‑20.106‑016 — TR‑gated consumption**  
TR SHALL consume DCB events only when:

- TR gating indicates a routing refresh is required, and  
- RB routes the TP to TR in the current cycle.

### **HLR‑20.106‑017 — Bounded feedback**  
DCB SHALL be a bounded feedback element; its influence SHALL occur only through TR and SHALL remain non‑expansive.

### **HLR‑20.106‑018 — Non‑recursive feedback**  
DCB feedback SHALL be bounded, non‑recursive, and SHALL NOT cause unbounded routing loops.

### **HLR‑20.106‑019 — No self‑modification**  
DCB SHALL NOT modify its own geometric input state or any persisted geometric fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑027 — Strip replay**  
Strip replay SHALL recompute DCB events from trajectory geometry and permitted MTP fields; no event persistence SHALL be required.

---

## 6A. TR interface contract (normative)

### **HLR‑20.106‑041 — Ephemeral event channel**  
DCB SHALL expose directional‑change events to TR only through an ephemeral event channel.

### **HLR‑20.106‑042 — RB and OB isolation**  
DCB SHALL NOT expose events to RB, OB‑family primitives, or any Pipeline‑B module.

### **HLR‑20.106‑043 — TR semantic isolation**  
TR SHALL treat DCB events strictly as geometric hints and SHALL NOT interpret them semantically.

### **HLR‑20.106‑044 — Stable ordering before consumption**  
DCB SHALL ensure event ordering is stable and deterministic before TR consumption.

### **HLR‑20.106‑051 — Testbench output discipline**  
DCB SHALL expose its directional‑change event list as the sole output for progressive lineup testing and SHALL NOT expose TP‑mutating outputs to the testbench.

---

## 7. Determinism and replay (normative)

### **HLR‑20.106‑021 — Recomputability**  
DCB events SHALL be recomputable from persisted trajectory geometry and permitted MTP fields without additional hidden state.

### **HLR‑20.106‑024 — Seed‑free emission**  
Event emission SHALL depend only on `(trajectory state, policy_signature)`; random seeds SHALL NOT influence event content or ordering.

### **HLR‑20.106‑029 — Allocation independence**  
Event emission SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order, container identity, or execution timing.

---

## 7A. Replay reconstruction contract (normative)

### **HLR‑20.106‑045 — Full reconstruction**  
DCB SHALL ensure all directional‑change events are fully reconstructable from persisted geometric state and `policy_signature` under replay.

### **HLR‑20.106‑046 — Replay equivalence**  
DCB SHALL ensure identical persisted geometry yields identical event sets under 20.206 and 20.207 replay rules.

### **HLR‑20.106‑047 — Replay independence**  
DCB SHALL ensure replay independence from TP allocation order, container identity, or execution timing.

---

## 8. Functional boundaries (normative)

DCB SHALL:

- observe geometric trajectory only  
- emit ephemeral geometric events only  
- never modify TP  
- never modify semantic_core  
- never modify routing fields  
- never modify truth‑evaluation fields  
- never modify messy‑input fields  
- never interact with `TP.next_context{}` or `TP.semantic.*`  

TR is the sole consumer of DCB events.  
RB SHALL NOT consume DCB events.

---

## 9. Conformance (normative)

Implementation conforms when:

- DCB is geometric‑only and non‑semantic  
- events are ephemeral, bounded, and recomputable  
- no TP writes occur  
- no forbidden reads occur  
- TR gating is honored  
- RB routing is unaffected without TR refresh  
- event emission is finite and bounded  
- seed changes do not alter events  
- strip replay recomputes identical events  
- numeric‑policy compliance is maintained  
- STPX cues and RBU meaning‑side fields are never read.

---

## 10. Verification focus (informative)

Verification checks:

- identical geometric state → identical event sets  
- TR gating disabled → events ignored without routing change  
- RB routing unchanged when TR does not consume events  
- forbidden reads rejected by rulechecker  
- bounded event emission per TP per cycle  
- numeric‑policy compliance under 20.95  
- replay equivalence under 20.206/20.207  
- DCB‑disabled policy matches a no‑event baseline for routing behavior.
