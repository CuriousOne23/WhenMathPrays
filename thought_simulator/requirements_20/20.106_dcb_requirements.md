# **20.106 — Directional Conversation Basin (DCB‑prm)**  
**Document ID:** 20.106  
**Version:** 2.1 (Geometric‑Overlay Optimization + Replay Alignment)  
**Status:** Normative — Geometric Meta‑Basin  
**Scope:** Ephemeral geometric observer for TR consumption only  

---

# **0. Informative Overview (No SHALLs)**

The Directional Conversation Basin (DCB‑prm) is a **geometric meta‑basin** in Pipeline A.  
It observes **trajectory geometry** of Thought Particles (TPs) and emits **ephemeral directional‑change events** for consumption by the Thought Router (TR).

DCB:

- provides **geometric routing hints**, not semantic meaning  
- performs **no OB extraction**, **no RB routing**, **no TR derivation**, **no TB interpretation**  
- produces **no TP writes**, **no persistent fields**, **no semantic content**  
- emits events that are **ephemeral**, **bounded**, **non‑recursive**, and **recomputable**  

DCB is a **non‑expansive geometric feedback element**.  
Its influence occurs **only through TR**, and only when **TR gating** is active.

---

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

DCB’s purpose is to:

- observe geometric trajectory state  
- detect curvature and directional deviation  
- emit ephemeral directional‑change events  
- provide TR with geometric hints for routing refresh  
- preserve Pipeline‑A determinism and replay equivalence  

DCB does **not** interpret meaning, stance, identity, or semantics.

---

# **2. Parent Scope** *(Informative)*

DCB belongs exclusively to the **Pipeline A geometric overlay**.

It:

- SHALL NOT read `exec_plan` or `exec_trace`  
- SHALL NOT modify TP or semantic_core  
- SHALL NOT interact with Pipeline‑B  
- SHALL NOT influence routing except indirectly through TR consumption  

DCB is strictly **geometric**, **bounded**, and **non‑semantic**.

---

# **3. Non‑Goals** *(Informative)*

DCB does **not**:

- interpret semantics  
- extract OB evidence  
- derive `TP.TR`  
- classify messy input  
- apply numeric thresholds directly  
- persist events on TP or semantic_core  

---

# **4. Dual‑Pipeline Boundary Discipline (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.106‑001 — Geometric Meta‑Basin Classification**  
DCB SHALL be classified as a geometric meta‑basin and SHALL NOT be classified as a semantic or stance basin.

### **HLR‑20.106‑002 — No Semantic Interpretation**  
DCB SHALL NOT perform meaning construction, semantic interpretation, or OB‑class evidence extraction.

### **HLR‑20.106‑003 — No OB Authority Overlap**  
DCB SHALL NOT overlap OB authority boundaries.

### **HLR‑20.106‑013 — No TP Writes**  
DCB SHALL NOT write to any TP field, semantic_core, metadata, routing fields, or truth‑evaluation fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑014 — Ephemeral Events Only**  
DCB directional‑change events SHALL be ephemeral and SHALL NOT persist on TP.

### **HLR‑20.106‑020 — No TR Dirty‑Flag Mutation**  
DCB SHALL NOT set `tr_needs_update`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑022 — Pipeline‑A Only**  
DCB SHALL operate only in Pipeline A and SHALL NOT read execution envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.106‑030 — Pipeline‑B Forbidden**  
Pipeline‑B modules SHALL NOT inject, modify, or consume DCB events.

---

# **4A. Numeric‑Policy Alignment (Normative)**  
*(New Section)*

### **HLR‑20.106‑038 — Numeric‑Policy Compliance**  
DCB SHALL treat curvature, deviation, and geometric invariants as numeric‑policy‑governed values defined in 20.95.

### **HLR‑20.106‑039 — No Threshold Definition**  
DCB SHALL NOT define numeric thresholds; invariant thresholds SHALL be derived exclusively from `policy_signature`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑040 — Replay‑Safe Numeric Behavior**  
DCB SHALL ensure numeric‑policy compliance during strip replay and deterministic recomputation.

---

# **5. Trajectory Geometry (Normative)**

DCB observes only **persisted geometric state**.

### **HLR‑20.106‑004 — Position**  
DCB SHALL depend on TP position as a geometric coordinate in trajectory space.

### **HLR‑20.106‑005 — Direction**  
DCB SHALL depend on the geometric tangent of the TP trajectory.

### **HLR‑20.106‑006 — Curvature**  
DCB SHALL depend on curvature as the rate of change of the direction vector.

### **HLR‑20.106‑007 — Multi‑Step Observation**  
DCB SHALL use consecutive persisted TP instances and MAY use permitted MTP trajectory‑projection fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑008 — Implementation‑Agnostic Geometry**  
DCB SHALL NOT prescribe numeric policy; numeric encoding SHALL follow 20.95.

### **HLR‑20.106‑025 — Approved Read Set**  
DCB SHALL read only:

- TP trajectory coordinates  
- direction vectors  
- step indices  
- `tp_id`  
- `lane_id`  
- permitted MTP trajectory‑projection fields  

### **HLR‑20.106‑035 — Geometry‑Only Dependency**  
DCB SHALL NOT read any meaning‑layer or execution‑layer fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑037 — Forbidden Reads**  
DCB SHALL NOT read:

- propositions  
- messy_input_record  
- thought_router_fields  
- routing_metadata  
- truth_hypotheses  
- semantic_core  

Any such read SHALL be a conformance failure.

---

# **6. Directional‑Change Events (Normative)**

## **6.1 Emission Rules**

### **HLR‑20.106‑009 — Cycle Observation**  
DCB SHALL observe trajectory geometry within the deterministic TS cycle.

### **HLR‑20.106‑010 — Invariant‑Bound Emission**  
DCB SHALL emit directional‑change events when curvature or directional deviation exceeds a bounded geometric invariant.

### **HLR‑20.106‑011 — Geometric‑Only Events**  
Events SHALL encode geometric signals only — no semantic labels or stance.

### **HLR‑20.106‑036 — Finite Emission**  
DCB SHALL NOT emit an unbounded number of events per TP per cycle.

---

## **6.2 Event Schema (Normative)**

DCB SHALL emit events with the following minimum schema:

```
directional_change_event {
  event_id,
  tp_id,
  cycle_id,
  step_index,
  deviation_class,     // CURVATURE | DIRECTION
  invariant_id,
  tangent_ref{},
  curvature_ref{},
  ephemeral: true
}
```

### **HLR‑20.106‑028 — Deterministic Ordering**  
Off‑TP diagnostic batches SHALL sort events by `(cycle_id, step_index, event_id)`.

---

## **6.3 Consumption Rules**

### **HLR‑20.106‑012 — Ordering**  
DCB SHALL execute after OB and before TR in the same cycle.

### **HLR‑20.106‑015 — RB Forbidden**  
RB SHALL NOT consume DCB events directly.

### **HLR‑20.106‑016 — TR‑Gated Consumption**  
TR SHALL consume DCB events only when:

- `tr_needs_update = true`, and  
- RB routes the TP to TR  

### **HLR‑20.106‑017 — Bounded Feedback**  
DCB SHALL be a bounded feedback element; influence occurs only through TR.

### **HLR‑20.106‑018 — Non‑Recursive Feedback**  
DCB feedback SHALL be bounded, non‑recursive, and non‑expansive.

### **HLR‑20.106‑019 — No Self‑Modification**  
DCB SHALL NOT modify its own geometric input state.

### **HLR‑20.106‑027 — Strip Replay**  
Strip replay SHALL recompute events from trajectory geometry; no persistence required.

---

# **6A. TR Interface Contract (Normative)**  
*(New Section)*

### **HLR‑20.106‑041 — Ephemeral Event Channel**  
DCB SHALL expose directional‑change events to TR only through the ephemeral event channel.

### **HLR‑20.106‑042 — RB and OB Isolation**  
DCB SHALL NOT expose events to RB, OB‑family primitives, or Pipeline‑B.

### **HLR‑20.106‑043 — TR Semantic Isolation**  
TR SHALL treat DCB events strictly as geometric hints and SHALL NOT interpret them semantically.

### **HLR‑20.106‑044 — Stable Ordering Before Consumption**  
DCB SHALL ensure event ordering is stable before TR consumption.

---

# **7. Determinism & Replay (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.106‑021 — Recomputability**  
Events SHALL be recomputable from persisted trajectory geometry and permitted MTP fields.

### **HLR‑20.106‑024 — Seed‑Free Emission**  
Event emission SHALL depend only on `(trajectory state, policy_signature)`; seed SHALL NOT influence events.

### **HLR‑20.106‑029 — Allocation Independence**  
Event emission SHALL NOT depend on TP creation order or container identity.

---

# **7A. Replay Reconstruction Contract (Normative)**  
*(New Section)*

### **HLR‑20.106‑045 — Full Reconstruction**  
DCB SHALL ensure all directional‑change events are fully reconstructable from persisted geometric state and `policy_signature`.

### **HLR‑20.106‑046 — Replay Equivalence**  
DCB SHALL ensure identical persisted geometry yields identical event sets under 20.206 and 20.207 replay rules.

### **HLR‑20.106‑047 — Replay Independence**  
DCB SHALL ensure replay independence from TP allocation order, container identity, or execution timing.

---

# **8. Functional Boundaries (Normative)**

DCB SHALL:

- observe geometric trajectory  
- emit ephemeral geometric events  
- never modify TP  
- never modify semantic_core  
- never modify routing fields  
- never modify truth‑evaluation fields  
- never modify messy‑input fields  
- never interact with TP.next_context{} or TP.semantic.*  

TR is the **sole consumer** of DCB events.  
RB SHALL NOT consume DCB events.

---

# **9. Conformance (Normative)**

Implementation conforms when:

- DCB is geometric‑only  
- events are ephemeral and recomputable  
- no TP writes occur  
- no forbidden reads occur  
- TR gating is honored  
- RB routing is unaffected without TR refresh  
- event emission is finite and bounded  
- seed changes do not alter events  
- strip replay recomputes identical events  
- numeric‑policy compliance is maintained  

---

# **10. Verification Focus (Informative)**

Verification checks:

- identical trajectory → identical events  
- `tr_needs_update = false` → events discarded  
- RB routing unchanged without TR refresh  
- forbidden reads rejected  
- bounded event emission  
- numeric‑policy compliance  
- replay equivalence under 20.206/20.207  
- DCB‑disabled policy matches no‑event baseline
