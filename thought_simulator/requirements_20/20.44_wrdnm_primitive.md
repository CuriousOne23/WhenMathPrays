# **20.44 — WrdNm Primitive (Word‑to‑Numeric Encoder)**  
**Document ID:** 20.44  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Normative  
**Parent:** tp_architecture.md  
**Scope:** Deterministic conversion of TP structured fields into numeric feature vectors for ISc  
**Purpose:** Define mandatory behavior, invariants, owned‑field writes, and deterministic mapping rules for the WrdNm primitive  
**Aligned with:** 20.15, 20.40 family, 20.45 (ISc), tp_path_a_map.md, tp_context_layer.md  

---

# **1. Purpose** *(Informative)*

WrdNm is the **Word‑to‑Numeric Encoder** in Path A.  
It converts **structured TP fields** produced by upstream primitives into **numeric feature vectors** required by ISc.  
WrdNm performs **bounded, deterministic, schema‑driven mapping** using small, fixed dictionaries and scalar tables.  
WrdNm does not perform semantic interpretation, truth evaluation, stance assignment, or any meaning‑layer work.

WrdNm is **position‑agnostic**: it does not depend on where a field was produced in Path A.  
It converts fields solely based on a **fixed schema** and **bounded vocabularies**.

---

# **2. Functional Overview** *(Informative)*

WrdNm:

- reads TP structured fields from all upstream primitives  
- applies deterministic categorical‑to‑ID, boolean‑to‑0/1, scalar‑to‑float, and hash‑based mappings  
- produces a numeric feature vector for each candidate  
- writes only WrdNm‑owned fields  
- never overwrites earlier TP content  
- never mutates upstream fields  
- ensures replay‑safe numeric encoding for ISc scoring  

WrdNm is a **pure encoder**, not a scoring or semantic primitive.

---

# **3. Normative Requirements (HLRs)**

---

## **3.1 WrdNm Definition**

### **HLR‑20.44‑001**  
WrdNm SHALL convert structured TP fields into numeric feature values using deterministic, bounded mappings defined in the WrdNm schema.

### **HLR‑20.44‑002**  
WrdNm SHALL remain position‑agnostic and SHALL NOT depend on the order or frequency of upstream primitive invocation.

### **HLR‑20.44‑003**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT perform semantic interpretation, meaning resolution, truth evaluation, stance assignment, or referent identity resolution.

---

## **3.2 Inputs**

### **HLR‑20.44‑004**  
WrdNm SHALL accept structured TP fields produced by IIInB, IE, CEx, CE, TPU, SOB, SROB, CnOB, SmOB, SSG, STPX, RBU, DCB, RB, TR, CTP, IdOB, and MCB.

### **HLR‑20.44‑005**  
WrdNm SHALL treat all upstream TP fields as read‑only.

### **HLR‑20.44‑006**  
WrdNm SHALL convert only fields listed in the WrdNm schema and SHALL ignore fields not present in the schema.

---

## **3.3 Deterministic Mapping Rules**

### **HLR‑20.44‑007**   
WrdNm SHALL convert categorical fields to float32 numeric IDs (fractional precision ≤ 1/1000) using bounded, deterministic dictionaries.

### **HLR‑20.44‑008**  
WrdNm SHALL convert boolean fields to numeric values 0 or 1.

### **HLR‑20.44‑009**  
WrdNm SHALL convert scalar fields to numeric floats using bounded scalar tables.

### **HLR‑20.44‑010**  
WrdNm SHALL convert long structured fields (e.g., summaries, histories) using deterministic, replay‑safe hash functions defined in WrdNm hashing rules.

### **HLR‑20.44‑011**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure identical TP inputs produce identical numeric outputs.

---

## **3.4 Schema Discipline**

### **HLR‑20.44‑012**  
WrdNm SHALL use a fixed schema file defining all fields eligible for numeric conversion and their mapping types.

### **HLR‑20.44‑013**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT infer new fields, SHALL NOT scan TP text, and SHALL NOT perform free‑form tokenization.

### **HLR‑20.44‑014**  
WrdNm SHALL convert each field only if the field exists in the TP and is listed in the schema.

---

## **3.5 Owned‑Field Write Discipline**

### **HLR‑20.44‑015**  
WrdNm SHALL write only WrdNm‑owned fields, including numeric feature vectors and WrdNm diagnostic records.

### **HLR‑20.44‑016**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT overwrite its own earlier records when invoked multiple times in Path A; each invocation SHALL append a new WrdNm record.

### **HLR‑20.44‑017**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT modify upstream fields or meaning‑layer fields.

### **HLR-20.44-031**
WrdNm SHALL write its outputs into the TP envelope `TP.wrdnm[]`.

---

## **3.6 Output Requirements**

### **HLR‑20.44‑018**  
WrdNm SHALL output a numeric feature vector containing all converted fields defined in the schema. Categorical fields SHALL be represented as float32 values with fractional precision ≤ 1/1000.

### **HLR‑20.44‑019**  
WrdNm output SHALL be valid input for ISc and SHALL remain stable across replay.

### **HLR‑20.44‑020**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure numeric feature vectors are complete for all fields present in the TP and listed in the schema.

---

## **3.7 Determinism and Replay Safety**

### **HLR‑20.44‑021**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure all dictionary lookups, scalar mappings, and hash functions are deterministic and replay‑safe.

### **HLR‑20.44‑022**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure numeric encoding remains invariant under repeated Path‑A cycles.

---

## **3.8 Forbidden Behavior**

### **HLR‑20.44‑023**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT perform semantic smoothing, semantic reconstruction, or generative filling of missing content.

### **HLR‑20.44‑024**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT consume routing_metadata, truth/done fields, or Pipeline‑B envelopes.

### **HLR‑20.44‑025**  
WrdNm SHALL NOT modify TP text or meaning‑layer propositions.

---

# **4. Mathematical Requirements (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.44‑026**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure each categorical field value $c_i$ maps to a numeric ID $n_i$ using:

$$
n_i = \mathrm{dict}(c_i)
$$

### **HLR‑20.44‑027**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure each scalar field value $s_i$ maps to a numeric float $f_i$ using:

$$
f_i = \mathrm{scalar\_map}(s_i)
$$

where $n_i$ SHALL be a float32 categorical ID with fractional precision ≤ 1/1000.

### **HLR‑20.44‑028**  
WrdNm SHALL ensure each hashed field $h_i$ maps to a deterministic hash value $H_i$:

$$
H_i = \mathrm{hash}(h_i)
$$

---

# **5. Outputs (Normative)**

### **HLR‑20.44‑029**  
WrdNm SHALL output:

- categorical IDs (float32, fractional precision ≤ 1/1000) 
- boolean numeric values  
- scalar numeric values  
- deterministic hash values  
- WrdNm feature vector  
- WrdNm diagnostic record  

### **HLR‑20.44‑030**  
WrdNm output SHALL be consumed by ISc as its numeric feature input.
