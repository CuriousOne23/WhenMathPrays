# 00.00.42 Document Addressing and Insertion Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the general policy for document addressing and controlled growth across all tiers of the Thought Simulator documentation tree.

The objective is to enable the addition of new documents when existing numeric prefixes become full or would result in unreadable or sparse numbering, without requiring renumbering of legacy items. This approach promotes stability, traceability, and independence of tiers where appropriate.

## 2. Tier Coupling and Independence

The following coupling rules govern numbering authority:

- **Coupled group**: Only the 30 (verification), 10.50 (requirements anchors), and 50 (design) tiers are tightly coupled. Numbering bands and prefixes in 30 and 10.50 derive from the 50 design tier. Cross-references and promotion paths between these three must remain aligned.

- **Independent tiers**:
  - 20 (requirements): Maintains its own legacy bands (`20.xxx_*`) and uses the subfield insertion mechanism described below for expansion. It does not drive or follow numbering from 40 or the 30/10.50/50 group.
  - 40 (playground): Fully independent for its internal module structure, program rows (e.g., `40.510-NNN` in the refactor program), and glue paths. 40 numbering does not dictate bands or prefixes for 20, 30, 10.50, or 50.

- 00 (governance) serves as the overarching registry and policy authority but does not impose numbering on the independent tiers.

These rules ensure that 40 and 20 remain self-governing while the verification/design promotion rail (30/10.50/50) stays coherent.

## 3. General Insertion Rule

When additional documents are required under an existing prefix and the current numeric band has no more room (or further additions would produce unreadable, non-sequential, or sparse numbering):

- Append a 3-digit subfield to create an extended address: `Tier.prefix.subfield_{slug}.md`

- The subfield is zero-padded to three digits (e.g., `010`, `020`, `030`).

- Default stride is 10. Gaps may be reserved for anticipated future use and must be recorded in the tier's ledger (see below).

- Deeper nesting is permitted if a sub-band itself fills: `Tier.prefix.subfield.subsubfield_*`.

- The corresponding identifier (HLR, row key, etc.) adopts the matching extended form (e.g., `HLR-20.xxx.yyy-*` or `40.510.yyy`).

- Example (20 tier): Under the legacy band `20.101`, a new document becomes `20.101.010_envelope_guards.md` with identifier `HLR-20.101.010-*`.

This rule applies uniformly to any tier or band that requires expansion.

## 4. Ledger, Inventory, and Update Requirements

- Each tier maintains a lightweight "next-free subfield" ledger inside its primary inventory or traceability document:
  - 20: `20.200_traceability_matrix.md`
  - 50: `50.00_design_traceability_index.md`
  - 30: `30.01_verification_inventory_index.md` (for its coupled bands)
  - 40: Equivalent ledger (to be maintained in the 40 governance documents, e.g., the master program guide or refactor program doc) when 40 bands require subfields.
  - 00: As needed for governance foundations.

- The ledger records the next available subfield value (and any reserved gaps) per crowded prefix.

- **Same-change-set rule**: 
  - Add the new subfield document.
  - Update the tier's inventory/traceability index (30.01, 50.00, 20.200, etc.).
  - Advance the ledger entry.
  All must occur in one atomic change set.

- Update inventories for structural changes (new/renamed/removed sub-banded items). Content-only edits within existing documents do not trigger inventory updates.

## 5. No Renumbering of Legacy Items

Existing documents using shorter prefixes (`20.xxx_*`, `40.xxx_*`, `50.xx_*`, etc.) are considered frozen. New documents under a full or crowded band must use the subfield extension rather than triggering a renumber of prior items.

This policy eliminates the need for large-scale renumbering efforts when growth occurs.

## 6. Relation to CI and Validation

The CI policy and structural checks defined in [00.00.41_documentation_tier_map_and_ci_policy.md](00.00.41_documentation_tier_map_and_ci_policy.md) must accommodate the generalized subfield forms.

- Validation scripts (e.g., `validate_30_inventory_index.py`, `validate_50_traceability_index.py`, `validate_30_10_50_pairing.py`, and the design traceability workflow) must be extended to correctly parse and compare dotted subfield addresses (`xxx.yyy`) in both filesystem paths and inventory tables.

- CI checks remain filesystem ↔ table diffs. They should continue to enforce that addition of a new subfield document is accompanied by the required inventory and ledger updates (blocking or warning mode as appropriate per the existing table in 00.00.41).

- The 30 ↔ 10.50 pairing and 50 design traceability rules continue to apply to sub-banded items within the coupled group.

- Allowlisted exceptions (governance bands, etc.) remain as before.

- A new or extended check may be added to verify that subfield usage is accompanied by ledger advancement, but the core "do not block routine content work" principle is preserved.

- GitHub Actions workflows that invoke these scripts do not require structural changes beyond ensuring the updated scripts are used; the blocking/warning modes stay the same.

## 7. Examples by Tier

- **20 (independent)**: `20.101.010_envelope_guards.md` (new insertion under legacy 20.101). Update 20.200 ledger and matrix.
- **40 (independent)**: `40.510.010_new_orchestration.md` (if a 40.510 band fills). Maintain ledger in 40 governance docs.
- **50 / 30 / 10.50 (coupled)**: `50.39.010_new_design.md` promotes to `30.39.010_*` and corresponding `10.50.39.010_*`. All updates coordinated through 50.00, 30.01, and 10.50 anchors per the coupled rules.

## 8. Relation to Other Governance Documents

- This policy is the single source of truth for addressing extensions.
- 00.00.41 references this document for numbering rules within the tier map and CI policy.
- Tier-specific inventories (20.200, 50.00, 30.01, and 40 equivalents) implement the ledgers and same-change-set requirements.
- Cross-tier links always use explicit inventory rows rather than assuming digit equality.

Future growth in any tier follows this subfield extension mechanism to keep the overall system stable and the defined couplings (30/10.50/50) and independences (40, 20) intact.
