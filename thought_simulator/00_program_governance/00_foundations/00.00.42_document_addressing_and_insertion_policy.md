# 00.00.42 Document Addressing and Insertion Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the **general, repository-wide rule for hierarchical document and directory addressing** across the entire `thought_simulator/` tree (and supporting governance in `00_program_governance/`).

Every document and every subdirectory receives a unique, stable, traceable identifier — functioning like a **zip code + street address + apartment/suite number**, a **mailing address**, a **phone number with extensions**, or an **IP address with subnetting and host portions**.

- The primary numeric prefix (e.g., `20.40`, `40.100`, `50.05`, `10.50.102`) acts as the "zip code + street".
- Subfield extensions (e.g., `.010`) provide the fine-grained "apartment" or "host" for insertions.
- This system guarantees traceability: any file or directory can be precisely located, referenced, inventoried, and grown over time without breaking existing links, HLR/LLR identifiers, promotion records, or cross-tier maps.

The core objective is controlled growth: new files or subdirectories can always be inserted in their logical position even when consecutive primary prefixes are already taken, **without ever renumbering legacy items**.

This is the single source of truth for addressing. It applies uniformly to the independent tiers (20, 40, 50) and the coupled verification/requirements pair (30 ↔ 10.50).

**Controlled renames** of existing addresses (without insertion) follow [00.00.43_controlled_identity_rename_policy.md](00.00.43_controlled_identity_rename_policy.md) and the identity name tables in `00_program_governance/00_identity/`.

## 2. Tier Coupling and Independence (Current Policy)

Numbering authority follows the independence rules established in [00.00.41](00.00.41_documentation_tier_map_and_ci_policy.md) and the tier-specific guides (40.05, 30.00, 50.05):

- **Fully independent tiers** (standalone naming namespaces):
  - **20** (requirements): Owns its own numbering completely.
  - **40** (playground / exploratory modules): Owns its own numbering completely. A `40.xxx` module does not force any particular number on 20, 30, 10.50, or 50.
  - **50** (design): Owns its own numbering completely for design specs and supporting documents.

- **Coupled pair (name must match)**:
  - **Only 30 and 10.50** must share the identical numeric band.
  - 10.50 (in `10_thought_simulator_req/50_design/`) is the canonical requirements anchor and comes first.
  - 30 verification modules take their band from the corresponding 10.50 peer (see `validate_30_10_50_pairing.py` and 30.00).

- **00** (governance) provides the overarching addressing policy and ledgers but does not dictate content numbers in the independent tiers.

This separation means that growth and insertion rules (this document) apply locally within each independent namespace or within the 30/10.50 coupled pair. Cross-tier "same number" alignment is no longer required except where explicitly mandated for the 30/10.50 pair.

## 3. Insertion Rule When Consecutive Prefixes Are Taken (The Core Policy)

**Primary rule**: Primary numeric prefixes (the part before any subfield) should remain as dense and human-readable as possible. When two consecutive primary prefixes are already occupied and a new file **or subdirectory** must be inserted logically between them, do **not** renumber existing items.

Instead, extend the earlier prefix using additional dotted numeric subfield segment(s). The first extension level typically uses a three-digit value (zero-padded, e.g. `010`), but the mechanism supports arbitrary further nesting when needed. This can result in four, five, or even six (or more) total numeric parts in the prefix.

- Format: `Tier.primary.sub1.sub2..._{slug}` (files) or with trailing `/` (directories). Each subfield segment is normally three digits (010, 020, ...).

- Example (user-specified consecutive case):
  - `20.40` and `20.41` already exist.
  - Need to insert between them → `20.40.010_new_item.md` or `20.40.010_new_module/`

- If the `.010` sub-band later fills and you need to insert between `20.40.010` and `20.40.020`, use a further level: `20.40.010.005_...` (or `20.40.010.005.001_...` etc.).

- Default stride at each level is 10 to leave insertion room.

- Identifiers adopt the full dotted address: `HLR-20.40.010-*`, `LLR-50.36.010.005-001`, `40.510.010.020-*` etc.

This rule applies to **both files and subdirectories** in every tier. There is no fixed limit on the number of dotted parts.

The full dotted form (e.g. `20.40.010`, `20.40.010.005`, `50.36.010.020.001`) is the stable, unique, traceable "address" for that item.

### Why this works like an addressing system
- **Zip-code / street level**: The primary band (`20.40`, `40.100`, `10.50.102`).
- **Apartment / suite / host**: The subfield extension level(s) (`.010`, `.010.005`, etc.). The system supports as many levels as needed (4, 5, or 6+ total parts).
- Traceability is preserved everywhere: inventories, cross-references, promotion records, CI checks, HLR/LLR tables, and human navigation all use the full dotted address.
- No renumbering cascades ever needed when the main sequence fills between two consecutive numbers.

This is the universal insertion mechanism for the entire `thought_simulator/` tree.

## 4. Ledger, Inventory, and Update Requirements

Every tier (or coupled pair) that uses subfields must maintain a lightweight "next-free subfield" ledger inside its primary inventory or traceability document:

- 20 (independent): `20.200_traceability_matrix.md` (or equivalent)
- 50 (independent): `50.00_design_traceability_index.md`
- 30/10.50 (coupled pair): `30.01_verification_inventory_index.md` + the 10.50 files themselves
- 40 (independent): Maintained in 40 governance (e.g., `40.05_master_program_guide.md` and/or `40.510_refactor.md` program rows)
- 00 (governance): As needed in the foundations documents

The ledger records, per crowded primary prefix:
- Next available value at the current deepest subfield level (segments are normally three digits)
- Any intentionally reserved gaps at each level

The system supports growing the depth as needed (e.g. moving from 3-part to 4-part, 5-part, etc. prefixes).

**Same-change-set rule (mandatory)**:
- Create the new file or subdirectory using the subfield address.
- Update the relevant inventory/traceability index (and any affected 30/10.50 pairing records).
- Advance (or create) the ledger entry for that primary prefix.
All three must be committed together in one change set.

Only structural changes (new/renamed/removed items) trigger inventory/ledger updates. Pure content edits inside an existing document do not.

## 5. No Renumbering of Legacy Items

Existing documents using shorter prefixes (`20.xxx_*`, `40.xxx_*`, `50.xx_*`, etc.) are considered frozen. New documents under a full or crowded band must use the subfield extension rather than triggering a renumber of prior items.

This policy eliminates the need for large-scale renumbering efforts when growth occurs.

## 6. Relation to CI, Validation, and Traceability

All structural validation, inventory, and traceability tooling (see [00.00.41](00.00.41_documentation_tier_map_and_ci_policy.md)) **must** treat the full dotted address (including three-digit subfields) as the canonical identifier.

- Scripts such as `validate_doc_naming_prefixes.py`, `validate_30_inventory_index.py`, `validate_50_traceability_index.py`, `validate_30_10_50_pairing.py`, `validate_20_traceability_matrix.py`, and the design traceability workflow must correctly parse, sort, and compare addresses of the forms:
  - `20.40`
  - `20.40.010`
  - `50.36.010`
  - `10.50.102.010`
  - `40.510.010`

- The naming prefix validator must accept valid subfield extensions under the appropriate tier (e.g., anything starting with `40.` under the 40 tier is allowed; the third level is legal).

- CI remains primarily "filesystem state vs. declared inventory/ledger" diffs. Addition of a subfield item without the corresponding inventory + ledger update in the same change set is a failure (blocking or warning per the table in 00.00.41).

- The 30 ↔ 10.50 pairing rule applies to sub-banded items exactly as to primary-band items: a promoted/approved `30.40.010_*` requires a matching `10.50.40.010_*` (or the band is recorded as pending).

- 40 and 50, being independent, maintain their own subfield ledgers; there is no automatic requirement that a `40.270.010` item produce a `30.40.010` or `50.40.010`.

- The "no renumbering of legacy" principle is absolute for CI purposes.

## 7. Traceability Metaphor and Repository-Wide Scope

The addressing system created by this policy turns the entire `thought_simulator/` tree into a single, navigable, version-controlled namespace:

- Primary band = coarse location (zip code + street)
- Subfield = precise location (apartment / room / host)
- Full dotted form + slug = globally unique, human- and machine-readable address

Every document and every subdirectory is addressable. Every cross-reference, every HLR/LLR, every row in 30.01 or 50.00, every promotion record, and every CI check uses (or can use) this address. This gives complete, stable traceability for the life of the project without periodic global renumbering exercises.

## 8. Concrete Examples

**Consecutive primary prefixes case (user-specified scenario)**:
- `20.40_*` and `20.41_*` already exist (as files or subdirectories).
- Need to insert a new item between them.
- Correct: `20.40.010_intermediate_artifact.md` or `20.40.010_new_module/`
- The full address `20.40.010` is now the stable identifier for that item.

**Other examples**:
- 20 (independent): `20.101.010_envelope_guards.md` under a crowded legacy band. Update the 20 ledger/matrix.
- 40 (independent): `40.100.010_additional_struct_variant.md` or a new `40.100.010_supporting_prototype/` (40 numbering is free).
- 50 (independent): `50.05.010_special_case_construction_note.md` (50 chooses its own sub-bands).
- Coupled (30/10.50): A new verification module under band 102 becomes `30.102.010_*` only after (or together with) `10.50.102.010_*`. 40 may have supplied evidence from `40.777_*` — the 40 number is irrelevant to the 30/10.50 address.

All examples follow the "no renumbering" rule.

## 9. Relation to Other Governance Documents

- This policy (`00.00.42`) is the **single source of truth** for the addressing and subfield insertion mechanism across the whole repository.
- [00.00.41](00.00.41_documentation_tier_map_and_ci_policy.md) references this document for generalized subfield support in the tier map and CI policy.
- Tier-specific process guides (40.05, 30.00, 50.05) describe how the addressing system is used within their flows.
- Inventories and ledgers (30.01, 50.00, 20 traceability matrix, 40 program docs) are the operational implementation of this policy.
- Cross-tier links and promotion records must use the full dotted address when subfields are in play; they never assume that a 40 number equals a 30 or 50 number.

All future structural growth in `thought_simulator/` follows this policy so that the system remains stable, traceable, and respectful of both the independent tiers and the single 30/10.50 name-coupling rule.
