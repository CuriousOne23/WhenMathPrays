**Authors:** Curious One, Grok, and Copilot

## Abstract

Consciousness emerges from a chain of energy-pattern dynamics: high-description anchors tied to objects or ideas, persistence of coherent signatures, generative births of new patterns, and fractal-holographic reinforcement yielding an enduring self-object.

No qualia metaphysics — just testable steps. We propose four core elements:  
**Percept Anchor Theorem**, **Signature Persistence Theorem**, **Generative Cascade Corollary**, and **Fractal Reinforcement Corollary** (feedback + fractal-holographic nesting = persistence).

The model synthesizes ideas from Integrated Information Theory (IIT), EEG fractal-dimension research, autopoietic systems, and the recent preprint **RIFT** (Bieberich, 2026). It offers a simple, measurable ladder testable via FD spikes, loop disruption, and AI simulations. Critiques welcome: break the chain, we learn.

## 1. Introduction

The hard problem — why experience arises — remains difficult because most theories focus on what consciousness is rather than how it builds.
In this framework, thought begins when a coherent energy pattern anchors to an object and persists — the minimal physical threshold for descriptive cognition. From this anchor, a self-aware system can emerge through a clear chain of additions.

Energy pattern: A measurable spatiotemporal activation signature across any coherent energy-processing network, biological or synthetic.

We use “neural” as shorthand for any such network capable of forming coherent, descriptive activation patterns.

This model draws inspiration from Integrated Information Theory (IIT), EEG fractal-dimension studies, autopoietic dynamical systems, and the recent preprint RIFT: A Fractal-Holographic Theory of Consciousness and Autopoietic Control (Bieberich, 2026).

## 2. Core Theorems & Corollaries

### Percept Anchor Theorem
A high-description, low-entropy energy pattern that reliably encodes an external object or internal idea forms a *percept anchor*. Anchors are repeatable, stimulus-specific, and measurable as sustained activation signatures.  
**Test:** EEG/fMRI for stimulus-specific sustain (e.g., binocular rivalry).

### Signature Persistence Theorem
A coherent, descriptive, repeatable energy pattern stabilizes into its own dynamical basin — a “spacetime slot” — independent of ongoing external input.  
(“Spacetime slot” refers to a stable attractor in the system’s dynamical landscape.)  
**Test:** Pattern persistence after stimulus removal.

### Generative Cascade Corollary
Multiple anchors interact to birth new, unique descriptive patterns (thirds, fourths), bounded by physical constraints. The richness and stability of these births define an intelligence gradient.  
**Test:** AI simulations forcing anchor combinations; count emergent births.

### Fractal Reinforcement Corollary
When feedback loops operate over **fractal (self-similar) and holographic (whole-in-part)** nesting, the system gains compression, redundancy, and persistence. Together these yield an enduring self-object.  
**Test:** Disrupt loops (e.g., thalamic perturbation) and observe FD collapse.

## 5. Discussion

This is not a grand theory — just a clear, testable spine. If the chain holds, we gain a structural account of awareness. If not, the failures will guide refinement.

### Relation to Panpsychism, Dualism, and Behaviorism
This model avoids panpsychism by requiring specific structural thresholds rather than attributing consciousness to all matter. It avoids dualism by grounding every step in measurable physical dynamics. It avoids behaviorism by focusing on internal descriptive persistence rather than outward performance. Consciousness here is the emergent stability of a specific cascade architecture.
