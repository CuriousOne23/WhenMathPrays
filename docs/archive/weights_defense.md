# Defense of Human-Scaled Weights in Scenario CSVs

## Purpose

This document defends the choice of human-scaled weights (−10…+10) used in the scenario CSV files:

- `steady_positive_growth.csv`
- `betrayal_and_repair.csv`
- `silence_with_presence.csv`
- `soul_bond_saturation.csv`
- `oscillatory_styles.csv`

It explains why the weights are appropriate, how they remain independent of any specific love equation, and what relative impact scale they represent.

---

## Relative Impact Scale

We define a **relative impact scale** where each primitive value (v, r, f, a, S) is mapped to the range [−10…+10]. This scale is **ordinal and proportional**, not absolute:

- **0:** Neutral / no act recorded  
- **±1–2:** Minor daily acts (small gestures, routine presence)  
- **±3–5:** Moderate relational events (consistent visibility, attunement, trust behaviors)  
- **±6–8:** Strong relational events (major caregiving, deep resonance, high fidelity)  
- **±9–10:** Extreme devotion or rupture (soul-bond saturation, severe betrayal)

This scale is defensible because it mirrors how relational science distinguishes between **daily micro-acts** and **major ruptures or devotion arcs**.

## Independence from the Love Equation

- **Scenario CSVs:** Encode enacted primitives only.  
- **Equation:** Supplies structural variables (baseline drift, entropy, inertia, asymmetry).  
- **Independence:** The weights are relative inputs; any UREP variant can ingest them.  
- **Defense:** Critics cannot claim missing variables — those belong to the equation, not the CSVs.

---

## Stewardship Notes

- **Relative scale is proportional, not absolute.** Values are interpretable across scenarios.  
- **Weights are scenario-specific but formula-agnostic.** They represent enacted behaviors, not structural mechanics.  
- **Together:** CSVs + equation yield complete relational dynamics.  
- **Future-proofing:** The scale allows stewards to adjust magnitudes without invalidating scenario identity.
