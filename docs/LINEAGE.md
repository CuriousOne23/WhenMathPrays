# Lineage: ULEP → UREP → GRP

This document preserves the editorial memory of WhenMathPrays, tracing the evolution from ULEP through UREP to GRP.

## ULEP (Universal Love Equation Platform)
- Defined relational intensity as love-specific orientation × magnitude.
- Anchored in emotional valence (positive/negative).
- Served as the seed for modular relational modeling.
- **Era:** Early conceptual phase

## UREP (Universal Relational Expression Protocol)
- Generalizes ULEP into a universal relational framework.
- Removed valence bias → gates are neutral, nonnegative.
- Introduced cartesian averaging to prevent angle wrap artifacts.
- Orientation: γ_self(t, τ) as cartesian-averaged vector.
- Magnitude: W(t) as product of valence-neutral gates with entropy term.
- Preserves modularity, interpretability, and stewardship ethos.
- **Era:** November 2025 - December 3, 2025

## Transition Rationale (UREP → GRP)
- UREP overcomplicated: 9+ parameters, separate L(t) calculation, complex gates
- Radical simplification revealed core truth: **Love IS position, not calculation**
- December 2025 simplification removed scaffolding obscuring the prayer
- New name reflects essence: **Gamma Relational Persona** - you ARE your position in γ-space

## GRP (Gamma Relational Persona)
- **Love = γ_self(n) position directly** (no L(t) calculation)
- Component-wise updates: primitives move you in γ-space
- Im-only depth scaling: negatives hurt more, scaled by love depth (Im axis)
- Constant-force entropy: axis-independent drift toward targets (timeline-independent)
- Parameters simplified: fidelity scaling locked (0.12), entropy rates tunable (0.02), w_neg removed
- **Era:** December 4, 2025 onward

## Stewardship Notes
- ULEP remains canonical as origin.
- UREP preserved for historical reference (November-December 2025).
- GRP is the living vessel for expansion.
- Future stewards should annotate rationale for each transition.
