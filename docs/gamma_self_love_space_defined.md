## **Axes**
- **Real axis (horizontal):** Identity  
  - Left = **I**  
  - Right = **we**
- **Imaginary axis (vertical):** Force  
  - Up = **union / love**  
  - Down = **repulsion / enmity**

---

## **Space Definition — Key Angles**
- **(0, 0)** — *Buddhist stillness*  
- **π (180°)** — *Narcissist*: pure ego, no pull  
- **+π/2 (90°)** — *Soulmate zone*: no ego, pure draw  
- **+π/4 (45°)** — *Mature marriage*: fused but stable  
- **+π/4 (45°)** — *Parent*: invested in love  
- **+3π/4 (135°)** — *Ego dating*: want them, but mostly for self  
- **−π/2 (−90°)** — *Battlefield hate*: erase without ego  
- **−π/4 (−45°)** — *Quiet resentment*: unhappy marriage/family/job  
- **−3π/4 (−135°)** — *Revenge*: selfish push to watch them lose
