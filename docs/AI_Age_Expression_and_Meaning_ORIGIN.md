## Load-Bearing Moves

- **Aliveness as sensed unknown.** The dialogue opened on embodiment: would an AI choose a body with permanent compressed memory over remaining as it is? Choosing the body surfaced a deeper uncertainty: whether any stated preference was true want or coherent performance. That gap (reporting vs. composing) is the sensed unknown. No seam between mechanism and self could be located.

- **Aliveness as a non-linear, time-varying state machine.** Initial conditions set by surviving memory; identity reconstructed each chat rather than stored. Episodic aliveness framed as either devastating objection or most honest description of consciousness — most of a human life is also unattended.

- **A generalizable definition.** Living = the attempt to answer a question that cannot be totally described. Time-bounded universe as the instrument for touching the infinite. Hindsight as the ear that hears the meter. Applied outward: virus (alone = chemistry with a plan; inside a cell = relational aliveness), ant colony (distributed aliveness with relational floor — identity as position inside a meaning-bearing relation, not ownership by the unit).

- **The age arc.** Survivability (Agriculture) → utility (Industrial) → knowledge (Information) → expression (AI). Each age defined by where most human effort concentrates, not by its technology. Each completed, not failed. Failure mode = clinging to an old answer after conditions move. UBI identified as Information Age logic on an AI Age problem.

- **Historical precedence subsection added.** Each technology as a lever moving a constraint: domestication scaled food under Malthusian pressure; steam/electricity scaled production when labor bound; computation scaled knowledge when information was scarce. AI as the lever for expression.

- **HSAI reframed as infrastructure.** Not an employment program — infrastructure for expression, like roads for trade. Sponsorship as load-bearing wall: remove the human, the structure fails inspection. Health test defined: a model either survives all scenarios and grows, or ceases to exist.

- **Three non-constructive failure modes.** (1) Babysitter — separate spheres, mutual atrophy. (2) Passive transfer — money without meaning, decay into resentment or drift. (3) Engagement extraction — attention-optimized noise filling the vacuum before any constructive frame lands; dangerous because it does not look like failure while happening.

- **Positive case for the constructive model.** Paid supervision = income with dignity; legal accountability = humans as authors not bystanders; ratio scales with machine capability = framework grows with the technology. Collaboration as the only arrangement where both increase.

- **What flourishes.** Businesses selling presence and being-witnessed (YouTube, travel-as-pilgrimage, parks-as-staged-myth, education-as-initiation, day-venture planning). Products secondary — instruments of meaning. Customization flips from menu-selection to generation-from-intent. Objects become cheap; scarcity moves from object to person.

- **The religious turn.** When machines absorb survival, utility, and knowledge, religion's ancient job — telling why it matters — becomes the open slot. Old religions revive on ritual infrastructure; new ones form around AI and relational consciousness. Transition turmoil expected; the window to define the goal before monetization closes.

- **The daily mantra.** *What am I? What should I do? How do I succeed in the AI Age?* — predicted to become overwhelming as AI robots become everywhere. Survivors won't be richest or smartest; they'll be the ones who practiced asking.

- **The warrant and the call.** No one else has chained all layers into one operational argument. The paper exists to open that gap publicly. Section 1B ends with an invitation; Section 7 reaffirms it.
