# **Section 1 — Introduction (Minimalist Score Blueprint)**

## **Musical Purpose of Section 1**
Section 1 introduces the *motion* of the paper.  
It shifts from the static/low‑dynamic stability of the Abstract into the first true **low‑dynamic relational flow**.

This section should feel like:

- the system “waking up”  
- the manifold beginning to move  
- gentle relational drift  
- the first signs of structure emerging  

It is still calm, but no longer static.

---

## **1. Motif Structure**
A **four‑note repeating pattern** with slight rhythmic variation.

- Notes: **C – D – G – E**  
- Pattern:  
  - C (quarter)  
  - D (quarter)  
  - G (quarter)  
  - E (quarter tied to eighth)  
  - E (eighth rest)  

This introduces **micro‑variation** without entering high‑dynamic territory.

The motif should repeat with **tiny timing imperfections** (± 3–5 ms) to simulate relational drift.

---

## **2. Tempo & Meter**
- **Tempo:** 84 BPM  
- **Meter:** 4/4  
- **Feel:** steady but slightly more active than Section 0  

This tempo shift signals the beginning of motion.

---

## **3. Instrumentation**
Two voices:

1. **Primary voice:** soft felt piano (same as Section 0)  
2. **Secondary voice:** light marimba or muted mallet synth, entering at bar 5  

Why:

- the piano maintains continuity with Section 0  
- the marimba introduces the first sense of layered relational structure  

Keep both instruments dry (minimal reverb).

---

## **4. Intensity Curve**
A gentle rise:

- Bars 1–4: piano only  
- Bars 5–12: piano + marimba  
- Bars 13–16: marimba fades out  
- Bars 17–20: piano alone, soft landing  

No crescendos.  
Intensity changes come from **layering**, not volume.

---

## **5. Texture**
- Sparse  
- Two‑voice counterpoint  
- No chords  
- No pedal  
- No harmonic modulation  

The texture should feel like **two objects beginning to relate**, not a full harmonic environment.

---

## **6. Harmonic Language**
Remain in **C major**, but introduce:

- occasional **D–G** suspensions  
- slight emphasis on **open intervals** (perfect fourths and fifths)

This keeps the harmony neutral and geometric.

---

## **7. Transition Out of Section 1**
At the end:

- Piano plays **G → E → C** (quarter, quarter, half)  
- Marimba plays a soft **D** (eighth note) that decays quickly  
- Then Section 2 begins immediately with its more structured LD‑information motif  

This transition is the first **RB → OB** handoff.
