# **Section 3 — Static Information (Minimalist Score Blueprint)**

## **Musical Purpose of Section 3**
This section represents **static information** — objects, definitions, fixed relations, and the absence of motion.

Musically, this must feel:

- stable  
- unmoving  
- almost architectural  
- low‑entropy  
- low‑dynamic  
- precise  
- unchanging  

This is the most “frozen” section of the entire composition.

---

## **1. Motif Structure**
A **two‑note alternating pattern**, extremely stable.

- Notes: **C – G**  
- Pattern:  
  - C (half note)  
  - G (half note)  
- Repeat with **no variation**  
- No rhythmic drift  
- No syncopation  
- No ornamentation  

This expresses the **binary, object‑like nature** of static information.

---

## **2. Tempo & Meter**
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM  
- **Meter:** 2/2 (“cut time”)  
- **Feel:** slow, architectural, grid‑like  

This creates a sense of **frozen structure**.

---

## **3. Instrumentation**
One instrument only:

- **Pure sine‑wave synth** *or* **very soft organ**

Why:

- no attack transients  
- no emotional coloration  
- no expressive dynamics  
- perfect for representing “static objects”  

Avoid piano here — it has too much expressive decay.

---

## **4. Intensity Curve**
Flat.  
Absolutely flat.

- No crescendos  
- No added layers  
- No density changes  
- No timbral shifts  

This is the **lowest‑entropy** section of the entire score.

---

## **5. Texture**
- Single voice  
- No harmony  
- No counterpoint  
- No pedal  
- No reverb (or extremely minimal)  

The texture must feel **mathematical**, not musical.

---

## **6. Harmonic Language**
Remain strictly in **C and G**.

No thirds.  
No color tones.  
No extensions.  
No modulations.

This is the **purest harmonic reduction** in the entire composition.

---

## **7. Transition Out of Section 3**
At the end:

- Hold **C** for a full measure  
- Add a very soft **E** (quarter note) at the end of the measure  
- This E is the **first sign of LD‑information** emerging  
- Immediately begin Section 4’s more active motif  

This is the **static → LD transition**.
