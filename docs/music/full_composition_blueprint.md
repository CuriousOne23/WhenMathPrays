- global intensity  
- curvature

This blueprint ensures that the entire score is **coherent, reproducible, and aligned** with the manuscript’s dynamic geometry.

6. Mapping Loop  
7. Cognitive Spacesuit  
8. Basin Navigation  
9. Implications for Science

The score follows a **single continuous arc** defined by:

- **Intensity** (height of conceptual activity)

- **Vector flow** (direction + magnitude of conceptual motion)  

These are visualized in `full_comp.md`.

# **3. Global Key Map**

The score uses a **minimal key architecture** to maintain conceptual clarity.

- **C major** → object‑like, static, structural  
- **C Lydian** → horizon expansion (Section 8)  
- **A minor** → agency, teleology (Section 11)  

Key transitions are intentionally sparse to avoid musical narrative overpowering conceptual structure.

# **4. Global Instrumentation Arc**

This is the **semantic evolution** of the instrumentation across the score.

### **0–3: Object‑like regime**
- Piano + pad only  
- No embodiment  
- No meaning  
- No perturbation  

### **4–6: Emergence**
- Piano becomes active  
- Marimba enters at 5  
- Sub‑bass enters at 6  

### **7–9: Geometric peak**
- Full system active  
- Airy synth enters at 8  
- Woodblock enters at 9  

### **10–11: Structural agency**
- Piano + marimba + pad  
- Airy synth returns at 11  
- No sub‑bass  
- No woodblock  

### **12–13: Meaning → resolution**
- Piano + pad + airy synth  
- Marimba returns only in 13  
- No embodiment  
- No perturbation  

This arc is detailed in `instrumentation_overlay.md` and `instrumentation_overview.md`.
