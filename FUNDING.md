# Funding

WhenMathPrays is an open-source project built and maintained by volunteers. We operate without salaries, advertising, or investor backing.

## Support the Mission

If this work resonates with you, consider supporting its continued development:

- **Open Collective:** [opencollective.com/whenmathprays](https://opencollective.com/whenmathprays)
- **GitHub Sponsors:** [github.com/sponsors/WhenMathPrays](https://github.com/sponsors/WhenMathPrays)
- **Patreon:** Support ongoing research and development
- **Ko-fi:** One-time contributions welcome

All funding goes directly toward:
- Server costs and infrastructure
- Documentation and educational materials
- Community support and outreach
- Research and model refinement

## Philosophy

This project exists to build open, living mathematics—models that trace emotion's arc with transparency and heart. No patents. No paywalls. Just resonance.

Thank you for being part of this journey.
