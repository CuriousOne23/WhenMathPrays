# 04.04 Fuzzy Embeddings

## 1. Purpose
Define the requirements for fuzzy embeddings — the continuous, high-dimensional vector representation that carries the semantic, contextual, and feature content of a ThoughtPoint.

## 2. Core Requirements

**EM-01: Structure and Properties**
- Every ThoughtPoint must maintain a fuzzy embedding vector $\mathbf{e} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ (recommended default dimensionality: 128–512).
- Embeddings must be **continuous and graded** (real-valued activations, not strictly binary).
- Must support superposition (multiple concepts active simultaneously with varying strengths).

**EM-02: Coupling with Other Systems**
- Embedding norm $\|\mathbf{e}\|$ must be coupled to the ThoughtPoint’s activation energy (see 04.03_energy_dynamics.md).
- Changes in energy due to splitting, merging, or basin effects must appropriately scale the embedding.
- Normalized entropy $H_{\%}$ must be computed primarily from the embedding distribution, but remain independent of raw norm changes.

**EM-03: Operations**

- **In Object Basins (OBs)**: Must support strong **sharpening and binding** — coherent dimensions are amplified, weak/irrelevant ones suppressed, resulting in clearer gestalts.
- **In Relational Basins (RBs)**: Must support **smooth drift and transformation** along relational gradients.
- **During Splitting (Fanout)**: The parent embedding must be partitioned/specialized and diluted proportionally across branches.
- **During Merging (Fanin)**: Incoming embeddings must be combined using a defined method (weighted average, vector sum + normalization, or interference-style).

**EM-04: Filtering and Routing**
- RB entry filters must operate on fuzzy similarity metrics (cosine similarity, dot product, or Mahalanobis distance).
- Filters must be **soft/probabilistic** rather than hard thresholds.
- Must support both single-token and multi-dimensional pattern matching.

## 3. Visualization and Observability
- Must support projection from high-D to 2D/3D space for visualization (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP, or custom learned projector).
- All major embedding transformations (sharpening, drift, split, merge) must be logged with:
  - Before/after vectors (or key statistics)
  - Top active dimensions
  - Norm change
  - Similarity scores

## 4. Capacity and Stability
- Must enforce per-basin embedding norm limits.
- Must apply saturation or compression when merging exceeds basin capacity.
- Must prevent embedding explosion or vanishing gradients.

## 5. Testability Requirements
- Must demonstrate smooth continuous change in RBs vs. rapid collapse/sharpening in OBs.
- Round-trip splitting + merging must preserve total activation mass within tolerance.
- Filter behavior must be measurable and reproducible.
