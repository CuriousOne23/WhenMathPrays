# Glossary

## Core Terms

**Relational Manifold**  
The continuous geometric space representing the landscape of thought, defined by potential energy $V(\mathbf{x})$.

**Object Basin (OB)**  
A deep, stable local minimum in the manifold. Represents coherent, discrete objects, concepts, or gestalts.

**Relational Basin (RB)**  
A flatter, higher-potential region connecting basins. Represents relational processing, exploration, and transformation.

**Inquiry Basin**  
A shallow, unstable region that holds unresolved questions and maintains cognitive tension.

**ThoughtPoint**  
The active entity navigating the manifold. Carries position, fuzzy embedding, energy, and entropy state.

**Fuzzy Embedding** ($\mathbf{e}$)  
High-dimensional continuous vector representing the semantic and feature content of a thought.

**Normalized Entropy** ($H_\\%$)  
$H_\\% = \frac{H_{\text{current}}}{H_{\text{initial}}} \times 100\%$ — primary measure of processing completion.

**Fanout**  
Maximum number of simultaneous outgoing branches (splitting) a basin can support.

**Fanin**  
Maximum number of simultaneous incoming branches (merging) a basin can accept.

**Damping Coefficient** ($\gamma$)  
Controls how quickly kinetic energy dissipates. High in OBs, low in RBs.

**Done RB**  
Terminal basin for completed thoughts. Packages final state for output.

**Feeling OB**  
Special Object Basin that attaches emotional valence and somatic markers, especially during stressed completion.

**Highway**  
Special low-damping or lossless path between important basins or partitions.

**Stability vs Instability**  
Core research focus: how the geometry of the manifold produces coherent thought versus chaotic or stuck states.

## Other Terms

- **Saddle**: Transition point between basins.
- **Potential Function** $V(\mathbf{x})$: Defines the energy landscape.
- **Clean Completion**: Low-entropy, high-coherence termination.
- **Stressed Completion**: Time-forced termination with higher remaining entropy.
